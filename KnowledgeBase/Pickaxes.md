# Pickaxes

The **Pickaxe** is a [tool](/Tools "Tools") obtained at the beginning of the game. It is used mainly for breaking [stones](/Stone "Stone"), particularly when [Mining](/Mining "Mining"), although it has other uses and can also remove certain objects. For example, the pickaxe can be used to pick up placed items, such as floor tiles, [sprinklers](/Crafting#Sprinklers "Crafting"), or [artisan equipment](/Crafting#Artisan_Equipment "Crafting"), much like an [axe](/Axes "Axes"). In a similar way, the pickaxe can also strike small plants like [weeds](/Weeds "Weeds") or other [crops](/Crops "Crops") on the farm to remove them.

Only the pickaxe can un-till soil that has previously been tilled (*i.e.,* it can remove strikes from a [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes")). This process is overall done in two stages; striking once will remove any crops on top, leaving the underlying tilled soil intact (including any [fertilizer](/Fertilizer "Fertilizer") present). Striking a second time tilled soil will revert the tile to an un-tilled state, removing any applied [fertilizer](/Fertilizer "Fertilizer"). The pickaxe also applies this two-stage removal process to for items placed on top of floor tiles, while the axe will remove both the object and the floor at once. Also, using the pickaxe on a [Monster](/Monsters "Monsters") will deal a small portion of damage, similar to other [weapons](/Weapons "Weapons"). However, another unique use case is hitting a [Crab](/Rock_Crab "Rock Crab") 5 times with a pickaxe which will knock off its shell, and cause the crab to flee rather than attack the player.

## Grades of Pickaxe

The Pickaxe may be upgraded at the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith"). The cost and resource requirements for each upgrade are shown in the table below. As a summary of the upgrade effects, with each upgrade level the number of hits required to break a stone or [node](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining") is reduced, and it becomes possible to break harder types of rocks.
