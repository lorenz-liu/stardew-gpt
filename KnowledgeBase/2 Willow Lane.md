**2 Willow Lane** is the home of sisters [Emily](/Emily "Emily") and [Haley](/Haley "Haley"). It's located along the southern road in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"), just next to [1 Willow Lane](/1_Willow_Lane "1 Willow Lane") where [Jodi](/Jodi "Jodi"), [Kent](/Kent "Kent") and their sons live.

The home is owned by Haley and Emily's parents, who have been traveling the world for the past two years with an unknown date of return. Haley and Emily have been caring for the house while they've been away.

## Sewing Machine

[![Sewing Interface.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/4d/Sewing_Interface.png/200px-Sewing_Interface.png)](/File:Sewing_Interface.png)

After the player acquires [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth"), exiting the [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") between 6am and 11am on a day when it's not raining will trigger a cutscene in which [Emily](/Emily "Emily") tells them about her sewing machine. Thereafter, the sewing machine can be used to [tailor](/Tailoring "Tailoring") or [dye](/Dyeing "Dyeing") clothing. The dye pots located to the right of the sewing machine are also unlocked.

After completing the ["Rock Rejuvenation"](/Quests#Rock_Rejuvenation "Quests") Story Board quest, the player will receive their own [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") that functions identically to Emily's Sewing Machine.
