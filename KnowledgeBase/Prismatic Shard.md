The **Prismatic Shard** is a [Mineral](/Minerals "Minerals"). It is one of the rarest items in the game, and has a variety of uses, including obtaining the [Galaxy Sword](/Galaxy_Sword "Galaxy Sword").

## Locations

Prismatic shards can be found in:

* 25% chance from a [Mystic Stone](/Mining#Mystic_Stone "Mining") in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") at floors 100+, [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), and [Quarry](/Quarry "Quarry").
* 25% chance from a [meteorite](/Random_Events#Meteorite "Random Events").
* 1/26 (≈3.8%) chance from a [treasure room](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern") in the Skull Cavern.
* 3.5% chance from an [Iridium Node](/Mining#Iridium_Node "Mining") in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), [Quarry](/Quarry "Quarry") and [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon").
* 0.4% chance from an [Omni Geode](/Omni_Geode "Omni Geode").
* 0.4% chance from a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box").
* 0.1% chance from [Serpents](/Serpent "Serpent") and [Mummies](/Mummy "Mummy") (and their dangerous variants). 0.05% chance from [Shadow Brutes](/Shadow_Brute "Shadow Brute") and [Shadow Shaman](/Shadow_Shaman "Shadow Shaman") (and their dangerous variants).
* 0.1% chance from [Wilderness Golems](/Wilderness_Golem "Wilderness Golem") and [Iridium Golems](/Iridium_Golem "Iridium Golem") after reaching [Combat](/Combat "Combat") Level 10.
* 0.79% chance from a [Golden Mystery Box](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box").
* 0.09% daily chance from a [Rainbow Trout](/Rainbow_Trout "Rainbow Trout") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") with a population of at least 9.
* 0.05% chance from any [monster](/Monsters "Monsters") after the player has reached the bottom of [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines").
* ≈0.012% chance from a [Fishing Treasure Chest](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") after reaching [fishing level 6](/Fishing#Fishing_Skill "Fishing").
* One in a chest at the top of the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon") the first time the player arrives there.
* Once per day from the [Statue Of True Perfection](/Statue_Of_True_Perfection "Statue Of True Perfection").
* Purchased from [Emily](/Emily "Emily") for data-sort-value="500">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 500 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg") when she hosts a [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shop "Desert Festival") during the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival").
* From the [Blessing Of The Butterfly](/Statue_Of_Blessings#List_of_Blessings "Statue Of Blessings") (up to a 17.5% chance).

## Uses

* A Prismatic Shard can be used in [the Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") to obtain the [Galaxy Sword](/Galaxy_Sword "Galaxy Sword"). The [Dwarvish Headstone](/Graveyard#Dwarvish_Headstone "Graveyard") in the graveyard contains a riddle explaining how to obtain it.
* A Prismatic Shard is one of the options used to complete [the Missing Bundle](/Bundles#The_Missing_Bundle "Bundles") to unlock the [Movie Theater](/Movie_Theater "Movie Theater").
* A Prismatic Shard can be offered to the [Dark Shrine of Selfishness](/Dark_Shrine_of_Selfishness "Dark Shrine of Selfishness") at the [Witch's Hut](/Witch%27s_Hut "Witch's Hut") to turn [children](/Children "Children") into doves (getting rid of them permanently).
* On [Thursdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Thursday "Days of the Week") the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") sells 1 [Magic Rock Candy](/Magic_Rock_Candy "Magic Rock Candy") for 3 Prismatic Shards.
* Prismatic Shards are used to enchant tools in the [Forge](/Forge "Forge").
* A Prismatic Shard can be placed into a [Junimo Hut](/Junimo_Hut "Junimo Hut") to cause the [Junimo](/Junimos "Junimos") to cycle through the rainbow resembling a Prismatic Shard.
* A Prismatic Shard can be donated to [Gunther](/Gunther "Gunther") at the [Museum](/Museum "Museum").
* A Prismatic Shard can be used in the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") to create prismatic clothing (see [Tailoring](#Tailoring) below).
* Prismatic Shards can be [gifted](/Friendship#Gifts "Friendship") as a universally loved gift (to everyone except [Haley](/Haley "Haley")).
* [Multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer") only: A Prismatic Shard can be used to craft a [Wedding Ring](/Wedding_Ring "Wedding Ring") to propose marriage to another player.

## Quests

4 Prismatic Shards are requested in [Mr. Qi](/Mr._Qi "Mr. Qi")'s [Four Precious Stones](/Quests#Four_Precious_Stones "Quests") quest.

## Notes

* Prismatic Shards cannot be duplicated in a [Crystalarium](/Crystalarium "Crystalarium"). According to the Lost Book *[Technology Report!](/Lost_Books#Technology_Report.21 "Lost Books")*, "the EMF from the shard interacts negatively with the crystalarium."

Can be produced by [Fish Ponds](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond").

Obtained from the [Statue Of True Perfection](/Statue_Of_True_Perfection "Statue Of True Perfection"). One can be obtained at the top of the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon"). Requested in [Four Precious Stones](/Quests#Four_Precious_Stones "Quests").

Can now be obtained from [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box"), [Golden Mystery Box](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box"), [Meteorite](/Random_Events#Meteorite "Random Events"), and [Iridium Golem](/Iridium_Golem "Iridium Golem"). Can now be purchased from [Emily](/Emily "Emily") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival").
* [1.6.3](/Version_History#1.6.3 "Version History"): Reduced rate of the [Meteorite](/Random_Events#Meteorite "Random Events") dropping a Prismatic Shard from 100% to 25%.
