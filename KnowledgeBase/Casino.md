The **Casino** is located in [the Calico Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") accessed through the back door of the [Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis"). Access is initially blocked by the [Bouncer](/Bouncer "Bouncer") until ["The Mysterious Qi" quest](/Quests#The_Mysterious_Qi "Quests") is completed.

[Mr. Qi](/Mr._Qi "Mr. Qi") is constantly present in the room. To his right is a machine to exchange gold for Qi coins, at a rate of data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g per data-sort-value="100">![QiCoin.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/6c/QiCoin.png/24px-QiCoin.png)100, or data-sort-value="10">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)10g per data-sort-value="1">![QiCoin.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/6c/QiCoin.png/24px-QiCoin.png)1 value. Qi coins are used for both gambling and purchasing items inside the Casino, and cannot be converted back to gold.

To the top-left of the room is a terminal that lists multiple statistics for [the Player](/The_Player "The Player").

There are two forms of gambling available, [slot machines](/Slot_Machine "Slot Machine") and a variant of BlackJack, [CalicoJack](/CalicoJack "CalicoJack"). There are two tables for CalicoJack, low stakes (data-sort-value="100">![QiCoin.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/6c/QiCoin.png/24px-QiCoin.png)100) to the left and high stakes (data-sort-value="1000">![QiCoin.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/6c/QiCoin.png/24px-QiCoin.png)1,000) to the right.

In the upper right corner of the room, to the right of the machine for exchanging Qi coins and next to a potted plant, stands a shady individual who offers to sell [Statues Of Endless Fortune](/Statue_Of_Endless_Fortune "Statue Of Endless Fortune") for data-sort-value="1000000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000,000g each.

## Store

The store is located in the top right of the casino.

In some resolution settings, you may be unable to access the store without using the 'use button' or zooming out (go to menu, options).

* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): Store now stocks [Modern Double Bed](/Modern_Double_Bed "Modern Double Bed").
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Casino now has limited stocks of items. Added fireworks, [wallpapers](/Wallpaper "Wallpaper"), and a [flooring](/Flooring "Flooring").
* [1.6.4](/Version_History#1.6.4 "Version History"): Qi club coins now show up in the shop menu.
