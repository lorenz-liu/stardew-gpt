The **Geode** is a [Mineral](/Minerals "Minerals") deposit that contains [minerals](/Minerals "Minerals"), [artifacts](/Artifacts "Artifacts"), ores, or basic [resources](/Category:Resources "Category:Resources"). It can be obtained from breaking rocks above ground in Stardew Valley and in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") (especially floors 1-39), or dropped by [Duggies](/Duggy "Duggy"). It may also be found in [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing"), in the [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") outside the [Museum](/Museum "Museum") or received as a gift at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"). A [Stonefish](/Stonefish "Stonefish") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") may produce 1 or 5 Geodes when its population reaches 9. Geodes can also be obtained by pickaxing [Geode Nodes](/Mining#Geode_Node "Mining"). 2-4 Geodes may occasionally be found in [Supply Crates](/Supply_Crate "Supply Crate") on the [Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps#Beach "Farm Maps") if the player has not upgraded the [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") yet. Geodes cannot be obtained from breaking rocks on Spring 1 of Year 1.[[1]](#cite_note-newgame-1)

The geode can be broken open by [Clint](/Clint "Clint") in the [Blacksmith shop](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith") for data-sort-value="25">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)25g or by using a [Geode Crusher](/Geode_Crusher "Geode Crusher").

Neither daily [luck](/Luck "Luck") nor luck [buffs](/Buffs "Buffs") affect the contents of a Geode.

### Resources

Note that obtaining Iron Ore from a Geode is only possible after reaching level 26 of [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"). Before reaching level 26, a geode that would give iron ore will instead give copper ore. Except for clay and stone, the following items will be obtained in the following amounts: 30% for 1, 30% for 3, 30% for 5, 9% for 10, and 1% for 20. Stone has a 50% chance to use those values and a 50% chance to give only 1. Only 1 clay can be obtained from a single geode.

## Tailoring

Geode is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Gray Hoodie](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt160.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3b/Shirt160.png/24px-Shirt160.png)](/File:Shirt160.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* Two Geodes may be requested by [Sunfish](/Sunfish "Sunfish") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 3 to 5.
* Five Geodes may be requested by [Stonefish](/Stonefish "Stonefish") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 5 to 7.
* Eight Geodes may be requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his fifth request, or as part of any request starting with the sixth.
