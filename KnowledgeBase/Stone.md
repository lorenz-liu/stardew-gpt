**Stone** is a basic [resource](/Category:Resources "Category:Resources") obtained by [mining](/Mining "Mining") rocks with a [pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes"). Stone can also be purchased from [Robin](/Robin "Robin") at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop"), or found in [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing"). [Stone Golems](/Stone_Golem "Stone Golem") and [Gray Slimes](/Slimes#Color-Based_Drops "Slimes") also drop Stone. Robin sells Stone in her [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="1">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 1 [Calico Egg](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg"). Fifty stones can be received as a gift from [Robin](/Robin "Robin") at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"). Stone may be obtained by digging up an [Artifact Spot](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot"). A [Stonefish](/Stonefish "Stonefish") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") may produce 30 Stone when the population reaches 9. Stone can also be acquired by fishing in the fountain left of the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") or gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") dog with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals").

Normal rocks yield between 1 and 3 stones. [Boulders](/Boulder "Boulder") yield 10 stones.

Stone is used in various [crafting](/Crafting "Crafting") recipes and is also required as a form of payment when purchasing certain buildings and building upgrades at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop"). The [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") will sell 1 [Fiber](/Fiber "Fiber") in exchange for 5 Stone on [Tuesdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Tuesday "Days of the Week").

## Purchase Price

The price of stone at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop") increases from data-sort-value="20">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)20g to data-sort-value="100">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100g starting in Year 2. The player will receive a [letter](/Letters "Letters") in the mail on Spring 1 of Year 2 saying "Notice: The price of raw materials at Robin's shop and [Clint's shop](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith") has increased." The gold obtained by selling stone to Robin or through the [Shipping Bin](/Shipping "Shipping") is unchanged.

## Bundles

99 Stone are required for the ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Construction Bundle](/Bundles#Construction_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Crafts Room](/Bundles#Crafts_Room "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Stone is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [![Shirt029.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/6f/Shirt029.png/24px-Shirt029.png)](/File:Shirt029.png) [Work Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring").

## Quests

* Robin may ask the player to mine 1000 pieces of stone within one week in the ["Robin's Resource Rush"](/Quests#Robin.27s_Resource_Rush "Quests") special order. The player may keep the harvested stone. If completed on time, Robin will mail the farmer the recipe to create a [Stone Chest](/Stone_Chest "Stone Chest").

## Tip

* One method of gathering large quantities of stone is repeatedly entering and exiting the 1st level of [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") and only breaking [boulders](/Boulder "Boulder") and silver rocks.

Added [Stone Sign](/Stone_Sign "Stone Sign") and [Garden Pot](/Garden_Pot "Garden Pot") recipes.
* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Used as a building material to the [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") and as an ingredient to the [Tree Fertilizer](/Tree_Fertilizer "Tree Fertilizer") and [Brick Floor](/Brick_Floor "Brick Floor") recipes. Can now be used in [Tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). Can be produced by Fish Ponds.
* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): Can now be used as decoration in [Fish Tanks](/Fish_Tank "Fish Tank"). Used as an ingredient to the [Bone Mill](/Bone_Mill "Bone Mill"), [Deluxe Retaining Soil](/Deluxe_Retaining_Soil "Deluxe Retaining Soil"), [Stone Walkway Floor](/Stone_Walkway_Floor "Stone Walkway Floor"), [Geode Crusher](/Geode_Crusher "Geode Crusher"), [Stone Chest](/Stone_Chest "Stone Chest"), and [Thorns Ring](/Thorns_Ring "Thorns Ring") recipes.
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Used as an ingredient to [Big Stone Chest](/Big_Stone_Chest "Big Stone Chest"), [Statue Of Blessings](/Statue_Of_Blessings "Statue Of Blessings"), and [Heavy Furnace](/Heavy_Furnace "Heavy Furnace") recipes. Can now be purchased at [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival"). No longer used for building Stone Cabin. Can now be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") dog with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals").
