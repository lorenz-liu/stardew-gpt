**Rock Crabs** are an [enemy](/Monsters "Monsters") found in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"). 60 rock crabs (any type) need to be killed for the [Monster Eradication Goal](/Adventurer%27s_Guild#Monster_Eradication_Goals "Adventurer's Guild") at the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild").

## Behavior

They disguise themselves as stones, sometimes surprising the player. When immobile, they are invulnerable to attack, though they can be hit with a [pickaxe](/Tools#Pickaxes "Tools") to knock them back, making them mobile again.

## Strategy

Rock Crabs may be differentiated from rocks by striking them with a [weapon](/Weapons "Weapons"). A Rock Crab will make a knocking sound and a zero damage indicator will pop up when hit with a weapon, while a rock will not.

Hidden Rock Crabs can also be differentiated visually from rocks by a lighter shadow, as well as the presence of two brown pixels on the lower right-hand side of the rock where usually these would be black. In the image below, the Rock Crab is on the left, and a normal rock is on the right.

[![HiddenRockCrab.png](/mediawiki/images/f/f9/HiddenRockCrab.png)](/File:HiddenRockCrab.png)

The Rock Crab can only be dealt damage if it is mobile, and not hiding beneath its shell. Strike an immobile Rock Crab with a [pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes") to make it mobile again.

Striking a Rock Crab with a [pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes") enough times will knock the shell off of its back, and will cause it to flee. Alternatively, setting off a [bomb](/Crafting#Bombs "Crafting") near a Rock Crab can remove its shell, and will cause it to flee as well.

[![RockCrabMinusRock.png](/mediawiki/images/a/a1/RockCrabMinusRock.png)](/File:RockCrabMinusRock.png)
