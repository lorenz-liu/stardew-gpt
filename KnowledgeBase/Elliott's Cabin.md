**Elliott's Cabin** is the home of [Elliott](/Elliott "Elliott"). It's located directly east when entering [The Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach").

During [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), and [Fall](/Fall "Fall"), players must first acquire two hearts of [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") with Elliott before being allowed inside his cabin. Players at any friendship level may enter during [Winter](/Winter "Winter") between 10am and 6pm.

## Trivia

* The painting on the wall of the cabin was painted by [Leah](/Leah "Leah").
* You can play Elliott’s piano, but only 4 notes. If several notes are played in a particular order, a [secret](/Secrets#Elliott.27s_Piano "Secrets") event occurs.
