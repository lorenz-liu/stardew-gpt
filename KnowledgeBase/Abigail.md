**Abigail** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives at [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). She's one of the twelve characters available to [marry](/Marriage "Marriage").

## Schedule

After the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island is unlocked, Abigail may randomly spend the day there. After leaving the Island at 6pm, Abigail will immediately go home to bed. Abigail never visits the Resort on [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days or her checkup day at [Harvey's Clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic").

## Relationships

Abigail lives with her parents, [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre") and [Caroline](/Caroline "Caroline"), in the apartment behind her father's shop. Her mother will comment that she is concerned about Abigail's interests and hobbies.

Abigail is friends with [Sam](/Sam "Sam") and [Sebastian](/Sebastian "Sebastian"), and will dance with Sebastian at the [Flower Dance](/Flower_Dance "Flower Dance") if the player doesn't ask her or Sebastian to dance.

### Two Hearts

![Two Hearts.png](/mediawiki/images/7/7c/Two_Hearts.png)

Enter [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") when Abigail is there, any day except Saturday.

| **Details** |
| --- |
| You enter Abigail's room and watch her get angry about a videogame. (This is just like the arcade version in [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon"), except that Abigail plays a second character and actively helps you). ### Four Hearts

![Four Hearts.png](/mediawiki/images/8/80/Four_Hearts.png)

| **Details** |
| --- |
| You see Abigail playing her flute. She asks what you're doing out in the rain.  * **"Just doing some work."** *(No effect on [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship").)* * **"Enjoying the weather."** *(+50 [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship").)* * **"I could ask you the same question."** *(+10 [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship").)*   Regardless of your response, she invites you to stand under the tree with her. You pull out a lyre (Abigail calls it "a mini harp!") and you play a duet as the camera pans out over the lake.  Note that if the player has cut down the tree near the mountain lake it will not be present during the 4 heart event, but Abigail will still reference the tree during the dialogue. |

#### After the 10-Heart Event

After viewing Abigail's 10-heart event, she will appear in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") on level 20 on days when it is raining. She will not appear in a [Multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer") game, only in single-player mode.

The first time the player encounters Abigail in the Mines, she has unique dialogue. Thereafter, there is a 15% chance to find her playing the flute, and an 85% chance of finding her walking in place. If she is not playing the flute, she will speak to the player.

## Marriage

:   *Main article: [Marriage](/Marriage "Marriage")*

Once married, Abigail will move into the [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse"). Like other marriage candidates, she will add her own room to the right of the bedroom. She'll also set up a small area behind the farmhouse where she'll sometimes go to practice her flute.

On rainy days, Abigail may offer you [Solar Essence](/Solar_Essence "Solar Essence"), [Bat Wing](/Bat_Wing "Bat Wing"), [Void Essence](/Void_Essence "Void Essence"), [Amethyst](/Amethyst "Amethyst"), or [Fire Quartz](/Fire_Quartz "Fire Quartz"). On rainy nights, she may offer you soup: [Parsnip Soup](/Parsnip_Soup "Parsnip Soup"), [Tom Kha Soup](/Tom_Kha_Soup "Tom Kha Soup"), [Trout Soup](/Trout_Soup "Trout Soup"), [Chowder](/Chowder "Chowder"), or [Lobster Bisque](/Lobster_Bisque "Lobster Bisque"). On days when Abigail stays inside the farmhouse all day, she may offer you a [Cherry Bomb](/Cherry_Bomb "Cherry Bomb"), a [Bomb](/Bomb "Bomb"), [Fried Mushroom](/Fried_Mushroom "Fried Mushroom"), or [Crab Cakes](/Crab_Cakes "Crab Cakes") to assist with your adventuring in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines").

## Trivia

* Giving her something she likes will result in her implying that she is going to eat the gift, regardless of what it is. This includes [diamond](/Diamond "Diamond"), an item that is the hardest mineral on the [Mohs hardness scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohs_scale_of_mineral_hardness "wikipedia:Mohs scale of mineral hardness").
* Abigail's top can be tailored using 1 [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") and 1 [Bone Flute](/Bone_Flute "Bone Flute"), creating the [Blue Long Vest](/Tailoring#Shirts "Tailoring").
* Abigail has a pet guinea pig named "David". If Abigail moves in with the player, another guinea pig named "David Jr." will be present in her farmhouse room, while "David" stays behind in her original room at Pierre's General Store.
* Abigail has a poster of Crono hanging above her TV; Crono is a character from the Super Nintendo game "Chrono Trigger."
* Abigail has a bow in her hair on her sprite, but no bow is shown on her portraits. This bow can, however, be seen in early versions of her portraits.
* [After the 10 heart cutscene](#After_the_10-Heart_Event), during rainy days in floor 20 of [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), Abigail will say [player name] (85% chance), which is helpful when using the [item ID glitch](/The_Player#Exploits "The Player"). Floor 20 can be exited and reentered repeatedly, with an 85% chance of Abigail saying [player name] each time.
