## Tailoring

Rhubarb can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Green Buttoned Vest](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt281b.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/b3/Shirt281b.png/24px-Shirt281b.png)](/File:Shirt281b.png) It can also be used as a red dye in the dye pots, located in [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s and [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Rhubarb is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Trivia

In reality, Rhubarb is a vegetable, as the edible part of the plant is the stalk.
