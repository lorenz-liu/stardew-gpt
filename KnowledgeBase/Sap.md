**Sap** is a [foraged](/Category:Foraging "Category:Foraging") item that drops from [trees](/Trees "Trees") when they are cut down. It is used in various [crafting](/Crafting "Crafting") recipes. Sap can be consumed, however it decreases [energy](/Energy "Energy") by 2 points.

Sap may also be obtained from tapping [Mahogany Trees](/Mahogany_Tree "Mahogany Tree") and all [Slimes](/Slimes "Slimes") except [Big Slimes](/Slimes#Big_Slimes "Slimes").

[Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent") sells a Sap in his [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="1">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 1 [Calico Egg](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg").

## Bundles

Five hundred Sap are required in the ![Bundle Yellow.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2b/Bundle_Yellow.png/24px-Bundle_Yellow.png) [Sticky Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Sticky_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Crafts Room (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Crafts_Room "Remixed Bundles").

## Tailoring

Sap is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt006.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/c3/Shirt006.png/24px-Shirt006.png) [Tan Striped Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a yellow dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the yellow dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

Sap is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
