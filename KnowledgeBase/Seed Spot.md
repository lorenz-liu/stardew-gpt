A **Seed Spot** is a tile that is guaranteed to provide 2-3 seeds when dug up with a [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes") (other tools do not work). Daily [luck](/Luck "Luck") influences the number of seeds obtained.[[1]](#cite_note-digspot-1) Seed Spots can be found in various tillable (but fallow) tiles on maps all over Stardew Valley (including [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island")). They have a 16.6% chance to spawn instead of [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") in certain locations.[[2]](#cite_note-spawnspot-2)

When dug up, the provided seed depends on the current date.

## Notes

* A Seed Spot is guaranteed to spawn in the [Backwoods](/Backwoods "Backwoods") on the 9th day of each season (at map position [18,18]).
