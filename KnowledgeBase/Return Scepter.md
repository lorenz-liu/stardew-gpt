The **Return Scepter** is a non-consumable [Warp Totem](/Warp_Totem "Warp Totem"). It warps [the Player](/The_Player "The Player") directly outside of their [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse").

It's purchasable from [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") in [the Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers") for data-sort-value="2000000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,000,000g, making it the most expensive inventory item in the game and second most expensive item, only behind the [Gold Clock](/Gold_Clock "Gold Clock"). It functions similarly to the [Warp Totem: Farm](/Warp_Totem:_Farm "Warp Totem: Farm"), but is not consumed when used and teleports the player to the [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") door instead of the totem warp point.

## Destination

The player will be teleported to the door outside the [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse"), or to their own [Cabin](/Cabin "Cabin").
