The **Dwarvish Translation Guide** is a special item unlocked by donating all 4 [Dwarf Scrolls](/Artifacts "Artifacts") to the [Museum](/Museum "Museum").

It unlocks the player's ability to understand the language of the dwarves. This allows the player to communicate with the [Dwarf](/Dwarf "Dwarf") in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), the Dwarf in the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon"), and to read the rightmost gravestone in the [Graveyard](/Graveyard "Graveyard").

Once obtained, it can be found in [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s Menu on the [Special Items & Powers](/Special_Items_%26_Powers "Special Items & Powers") tab.

## Trivia

* In the "rewards" screen shown after donating all 4 scrolls to the [Museum](/Museum "Museum"), the item type of the Dwarvish Translation Guide reads "Crafting", even though it cannot be crafted.

## Tip

* Floors 15, 55, 95 in the Mines have tillable soil near the entrance to the floor. The player can repeatedly revisit these floors via the elevator to till the soil and farm for Dwarf Scrolls I and IV.
