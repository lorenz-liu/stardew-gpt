The **Rainbow Shell** is found via [foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") at [The Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") in the Summer. It may also spawn on the [Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps#Beach "Farm Maps") during any season or in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") after activating the [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") or during the [Danger In The Deep](/Quests#Danger_In_The_Deep "Quests") quest. It may also be sent in the mail as a gift from [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius") or received as a gift from [Jas](/Jas "Jas"), [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent") or [Leo](/Leo "Leo") at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"). A [Rainbow Trout](/Rainbow_Trout "Rainbow Trout") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") has a 2% chance to produce 1 Rainbow Shell daily when the population of the pond reaches 9. It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") turtle with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals"). It may also randomly appear at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="300"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)900–1,500g.

## Bundles

Rainbow Shell is not used in any [Bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Recipes

Rainbow Shell is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Rainbow Shell is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [![Shirt134.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/7f/Shirt134.png/24px-Shirt134.png)](/File:Shirt134.png) [Bikini Top](/Tailoring "Tailoring").

Using a Rainbow Shell with the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to [dye](/Dyeing "Dyeing") clothing will open a menu where any color can be chosen using slider buttons.

## Quests

* 1 Rainbow Shell is used in [The Mysterious Qi](/Quests#The_Mysterious_Qi "Quests") quest.
* 1 Rainbow Shell may be requested by [Blobfish](/Blobfish "Blobfish") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 7 to 10.

## Notes

* An exploit to get the Rainbow Shell is to wait until Demetrius sends a gift in the mail, then reload the game until the shell is received.
