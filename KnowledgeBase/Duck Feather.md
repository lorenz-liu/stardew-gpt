The **Duck Feather** is an [animal product](/Category:Animal_Products "Category:Animal Products") obtained from a [Duck](/Duck "Duck") with sufficient [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals"), [mood](/Animals#Mood "Animals"), and [daily luck](/Luck "Luck"). It can also occasionally be purchased from the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="250"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)750–1,250g. Additionally, if the player is married to [Emily](/Emily "Emily"), she will occasionally gift one to the player. The [Statue Of Endless Fortune](/Statue_Of_Endless_Fortune "Statue Of Endless Fortune") produces one Duck Feather on [Leo](/Leo "Leo")'s birthday, [Summer 26](/Summer "Summer"). It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") cat with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals").

[Luck buffs](/Luck#Luck_Buffs "Luck") do not influence the chances of getting a duck feather.

## Bundles

Duck Feather is used in the ![Bundle Teal.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/ce/Bundle_Teal.png/24px-Bundle_Teal.png) [Dye Bundle](/Bundles#Dye_Bundle "Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board](/Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Bundles").

## Recipes

Duck Feather is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Duck Feather is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt012.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/72/Shirt012.png/24px-Shirt012.png) [Lime Green Striped Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a green dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the green dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

Duck Feather is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
