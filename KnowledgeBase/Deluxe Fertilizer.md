**Deluxe Fertilizer** is a [Fertilizer](/Fertilizer "Fertilizer") that improves the chance to grow a [quality crop](/Crops#Crop_Quality "Crops"). It can be placed on tilled soil before or after planting a seed, or at any stage of crop growth. Use of Deluxe Fertilizer allows for the chance to gain iridium quality crops on harvest. Using this fertilizer is the only way to grow an iridium quality crop.

Deluxe Fertilizer affects only the basic harvest, which means that extra crops gained at harvest (*i.e.,* [Coffee Bean](/Coffee_Bean "Coffee Bean"), [Blueberry](/Blueberry "Blueberry"), [Cranberries](/Cranberries "Cranberries")) will be of regular quality.

The recipe can be purchased for data-sort-value="20">![Qi Gem.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/78/Qi_Gem.png/24px-Qi_Gem.png) 20.

## Tailoring

Deluxe Fertilizer can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable ![Shirt174.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/65/Shirt174.png/24px-Shirt174.png) [High-Waisted Shirt](/Tailoring#Shirts "Tailoring").
