The **Dwarf** is a [valley resident](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"). Initially the way is blocked off by an unbreakable rock, just inside the entrance. After upgrading to at least a [steel pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes"), the stone can be broken. A [cherry bomb](/Cherry_Bomb "Cherry Bomb") (or stronger bomb) will also break the rock.

The Dwarf will start out speaking Dwarvish, which is unreadable by the player. To understand Dwarvish, the 4 [Dwarvish scrolls](/Artifacts "Artifacts") must be acquired from within the mines. After donating them to the [museum](/Museum "Museum"), [Gunther](/Gunther "Gunther") will reward the player with the [Dwarvish Translation Guide](/Dwarvish_Translation_Guide "Dwarvish Translation Guide") to use which allows for understanding the Dwarf's speech. Purchases from the Dwarf's shop cannot be made and friendship cannot be affected with the Dwarf until Dwarvish is understood.

## Schedule

The Dwarf lives just to the east of the entrance to [the mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") and does not move from there.

## Relationships

Due to a 1000-year war between the Dwarves and the [shadow people](/Shadow_Brute "Shadow Brute"), The Dwarf is sometimes suspicious of the player, believing they may be a spy sent by the shadow people.

The Dwarf appears to have some respect for the wizard, as seen in their heart event.

The Dwarf harbors dislike for [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus"), on account of the aforementioned war between their people, the outcome of which caused the Dwarf race to disappear, leaving behind only relics (found as [artifacts](/Artifacts "Artifacts")).

## Heart Events

### 50 Friendship Points

![Zero Hearts.png](/mediawiki/images/4/42/Zero_Hearts.png)

After earning 50 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points with the Dwarf, enter [the Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers"). A [Stardrop](/Stardrop "Stardrop") must have been purchased from [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus").

| **Details** |
| --- |
| The Dwarf finds Krobus in the Sewers and blames the Shadow People for the death of their family. Krobus retorts by saying that he only resorted to violence after the dwarves drove them from their ancestral homeland. Krobus gives an apology for what happened to the Dwarf's family but the Dwarf doesn't accept it. They both start fighting. The player descends into the Sewers and gets between them. The Dwarf yells out, "Let me at him! He's despicable." Krobus retorts, "Come and get it, shrimpy." Suddenly, the [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard") appears and stops the fight. He reminds them that "The Elemental Wars have long been finished." and tells them to make peace for the sake of the humans that live around them. The Dwarf consents to this and promises to stay away from the Sewers. Krobus once again apologizes for the past and states that he has no personal gripe with the Dwarf. The Wizard casts a 'Seal of Promise' to finalize the agreement. [Sewer Confrontation Krobos Dwarf.png](/File:Sewer_Confrontation_Krobos_Dwarf.png) |
