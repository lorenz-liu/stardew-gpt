**Pam** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives in the [trailer](/Trailer "Trailer") just west of the river in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). She was the driver of the [Pelican Town bus](/Bus_Stop "Bus Stop") before it broke down. Once the bus service has been restored, Pam will manage the [bus stop](/Bus_Stop "Bus Stop") just east of [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"), opening the way to [the Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert").

While the bus service is not active, Pam can usually be found in her home until 12:00pm. She'll then walk over to [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart") for a few hours, leaving there at 4:00pm to walk to [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon"). Pam is one of [Gus](/Gus "Gus")' most loyal customers. She spends every evening in his establishment.

## Schedule

After the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island is unlocked, Pam may randomly spend the day there. After leaving the Island at 6pm, Pam will immediately go home to bed. Pam never visits the Resort on [Fall](/Fall "Fall") 15, [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days, or her checkup day at [Harvey's Clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic").

### Nine Hearts

![Nine Hearts.png](/mediawiki/images/0/07/Nine_Hearts.png)

Purchase the "[Community Upgrade](/Carpenter%27s_Shop#Community_Upgrades "Carpenter's Shop")" from the Carpenter's Shop. After earning 9 hearts of [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") with Pam, enter [Pam's house](/Trailer "Trailer") at least 4 days after the Community Upgrade is completed.

| **Details** |
| --- |
| You find Pam praying before a [Sign of the Vessel](/Sign_of_the_Vessel "Sign of the Vessel") statue. She confesses that she loves the new house, but hasn't been able to cut back on her drinking. She says she thought the new house would change everything, but it didn't, so she ordered the statue. She then turns to the player for a response.  * **I'm glad you're feeling hopeful** *(No effect on [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship").)*   Pam says she's "getting sappy" in her old age, and the cutscene ends.   * **Sorry Pam, but Yoba isn't real...** *(-1000 [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship").)*   Pam becomes angry and asks "What in the void is wrong with you?" before declaring her faith in Yoba and ordering you to leave. |

Trivia: The Yoba statue can be found later near Pam's bed on the second floor of the house, and can be removed and obtained by the player by striking it with a [tool](/Tools "Tools").

## Quests

* ["Pam is Thirsty"](/Quests#Pam_is_Thirsty "Quests"): Pam will send you a letter on the 14th of [Summer](/Summer "Summer") requesting a [Pale Ale](/Pale_Ale "Pale Ale"). The reward is data-sort-value="350">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)350g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* ["Pam Needs Juice"](/Quests#Pam_Needs_Juice "Quests"): Pam will send you a letter on the 19th of [Fall](/Fall "Fall"), Year 2, requesting a [Battery Pack](/Battery_Pack "Battery Pack"). The reward is data-sort-value="400">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)400g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* Pam may randomly request an item at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). The reward is 3x the item's base value and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* ["The Strong Stuff"](/Quests#The_Strong_Stuff "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor") during [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), Pam may randomly request that the player brew 12 [Potato Juice](/Juice "Juice") and place them in the kitchen in her house. The reward is data-sort-value="3000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)3,000g, 250 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points, and the F.I.B.S. [television](/Television "Television") channel.
