**Iridium Crabs** are an [enemy](/Monsters "Monsters") found in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), starting at level 26. When killed, they may drop [Crab Cakes](/Crab_Cakes "Crab Cakes") and up to 3 [Iridium Ore](/Iridium_Ore "Iridium Ore"). They may also very rarely drop a [Diamond](/Diamond "Diamond") or [Prismatic Shard](/Prismatic_Shard "Prismatic Shard") when killed. 60 rock crabs (any type) need to be killed for the [Monster Eradication Goal](/Adventurer%27s_Guild#Monster_Eradication_Goals "Adventurer's Guild") at the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild").

## Behavior

They disguise themselves as [Iridium Nodes](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining"), sometimes surprising the player. When immobile, they are invulnerable to attack, though they can be hit with a [pickaxe](/Tools#Pickaxes "Tools") to knock them back, making them mobile again.

## Strategy

Iridium Crabs may be differentiated from [Iridium Nodes](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining") by striking them with a [Sword](/Weapons#Sword "Weapons"). An Iridium Crab will make a knocking sound and a zero damage indicator will pop up when hit with a sword, while an Iridium Node will not.

The Iridium Crab can only be dealt damage if it is mobile, and not hiding beneath its shell. Strike an immobile Iridium Crab with the [pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes") to make it mobile again.

Striking an Iridium Crab with a [pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes") enough times will knock the shell off of its back, and will cause it to flee. Alternatively, setting off a [bomb](/Crafting#Bombs "Crafting") near an Iridium Crab can remove its shell, and will cause it to flee as well. A [Mega Bomb](/Mega_Bomb "Mega Bomb") is particularly effective at removing Iridium Crab shells.
