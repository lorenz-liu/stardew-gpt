The **Bomb** is a one-time use [explosive item](/Crafting#Bombs "Crafting"). It damages certain items within its radius. Other items are impervious to its effects.

Bombs can be [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting") or purchased from the [Dwarf](/Dwarf "Dwarf") (both in the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon") and [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines")) for data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g. They are also offered by the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader"), who will trade five [Quartz](/Quartz "Quartz") for one Bomb. [Bats](/Bats "Bats"), [Lava Crabs](/Lava_Crab "Lava Crab"), [Squid Kids](/Squid_Kid "Squid Kid"), and [Serpents](/Serpent "Serpent") can drop Bombs when slain. Ten Bombs may occasionally be found in [treasure rooms](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern") in the Skull Cavern. [Crates and barrels](/The_Mines#Crates_and_Barrels "The Mines") in the ["dangerous" Mines](/The_Mines#Difficulty_Levels "The Mines") or [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") can drop one. 3-5 Bombs may occasionally be found in [Supply Crates](/Supply_Crate "Supply Crate") on the [Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps#Beach "Farm Maps") provided the player has upgraded the [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") exactly once. A Bomb may occasionally be obtained from the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="50"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150–1,000g. Five Bombs may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box"). Twelve or fifteen Bombs can be given as a prize from the [prize machine](/Mayor%27s_Manor#Prize_Machine "Mayor's Manor") in the Mayor's Manor. [Kent](/Kent "Kent") may randomly send one in the mail as a gift.

If a player has read the [Dwarvish Safety Manual](/Dwarvish_Safety_Manual "Dwarvish Safety Manual"), they will take 25% less damage from a Bomb.

## Effects

Note that the following is not an exhaustive list:

* Rocks and [ore/gem nodes](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining") will drop their respective ores, gems, etc.
* Mushrooms, [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot"), and [foraged minerals](/Minerals#Foraged_Minerals "Minerals") will be destroyed.
* Any untilled dirt tiles within a significantly smaller radius will become tilled. In [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") and [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), any items (*e.g.,* [clay](/Clay "Clay"), [artifacts](/Artifacts "Artifacts")) will be dropped at the same rate as a player using a hoe; elsewhere, nothing will drop (unless the spot in question is the subject of a [Secret Note](/Secret_Note "Secret Note")).
* Crafted [artisan](/Crafting#Artisan_Equipment "Crafting") and [refining](/Crafting#Refining_Equipment "Crafting") equipment will be destroyed.
* [Heaters](/Heater "Heater") will be destroyed.
* [Auto-Grabbers](/Auto-Grabber "Auto-Grabber") will **not** be destroyed, whether empty or containing items.
* [Trees](/Trees "Trees") will be destroyed when their health reaches zero (five Bombs will knock down a fully grown tree, and three more will destroy the stump). Trees will drop [wood](/Wood "Wood"), [sap](/Sap "Sap"), and any seeds they contained.

## Radius

The Bomb has a radius of 5 (diameter of 11).

## Bundles

Bomb is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

A Bomb can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Shirt155.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Shirt155.png/24px-Shirt155.png) [Bomber Jacket](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used as a black [dye](/Dyeing "Dyeing") when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

Bomb is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
