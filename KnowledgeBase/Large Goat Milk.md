**Large Goat Milk** (called **L. Goat Milk** in-game) is an [animal product](/Category:Animal_Products "Category:Animal Products") obtained from a [Goat](/Goat "Goat") with sufficient [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals") and [mood](/Animals#Mood "Animals"). It can also randomly rotate into the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart")'s stock for data-sort-value="345"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,035–1,725g.

## Bundles

* Large Goat Milk is an option for the ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Animal Bundle](/Bundles#Animal_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Pantry](/Bundles#Pantry "Bundles").
* Ten Milk (any size, including [Goat Milk](/Goat_Milk "Goat Milk") and Large Goat Milk) are used in the ![Bundle Yellow.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2b/Bundle_Yellow.png/24px-Bundle_Yellow.png) [Home Cook's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Home_Cook.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Remixed Bundles").

:   *See also: [Animal Products Profitability](/Animal_Products_Profitability "Animal Products Profitability")*

The quality of the milk is not a factor.

Goat Cheese may be aged in [Casks](/Cask "Cask") to increase its quality.

## Tailoring

Large Goat Milk is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt025.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/9a/Shirt025.png/24px-Shirt025.png) [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a white dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

Large Goat Milk is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
