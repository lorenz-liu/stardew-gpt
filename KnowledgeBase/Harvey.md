**Harvey** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). He runs the town's [medical clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic") and is passionate about the health of the townsfolk. He's one of the twelve characters available to [marry](/Marriage "Marriage").

## Schedule

On Tuesdays and Thursdays Harvey gives villagers their annual or quarterly checkups. On most other days he spends the morning manning the counter at the clinic, and the afternoon taking exercise around town.

On rainy days, Harvey leaves the clinic and walks upstairs to his room at 12pm, regardless of whether or not there are patients in the clinic. Later, he stops by [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon").

After the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island is unlocked, Harvey may randomly spend the day there. After leaving the Island at 6pm, Harvey will immediately go home to bed. Harvey never visits the Resort on Tuesdays, Thursdays, or [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days.

## Relationships

Harvey works with [Maru](/Maru "Maru") at the clinic, and can be seen standing with her at the [Luau](/Luau "Luau").

Harvey will dance with Maru at the [Flower Dance](/Flower_Dance "Flower Dance") if neither of them are dancing with the player.

## Marriage

:   *Main article: [Marriage](/Marriage "Marriage")*

Once married, Harvey will move into the [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse"). Like other marriage candidates, he will add his own room to the right of the bedroom. He'll also set up a small garden behind the farmhouse where he'll sometimes go to read. He will continue to work at the [clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic") and travel to [town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") to work there on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

On mornings when Harvey stays inside the farmhouse all day, he may offer you [Complete Breakfast](/Complete_Breakfast "Complete Breakfast"). On rainy nights he may offer you dinner: [Salmon Dinner](/Salmon_Dinner "Salmon Dinner"), [Crispy Bass](/Crispy_Bass "Crispy Bass"), [Fried Eel](/Fried_Eel "Fried Eel"), [Carp Surprise](/Carp_Surprise "Carp Surprise"), or [Vegetable Medley](/Vegetable_Medley "Vegetable Medley").

## Trivia

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/96/Harvey_Location_Cutscene.jpg/200px-Harvey_Location_Cutscene.jpg)](/File:Harvey_Location_Cutscene.jpg)

Harvey's coordinates

* During the eight-heart event in his room, he mentions that his coordinates are 52 North, 43.5 East. In the real world, this would put his location in southwest Russia, between Ukraine and Kazakhstan.
* When married to the player, he may admit a fondness for jazz music, as he listens to it when finalizing his medical reports.
* Despite telling others to eat healthily, his fridge is filled with convenience food and his microwave oven is well-used.
* Inside the [clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic"), you can find several plaques on the wall. One states that the clinic is licensed by the Ferngill Republic Health Authority. Another is an advertisement for proper sneeze etiquette. A third in the back is an advertisement for a fictional drug Pravoloxinone which has the side-effect of "Complete disintegration of one or more bones" for 1 in 500 patients. Yet another near the back right of the clinic is described as a "The sign of the vessel", meant to comfort any patients believing in Yoba.
* Harvey's radio has a tape inside of it named "Anthology of Classic Jazz", and his TV is set to the history station.
* In the left-most corner of Harvey's apartment, there's a poster stated to be a "comparison between fighter planes of the [Ferngill Republic](/Setting#Nations "Setting") and the [Gotoro Empire](/Gotoro_Empire "Gotoro Empire")".
* In [Secret Note #7](/Secret_Notes#Secret_Note_.237 "Secret Notes"), it is confirmed that he is one of three 'older bachelors' in Pelican Town.
* Despite loving [Coffee](/Coffee "Coffee"), he only likes the [Triple Shot Espresso](/Triple_Shot_Espresso "Triple Shot Espresso").
