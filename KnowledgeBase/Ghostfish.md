The **Ghostfish** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") on the 20th and 60th floor during all seasons. A [Ghost](/Ghost "Ghost") may also drop a Ghostfish (8% chance) when slain. It can also randomly be found at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="45"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)135–1,000g.

## Fish Pond

Ghostfish can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 2 days. The initial pond capacity is 1 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing four quests. The primary output is white Ghostfish [Roe](/Roe "Roe"), but [Quartz](/Quartz "Quartz") is possible starting at population 3, and several more valuable items are possible starting at population 9.

## Quests

* Two Ghostfish may be requested by [Dorado](/Dorado "Dorado"), [Lingcod](/Lingcod "Lingcod"), or [Pike](/Pike "Pike") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 7 to 10.
* Three Ghostfish may be requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his fifth request, or as part of any request starting with the sixth.
