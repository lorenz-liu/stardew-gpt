**Juice** is an [Artisan Good](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods") made from placing a [vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") or [foraged item](/Foraging#Foraged_Items "Foraging") inside a [Keg](/Keg "Keg"), taking approximately 4 [days](/Day_Cycle "Day Cycle"). Its selling price is the base value of the ingredient input multiplied by 2.25 (3.15 with the [Artisan](/Skills#Farming "Skills") profession).

The [energy](/Energy "Energy") and [health](/Health "Health") restored by Juice is equal to 2 × Base Ingredient Value for edible vegetables and foraged items. For inedible vegetables, Juice will restore energy equal to exactly Base [Vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") Value and health equal to 45% Base [Vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") Value.[[1]](#cite_note-juice_energy-1) The color of the Juice also depends on the item used.

Although [Wheat](/Wheat "Wheat"), [Hops](/Hops "Hops"), and [Tea Leaves](/Tea_Leaves "Tea Leaves") are classified as [vegetables](/Vegetables "Vegetables"), when used with a [Keg](/Keg "Keg"), they produce [Beer](/Beer "Beer"), [Pale Ale](/Pale_Ale "Pale Ale"), and [Green Tea](/Green_Tea "Green Tea") respectively.

The following foraged items can be made into Juice: [Cave Carrot](/Cave_Carrot "Cave Carrot"), [Dandelion](/Dandelion "Dandelion"), [Ginger](/Ginger "Ginger"), [Hazelnut](/Hazelnut "Hazelnut"), [Leek](/Leek "Leek"), [Spring Onion](/Spring_Onion "Spring Onion"), [Snow Yam](/Snow_Yam "Snow Yam"), [Wild Horseradish](/Wild_Horseradish "Wild Horseradish"), and [Winter Root](/Winter_Root "Winter Root").

Juice cannot be put in a [cask](/Cask "Cask").

## Bundles

Juice is one of the options for the ![Bundle Orange.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/95/Bundle_Orange.png/24px-Bundle_Orange.png) [Brewer's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Brewer.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Pantry (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Pantry "Remixed Bundles").

## Recipes

Juice is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Juice is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [![Shirt209.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a3/Shirt209.png/24px-Shirt209.png)](/File:Shirt209.png) [Sports Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a green dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* [Pam](/Pam "Pam") requests 12 Potato Juice in the ["The Strong Stuff"](/Quests#The_Strong_Stuff "Quests") Special Order.
* One Juice is requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his fourth request. It may also be requested as part of any request starting with the sixth.

## Trivia

Juice sold by the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") will not be of any ingredients type, will sell for data-sort-value="150">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150g (or data-sort-value="210">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)210g with the [Artisan Profession](/Skills#Farming "Skills")), and will restore 75 [energy](/Energy "Energy") and 33 [health](/Health "Health").
