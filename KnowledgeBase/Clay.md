**Clay** is a resource that can be found by tilling dirt, sand, or [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot"), by mining [Clay Nodes](/Mining#Clay_Node "Mining") at the [Ginger Island Dig Site](/Ginger_Island#Dig_Site "Ginger Island"), or from cracking open [Geodes](/Minerals#Geodes "Minerals"). It can also be received as a gift from [Jas](/Jas "Jas"), [Leo](/Leo "Leo") or [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent") at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"). [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent") sells Clay in his [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="1">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 1 [Calico Egg](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg"). It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") dog with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals").

## Bundles

Clay is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Clay is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [![Shirt001.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/25/Shirt001.png/24px-Shirt001.png)](/File:Shirt001.png) [Shirt](/Tailoring#Shirts "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Two, three, or five Clay may be requested by several fish in [Fish Pond quests](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond").

## Tips

* It may be worth considering digging for clay in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), as this has a higher clay rate than outside of the mines, can easily be reset, and will additionally produce [Cave Carrots](/Cave_Carrot "Cave Carrot") and other items such as certain [Artifacts](/Artifacts "Artifacts") depending on floor level. [Bombs](/Bomb "Bomb") or other explosives can be used to dig the dirt faster.
