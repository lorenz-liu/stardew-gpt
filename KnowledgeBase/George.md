**George Mullner** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town").

## Schedule

The 23rd of every season, George has an appointment at [Harvey's Clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic"), accompanied by [Evelyn](/Evelyn "Evelyn"). George never visits the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island.

## Relationships

George is married to [Evelyn](/Evelyn "Evelyn") and lives with his grandson, [Alex](/Alex "Alex"). Alex's deceased mother [Clara](/Clara_Mullner "Clara Mullner") was his daughter.

## Quests

* ["Knee Therapy"](/Quests#Knee_Therapy "Quests"): George will send you a letter on the 25th of [Summer](/Summer "Summer") requesting a [Hot Pepper](/Hot_Pepper "Hot Pepper"). The reward is data-sort-value="200">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)200g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* George may randomly request an item at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). The reward is 3x the item's base value and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* George is to be gifted the [Advanced TV Remote](/Quests#Advanced_TV_Remote "Quests") in [The Pirate's Wife](/Quests#The_Pirate.27s_Wife "Quests") quest.

## Trivia

* George will send you recipes for [Fried Eel](/Fried_Eel "Fried Eel") and [Spicy Eel](/Spicy_Eel "Spicy Eel"), which are ironically two of his wife's hated gifts.
* When moving, George will make the footstep noise as if he is walking, despite being in a wheelchair.
