# Books

:   *Not to be confused with [Lost Books](/Lost_Books "Lost Books").*

**Books**, when read, give players permanent advantages. They may grant abilities, quality-of-life upgrades, or experience points (XP) in the various game [skills](/Skills "Skills").

Books can be obtained in a variety of ways. Once found, a Book can be read by selecting it in the top row of inventory and selecting the "use" button (right-click on PC) as if consuming food. All books can be gifted to [Penny](/Penny "Penny") as a loved gift.

In [Multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer"), the effects from a book will only apply to the player that reads them. Individual players will need to read individual books to receive their effects.

## Power Books

There are 19 power books. The first time each particular book is read, the player earns a power and the book is listed on the [Special Items & Powers](/Special_Items_%26_Powers "Special Items & Powers") player tab. Subsequent readings of most power books (besides the [Animal Catalogue](/Animal_Catalogue "Animal Catalogue") and [Price Catalogue](/Price_Catalogue "Price Catalogue")) grant XP in specific skills. A thumbs-up icon on a power book indicates that the book has been read at least once.

The Well-Read [Achievement](/Achievement "Achievement") is awarded after reading all 19 power books at least once.

## Skill Books

Reading the [Queen Of Sauce Cookbook](/Queen_Of_Sauce_Cookbook "Queen Of Sauce Cookbook") teaches all of the [Queen of Sauce](/Queen_of_Sauce "Queen of Sauce") recipes not yet known, regardless of their schedule of TV broadcast.

Reading the [Book Of Stars](/Book_Of_Stars "Book Of Stars") gives 250 XP in all skills. If the player has unlocked the Mastery Cave, they will instead earn 1,125 [Mastery](/Skills#Mastery "Skills") points. Reading the [Stardew Valley Almanac](/Stardew_Valley_Almanac "Stardew Valley Almanac") gives 125 XP in Farming, or 125 Mastery points. Reading each of the other 4 skill books gives 250 XP in that skill or 250 Mastery points.

Reading skill books does **not** count toward any [Achievement](/Achievement "Achievement").
