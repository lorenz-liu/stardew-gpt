The **Duck Egg** is an [animal product](/Category:Animal_Products "Category:Animal Products") obtained from a [Duck](/Duck "Duck"). Duck Eggs may also be available at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="95"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)285–1,000g. One Duck Egg can sometimes be purchased for data-sort-value="2500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,500g from [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre")'s booth at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star").

Duck Eggs can be hatched in an [incubator](/Incubator "Incubator"), which comes with a Big or Deluxe [Coop](/Coop "Coop").

## Tailoring

Duck Egg can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable ![Shirt270.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/14/Shirt270.png/24px-Shirt270.png) [Green Jacket Shirt](/Tailoring#Shirts "Tailoring"). It can be used as a white [dye](/Dyeing "Dyeing") when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

* ["Gus' Famous Omelet"](/Quests#Gus.27_Famous_Omelet "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), [Gus](/Gus "Gus") may randomly request that the player collect and deliver 24 Eggs (of any type) to the fridge in [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon"). The reward is data-sort-value="3000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)3,000g and a [Mini-Fridge](/Mini-Fridge "Mini-Fridge").
* Five Eggs (any type, excluding Dinosaur) may be requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his third request, or as part of any request starting with the sixth.
