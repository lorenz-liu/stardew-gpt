**Aquamarine** is a [Mineral](/Minerals "Minerals"). It can be obtained from [Aquamarine Nodes](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining") and [Gem Nodes](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining") in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") (floors 40+), from [panning](/Pans "Pans"), from a [Gem Bird](/Ginger_Island#Gem_Birds "Ginger Island") on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") during [rainy days](/Weather#Weather_Types "Weather"), and from [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing"). It has a 10% chance to be dropped by a slain [Dwarvish Sentry](/Dwarvish_Sentry "Dwarvish Sentry") in the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon"). It may occasionally be found in a [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") after the player has reached the bottom of the mines.

The [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") in the [Calico Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") will trade one [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in exchange for three Aquamarine on [Wednesday](/Days_of_the_Week#Wednesday "Days of the Week").

## Bundles

* Aquamarine is used in the ![Bundle Teal.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/ce/Bundle_Teal.png/24px-Bundle_Teal.png) [Dye Bundle](/Bundles#Dye_Bundle "Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board](/Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Bundles").
* Aquamarine is one of the options for the ![Bundle Yellow.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2b/Bundle_Yellow.png/24px-Bundle_Yellow.png) [Treasure Hunter's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Treasure_Hunter.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Boiler Room (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Boiler_Room "Remixed Bundles").

## Forging

Aquamarine can be used at the [Forge](/Forge "Forge") to increase the critical hit chance of a melee weapon.

## Tailoring

Aquamarine is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt145.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a7/Shirt145.png/24px-Shirt145.png)](/File:Shirt145.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a blue dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* Aquamarine may be randomly requested during any season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="540">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)540g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points. Aquamarine will never be requested until you have reached floors 40+ of [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines").
* 2 Aquamarine may be requested by several ocean fish in [Fish Pond quests](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 7 to 10.
* May be used in the [Gem Birds](/Ginger_Island#Gem_Birds "Ginger Island") puzzle on Ginger Island.
