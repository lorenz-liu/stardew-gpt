The **Bone Mill** is a piece of [Refining Equipment](/Crafting#Refining_Equipment "Crafting") that produces fertilizers from [Bone Fragments](/Bone_Fragment "Bone Fragment"), fossils, and specific [artifacts](/Artifacts "Artifacts"). It takes ![Time Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Time_Icon.png/24px-Time_Icon.png) 240m (4h) to process.[[1]](#cite_note-bonemilloutput-1)

A Bone Mill may be [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting") after receiving the recipe by mail from [Gunther](/Gunther "Gunther") the day after completing his ["Fragments of the past"](/Quests#Fragments_of_the_past "Quests") Special Order.
