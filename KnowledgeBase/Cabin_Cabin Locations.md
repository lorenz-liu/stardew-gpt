# Cabin/Cabin Locations

When choosing to play [Multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer") from the main menu, or choosing "Multiplayer Options" from the [Advanced Options](/Options "Options") menu, [cabins](/Cabin "Cabin") will spawn at different locations depending on choice of [Farm Map](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps") and choice of "Close" or "Separate" option in the menu.

The table below shows the placement of cabins when choosing 3 or 7 cabins respectively, for "Close" and "Separate" options.
