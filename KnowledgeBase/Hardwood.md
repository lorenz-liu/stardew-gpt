**Hardwood** is a [resource](/Category:Resources "Category:Resources"). It is obtained by chopping down a [Mahogany Tree](/Mahogany_Tree "Mahogany Tree") or a [Mystic Tree](/Mystic_Tree "Mystic Tree") with any [axe](/Axes "Axes"), a [Large Stump](/Large_Stump "Large Stump") with a [Copper Axe](/Axes "Axes") or better, or a [Large Log](/Large_Log "Large Log") with a [Steel Axe](/Axes "Axes") or better.

Six [Large Stumps](/Large_Stump "Large Stump") respawn daily in the [Secret Woods](/Secret_Woods "Secret Woods"), making it possible to obtain 12 Hardwood per day. Eight Large Stumps respawn daily on the left side of the [Forest Farm Map](/Farm_Maps#Forest "Farm Maps"), and one Large Stump respawns daily in the top left corner of the [Four Corners Farm Map](/Farm_Maps#Four_Corners "Farm Maps"). These yield an additional 16 and 2 Hardwood per day, respectively.

On [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island"), there are wild [Mahogany Trees](/Mahogany_Tree "Mahogany Tree") that will regrow after being chopped for Hardwood. Hardwood is also readily available from various trees, stumps, and logs (using the appropriate [axes](/Axes "Axes")) on Floors 41-69 of [the Dangerous Mines](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines"). All [trees](/Trees "Trees") have a chance of dropping Hardwood with the [Lumberjack Profession](/Foraging#Foraging_Skill "Foraging"); this can be done with any axe or any [bombs](/Crafting#Bombs "Crafting").

Hardwood can also be received as loot by breaking [barrels and boxes](/The_Mines#Crates_and_Barrels "The Mines") in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") or [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"). [Robin](/Robin "Robin") may gift the player 25 Hardwood at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"). It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") dog with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals"). A [Woodskip](/Woodskip "Woodskip") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") may produce 5 Hardwood when the population of the pond reaches 6. Ten Hardwood may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box"). Up to 100 Hardwood can be purchased from [Leah](/Leah "Leah")'s [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="3">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 3 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg") each. The [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") trades 20 Hardwood for 1 [Woody's Secret](/Woody%27s_Secret "Woody's Secret"), provided the player has already obtained the power from that book.

## Bundles

Ten Hardwood are required for the ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Construction Bundle](/Bundles#Construction_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Crafts Room](/Bundles#Crafts_Room "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Hardwood is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt047.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/b7/Shirt047.png/24px-Shirt047.png) [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a brown dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the orange dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

* [Robin](/Robin "Robin") requests 10 Hardwood by mail on the 21st of [Winter](/Winter "Winter") in the ["Robin's Request" Quest](/Quests#Robin.27s_Request "Quests"). The reward is data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* [Robin](/Robin "Robin") requests 80 Hardwood in the ["Robin's Project"](/Quests#Robin.27s_Project "Quests") Special Order. The reward is data-sort-value="2000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,000g and a [Deluxe Red Double Bed](/Deluxe_Red_Double_Bed "Deluxe Red Double Bed").
* [Willy's Boat](/Fish_Shop#Willy.27s_Boat "Fish Shop") needs 200 Hardwood to repair the boat's outer hull.
* 10 Hardwood are requested by [Woodskip](/Woodskip "Woodskip") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 1 to 3.
* 5 Hardwood may be requested by [Crayfish](/Crayfish "Crayfish"), [Periwinkle](/Periwinkle "Periwinkle"), or [Snail](/Snail "Snail") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 5 to 7.
* 100 Hardwood is needed to repair the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") in ["The Giant Stump"](/Quests#The_Giant_Stump "Quests") quest.

## Notes

* A [Wood Chipper](/Wood_Chipper "Wood Chipper") can be used to convert Hardwood into [Wood](/Wood "Wood"), [Maple Syrup](/Maple_Syrup "Maple Syrup"), [Oak Resin](/Oak_Resin "Oak Resin"), or [Pine Tar](/Pine_Tar "Pine Tar").
* Five Hardwood can be obtained by purchasing a [Cork Bobber](/Cork_Bobber "Cork Bobber") and placing it in a [Deconstructor](/Deconstructor "Deconstructor").
