**Bugs (dangerous)** are an [enemy](/Monsters "Monsters") found in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") after activating the [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") or during the [Danger In The Deep](/Quests#Danger_In_The_Deep "Quests") quest. 80 cave insects (any type) need to be killed for the [Monster Eradication Goal](/Adventurer%27s_Guild#Monster_Eradication_Goals "Adventurer's Guild") at the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild").

## Behavior

They initially behave similar to their weaker counterpart the [Bug](/Bug "Bug"). They fly up and down or left and right in a fixed path. Once they get hit they will rotate and change their flight path, potentially hitting the player.

## Strategy

Hit them from the front as to not get hit once they rotate.
