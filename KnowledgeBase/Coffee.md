**Coffee** is a drink made by placing 5 [Coffee Beans](/Coffee_Bean "Coffee Bean") inside a [Keg](/Keg "Keg"), or purchased at [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon") for data-sort-value="300">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)300g. At the [Night Market](/Night_Market "Night Market"), one free cup of coffee per night can be obtained from the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader"). Coffee is sold at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") in [George](/George "George")'s and [Harvey](/Harvey "Harvey")'s [shops](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival"). One Coffee is a reward for submitting an [Egg Rating](/Desert_Festival#Skull_Cavern "Desert Festival") of 5-9 to [Gil](/Gil "Gil") during the Desert Festival. If the player owns a [Coffee Maker](/Coffee_Maker "Coffee Maker"), they can acquire one free cup of coffee every morning. 1-2 Coffee may occasionally be found in [Supply Crates](/Supply_Crate "Supply Crate") on the [Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps#Beach "Farm Maps") if the player has not upgraded the [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") yet. 3 Coffee is a guaranteed gold tier reward on the 12th for the [SquidFest](/SquidFest "SquidFest") festival. Wearing a [Hot Java Ring](/Hot_Java_Ring "Hot Java Ring") gives a 25% chance for monsters to drop Coffee when killed. Three Coffee may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box").

When consumed, it grants the player +1 [Speed](/Speed#Player_Speed "Speed") for 1m 23s. The speed of the player’s [horse](/Animals#Horse "Animals") is also increased. As a drink, its [buff](/Buffs "Buffs") stacks with any food buffs (including Speed), but replaces any other drink buffs (*i.e.,* from [Triple Shot Espresso](/Triple_Shot_Espresso "Triple Shot Espresso"), [Ginger Ale](/Ginger_Ale "Ginger Ale") or [Green Tea](/Green_Tea "Green Tea")).

Unlike all other Keg products, Coffee is not considered to be an [Artisan Good](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods"). Therefore, its sale price does not benefit from the [Artisan](/Skills#Farming "Skills") profession. Also, Coffee cannot be sold to the [General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"); it must be sold using the [Shipping Bin](/Shipping "Shipping").

Note that 5 beans of the same quality (normal, silver, or gold) must be used to make coffee; the keg will not accept beans that are not in one stack of 5.

## Tailoring

Coffee can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [![Shirt269.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/42/Shirt269.png/24px-Shirt269.png)](/File:Shirt269.png) [Pour-Over Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").
