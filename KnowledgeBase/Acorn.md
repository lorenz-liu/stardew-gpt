The **Acorn** is a seed that grows into an [Oak Tree](/Oak_Tree "Oak Tree"). It can be obtained by shaking/chopping down an [Oak Tree](/Oak_Tree "Oak Tree") at [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") level 1, in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can"), or by using an [Axe](/Axes "Axes") or [Pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes") to dig up an Acorn dropped by a fully grown [Oak Tree](/Oak_Tree "Oak Tree") on [The Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"). One to five acorns may be produced in a [Woodskip](/Woodskip "Woodskip") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") when the population reaches 9.

Acorns can be planted in tillable ground outside [The Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"). The ground must be un-tilled to plant the seed.

An acorn has approximately a 20% chance to grow to the next stage each night during [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), or [Fall](/Fall "Fall"). There are 5 stages of growth in total. The median time to maturity is about 18 days, with over 90% of seeds maturing in less than 32 growing days.

The use of [Tree Fertilizer](/Tree_Fertilizer "Tree Fertilizer") guarantees growth by one stage each night, including during [Winter](/Winter "Winter").

## Tailoring

Acorn can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Beanie.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/5/51/Beanie.png/24px-Beanie.png) [Beanie](/Beanie "Beanie"). It can also be used as an orange dye in the dye pots located in [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s and [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Two or three Acorns may be requested by several fish in [Fish Pond quests](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond").
