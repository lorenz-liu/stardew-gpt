**Marnie** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives at [Marnie's Ranch](/Marnie%27s_Ranch "Marnie's Ranch") in the northeast area of [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest"), near the southwest entrance to [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town").

Marnie runs her own shop at Marnie's Ranch from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM each day, except Monday and Tuesday, although the building is still open on these days. She sells [animals](/Animals "Animals") and animal supplies.

## Schedule

After the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island is unlocked, Marnie may randomly spend Monday and/or Tuesday there. After leaving the Island at 6pm, Marnie will immediately go home to bed. Marnie never visits the Resort on [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days.

## Relationships

Marnie lives with her niece, [Jas](/Jas "Jas"), and her nephew, [Shane](/Shane "Shane"). When it is not raining, she exercises with [Caroline](/Caroline "Caroline"), [Jodi](/Jodi "Jodi"), [Emily](/Emily "Emily"), and [Robin](/Robin "Robin") on Tuesdays at [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store").

[Mayor Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis") and Marnie have a very close relationship. Although Marnie claims to be single during the [Flower Dance](/Flower_Dance "Flower Dance"), Lewis and Marnie's 6-heart event confirms that they are dating. Lewis visits Marnie's Ranch to attend to her "business needs" on occasion, and the two can often be found drinking together at [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon") in the evening. In one of the [story quests](/Quests#Mayor.27s_.E2.80.9CShorts.E2.80.9D "Quests"), Lewis asks you to find his lucky purple shorts, which can be found in Marnie's bedroom.

![Three Hearts.png](/mediawiki/images/9/9a/Three_Hearts.png)

### Six Hearts

![Six Hearts.png](/mediawiki/images/a/a5/Six_Hearts.png)

On a sunny day, enter the town between 7pm and 11pm.

Note: Only happens if [Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis") is also at 6 hearts.

| **Details** |
| --- |
| You will appear behind Lewis' house. Lewis and Marnie are talking by the river about making their romance public. Lewis says it would undermine his authority, while Marnie says he's too concerned for his job. In the end, Marnie says she'll keep their relationship a secret. Afterwards, you'll pop up and scare both Lewis and Marnie. Lewis asks you if you heard anything, and you are presented with a choice.  * **Yes... but I'll keep it a secret.** *(+50 [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") with Lewis.)* * **Yes... and I'm going to tell everyone.** *(-100 [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") with Lewis.)*   If you choose to keep the secret, Lewis will thank you. If you say you'll tell everyone in town, Lewis will cry. Afterwards, Marnie will ask why you were behind the house, and your character will run away, leaving the two alone and confused. |

## Quests

* ["Cow's Delight"](/Quests#Cow.27s_Delight "Quests"): On the 3rd of [Fall](/Fall "Fall"), Marnie will send you a letter requesting a single bunch of [Amaranth](/Amaranth "Amaranth"). The reward is data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* Marnie may randomly request an item at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). The reward is 3x the item's base value and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
