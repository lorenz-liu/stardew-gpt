The **Ice Cream Stand** is located directly to the east of the bridge leading towards the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith") shop and the [Museum](/Museum "Museum"). It is only open during [Summer](/Summer "Summer") on non-rainy days, when it is not Wednesday, and when [Alex](/Alex "Alex") is behind the counter from 1pm to 5pm. When it is open, [Ice Cream](/Ice_Cream "Ice Cream") can be purchased, and [Haley](/Haley "Haley") can often be found next to the stand, making it a good place to look for her during the summer.

If the player marries Alex he will no longer work there and they will not be able to buy Ice Cream. Haley will still go to the shop even if Alex is married.

## Bug

* During [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), if the player parks their [horse](/Animals#Horse "Animals") behind the ice cream stand when nobody is there, they can buy ice cream at any time. The horse must be parked in the correct spot near the center at the back of the stand.
