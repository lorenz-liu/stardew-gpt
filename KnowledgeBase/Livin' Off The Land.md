**Livin' Off The Land** is a show that airs every Monday and Thursday on [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s [TV](/Television "Television"). It provides tips and tricks to new players to help them get acquainted with the game's mechanics.

After two years, the host announces his retirement, and the episodes repeat. Two of the tips are repeated within the two-year cycle.
