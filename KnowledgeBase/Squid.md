The **Squid** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught in the ocean at [The Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") or on the [Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps#Beach "Farm Maps") in the evening during [Winter](/Winter "Winter"). It can also randomly be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during Winter, or at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="80"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)240–1,000g. Squid can be caught during the entire day on Winter 12th and 13th during the [SquidFest](/SquidFest "SquidFest") festival. [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre") sells five Squid for data-sort-value="10">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 10 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg") each during the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival").

When placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), Squid will produce [Squid Ink](/Squid_Ink "Squid Ink") instead of [Roe](/Roe "Roe"), although Squid Roe can be found in [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") after obtaining the power from the [Jewels Of The Sea](/Jewels_Of_The_Sea "Jewels Of The Sea") book.

Squid can be caught regardless of time of day or season using [Magic Bait](/Magic_Bait "Magic Bait").

## Fish Pond

Squid can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 3 days. The initial pond capacity is 3 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing two quests. Instead of producing [Roe](/Roe "Roe"), squid ponds produce [Squid Ink](/Squid_Ink "Squid Ink").

## Quests

* [Willy](/Willy "Willy") requests a squid by mail on the 2nd of [Winter](/Winter "Winter") in the ["Catch a Squid" Quest](/Quests#Catch_a_Squid "Quests"). The reward is data-sort-value="800">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)800g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* **Item Delivery:** One Squid may be randomly requested in [Winter](/Winter "Winter") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="240">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)240g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* **Fishing:** [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius") or [Willy](/Willy "Willy") may randomly request 1-4 Squid during [Winter](/Winter "Winter") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="80">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)80g per fish. (You get to keep the fish).
* **Aquatic Overpopulation:** [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius") may request 10 Squid to be caught in [Winter](/Winter "Winter") at the [Special Orders board](/Quests#List_of_Special_Orders "Quests") outside the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor") for a reward of gold equivalent to selling the fish and the [Farm Computer](/Farm_Computer "Farm Computer") recipe.
