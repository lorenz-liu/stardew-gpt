# Trash Cans

:   *For the trash cans found around [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"), see [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can").*

The **Trash Can** is a [tool](/Tools "Tools") used to delete items from the [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory") menu, and when upgraded, reclaim some of the items' gold value.

Every player starts with a basic trash can in their inventory, which can be upgraded at the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith") for resources and gold, shown in the table below.

Unlike the other upgradeable tools, the current trash can remains available during the time [Clint](/Clint "Clint") takes to upgrade it.

## Notes

* Even when the Trash Can has been upgraded, [Flooring](/Flooring "Flooring"), [Furniture](/Furniture "Furniture"), and [Wallpaper](/Wallpaper "Wallpaper") give no money when trashed.
* The amount of money reclaimed by a Trash Can *is* affected by professions that affect the sell price of items (*e.g.,* Fisher, Tiller, etc.).
