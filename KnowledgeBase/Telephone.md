The **Telephone** is a piece of equipment that allows players to check store hours and inventory.[[1]](#cite_note-outgoing-1) It can be purchased from the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop") for data-sort-value="2000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,000g. It may be placed anywhere in Stardew Valley, indoors or out.

Phone calls that can be received include scam phone calls, calls from the bear (from [Bear's Knowledge](/Bear%27s_Knowledge "Bear's Knowledge")), from [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent"), [Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis"), or [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre"), from the [hat mouse](/Abandoned_House "Abandoned House"), or a message in a foreign language.[[2]](#cite_note-incoming-2)

Calling a merchant does not affect [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points and does not count as talking to that villager for the day.

The Telephone cannot be used during a [Green Rain](/Weather#Green_Rain "Weather") day.

## Outgoing Calls

[![](/mediawiki/images/c/cb/Answering_Machine.png)](/File:Answering_Machine.png)

Answering Machine portrait

Merchants (or their answering machine) will tell the player their hours for the day; the player can then select additional options depending on the shop. Answering machine messages will vary for days when a merchant is busy, festival days, etc.[[3]](#cite_note-quotes-3)

Call [Robin](/Robin "Robin") to check the hours and stock at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop").

## Bugs

* If the player calls a merchant and checks the store stock while that merchant is away on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island"), the store interface may use the merchant's island portrait.
* It is possible to use the phone to call a merchant while inside their own store. They will have no reaction to this.
