The **Pearl** is a special item that can be obtained in a number of ways.

## Locations

Pearls can be found in:

* 7% chance from [Golden Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Golden_Treasure_Chests "Fishing").[[1]](#cite_note-chestchance-1)
* 0.4% chance while fishing in the [submarine ride](/Night_Market#Fishing_Submarine "Night Market") at the [Night Market](/Night_Market "Night Market") [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") (0.9% if using a [Curiosity Lure](/Curiosity_Lure "Curiosity Lure")).
* 1.7-1.9% chance from a [Blobfish](/Blobfish "Blobfish") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") with a population of at least 9.
* 0.49% Chance from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box").
* 0.94% Chance from opening a [Golden Mystery Box](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box").
* 3.7% chance from an [Artifact Trove](/Artifact_Trove "Artifact Trove").
* Received as a guaranteed gold tier reward on the 12th of Winter for the [SquidFest](/SquidFest "SquidFest") festival.
* One after solving [Journal Scrap #6](/Journal_Scraps#Journal_Scrap_.236 "Journal Scraps") on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island").
* One from [Willy](/Willy "Willy") after filling a Fish Pond with 10 [Crabs](/Crab "Crab").
* Foraging in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") after activating the [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") or during the [Danger In The Deep](/Quests#Danger_In_The_Deep "Quests") quest.
* One by clicking on the clam shells in the correct sequence in the Mermaid Boat at the Night Market. The correct sequence is listed in [Secret Note #15](/Secret_Notes#Secret_Note_.2315 "Secret Notes"). Only one Pearl can be obtained from the Mermaid Boat per [save file](/Saves "Saves"), or one per player in [Multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer").

## Uses

Pearls can be [tailored](/Tailoring "Tailoring"), [gifted](/Friendship#Gifts "Friendship") as a universally loved gift, sold for data-sort-value="2500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,500g, or placed into [Fish Tanks](/Fish_Tank "Fish Tank"). The [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") in the [Calico Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") sells one [Birch Double Bed](/Birch_Double_Bed "Birch Double Bed") for one Pearl.

## Bundles

The Pearl is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

The Pearl is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Bridal Veil.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/05/Bridal_Veil.png/24px-Bridal_Veil.png) [Bridal Veil](/Bridal_Veil "Bridal Veil").

## Quests

The Pearl is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## History

* [1.3.27](/Version_History#1.3.27 "Version History"): Introduced.
* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Added [Artifact Trove](/Artifact_Trove "Artifact Trove") as a source. Can now be used in [Tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). Can be produced by [Fish Ponds](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond").
* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): Added [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") (journal scrap) and [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") to sources. One can now be traded with the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for a [Birch Double Bed](/Birch_Double_Bed "Birch Double Bed"). Chance of catching Pearls in the [submarine ride](/Night_Market#Fishing_Submarine "Night Market") at the [Night Market](/Night_Market "Night Market") can now be increased by using a [Curiosity Lure](/Curiosity_Lure "Curiosity Lure").
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Can now be obtained from [Golden Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Golden_Treasure_Chests "Fishing"), [Mystery Boxes](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") and [Golden Mystery Boxes](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box"). Now a prize for the [SquidFest](/SquidFest "SquidFest") festival. Can now be used as decoration in [Fish Tanks](/Fish_Tank "Fish Tank").
