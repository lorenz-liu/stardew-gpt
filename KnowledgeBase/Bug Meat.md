**Bug Meat** is a [Monster Loot](/Monster_Loot "Monster Loot") item which is dropped from any type of [insect](/Adventurer%27s_Guild#Cave_Insect "Adventurer's Guild"), including [Armored Bugs](/Armored_Bug "Armored Bug") and [Dangerous Armored Bugs](/Armored_Bug_(dangerous) "Armored Bug (dangerous)") if the player has forged their weapon with a [Bug Killer enchantment](/Forge#Combat_enchantments "Forge").

[Dorado](/Dorado "Dorado"), [Lingcod](/Lingcod "Lingcod"), or [Pike](/Pike "Pike") [Fish Ponds](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") may produce 20 Bug Meat when the population of the pond reaches 9.

[Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent") sells Bug Meat in his [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="1">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 1 [Calico Egg](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg").

## Bundles

Bug Meat is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Bug Meat is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt027.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/5/5a/Shirt027.png/24px-Shirt027.png) [Pink Striped Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a dark pink dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the purple dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

* Ten Bug Meat may be requested by several fish in [Fish Pond quests](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond").
* [Willy](/Willy "Willy") requests 100 Bug Meat in the ["Juicy Bugs Wanted!"](/Quests#Juicy_Bugs_Wanted "Quests") Special Order. The rewards are data-sort-value="3000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)3,000g and the [Quality Bobber](/Quality_Bobber "Quality Bobber") recipe.
* Twenty Bug Meat may be requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his fifth request, or as part of any request starting with the sixth.
