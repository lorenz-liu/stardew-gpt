**Large Logs** are fallen trees found on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"). A single large log is found in [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") (blocking the entrance to the [Secret Woods](/Secret_Woods "Secret Woods")). Another large log is found blocking a [secret passage](/The_Alleyway_Buffet "The Alleyway Buffet") to the east between the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith") and [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart"). Large Logs can also be found on floors 41-69 of [the Dangerous Mines](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines"). A [Steel Axe](/Axes "Axes") (or better quality) is required to remove a large log, yielding 8 [Hardwood](/Hardwood "Hardwood") (10 with the [Forester](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") profession) and 25 [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") Experience points. Large Logs will never reappear once removed.

Entrance to the [Secret Woods](/Secret_Woods "Secret Woods") location with the log blocking it.

## Trivia

Destroying a large log takes:

* 9 hits with a Steel Axe
* 7 hits with a Gold Axe
* 6 hits with an Iridium Axe
* 5 hits with a [Powerful](/Forge#Enchantments "Forge") Gold Axe
* 3 hits with a [Powerful](/Forge#Enchantments "Forge") Iridium Axe
