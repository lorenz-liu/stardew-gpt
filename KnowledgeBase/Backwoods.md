# Backwoods

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2e/BackwoodsForageLocations.png/400px-BackwoodsForageLocations.png)](/File:BackwoodsForageLocations.png)

Locations in the Backwoods where forageable items can spawn.

The **Backwoods** is an exterior region in the northwest part of Stardew Valley. It consists of two disconnected sections. The higher elevation section is a path connecting [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") to [the Mountain](/The_Mountain "The Mountain") in an L-shaped curve. The lower section (outlined in blue in the map) can initially only be reached by heading west along the road from the [Bus Stop](/Bus_Stop "Bus Stop"), and provides access to [The Tunnel](/The_Tunnel "The Tunnel"). After the shortcuts [Community Upgrade](/Carpenter%27s_Shop#Community_Upgrades "Carpenter's Shop") is completed, a staircase connects the lower and higher elevations.

## Foraging

[Forageable](/Foraging "Foraging") items found on the ground in the Backwoods are:[[1]](#cite_note-forage_chance-1)

* In [Spring](/Spring "Spring"): ![Leek.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/5/57/Leek.png/24px-Leek.png) [Leek](/Leek "Leek") (58%), and ![Wild Horseradish.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/90/Wild_Horseradish.png/24px-Wild_Horseradish.png) [Wild Horseradish](/Wild_Horseradish "Wild Horseradish") (42%)
* In [Summer](/Summer "Summer"): ![Grape.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/c2/Grape.png/24px-Grape.png) [Grape](/Grape "Grape") (62%), and ![Spice Berry.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/c6/Spice_Berry.png/24px-Spice_Berry.png) [Spice Berry](/Spice_Berry "Spice Berry") (38%)
* In [Fall](/Fall "Fall"): ![Hazelnut.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/31/Hazelnut.png/24px-Hazelnut.png) [Hazelnut](/Hazelnut "Hazelnut") (53%), ![Common Mushroom.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2e/Common_Mushroom.png/24px-Common_Mushroom.png) [Common Mushroom](/Common_Mushroom "Common Mushroom") (24%), and ![Wild Plum.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3b/Wild_Plum.png/24px-Wild_Plum.png) [Wild Plum](/Wild_Plum "Wild Plum") (24%)
* In [Winter](/Winter "Winter"): ![Holly.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/b8/Holly.png/24px-Holly.png) [Holly](/Holly "Holly") (43%), ![Crocus.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2f/Crocus.png/24px-Crocus.png) [Crocus](/Crocus "Crocus") (35%), and ![Crystal Fruit.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/16/Crystal_Fruit.png/24px-Crystal_Fruit.png) [Crystal Fruit](/Crystal_Fruit "Crystal Fruit") (22%)

Forageable items spawn at an average rate of 0.4 per night in spring, summer, and fall; in winter the rate is 0.25 per night.[[2]](#cite_note-forage_spawn-2) They spawn only in the higher-elevation section of the Backwoods. The map shows the possible locations as red and magenta tiles; when the original trees are present, the magenta tiles have a 90% smaller chance of spawning items because they are identified as being behind the original trees.

There are also numerous [Salmonberry](/Salmonberry "Salmonberry") and [Blackberry](/Blackberry "Blackberry") bushes in both sections of the Backwoods.

## Artifact Spots

The only [Artifact](/Artifacts "Artifacts") that can be found by digging up [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") in the Backwoods is:

* ![Skeletal Hand.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Skeletal_Hand.png/24px-Skeletal_Hand.png) [Skeletal Hand](/Skeletal_Hand "Skeletal Hand") (4-8%[[3]](#cite_note-artifact_extra-3))

Other possible items are:

* 1-3 ![Clay.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a2/Clay.png/24px-Clay.png) [Clay](/Clay "Clay") (18-39%[[3]](#cite_note-artifact_extra-3))
* ![Lost Book.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/f3/Lost_Book.png/24px-Lost_Book.png) [Lost Book](/Lost_Books "Lost Books") (20% + 5-9%[[3]](#cite_note-artifact_extra-3)); once all Lost Books have been found, any potential Lost Book is replaced by ![Mixed Seeds.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2e/Mixed_Seeds.png/24px-Mixed_Seeds.png) [Mixed Seeds](/Mixed_Seeds "Mixed Seeds").
* Only in [Winter](/Winter "Winter"): ![Winter Root.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/11/Winter_Root.png/24px-Winter_Root.png) [Winter Root](/Winter_Root "Winter Root") (24%)
* Only in [Winter](/Winter "Winter"): ![Snow Yam.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Snow_Yam.png/24px-Snow_Yam.png) [Snow Yam](/Snow_Yam "Snow Yam") (16%)
* 1-3 ![Stone.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/d4/Stone.png/24px-Stone.png) [Stone](/Stone "Stone") (6-13%[[3]](#cite_note-artifact_extra-3))
* 1-3 ![Copper Ore.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/78/Copper_Ore.png/24px-Copper_Ore.png) [Copper Ore](/Copper_Ore "Copper Ore") (3-7%[[3]](#cite_note-artifact_extra-3))
* Only in [Spring](/Spring "Spring"): 2-5 ![Rice Shoot.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/84/Rice_Shoot.png/24px-Rice_Shoot.png) [Rice Shoot](/Rice_Shoot "Rice Shoot")s (5%)
* 1-3 ![Coal.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a7/Coal.png/24px-Coal.png) [Coal](/Coal "Coal") (2.4-5%[[3]](#cite_note-artifact_extra-3))
* An unseen ![Secret Note.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/ec/Secret_Note.png/24px-Secret_Note.png) [Secret Note](/Secret_Notes "Secret Notes"): up to 3% chance, only if the player has a [Magnifying Glass](/Magnifying_Glass "Magnifying Glass").

Artifact spots spawn only in the higher-elevation section of the Backwoods, at an average rate of 0.06 per night, except in winter when the average rate increases to 0.4 per night.[[4]](#cite_note-artifact_spawn-4)

## Villager Paths

No [villagers](/Villagers "Villagers") walk through the Backwoods, so items can be [safely placed](/Crafting#Crafted_goods_outside_the_farm "Crafting") in any location.

## Quests

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/dd/Blackberry_Basket_JA.jpg/300px-Blackberry_Basket_JA.jpg)](/File:Blackberry_Basket_JA.jpg)

Linus' Blackberry Basket

* After receiving [Linus](/Linus "Linus")' letter and accepting the [blackberry basket quest](/Quests#Blackberry_Basket "Quests"), the basket is located in the lower section of the Backwoods at the left side of the screen. Although it can be seen from the upper path, the basket can only be reached by exiting the Bus Stop to the west along the road.

* The Backwoods is the scene of [Abigail](/Abigail "Abigail")'s 14-heart event.
