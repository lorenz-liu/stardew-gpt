The **Taro Root** is a [vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from [Taro Tuber](/Taro_Tuber "Taro Tuber") after 10 days, or 7 days if the crop is planted near water. Taro Root plants do not need to be watered if they are planted near water.

## Tailoring

A Taro Root can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Shirt And Belt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt159.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/99/Shirt159.png/24px-Shirt159.png)](/File:Shirt159.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* Three Taro Roots may be requested by [Blue Discus](/Blue_Discus "Blue Discus") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 3 to 5.
* Ten Taro Roots may be requested by [Blue Discus](/Blue_Discus "Blue Discus") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 5 to 10.
* [Caroline](/Caroline "Caroline") may request 100 Taro Roots in the ["Island Ingredients"](/Quests#Island_Ingredients "Quests") Special Order. The reward is gold equivalent to 50% of base crop price and the [Solar Panel](/Solar_Panel "Solar Panel") recipe.

## Trivia

* After harvesting a Taro Root, the ground underneath it will remain watered for the rest of the day.
