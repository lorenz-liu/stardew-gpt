**Blue Grass Starters** will instantly place a patch of [Blue Grass](/Grass#Blue_Grass "Grass") when used on the ground. Over time, a patch of blue grass will grow and spread to surrounding tiles on the farm. Blue Grass can be harvested similarly to normal [Grass](/Grass "Grass") with the [Scythe](/Scythe "Scythe") to produce [Hay](/Hay "Hay"), once a [Silo](/Silo "Silo") is built. Blue Grass Starters can be planted in Winter, but they won't grow and spread to surrounding tiles.

## Tailoring

Blue Grass Starter is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a blue dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a blue dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.
