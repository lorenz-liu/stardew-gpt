**Complete Breakfast** is a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit").

Complete Breakfast may randomly appear in [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus")' shop on [Saturdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Saturday "Days of the Week"), in the [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") outside the saloon, or in [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon")'s rotating stock. The [Statue Of Endless Fortune](/Statue_Of_Endless_Fortune "Statue Of Endless Fortune") produces one Complete Breakfast per year on [Alex](/Alex "Alex")'s birthday, [Summer 13](/Summer "Summer"). Five Complete Breakfasts are the reward for completing the ![Bundle Yellow.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2b/Bundle_Yellow.png/24px-Bundle_Yellow.png) [Home Cook's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Home_Cook.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Remixed Bundles"). One Complete Breakfast may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box").

In total ingredients, cooking a Complete Breakfast from scratch requires 2 [Eggs](/Egg "Egg"), 1 [Oil](/Oil "Oil"), 1 [Wheat Flour](/Wheat_Flour "Wheat Flour"), 1 [Potato](/Potato "Potato"), and 1 [Milk](/Milk "Milk").

Complete Breakfast is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Recipes

Complete Breakfast is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Complete Breakfast is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt000.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/db/Shirt000.png/24px-Shirt000.png) [Classic Overalls](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a yellow dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the yellow dye pot at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"), for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").
