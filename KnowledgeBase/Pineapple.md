The **Pineapple** is a [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from [Pineapple Seeds](/Pineapple_Seeds "Pineapple Seeds") after 14 days. It grows during Summer on [The Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") or at any time on [Ginger Island's Farm.](/Ginger_Island#The_Farm "Ginger Island")

## Quests

* One Pineapple may be requested by [Lionfish](/Lionfish "Lionfish") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 3 to 5.
* [Caroline](/Caroline "Caroline") may request 100 Pineapples in the ["Island Ingredients"](/Quests#Island_Ingredients "Quests") Special Order. The reward is gold equivalent to 50% of base crop price and the [Solar Panel](/Solar_Panel "Solar Panel") recipe.

## Tailoring

Pineapple can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Farmer Pants](/Tailoring#Pants "Tailoring"). [![Farmer Pants.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/78/Farmer_Pants.png/36px-Farmer_Pants.png)](/File:Farmer_Pants.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").
