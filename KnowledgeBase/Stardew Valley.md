# Stardew Valley

[![Main Logo.png](/mediawiki/images/6/68/Main_Logo.png)](/File:Main_Logo.png)

**Stardew Valley** is an open-ended country-life RPG. You've inherited your grandfather's old farm plot in Stardew Valley. Armed with hand-me-down tools and a few coins, you set out to begin your new life. Can you learn to live off the land and turn these overgrown fields into a thriving home? It won't be easy. Ever since Joja Corporation came to town, the old ways of life have all but disappeared. The community center, once the town's most vibrant hub of activity, now lies in shambles. But the valley seems full of opportunity. With a little dedication, you might just be the one to restore Stardew Valley to greatness!

## History

### Development

| Period | Summary |
| --- | --- |
| 2012–2016 ([1.0](/Version_History#1.0 "Version History")–[1.2.33](/Version_History#1.2.33 "Version History")) | The game was developed entirely by [ConcernedApe](/ConcernedApe "ConcernedApe") (Eric Barone) up to Stardew Valley [1.2.33](/Version_History#1.2.33 "Version History"), including all art, sound effects, and music. The game is programmed in C# using the Microsoft XNA framework (on Windows) and MonoGame (on Linux and macOS). The art was drawn using Paint.NET, and the game's music and sound effects were composed in Propellerhead Software's Reason. It took four years for ConcernedApe to develop the first public release, during which he posted development updates on [stardewvalley.blogspot.com](https://stardewvalley.blogspot.com/) (2012-2013) and [the game's official website](https://stardewvalley.net/blog/) (2013-present). It was released to the public on February 26th, 2016. |
| 2018–2019 ([1.3](/Version_History#1.3.27 "Version History")–[1.3.36](/Version_History#1.3.36 "Version History")) | For Stardew Valley 1.3, ConcernedApe focused on the gameplay and content changes while Tom Coxon (then at Chucklefish) developed the multiplayer code. |
| 2019–2020 ([1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History")–[1.4.5](/Version_History#1.4.5 "Version History")) | In early 2019, ConcernedApe formed a small development team to continue updating the game. Versions [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History") through [1.4.5](/Version_History#1.4.5 "Version History") were developed by ConcernedApe, Arthur Lee (*a.k.a.* Mr. Podunkian), and Alex Erlandson (a.k.a. zillix). |
| 2020–2021 ([1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History")–[1.5.6](/Version_History#1.5.6 "Version History")) | Versions [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History") through [1.5.5](/Version_History#1.5.5 "Version History") were developed by ConcernedApe and Arthur Lee (*a.k.a.* Mr. Podunkian). |
| 2024 ([1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History")–[1.6.15](/Version_History#1.6.15 "Version History")) | Versions [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History") through [1.6.15](/Version_History#1.6.15 "Version History") were developed by ConcernedApe, Jesse Plamondon-Willard (*a.k.a.* Pathoschild), Devin Hedegaard, Arthur Lee (*a.k.a.* Mr. Podunkian; [1.6.0 only](https://x.com/MrPodunkian/status/1544524948626018305)), Jared Flores, and Casey Warrington. |

### Porting

The official updates on PC are ported to other platforms by specialized porting companies. [The Secret Police](https://twitter.com/sepogames) ported mobile versions 1.0 through 1.4, and [Sickhead Games](https://www.sickheadgames.com/) ported mobile version 1.5. Sickhead Games also ported all console versions.

### Publishers

Stardew Valley was initially published by London game studio [Chucklefish](https://chucklefish.org), best known for developing the space sandbox-adventure game Starbound. Stardew Valley is now self-published by ConcernedApe on all platforms (PC, Xbox One, PlayStation 4, PS Vita as of 14 December 2018;[[1]](#cite_note-1) Switch as of 01 October 2019;[[2]](#cite_note-2) iOS as of 24 October 2021;[[3]](#cite_note-3) Android as of 17 March 2022[[4]](#cite_note-4)).

### Supported platforms

Stardew Valley is available on PC (Linux, macOS, and Windows), console (PS4, PS Vita, Xbox One, and Switch), and mobile (Android and iOS). A Wii U port was planned but canceled in favor of the Nintendo Switch.

Dates of first release:

* Windows: 26 February 2016;
* Linux and Mac: 29 July 2016;
* PS4 and Xbox One: 14 December 2016;
* Switch: 05 October 2017;
* PS Vita: 22 May 2018 (US), 24 May 2018 (Europe);
* iOS: 24 October 2018;
* Android: 13 March 2019.
