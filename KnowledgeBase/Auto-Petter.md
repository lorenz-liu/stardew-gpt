The **Auto-Petter** is a [tool](/Tools "Tools") used to automatically pet [barn](/Barn "Barn") and [coop](/Coop "Coop") [animals](/Animals "Animals") every day. It can be purchased from [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart") for data-sort-value="50000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)50,000g after completing the [Joja Community Development Form](/Joja_Community_Development_Form "Joja Community Development Form"). It can also be found in [Skull Cavern treasure chests](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern") (3.6% chance) and [Golden Mystery Boxes](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box") (0.5% chance). Additionally, it can be obtained by killing monsters as a [special item](/The_Mines#Special_Items "The Mines") in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") during the [Danger In The Deep quest](/Quests#Danger_In_The_Deep "Quests") or after activating the [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines").

Auto-Petters can be placed anywhere, but will not do anything unless placed inside a barn or coop. To remove an Auto-Petter, it must be struck with a [tool](/Tools "Tools") or by repeated left-clicking.

Auto-Petters cannot be sold or [deconstructed](/Deconstructor "Deconstructor").

## Animal Friendship

The Auto-Petter will prevent animals from losing [Friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals") or Mood due to lack of petting and animals will gain approximately the same Mood boost compared to manually petting. However, they will only gain 8 Friendship points (just over half of the normal amount of Friendship points) from petting this way. They will gain 15 Friendship points if they are also manually petted, exactly the same as the normal amount of Friendship points. Mood gains from an autopetter stacks with manual.[[1]](#cite_note-friendship-1) Mood gain from an autopetter is not affected by the [Coopmaster or Shepherd Professions](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming").
