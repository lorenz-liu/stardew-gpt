**Junimos** are forest spirits that have taken up residence in the broken down [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center").

[The Player](/The_Player "The Player") can complete [Bundles](/Bundles "Bundles") for the Junimos by collecting and offering various items found in the Valley. When a bundle is completed, a Junimo will come and collect it, then stash it away in the hut in the main room of the Community Center. Completing a bundle will reward the player with an item. Completing all bundles in a room will reward the player with an improvement to the valley, and a star toward Community Center completion. After placing a star, a Junimo will follow the player around until the player leaves the Community Center. After all stars have been placed, the Community Center will be repaired, and the Junimos will return to the forest.

During the night after all bundles in a room of the Community Center are completed, the player will see a cutscene in which the Junimos make the indicated improvement to the town. Completing a bundle on the day a child is due for birth or adoption may prevent the cutscene from triggering.

If the player buys a [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart") membership, the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") will be turned into a [Joja Warehouse](/Joja_Warehouse "Joja Warehouse") and the player will no longer see any Junimos unless they construct a [Junimo Hut](/Junimo_Hut "Junimo Hut").

## Junimo Hut

[![Junimo Hut.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/73/Junimo_Hut.png/80px-Junimo_Hut.png)](/File:Junimo_Hut.png)

:   *Main article: [Junimo Hut](/Junimo_Hut "Junimo Hut")*

Players can purchase a [Junimo Hut](/Junimo_Hut "Junimo Hut") from the [Wizard](/Wizard%27s_Tower "Wizard's Tower") after completing the [Goblin Problem Quest](/Quests#Goblin_Problem "Quests"). Once placed, Junimos will live in the hut on the player's [farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"). They'll automatically harvest any ripe crops within range of the hut and store them inside, where they can be gathered by the player at any time.

## Secrets

* The player can collect a decorative [Junimo Plush](/Junimo_Plush "Junimo Plush") by interacting with a bush north of the playground in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") on the 28th of any [season](/Seasons "Seasons") at 12:00pm.
* On the title screen, clicking any of the leaves ten times will cause three Junimos to appear and wave.
* [Secret Note #14](/Secret_Notes#Secret_Note_.2314 "Secret Notes") leads the player to find a [Stone Junimo](/Stone_Junimo "Stone Junimo") statue.

## Trivia

* The Player can hit Junimos with a [Slingshot](/Slingshot "Slingshot") or [Master Slingshot](/Master_Slingshot "Master Slingshot"), but they will not show a hate bubble. The ammo will hit the Junimo and a bursting animation will appear.
* When the player stands in front of a Junimo carrying a completed bundle to their hut in the community center, the Junimo will speed up like a villager would.
* [Sam's](/Sam "Sam") 14 heart event mentions Sam making music for a TV show called the Happy Junimo Show, this indicates that Junimos may be fairytale creatures in the world of Stardew Valley, that are thought to not be real but are. [Penny](/Penny "Penny") has an open book in her room that is titled "Fairys, Junimos and other Fables" which also adds to this idea.
* A Junimo appears as the main playable character in the in-game arcade game [Junimo Kart](/Junimo_Kart "Junimo Kart").
* The [Junimo Catalogue](/Junimo_Catalogue "Junimo Catalogue") allows the player to obtain a wide range of Junimo-themed furniture items for use in decorating their house.
* According to the item's in-game description, [Raisins](/Raisins "Raisins") are said to be the Junimos' favourite food.
* A Junimo appears as a pet in the video game *Terraria*, as part of a crossover with Stardew Valley.
