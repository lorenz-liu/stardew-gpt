**Large Stumps** are the remains of long-dead trees that are found on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"), the [Secret Woods](/Secret_Woods "Secret Woods") and [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"). A [Copper Axe](/Axes "Axes") (or better quality) is required to remove a large stump, yielding 2 [Hardwood](/Hardwood "Hardwood") and 25 [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") Experience points. Players with the [Forester](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") profession have a 50% chance of receiving one extra piece of hardwood. Stumps also have a 10% chance of dropping [Mahogany Seeds](/Mahogany_Seed "Mahogany Seed").

Certain large stumps will respawn daily, providing a renewable source for hardwood:

* All six large stumps in the Secret Woods respawn.
* On the [Forest Farm](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps"), there are eight large stumps at the west side that respawn. On the [Four Corners Farm](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps"), there is one large stump in the top left quadrant of the map that respawns.
* Large stumps in the Mines (floors 41-69 with the [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") activated) can be found.

All other large stumps on the Farm disappear permanently after being removed.

## Trivia

* Destroying a large stump takes:
  + 7 hits with a [Copper Axe](/Axes "Axes")
  + 5 hits with a [Steel Axe](/Axes "Axes")
  + 4 hits with a [Gold Axe](/Axes "Axes")
  + 3 hits with an [Iridium Axe](/Axes "Axes")
  + 2 hits with a [Powerful](/Forge#Enchantments "Forge") Iridium Axe
