The **Carpenter's Shop** is located in [the Mountain](/The_Mountain "The Mountain") area north of [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). [Robin](/Robin "Robin") is the town carpenter and runs the shop, which is inside her home where she lives with [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius"), [Maru](/Maru "Maru"), and [Sebastian](/Sebastian "Sebastian"). The shop is open most days from 9:00am to 5:00pm.

Robin sells [wood](/Wood "Wood"), [stone](/Stone "Stone"), and [furniture](/Furniture "Furniture"). She can also upgrade the [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") and construct new farm buildings. She will buy building-related Resources,[[1]](#cite_note-purchases-1) namely [Clay](/Clay "Clay"), [Fiber](/Fiber "Fiber"), [Hardwood](/Hardwood "Hardwood"), [Stone](/Stone "Stone"), and [Wood](/Wood "Wood") (all other Resources are purchased by the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith") instead).

She will begin construction the day after buildings are purchased, but she does not work on [festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days. Most construction projects are finished in 2 days (by the start of the second day after placing an order). During construction or upgrades, Robin may be unreachable for [dialogue or gifting](/Friendship "Friendship").

## Shop Closure

The house is accessible from 9:00am to 8:00pm every day, even when the shop is closed.

The shop is closed on Tuesdays (except when it rains), as [Robin](/Robin "Robin") goes to [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") to exercise with [Caroline](/Caroline "Caroline"), [Jodi](/Jodi "Jodi"), [Emily](/Emily "Emily"), and [Marnie](/Marnie "Marnie"). However, she walks past the sales counter at 9:40am on her way. It is possible to shop at that time by entering before then and use the counter just as she passes. Similarly, Robin returns home at 8:00pm, and it is possible to shop as she enters her home and passes the sales counter.

The shop closes at 4:00pm on Fridays, when [Robin](/Robin "Robin") and [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius") go to [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon"). By entering the house before 4:00pm and missing her during open hours, it is possible to still shop by catching her as she passes the sales counter on the way out, just as on Tuesday mornings.

The shop is always closed on Thursday, [Summer](/Summer "Summer") 18, when Robin goes to [Harvey's Clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic"). She leaves the Clinic at 4pm to walk home, and passes the sales counter at approximately 5:50pm. It is possible to shop when she passes the sales counter.

The shop is also closed on all festival days. If she was still working on a job on the farm the day before, then these closures will delay the job completion by one day. However, Robin does work on regular Tuesdays when she already has a job lined up, so there is no delay in that case.

The shop is closed all day any day that she is working on a job on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm").

## Shop Inventory

In addition to Permanent Stock, which Robin has for sale every day, she has a rotating stock of [Furniture](/Furniture "Furniture") that changes daily.

### Permanent Stock

Note that the prices of [wood](/Wood "Wood") and [stone](/Stone "Stone") increase in Year 2. The player will receive a letter in the mail on Spring 1 of Year 2 saying "The price of raw materials at Robin's shop and [Clint's shop](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith") has increased." The gold obtained by selling wood or stone to Robin or through the [Shipping Bin](/Shipping "Shipping") remains unchanged in Year 2+.

## Farm Buildings

:   *For the magical Buildings see [Wizard's Tower](/Wizard%27s_Tower#Buildings "Wizard's Tower")*

Farm buildings have a variety of uses. Some are required to house [Animals](/Animals "Animals"). Both [Coops](/Coop "Coop") and [Barns](/Barn "Barn") can be upgraded to house more/different animals. The player may build as many farm buildings as space allows, including multiple buildings of the same type. The construction time needed for each building is visible in the construction menu.

While attempting to place a building, whether buying or moving it, all placement indicator tiles must be free of obstructions and marked as green; obstructed tiles are red. Obstructions can include rocks, twigs, and weeds, as well as *any* player placed items such as torches or furniture. The only objects that do not block building placement are tree seeds, crafted [flooring and paths](/Crafting#Decor "Crafting"), and [artifact spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot"), but the items are destroyed in the process. Farm animals and the [cat or dog](/Animals#Cat_or_Dog "Animals") obstruct building placement, but clicking to place the building on top of them moves them aside.

The buttons in the bottom-right of the Carpenter's Shop construction menu allow the player to [paint](#Painting), move, or demolish existing buildings. Starter farm buildings (including the [Greenhouse](/Greenhouse "Greenhouse") and default [Shipping Bin](/Shipping "Shipping")) can also be moved in the same construction menu at the Carpenter's Shop. Moving a building applies instantly and is free, requiring no construction time, gold, or resources other than the travel time to the Carpenter's Shop. The building's contents move along with the building, all as a unit; a building does not need to be empty in order to be moved.

Tip: Building a [Silo](/Silo "Silo") before building a [Coop](/Coop "Coop") or [Barn](/Barn "Barn") allows [Hay](/Hay "Hay") to be stockpiled before it's required to feed animals.

## Painting

The farmhouse, stable, and some farm buildings can be painted at the Carpenter's Shop by selecting "Paint Buildings" under the construction menu. This action applies instantly and is free, requiring no construction time, gold, or resources.

* Sheds, barns, coops, and cabins must be fully upgraded to be painted.
* The farmhouse can only have its roof painted until the exterior is fully upgraded.

Shown below are the areas where buildings can be painted. Players can paint any combination of the "building" (red area), "trim" (blue/purple area), and/or "roof" (green area).

The usual color, saturation, and shading sliders apply, and colors can be saved and applied to other parts of the building (or other buildings). Clicking the dotted icon will restore the default colors.

## Community Upgrades

### Trailer

:   *See also: [Trailer](/Trailer "Trailer")*

After fully upgrading the [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") and completing the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") (or the [Joja Warehouse](/Joja_Warehouse "Joja Warehouse")), players are able to purchase a "Community Upgrade" at the Carpenter's Shop for data-sort-value="500000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500,000g and 950 [Wood](/Wood "Wood"). Robin then builds a new house for [Pam](/Pam "Pam") (and [Penny](/Penny "Penny"), if the player is not [married](/Marriage "Marriage") to Penny). This takes three days. Note that in a [multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer") game only the host player is able to purchase this upgrade.

### Town Shortcuts

After completing the community upgrade, the player may purchase a second upgrade for data-sort-value="300000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)300,000g that creates shortcuts to help navigate the valley more quickly. This takes three days.

* From [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") (next to Leah's cottage) to [the Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach"), an east/west path along the water passable on horseback. It is possible to take this path to the beach even at times that a festival is being set up in town, the only non-warping way to get there at those times.
* From [the Mountains](/The_Mountain "The Mountain") (southwest of the quarry bridge) to [Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") (north of [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart")), a riverside path too narrow for a horse.
* From [the Mountains](/The_Mountain "The Mountain") (along the fence east of the Carpenter's Shop) to [Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") (north of [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart") and across the river), a riverside path too narrow for a horse.
* From the [Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") (south of the museum) to [the Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") (Between the Tide Pool trees), a narrow path hidden in foliage, impassable on horseback.
* From the [Bus Stop](/Bus_Stop "Bus Stop") (by the tunnel) to the [Backwoods](/Backwoods "Backwoods"), a flight of steps passable on horseback.
* From the island in [the Mountains](/The_Mountain "The Mountain") to south of the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild"), a wood plank bridge too narrow for a horse.

*(It's possible to use the town-to-beach tide pool shortcut without ever fixing the bridge on the beach)*

## Trivia
