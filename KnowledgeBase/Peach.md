The **Peach** is a [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") grown by planting a [Peach Sapling](/Peach_Sapling "Peach Sapling"), waiting 28 days for it to grow into a [Peach Tree](/Peach_Tree "Peach Tree"), and harvesting the tree during the [Summer](/Summer "Summer").

A Peach may be purchased at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="140"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)420–1,000g or found in [The Farm Cave](/The_Cave "The Cave"), if the fruit bat option is chosen.

## Bundles

Peach is an option for the ![Bundle Purple.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a1/Bundle_Purple.png/24px-Bundle_Purple.png) [Artisan Bundle](/Bundles#Artisan_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Pantry](/Bundles#Pantry "Bundles").

## Recipes

Peach is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Peach can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Relaxed Fit Shorts](/Tailoring#Pants "Tailoring"). [![Relaxed Fit Shorts.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/4a/Relaxed_Fit_Shorts.png/36px-Relaxed_Fit_Shorts.png)](/File:Relaxed_Fit_Shorts.png) It also can be used as a red dye color in the Dye Pots, found inside [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Peach is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
