The **Treasure Chest** is a special item that can be obtained in several ways.

## Locations

Treasure chests can be found from the following sources:

* 0.24% chance from a [Fishing Treasure Chest](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") after reaching [Fishing level 2](/Fishing#Fishing_Skill "Fishing").
* 3.7% chance from an [Artifact Trove](/Artifact_Trove "Artifact Trove").
* 0.8% chance from a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box").
* 1.57% chance from a [Golden Mystery Box](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box").
* 0.3% chance from a [Spook Fish](/Spook_Fish "Spook Fish") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") with a population of at least 9.
* One after finding [Secret Note #16](/Secret_Notes#Secret_Note_.2316 "Secret Notes") and [digging](/Hoes "Hoes") at the red "X" depicted in the note.
* Received as a guaranteed gold tier reward on the 13th of Winter for the [SquidFest](/SquidFest "SquidFest") festival.
* Randomly obtained from the [prize machine](/Mayor%27s_Manor#Prize_Machine "Mayor's Manor") in the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor") after the player has received 22 prizes.

## Bundles

The Treasure Chest is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

The Treasure Chest is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Pirate Hat.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/04/Pirate_Hat.png/24px-Pirate_Hat.png) [Pirate Hat](/Pirate_Hat "Pirate Hat"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

The Treasure Chest is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Trivia

* If a Treasure Chest is gifted to [Linus](/Linus "Linus"), he will not accept the gift and will give it back to the player.

## History

* [1.0](/Version_History#1.0 "Version History"): Introduced.
* [1.3.27](/Version_History#1.3.27 "Version History"): Can be obtained after finding [Secret Note #16](/Secret_Notes#Secret_Note_.2316 "Secret Notes").
* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Added [Artifact Trove](/Artifact_Trove "Artifact Trove") as a source. Can now be used in [Tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). Can be produced by Fish Ponds.
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Now a prize for the [SquidFest](/SquidFest "SquidFest") festival. Added as possible prize from [Mystery Boxes](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") and [Golden Mystery Boxes](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box"). No longer a universally hated gift; is now disliked by Linus and liked by everyone else. Can now be obtained from the [prize machine](/Mayor%27s_Manor#Prize_Machine "Mayor's Manor"). Can now be used as decoration in [Fish Tanks](/Fish_Tank "Fish Tank").
