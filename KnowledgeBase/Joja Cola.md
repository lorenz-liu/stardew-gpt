**Joja Cola** is a type of [trash](/Trash "Trash") that can be caught using a [Fishing Pole](/Tools#Fishing_Poles "Tools") during all seasons, in any location. Players trying to intentionally catch Joja Colas have a better chance at a trash-only location (*i.e.,* a Farm Pond on the [Standard Farm Map](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps")), or when casting close to the shore.

It can be purchased for data-sort-value="75">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)75g from [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart") or from the [Vending Machine](/The_Stardrop_Saloon#Joja_Vending_Machine "The Stardrop Saloon") in [The Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon"). [Sam](/Sam "Sam") sells Joja Cola in his [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="2">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 2 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg"). It can be found in any [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can"), with a higher chance in the JojaMart Garbage Can. It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") cat with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals").

Players who choose to complete the [Joja Warehouse](/Joja_Warehouse "Joja Warehouse") instead of the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") will be rewarded with a [Soda Machine](/Soda_Machine "Soda Machine") that produces one Joja Cola per day.

Unlike other trash items, Joja Cola cannot be obtained from [Crab Pots](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot") or [Fish Ponds](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"); it also cannot be [recycled](/Recycling_Machine "Recycling Machine").

## Tailoring

Joja Cola is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt105.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/99/Shirt105.png/24px-Shirt105.png) [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a blue dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the blue dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Trivia

* Joja Cola was featured in [strip #33](http://wumbusworld.com/index.php?strip=33) of a series of webcomics called [Wumbus World](http://wumbusworld.com/index.php?strip=1), created by [ConcernedApe](/ConcernedApe "ConcernedApe") in 2011. The newspaper ad it was featured in showed a pink can with a blue label and had the following text: "TRY **NEW** JOJA COLA! Now with 3x more [aspartame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/aspartame "wikipedia:aspartame") for a sweet blast."
* The Joja Cola slogan is "Fuel your life."
* Joja Cola was added to the game [Terraria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terraria "wikipedia:Terraria") with ConcernedApe's permission in October 2022.
* Despite [Shane](/Shane "Shane") hating Joja Cola as a gift, he is seen surrounded by empty Joja Cola cans in his fourteen hearts event.
