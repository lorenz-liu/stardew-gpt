The **Chub** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught in [the mountain lake](/The_Mountain "The Mountain") and the [Cindersap Forest River](/Cindersap_Forest#River "Cindersap Forest") during all seasons. Unlike most fish found in the Forest River, it cannot be caught in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") cat with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals"). It may also randomly appear in [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus")' shop on [Wednesdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Wednesday "Days of the Week") for data-sort-value="200">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)200g, or at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="50"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150–1,000g.

## Fish Pond

Chub can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 2 days. The initial pond capacity is 3 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing three quests. The only possible output is dark gray Chub [Roe](/Roe "Roe").

## Quests

* **Fishing:** [Willy](/Willy "Willy") may randomly request 1-4 chubs during any season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="50">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)50g per fish. (You get to keep the fish).
* Two Chub may be requested by [Dorado](/Dorado "Dorado"), [Lingcod](/Lingcod "Lingcod"), or [Pike](/Pike "Pike") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 3 to 5.
