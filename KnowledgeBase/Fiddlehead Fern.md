The **Fiddlehead Fern** is a [vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") that can be found:

* [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") in the [Secret Woods](/Secret_Woods "Secret Woods") during [Summer](/Summer "Summer") (78%).
* Foraging in the [Prehistoric floors](/Skull_Cavern#Prehistoric_Floors "Skull Cavern") of the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern").
* Foraging on levels 41-69 of [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") while the [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") is active.
* Foraging in the [Ginger Island jungle](/Ginger_Island#Island_East "Ginger Island") in all [seasons](/Seasons "Seasons").
* Occasionally at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="90"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)270–1,000g.
* By chopping [Green Rain Trees (type 3)](/Green_Rain_Trees "Green Rain Trees") during [Green Rain](/Weather "Weather").
* By placing a [Tapper](/Tapper "Tapper") on fully-grown [Green Rain Trees (type 3)](/Green_Rain_Trees "Green Rain Trees").
* In [Kent](/Kent "Kent")'s [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="10">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 10 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg").

The Fiddlehead Fern is the only foraged item categorized as a vegetable. Being a vegetable, its sell price benefits from the [Tiller](/Skills#Farming "Skills") 10% bonus and it can be turned into [Pickles](/Jellies_and_Pickles "Jellies and Pickles") or [Juice](/Juice "Juice") using a [Preserves Jar](/Preserves_Jar "Preserves Jar") or [Keg](/Keg "Keg"). However, it also receives several [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging")-related benefits, because it is obtained by foraging. Specifically, 7 Foraging XP is granted per fern and it benefits from the [Gatherer](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") double-harvest bonus and [Botanist](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") iridium-quality bonus.

## Tailoring

Fiddlehead Fern is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create ![Shirt007.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/b1/Shirt007.png/24px-Shirt007.png) [Green Overalls](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a green dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the green dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").
