The **Chanterelle** is found via [foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") in the [Secret Woods](/Secret_Woods "Secret Woods") in the [Fall](/Fall "Fall"), grown in [The Farm Cave](/The_Cave "The Cave") if the Mushroom option is chosen, or produced by a [Mushroom Log](/Mushroom_Log "Mushroom Log"). It may also be found in the [Ginger Island Mushroom Cave](/Ginger_Island#Dig_Site "Ginger Island").

## Tailoring

Chanterelle is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create an ![Shirt015.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/d9/Shirt015.png/24px-Shirt015.png) [Orange Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is an orange dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the orange dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").
