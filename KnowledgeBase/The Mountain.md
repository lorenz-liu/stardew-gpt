# The Mountain

[![The Mountain.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/b5/The_Mountain.png/500px-The_Mountain.png)](/File:The_Mountain.png)

**The Mountain** is an exterior region in the northern part of Stardew Valley. Initially, there are only two exits: to the south leading to [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"), and to the west leading to the [Backwoods](/Backwoods "Backwoods"). However, over the course of the game, several events open up new routes:

* On [Spring](/Spring "Spring") 5, debris blocking the bridge north of the lake is cleared, providing access to [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") and the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild").
* On [Summer](/Summer "Summer") 3, an earthquake opens a pass north from the Mountain to the [Railroad](/Railroad "Railroad").
* The bridge leading to the [Quarry](/Quarry "Quarry") at the east end of the map can be repaired (by completing all [Crafts Room Bundles](/Bundles#Crafts_Room "Bundles") or purchasing "Bridge" from the [Joja Community Development Form](/Joja_Community_Development_Form "Joja Community Development Form") for data-sort-value="25000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)25,000g).
* The Glittering Boulder blocking the water flow west of the mine entrance can be removed (by completing all [Fish Tank Bundles](/Bundles#Fish_Tank "Bundles") or purchasing "Panning" from the Joja Community Development Form for data-sort-value="20000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)20,000g).
* After completing the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") or purchasing the [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart") subscription for data-sort-value="5000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)5,000g you can purchase a "[Community Upgrade](/Carpenter%27s_Shop#Community_Upgrades "Carpenter's Shop")" which will upgrade the [trailer](/Trailer "Trailer") and open up some hidden passages. One will be located south of the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild") and will lead to the river waterfall north of [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart").

## Foraging

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/28/MountainForageLocations.png/500px-MountainForageLocations.png)](/File:MountainForageLocations.png)

Locations at the Mountain where forageable items can spawn.

[Forageable](/Foraging "Foraging") items found on the ground in the Mountain are:[[1]](#cite_note-forage_chance-1)

* In [Spring](/Spring "Spring"): ![Leek.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/5/57/Leek.png/24px-Leek.png) [Leek](/Leek "Leek") (58%), and ![Wild Horseradish.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/90/Wild_Horseradish.png/24px-Wild_Horseradish.png) [Wild Horseradish](/Wild_Horseradish "Wild Horseradish") (42%)
* In [Summer](/Summer "Summer"): ![Grape.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/c2/Grape.png/24px-Grape.png) [Grape](/Grape "Grape") (62%), and ![Spice Berry.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/c6/Spice_Berry.png/24px-Spice_Berry.png) [Spice Berry](/Spice_Berry "Spice Berry") (38%)
* In [Fall](/Fall "Fall"): ![Hazelnut.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/31/Hazelnut.png/24px-Hazelnut.png) [Hazelnut](/Hazelnut "Hazelnut") (53%), ![Common Mushroom.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2e/Common_Mushroom.png/24px-Common_Mushroom.png) [Common Mushroom](/Common_Mushroom "Common Mushroom") (24%), and ![Wild Plum.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3b/Wild_Plum.png/24px-Wild_Plum.png) [Wild Plum](/Wild_Plum "Wild Plum") (24%)
* In [Winter](/Winter "Winter"): ![Crocus.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2f/Crocus.png/24px-Crocus.png) [Crocus](/Crocus "Crocus") (40%), ![Crystal Fruit.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/16/Crystal_Fruit.png/24px-Crystal_Fruit.png) [Crystal Fruit](/Crystal_Fruit "Crystal Fruit") (38%), and ![Holly.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/b8/Holly.png/24px-Holly.png) [Holly](/Holly "Holly") (22%)

Forageable items spawn at an average rate of 0.5 per night.[[2]](#cite_note-forage_spawn-2) The map shows the possible locations as red and magenta tiles; when the original trees are present, the magenta tiles have a 90% smaller chance of spawning items because they are identified as being behind the original trees.

## Fishing

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2e/MountainLakeFishingZones.png/400px-MountainLakeFishingZones.png)](/File:MountainLakeFishingZones.png)

[Distances from land](/Fishing#Distance_from_Land "Fishing") in the Mountain Lake.

A large lake containing many types of [fish](/Fish "Fish") fills the middle of the Mountain map. The [Carp](/Carp "Carp") found here are particularly easy to catch, making the lake a good location to practice [fishing](/Fishing "Fishing") early in the game. A [Legendary Fish](/Fish#Legendary_Fish "Fish"), the [Legend](/Legend "Legend"), is also found here, as well as the [Legend II](/Legend_II "Legend II"), one of the five [legendary fish II](/Fish#Legendary_Fish_II "Fish").
