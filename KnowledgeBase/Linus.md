## Relationships

Linus generally considers himself an outcast of the town who feels unwelcome and misunderstood, but wishes to be accepted. He does not trust strangers nor seem to socialize with many [villagers](/Villagers "Villagers"). He tells [the Player](/The_Player "The Player") of incidents in which his [tent](/Tent "Tent") was vandalized or destroyed. During [festivals](/Festivals "Festivals"), he is usually alone and away from the main festivities.

Linus and the [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard") stand next to each other at the [Spirit's Eve](/Spirit%27s_Eve "Spirit's Eve") festival.

[Robin](/Robin "Robin") mentions that she does not mind Linus living behind their house as long as he does not bother them.

In [Winter](/Winter "Winter"), [Penny](/Penny "Penny") asks the player if they know Linus and expresses sympathy for him because "everyone ignores him". [Emily](/Emily "Emily") expresses sympathy for Linus on Tuesdays in [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), saying, "I wish everyone could learn to be accepting of others."

During [Leo](/Leo "Leo")'s six heart event, Linus, [Willy](/Willy "Willy"), and [the Player](/The_Player "The Player") succeed in inviting [Leo](/Leo "Leo") to live on the mainland, where he becomes neighbors with Linus. Linus and Leo bond over their love of nature, with Linus teaching him everything he knows and Leo occasionally referring to him as "Uncle Linus". They stand together at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star").

### Zero Hearts

![Zero Hearts.png](/mediawiki/images/4/42/Zero_Hearts.png)

After you have 50 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points with Linus, enter the town between 8pm - 12am on a day that's not raining. (This event cannot trigger before [Spring](/Spring "Spring") 7, Year 1)

| **Details** |
| --- |
| [George](/George "George") will ask you to scare off some raccoons who are stealing from his trash cans. Instead, you discover Linus, who is extremely embarrassed, but explains that if he didn't eat the food, it would go to waste. You then get the option to tell him what you think about his actions. Note that none of the choices will affect friendship with Linus. After you leave, he proceeds to try to steal from the Saloon's trash can, where he is caught by [Gus](/Gus "Gus"), who gives him a basket of zucchini fritters saying that he doesn't want any villager to go hungry. |

![Eight Hearts.png](/mediawiki/images/3/39/Eight_Hearts.png)

## Quests

* ["Blackberry Basket"](/Quests#Blackberry_Basket "Quests"): Linus sends the player a [letter](/Letters "Letters") on [Fall](/Fall "Fall") 8 that says he lost his blackberry basket. The reward for returning it to him is one heart of [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") (250 friendship points).
* Linus may randomly request an item at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). The reward is 3x the item's base value and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* ["Community Cleanup"](/Quests#Community_Cleanup "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), Linus may randomly request that the player collect 20 [trash](/Trash "Trash") items and place them in the bin at the [train platform](/Railroad "Railroad"). The reward is data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g, 250 [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points, and the [Fiber Seeds](/Fiber_Seeds "Fiber Seeds") recipe.

## Trivia

* The [campfire](/Campfire "Campfire") in front of Linus' tent can be toggled on or off by the player. This seems to have no impact on the game, such as special dialogues or an effect on friendship. Linus does not re-light or put out the fire.
* If Linus sends the player a [Catfish](/Catfish "Catfish") as a gift in the mail, he will say the mountain lake has been kind to him. However, Catfish cannot be caught in the mountain lake.
* If the [Treasure Chest](/Treasure_Chest "Treasure Chest") is gifted to Linus, he will not accept the gift and will give it back to the player.
* If Linus sees the player digging through a garbage can, instead of losing 25 friendship points with him, the player will gain 5 friendship points with him. However, the only times Linus is near a trash can is during the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") and while walking to the [Night Market](/Night_Market "Night Market") on Winter 15.
