The **Wizard's Tower** is the home of the [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard"). It is located at the western edge of [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") and is open from 6am-11pm.

The door to the Wizard's Tower is locked until the day the player receives in the mail an invitation from the Wizard to visit him there. This day is the first non-holiday after the player enters the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") and reads the golden scroll in the Crafts Room, or else purchases a [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart") Membership. The visit unlocks the player's ability to read the [Junimo](/Junimos "Junimos") language, which in turn enables bundles to be donated to the Community Center.

## Basement Room

After reaching [four hearts](/Friendship "Friendship") with the [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard"), players will gain access to a basement where they can change their name, gender, favorite thing, and the appearance they set at character creation. [The Shrine of Illusions](/The_Shrine_of_Illusions "The Shrine of Illusions") requires an offering of data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g each time it is used.

In late game, after players complete the quest [Goblin Problem](/Quests#Goblin_Problem "Quests") to gain passage to the [Witch's Hut](/Witch%27s_Hut "Witch's Hut") a teleportation rune will activate in the basement, allowing free passage between the basement room and the Witch's Hut anytime.

After obtaining the [Far Away Stone](/Far_Away_Stone "Far Away Stone") the player can use it on the stone in the corner to obtain the [Meowmere](/Meowmere "Meowmere").

## Buildings

:   *For the traditional farm Buildings, see [Farm Buildings](/Carpenter%27s_Shop#Farm_Buildings "Carpenter's Shop").*

After retrieving the Wizard's [Magic Ink](/Magic_Ink "Magic Ink"), he'll make some unique buildings available for sale. After unlocking [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") as well, the [Island Obelisk](/Island_Obelisk "Island Obelisk") will become available. These buildings require no build time, but are very expensive.

To summon buildings, interact with the magic book inside the tower. The construction menu opened by the book functions the same as that in the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop"), allowing the player to move, paint, or demolish farm buildings.
