The **Red Mullet** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught in the ocean at [The Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") or on the [Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps#Beach "Farm Maps") during [Summer](/Summer "Summer") and [Winter](/Winter "Winter"). It may also randomly appear at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="75"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)225–1,000g or in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during Summer or Winter.

Red Mullet can be caught regardless of time of day or season using [Magic Bait](/Magic_Bait "Magic Bait").

## Tailoring

Red Mullet can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Sailor Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt259a.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/36/Shirt259a.png/24px-Shirt259a.png)](/File:Shirt259a.png) It can also be used as a red dye in the dye pots, located in [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s and [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Red Mullet can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 2 days. The initial pond capacity is 3 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing three quests. The only possible output is red-colored Red Mullet [Roe](/Roe "Roe").

## Quests

* **Item Delivery:** One red mullet may be randomly requested in [Summer](/Summer "Summer") or [Winter](/Winter "Winter") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="225">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)225g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* **Fishing:** [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius") or [Willy](/Willy "Willy") may randomly request 1-4 red mullets during [Summer](/Summer "Summer") or [Winter](/Winter "Winter") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="75">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)75g per fish. (You get to keep the fish).
* **Aquatic Overpopulation:** [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius") may request 10 Red Mullet to be caught in [Summer](/Summer "Summer") at the [Special Orders board](/Quests#List_of_Special_Orders "Quests") outside the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor") for a reward of gold equivalent to selling the fish and the [Farm Computer](/Farm_Computer "Farm Computer") recipe.
