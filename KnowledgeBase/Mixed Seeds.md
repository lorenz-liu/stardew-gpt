**Mixed Seeds** are a random type of seed capable of producing a specific set of crops (listed below). The crop that will grow from a Mixed Seed is decided when it is planted. Mixed Seeds collected in any season always grow to become crops of the season in which they are planted, including those planted in the [Greenhouse](/Greenhouse "Greenhouse") and indoors via the [Garden Pot](/Garden_Pot "Garden Pot"). Mixed Seeds cannot be planted in winter except in the Greenhouse and indoors in garden pots, where they become one of the random crops from any season. Mixed Seeds planted on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") are always the same no matter the season.

## Sources

* Harvesting [Weeds](/Weeds "Weeds") with a [Scythe](/Scythe "Scythe"), [Axe](/Axes "Axes"), [Pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes"), [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes"), [Sword](/Weapons#Sword "Weapons"), or [bomb](/Crafting#Bombs "Crafting") may drop mixed seeds if no [Fiber](/Fiber "Fiber") drops (9% chance if [Raccoon Journal](/Raccoon_Journal "Raccoon Journal") has been read, otherwise 5%).[[1]](#cite_note-weedchance-1)
* [Tilling](/Hoes "Hoes") dirt or sand.
* [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing").
* Sold by [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") on [Thursdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Thursday "Days of the Week") for data-sort-value="30">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)30g each.
* 1/5 chance of being sold at the shop at the [Stardew Valley Fair](/Stardew_Valley_Fair "Stardew Valley Fair") for data-sort-value="1000">![Token.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/29/Token.png/24px-Token.png)1,000 for 24 seeds.
* [Wilderness Golems](/Wilderness_Golem "Wilderness Golem") and [Iridium Golems](/Iridium_Golem "Iridium Golem") on the [Wilderness Farm map](/Farm_Maps#Wildness_Farm "Farm Maps") have a 50% chance of dropping Mixed Seeds, and a 20% chance (after reaching [Combat](/Combat "Combat") Level 5) for a second one when slain.
* [Crayfish](/Crayfish "Crayfish"), [Periwinkle](/Periwinkle "Periwinkle"), or [Snail](/Snail "Snail") [Fish Ponds](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") may produce 2-3 Mixed Seeds when the population of the pond reaches 5.
* Whenever the [Seed Maker](/Seed_Maker "Seed Maker") is used with any crop, there is a 1.99% chance you will get 1-4 Mixed Seeds instead of the crop's seeds.
* 3-5 Mixed Seeds may occasionally be found in [Supply Crates](/Supply_Crate "Supply Crate") on the [Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps#Beach "Farm Maps").
* 10 Mixed Seeds may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box").
* Sold by the [Calico Egg Merchant](/Desert_Festival#Calico_Egg_Merchant "Desert Festival"), [Evelyn](/Evelyn "Evelyn"), and [Jodi](/Jodi "Jodi") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival").
* Sold by the [Raccoon's wife](/Giant_Stump#Raccoon_Wife.27s_Shop "Giant Stump") after completing the fourth request.
* 15 from the prize machine in the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor") (50% chance to be the 3rd prize).

During Spring, on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"), [Parsnip Seeds](/Parsnip_Seeds "Parsnip Seeds") are twice as likely to be chosen as Cauliflower or Potato Seeds.[[2]](#cite_note-mixedseeds-2)

## Quests

Three or five Mixed Seeds may be requested by several fish in [Fish Pond quests](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond").

## Trading

Ten Mixed Seeds can be traded to the [Raccoon's wife](/Giant_Stump#Raccoon_Wife.27s_Shop "Giant Stump") for 1 [Raccoon Hat](/Raccoon_Hat "Raccoon Hat"), after the [third request](/Giant_Stump#Raccoon.27s_Requests "Giant Stump") from the Raccoon family is completed.
