Emily is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). She's one of the twelve characters available to [marry](/Marriage "Marriage"). Her home is south of the town square, right next to [Jodi](/Jodi "Jodi")'s house, at the address [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). She works most evenings at [The Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon") starting at about 4:00 PM.

Emily loves to make her own clothing, but fabric can be difficult to come by in town. Among her favorite [gifts](/Friendship "Friendship") are [cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") and [wool](/Wool "Wool").

After the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island is unlocked, Emily may randomly spend the day there. After leaving the Island at 6pm, Emily will immediately go home to bed. Emily never visits the Resort on [Fall](/Fall "Fall") 15, [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days, or her checkup day at [Harvey's Clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic").

## Relationships

Emily lives with her sister [Haley](/Haley "Haley"), and together they care for their [parents' home](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"), who have been traveling the world for the past two years. She works with [Gus](/Gus "Gus"), who employs her part-time at [the saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon").

She is also friends with [Sandy](/Sandy "Sandy"), the two mention each other in dialogues, and she visits [The Calico Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") on Sandy's birthday each year. [Sandy](/Sandy "Sandy"), at her first meeting with the player, claims to have been aware of the farmer's presence even before access to [The Calico Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") was opened, saying that Emily had written to her about the newcomer.

Several dialogues and cutscenes reveal that [Clint](/Clint "Clint") has romantic interest in Emily, though he is too shy to tell her.

She will dance with [Shane](/Shane "Shane") at the [Flower Dance](/Flower_Dance "Flower Dance") if neither of them are dancing with the player.

## Marriage

:   *Main article: [Marriage](/Marriage "Marriage")*

Once married, Emily will move into the [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse"). Like other marriage candidates, she will add her own room to the right of the bedroom. She'll also set up a small crystal garden behind the farmhouse where she'll sometimes go to meditate. Note that Emily's parrot will not be visible to anyone in [multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer") who has not completed her 4 heart event.

On rainy mornings, Emily may offer you [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth"), [Wool](/Wool "Wool"), [Duck Feather](/Duck_Feather "Duck Feather"), [Refined Quartz](/Refined_Quartz "Refined Quartz"), or [Bean Hotpot](/Bean_Hotpot "Bean Hotpot"). On rainy nights, she may offer you dessert that is "raw", "gluten-free", and "sweetened only with cactus syrup": [Rice Pudding](/Rice_Pudding "Rice Pudding"), [Blueberry Tart](/Blueberry_Tart "Blueberry Tart"), [Cookie](/Cookie "Cookie"), or [Chocolate Cake](/Chocolate_Cake "Chocolate Cake"). On days when Emily stays inside the farmhouse all day, she may offer you [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth"), [Wool](/Wool "Wool"), [Duck Feather](/Duck_Feather "Duck Feather"), [Refined Quartz](/Refined_Quartz "Refined Quartz"), [Bean Hotpot](/Bean_Hotpot "Bean Hotpot"), [Coffee](/Coffee "Coffee"), or an [Omni Geode](/Omni_Geode "Omni Geode").

## Quests

* ["Fresh Fruit"](/Quests#Fresh_Fruit "Quests"): Emily will send you a letter on the 6th of [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), year 2, requesting an [Apricot](/Apricot "Apricot"). The reward is data-sort-value="600">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)600g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* Emily may randomly request an item at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). The reward is 3x the item's base value and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* Although not requested by Emily, fulfilling the quest ["Clint's Attempt"](/Quests#Clint.27s_Attempt "Quests") will reward you with 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart with Emily. Note that fulfilling this quest does not count against Emily's 2 gift per week limit.
* ["Rock Rejuvenation"](/Quests#Rock_Rejuvenation "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), Emily may randomly request that the player deliver to her 1 each of [Ruby](/Ruby "Ruby"), [Topaz](/Topaz "Topaz"), [Emerald](/Emerald "Emerald"), [Jade](/Jade "Jade"), and [Amethyst](/Amethyst "Amethyst"). The reward is data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g, 250 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points, and a [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine"). The player must have access to the Sewing Machine in Emily's house for this quest to appear.
* ["Saying 'Hello'"](/Quests#Types "Quests"): A rare quest that appears on the "Help Wanted" board after completing the ["Introductions"](/Quests#Introductions "Quests") quest. Emily will ask you to say 'Hi' to everyone in town. (This excludes the [Dwarf](/Dwarf "Dwarf"), [Kent](/Kent "Kent"), [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus"), [Leo](/Leo "Leo"), [Sandy](/Sandy "Sandy"), and the [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard").) The reward is 100 [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points with everyone.
