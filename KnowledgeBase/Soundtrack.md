# Soundtrack

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/75/Soundtrack_Album_Cover.png/300px-Soundtrack_Album_Cover.png)](/File:Soundtrack_Album_Cover.png)

Album cover for CD soundtrack. Artwork by [Kari Fry](https://www.fangamer.com/collections/kari-fry)

The **Stardew Valley Official Soundtrack (OST)** is composed by [ConcernedApe](/ConcernedApe "ConcernedApe"). It's available on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/440820/Stardew_Valley_Soundtrack/) in select languages and via the [official merchandise shop](https://www.stardewvalley.net/shop/). It's also available through multiple digital music platforms courtesy of ConcernedApe.

The original soundtrack included 70 songs. Songs added to the soundtrack since then:

* Four songs from v1.1 in November 2017
* Three songs from v1.3 in August 2018
* Fourteen songs from v1.4 in November 2019
* Nine songs from v1.5 in December 2020

The player can use a [Mini-Jukebox](/Mini-Jukebox "Mini-Jukebox") on [the farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"), any interior of a [building](/Category:Farm_buildings "Category:Farm buildings"), or [the cave](/The_Cave "The Cave") to continuously play the music of their choice. Only tracks that have been heard before can be selected.

## Triggering Conditions

In addition to the Weather, Season and Time conditions mentionned in the table above, the soundtrack triggering rules are as follows.

* **Three seasonal tracks** exist for each season. Only one can be played on a given day. The track will not loop when finished, but if no music is playing, the track will trigger when entering any seasonal-track area (except for Town, see below, and given the weather and time conditions are met). The same conditions apply to the Ginger Island track.

* **Some locations are silent**, always or on given conditions (cutscenes and ambient sounds apart):
  + The following location are always silent: [Basement Room of the Wizard](/Wizard%27s_Tower#Basement_Room "Wizard's Tower"), [Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach"), [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith"), [Casino](/Casino "Casino"), [Elliott's Cabin](/Elliott%27s_Cabin "Elliott's Cabin"), [Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop") and its [back room](/Fish_Shop#Willy.27s_Boat "Fish Shop"), [Jungle](/Ginger_Island#Island_East "Ginger Island"), [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart"), [Mutant Bug Lair](/Mutant_Bug_Lair "Mutant Bug Lair"), [Pirate Cove](/Ginger_Island#Pirate_Cove "Ginger Island") (when empty), [Qi's Walnut Room](/Qi%27s_Walnut_Room "Qi's Walnut Room"), [Quarry Mine](/Quarry_Mine "Quarry Mine"), [Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers"), [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") entrance, [Spa](/Spa "Spa"), [Tunnel](/The_Tunnel "The Tunnel"), [Witch's Hut](/Witch%27s_Hut "Witch's Hut"), [Witch's Swamp](/Witch%27s_Swamp "Witch's Swamp")
  + The [Railroad](/Railroad "Railroad") is silent except during Winter.
  + Outside areas and most stores are silent during ![Rain.png](/mediawiki/images/1/1d/Rain.png) [Rain](/Weather "Weather") and ![Thunderstorm.png](/mediawiki/images/7/7a/Thunderstorm.png) [Storm](/Weather "Weather") days.
  + If a non-seasonal-track is playing, and the player enters a seasonal-track area ([Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") apart), there will be silence (*e.g.,* entering the [Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") from [Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") when the Town theme is playing, exiting the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild"), the [Secret Woods](/Secret_Woods "Secret Woods"), the [Wizard's Tower](/Wizard%27s_Tower "Wizard's Tower")...). Exiting [Treehouse](/Treehouse "Treehouse") makes exception to the rule: the track playing before the entering plays.

* **In [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town")**, music triggering works as follows:
  + If the seasonal track is playing, it continues playing when entering town.
  + If no track is playing and the player enters [Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") from the [Bus Stop](/Bus_Stop "Bus Stop"), the [Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") or [the Mountain](/The_Mountain "The Mountain"), the seasonal track will be triggered if it is before 12 PM. Otherwise, the Town theme will be triggered (given other weather, season and time conditions are met).
  + If the player enters [Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") from [the Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach"), [the Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers"), or by exiting the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith"), the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center"), [Harvey's Clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic"), [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart"), the [Movie Theater](/Movie_Theater "Movie Theater") or the [Museum](/Museum "Museum"), the Town theme will be triggered (given the weather, season and time conditions are met). It will also be triggered when exiting any town building if no music was previously playing.
  + The Town theme auto-loops while staying in town or one of its buildings (even after 6 PM, but the track will then abruptly stop at 7 PM if outside and at 8 PM if in a building).

* **The track will not change**, nor will be silenced, entering the following locations:
  + Any interior on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") ([buildings](/Category:Farm_buildings "Category:Farm buildings") and the [cave](/The_Cave "The Cave")), [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") entrance, the [Tent](/Tent "Tent") and most [Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") houses ([1 River Road](/1_River_Road "1 River Road"), [1 Willow Lane](/1_Willow_Lane "1 Willow Lane"), [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"), [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"), [Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon") before 5 PM, [Trailer](/Trailer "Trailer"))
  + [Marnie's Ranch](/Marnie%27s_Ranch "Marnie's Ranch") and [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop"). In those, *Country Shop* triggers on entering only if no music was previously playing.
  + [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") on entering, but the Town theme will play after exiting.
  + The [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon") entrance, if the previous track is one of the three volcano tracks.

* In **[The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines")** and the **[Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern")**, music triggering is dependent of mine level and time since music last played. On floor 2, music will always begin playing. On floors 1, 11, 40, 80, and 120, the game music will stop playing. On floors 31-39, 71-79, 111-119, and floors that are divisible by 5, the game music will continue playing but no new tracks will be played. On all remaining floors, there is a 50% chance to play a new song if it has been at least 2.5 minutes since the last song in the Mines began. Taking the elevator to a different section of the Mines will also cause the game music to stop playing.[[9]](#cite_note-mines_code_reference-9)
