**Shadow Shamans (dangerous)** are an [enemy](/Monsters "Monsters") found in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") after activating the [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") or during the [Danger In The Deep](/Quests#Danger_In_The_Deep "Quests") quest. 150 void spirits (any type) need to be killed for the [Monster Eradication Goal](/Adventurer%27s_Guild#Monster_Eradication_Goals "Adventurer's Guild") at the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild").

## Behavior

Dangerous Shadow Shamans are users of magic and behave identically to their weaker counterpart, the [Shadow Shaman](/Shadow_Shaman "Shadow Shaman"). They have a healing spell that heals the shaman itself and all enemies in the immediate area for 250 health. They also throw a green fireball that gives the player the ![Jinxed.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/5/5d/Jinxed.png/24px-Jinxed.png) [Jinxed](/Buffs#Jinxed "Buffs") debuff when hit (Jinxed debuff reduces the player's [Defense](/Defense "Defense") by 8). A ring of leaves appears around the Shadow Shaman when it is about to cast a spell.

## Strategy

Kill them first in combat, to prevent them from healing others. If you are hit by a curse spell, it is advisable to retreat before fighting other enemies.
