The **Training Rod** is a special [tool](/Tools "Tools") used to catch [fish](/Fish "Fish"). It can be purchased for data-sort-value="25">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)25g from [Willy's Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop").

Alternatively, on or after [Spring](/Spring "Spring") 28 Year 1, if the player has been given the [Bamboo Pole](/Bamboo_Pole "Bamboo Pole") but has not reached [Fishing level 1](/Skills#Fishing "Skills"), entering the farm before 11:30 am on a sunny day will cause Willy to visit and give the player a Training Rod and a fishing lesson which gives them Fishing level 1.

This rod is useful if the player has difficulty with the fishing minigame, as it sets the player's fishing skill equal to level 5 for the purposes of determining how big to make the green bar (136 pixels) if they have not reached level 5 yet. It cannot have [bait](/Bait "Bait") or [tackle](/Tackle "Tackle") attached to it. However, using the Training Rod causes the progress bar to decrease 33% slower compared to other rods, having the same effect as the [Trap Bobber](/Trap_Bobber "Trap Bobber").[[1]](#cite_note-tbobber-1)

The Training Rod can catch only basic fish (fish whose difficulty is lower than 50), and all fish caught will be of normal quality. However, fish caught with the Training Rod grant the same XP as with any other fishing rod, and count towards [Achievements](/Achievements "Achievements") and the Fish [Collection](/Collections "Collections"). [Treasure chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") can also be caught with this rod. The spawn rate for all eligible fish also increases by 10%.[[2]](#cite_note-spawnrate-2)

## Fish

The Training Rod can catch the following fish:

[Anchovy](/Anchovy "Anchovy"), [Bream](/Bream "Bream"), [Bullhead](/Bullhead "Bullhead"), [Carp](/Carp "Carp"), [Chub](/Chub "Chub"), [Herring](/Herring "Herring"), [Perch](/Perch "Perch"), [Rainbow Trout](/Rainbow_Trout "Rainbow Trout"), [Red Snapper](/Red_Snapper "Red Snapper"), [Sardine](/Sardine "Sardine"), [Sea Cucumber](/Sea_Cucumber "Sea Cucumber"), [Shad](/Shad "Shad"), [Smallmouth Bass](/Smallmouth_Bass "Smallmouth Bass"), [Sunfish](/Sunfish "Sunfish"), [Walleye](/Walleye "Walleye")

Like with other rods, the Training Rod can also catch [Trash](/Trash "Trash"), [Green Algae](/Green_Algae "Green Algae"), [White Algae](/White_Algae "White Algae"), [Seaweed](/Seaweed "Seaweed"), [Sea Jelly](/Sea_Jelly "Sea Jelly"), and [River Jelly](/River_Jelly "River Jelly").

## Notes

* Using the Training Rod in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") will only yield [Trash](/Trash "Trash"), because all normal fishing catches, including [Green Algae](/Green_Algae "Green Algae"), [White Algae](/White_Algae "White Algae"), and [Cave Jelly](/Cave_Jelly "Cave Jelly"), are excluded from the drop pool.[[3]](#cite_note-mineshaft-3)
