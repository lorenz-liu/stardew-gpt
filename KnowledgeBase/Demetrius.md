**Demetrius** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who resides at [24 Mountain Road](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop"), on [The Mountain](/The_Mountain "The Mountain"), with his wife [Robin](/Robin "Robin"), daughter [Maru](/Maru "Maru"), and step-son [Sebastian](/Sebastian "Sebastian").

Demetrius is a scientist who studies the valley's local wildlife. He can often be found working in his laboratory or outdoors taking notes.

## Schedule

After the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island is unlocked, Demetrius may randomly spend the day there. After leaving the Island at 6pm, Demetrius will immediately go home to bed. Demetrius never visits the Resort on [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days or his checkup day at [Harvey's Clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic").

## Relationships

Demetrius is married to [Robin](/Robin "Robin") and lives with his daughter [Maru](/Maru "Maru") and step-son [Sebastian](/Sebastian "Sebastian"). Maru is his daughter with Robin, and Sebastian is Robin's son from an earlier relationship. It is assumed that Demetrius is Robin's second husband, because there are 2 books in the bookcase of their room titled "Maximizing Your Second Marriage" and "Practical Tips For First-Time Step-Dads." Demetrius is very protective of Maru, as shown in her two heart event.

## Events

When your total earnings reach data-sort-value="25000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)25,000g Demetrius will come by your farm in the morning and tell you about some research he needs help with. He will then volunteer to improve [The Cave](/The_Cave "The Cave") to either attract Fruit Bats or to produce Mushrooms.

During the Summer, Demetrius will send you a letter informing you about the [Dance of the Moonlight Jellies](/Dance_of_the_Moonlight_Jellies "Dance of the Moonlight Jellies").

## Quests

* ["Crop Research"](/Quests#Crop_Research "Quests"): Demetrius will send you a letter on the 20th of [Summer](/Summer "Summer") requesting that you bring him a [Melon](/Melon "Melon"). The reward is data-sort-value="550">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)550g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* ["Aquatic Research"](/Quests#Aquatic_Research "Quests"): Demetrius will send you a letter on the 6th of [Summer](/Summer "Summer") in year 2 requesting that you bring him a [Pufferfish](/Pufferfish "Pufferfish"). The reward is data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* Demetrius may request that you slay a certain number of [Monsters](/Monsters "Monsters") or bring him a random item at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). The reward for slaying monsters varies by the number/type of monster, but is always an amount of gold. The reward for an item delivery quest is 3x the item's base value and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* ["Aquatic Overpopulation"](/Quests#Aquatic_Overpopulation "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), Demetrius may randomly request that the player catch 10 season-specific fish. The reward is [gold](/Gold "Gold") equivalent to selling the fish and the [Farm Computer](/Farm_Computer "Farm Computer") recipe.
* ["Biome Balance"](/Quests#Biome_Balance "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), Demetrius may randomly request that the player catch 20 [River](/Category:River_fish "Category:River fish"), [Ocean](/Category:Ocean_fish "Category:Ocean fish"), or [Lake](/Category:Lake_fish "Category:Lake fish") fish. The reward is data-sort-value="1500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,500g and the [Farm Computer](/Farm_Computer "Farm Computer") recipe.

## Trivia

* If [Robin](/Robin "Robin") is constructing something on your farm on the nights she and Demetrius go to [the saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon"), he'll go and dance by himself.
* Demetrius never mentions [Sebastian](/Sebastian "Sebastian") in his dialogue outside of cutscenes.
* Despite Demetrius's six-heart event, [Tomato](/Tomato "Tomato") is considered a vegetable crop by the game.
* If the player has successfully incubated a [Dinosaur Egg](/Dinosaur_Egg "Dinosaur Egg"), speaking with Demetrius will result in him giving the player [Triple Shot Espresso](/Triple_Shot_Espresso "Triple Shot Espresso").
