The **Merpeople** are creatures that live in the [Ocean](/The_Beach "The Beach"). The [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard") refers to them in his dialogue at the [Luau](/Luau "Luau").

It's theorized that the [green creature](/Secrets#Beach_Creature "Secrets") that very rarely appears south of the docks is a merperson, but this has never been officially confirmed. The mermaids at the [Night Market](/Night_Market "Night Market") (added with [version 1.3](/Version_History#1.3.27 "Version History")) and the mermaid on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") (added with [version 1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History")) look very different from this green creature (added before both of these), indicating the green creature may be another species.
