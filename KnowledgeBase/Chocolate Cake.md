**Chocolate Cake** is a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit"). Three Chocolate Cakes are the reward for completing the ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [2,500 Bundle](/Bundles#g2500_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Vault](/Bundles#Vault "Bundles").

**Note**: this recipe is [profitable](/Cooking#Profitable_Recipes "Cooking") - it will always result in profit if using the lowest quality [Egg](/Egg "Egg") as well as [Wheat Flour](/Wheat_Flour "Wheat Flour") and [Sugar](/Sugar "Sugar") from the [Mill](/Mill "Mill").

Chocolate Cake may randomly appear in [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus")' shop on [Saturdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Saturday "Days of the Week"), in the [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") outside the saloon, or in [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon")'s rotating stock. One Chocolate Cake may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box").

## Bundles

Chocolate Cake is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Chocolate Cake is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Party Hat (blue).png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/fe/Party_Hat_%28blue%29.png/24px-Party_Hat_%28blue%29.png) [Party Hat (blue)](/Party_Hat_(blue) "Party Hat (blue)"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Chocolate Cake is needed for the quest "Haley's Cake-Walk" during her [14-Heart event](/Haley#Fourteen_Hearts "Haley").
