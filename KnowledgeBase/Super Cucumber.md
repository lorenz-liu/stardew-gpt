The **Super Cucumber** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught in the ocean at [The Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") or on the [Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps#Beach "Farm Maps") during [Summer](/Summer "Summer") and [Fall](/Fall "Fall") evenings, and on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") South, Southeast, West (ocean), and the Pirate Cove during any season. It can also randomly be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during Summer and Fall, or at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="250"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)750–1,250g. There is a 4% chance to catch a Super Cucumber during the deep-sea submarine ride at the [Night Market](/Night_Market "Night Market") during [Winter](/Winter "Winter") 15-17 (7% if using a [Curiosity Lure](/Curiosity_Lure "Curiosity Lure")). The [Statue Of Endless Fortune](/Statue_Of_Endless_Fortune "Statue Of Endless Fortune") produces one Super Cucumber per year on the [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard")'s birthday, [Winter 17](/Winter "Winter").

Super Cucumber can be caught regardless of time of day or season using [Magic Bait](/Magic_Bait "Magic Bait").

## Tailoring

Super Cucumber is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a dyeable ![Shirt142.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/65/Shirt142.png/24px-Shirt142.png) [Tube Top](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a purple dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the purple dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Fish Pond

Super Cucumbers can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 5 days. The initial pond capacity is 3 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing three quests. When the population of the pond reaches 3, the color of the water changes to purple. The primary output is purple Super Cucumber [Roe](/Roe "Roe"), but more valuable items are possible starting at population 9.

## History

* [1.3.27](/Version_History#1.3.27 "Version History"): Added chance to catch during the [Night Market](/Night_Market "Night Market").
* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Fixed bug where an entire stack of Super Cucumbers would be consumed when interacting with the brown box. Added iridium quality. Can now be used in [Tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring").
* [1.4.4](/Version_History#1.4.4 "Version History"): Fixed a mistake in the game data that prevented super cucumber-specific [fish pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") data from being applied (in earlier versions, super cucumber used the same fish pond data as all generic ocean fish).
* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): Can now be caught on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") during any season on Island West (ocean), South, Southeast, and in the Pirate Cove. Chance of catching it in the submarine can now be increased by using a [Curiosity Lure](/Curiosity_Lure "Curiosity Lure").
* [1.5.5](/Version_History#1.5.5 "Version History"): Fixed typo in [Livin' Off The Land](/Livin%27_Off_The_Land "Livin' Off The Land") [television](/Television "Television") channel incorrectly stating that the Super Cucumber comes out during [Winter](/Winter "Winter").
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Fixed bug allowing all Night Market fish to be hooked with the [Training Rod](/Training_Rod "Training Rod") during the submarine ride. Elliott now hates Super Cucumber.
