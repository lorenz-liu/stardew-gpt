# Shop Schedules

Below is a list of all **Shop Schedules**, including when they are open or closed.

Note: All buildings and homes in Stardew Valley are "locked" on all [festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days where the festival begins before 7pm (*i.e.,* all festivals other than [Spirit's Eve](/Spirit%27s_Eve "Spirit's Eve") and [Dance of the Moonlight Jellies](/Dance_of_the_Moonlight_Jellies "Dance of the Moonlight Jellies")). [The Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers"), [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center"), [Movie Theater](/Movie_Theater "Movie Theater"), and the [Spa](/Spa "Spa") will remain open. Shops and homes remain locked all day, even if the player does not attend the festival.

The [Night Market](/Night_Market "Night Market") festival is unique, in that no homes or shops are locked during festival days ([Winter](/Winter "Winter") 15-17).

Obtaining the [Key To The Town](/Key_To_The_Town "Key To The Town") expands access to shops, but (despite its description) it has some restrictions.
