# Gold

:   *Not to be confused with [Gold Ore](/Gold_Ore "Gold Ore") or [Gold Bars](/Gold_Bar "Gold Bar").*

**Gold** [![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)](/File:Gold.png) is the primary currency of [Stardew Valley](/Stardew_Valley "Stardew Valley"). The amount of gold a player has is displayed at the top right of the screen, directly below the time. All players start with data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g. "Total Earnings" and "Current Funds" can be viewed at any time on the [Inventory](/Inventory "Inventory") screen of the pause menu.

Most shops accept Gold as payment for their goods and services. Exceptions include the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader"), the [Island Trader](/Island_Trader "Island Trader"), the [Raccoon Wife's Shop](/Giant_Stump#Raccoon_Wife.27s_Shop "Giant Stump"), the booths at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") and [Stardew Valley Fair](/Stardew_Valley_Fair "Stardew Valley Fair"), the [Casino](/Casino "Casino"), and [Qi's Walnut Room](/Qi%27s_Walnut_Room "Qi's Walnut Room").

## Earning Gold

The primary method of earning gold is to collect items by [farming](/Farming "Farming"), [fishing](/Fishing "Fishing"), [foraging](/Foraging "Foraging"), [mining](/Mining "Mining"), or engaging in [combat](/Combat "Combat"), then selling the items by placing them in the [shipping box](/Shipping "Shipping") on the farm. Gold is earned after the player goes to sleep for the night, and the amounts for each item are displayed on a summary screen.

Alternatively, items can be sold to various merchants throughout Stardew Valley, and gold can be collected immediately. Each merchant will purchase only specific items that correspond to the type of items they sell: [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") buys edibles, the [Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop") buys [Fish](/Fish "Fish"), the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith") buys metal ores and gems, etc.

The player may also complete [quests](/Quests "Quests") to earn gold. Quests are posted on the "Help Wanted" board outside of Pierre's General Store, on the "Special Orders" board south of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), or delivered directly to the player's mailbox on the farm. Upon completion of a quest, the player may earn gold by opening the Journal, selecting the completed quest, and clicking on the reward icon.

The player will also receive gold in the mail. The player's [mother or father](/Mom_and_Dad "Mom and Dad") will send money after the player earns specific amounts of gold, [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre") will randomly send 'Cash-back Rewards Program' rebates, and [Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis") will randomly send gold from the 'Stardew Valley Agricultural Fund' for being a member of the community.

The player can discard unwanted items using the [Trash Can](/Trash_Cans "Trash Cans") in their inventory. Normally, an item discarded in this way does not return any gold. However, by upgrading the Trash Can at the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith"), the player can recover some of the item's value in gold.

If the player has the [Parrot Egg](/Parrot_Egg "Parrot Egg") trinket equipped, all monsters have a chance of dropping gold when slain.
