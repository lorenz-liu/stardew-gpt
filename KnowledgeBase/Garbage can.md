# Garbage Can

[![Garbage Can.png](/mediawiki/images/7/79/Garbage_Can.png)](/File:Garbage_Can.png)

:   *For the upgradeable garbage cans found in the player menu, see [Trash Cans](/Trash_Cans "Trash Cans").*

**Garbage Cans** can be found next to buildings in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). If you "use" one (right-click on it), you can sometimes find a valuable item. There are eight garbage cans in total:
[The Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon"), [1 River Road](/1_River_Road "1 River Road"), the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), [1 Willow Lane](/1_Willow_Lane "1 Willow Lane"), [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"), the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith"), the [Museum](/Museum "Museum") and [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart")/[Movie Theater](/Movie_Theater "Movie Theater").

Garbage cans have predetermined contents each day. The chance for this predetermined item to drop is affected by daily [luck](/Luck "Luck"). In addition, certain garbage cans have special drops which have a chance to replace the predetermined drop. This chance is partially affected by daily luck. The first reading of [The Alleyway Buffet](/The_Alleyway_Buffet "The Alleyway Buffet") will increase the chance to find items by 20%.[[1]](#cite_note-trashpower-1)

Garbage Cans will play an animation when interacted with. The top comes off briefly, revealing that the trash inside changes color with the [seasons](/Seasons "Seasons"). A Garbage Can's lid may randomly pop off extra high and play an extra loud sound. In addition, if a [Garbage Hat](/Garbage_Hat "Garbage Hat") is found, the can's lid will make an exploding noise, and the lid will disappear for a period of time.

[![Garbage Can Composite.png](/mediawiki/images/8/89/Garbage_Can_Composite.png)](/File:Garbage_Can_Composite.png)

Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter

If you interact with a garbage can near any [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") other than [Linus](/Linus "Linus"), you will repulse the villager and lose 25 [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points with them. The villager must be in a 15x15 square centered at your character to be offended. If Linus catches you digging through a Garbage Can, you will gain 5 friendship points with him.[[2]](#cite_note-villagerreactions-2)

[Linus](/Linus "Linus")' attitude towards digging through garbage cans is revealed in his 50-points heart event, but it rarely matters since (other than during the cutscene) he can only be seen near a Garbage Can during the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival"), and on Winter 15, walking to the [Night Market](/Night_Market "Night Market").
