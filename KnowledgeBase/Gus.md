**Gus** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives and works at [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon") in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). He owns the establishment.

## Schedule

After the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island is unlocked, Gus may randomly spend the day there. (He will leave a cash box on the bar, so the player is still able to make purchases.) After leaving the Island at 6pm, Gus will tend bar until 12:30am, when he goes to bed. Gus never visits the Resort on [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days or his checkup day at [Harvey's Clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic").

## Relationships

Gus employs [Emily](/Emily "Emily") part-time to help at the saloon. [Pam](/Pam "Pam") is also a frequent customer, and can often be seen chatting with Gus.

### Four Hearts

![Four Hearts.png](/mediawiki/images/8/80/Four_Hearts.png)

Enter [the Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon") during open hours, when Gus is inside.

Note: Only occurs if your [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") with [Pam](/Pam "Pam") is at 2 hearts or more.

| **Details** |
| --- |
| When you enter the bar, Gus will be sitting at one of the tables looking dejected. When you ask what's wrong, he'll tell you that the saloon is having hard times financially. He'll admit that at least one problem is that Pam isn't paying off her tab and he's afraid to confront her because they are friends. Pam will then enter and ask for a drink. Gus will be flustered and concede, but you will get the choice to either say:  * **You need to pay your tab right now!** *(-50 [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") with Pam.)* * **The saloon isn't doing well, financially** *(+15 [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") with Pam.)* Pam will be somewhat upset but she will pay her tab off and Gus will give her the drink she asked for.   Your relationship with Gus will not be affected either way. |

### Five Hearts

![Five Hearts.png](/mediawiki/images/7/72/Five_Hearts.png)

Exit the farmhouse on a non-rainy day between 6am and 11:30am.

| **Details** |
| --- |
| Gus appears with a pot of sauce. He draws an analogy between cooking and the player's appearance in Stardew Valley, saying that adding a new ingredient may ruin the sauce or create something new and delicious. He explains that he's come to think of the player as a good friend, and says he found an old [Mini-Jukebox](/Mini-Jukebox "Mini-Jukebox") while cleaning [the Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon"). He gives the player the jukebox, and the recipe to make more. |

## Quests

* ["Wanted: Lobster"](/Quests#Wanted_Lobster "Quests"): Gus will send you a letter on the 6th of [Fall](/Fall "Fall"), Year 2, requesting a [Lobster](/Lobster "Lobster"). The reward offered in the letter is data-sort-value="800">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)800g but he actually gives data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart upon completion.
* ["Fish Stew"](/Quests#Fish_Stew "Quests"): Gus will send you a letter on the 26th of [Winter](/Winter "Winter") requesting an [Albacore](/Albacore "Albacore"). The reward is data-sort-value="400">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)400g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* ["Exotic Spirits"](/Quests#Exotic_Spirits "Quests"): Gus will send you a letter on the 19th of [Winter](/Winter "Winter"), Year 2, requesting a [Coconut](/Coconut "Coconut"). The reward is data-sort-value="600">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)600g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* Gus may randomly request an item at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). The reward is 3x the item's base value and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* Gus is to be gifted the [Gourmet Tomato Salt](/Quests#Gourmet_Tomato_Salt "Quests") in [The Pirate's Wife](/Quests#The_Pirate.27s_Wife "Quests") quest.
* ["Gus' Famous Omelet"](/Quests#Gus.27_Famous_Omelet "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), Gus may randomly request that the player collect and deliver 24 [Eggs](/Egg "Egg") to the fridge in [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon"). The reward is data-sort-value="3000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)3,000g and a [Mini-Fridge](/Mini-Fridge "Mini-Fridge").
