The **Red Mushroom** is a type of mushroom obtainable by:

* [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") on [Mushroom Floors](/The_Mines#The_Mushroom_Floor "The Mines") (starting at floor 81).
* Found on levels 21-69 of the Mines while the [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") is enabled, or the [Danger in The Deep](/Quests#Danger_In_The_Deep "Quests") quest is active.
* In the [Secret Woods](/Secret_Woods "Secret Woods") during [Summer](/Summer "Summer") and (more rarely) [Fall](/Fall "Fall").
* In the [Forest Farm](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps") during Fall.
* Inside of the [Ginger Island mushroom cave](/Ginger_Island#Dig_Site "Ginger Island").
* Searching [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during any season.
* Collected from the mushroom bins in [the Cave](/The_Cave "The Cave") if the mushroom option was chosen.
* By [tapping](/Tapper "Tapper") a [Mushroom Tree](/Mushroom_Tree "Mushroom Tree").
* Collected from a [Mushroom Log](/Mushroom_Log "Mushroom Log").
* By cutting down a Mushroom Tree with the [Shaving](/Forge#Tool_enchantments "Forge") enchantment.
* Purchased at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="75"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)225–1,000g.

## Tailoring

Red Mushroom is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Spotted Headscarf.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a7/Spotted_Headscarf.png/24px-Spotted_Headscarf.png) [Spotted Headscarf](/Spotted_Headscarf "Spotted Headscarf"). It can also be used as a red dye color at the dye pots in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, located at [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* The Red Mushroom may be randomly requested during any season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="225">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)225g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* 2-3 Red Mushrooms may be requested by [Catfish](/Catfish "Catfish"), [Spook Fish](/Spook_Fish "Spook Fish"), or [Woodskip](/Woodskip "Woodskip") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 3 to 5.
