The **Eel** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught during [rainy](/Weather "Weather") weather in the ocean at [The Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") (or on the [Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps#Beach "Farm Maps")) during [Spring](/Spring "Spring") or [Fall](/Fall "Fall") from 4 PM to 2 AM. It can also randomly be found at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="85"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)255–1,000g or in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during Spring or Fall.

An Eel can be caught regardless of weather, time, or season using [Magic Bait](/Magic_Bait "Magic Bait").

## Tailoring

Eel can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Sailor Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt259a.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/36/Shirt259a.png/24px-Shirt259a.png)](/File:Shirt259a.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Eels can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 3 days. The initial pond capacity is 3 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing three quests. The only possible output is brown Eel [Roe](/Roe "Roe").
