The **Apricot Sapling** is a [Fruit Tree](/Fruit_Trees "Fruit Trees") sapling that takes 28 days to grow into an [Apricot Tree](/Apricot_Tree "Apricot Tree"), after which it will produce one fruit each day during the [Spring](/Spring "Spring"). Like all Fruit Trees, it will produce fruit all year round if planted in the [Greenhouse](/Greenhouse "Greenhouse").

It can be purchased from [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") and occasionally from the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart"). It may occasionally be found in [treasure rooms](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern") in the Skull Cavern. One Apricot Sapling may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") or [Golden Mystery Box](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box").
