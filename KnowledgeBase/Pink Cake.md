**Pink Cake** is a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit"). It can also be received as a gift at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star") or may randomly appear in [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus")' shop on [Saturdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Saturday "Days of the Week") or in [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon")'s rotating stock. It can also randomly be found in the [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") outside the saloon. Five Pink Cakes can be purchased from [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="20">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 20 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg"). One Pink Cake may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box"). If the player's character is male, after he earns data-sort-value="120000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)120,000g he will receive a [letter from mom](/Mom_and_Dad "Mom and Dad") with a Pink Cake enclosed.

3 Pink Cakes are the reward for completing the ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Chef's Bundle](/Bundles#Chefs_Bundle "Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board](/Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Bundles"). Pink cake is also sometimes contained within [Big Slimes](/Slimes#Big_Slimes "Slimes") (0.1% chance). Killing the big slime will result in a receiving a Pink Cake, or two if the player has a [Burglar's Ring](/Burglar%27s_Ring "Burglar's Ring") equipped.

The [Statue Of Endless Fortune](/Statue_Of_Endless_Fortune "Statue Of Endless Fortune") produces three Pink Cakes per year, on the birthdays of [Haley](/Haley "Haley"), [Jas](/Jas "Jas"), and [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent").

**Note**: this recipe is [profitable](/Cooking#Profitable_Recipes "Cooking") - it will always result in profit if using the lowest quality ingredients as well as [Wheat Flour](/Wheat_Flour "Wheat Flour") and [Sugar](/Sugar "Sugar") from the [Mill](/Mill "Mill").

## Tailoring

Pink Cake is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt051.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/32/Shirt051.png/24px-Shirt051.png)](/File:Shirt051.png) It can also be used in the dye pots to fill the red pot, found in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").
