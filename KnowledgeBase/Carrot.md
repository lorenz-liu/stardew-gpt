The **Carrot** is a [vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from [Carrot Seeds](/Carrot_Seeds "Carrot Seeds") after 3 days. It can be fed to a [Horse](/Horse "Horse") to increase its ![Speed.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/94/Speed.png/24px-Speed.png) [Speed](/Speed "Speed") (+0.4) for the rest of the day.[[1]](#cite_note-feedhorse-1)

## Bundles

Carrot may be chosen as one of the options for the ![Bundle Green.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/b3/Bundle_Green.png/24px-Bundle_Green.png) [remixed Spring Crops Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Spring_Crops_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Pantry](/Remixed_Bundles#Pantry "Remixed Bundles").

## Recipes

Carrot is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Carrot can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a dyeable ![Shirt159.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/99/Shirt159.png/24px-Shirt159.png) [Shirt And Belt](/Tailoring#Shirts "Tailoring"). It can also be used as an orange [dye](/Dyeing "Dyeing") when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the orange dye pot located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Carrot is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
