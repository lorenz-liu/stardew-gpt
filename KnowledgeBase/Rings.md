# Rings

**Rings** are equippable [clothing](/Clothing "Clothing") items that give players passive benefits. Any two rings can be equipped at any one time. They are equipped in the player's [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory"). Some ring effects stack. Rings can also be [combined](/Forge#Combined_Rings "Forge").
