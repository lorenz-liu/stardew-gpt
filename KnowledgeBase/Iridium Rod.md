The **Iridium Rod** is a [tool](/Tools "Tools") used to catch [fish](/Fish "Fish"). It can be purchased from [Willy's Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop") for data-sort-value="7500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)7,500g after reaching [Fishing level 6](/Skills#Fishing "Skills"). It can have both [bait](/Bait "Bait") and [tackle](/Tackle "Tackle") attached to it.

## Secret

| **Details** |
| --- |
| Putting [Lucky Purple Shorts](/Secrets#Lucky_Purple_Shorts "Secrets") in the tackle slot of a fishing rod changes the [bobber style](/Fish_Shop#Bobber_Machine "Fish Shop") to a unique Lucky Purple Shorts bobber. This cannot be done with Trimmed Lucky Purple Shorts. |
