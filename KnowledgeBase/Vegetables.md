# Vegetables

**Vegetable** is the category used for many of the [Crops](/Crops "Crops") grown on the farm. One [foraged](/Foraging "Foraging") item, [Fiddlehead Fern](/Fiddlehead_Fern "Fiddlehead Fern"), is also considered to be a vegetable. Other foraged items that are real-life vegetables (*e.g.,* [Leeks](/Leek "Leek"), [Spring Onions](/Spring_Onion "Spring Onion")) are categorized as [Forage](/Foraging#Foraged_Items "Foraging") instead of Vegetable and therefore are not included here.

All vegetables share several in-game characteristics.

* Their sell prices are increased by 10% if the player has the [Tiller](/Skills#Farming "Skills") profession.
* They can be placed in a [Preserves Jar](/Preserves_Jar "Preserves Jar") to make [Pickles](/Jellies_and_Pickles "Jellies and Pickles").
* They can be placed in a [Keg](/Keg "Keg"). In most cases, the product is [Juice](/Juice "Juice"), except: [Wheat](/Wheat "Wheat") produces [Beer](/Beer "Beer"), [Hops](/Hops "Hops") produce [Pale Ale](/Pale_Ale "Pale Ale") and [Tea Leaves](/Tea_Leaves "Tea Leaves") produce [Green Tea](/Green_Tea "Green Tea").
* Vegetables (other than [Hops](/Hops "Hops"), [Tea Leaves](/Tea_Leaves "Tea Leaves"), [Unmilled Rice](/Unmilled_Rice "Unmilled Rice"), and [Wheat](/Wheat "Wheat")) are a [universally liked gift](/Friendship#Universal_Likes "Friendship"); all villagers other than [Abigail](/Abigail "Abigail"), [Haley](/Haley "Haley"), [Jas](/Jas "Jas"), [Sam](/Sam "Sam"), and [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent") like them. There are various individual exceptions, however, as detailed at [Universal Likes exceptions](/Friendship#Universal_Likes_exceptions "Friendship"), and on the individual vegetable and villager pages.
* Vegetables are one category used when scoring items in the [Grange Display](/Stardew_Valley_Fair#Grange_Display "Stardew Valley Fair") at the [Stardew Valley Fair](/Stardew_Valley_Fair "Stardew Valley Fair").
* They can be sold at the [General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") (as well as in the [Shipping Bin](/Shipping "Shipping")).
* All shipped vegetables are added to the [Items Shipped](/Collections#Items_Shipped "Collections") page of the Collections tab.
