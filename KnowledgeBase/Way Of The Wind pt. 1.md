The **Way Of The Wind pt. 1** is a power [book](/Books "Books") that can be purchased from the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") for data-sort-value="15000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)15,000g.

The first reading grants the player a power that gives them a permanent +0.25 ![Speed.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/94/Speed.png/24px-Speed.png) [Speed](/Speed "Speed") buff.[[1]](#cite_note-windpower-1) Once read, it can be found in [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s Menu on the [Special Items & Powers](/Special_Items_%26_Powers "Special Items & Powers") tab. Each subsequent reading gives the player 20 [XP](/Skills "Skills") in each of the 5 skills.

After reading this book for the first time, the player is able to purchase [Way Of The Wind pt. 2](/Way_Of_The_Wind_pt._2 "Way Of The Wind pt. 2") from the Bookseller.

## Bundles

Way Of The Wind pt. 1 is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Way Of The Wind pt. 1 is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used as an iron [dye](/Dyeing "Dyeing") when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.
