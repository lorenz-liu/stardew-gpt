The **Barn** is a type of [farm building](/Carpenter%27s_Shop#Farm_Buildings "Carpenter's Shop") purchasable and upgradeable from [Robin](/Robin "Robin") at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop").

## Construction

The Barn takes three days to initially construct. During this time, Robin is unreachable for [dialogue or gifting](/Friendship "Friendship").

Upgrading the Barn requires two days. During this time, the barn can be used as normal. [Robin](/Robin "Robin") can be found inside the Barn for purposes such as [dialogue or gifting](/Friendship "Friendship").

The total cost of a Deluxe Barn, built from scratch, is data-sort-value="43000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)43,000g ![Wood.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/df/Wood.png/24px-Wood.png) [Wood](/Wood "Wood") (1350) ![Stone.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/d4/Stone.png/24px-Stone.png) [Stone](/Stone "Stone") (650); or, if all wood and stone are bought from Robin during year 1, data-sort-value="69500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)69,500g, and data-sort-value="175500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)175,500g if bought in year 2+.

## Features

The barn houses [cows](/Cow "Cow"), [goats](/Goat "Goat"), [sheep](/Sheep "Sheep"), [pigs](/Pig "Pig"), and [ostriches](/Ostrich "Ostrich"). Once you have unlocked an animal by building the barn needed to house it, that animal can be placed in any other non-upgraded barns you have built as well.

[Barn Animals](/Animals#Barn_Animals "Animals") may be stuck outside at night if the barn doors are closed. The conditions for this are not clear (see [Animals trapped outside](/Animals#Animals_Trapped_Outside "Animals")). If an animal is stuck outside the barn at night, the next day their mood will be "grumpy." Animals stuck outside are also subject to [Wild Animal Attacks](/Animals#Wild_Animal_Attacks "Animals").

The Barn can also be used in a manner similar to the [Shed](/Shed "Shed") for storing [Chests](/Chest "Chest"), [Artisan Equipment](/Crafting#Artisan_Equipment "Crafting"), [Furniture](/Furniture "Furniture"), etc.. The animals are undisturbed by the clutter, even if their paths are blocked.

## Bugs

It is possible to have 13 animals in a barn at once, if the player has a barn filled with 11 animals and an existing animal gives birth the same night as an [Ostrich Egg](/Ostrich_Egg "Ostrich Egg") hatches.
