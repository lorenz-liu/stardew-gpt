The Henchman is a goblin that guards the [Witch's Hut](/Witch%27s_Hut "Witch's Hut") inside the [Witch's Swamp](/Witch%27s_Swamp "Witch's Swamp"). Players will encounter him after travelling to the swamp, but cannot pass around him.

Discovering how to get past the Henchman is the objective of the quest [Goblin Problem](/Quests#Goblin_Problem "Quests"). A [lost book](/Lost_Books "Lost Books") at the [library](/Museum "Museum") reveals that Goblins love [Void Mayonnaise](/Void_Mayonnaise "Void Mayonnaise"), so after being given [Void Mayonnaise](/Void_Mayonnaise "Void Mayonnaise") he'll despawn from the map, allowing the player to pass and enter the [Witch's Hut](/Witch%27s_Hut "Witch's Hut"). He is not encountered again after the quest is completed.

## Quests

The questline to access the Witch's hut involves two quests, which become available after completing all of the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") or [Joja Warehouse](/Joja_Warehouse "Joja Warehouse") tasks.
