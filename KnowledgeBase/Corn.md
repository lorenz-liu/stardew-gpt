## Multi-Seasonal Crop

Corn may be planted in both [Summer](/Summer "Summer") and [Fall](/Fall "Fall"). There is no difference in its behavior in either season.

If a Corn plant (of any growth stage) is in the ground on the 28th of Summer, it will continue growing on the 1st of Fall as though nothing had changed. Fertilizer in the ground underneath a Corn plant on Summer 28 will not disappear on Fall 1.

## Tailoring

Corn is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a dyeable ![Shirt128.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/c7/Shirt128.png/24px-Shirt128.png) [Striped Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a yellow dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the yellow dye pot at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane") for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Trivia

* Normal quality Corn sells for data-sort-value="50">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)50g, but 2 packets of [Corn Seeds](/Corn_Seeds "Corn Seeds") sell for a total of data-sort-value="150">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150g.
