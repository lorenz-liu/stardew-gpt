The **Vampire Ring** is a [ring](/Rings "Rings") that can be obtained as a reward from [Gil](/Gil "Gil") at the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild") after completing the [Monster Eradication Goal](/Adventurer%27s_Guild#Monster_Eradication_Goals "Adventurer's Guild") of killing 200 [Bats](/Bats "Bats"). After that, it can be purchased from Marlon for data-sort-value="15000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)15,000g.

The Vampire Ring restores 2 [health](/Health "Health") after slaying a monster.[[1]](#cite_note-effect-1)

Effects from multiple Vampire Rings stack. When equipped with multiple Vampire Rings, each will provide 2 points of health restoration for every monster slain.[[2]](#cite_note-stacking-2)

## Dyeing

The Vampire Ring can be used as a red dye in the dye pots, located at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").
