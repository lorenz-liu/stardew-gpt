The **Morel** is a mushroom found in the [Spring](/Spring "Spring") via [foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") in the [Secret Woods](/Secret_Woods "Secret Woods") or on the [Forest Farm](/Farm_Maps#Forest "Farm Maps"). It can also be found in [the Farm Cave](/The_Cave "The Cave") after choosing the mushroom option, or produced by a [Mushroom Log](/Mushroom_Log "Mushroom Log"). One Morel may be purchased from [George](/George "George")'s [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="30">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 30 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg").

## Tailoring

The Morel is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [![Shirt257.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/cb/Shirt257.png/24px-Shirt257.png)](/File:Shirt257.png) [Morel Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

The Morel is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
