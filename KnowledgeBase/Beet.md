The **Beet** is a [vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from [Beet Seeds](/Beet_Seeds "Beet Seeds") after 6 days. Beet Seeds can be purchased from the [Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis").

Players have the ability to craft [Sugar](/Sugar "Sugar") from Beets by using a [Mill](/Mill "Mill"). Once placed in the mill, one Beet will make three sugar. Sugar will appear in the box on the right side of the mill the following morning.

## Tailoring

Beet can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Button Down Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt140.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/f9/Shirt140.png/24px-Shirt140.png)](/File:Shirt140.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a purple dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* 10 Beets are needed for the 2nd part of ["The Mysterious Qi" Quest](/Quests#The_Mysterious_Qi "Quests").
