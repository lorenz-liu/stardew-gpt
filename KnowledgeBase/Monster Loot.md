# Monster Loot

**Monster Loot** is dropped from slain [Monsters](/Monsters "Monsters") in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") and [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"). It can be sold at the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild") or by depositing it in the [Shipping](/Shipping "Shipping") bins. Monster Loot may also be [gifted](/Friendship#Gifts "Friendship") (as an universal disliked or hated gift) or used for [Crafting](/Crafting "Crafting").

[Monsters](/Monsters "Monsters") may drop many other items when slain, but the following items are classified as "Monster Loot" in-game.
