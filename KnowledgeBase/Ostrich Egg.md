**Ostrich Eggs** are [animal products](/Category:Animal_Products "Category:Animal Products") obtained from [Ostriches](/Ostrich "Ostrich") or by solving [Journal Scrap #10](/Journal_Scraps#Journal_Scrap_.2310 "Journal Scraps") on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island"). It can also be found in [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon") rare chests.

An Ostrich Egg can be placed in a [Mayonnaise Machine](/Mayonnaise_Machine "Mayonnaise Machine") to produce 10 [Mayonnaise](/Mayonnaise "Mayonnaise"). The quality of the Mayonnaise will be equal to the quality of the Ostrich Egg used.

Putting an Ostrich Egg into an [Ostrich Incubator](/Ostrich_Incubator "Ostrich Incubator") in a [Barn](/Barn "Barn") will hatch an Ostrich in ![Time Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Time_Icon.png/24px-Time_Icon.png) 15,000m (9.5d). This is cut to ![Time Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Time_Icon.png/24px-Time_Icon.png) 7,500m (4.75d) with the [Coopmaster Profession](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming").

## Tailoring

Ostrich Egg can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Warrior Helmet.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/68/Warrior_Helmet.png/24px-Warrior_Helmet.png) [Warrior Helmet](/Warrior_Helmet "Warrior Helmet"). It can be used as a white [dye](/Dyeing "Dyeing") when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

* ["Gus' Famous Omelet"](/Quests#Gus.27_Famous_Omelet "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), [Gus](/Gus "Gus") may randomly request that the player collect and deliver 24 Eggs (of any type) to the fridge in [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon"). The reward is data-sort-value="3000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)3,000g and a [Mini-Fridge](/Mini-Fridge "Mini-Fridge").
* Five Eggs (any type, excluding Dinosaur) may be requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his third request, or as part of any request starting with the sixth.
