**Fire Quartz** is a [Mineral](/Minerals "Minerals"). It can be foraged in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") (floors 80+), in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), or found inside a [Magma Geode](/Magma_Geode "Magma Geode") or [Omni Geode](/Omni_Geode "Omni Geode"). It can also be found by [panning](/Pans "Pans") or inside [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing"). It may occasionally be found in a [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") after the player reaches floor 80 of the mines. It can also be gifted randomly by the [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard") in the mail, once the player has met him. One Fire Quartz can sometimes be purchased for data-sort-value="2500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,500g from [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre")'s booth at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star").

Fire Quartz can be destroyed by [bombs](/Crafting#Bombs "Crafting").

Fire Quartz can be used along with 1 [coal](/Coal "Coal") in a [furnace](/Furnace "Furnace") to produce 3 [Refined Quartz](/Refined_Quartz "Refined Quartz"), taking 1.5 in-game hours to smelt.

## Smelting

To smelt Fire Quartz, equip it and activate the [furnace](/Furnace "Furnace") while there is [Coal](/Coal "Coal") in inventory. One unit of Coal is required for each smelting operation, regardless of the material or duration.

## Tailoring

Fire Quartz is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [![Shirt057.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/39/Shirt057.png/24px-Shirt057.png)](/File:Shirt057.png) [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can also be used as a red dye color at the dye pots in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, located at [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* Fire Quartz may be randomly requested during any season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="300">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)300g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points. Fire Quartz will never be requested until you have reached level 80+ of [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines").
* Three Fire Quartz are requested by [Lava Eel](/Lava_Eel "Lava Eel") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 1 to 3.
