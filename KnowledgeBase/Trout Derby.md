# Trout Derby

[![Trout Derby.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/1d/Trout_Derby.png/500px-Trout_Derby.png)](/File:Trout_Derby.png)

The **Trout Derby** is a [fishing](/Fishing "Fishing") mini [festival](/Festivals "Festivals") that occurs on the 20th and 21st of [Summer](/Summer "Summer"). The festival begins at 6:10am and ends at 2:00am on both days. The festival takes place in [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") by the river near [Marnie's Ranch](/Marnie%27s_Ranch "Marnie's Ranch"). During the festival, players can fish in the river to catch [Rainbow Trout](/Rainbow_Trout "Rainbow Trout"), which can be caught all day long and regardless of weather.

Each Rainbow Trout catch (from anywhere with the exception of from a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond")) has a 33% chance to give the player a ![Golden Tag.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/98/Golden_Tag.png/24px-Golden_Tag.png) [Golden Tag](/Golden_Tag "Golden Tag"). This chance is multiplied by the number of Rainbow Trout caught, so it increases to 66% if 2 Rainbow Trout are caught at once and to 99% if 3 are caught at once.[[1]](#cite_note-goldentag-1) Only 1 Golden Tag can be caught per catch even if the player caught multiple Rainbow Trout at once. When a Golden Tag is obtained, if the player did not get a [Fishing Treasure Chest](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") it will be caught with the fish itself. Otherwise it will be obtained with the treasure chest that appears while catching the Rainbow Trout. While Golden Tags can be obtained from Rainbow Trouts in other locations, with the exception of Farm Maps with Cindersap Forest fish (Riverland, Hilltop, Forest, and Meadowlands), Rainbow Trout can only be caught with their normal restrictions in these areas.

Golden Tags can be exchanged for prizes at the [booth](#Derby_Booth).

Golden Tags carry over from day to day and can be saved for subsequent years.

Unlike other festivals, shops and homes throughout Stardew Valley are not closed on the days of the event, and time continues to pass during the festival. [Animals](/Animals "Animals") also need to be fed during the event; the game will not automatically consider them to be fed.

## Derby Booth

When interacting with the booth, the player can choose to either exchange a Golden Tag for a [prize](#Prizes) or receive an [explanation](#Quotes) of the event.

### Prizes

The player exchanges Golden Tags 1 by 1 for the available prizes. Each Golden Tag needs to be exchanged individually, even if they have more than one in their [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory").

The first prize the player receives will always be 1 ![Tent Kit.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/05/Tent_Kit.png/24px-Tent_Kit.png) [Tent Kit](/Tent_Kit "Tent Kit"). Afterwards, the player can get one of 10 possible prizes listed below. The first of these prizes is given at random, with the next prize guaranteed to be the one listed next on this list, with 1 [Bucket Hat](/Bucket_Hat "Bucket Hat") rewarded after 3 [Omni Geodes](/Omni_Geode "Omni Geode").[[2]](#cite_note-prizepool-2) After receiving all 10 listed prizes, the player will earn the prizes again in the same order.

## Tips

[Challenge Bait](/Challenge_Bait "Challenge Bait") will be most effective, followed by [Rainbow Trout Bait](/Targeted_Bait "Targeted Bait") with optional tackle, while [Training Rod](/Training_Rod "Training Rod") is a last resort for a farmer with a low fishing level.

* Having no [luck](/Luck "Luck") buffs, low [fishing](/Fishing "Fishing") level, and fishing close to the shore will improve the chances of catching Rainbow Trout. The [Training Rod](/Training_Rod "Training Rod") will improve the chances as well, although it would only be useful if bait and tackle are unavailable.
* The player can craft a [Bait Maker](/Bait_Maker "Bait Maker") to obtain some [Rainbow Trout Bait](/Targeted_Bait "Targeted Bait"), which will significantly increase the amount of Rainbow Trout the player can catch each day. The player can also bring the [Bait Maker](/Bait_Maker "Bait Maker") to the festival, so more targeted bait can be readily made from the Rainbow Trout caught there.
* Using [Challenge Bait](/Challenge_Bait "Challenge Bait") and getting a perfect catch will almost guarantee getting a Golden Tag, as then 3 [Rainbow Trout](/Rainbow_Trout "Rainbow Trout") are caught at once. The player can use [Cork Bobbers](/Cork_Bobber "Cork Bobber") or Fishing [Buffs](/Buffs "Buffs") to increase the size of the fishing bar, making it more likely to get a perfect.
  + If the player can get a perfect catch every single time, Challenge Bait will get them more golden tags on average than any other bait.
