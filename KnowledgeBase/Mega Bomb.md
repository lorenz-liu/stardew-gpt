The **Mega Bomb** is a one-time use [explosive item](/Crafting#Bombs "Crafting"). It damages certain items within its radius. Other items are impervious to its effects.

Mega Bombs can be [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting") or purchased from the [Dwarf](/Dwarf "Dwarf") (in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") and in the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon")) for data-sort-value="1600">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,600g. They are also offered by the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader"), who will trade five [Iridium Ore](/Iridium_Ore "Iridium Ore") for one Mega Bomb. They can be purchased from the [Calico Egg Merchant](/Desert_Festival#Calico_Egg_Merchant "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="15">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 15 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg"). [Squid Kids](/Squid_Kid "Squid Kid"), [Iridium Bats](/Bats "Bats"), [Haunted Skulls](/Haunted_Skull "Haunted Skull"), and [Magma Duggies](/Magma_Duggy "Magma Duggy") may also drop a Mega Bomb when slain. Mega Bombs can be won from the Crane Game inside the [Movie Theater](/Movie_Theater "Movie Theater"), during any season. Five Mega Bombs may occasionally be found in [Treasure Rooms](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern") in the Skull Cavern. 3-5 Mega Bombs may occasionally be found in [Supply Crates](/Supply_Crate "Supply Crate") on the [Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps#Beach "Farm Maps") provided the player has upgraded the [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") at least two times. A Bomb may occasionally be purchased from the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="50"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150–1,000g. Five Mega Bombs may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") or [Golden Mystery Box](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box"). Six or eight Mega Bombs can be given as a prize from the [prize machine](/Mayor%27s_Manor#Prize_Machine "Mayor's Manor") in the Mayor's Manor. [Kent](/Kent "Kent") may randomly send one in the mail as a gift.

Tip: Due to the wide blast area it is often a challenge to use the Mega Bomb without taking damage. Ensure ample healing supplies are available when using this. If a player has read the [Dwarvish Safety Manual](/Dwarvish_Safety_Manual "Dwarvish Safety Manual"), they will take 25% less damage from a Mega Bomb.

## Effects

Note that the following is not an exhaustive list:

* Rocks and [ore/gem nodes](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining") will drop their respective ores, gems, etc.
* Mushrooms, [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot"), and [foraged minerals](/Minerals#Foraged_Minerals "Minerals") will be destroyed.
* Any untilled dirt tiles within a significantly smaller radius will become tilled. In [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") and [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), any items (*e.g.,* clay, artifacts) will be dropped at the same rate as a player using a hoe; elsewhere, nothing will drop (unless the spot in question is the subject of a [Secret Note](/Secret_Note "Secret Note")).
* Crafted [artisan](/Crafting#Artisan_Equipment "Crafting") and [refining](/Crafting#Refining_Equipment "Crafting") equipment will be destroyed.
* [Heaters](/Heater "Heater") will be destroyed.
* [Auto-Grabbers](/Auto-Grabber "Auto-Grabber") will *not* be destroyed, whether empty or containing items.
* [Trees](/Trees "Trees") will be destroyed when their health reaches zero (four Mega Bombs will knock down a fully grown tree, and two more will destroy the stump). Trees will drop [wood](/Wood "Wood"), [sap](/Sap "Sap"), and any seeds they contained.

## Radius

The Mega Bomb has a radius of 6-8 tiles.

## Bundles

Mega Bomb is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

A Mega Bomb can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Shirt155.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Shirt155.png/24px-Shirt155.png) [Bomber Jacket](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a red dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a red dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

* Two Mega Bombs are requested by [Lava Eel](/Lava_Eel "Lava Eel") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 5 to 7.

## Trivia

* The [Bouncer](/Bouncer "Bouncer") guarding the [Casino](/Casino "Casino") will drop a Mega Bomb to ward off the player if they use glitches to try to enter the Casino without permission.
