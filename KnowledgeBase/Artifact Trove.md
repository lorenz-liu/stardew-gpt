The **Artifact Trove** is an [artifact](/Artifacts "Artifacts") container that can be traded from the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for 5 [Omni Geodes](/Omni_Geode "Omni Geode") each. The [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") may trade 3 Artifact Troves for 1 [Treasure Appraisal Guide](/Treasure_Appraisal_Guide "Treasure Appraisal Guide"), provided the player has already obtained the power from that book. Up to 5 can be purchased from [George](/George "George")'s [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="10">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 10 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg") each.

It can also be obtained by [panning](/Pans "Pans"), dropped by [Haunted Skulls](/Haunted_Skull "Haunted Skull") if all [Vault Bundles](/Bundles#Vault "Bundles") have been completed, found in [Supply Crates](/Supply_Crate "Supply Crate") on the [Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps#Beach "Farm Maps") once the player has upgraded the [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") at least two times, or dug up from [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island"). Four or five Artifact Troves can be obtained as a prize from the [Prize Machine](/Mayor%27s_Manor#Prize_Machine "Mayor's Manor"). Five Artifact Troves may occasionally be found in [treasure rooms](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern") in the Skull Cavern. It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") dog with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals").

Although most of the contents are artifacts, there are a few items, namely the [Golden Pumpkin](/Golden_Pumpkin "Golden Pumpkin"), [Pearl](/Pearl "Pearl"), [Treasure Appraisal Guide](/Treasure_Appraisal_Guide "Treasure Appraisal Guide"), and [Treasure Chest](/Treasure_Chest "Treasure Chest"), which are not.

The Artifact Trove can be broken open at the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith") for data-sort-value="25">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)25g to obtain the contents inside. It cannot be broken open using a [Geode Crusher](/Geode_Crusher "Geode Crusher").

## Bundles

Artifact Trove is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Artifact Trove is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Shirt290.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Shirt290.png/24px-Shirt290.png) [Trinket Shirt](/Tailoring#Shirts "Tailoring").

## Quests

Artifact Trove is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Notes

* By multiplying the sell price of each item by the chance to obtain it, the average profit of opening an Artifact Trove without having read the [Treasure Appraisal Guide](/Treasure_Appraisal_Guide "Treasure Appraisal Guide") is data-sort-value="472.4">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)472.4g, or data-sort-value="447.4">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)447.4g when the data-sort-value="25">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)25g opening cost is factored in. The profit after having read the guide is data-sort-value="660.4">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)660.4g, or data-sort-value="635.4">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)635.4g when the data-sort-value="25">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)25g opening cost is factored in.
* Since the average profit when breaking open an Omni Geode is data-sort-value="96.4">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)96.4g, and it takes five Omni Geodes to trade for one Artifact Trove (*i.e.,* data-sort-value="482.0">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)482g) at the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader"), you will typically lose data-sort-value="35">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)35g every time you trade for one (if you have not yet read the [Treasure Appraisal Guide](/Treasure_Appraisal_Guide "Treasure Appraisal Guide")). After having read it however, you will typically earn data-sort-value="153">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)153g per trade. This assumes you sell the contents.
