The **Ancient Sword** is an [Artifact](/Artifacts "Artifacts") that can be found by digging up an [Artifact Spot](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") in [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") or [the Mountains](/The_Mountain "The Mountain"), and from [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") or [Artifact Troves](/Artifact_Trove "Artifact Trove"). It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") dog with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals").

## Donation

Donation of this item contributes to the total count of donations for the [Museum](/Museum "Museum").

## Bundles

Ancient Sword is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Recipes

Ancient Sword is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Ancient Sword is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt034.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/8f/Shirt034.png/24px-Shirt034.png) [Green Tunic](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a gray dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

Ancient Sword is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Trivia

The green tunic [tailored](/Tailoring "Tailoring") from ancient sword and [cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") is similar to the green tunic worn by Link in *The Legend of Zelda* series of video games.
