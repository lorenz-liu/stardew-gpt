**Clint** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). He owns and runs the local [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith").

## Schedule

Clint is unavailable for shopping, tool upgrades, or geode processing on Fridays after the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") is restored, unless it is raining.

After the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island is unlocked, Clint may randomly spend Friday there. After leaving the Island at 6pm, Clint will immediately go home to bed.

## Relationships

Clint often visits [the saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon") in order to socialize with [Emily](/Emily "Emily"), who he has a crush on but is nervous to approach. A dialogue with [Gus](/Gus#Quotes "Gus") indicates that he suspects Clint's crush but has decided to not ask him about it. A note found in Clint's bedroom confirms his feelings, reading:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| “ | “Dear Emily, I know you only think of me as a friend.  It's my fault. I'm too shy. I'll never have the courage to tell you the truth.  That's why I'm writing this letter to myself that I'll certainly crumple up and toss in the corner.” |

## Quests

* ["Forging Ahead"](/Quests#Forging_Ahead "Quests"): The morning after you collect your first [Copper Ore](/Copper_Ore "Copper Ore"), Clint will visit and give you the blueprints for [crafting](/Crafting "Crafting") a [Furnace](/Furnace "Furnace").
* ["Clint's Attempt"](/Quests#Clint.27s_Attempt "Quests"): Clint will send you a letter on the 6th of [Winter](/Winter "Winter") asking that you give [Emily](/Emily "Emily") an [Amethyst](/Amethyst "Amethyst") and tell her it's from him. The reward is 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart with Emily.
* ["A Favor for Clint"](/Quests#A_Favor_For_Clint "Quests"): Clint will send you a letter on the 17th of [Winter](/Winter "Winter") asking you to bring him an [Iron Bar](/Iron_Bar "Iron Bar"). The reward is data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* Clint may randomly request ores or [coal](/Coal "Coal") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). The reward is an amount of gold that varies by requested item/amount.
  + Note that you do not need to have any ore/coal in inventory to finish the quest. Once the required items are gathered, simply talking to Clint will complete the quest. Handing him any amount of ore or coal will always result in gifting him one of them.
* Clint may randomly request that you kill a certain number of [Monsters](/Monsters "Monsters") or bring him a random item at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests"). The reward for killing monsters is an amount of gold that varies by the type and number of monsters requested. The reward for item delivery is 3x the item's base value and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* ["Cave Patrol"](/Quests#Cave_Patrol "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), Clint may randomly request that the player slay 50 [Bats](/Bats "Bats"), [Dust Sprites](/Dust_Sprite "Dust Sprite"), [Skeletons](/Skeleton "Skeleton"), or [Grubs](/Grub "Grub"). The reward is data-sort-value="6000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)6,000g and the [Geode Crusher](/Geode_Crusher "Geode Crusher") recipe.

## Trivia

* According to a tape in a boombox in Clint's room, he is a fan of the blues.
* As shown in the picture of him holding a Joja Cola, Clint has only four fingers on his left hand.
* Clint's 7 heart recipe is the Bean Hotpot, and Emily has a quote during Winter asking if the player knows how to cook that recipe. This hints that Clint has perhaps introduced her to the dish, or learned it for her.
* Developer ConcernedApe has stated that Clint's age is "late 30s-40ish" ([source](https://www.reddit.com/r/StardewValley/comments/q804ir/)).
* Clint's apron can be tailored with 1 [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") and 1 [Fiddlehead Risotto](/Fiddlehead_Risotto "Fiddlehead Risotto"), creating the [Blacksmith Apron](/Tailoring#Shirts "Tailoring").
