**Alex** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives in the house southeast of [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). He's one of the twelve characters available to [marry](/Marriage "Marriage").

## Schedule

During [Spring](/Spring "Spring") and [Fall](/Fall "Fall"), Alex exits his home at 8am unless it is raining. He stands by the tree to the right of his house, playing with his gridball, and he moves towards the left of the fenced-in area in the afternoon.

During [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), he can be found on [the beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") in the morning and running the [Ice Cream Stand](/Ice_Cream_Stand "Ice Cream Stand") to the left of the [museum](/Museum "Museum") in the afternoon. On the 16th of [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), he has an appointment at the [clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic").

During [Winter](/Winter "Winter"), he works out at the [Spa](/Spa "Spa") almost every day. When he is not there, he will be at his house.

After the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") is unlocked, Alex may randomly spend the day there. After leaving the Island at 6pm, Alex will immediately go home to bed. Alex never visits the Resort on [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days or his checkup day at [Harvey's Clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic").

## Relationships

Alex lives with his grandparents, [George](/George "George") and [Evelyn](/Evelyn "Evelyn"). He is friends with [Haley](/Haley "Haley"), and will dance with her at the [Flower Dance](/Flower_Dance "Flower Dance") if neither of them are dancing with the player. He also has a dog named [Dusty](/Dusty "Dusty"). You can find Dusty's name from [Pam](/Pam "Pam") or from Alex in a cutscene.

| Parents |
| --- |
| *(Spoilers)* He previously lived with his mother [Clara](/Clara_Mullner "Clara Mullner"), whom he had a very close relationship with. He expresses a deep hatred for his father, who was an alcoholic and verbally abusive to him. Twelve years prior to [the player](/The_Player "The Player") arriving in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"), Alex moved in with [George](/George "George") and [Evelyn](/Evelyn "Evelyn") after his mother passed away. |

### Group Ten-Heart Event

![Ten Hearts.png](/mediawiki/images/c/c5/Ten_Hearts.png)

If the player is unmarried and has given a bouquet to all available bachelors, raised [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") with each bachelor to 10 hearts, and seen each bachelor's 10-heart event, then entering [The Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon") will trigger a cutscene. If Alex is the final bachelor you share a Ten-Heart Event with, the Group Ten-Heart Event will be unavoidable as it is triggered immediately afterwards.

| **Details** |
| --- |
| If the player has a [Rabbit's Foot](/Rabbit%27s_Foot "Rabbit's Foot") in inventory, the cutscene will consist of a friendly game of pool. If the player does not have a [Rabbit's Foot](/Rabbit%27s_Foot "Rabbit's Foot") in inventory, all bachelors will express anger about the player dating them all at one time. Regardless of the player's dialogue choice(s), all bachelors will decide to give the player the "cold shoulder" for about a week after the event. They will give angry dialogue when interacted with, and refuse gifts. After about a week, all bachelors will forgive the player, and dialogues return to normal. |

This event will trigger only one time per [save file](/Saves "Saves"). This event will not trigger if you are married or have given a [Wilted Bouquet](/Wilted_Bouquet "Wilted Bouquet") or [Mermaid's Pendant](/Mermaid%27s_Pendant "Mermaid's Pendant") to one of the marriage candidates.

## Marriage

:   *Main article: [Marriage](/Marriage "Marriage")*

Once married, Alex will move into the [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse"). Like other marriage candidates, he will add his own room to the right of the bedroom. He'll also setup a workout space behind the farmhouse where he'll sometimes go to lift weights.

On rainy nights, Alex may offer you dinner: [Survival Burger](/Survival_Burger "Survival Burger"), [Dish O' The Sea](/Dish_O%27_The_Sea "Dish O' The Sea"), [Fried Eel](/Fried_Eel "Fried Eel"), [Crispy Bass](/Crispy_Bass "Crispy Bass"), or [Baked Fish](/Baked_Fish "Baked Fish"). On days when he stays inside the farmhouse all day, he may make you breakfast: [Omelet](/Omelet "Omelet"), [Hashbrowns](/Hashbrowns "Hashbrowns"), or [Pancakes](/Pancakes "Pancakes").

## Questions

**Wednesday**

:   **Male players:**

"I'd ask you to throw the ball around, but you don't really seem like the sports type."

:   **Female players:**

“If you weren’t a girl I’d ask you to play catch.”

* **I'm fine just watching you from a distance.** *(No effect on [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship").)*

Alex responds: "Yeah, some people just aren't made for sports."

* **I want to play catch with you!** *(+15 [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship").)*

Alex responds: "Really? I guess I underestimated you. My arm's a little sore, but maybe next time."

* **(angry) What's that supposed to mean?** *(No effect on [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship").)*

Alex responds: "Uh...Nevermind."

**Summer, Wednesday**

:   **Male players:**

"Hey farm guy, I've got a question for you. Do you think I'll ever turn pro?"

:   **Female players:**

"Hey farm girl, I've got a question for you. Do you think I'll ever turn pro?"

* **You're destined to be a sports legend** *(+30 [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship").)*

Alex responds: "Hey, Now that's what I'm talking about!"

* **Maybe if you practice a lot** *(No effect on [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship").)*

Alex responds: "Yeah, well I'm so good I'm not going to have to practice all that much."

* **No, you'll most likely fail and become a salesman** *(-30 [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship").)*

Alex responds: "That's insane. You're just jealous that I'm talented and popular and you're not. Get away from me."

**Fall, Wednesday**

"Do you think ladies like my haircut? / What do you think about my haircut?"

* **It looks very fashionable.** *(+30 [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship").)*

Alex responds: "Yeah, I know. That's why I have it like this."

* **It looks like some kind of fungal growth.** *(-30 [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship").)*

Alex responds: "Yeah, right. You're just jealous that I look so good."
