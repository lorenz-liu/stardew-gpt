The **Lobster** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught using a [Crab Pot](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot") in any [saltwater](/Crab_Pot#Saltwater "Crab Pot") body of water. It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") turtle with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals"), or randomly found at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="120"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)360–1,000g. The [Statue Of Endless Fortune](/Statue_Of_Endless_Fortune "Statue Of Endless Fortune") produces one Lobster per year on [Elliott](/Elliott "Elliott")'s birthday, [Fall 5](/Fall "Fall").

Like all fish caught from the crab pot, it cannot be eaten unless it is used in a [Cooking](/Cooking "Cooking") recipe or [smoked](/Smoked_Fish "Smoked Fish").

## Tailoring

Lobster can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Sailor Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt261a.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3d/Shirt261a.png/24px-Shirt261a.png)](/File:Shirt261a.png) It can also be used as a red dye color at the dye pots in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, located at [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Lobsters can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 3 days. The initial pond capacity is 3 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing three quests. Typical output includes red Lobster [Roe](/Roe "Roe"), [Seaweed](/Seaweed "Seaweed"), and various [Trash](/Trash "Trash") items, but more valuable items are possible starting at population 5.
