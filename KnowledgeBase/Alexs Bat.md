**Alex's Bat** is a [club weapon](/Weapons#Club "Weapons") sold by [Alex](/Alex "Alex") for data-sort-value="70">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 70 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg") during the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival"). Note that the Grand Slam featured in the description is not featured anywhere else in the game.

## History

* [1.1](/Version_History#1.1 "Version History"): Introduced but unobtainable.
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Now attainable during Desert Festival.
