The **Midnight Carp** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught in [the mountain lake](/The_Mountain "The Mountain") and the [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") pond during [Fall](/Fall "Fall") and [Winter](/Winter "Winter") late at night. It can also be caught on many of the non-standard [Farm Maps](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps"): Forest, Riverland, Wilderness, and the large pond on the Four Corners Map. It can also be caught during any season in [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") North and West (freshwater). It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") cat with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals"). It can also randomly be found at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="150"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)450–1,000g or in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during Fall or Winter.

Midnight Carp can be caught regardless of time of day or season using [Magic Bait](/Magic_Bait "Magic Bait").

## Fish Pond

Midnight Carp can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 2 days. The initial pond capacity is 3 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing two quests. The primary output is violet Midnight Carp [Roe](/Roe "Roe"). However, [River Jelly](/River_Jelly "River Jelly") is also possible starting at population 7.

## Tailoring

Midnight Carp can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Shirt157.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/0e/Shirt157.png/24px-Shirt157.png) [Fishing Vest](/Tailoring#Shirts "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a purple dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as an iridium dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

* **Item Delivery:** One Midnight Carp may be randomly requested in [Fall](/Fall "Fall") or [Winter](/Winter "Winter") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="450">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)450g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* **Aquatic Overpopulation:** [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius") may request 10 Midnight Carp to be caught in [Fall](/Fall "Fall") at the [Special Orders board](/Quests#List_of_Special_Orders "Quests") outside the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor") for a reward of gold equivalent to selling the fish and the [Farm Computer](/Farm_Computer "Farm Computer") recipe.
