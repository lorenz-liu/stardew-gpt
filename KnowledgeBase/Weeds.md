**Weeds** are wild plants which are spawned randomly and may spread if not cleared. Weeds have a 50% chance to drop 1 [Fiber](/Fiber "Fiber") when cut with a [Scythe](/Scythe "Scythe"), [Axe](/Axes "Axes"), [Pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes"), [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes"), [Dagger](/Weapons#Dagger "Weapons") or [Sword](/Weapons#Sword "Weapons"), or blown up by a [bomb](/Crafting#Bombs "Crafting"). If a weed does not drop Fiber, there is a 5% chance it will drop [Mixed Seeds](/Mixed_Seeds "Mixed Seeds"). If a weed drops neither Fiber nor Mixed Seeds during [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), there is a 5% chance it will drop [Mixed Flower Seeds](/Mixed_Flower_Seeds "Mixed Flower Seeds").[[1]](#cite_note-cutweed-1) Both the Mixed Seeds and Mixed Flower Seeds chances are increased to 9% if the player has read the [Raccoon Journal](/Raccoon_Journal "Raccoon Journal").

Weeds in the [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") Jungle have an 8% chance to drop [Taro Tubers](/Taro_Tuber "Taro Tuber") and a 1% chance to drop a [Mummified Frog](/Mummified_Frog "Mummified Frog"). These weeds also appear rarely in the [Dangerous mines](/Dangerous_mines "Dangerous mines").

Cutting weeds with [weapons](/Weapons "Weapons") [enchanted](/Forge#Combat_enchantments "Forge") with "Haymaker" has an extra 50% chance of dropping a fiber, and 33% chance of hay.

There's also a 0.001% chance to find a [Living Hat](/Living_Hat "Living Hat") when cutting weeds.

Any weeds above ground disappear in [Winter](/Winter "Winter").

## Spawning and Spreading

Lone weeds will spawn randomly on [The Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") and on [Ginger Island West](/Ginger_Island#Island_West "Ginger Island"). They can appear on any tillable tile. On The Farm, this only occurs on day 1 of each season (except [Winter](/Winter "Winter"))[[2]](#cite_note-farmday-2). On Ginger Island, this only occurs on [Mondays](/Days_of_the_Week#Monday "Days of the Week")[[3]](#cite_note-islandday-3). When the [Gold Clock](/Gold_Clock "Gold Clock") is placed, it prevents weeds from appearing on the farm and the Ginger Island farm.

Besides the farms, other areas in the game will also spawn weeds without being restricted by the Gold Clock. Weeds will spawn in specific areas of [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"), [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") and the [Railroad](/Railroad "Railroad") at the start of the game, and each [Spring](/Spring "Spring") 1. Weeds may spawn everyday in the [Mutant Bug Lair](/Mutant_Bug_Lair "Mutant Bug Lair") and [Ginger Island jungle](/Ginger_Island#Island_East "Ginger Island"), but in very limited quantities. Weeds in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") respawn when the player returns to the top of the mines (*i.e.*, floor 0, where the Dwarf is).

If weeds aren't cleared, they may multiply and spread to an adjacent tillable tile[[4]](#cite_note-spawndebris-4). This can happen on any day, but especially on day 1 of each season, when it is raining, and during the [Summer](/Summer "Summer"). Spreading weeds can destroy items placed by the player, including [crafted items](/Crafting "Crafting") and [furniture](/Furniture "Furniture") – [Fences](/Crafting#Fences "Crafting") and [Chests](/Chest "Chest") are the only items placed by the player that cannot be destroyed. If an item was destroyed, a pop-up message will be displayed at the lower left of the screen when the player wakes up the next morning; the message will not say exactly which item was destroyed.

Weeds prevent the growth of [Fruit Trees](/Fruit_Trees "Fruit Trees") if they spawn or spread to the 3x3 grid around the tree. If this occurs, a pop-up message will be displayed at the lower left of the screen when the player wakes up the next morning indicating which type of fruit tree couldn't grow.

Only fences, chests, grass, and fixed objects such as buildings and trees stop the natural spread of weeds. [Flooring/paths](/Crafting#Decor "Crafting") and tilled soil do **not** stop the spread.

## Special Weeds

The weed shown below, which can be found only on the [Forest and Four Corners maps](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps"), will always drop 1 [Mixed Seeds](/Mixed_Seeds "Mixed Seeds") when cut. Each night, six random objects on the farm are chosen. Any of them that are weeds (and are in the upper-left quadrant if in the Four Corners map), are replaced by these special weeds.[[2]](#cite_note-farmday-2)

[![](/mediawiki/images/a/ab/WeedsMixedSeeds.png)](/File:WeedsMixedSeeds.png)

Special Weed  
(Spring, Summer, Fall)

During the [Green Rain](/Weather#Green_Rain "Weather") event, special weeds are spawned and have a 10% chance to drop [Moss](/Moss "Moss") when cut.[[1]](#cite_note-cutweed-1) The event also spawns Large Weeds that drop 2-3 [Moss](/Moss "Moss") and 2-3 [Fiber](/Fiber "Fiber") when destroyed. Large Weeds have a 5% chance to drop a [Mossy Seed](/Mossy_Seed "Mossy Seed") as well.[[5]](#cite_note-resourceclump-5) Both the green rain weed variants and large weeds will disappear after the green rain ends.
