**Mystery Boxes** are drop crates released by [Mr. Qi](/Mr._Qi "Mr. Qi"). After spending 50 days in the valley or after collecting the second [Prize Ticket](/Prize_Ticket "Prize Ticket") from the billboard,[[1]](#cite_note-gotFirstBillboardPrizeTicket-1) the player will see a cutscene with [Mr. Qi](/Mr._Qi "Mr. Qi") and will be informed by a pop up that Mystery Boxes can now be found. Afterwards, they can be obtained in a variety of ways. After opening more than 10 boxes, they can contain any item found in [Golden Mystery Boxes](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box") (other than [Golden Animal Crackers](/Golden_Animal_Cracker "Golden Animal Cracker") or [Auto-Petters](/Auto-Petter "Auto-Petter")).

Mystery Boxes can be cracked by [Clint](/Clint "Clint") in the [Blacksmith shop](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith") for data-sort-value="25">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)25g, similar to [Geodes](/Minerals#Geodes "Minerals"). However, unlike Geodes, they cannot be broken open using a [Geode Crusher](/Geode_Crusher "Geode Crusher").

## Locations

The following table lists the different actions that can spawn a Mystery Box if the player does not have [Foraging Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave").

The "Chance" column below indicates the probabilities that the game will *attempt* to spawn a Mystery Box. When such an attempt is made, the game performs a further check to see if it succeeds. If the player has [Foraging Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave"), the check will fail (and will attempt to spawn a [Golden Mystery Box](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box") instead). Otherwise, the probability of succeeding is 66% if the player does not have the power obtained from the [Book of Mysteries](/Book_of_Mysteries "Book of Mysteries") and 88% if they do.[[2]](#cite_note-rollMysteryBox-2)

Also note that the chances of obtaining Mystery Boxes from some of the sources depends on and increases with higher daily [luck](/Luck "Luck"), and in some cases, with [Luck Buffs](/Luck#Luck_Buffs "Luck").

| Activity | Chance |
| --- | --- |
| Regular [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") [[3]](#cite_note-fishing-3) | 9-17% (dependent on daily luck) |
| [Golden Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Golden_Treasure_Chests "Fishing") [[3]](#cite_note-fishing-3) | 12-21% (dependent on daily luck) |
| [Panning](/Pans "Pans") [[4]](#cite_note-panning-4) | 5% |
| Shaking [trees](/Trees "Trees") with seeds [[5]](#cite_note-trees-5) | 3% |
| [Monsters](/Monsters "Monsters") [[6]](#cite_note-monsters-6) | `0.01 + (Daily Luck / 10) + (Luck Buffs / 125)` |
| Breaking containers in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), and [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon") [[7]](#cite_note-containers-7) | `0.0081 + (Daily Luck / 15)` |
| Chopping [trees](/Trees "Trees") (Each Axe hit) [[8]](#cite_note-axe-8) | 0.5% |
| [Present train cars](/Railroad#Trains "Railroad") during [Winter](/Winter "Winter") [[9]](#cite_note-train-9) | 2% |

While train cars do have the same check for success as the other methods listed in the table, they will only ever drop Mystery Boxes.

Many other ways to obtain Mystery Boxes, even after claiming [Foraging Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave"), are listed below. These chances are not subject to additional checks for success.

* Purchasing from the [Calico Egg Merchant](/Desert_Festival#Calico_Egg_Merchant "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival").
* Reward from the [Trout Derby](/Trout_Derby "Trout Derby") and [SquidFest](/SquidFest "SquidFest") festivals.
* Trading a [Book of Mysteries](/Book_of_Mysteries "Book of Mysteries") with the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller").
* Digging [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") (0-11.25% chance, depending on [Daily Luck](/Luck#Daily_Luck "Luck")).[[10]](#cite_note-artifactspot-10)
* Opening [treasure chests](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern") in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), if Foraging Mastery hasn't been claimed (3.6% chance, increased if Farming and/or Combat Mastery have not been claimed).[[11]](#cite_note-skullcavern-11)
* Opening [Golden Mystery Boxes](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box") or other Mystery Boxes.
* Completing [Raccoon](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") requests beyond the fifth one.
* Completing the ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Winter Star Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Winter_Star "Remixed Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Remixed Bundles").
* Using the [prize machine](/Mayor%27s_Manor#Prize_Machine "Mayor's Manor") in the Mayor's Manor.
* If a player chops down a [giant](/Crops#Giant_Crops "Crops") [Qi Fruit](/Qi_Fruit "Qi Fruit") when the ["Qi's Crop" quest](/Quests#Qi.27s_Crop "Quests") is not active, then they will receive one Mystery Box.[[12]](#cite_note-qicrop-12)
* If a player places any [Milk](/Milk "Milk") or [Cookies](/Cookie "Cookie") on a [table](/Furniture#Tables "Furniture") or [long table](/Furniture#Long_Tables "Furniture") during the night of Winter 24th, then they will be exchanged for a Mystery Box.[[13]](#cite_note-milkcookie-13)

## Bundles

Five Mystery Boxes are required for the ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Helper's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Helper.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Remixed Bundles").

## Quests

The Mystery Box is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Tailoring

Mystery Box is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a blue dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a dark cyan dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Trading

Three Mystery Boxes can be traded to the [Raccoon's wife](/Giant_Stump#Raccoon_Wife.27s_Shop "Giant Stump") for 1 [Golden Mystery Box](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box"), after the [sixth request](/Giant_Stump#Raccoon.27s_Requests "Giant Stump") from the Raccoon family is completed and [Foraging Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave") is claimed.
