The **Rabbit** is a farm [animal](/Animals "Animals") that lives in a [Deluxe Coop](/Coop "Coop"). Rabbits can be purchased at [Marnie's Ranch](/Marnie%27s_Ranch "Marnie's Ranch") for data-sort-value="8000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)8,000g. A rabbit cannot reproduce. A rabbit makes a soft sniffing sound when it is petted.

## Produce

:   *Main article: [Animals#Produce](/Animals#Produce "Animals")*

Rabbits who eat every day mature after 6 nights have passed. A mature and fed rabbit produces [Wool](/Wool "Wool") every 4 days, which sells for data-sort-value="340">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)340g. They will drop it on their own so they do not need to be sheared. Once sufficient [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals") and happiness are reached, a rabbit may produce a [Rabbit's Foot](/Rabbit%27s_Foot "Rabbit's Foot") instead of wool, which sells for data-sort-value="565">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)565g.

Wool can be placed in a [Loom](/Loom "Loom") to produce [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth"), which sells for data-sort-value="470">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)470g.

## Selling

Rabbits can be sold. The selling price is dependent on how many hearts the animal has (data-sort-value="10400">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)10,400g max).

To sell a coop or barn animal, right-click the animal after petting. The friendship/mood interface appears. Move the cursor over the gold coin button at the right to see how much the animal can be sold for. Click that button and the confirmation that follows to complete the sale.
