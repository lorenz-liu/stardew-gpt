# Crafting

:   *For food items prepared in the kitchen, see [Cooking](/Cooking "Cooking").*

**Crafting** is the activity of creating a new item as specified by a crafting recipe. Similar to but separate from Cooking, each recipe lists a set of ingredient items that are consumed in each crafting action. The player knows ten crafting recipes at the start of the game, and must learn all others by gaining [skills](/Skills "Skills"), developing friendships with townsfolk, and purchasing the rest from shops. Unlike cooked items, most (not all) crafted items are inedible.

The **Crafting menu** can be accessed by pausing the game (using E/ESC on PC, ![Switch X.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/94/Switch_X.png/24px-Switch_X.png) on Switch, ![Switch B.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/bc/Switch_B.png/24px-Switch_B.png) on Xbox, ![PS4 Circle.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/18/PS4_Circle.png/24px-PS4_Circle.png) on PlayStation) and navigating to the tab with a hammer icon. The recipes the player knows will be saturated, while recipes with missing ingredients are faded grey.

By mousing over each item, a popup shows the materials that you will need. Ingredient names written in red text are not currently in the player's inventory.

To craft an item, click on the item's icon once you have all the required ingredients. The newly crafted item will appear beneath the player's cursor, and the player can then place it inside their inventory. The crafting screen may expand to multiple pages once the player acquires a larger selection of recipes.

Crafting recipes are gained by leveling up [skills](/Skills "Skills"), completing [special orders](/Quests#List_of_Special_Orders "Quests"), purchased from merchants, or received as gifts by gaining [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points with [villagers](/Villagers "Villagers").

## Fences

Fences block players, farm animals, and the spread of grass. The different fence types differ only in appearance and durability.

Fences will decay over time. When freshly placed, they'll drop the item when demolished. Otherwise they'll not drop an item, and will eventually become visibly damaged. Fences will disappear entirely if left in a damaged state for too long. A shortcut to replacing a damaged fence (other than hitting it with a tool and then replacing it) is to "use" the broken fence while a new fence is equipped in inventory. Your spouse may fix some broken-state fences daily before you leave the house.

[Gates](/Gate "Gate") can be used to pass through fences, and closed to keep livestock (and other ground-pathing entities) contained. [Torches](/Torch "Torch") can be placed on a fence.

Having a [Gold Clock](/Gold_Clock "Gold Clock") on [the farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") will prevent fences from decaying.

## Sprinklers

Sprinklers water crops automatically every morning at 6am. Sprinklers can be used to automatically water troughs in the [Slime Hutch](/Slime_Hutch "Slime Hutch") but they cannot water a [pet's](/Animals#Pets "Animals") water bowl. Sprinklers will also not water plants in [Garden Pots](/Garden_Pot "Garden Pot"). They cannot be placed on sand.

## Seeds

Craftable seasonal wild seeds will grow the fruits/vegetables/flowers normally found as forage during the season. Craftable [Ancient Seeds](/Ancient_Seeds "Ancient Seeds") will grow [Ancient Fruit](/Ancient_Fruit "Ancient Fruit"). Craftable [Grass Starter](/Grass_Starter "Grass Starter") acts the same as that purchased from [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"); once planted, it will grow and spread grass on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm").

Note: On [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island"), growing seasonal wild seeds will often destroy the tilled soil (and fertilizer), leaving untilled soil in their place after harvest.

## Achievements

There are 3 [Achievements](/Achievements "Achievements") associated with Crafting:

* D.I.Y. (Craft 15 different items)
* Artisan (Craft 30 different items)
* Craft Master (Craft every item)

The [Wedding Ring](/Wedding_Ring "Wedding Ring") does not count towards any of these achievements.

In the [Options](/Options "Options") menu, checking the box next to "Show Advanced Crafting Information" will show how many items the player has crafted. Alternatively, users with access to their [saved game files](/Saves "Saves") may find the "Stardew Checkup" utility helpful for tracking their Crafting achievement progress. The utility is located at: <https://mouseypounds.github.io/stardew-checkup/>

## Crafted goods outside the farm

It is possible to place crafted items outside [the farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"). If a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") walks through the square where a crafted item (other than a chest) was placed, the item will be destroyed.[[1]](#cite_note-villagerpathnonchest-1) Chests will be moved out of the way.[[2]](#cite_note-villagerpathchest-2) Areas that aren't visited by [villagers](/Villagers "Villagers"), so are safe to place crafted items in, are the [Backwoods](/Backwoods "Backwoods"), [the Tunnel](/The_Tunnel "The Tunnel"), the southern half of [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest"), the [Quarry](/Quarry "Quarry"), and the [Railroad](/Railroad "Railroad") above the tracks.
