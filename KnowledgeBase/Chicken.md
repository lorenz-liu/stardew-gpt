The **Chicken** is a farm [animal](/Animals "Animals") that lives in a [Coop](/Coop "Coop"). Chickens can be purchased at [Marnie's Ranch](/Marnie%27s_Ranch "Marnie's Ranch") for data-sort-value="800">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)800g or hatched by placing an [Egg](/Egg "Egg") into an [Incubator](/Incubator "Incubator"), taking ![Time Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Time_Icon.png/24px-Time_Icon.png) 9,000m (5d 16h 40m) to incubate, or ![Time Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Time_Icon.png/24px-Time_Icon.png) 4,500m (2d 21h 40m) with the [Coopmaster Profession](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming"). Additionally, two chickens are present on the farm by default on the Meadowlands [Farm Map](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps").

## Color

Chickens are colored White, Brown, or Blue. The Blue variety can be obtained only after seeing [Shane](/Shane "Shane")'s 8-heart event. The color of the chicken is assigned randomly by the game when a chicken is purchased.

While selecting a coop for a Chicken purchased from Marnie's Ranch, a message is shown stating "Choose a Coop for your new White/Brown/Blue Chicken". Cancelling the purchase returns the player to the purchase window, where they can try again for the desired color.

An [Incubator](/Incubator "Incubator") will hatch a brown chicken from a [Brown Egg](/Egg "Egg") and a white chicken from a regular Egg (or the corresponding Large variants). This means blue chickens cannot be obtained using an Incubator.[[1]](#cite_note-1)

White and blue chickens produce white eggs, and brown chickens produce brown eggs, so it makes a difference in completing the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") [Bundles](/Bundles "Bundles"), especially if [Remixed Bundles](/Options#Advanced_Options "Options") is not chosen at start of game.

## Produce

:   *Main article: [Animals#Produce](/Animals#Produce "Animals")*

Chickens who eat every day mature after 3 nights have passed. A mature and fed chicken produces a white or brown [Egg](/Egg "Egg") every day, which can be placed in an [Incubator](/Incubator "Incubator") to hatch another chicken, or sold for data-sort-value="50">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)50g. Once sufficient [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals") and happiness is reached, it has a chance to produce a [Large Egg](/Large_Egg "Large Egg") or [Large Brown Egg](/Large_Egg "Large Egg") which sells for data-sort-value="95">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)95g.

An Egg can be placed in the [Mayonnaise Machine](/Mayonnaise_Machine "Mayonnaise Machine") to produce [Mayonnaise](/Mayonnaise "Mayonnaise"), which sells for data-sort-value="190">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)190g or data-sort-value="285">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)285g depending on quality.

## Blue Chickens

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/36/BlueChick.png/75px-BlueChick.png)](/File:BlueChick.png)

A blue chick

After seeing [Shane](/Shane "Shane")'s 8-heart event, each chicken you purchase from [Marnie](/Marnie "Marnie") has a 25% chance of being blue. Aside from appearance, blue chickens are identical to regular white and brown chickens. One subtle benefit to this alternative color is that blue chickens are much easier to spot in a field of grass.

Blue chickens are hatched as blue chicks.

## Selling

It is possible to sell Chickens. The amount of [gold](/Gold "Gold") obtained for doing so is dependent on how many hearts the animal has. The more hearts; the higher amount of gold fetched for selling it. The amount of gold obtained for selling a maximum-heart Chicken is data-sort-value="1040">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,040g.

To sell a coop or barn animal, right-click the animal after petting. The friendship/mood interface appears. Move the cursor over the gold coin button at the right to see how much you can sell the animal for. Click that button and the confirmation that follows to complete the sale.
