An **Earth Crystal** is a type of [Mineral](/Minerals "Minerals"). It can be foraged in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") (especially floors 1 - 39), or obtained from [Geodes](/Geode "Geode") and [Omni Geodes](/Omni_Geode "Omni Geode"), dropped from [Duggies](/Duggy "Duggy") in the Mines (floors 1 - 29), dropped from [Haunted Skulls](/Haunted_Skull "Haunted Skull"), or from [panning](/Pans "Pans"). It can also be found in [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") or in a [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can").

Earth Crystals can be destroyed by [bombs](/Crafting#Bombs "Crafting").

## Tailoring

Earth Crystal is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [![Shirt094.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/aa/Shirt094.png/24px-Shirt094.png)](/File:Shirt094.png) [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* Earth Crystal may be randomly requested during any season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="150">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* 5 Earth Crystal are requested by [Stonefish](/Stonefish "Stonefish") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 3 to 5.
* 1-2 Earth Crystal may be requested by [Bullhead](/Bullhead "Bullhead"), [Catfish](/Catfish "Catfish"), or [Largemouth Bass](/Largemouth_Bass "Largemouth Bass") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 5 to 7.

## History

* [1.1](/Version_History#1.1 "Version History"): Used as a building material to the [Earth Obelisk](/Earth_Obelisk "Earth Obelisk").
* [1.3.27](/Version_History#1.3.27 "Version History"): Can be found in [Omni Geodes](/Omni_Geode "Omni Geode").
* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): [Sturdy Ring](/Sturdy_Ring "Sturdy Ring") does not use Earth Crystal to craft anymore. Can now be used in [Tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). Can be requested in [Fish Pond quests](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond").
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Can be obtained from [Iridium Golems](/Iridium_Golem "Iridium Golem"). Is no longer a possible Midnight Carp Fish Pond request.
