**Solar Essence** is a [Monster Loot](/Monster_Loot "Monster Loot") item which is dropped from the [Ghost](/Ghost "Ghost"), [Iridium Bat](/Bats "Bats"), [Metal Head](/Metal_Head "Metal Head"), [Metal Head (dangerous)](/Metal_Head_(dangerous) "Metal Head (dangerous)"), [Haunted Skull](/Haunted_Skull "Haunted Skull"), [Squid Kid](/Squid_Kid "Squid Kid"), [Squid Kid (dangerous)](/Squid_Kid_(dangerous) "Squid Kid (dangerous)"), [Mummy](/Mummy "Mummy"), [Hot Head](/Hot_Head "Hot Head"), and [Blue Squid](/Blue_Squid "Blue Squid"). [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") also sells 10 a day for data-sort-value="80">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)80g each. [Sunfish](/Sunfish "Sunfish") may randomly produce a Solar Essence in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") when the pond population reaches 10 fish.

## Tailoring

Solar Essence is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [![Shirt219.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/77/Shirt219.png/24px-Shirt219.png)](/File:Shirt219.png) [Yoba Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* A Solar Essence is needed for the 3rd part of ["The Mysterious Qi" Quest](/Quests#The_Mysterious_Qi "Quests").
* 5 Solar Essence may be requested by several fish in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 7 to 10.
