**Gil** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives in the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild") in [the Mountain](/The_Mountain "The Mountain"), north of [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). He rewards players with special [rings](/Rings "Rings"), [hats](/Hats "Hats") and other valuable items when they complete [Monster Eradication Goals](/Adventurer%27s_Guild#Monster_Eradication_Goals "Adventurer's Guild").

## Schedule

The Adventurer's Guild is open from 2pm to 2am. While it's open, Gil can be found sitting in his rocking chair near the front desk.

Unlike [Marlon](/Marlon "Marlon"), Gil does not attend any [festivals](/Festivals "Festivals"), except the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival"). Gil sometimes accompanies Marlon to the [Movie Theater](/Movie_Theater "Movie Theater"), where he frequently falls asleep.

## Relationships

Gil lives in the Adventurer's Guild with [Marlon](/Marlon "Marlon").

Players cannot give gifts to raise [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points with Gil.
