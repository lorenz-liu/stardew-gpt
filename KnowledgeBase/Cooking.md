# Cooking

:   *For an index to the completed cooking collection, see [Collections#Cooking](/Collections#Cooking "Collections").*

**Cooking** is a game mechanic that becomes available after upgrading the [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") for the first time. The house upgrade adds a kitchen, which includes a stove and a refrigerator. The stove is the cooking interface, while the refrigerator provides 36 slots of storage space, similar to a [chest](/Chest "Chest"). The refrigerator is unique in that it is directly linked with the cooking interface, as if it were an extension of the player's inventory. Only [ingredients](/Cooking#Ingredients "Cooking") in the player's inventory, refrigerator or [Mini-Fridge](/Mini-Fridge "Mini-Fridge") are seen as usable items for a [recipe](/Cooking#Recipes "Cooking").

Alternatively, after reaching [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") level 3 the player can use a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit") to cook anywhere.

The player must learn each recipe before it is unlocked for cooking, with the exception of Fried Egg, which is known by the player upon starting a new save file. Locked recipes are displayed as a black silhouette of the recipe icon, with a tooltip description that says "???". Unlocked recipe icons will appear greyed-out if any of the ingredients are missing, but the tooltip will provide information regarding the recipe. Any missing ingredients will be highlighted in red. If all the ingredients required for a recipe are present either in the player's inventory or the refrigerator (or a combination of the two) the recipe icon will become fully highlighted.

## Buffs

Only one food’s [buffs](/Buffs "Buffs") can be active at a time, so eating a food with buffs will wipe out any active food buffs and replace them with the buffs from the newly eaten food. If a newly eaten food has no buffs, any existing buffs will remain in effect. Drink buffs are handled separately, so buffs from one drink ([Triple Shot Espresso](/Triple_Shot_Espresso "Triple Shot Espresso"), [Coffee](/Coffee "Coffee"), [Green Tea](/Green_Tea "Green Tea") or [Ginger Ale](/Ginger_Ale "Ginger Ale")) can be stacked with one food’s buffs.

Buff duration varies for each food and drink (see [table below](#Recipes))[[1]](#cite_note-processtime-1), but is the same for all buff effects of a given food or drink, except [Squid Ink Ravioli](/Squid_Ink_Ravioli "Squid Ink Ravioli"). Using [Qi Seasoning](#Qi_Seasoning) increases the duration by 50%.

## Achievements

There are three [Achievements](/Achievements "Achievements") associated with Cooking:

* Cook (Cook 10 different recipes)
* Sous Chef (Cook 25 different recipes)
* Gourmet Chef (Cook every recipe)

## Qi Seasoning

[Qi Seasoning](/Qi_Seasoning "Qi Seasoning"), which can be purchased from [Qi's Walnut Room](/Qi%27s_Walnut_Room "Qi's Walnut Room") for data-sort-value="10">![Qi Gem.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/78/Qi_Gem.png/24px-Qi_Gem.png) 10 for a stack of ten, makes cooked food Gold quality, increasing its health and energy values by 80% and its sell price by 50%. It will also increase the buffs given (excluding [Speed](/Speed "Speed") buffs) by one level. For example, [Seafoam Pudding](/Seafoam_Pudding "Seafoam Pudding") would give +5 fishing rather than +4 if cooked with Qi Seasoning. It will be used automatically if it is in inventory, the refrigerator, or a [Mini-Fridge](/Mini-Fridge "Mini-Fridge") when cooking. Running out of Qi Seasoning will result in a message saying: "You've used up all of your Qi Seasoning."

## Ingredients

A minimum of 88 different types of items are needed to cook all 81 dishes in the game, including:

* 26 crop products
* 13 foraged goods
* 4 tree fruits
* 2 animal products
* 4 artisan goods
* 3 miscellaneous items (maple syrup, squid ink, moss)
* 17 items obtained from fishing
* 8 crab pot items
* 5 items bought from [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store")
* 6 dishes used as ingredients

These tables assume crafted dishes will be used to make other crafted dishes. For example, 1 [Potato](/Potato "Potato") would be used to make [Hashbrowns](/Hashbrowns "Hashbrowns"), then those Hashbrowns would be used to make [Complete Breakfast](/Complete_Breakfast "Complete Breakfast"). 1 more Potato would be needed to make Hashbrowns for [Dish O' The Sea](/Dish_O%27_The_Sea "Dish O' The Sea"). When finished, the Gourmet Chef achievement would be earned for cooking every dish, but there would be no Hashbrowns because they were used to make the other dishes.

Generally, only fish that sell for less than data-sort-value="28">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)28g will be profitable to turn into [Sashimi](/Sashimi "Sashimi"). In practice, this means regular and silver quality [Periwinkle](/Periwinkle "Periwinkle") with neither the Fisher or Angler professions, and only regular quality with the Fisher profession. No fish are more profitable to turn into Sashimi with the Angler profession. If the selling or buying price of coal is factored in, the amount of fish worth turning into Sashimi will rise.

Aside from these cases, cooking for profit only becomes attractive if the ingredient production greatly exceeds the processing capacity.

## Quests

data-sort-value="100000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100,000g worth of freshly cooked items are requested in the [Qi's Cuisine](/Quests#Qi.27s_Cuisine "Quests") quest. During this quest, cooking will create dishes with the word "Fresh" at the beginning of their names (*i.e.,* Fresh Fried Egg, Fresh Omelet, etc.), which cannot stack with food items cooked before the start of the quest.

## Notes

* Every recipe searches for ingredients in the following locations, always in the same order from the *bottom right* slot to the top left slot:

1. first in the player's inventory
2. then in the main refrigerator
3. finally in any [Mini-Fridges](/Mini-Fridge "Mini-Fridge")

* When auto-sorted with the Organize button, this bottom-right-first order will automatically consume lower quality items of the same type. However, for recipes with substitutes, place ingredients accordingly as desired towards the bottom right, in order to avoid using more valuable ingredients in less valuable recipes, or in recipes that accept a wide variety of ingredients. As an example, [Tuna](/Tuna "Tuna") (which is required for [Fish Taco](/Fish_Taco "Fish Taco")) is accepted in [Sashimi](/Sashimi "Sashimi") which accepts any type of fish. However, by placing a [Mussel](/Mussel "Mussel") in the bottom right slot, the Mussel will be picked first and save the Tuna for the more exclusive and valuable recipe.
* When searching multiple [Mini-Fridges](/Mini-Fridge "Mini-Fridge"), the game picks from the last placed one in the exact chronological order in which the fridges were first placed, and then works backwards. The location of the fridge in the farmhouse does not matter.

## Bugs

* The game has a limited display area for active buffs at the top right of the screen (about the width of 3½ buff icons). For any buff icons active outside of that range, the source and timer will not be displayed when hovered over.
