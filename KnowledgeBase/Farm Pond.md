# Farm Pond

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/80/Farm_Pond.jpg/200px-Farm_Pond.jpg)](/File:Farm_Pond.jpg)

Farm Pond with Crab Pots

**Farm Pond** refers to any of the bodies of water found on the player's [farm](/The_Farm "The Farm").

On all maps, [Crab Pots](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot") may be placed in the Farm Pond and they will act as if placed in any other fresh water source. All ponds can also be used to refill [Watering Cans](/Watering_Cans "Watering Cans") and to irrigate [Rice Shoots](/Rice_Shoot "Rice Shoot") or [Taro Tubers](/Taro_Tuber "Taro Tuber").

Below is a list of [fish](/Fish "Fish") caught by [fishing](/Fishing "Fishing") on the pond of each [Farm Map](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps")[[1]](#cite_note-fish-1):

* **Standard Farm**: only [trash](/Trash "Trash").
* **Riverland Farm**:
  + 70% of the time, town river [fish](/Fish "Fish"), *i.e.,* fish that are found in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town").
  + 30% of the time, pond fish, *i.e.,* fish that are found in the [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") pond.
* **Forest Farm**:
  + 5% of the time (modified by daily [Luck](/Luck "Luck")), [Woodskip](/Woodskip "Woodskip").
  + 45% of the time, pond fish, *i.e.,* fish that are found in the [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") pond.
  + Otherwise, only trash.
* On the **Hilltop Farm**:
  + 50% of the time, forest river fish, *i.e.,* fish that are found in the [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") river.
  + 50% of the time, only trash.
* **Wilderness Farm**:
  + 35% of the time, lake fish, *i.e.,* fish that are found in [The Mountain](/The_Mountain "The Mountain") lake.
  + 65% of the time, only trash.
* **Four Corners Farm**:
  + The large pond in the southwest quadrant is similar to the Forest Farm pond: it provides a 50% chance to catch forest pond fish, *i.e.,* fish that are found in the [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") pond.
  + Only trash can be caught in the small ponds in the other three quadrants.
* **Meadowlands Farm**:
  + left pond gives you [trash](/Trash "Trash")
  + middle pond gives you [Crayfish](/Crayfish "Crayfish")
  + right pond gives you [Periwinkle](/Periwinkle "Periwinkle")
* **Beach Farm**: only [trash](/Trash "Trash") from either of the two ponds.
