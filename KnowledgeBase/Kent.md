**Kent** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). He is away serving in the army throughout the first year and returns to live in the town during spring of year two. Year one references to him include the contents of the drawer in [Jodi](/Jodi "Jodi")'s bedroom and dialogue from [Sam](/Sam "Sam") and [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent").

## Trivia

* Kent has [PTSD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-traumatic_stress_disorder "wikipedia:Post-traumatic stress disorder") from the time he's served in the war. This can be noted by his reaction to the popcorn sound in his Three Heart Event and his dislike of open fields in the Flower Dance. Kent can also explain in a dialogue to the player that his fatigue is due to difficulty sleeping, a symptom of PTSD.
  + Despite his dislike of the sound of popcorn, it is a loved concession when given to him at the [Movie Theater](/Movie_Theater "Movie Theater"). This is most likely because he still loves the snack, but if he were around while it's being cooked it would trigger his PTSD.
* The chevrons on Kent's sleeve can be interpreted in several ways. Several countries use chevrons (whether pointed upward or downward) to indicate the rank of Corporal, including [America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Army_enlisted_rank_insignia "wikipedia:United States Army enlisted rank insignia"), [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Army_enlisted_rank_insignia "wikipedia:Australian Army enlisted rank insignia"), [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_ranks_of_Brazil#NCO_and_enlisted_grades "wikipedia:Military ranks of Brazil"), [Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Defence_Forces_rank_insignia "wikipedia:Irish Defence Forces rank insignia"), and the [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Army_other_ranks_rank_insignia#Ranks "wikipedia:British Army other ranks rank insignia"). The [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranks_in_the_French_Air_and_Space_Force "wikipedia:Ranks in the French Air and Space Force"), [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Army_ranks "wikipedia:Italian Army ranks"), [Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comparative_military_ranks_of_Korea#Army_ranks_2 "wikipedia:Comparative military ranks of Korea"), and [Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%87avu%C5%9F "wikipedia:Çavuş") military forces use variations of gold (or red) chevrons to indicate the rank of Sergeant. In the [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_China_Armed_Forces_rank_insignia#Enlisted "wikipedia:Republic of China Armed Forces rank insignia") and [Japanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_ranks_and_insignia_of_the_Japan_Self-Defense_Forces#Warrant_Officer_.26_Enlisted_.EF.BC.88.E5.87.86.E5.B0.89.E3.81.8A.E3.82.88.E3.81.B3.E6.9B.B9.E5.A3.AB.EF.BC.89 "wikipedia:Military ranks and insignia of the Japan Self-Defense Forces") military forces, two chevrons below a star or other symbol indicate the rank of Private First Class. The [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranks_and_insignia_of_the_Russian_Federation%27s_armed_forces_1994%E2%80%932010 "wikipedia:Ranks and insignia of the Russian Federation's armed forces 1994–2010") military used chevrons to indicate various ranks in various branches until 2010.
* In a dialogue that occurs on Fridays, [Sam](/Sam "Sam") reveals that Kent used to be a garbage man when they lived in Zuzu City.
* In a dialogue that occurs on Mondays in Summer, Sam says that Kent took him on trips to the desert when he was younger.
* Despite the fact Kent likes eggs, he tells you during the [Egg Festival](/Egg_Festival "Egg Festival") that he isn't a big fan of eggs.

* ["A Soldier's Star](/Quests#A_Soldier.27s_Star "Quests"): Kent will send you a letter on the 15th of [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), year 2, requesting a [Starfruit](/Starfruit "Starfruit"). The reward is data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* Kent may randomly request an item at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). The reward is 3x the item's base value and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* Kent is to be gifted the [War Memento](/Quests#War_Memento "Quests") in [The Pirate's Wife](/Quests#The_Pirate.27s_Wife "Quests") quest.
