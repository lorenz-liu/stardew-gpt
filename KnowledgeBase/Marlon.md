**Marlon** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives in the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild") north of [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). He will buy any monster drops you get in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") at the counter inside [the guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild").

Players meet him the first time they enter the mines. He gifts them a [Rusty Sword](/Rusty_Sword "Rusty Sword"), and instructs them to descend into the mines exploring. Players must first complete the quest [Initiation](/Quests#Initiation "Quests") before being allowed admittance to the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild").

After purchasing the [Slime Hutch](/Slime_Hutch "Slime Hutch"), Marlon will visit the player, providing them with a [Slime Egg](/Slime_Egg "Slime Egg") to start breeding slimes.

## Schedule

The Adventurer's Guild is open from 2pm-2am. While it's open, Marlon can be found inside at the counter. He never leaves his spot behind the counter except to go to town [Festivals](/Festivals "Festivals"), when the Guild closes entirely.

## Relationships

Marlon lives in the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild") with [Gil](/Gil "Gil"), who can usually be found rocking in his chair next to the counter Marlon works at.

Players cannot give gifts to raise [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points with Marlon.

## Item Recovery Service

If the player passes out from low health in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), [Quarry Mine](/Quarry_Mine "Quarry Mine"), [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") or [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon"), Marlon will hold lost items in an "Item Recovery Service" option in the Adventurer's Guild Menu. Players may choose to purchase *one* lost item, for the same price as it would be sold through the [Shipping Box](/Shipping "Shipping"). Items will stay in Marlon's menu until the next time the player passes out from low health, when they will be replaced by any new items lost. Items are not lost if the day ends at 2am; only the gold fee is deducted.

## Quests

* Marlon randomly offers two of the following [Skull Cavern quests](/Desert_Festival#Skull_Cavern "Desert Festival") during each day of the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival"):
  + "Monster Hunt": He challenges the player to slay 10 [Serpents](/Serpent "Serpent"), [Purple Slimes](/Slime "Slime"), or [Mummies](/Mummy "Mummy"). The reward is data-sort-value="35">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 35.
  + "Deep Dive": He challenges the player to reach level 30 of the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"). The reward is data-sort-value="50">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 50.
  + "Treasure Hunt": He challenges the player to collect either 12 [Omni Geodes](/Omni_Geode "Omni Geode") or 15 [Iridium Ore](/Iridium_Ore "Iridium Ore"). The reward is data-sort-value="40">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 40.
