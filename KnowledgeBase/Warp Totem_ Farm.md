The **Warp Totem: Farm** is a [Warp Totem](/Warp_Totem "Warp Totem") that teleports the player to the farm by selecting it in inventory and right-clicking. It can be [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting") when the recipe becomes available at [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") Level 8.

## Other Sources

* 1-3 Warp Totem: Farm may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box").
* Three Warp Totem: Farm may may be received from opening a [Golden Mystery Box](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box").
* Warp Totem: Farms can be purchased at the [Casino](/Casino "Casino") for data-sort-value="1000">![QiCoin.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/6c/QiCoin.png/24px-QiCoin.png)1,000 each.
* Five Warp Totem: Farms is the reward after donating 80 items to the [Museum](/Museum "Museum").
* A Warp Totem: Farm can be won from the Crane Game inside the [Movie Theater](/Movie_Theater "Movie Theater") during any season.
* A [Blobfish](/Blobfish "Blobfish") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") has a 1.7-1.9% chance to produce five Warp Totem: Farms if the population is at least 9.
* Five Warp Totem: Farms may occasionally be found in [treasure rooms](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern") in the Skull Cavern.
* Starting [Summer](/Summer "Summer") 1 of year 1 there is a 10% chance for a Warp Totem: Farm to replace [Clay](/Clay "Clay") in an [Artifact Spot](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot").
* The [Island Trader](/Island_Trader "Island Trader") sells Warp Totem: Farms for 5 [Taro Roots](/Taro_Root "Taro Root").
* Three Warp Totem: Farm is one of the prizes from the [Trout Derby](/Trout_Derby "Trout Derby") festival.

## Bundles

The Warp Totem: Farm is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

The Warp Totem: Farm can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Totem Mask.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/cc/Totem_Mask.png/24px-Totem_Mask.png) [Totem Mask](/Totem_Mask "Totem Mask"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

The Warp Totem: Farm is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
