# Tailoring

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/4d/Sewing_Interface.png/200px-Sewing_Interface.png)](/File:Sewing_Interface.png)

The sewing machine interface.

:   *For instructions on how to dye clothing using the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") or Dye Pots, see [Dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").*

**Tailoring** involves creating new shirts, pants, skirts, shorts, and hats at the sewing machine located inside [Emily and Haley's house](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane") or the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") received as a reward for the ["Rock Rejuvenation"](/Quests#Rock_Rejuvenation "Quests") special order. Boots and shoes can also be custom-tailored, which does not create a new item, but transfers the stats from one pair to another.

Tailoring and Dyeing are unlocked in a cutscene the first sunny day after the player acquires at least 1 [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth"). [Emily](/Emily "Emily") will appear in front of the player's house and tell them that they can tailor clothes using the sewing machine at her house.

Each item requires [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed and one other item on the spool. Prismatic clothing is *random*, so Cloth + Prismatic Shard can produce any of the 5 prismatic clothing items (including duplicates).

Shirts, Pants, and [Hats](/Hats "Hats") cannot be sold anywhere in Stardew Valley. [Boots/Shoes](/Footwear "Footwear") can be sold to the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild").

## Shirts

Shirts chosen at character creation at the start of the game are not dyeable. Their corresponding tailored versions may be dyeable after being created using a sewing machine.

The [Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis") sells a random Shirt each day of the week for data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g. Shirts that can be chosen at character creation but cannot be Tailored may be purchased at the Oasis.

There is a small, fixed chance to find a random cosmetic item when the player kills a monster, breaks a crate, tills an artifact spot, or shakes or chops a tree, as well as a higher but more variable chance when panning.[[1]](#cite_note-cosmetic_item_drops-1) The possible cosmetic items include most shirts, as well as some hats. Pants and Boots are not obtainable from these drops. [Furniture](/Furniture "Furniture") may also be dropped.[[2]](#cite_note-cosmetic_item_types-2)

Note that there are three shirts called "Bandana Shirt", none of which are dyeable. The non-dyeable "Sailor Shirt" chosen at character creation is visually nearly identical to the dyeable "Sailor Shirt" that can be created with a variety of fish, and has a dyeable neckerchief.

For dyeable shirts that can be created with more than one item, the resulting color is dependent on the item used to Tailor it.

## Boots

[Boots and shoes](/Footwear "Footwear") cannot be created, but the stats can be transferred from one pair to another. To custom-tailor boots or shoes, place the item with the desired stats on the spool at the upper right and the item to be tailored in the feed at the lower left. The item on the spool will be consumed in the process. The resulting item in the feed will be called "Custom-tailored [name of shoe/boot]".

## Notes

Some items that cannot be used to tailor clothing will not be greyed out until cloth is placed in the feed. Similarly, invalid tailoring items placed on the spool will cause cloth to be greyed out.

## References

1. [↑](#cite_ref-cosmetic_item_drops_1-0) Monsters, crates, artifact spots and trees use `Utility::trySpawnRareObject` to drop cosmetic items, with fixed odds ranging from 0.066% to 0.4% depending on activity. Panning instead calls `Utility::getRandomCosmeticItem` directly, with odds controlled by `Tools/Pan::getPanItems`, ranging from 0% to 13.4% depending on pan upgrades, luck, and daily luck.
2. [↑](#cite_ref-cosmetic_item_types_2-0) The function `Utility::getRandomCosmeticItem` is used to determine the type of cosmetic item. 20% will be furniture, 20% will be from a subset of tailorable Hats, and 60% will be from a subset of Shirts, covering most shirts but with some exclusions (excluding all shirts with gendered variants, all shirts that can be chosen at character creation, the seven shirts tailored using items only available after reaching Ginger Island, Emily's Magic Shirt, Magic Sprinkle Shirt, the Prismatic Shirts with dark or light sleeves, and non-numerical IDs such as the Mystery Shirt).
3. [↑](#cite_ref-softedgepullover_3-0) One of Emily's makeover outfits (see `Data/MakeoverOutfits.xnb`) for male farmers gives a minor variant of Basic Pullover (M) with the ID `(S)SoftEdgePullover`. This is dyable like the regular Basic Pullover (M) but its sprite differs by 2 pixels, and it has a unique, non-numerical ID, and so cannot be obtained from random cosmetic drops.

## History

* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Introduced new shirts, hats, shorts, skirts, and pants. Prior to 1.4, shirts and pants could only be selected on the new game character customization menu or at [the Shrine of Illusions](/The_Shrine_of_Illusions "The Shrine of Illusions") and were not separate items. Prior to 1.4, hats were only available from the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild"), at certain festivals, from the [Abandoned House](/Abandoned_House "Abandoned House"), and in a small number of other locations.
* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): Introduced new shirts, hats, and glasses.
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): New tailoring recipes added. Certain hats and shirts can be dropped randomly by monsters, crates and barrels, trees, Artifact Spots, or panning. Placing a Staircase in the player's pants slot no longer produces Trimmed Lucky Purple Shorts.
