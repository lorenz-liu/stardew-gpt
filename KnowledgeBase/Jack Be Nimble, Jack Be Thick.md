**Jack Be Nimble, Jack Be Thick** is a power [book](/Books "Books") that can be obtained by digging [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot"). The chance for the book to drop starts at 0.8% and increases by 0.2% for each spot dug until it is dropped. Then the chance stays at 1.3% permanently. It cannot drop until the player has dug more than 2 Artifact Spots.[[1]](#cite_note-artifactspot-1)

It can also be purchased from the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") for data-sort-value="20000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)20,000g starting in Year 3 (≈9% chance to appear).[[2]](#cite_note-y3chance-2)

The first reading grants the player a power that gives them a permanent +1 ![Defense.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/66/Defense.png/24px-Defense.png) [Defense](/Defense "Defense") buff. Once read, it can be found in [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s Menu on the [Special Items & Powers](/Special_Items_%26_Powers "Special Items & Powers") tab. Each subsequent reading gives the player 100 [Combat](/Combat "Combat") [XP](/Skills "Skills").

## Bundles

Jack Be Nimble, Jack Be Thick is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Jack Be Nimble, Jack Be Thick is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a brown dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

Jack Be Nimble, Jack Be Thick is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Trading

One Jack Be Nimble, Jack Be Thick can be traded to the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") for 3 [Stuffing](/Stuffing "Stuffing"), provided the player has already obtained the power from this book.
