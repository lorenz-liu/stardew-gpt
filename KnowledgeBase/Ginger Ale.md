**Ginger Ale** is a [cooked](/Cooking "Cooking") drink. It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit").

When consumed, it grants the player +1 [luck](/Luck "Luck") for 5m. As a drink, its [buff](/Buffs "Buffs") stacks with any food buffs (including Speed), but replaces any other drink buffs (*i.e.,* from [Coffee](/Coffee "Coffee") or [Green Tea](/Green_Tea "Green Tea")).

## Tailoring

Ginger Ale is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [![Ginger Overalls.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/eb/Ginger_Overalls.png/24px-Ginger_Overalls.png)](/File:Ginger_Overalls.png) [Ginger Overalls](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a green dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Notes

* Ginger and ginger ale are common remedies for nausea. Though it is not specifically noted in-game, eating [Ginger](/Ginger "Ginger") or drinking Ginger Ale cures the [Nauseated debuff](/Buffs#Nauseated "Buffs"). This is hinted at by ![Birdie Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/97/Birdie_Icon.png/24px-Birdie_Icon.png) [Birdie](/Birdie "Birdie"), the pirate's wife who lives on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island").
