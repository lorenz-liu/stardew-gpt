The **Golden Animal Cracker** is an item that can be obtained in a variety of ways after claiming [Farming Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave").

## Locations

After claiming Farming Mastery, Golden Animal Crackers can be found by:

* 7% chance from regular [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing").[[1]](#cite_note-chestchance-1)
* 9% chance from [Golden Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Golden_Treasure_Chests "Fishing").[[1]](#cite_note-chestchance-1)
* 2% chance from [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") treasure rooms.[[2]](#cite_note-treasureroom-2)
* 0.5% chance from [Golden Mystery Boxes](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box").[[3]](#cite_note-goldmystery-3)
* ≈0.9% chance from [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot").[[4]](#cite_note-rare_object-4)
* ≈0.2% chance from shaking [trees](/Trees "Trees") with seeds.[[4]](#cite_note-rare_object-4)
* ≈0.033% chance from chopping trees (each axe hit).[[4]](#cite_note-rare_object-4)
* ≈0.15% chance from breaking [crates and barrels](/The_Mines#Crates_and_Barrels "The Mines") in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), and [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon").[[4]](#cite_note-rare_object-4)
* ≈0.15% chance from slaying [monsters](/Monsters "Monsters").[[4]](#cite_note-rare_object-4)

Daily [luck](/Luck "Luck") affects the chance to obtain a Golden Animal Cracker from some of these sources, indicated by an "≈". The exact chance of obtaining a Golden Animal Cracker from those sources is equal to the given probability multiplied by  `1 + Daily Luck`.

## Use

Once given to any animal that is not a [pig](/Pig "Pig"), the animal will produce 2 times its normal output according to its normal schedule. The Golden Animal Cracker does not speed up output. Baby animals can receive a Golden Animal Cracker and will produce double output when they mature. When giving the cracker, a music note icon will appear above the animal to indicate that the cracker has been given to them. Pigs cannot receive a Golden Animal Cracker. Attempting to give a cracker to a pig will result in a "?" appearing above the pig.

For all animals that have received a cracker, an icon of the cracker will appear when their information screen is displayed and the same icon will show for the animal on [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s Menu on the Animals tab.

A Golden Animal Cracker can also be thrown into a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), having the same effect as giving it to a farm animal. The basket holding the fish pond's produce will turn gold and an icon of the cracker will appear in the fish pond's menu when brought up.

The Golden Animal Cracker cannot be used on an animal or a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") more than once. If an animal with a Golden Animal Cracker is sold, the cracker will permanently disappear. This also applies if a Fish Pond is demolished or emptied with the “empty pond” option. While emptying a Fish Pond without clicking that option will *not* make the cracker disappear, it will then not be possible to put a different fish species in the pond.

## Bundles

Golden Animal Cracker is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Golden Animal Cracker is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a gold dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

Golden Animal Cracker is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
