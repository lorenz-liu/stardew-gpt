## Notes

* Despite being only one block wide, it is still possible to ride a [horse](/Animals#Horse "Animals") through any orientation of gate in any direction.
* Breaking the gate with a [pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes") does not destroy it, so you can break it and immediately replace it the day before it degrades.
* A gate can replace a placed fence piece by selecting the gate in inventory and clicking on the fence piece. If surrounded by other [Stone Fence](/Stone_Fence "Stone Fence") or [Hardwood Fence](/Hardwood_Fence "Hardwood Fence") pieces, the gate will take on the color of the fence instead of the normal dark brown color.

* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Two gates can now be placed next to each other, allowing for double-width gates.
