## Tailoring

Radish is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a dyeable [Tank Top](/Tailoring "Tailoring").

* Male Version: [![Shirt129.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/23/Shirt129.png/24px-Shirt129.png)](/File:Shirt129.png)
* Female Version: [![Shirt130.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/eb/Shirt130.png/24px-Shirt130.png)](/File:Shirt130.png)

It is a red dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the red dye pot at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane") for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

* The Radish may be randomly requested in [Summer](/Summer "Summer") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="270">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)270g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
