A **Radioactive Bar** is crafted by smelting 5 [Radioactive Ore](/Radioactive_Ore "Radioactive Ore") and 1 [Coal](/Coal "Coal") in a [Furnace](/Furnace "Furnace").

A Radioactive Bar can be [shipped](/Shipping "Shipping") or sold to [Clint](/Clint "Clint") at the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith") Shop for data-sort-value="3000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)3,000g (data-sort-value="4500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)4,500g with the [Blacksmith profession](/Mining#Mining_Skill "Mining")).

10 Radioactive Bars can be traded to the [Island Trader](/Island_Trader "Island Trader") for one [Galaxy Soul](/Galaxy_Soul "Galaxy Soul") on the last day of a season after killing at least 50 [dangerous monsters](/Monsters#Dangerous_Monsters "Monsters").

## Tailoring

Radioactive Bar is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create ![Radioactive Goggles.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/4d/Radioactive_Goggles.png/24px-Radioactive_Goggles.png) [Radioactive Goggles](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a lime dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the green dye pot at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

Radioactive Bar is not used in any quests.

## Trivia

* The description is most likely a reference to the often-parodied California Proposition 65 warning: *Known to the state of California to cause cancer, birth defects, or other reproductive harm.*
