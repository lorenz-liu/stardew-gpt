**Bear's Knowledge** permanently increases the sell price of [blackberries](/Blackberry "Blackberry") and [salmonberries](/Salmonberry "Salmonberry") by 3x. The sell price of [Artisan Goods](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods") made from blackberries or salmonberries is not affected.

To obtain Bear's Knowledge, the player must find [Secret Note #23](/Secret_Notes#Secret_Note_.2323 "Secret Notes"), then go to the [Secret Woods](/Secret_Woods "Secret Woods") between 6am and 7pm with [Maple Syrup](/Maple_Syrup "Maple Syrup") in inventory. A cutscene ensues, in which a bear takes the maple syrup, and thanks the player by giving his special knowledge of berries.

Once obtained, it can be found in the [Special Items & Powers](/Special_Items_%26_Powers "Special Items & Powers") tab in the [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s Menu.

## Trivia

* If the player has obtained Bear's Knowledge and also has a [telephone](/Telephone "Telephone"), they may receive a phone call from the bear.
