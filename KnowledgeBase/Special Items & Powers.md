# Special Items & Powers

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/d5/Special_Items_%26_Powers.png/300px-Special_Items_%26_Powers.png)](/File:Special_Items_%26_Powers.png)

Special Items & Powers tab

The **Special Items & Powers** tab of the in-game menu displays the special items, [book](/Books "Books") powers, and mastery powers [the Player](/The_Player "The Player") has collected.

The [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") who visits the town twice every month sells many of them, but most of them are only available starting in Year 3 and only have a ≈9% chance to appear.

## Mastery Powers

Five additional powers can be obtained by visiting the [Mastery Cave](/Mastery_Cave "Mastery Cave") after reaching level 10 in all five [skills](/Skills "Skills"). Each [mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave") requires an additional level for which increasingly more experience is required.

| Image | Name | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mastery Icon.png | [Farming Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave") | You can now find [Golden Animal Crackers](/Golden_Animal_Cracker "Golden Animal Cracker"), which permanently doubles a farm animal's produce. Doesn't work on [pigs](/Pig "Pig"). |
| Mastery Icon.png | [Mining Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave") | [Gem-bearing rocks](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining") now grant twice the gems. |
| Mastery Icon.png | [Foraging Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave") | You can now find [Golden Mystery Boxes](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box"), which contain superior items. |
| Mastery Icon.png | [Fishing Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave") | You can now encounter [Golden Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Golden_Treasure_Chests "Fishing"). |
| Mastery Icon.png | [Combat Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave") | Unlocks a new equipment slot for [trinkets](/Trinkets "Trinkets"). Trinkets can be found while adventuring and grant special powers. |
