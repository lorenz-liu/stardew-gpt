**Coral** is a [forageable](/Foraging "Foraging") item found year-round in the [Tide Pool](/The_Beach#Tide_Pools "The Beach") section of [the Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach"), *i.e.,* to the east of the wooden footbridge[[1]](#cite_note-location-1), the southwest area of [Ginger Island West](/Ginger_Island#Island_West "Ginger Island"), or in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") after activating the [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") or during the [Danger In The Deep](/Quests#Danger_In_The_Deep "Quests") quest. During [Summer](/Summer "Summer") 12-14, extra Coral appears anywhere on the beach (including possibly the western section).

The average spawn rate for Coral in the tide pool section of the beach is 0.93/day. An additional 3.2 (on average) Coral per day will spawn during Summer 12-14. These spawn rates are not affected by the presence of other forageable items on the beach; in particular, Coral are not part of the standard six item per map limit on forageable items. 7 [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") XP are granted for every Coral picked up, and all Foraging bonuses apply to Coral.

Several [Crab Pot](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot") [fish](/Fish "Fish") may produce 1 Coral in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") when the population of the pond reaches 5. It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") turtle with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals").

## Tailoring

Coral can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Strapped Top](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt139.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/66/Shirt139.png/24px-Shirt139.png)](/File:Shirt139.png) It can be also used as a red dye color at the dye pots in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Although Coral is not otherwise considered to be [fish](/Fish "Fish"), it can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where it will reproduce every 2 days. The initial pond capacity is 10 Coral; no quests are necessary to increase the pond capacity. Instead of producing [Roe](/Roe "Roe"), Coral ponds produce [Dolomite](/Dolomite "Dolomite") and [Limestone](/Limestone "Limestone"), but only once the population reaches 9.

## Quests

* One to three Coral may be requested by several fish in [Fish Pond quests](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond").
* Four Coral may be requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his fifth request, or as part of any request starting with the sixth.
