**Moss** is a [resource](/Category:Resources "Category:Resources") obtained from moss-covered trees. Each day in [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), and [Fall](/Fall "Fall"), common [trees](/Trees "Trees") can randomly grow moss. Moss grows slower on sunny summer days. Additionally, two types of [Green Rain Trees](/Green_Rain_Trees "Green Rain Trees") can grow Moss. Moss growth is accelerated for these two trees, and when covered in moss and planted near common trees accelerate moss growth on the common trees. [[1]](#cite_note-mossGrowthSource-1) The trees do not need to be felled to collect the Moss. Moss can be removed and collected with any [tool](/Tools "Tools"), [weapon](/Weapons "Weapons"), or [bomb](/Crafting#Bombs "Crafting"). One Tree drops 1 to 2 pieces of Moss.

Players can use [Vinegar](/Vinegar "Vinegar") on a tree to prevent that tree from growing Moss.

Using an [Axe](/Axes "Axes") on a tapped tree covered in Moss will remove the [Tapper](/Tapper "Tapper") and the Moss in one hit, while a [Pickaxe](/Pickaxe "Pickaxe") will remove only the tapper on the first hit. Using any other methods will harvest moss without collecting the tapper.

Moss becomes much more common during the [Green Rain](/Weather#Green_Rain "Weather") event, covering most mossy trees. This weather event also spawns exclusive [weeds](/Weeds#Special_Weeds "Weeds") that have a 10% chance to drop Moss.[[2]](#cite_note-cutweed-2) Additionally, Large Weeds spawned by the event will drop 2-3 Moss when cut.[[3]](#cite_note-resourceclump-3)

Each Moss collected gives the player 1 [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") XP.

## Moss Growth

For moss to grow on a tree, it must be a fully grown [Oak Tree](/Oak_Tree "Oak Tree"), [Maple Tree](/Maple_Tree "Maple Tree"), [Pine Tree](/Pine_Tree "Pine Tree"), or [Green Rain Tree](/Green_Rain_Trees "Green Rain Trees") Type 1 or 2.[[4]](#cite_note-mossPossibleTrees-4) No other types of trees can grow moss. The tree's [growth stage](/Trees#Growth_Cycle "Trees") must also be at least 14.

At the start of each day, all eligible trees will have a chance to grow Moss. The chance for a tree to grow moss varies depending on the weather and season. The growth chance is 10% on [Sunny](/Sunny "Sunny") days, which decreases to 3.3% if it is [Summer](/Summer "Summer"). On [Rainy](/Rain "Rain") days, the growth chance is 20%.[[1]](#cite_note-mossGrowthSource-1)

Trees will have a 50% chance to receive a 10% boost to their moss growth chance if one of the following conditions are met:

* The tree is a (non-temporary) Type 1 or Type 2 [Green Rain Tree](/Green_Rain_Trees "Green Rain Trees").
* The tree's surrounding 5x5 grid contains a (non-temporary) Type 1 or Type 2 [Green Rain Tree](/Green_Rain_Trees "Green Rain Trees") that has Moss on it.

During a [Green Rain](/Weather "Weather"), all trees that are able to grow Moss will instantly grow Moss, regardless of their growth stage.

During [Winter](/Winter "Winter"), Moss cannot grow at all (except inside the [Greenhouse](/Greenhouse "Greenhouse")), and all pre-existing Moss on trees is removed.

Collecting moss from a tree reduces its growth stage to 12 - the amount of Moss it dropped.

## Tailoring

Moss is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). Moss is a green [dye](/Dyeing "Dyeing") when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the green dye pot at [Emily's and Haley's house](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane") for use in dyeing.

## Quests

Ten Moss may be requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his fifth request, or as part of any request starting with the sixth.

## Trading

Once unlocked, the [Raccoon's wife](/Giant_Stump#Raccoon_Wife.27s_Shop "Giant Stump") at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") will trade for Moss.
