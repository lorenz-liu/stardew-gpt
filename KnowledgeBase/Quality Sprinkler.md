The **Quality Sprinkler** is a [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting") item that waters the 8 adjacent tiles every morning (a 3x3 area). Only tilled tiles will be watered. The Quality Sprinkler is unlocked at [Farming](/Farming "Farming") level 6, and is a reward for completing the ![Bundle Yellow.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2b/Bundle_Yellow.png/24px-Bundle_Yellow.png) [Summer Crops Bundle](/Bundles#Summer_Crops_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Pantry](/Bundles#Pantry "Bundles") and the ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Garden Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Garden_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Pantry (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Pantry "Remixed Bundles"). It can also randomly rotate into stock at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="450"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,350–2,250g. Four Quality Sprinklers may occasionally be found in [treasure rooms](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern") in the Skull Cavern. One Quality Sprinkler is one of the prizes from the [Trout Derby](/Trout_Derby "Trout Derby") festival. One Quality Sprinkler may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") or [Golden Mystery Box](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box"). Four Quality Sprinklers can be obtained as the seventh prize from the [Prize Machine](/Prize_Ticket#Prizes "Prize Ticket") in the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor") (33% chance).

[Sprinklers](/Crafting#Sprinklers "Crafting") cannot be used to water [Garden Pots](/Garden_Pot "Garden Pot") or a [pet's](/Animals#Pets "Animals") water bowl. Sprinklers cannot be placed on sand.

A [Torch](/Torch "Torch") can be placed on a Quality Sprinkler to provide minimal lighting in the dark.

## Upgrades

Upgrades can be attached onto a placed sprinkler. Picking up the sprinkler will remove the upgrade without destroying it.

Only one upgrade can be used at a time on a single sprinkler.
