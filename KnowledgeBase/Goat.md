The **Goat** is an [animal](/Animals "Animals") that lives in a [Big Barn](/Barn "Barn"). Goats can be purchased at [Marnie's Ranch](/Marnie%27s_Ranch "Marnie's Ranch") for data-sort-value="4000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)4,000g. Goats can become pregnant and give [birth](/Animals#Animal_Births "Animals").

## Produce

:   *Main article: [Animals#Produce](/Animals#Produce "Animals")*

Goats who eat every day mature after 5 nights have passed. A mature and fed goat produces [Goat Milk](/Goat_Milk "Goat Milk") every 2 days. Once sufficient [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals") and happiness are reached, they will produce [Large Goat Milk](/Large_Goat_Milk "Large Goat Milk"). Goat Milk can be collected using a [Milk Pail](/Milk_Pail "Milk Pail"). Milking a goat by hand increases friendship by 5 points. Alternatively, an [Auto-Grabber](/Auto-Grabber "Auto-Grabber") will automatically milk every Goat in a Barn.

Goat Milk can be placed in a [Cheese Press](/Cheese_Press "Cheese Press") to produce [Goat Cheese](/Goat_Cheese "Goat Cheese"), which sells for data-sort-value="400">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)400g or data-sort-value="600">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)600g depending on quality.

## Reproduction

Goats, like [cows](/Cow "Cow"), [sheep](/Sheep "Sheep"), and [pigs](/Pig "Pig"), have a low chance to reproduce. The more animals there are inside the same [barn](/Barn "Barn"), the higher the chance an animal will give birth.
When it occurs, the player will receive a notice at the bottom of the screen before the day starts, stating "During the night, [parent's name] gave birth to a baby goat." Other than this, there is no indication that a goat is pregnant.

## Selling

Goats can be sold. The [selling price](/Gold "Gold") is dependent on how many [hearts](/Friendship "Friendship") the animal has (data-sort-value="5200">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)5,200g max).

To sell a coop or barn animal, right-click the animal after petting. The friendship/mood interface appears. Move the cursor over the gold coin button at the right to see how much you can sell the animal for. Click that button and the confirmation that follows to complete the sale.
