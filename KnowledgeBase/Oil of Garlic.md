**Oil of Garlic** is a [drinkable crafted item](/Crafting#Edible_Items "Crafting"). The recipe is earned at [Combat](/Combat "Combat") skill level 6. The [Dwarf](/Dwarf "Dwarf") also sells Oil of Garlic for data-sort-value="3000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)3,000g each.

Drinking Oil of Garlic gives the [Oil of Garlic Buff](/Buffs#Oil_of_Garlic "Buffs") which lasts for 10 minutes (real-time). The Oil of Garlic Buff prevents enemies from spawning in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), except on [Dungeon Floors](/The_Mines#Dungeon_Floors "The Mines").[[1]](#cite_note-monsterspawn-1) In [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), it prevents floors from being [infested](/The_Mines#Infested_Floors "The Mines"), which does not include [Prehistoric Floors](/Skull_Cavern#Prehistoric_Floors "Skull Cavern")[[2]](#cite_note-floorinfested-2), and also prevents [swarms](/The_Mines#Swarms "The Mines") of monsters on any level[[3]](#cite_note-swarmspawn-3). The Buff has no effect in [Dangerous Mines](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") or [Dangerous Skull Cavern](/Quests#Skull_Cavern_Invasion "Quests").[[4]](#cite_note-excludedangerous-4)

It is a special-effect buff, meaning that drinking Oil of Garlic will not remove any buffs from other drinks, or vice-versa.

**Note**: this recipe is profitable - it will always result in profit when using the lowest quality ingredients.

## Tailoring

Oil of Garlic can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Dark Highlight Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt263a.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/91/Shirt263a.png/24px-Shirt263a.png)](/File:Shirt263a.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a green dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Oil of Garlic is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
