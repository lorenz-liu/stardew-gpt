**Trout Soup** is a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit").

It can be purchased at [Willy's Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop") for data-sort-value="250">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)250g and can randomly appear in [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus")' shop on [Saturdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Saturday "Days of the Week") for data-sort-value="50">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)50g-data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g, in [The Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon")'s rotating stock for data-sort-value="200">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)200g, or at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="100"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)300–1,000g. It can also randomly be found in the [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") outside the saloon. One Trout Soup may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box").

**Note**: this recipe is [profitable](/Cooking#Profitable_Recipes "Cooking") without the [Angler profession](/Skills#Fishing "Skills") - as then it will always result in profit when using the lowest quality ingredients.

## Tailoring

Trout Soup is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Ranger Uniform](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt241.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/13/Shirt241.png/24px-Shirt241.png)](/File:Shirt241.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").
