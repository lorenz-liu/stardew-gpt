The **Golden Scythe** is a [tool](/Tools "Tools") obtained at the end of the [Quarry Mine](/Quarry_Mine "Quarry Mine") from the statue of the Grim Reaper.

The Golden Scythe has the following benefits:

* Its radius is larger than the standard [Scythe](/Scythe "Scythe")[[1]](#cite_note-areaofeffect-1) and it is more likely to cut all grass in its radius.
* 75% chance per swipe to harvest hay from grass, up from 50%.[[2]](#cite_note-haychance-2)
* Inflicts 13 damage, up from 1-3.

The most powerful scythe, called the [Iridium Scythe](/Iridium_Scythe "Iridium Scythe"), can be unlocked once [the Player](/The_Player "The Player") has obtained [Farming Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave").
