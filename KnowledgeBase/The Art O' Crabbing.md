**The Art O' Crabbing** is a power [book](/Books "Books") that can be obtained as an iridium-tier reward on [Winter](/Winter "Winter") 12th or 13th during [SquidFest](/SquidFest "SquidFest"), provided the player has not received it from the festival before. It can also be purchased from the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") for data-sort-value="20000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)20,000g starting in Year 3 (≈9% chance to appear).[[1]](#cite_note-y3chance-1)

The first reading grants the player a power that gives [Crab Pots](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot") a 25% chance to yield double the amount of items. This stacks with the doubling effect from [Wild Bait](/Wild_Bait "Wild Bait"). Each subsequent reading gives the player 100 [Fishing](/Fishing "Fishing") [XP](/Skills "Skills"). Once read, it can be found in [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s Menu on the [Special Items & Powers](/Special_Items_%26_Powers "Special Items & Powers") tab.

## Tailoring

The Art O' Crabbing is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used as a black [dye](/Dyeing "Dyeing") when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.
