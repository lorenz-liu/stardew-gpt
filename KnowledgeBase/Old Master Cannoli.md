# Old Master Cannoli

[![](/mediawiki/images/a/a2/Old_Master_Cannoli_Anim.gif)](/File:Old_Master_Cannoli_Anim.gif)

The statue when it is gifted a sweet gem berry

The stone statue of **Old Master Cannoli** is located in the northwest corner of the [Secret Woods](/Secret_Woods "Secret Woods").

Before obtaining a [Stardrop](/Stardrop "Stardrop") from the statue, the statue reads:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| “ | “--Old Master Cannoli-- ...Still searching for the sweetest taste...” |

If the statue is gifted a [Sweet Gem Berry](/Sweet_Gem_Berry "Sweet Gem Berry"), which is grown from a [Rare Seed](/Rare_Seed "Rare Seed") first obtained from the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="200"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)600–1,000g, its mouth opens and its eyes turn red. Interacting with the statue again gives [the player](/The_Player "The Player") a Stardrop. This happens once, and then the player can no longer interact with the statue and it will return to its original form. In a [multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer") game, all players can receive a Stardrop with the use of one sweet gem berry.

## Trivia

Old Master Cannoli may be a reference to Obi-Wan Kenobi from [Star Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars "wikipedia:Star Wars"), as their names and appearances are similar.
