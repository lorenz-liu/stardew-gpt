The **Wood Sign** is a [craftable](/Crafting "Crafting") item that can display the image of any item that can be held in [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory"). An image can be placed on the sign by holding an item in inventory and right-clicking on the sign. The item will not be consumed.

Wood Signs can be placed anywhere in Stardew Valley, indoors or outdoors. If placed in a villager's path, the sign will be destroyed. See "[Crafted goods outside the farm](/Crafting#Crafted_goods_outside_the_farm "Crafting")" for maps of areas safe from villager pathing.

Placing a Wood Sign on a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") will turn it into a counter of how many [fish](/Fish "Fish") are inside, with an image of the fish visible on the sign, and the number of fish in the pond in the lower right corner.
