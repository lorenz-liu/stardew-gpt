**Milk** is an [animal product](/Category:Animal_Products "Category:Animal Products") obtained from a [Cow](/Cow "Cow") by using a [Milk Pail](/Milk_Pail "Milk Pail"). It can also be obtained from [Sandy](/Sandy "Sandy") on Saturdays, who will comment that the player looks thirsty. Additionally, it is sold by [Alex](/Alex "Alex") during the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="10">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 10 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg").

When a cow has a sufficient [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals") and [mood](/Animals#Mood "Animals") level, it can produce a [Large Milk](/Large_Milk "Large Milk"), which is more valuable.

## Tailoring

Milk is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [![Shirt071.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/f4/Shirt071.png/24px-Shirt071.png)](/File:Shirt071.png) [White Overalls Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring").

## Quests

* Milk may be randomly requested during any season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="375">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)375g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points. Milk will never be requested unless you own a [Cow](/Cow "Cow") or [Goat](/Goat "Goat") (Owning a goat will result in cow's milk being requested).
