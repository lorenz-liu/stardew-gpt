The **Duck** is an [animal](/Animals "Animals") that lives in a [Big Coop](/Coop "Coop"). Ducks can be purchased at [Marnie's Ranch](/Marnie%27s_Ranch "Marnie's Ranch") for data-sort-value="1200">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,200g or be hatched by placing a [Duck Egg](/Duck_Egg "Duck Egg") into an [Incubator](/Incubator "Incubator"), taking ![Time Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Time_Icon.png/24px-Time_Icon.png) 9,000m (5d 16h 40m) to incubate, or ![Time Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Time_Icon.png/24px-Time_Icon.png) 4,500m (2d 21h 40m) with the [Coopmaster Profession](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming").

Ducks are able to swim in bodies of water on the farm.

## Produce

:   *Main article: [Animals#Produce](/Animals#Produce "Animals")*

Ducks who eat every day mature after 5 nights have passed. A mature and fed duck produces a [Duck Egg](/Duck_Egg "Duck Egg") every 2 days, which can be placed in an [Incubator](/Incubator "Incubator") to hatch another duck, or sold for data-sort-value="95">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)95g. Once sufficient [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals") and happiness is reached, a duck may produce a [Duck Feather](/Duck_Feather "Duck Feather") instead of a Duck Egg, which sells for data-sort-value="250">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)250g.

A Duck Egg can be placed in a [Mayonnaise Machine](/Mayonnaise_Machine "Mayonnaise Machine") to produce [Duck Mayonnaise](/Duck_Mayonnaise "Duck Mayonnaise"), which sells for data-sort-value="375">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)375g.

## Selling

It is possible to sell Ducks. The amount of [gold](/Gold "Gold") obtained for doing so is dependent on how many hearts the animal has. The more hearts; the higher amount of gold fetched for selling it. The amount of gold obtained for selling a maximum-heart Duck is data-sort-value="1560">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,560g and for a recently hatched Baby Duck without any hearts data-sort-value="395">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)395g.

To sell a coop or barn animal, right-click the animal after petting. The friendship/mood interface appears. Move the cursor over the gold coin button at the right to see how much the animal can be sold for. Click that button and the confirmation that follows to complete the sale.

## Trivia

* In the game, the female, egg-laying ducks have green heads. In actuality, male [Mallard ducks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mallard "wikipedia:Mallard") have the characteristic green heads. Females are brown. However, domesticated Cayuga ducks have green feathers in both genders.
* Ducklings make the same noises as adult ducks.
* Ducklings will follow fully grown ducks around the farm.
* Ducks are unique in that if they swim on water, such as on the [Farm Pond](/Farm_Pond "Farm Pond"), [Riverland or Beach Farms](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps"), they do not need to eat [hay](/Hay "Hay") or [grass](/Grass "Grass") to be fed overnight. This is indicated by a pecking animation while the duck floats on the surface.
