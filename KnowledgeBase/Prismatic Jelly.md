**Prismatic Jelly** will always drop from killing non-special [Prismatic Slimes](/Slimes#Prismatic_Slime "Slimes"). They are only obtainable while the [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard")'s ["Prismatic Jelly"](/Quests#Prismatic_Jelly "Quests") Special Order quest is active.

Only one Prismatic Jelly is required for the Special Order, and any not handed to the Wizard will disappear once the order is complete or expired.

While the special order is active, each slime spawned in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), [Quarry Mine](/Quarry_Mine "Quarry Mine"), or [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") has a 1.2% chance to be replaced with a [Prismatic Slime](/Slimes#Prismatic_Slime "Slimes"), depending on [daily luck](/Luck "Luck"). The exact chance is `0.012 + Daily Luck / 10.0` [[1]](#cite_note-prismaticchance-1). However, the chance cannot drop below 0.01.

## Trivia

* It is possible for a Prismatic Slime to be a [special slime](/Slimes#Special_Slimes "Slimes"). In this case, the Prismatic Slime will only drop one of the [special items](/The_Mines#Special_Items "The Mines") and will not drop the intended Prismatic Jelly.
* **PC only:** Prismatic Jelly can be spawned using the [item ID glitch](/The_Player#Exploits "The Player") and gifted as a [universally hated](/Friendship#Universal_Hates "Friendship") gift.
* When killing a Prismatic Slime while wearing the [Burglar's Ring](/Burglar%27s_Ring "Burglar's Ring"), the slime will drop two Prismatic Jellies.
* Since the [Quarry Mine](/Quarry_Mine "Quarry Mine") is reset at the end of the day, instead of when leaving the floor like in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") or [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), a prismatic slime spawned there will persist for the entire day. If the player passes out from low health, the Quarry Mine can be revisited for another chance to kill the prismatic slime.
