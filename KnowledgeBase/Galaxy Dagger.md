The **Galaxy Dagger** is a [dagger weapon](/Weapons#Dagger "Weapons") that can be purchased at the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild") for data-sort-value="35000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)35,000g after obtaining a [Galaxy Sword](/Galaxy_Sword "Galaxy Sword").

It can be combined with ![Galaxy Soul.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/17/Galaxy_Soul.png/24px-Galaxy_Soul.png) [Galaxy Soul](/Galaxy_Soul "Galaxy Soul") (3) and ![Cinder Shard.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/fd/Cinder_Shard.png/24px-Cinder_Shard.png) [Cinder Shard](/Cinder_Shard "Cinder Shard") (60) in the [Forge](/Forge "Forge") to create an ![Infinity Dagger.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/d6/Infinity_Dagger.png/24px-Infinity_Dagger.png) [Infinity Dagger](/Infinity_Dagger "Infinity Dagger").
