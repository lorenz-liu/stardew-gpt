The **Treasure Totem** is a [crafted item](/Crafting#Consumables "Crafting") that spawns up to 16 [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") in a circle surrounding the player when used on tillable ground outdoors. The circle spawns 3 spaces each to the north, east, south, and west, as well as 1 space each to the northeast, southeast, southwest, and northwest. If an obstacle blocks any of the 16 tiles, that Artifact Spot will not appear. The crafting recipe is obtained from claiming [Foraging Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave").

## Bundles

Treasure Totem is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

A Treasure Totem can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create the ![Totem Mask.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/cc/Totem_Mask.png/24px-Totem_Mask.png) [Totem Mask](/Totem_Mask "Totem Mask"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a brown dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

Treasure Totem is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Tips

Using a Treasure Totem in the [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") Dig Site with a Generous Hoe Enchantment can yield 3000g of treasure on average. This is done by converting 5x [Omni Geode](/Omni_Geode "Omni Geode") to an [Artifact Trove](/Artifact_Trove "Artifact Trove") at the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") and opening them at the [blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith").
