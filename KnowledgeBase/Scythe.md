The **Scythe** is a [tool](/Tools "Tools") used to cut [grass](/Grass "Grass"), [weeds](/Weeds "Weeds"), young [trees](/Trees "Trees"), and [moss](/Moss "Moss"). It is also required for harvesting certain crops (*i.e.,* [Amaranth](/Amaranth "Amaranth"), [Kale](/Kale "Kale"), [Wheat](/Wheat "Wheat")). One Scythe is included in every player's [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory") at the start of the game. Similar to using a [Sword](/Sword "Sword"), equipping and using the Scythe will quickly swing it in a small radius in front of the player without consuming any [energy](/Energy "Energy").

If the player has a [Silo](/Silo "Silo"), cutting each plot of Grass growing on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") has a 50% chance of adding one piece of [Hay](/Hay "Hay") to the silo, or two pieces of Hay if cutting down [Blue Grass](/Grass#Blue_Grass "Grass").

A Scythe may be used as a [weapon](/Weapons "Weapons") to inflict a small amount of damage against [monsters](/Monsters "Monsters").

The Scythe cannot be upgraded at the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith"), but better scythes may be found. The [Golden Scythe](/Golden_Scythe "Golden Scythe"), a more powerful scythe, is found in the [Quarry Mine](/Quarry_Mine "Quarry Mine"), accessible after the [Quarry](/Quarry "Quarry") is unlocked. The most powerful scythe, the [Iridium Scythe](/Iridium_Scythe "Iridium Scythe"), is received once [the Player](/The_Player "The Player") has obtained [Farming Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave").
