The **Bookseller** is a shop that sells [books](/Books "Books"). It appears twice every [season](/Seasons "Seasons") on random dates marked by a balloon [![Bookseller Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/4/4a/Bookseller_Icon.png)](/File:Bookseller_Icon.png) on the [calendar](/Calendar "Calendar"). The shop sells a random assortment of books each time. The player can also trade in their books here. The Bookseller is located above the [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart")/[Movie Theater](/Movie_Theater "Movie Theater").

On the days the Bookseller visits, at the start of the day there will be a message saying "The Bookseller is in town today". The Bookseller is open all day.

## Visiting Days

The Bookseller visits twice every [season](/Seasons "Seasons") randomly on the following possible dates:[[1]](#cite_note-dates-1)

| Season | Possible days |
| --- | --- |
| Spring.png [Spring](/Spring "Spring") | 11, 12, 21, 22, 25 |
| Summer.png [Summer](/Summer "Summer") | 9, 12, 18, 25, 27 |
| Fall.png [Fall](/Fall "Fall") | 4, 7, 8, 9, 12, 19, 22, 25 |
| Winter.png [Winter](/Winter "Winter") | 5, 11, 12, 19, 22, 24 |
