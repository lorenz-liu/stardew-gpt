The **Spook Fish** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught in the submarine ride at the [Night Market](/Night_Market "Night Market"), which takes place at [The Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") during [Winter](/Winter "Winter") 15-17. It can also be caught by using [Magic Bait](/Magic_Bait "Magic Bait") in the southwest corner of the beach, from the left-most pier, in the southwestern corner.

In the submarine ride at the Night Market, there is a 16% chance to hook a Spook Fish (18% if using a [Curiosity Lure](/Curiosity_Lure "Curiosity Lure")). If using Magic Bait in the southwest corner of the beach, there is a 3.3% chance to hook a Spook Fish (10.2% if using a Curiosity Lure).[[1]](#cite_note-requirements-1)

## Tailoring

Spook Fish can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Fishing Hat.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/d8/Fishing_Hat.png/24px-Fishing_Hat.png) [Fishing Hat](/Fishing_Hat "Fishing Hat"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a blue dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Spook Fish can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 4 days. The initial pond capacity is 3 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing three quests. The primary output is blue Spook Fish [Roe](/Roe "Roe"), but [Treasure Chests](/Treasure_Chest "Treasure Chest") are also possible starting at population 9.
