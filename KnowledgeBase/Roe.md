**Roe** are fish eggs that are produced by [fish](/Fish "Fish") in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"). They can also be found in [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") after obtaining the power from the [Jewels Of The Sea](/Jewels_Of_The_Sea "Jewels Of The Sea") book (25% chance).[[1]](#cite_note-chest-1)

The selling price of Roe depends on the fish's base sell price. The equation is `30 + (base fish sell price / 2)`, rounded down to the next nearest integer. The health and energy gained from eating roe are the same for all types of roe.

All types of roe can be placed in a [Preserves Jar](/Preserves_Jar "Preserves Jar") to create a higher-value [Artisan Good](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods"): [Sturgeon](/Sturgeon "Sturgeon") Roe creates [Caviar](/Caviar "Caviar"); other roe create fish-specific varieties of [Aged Roe](/Aged_Roe "Aged Roe").

Roe does not belong to any category and therefore it does not benefit from any Profession. It also cannot be sold to [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"), but it can be sold to the [Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop") or placed in the [Shipping Bin](/Shipping "Shipping"). At the [Stardew Valley Fair](/Stardew_Valley_Fair "Stardew Valley Fair"), Roe is considered uncategorised (trash), and gives 2-3 points for judging.

## Roe Types

All fish in Fish Ponds produce roe except for the two varieties of Squid (which produce [Squid Ink](/Squid_Ink "Squid Ink") instead). Of the two non-fish that can be put in Fish Ponds, [Sea Urchins](/Sea_Urchin "Sea Urchin") produce roe, but [Coral](/Coral "Coral") doesn't.

However, after obtaining the power from the [Jewels Of The Sea](/Jewels_Of_The_Sea "Jewels Of The Sea") book, all fish roes, including [Squid](/Squid "Squid") and [Midnight Squid](/Midnight_Squid "Midnight Squid") roes, may be found in [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing"). Note that Sea Urchin Roe is not included.

The price and color of each roe is dependent upon the fish. In general, the color matches the [dye](/Dyeing "Dyeing") color of the fish;[[2]](#cite_note-roe_color-2) [Sturgeon](/Sturgeon "Sturgeon") are an exception and have black-colored roe, which is used as a blue dye in Dye Pots. Orange is used for any fish that does not have a dye color, although this is not the same color as the generic Roe image, which is a mix of orange and pink eggs. Despite the variety of displayed colors, all types of roe create orange dye.

## Tailoring

Roe can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Polka Dot Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt272.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/5/57/Shirt272.png/24px-Shirt272.png)](/File:Shirt272.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").
