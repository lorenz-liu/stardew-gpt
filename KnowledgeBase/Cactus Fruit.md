The **Cactus Fruit** is found via [foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") in [the Calico Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert"). It can also be bought at the [Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis") on Tuesdays for data-sort-value="150">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150g or randomly at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="75"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)225–1,000g. It can randomly be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") after unlocking The Desert. The [Statue Of Endless Fortune](/Statue_Of_Endless_Fortune "Statue Of Endless Fortune") produces one Cactus Fruit per year on [Sam](/Sam "Sam")'s birthday, [Summer 17](/Summer "Summer").

Cactus Fruit may also be grown from [Cactus Seeds](/Cactus_Seeds "Cactus Seeds"). Cactus Fruit grown from Cactus Seeds benefits from the [Tiller Profession](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming") bonus.

## Tailoring

Cactus Fruit is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a dyeable [80's Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring").

* Female version: [![Shirt136.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/31/Shirt136.png/24px-Shirt136.png)](/File:Shirt136.png)
* Male version: [![Shirt152.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/48/Shirt152.png/24px-Shirt152.png)](/File:Shirt152.png)

It is a green dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the green dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

* Cactus Fruit may be randomly requested after the player has completed the [Vault Bundles](/Bundles#Vault "Bundles") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="225">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)225g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* 3 Cactus Fruit may be requested by [Sandfish](/Sandfish "Sandfish") or [Scorpion Carp](/Scorpion_Carp "Scorpion Carp") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 3 to 5.
