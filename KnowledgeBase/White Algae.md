**White Algae** is an item which can be caught with a [Fishing Pole](/Tools#Fishing_Poles "Tools") during all seasons at any underground location (Floors 20 & 60 of [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), [the Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers"), the [Mutant Bug Lair](/Mutant_Bug_Lair "Mutant Bug Lair"), or the [Witch's Swamp](/Witch%27s_Swamp "Witch's Swamp")).

White Algae can also be dropped by several [Monsters](/Monsters "Monsters"): [Grubs](/Grub "Grub"), [Mutant Grubs](/Mutant_Grub "Mutant Grub"), [Flies](/Cave_Fly "Cave Fly"), [Mutant Flies](/Mutant_Fly "Mutant Fly"), [Bugs](/Bug "Bug"), and Red, Purple, Copper, and Iron [Slimes](/Slimes "Slimes"). A [Ghostfish](/Ghostfish "Ghostfish") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") may produce 5 White Algae when the population of the pond reaches 9.

White Algae is displayed in the [Fish section](/Collections#Fish "Collections") of the [collection](/Collections "Collections") tab, and counts towards the four fishing [Achievements](/Fishing#Achievements "Fishing").[[1]](#cite_note-quasi_fish-1) However, it is otherwise not considered to be a fish, and therefore cannot be sold to [Willy](/Willy "Willy") at the [Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop"), does not benefit from fish price bonuses, cannot be used in place of "Any Fish" in [recipes](/Cooking#Recipes "Cooking"), and cannot be put in a [Bait Maker](/Bait_Maker "Bait Maker"), [Fish Smoker](/Fish_Smoker "Fish Smoker"), or [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond").

## Tailoring

White Algae can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [![Shirt248.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/ad/Shirt248.png/24px-Shirt248.png)](/File:Shirt248.png) [Collared Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring").

## Quests

* Five White Algae are requested by [Ghostfish](/Ghostfish "Ghostfish") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 5 to 7.
* Five White Algae may be requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his third request, or as part of any request starting with the sixth.
