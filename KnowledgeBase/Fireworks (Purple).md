**Fireworks (Purple)** are an item that can be purchased from [Qi's Walnut Room](/Qi%27s_Walnut_Room "Qi's Walnut Room") for data-sort-value="1">![Qi Gem.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/78/Qi_Gem.png/24px-Qi_Gem.png) 1 or from the [Casino](/Casino "Casino") for data-sort-value="200">![QiCoin.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/6c/QiCoin.png/24px-QiCoin.png)200. When used, the firework is placed on the ground. After a short delay, it goes into the air and creates purple sparkles in the shape of a star. The player's screen is also briefly tinted purple.

## Bundles

Fireworks (Purple) are not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Fireworks (Purple) can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Party Hat (blue).png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/fe/Party_Hat_%28blue%29.png/24px-Party_Hat_%28blue%29.png) [Party Hat (blue)](/Party_Hat_(blue) "Party Hat (blue)"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a purple dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a purple dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

Fireworks (Purple) are not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
