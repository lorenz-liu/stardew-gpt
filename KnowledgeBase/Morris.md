**Morris** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who works at [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart") in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). He's the manager and also acts as the customer service representative. He'll offer to sell the player a [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart") membership for data-sort-value="5000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)5,000g. If the player chooses to purchase this membership a [Joja Warehouse](/Joja_Warehouse "Joja Warehouse") will replace the abandoned [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center").

If the player completes the Community Center, the local JojaMart goes out of business and Morris leaves town.

## Schedule

While [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart") is open he can be found just inside to the right. The store's hours of operation are 9am to 11pm everyday.

## Gifts

Players cannot give gifts to raise [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points with Morris.
