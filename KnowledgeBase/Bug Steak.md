## Tailoring

Bug Steak is used in the spool of a [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create ![Goggles.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/1a/Goggles.png/24px-Goggles.png) [Goggles](/Goggles "Goggles"). It can be also used as a red dye color at the dye pots in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Bug Steak is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Trivia

Most food recovers [health](/Health "Health") using the formula 45% × [energy](/Energy "Energy"). Bug Steak, [Life Elixir](/Life_Elixir "Life Elixir"), and the [Stardrop](/Stardrop "Stardrop") are the only exceptions. Bug Steak restores health of 68% × energy.
