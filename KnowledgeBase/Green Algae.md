**Green Algae** is an item that can be caught using a [Fishing Pole](/Tools#Fishing_Poles "Tools") during all seasons at any freshwater location, with the exception of the [Farm Pond](/Farm_Pond "Farm Pond") on the [Standard Farm](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps"). It is never caught in [Crab Pots](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot").

It is also dropped from [Green Slimes](/Slimes "Slimes") (10% chance), and may be produced in [Slimejack](/Slimejack "Slimejack"), [Crayfish](/Crayfish "Crayfish"), [Snail](/Snail "Snail"), or [Periwinkle](/Periwinkle "Periwinkle") [Fish Ponds](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"). It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") cat with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals"). It can also be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can").

Green Algae is displayed in the [Fish section](/Collections#Fish "Collections") of the [collection](/Collections "Collections") tab, and counts towards the four fishing [Achievements](/Fishing#Achievements "Fishing").[[1]](#cite_note-quasi_fish-1) However, it is otherwise not considered to be a fish, and therefore cannot be sold to [Willy](/Willy "Willy") at the [Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop"), does not benefit from fish price bonuses, cannot be used in place of "Any Fish" in [recipes](/Cooking#Recipes "Cooking"), and cannot be put in a [Bait Maker](/Bait_Maker "Bait Maker"), [Fish Smoker](/Fish_Smoker "Fish Smoker"), or [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond").

## Tailoring

Green Algae can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [![Shirt248.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/ad/Shirt248.png/24px-Shirt248.png)](/File:Shirt248.png) [Collared Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a green dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* Green Algae may be randomly requested during any season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="45">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)45g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* 2-3 Green Algae may be requested by [Bullhead](/Bullhead "Bullhead"), [Carp](/Carp "Carp"), or [Largemouth Bass](/Largemouth_Bass "Largemouth Bass") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 3 to 5.
