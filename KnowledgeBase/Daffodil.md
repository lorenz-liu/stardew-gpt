The **Daffodil** is found via [foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") in the [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), or grown from [Spring Seeds](/Spring_Seeds "Spring Seeds"). Up to ten can also be purchased from [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre") at the [Flower Dance](/Flower_Dance "Flower Dance") for data-sort-value="50">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)50g each. Up to five may be purchased from [Evelyn](/Evelyn "Evelyn")'s [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="15">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 15 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg") each. It can randomly be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during Spring. The [Statue Of Endless Fortune](/Statue_Of_Endless_Fortune "Statue Of Endless Fortune") produces one Daffodil per year on [Sandy](/Sandy "Sandy")'s birthday, [Fall 15](/Fall "Fall"). Although it is a flower in its description (and in real life), the game classifies it as "Forage" and not "Flower."

## Healing

The Daffodil is edible, but does not increase [Energy](/Energy "Energy") or [Health](/Health "Health").

## Tailoring

Daffodil is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [![Shirt067.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/e2/Shirt067.png/24px-Shirt067.png)](/File:Shirt067.png) [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Daffodil may be randomly requested in [Spring](/Spring "Spring") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="90">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)90g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
