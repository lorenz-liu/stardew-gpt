The **Largemouth Bass** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught in the [mountain lake](/The_Mountain "The Mountain") during all seasons. It comprises approximately 19% to 20% of all catches there during [Spring](/Spring "Spring") and [Fall](/Fall "Fall"), but drops to around 15% during [Summer](/Summer "Summer") and [Winter](/Winter "Winter"). It is most likely to be caught when casting farther from shore.[[1]](#cite_note-spawnrate-1) It can also be caught on the [Wilderness Farm](/Farm_Maps#Wilderness "Farm Maps"). It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") cat with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals"). It may be randomly found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can"), or at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="100"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)300–1,000g. [Linus](/Linus "Linus") may randomly send a Largemouth Bass in the mail as a gift.

Largemouth Bass can be caught regardless of time of day using [Magic Bait](/Magic_Bait "Magic Bait").

## Tailoring

Largemouth Bass can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Fishing Vest](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt157.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/0e/Shirt157.png/24px-Shirt157.png)](/File:Shirt157.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a green dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Largemouth Bass can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 3 days. The initial pond capacity is 3 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing three quests. The only possible output is green Largemouth Bass [Roe](/Roe "Roe").

## Quests

* [Jodi](/Jodi "Jodi") requests a Largemouth Bass in the ["Fish Casserole" quest](/Quests#Fish_Casserole "Quests") once you've reached 4 hearts with her. The reward is a cutscene at Jodi's house.
* **Item Delivery:** One Largemouth Bass may be randomly requested during any season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="300">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)300g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* **Fishing:** [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius") or [Willy](/Willy "Willy") may randomly request 1-4 Largemouth Bass during any season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="100">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100g per fish. (You get to keep the fish).
* **Aquatic Overpopulation:** [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius") may request 10 Largemouth Bass to be caught in [Spring](/Spring "Spring") at the [Special Orders board](/Quests#List_of_Special_Orders "Quests") outside the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor") for a reward of gold equivalent to selling the fish and the [Farm Computer](/Farm_Computer "Farm Computer") recipe.
* Two Largemouth Bass may be requested by [Dorado](/Dorado "Dorado"), [Lingcod](/Lingcod "Lingcod"), or [Pike](/Pike "Pike") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 5 to 7.
