The **Cow** is an [animal](/Animals "Animals") that lives in a [Barn](/Barn "Barn"). Cows can be purchased at [Marnie's Ranch](/Marnie%27s_Ranch "Marnie's Ranch") for data-sort-value="1500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,500g. Cows come in two varieties: White and Brown. When purchasing a cow, the game decides randomly whether you will get a white or brown cow (50% chance). Cows can become pregnant and give [birth](/Animals#Animal_Births "Animals").

## Produce

:   *Main article: [Animals#Produce](/Animals#Produce "Animals")*

Cows who eat every day mature after 5 nights have passed. A mature and fed cow produce [Milk](/Milk "Milk") every day. Milk does not vary depending on the color of the Cow, unlike [Eggs](/Egg "Egg"). Once sufficient [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals") and happiness is reached, they will produce [Large Milk](/Large_Milk "Large Milk"). Milk can be collected using a [Milk Pail](/Milk_Pail "Milk Pail"). Milking a cow by hand increases friendship by 5 points. Alternatively, an [Auto-Grabber](/Auto-Grabber "Auto-Grabber") will automatically milk every cow in a Barn.

Milk can be placed in a [Cheese Press](/Cheese_Press "Cheese Press") to produce [Cheese](/Cheese "Cheese"), which sells for data-sort-value="230">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)230g or data-sort-value="345">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)345g depending on quality.

## Reproduction

Cows, like [pigs](/Pig "Pig"), [sheep](/Sheep "Sheep"), and [goats](/Goat "Goat"), have a low chance to reproduce and can produce baby cows. The more animals there are inside the same [barn](/Barn "Barn"), the higher the chance an animal will give birth.
When it occurs, the player will receive a notice at the bottom of the screen before the day starts, stating "During the night, [parent's name] gave birth to a baby cow." Other than this, there is no indication that a cow is pregnant.

## Selling

Cows can be sold. The [selling price](/Gold "Gold") is dependent on how many [hearts](/Animals#Friendship "Animals") the animal has (data-sort-value="1950">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,950g max).

To sell a coop or barn animal, right-click the animal after petting. The friendship/mood interface appears. Move the cursor over the gold coin button at the right to see how much you can sell the animal for. Click that button and the confirmation that follows to complete the sale.
