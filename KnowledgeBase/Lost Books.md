# Lost Books

[![](/mediawiki/images/f/f3/Lost_Book.png)](/File:Lost_Book.png)

Lost Book

**Lost Books** are items that can be found by using a [hoe](/Hoes "Hoes") to dig up [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot"). They can also be found in [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") or by [tilling](/Hoes "Hoes") soil in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") or [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"). There are 21 Lost Books total.

Lost Books are automatically added to the bookcase shelves in the [Museum](/Museum "Museum"), and can be read by examining individual tiles of the bookcases. They often provide information related to [gameplay](/Category:Gameplay "Category:Gameplay").

At the game's start, the Library has only a single title in its shelves: "Tips on Farming" (found below with the list of lost books). The other books are always found in the order in which they are listed in the following table.

## Notes

* The description for a Lost Book can be seen if it is obtained in a [Fishing Treasure Chest](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing"): "Writings from a wide variety of time periods."
* The last book contains a secret. What appears to be gibberish is actually a set of acrostics where the first letters of each word spell out the cryptic directions to finding three [Secret Statues](/Secrets#Locked_Boxes "Secrets") located around Pelican Town:

| **Details** |
| --- |
| * **Super Cucumber Town** * **Duck Mayo Saloon** * **Strange Bun Toybox**   A different secret statue will be received as a reward for depositing each required item into its respective locked box hidden throughout town. For example, placing [Duck Mayonnaise](/Duck_Mayonnaise "Duck Mayonnaise") into a hidden purple box in the back of the [Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon") will give the secret item [??Pinky Lemon??](/%3F%3FPinky_Lemon%3F%3F "??Pinky Lemon??"). |
