# Fertilizer

:   *See also: [Crop Growth Calendars](/Crop_Growth_Calendars "Crop Growth Calendars")*

**Fertilizer** is used to support [crop](/Crops "Crops") and [tree](/Trees "Trees") growth, assisting [farming](/Farming "Farming"). There are three types of crop fertilizer, affecting respectively crop quality, growth speed and water retention. There is only one tree fertilizer type, affecting growth speed. A fertilizer is used by placing it on tilled soil or the desired tree. Some crop fertilizers must be placed before the planted seed sprouts, the other fertilizers can be used at any time during plant growth. Each crop fertilizer type has three tiers, and the tree fertilizer has only one tier. Crop fertilizers affect all crops, including crops grown from [Wild Seeds](/Crafting#Seeds "Crafting").

## Mechanics

All fertilizer can be [crafted](/Crafting#Fertilizer "Crafting"). Recipes for first tier fertilizers are learned once the required level of [Farming](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming") or [Foraging](/Foraging#Foraging_Skill "Foraging") skill is reached. Second tier recipes are learned once the required level of [Farming](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming") skill is reached. Third tier recipes are purchased on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island"). First and second tier crop fertilizers can also be purchased at [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") year-round, or at [The Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis") on specific [days of the week](/Days_of_the_Week "Days of the Week").

Only one type of fertilizer may be used per soil tile. Fertilizer remains in the soil all season. When the season changes, fertilizer normally disappears. However, there are two exceptions in which fertilizer will remain in the soil:

* If a multi-season [crop](/Crops "Crops") (*i.e.,* [Ancient Fruit](/Ancient_Fruit "Ancient Fruit"), [Coffee Bean](/Coffee_Bean "Coffee Bean"), [Corn](/Corn "Corn"), [Sunflower](/Sunflower "Sunflower"), or [Wheat](/Wheat "Wheat")) is planted in the fertilized tile, and the new season is one in which the crop grows, the fertilizer will remain.
* Fertilizer placed in the [Greenhouse](/Greenhouse "Greenhouse") will remain in the soil unless it is un-tilled by a pickaxe.

*Note* that crop quality is determined at harvest time and not at planting time. Prior to 1.5, fertilizer used in the [Greenhouse](/Greenhouse "Greenhouse") on crops which would mature out of their normal growing season might disappear before harvest and have no effect.

Speed-Gro affects only the growth and first harvest of a multi-harvest crop. Quality fertilizers ([Basic Fertilizer](/Basic_Fertilizer "Basic Fertilizer"), [Quality Fertilizer](/Quality_Fertilizer "Quality Fertilizer"), and [Deluxe Fertilizer](/Deluxe_Fertilizer "Deluxe Fertilizer")) affect all harvests of a multi-harvest crop, but if the crop produces multiple fruits or vegetables *per harvest* only one of the items will have improved quality. For example, when a blueberry crop, planted with quality fertilizer, produces a second harvest, and three blueberries are harvested from it, then only one will have improved quality.

Removing a crop with a [Tool](/Tools "Tools") like the [axe](/Axes "Axes") does not remove the fertilizer. A second hit with a tool, such as a [Pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes") is required to remove the fertilizer and make the tile fallow.

For the complete formula, please see [Farming](/Farming#Complete_Formula_2 "Farming").

## Fertilizer Cost

:   *See also: [When to turn fish into Quality Fertilizer](/Quality_Fertilizer#When_to_turn_fish_into_Quality_Fertilizer "Quality Fertilizer")*

With the exception of [Quality Fertilizer](/Quality_Fertilizer "Quality Fertilizer"), crafting Fertilizer is always cheaper than purchasing it.

Each time you craft [Speed-Gro](/Speed-Gro "Speed-Gro") or [Deluxe Speed-Gro](/Deluxe_Speed-Gro "Deluxe Speed-Gro"), you receive 5 fertilizers. Each time you craft [Quality Retaining Soil](/Quality_Retaining_Soil "Quality Retaining Soil"), you receive 2 fertilizers, though crafting just one would still be cheaper than purchasing it.
