The **Salmon** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught in the river (in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") or [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest")) during [Fall](/Fall "Fall"). It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") cat with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals"). It may also randomly be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during Fall, or at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="75"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)225–1,000g.

It can be caught in the waterfalls in southern [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") during Fall at any time.[[1]](#cite_note-waterfall-1)

Salmon can be caught regardless of time of day or season using [Magic Bait](/Magic_Bait "Magic Bait").

## Tailoring

Salmon is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Faded Denim Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt279.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/94/Shirt279.png/24px-Shirt279.png)](/File:Shirt279.png) It can also be used as a red dye in the dye pots, located in [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s and [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Salmon can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 2 days. The initial pond capacity is 3 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing three quests. The only possible output is light red Salmon [Roe](/Roe "Roe").

## Quests

* **Item Delivery:** One salmon may be randomly requested in [Fall](/Fall "Fall") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="225">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)225g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* **Fishing:** [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius") or [Willy](/Willy "Willy") may randomly request 1-4 salmon during [Fall](/Fall "Fall") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="75">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)75g per fish. (You get to keep the fish).
* **Aquatic Overpopulation:** [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius") may request 10 Salmon to be caught in [Fall](/Fall "Fall") at the [Special Orders board](/Quests#List_of_Special_Orders "Quests") outside the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor") for a reward of gold equivalent to selling the fish and the [Farm Computer](/Farm_Computer "Farm Computer") recipe.
