The **Wild Plum** is a [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") found via [foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") throughout Stardew Valley in the [Fall](/Fall "Fall"). It can also be grown from [Fall Seeds](/Fall_Seeds "Fall Seeds") and can be found at any time (randomly) in the [Farm Cave](/The_Cave "The Cave") if the fruit bat option is chosen. It can also randomly be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during Fall.

## Tailoring

Wild Plum can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [![Farmer Pants.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/78/Farmer_Pants.png/36px-Farmer_Pants.png)](/File:Farmer_Pants.png) [Farmer Pants](/Tailoring#Pants "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a purple dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* The Wild Plum may be randomly requested in [Fall](/Fall "Fall") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="240">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)240g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.

## History

* [1.0](/Version_History#1.0 "Version History"): Introduced.
* [1.1](/Version_History#1.1 "Version History"): Labeled as a fruit, allowing it to be made into [wine](/Wine "Wine") and [jelly](/Jellies_and_Pickles "Jellies and Pickles").
* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Changed from Forage to Fruit in Brazilian-Portuguese, Chinese, German, Japanese, Spanish, and Russian. Can now be used in [Tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring").
* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): Is now an option in the Forager's Bundle.
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Forager's Bundle now only requires 15 Wild Plums instead of 20.
