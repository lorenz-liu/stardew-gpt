# Speed

There are two distinct **Speed** statistics:

* [![Speed w.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/26/Speed_w.png/24px-Speed_w.png)](/File:Speed_w.png) **Weapon Speed** refers to how long it takes to hit an enemy with a weapon.
* [![Speed.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/94/Speed.png/24px-Speed.png)](/File:Speed.png) **Player Speed** refers to how fast the player moves.

Each can be affected in a variety of ways.

## Speed w.png Weapon Speed

**Weapon Speed** refers to how long it takes to hit an enemy with a weapon, and how long it takes to recover before hitting again.

By default with +0 Weapon Speed, [swords](/Weapons#Sword "Weapons") take 400 milliseconds[[1]](#cite_note-defaultSpeed-1) per action and [clubs](/Weapons#Club "Weapons") take 720 milliseconds[[2]](#cite_note-baseClubSpeed-2) per action (equivalent to -8 buff). [Daggers](/Weapons#Dagger "Weapons") are much faster than swords. [Slingshots](/Weapons#Slingshot "Weapons") take 300 milliseconds[[3]](#cite_note-GetRequiredChargeTime-3) per action and cannot be modified.

Each point of Weapon Speed reduces the time taken by 40 milliseconds,[[4]](#cite_note-millisecondsPerSpeedPoint-4) meaning the maximum effective buff is +10 for swords, though the practical performance limit may be lower (maximum effective buff of around +4 for swords or +12 for clubs).

This also means that at first, Weapon Speed has a significantly smaller effect on clubs because the total increase in damage per second (when striking continuously) is smaller as a percentage: a sword with +1 Weapon Speed is capable of 11% more damage per second, whereas a club with +1 Weapon Speed is only capable of 6% more damage per second. As buff increases, swords hit the practical performance limit faster, and clubs continue improving with higher buff.

The player's movement speed buff (described below) is also applied to weapons,[[5]](#cite_note-setFarmerAnimating-5) and effectively stacks with the Weapon Speed buff. For example, a weapon with speed +2 would be just as fast as a weapon with speed +1 while under the effects of [Coffee](/Coffee "Coffee") for an additional +1.

### Forge

Weapon Speed can be increased further by using an [Emerald](/Emerald "Emerald") at the [Forge](/Forge "Forge") up to 3 times, for a maximum of +7 additional speed.

Some tools can similarly be [enchanted](/Forge#Tool_enchantments "Forge") using a [Prismatic Shard](/Prismatic_Shard "Prismatic Shard") to be 33% faster.

## Speed.png Player Speed

**Player Speed** refers to how fast the player moves across the world.

The player's base speed is:

* 2 when walking
* 5 when running
* 6.6 when riding a [Horse](/Animals#Horse "Animals") (7 if the horse was fed a [carrot](/Carrot "Carrot") that day)

A variety of positive and negative [Buffs](/Buffs "Buffs") can change the player speed, as detailed under [Food and Drink](#Food_and_Drink), [Buffs](#Buffs), and [Permanent Buffs](#Permanent_Buffs).

Speed is also affected by some [terrains](#Terrain), but not when riding a horse.

Although terrain and/or debuffs may decrease a player speed, the speed is never decreased below 1.

### Terrain

Three types of terrain can alter the player's speed, but only if the player is not riding a horse.

* **[Flooring](/Crafting#Decor "Crafting")** has a +0.1 Speed boost, but only when located outside on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm").
* **[Grass](/Grass "Grass")** has a -1 Speed penalty.
* **[Crops](/Crops "Crops")** have a -1 Speed penalty, but only if the player is running. Seeds do not count as crops.

Reading [Ol' Slitherlegs](/Ol%27_Slitherlegs "Ol' Slitherlegs") grants a power which will reduce the Speed penalty to -0.33 while moving through either [Grass](/Grass "Grass") or [Crops](/Crops "Crops").
