The **Iridium Scythe** is a tool that is obtained after claiming [Farming Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave").

The Iridium Scythe has the following benefits:

* Can harvest items grown on tilled soil within its swing range, including regular [crops](/Crops "Crops"), those grown in [Garden Pots](/Garden_Pot "Garden Pot"), [Spring Onions](/Spring_Onion "Spring Onion"), and other forageables grown from [Wild Seeds](/Crafting#Seeds "Crafting").
* Its radius is larger than the [Golden Scythe](/Golden_Scythe "Golden Scythe")[[1]](#cite_note-weaponsData-1) and it is more likely to cut all grass in its radius.
* 100% chance per swipe to harvest hay from grass, up from 75%.[[2]](#cite_note-hayChance-2)
* Inflicts 20 damage, up from 13.[[1]](#cite_note-weaponsData-1)

It is the most powerful version of the Scythe. It increases the effectiveness of the [Scythe](/Scythe "Scythe") and [Golden Scythe](/Golden_Scythe "Golden Scythe") by a substantial amount and adds a purple hue.
