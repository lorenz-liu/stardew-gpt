# The Cave

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/eb/Cave_Entrance.png/200px-Cave_Entrance.png)](/File:Cave_Entrance.png)

The entrance to the cave near the [Greenhouse](/Greenhouse "Greenhouse").

**The Farm Cave** is an underground location accessed through a passage near the northern edge of [The Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"). Initially, it is just a small empty cave. After [the player](/The_Player "The Player") accumulates data-sort-value="25000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)25,000g in total earnings, [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius") will visit the farm in the morning and inform the player that he would like to use the cave to set up research specimens. He'll give the player the option to choose between fruit bats (which leave [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") in the cave) or [mushrooms](/Foraging "Foraging"). This choice is permanent and cannot be reversed.

## Fruit bats

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a2/CaveFruitBats.png/200px-CaveFruitBats.png)](/File:CaveFruitBats.png)

Interior of the Fruit Bat cave

Fruit bats leave various fruit in the Cave on the farm overnight. This is a great option for making [Artisan Goods](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods") as it gives a slow but steady supply of [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits"), particularly foraged berries, even out of their respective seasons. It also helps complete the ![Bundle Purple.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a1/Bundle_Purple.png/16px-Bundle_Purple.png) [Artisan Bundle](/Bundles#Artisan_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Pantry](/Bundles#Pantry "Bundles") in the first year or two, before the player has enough money to buy all the necessary [Fruit Trees](/Fruit_Trees "Fruit Trees").

[Fruit Tree](/Fruit_Trees "Fruit Trees") fruits make excellent gifts for villagers because they are universal likes, readily accessible early in the game, and do not require a [kitchen](/Cooking "Cooking"). Gifting may be the best late-game use for fruit, other than making [Artisan Goods](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods"), [Shipping](/Shipping "Shipping") them, finishing the [Bundles](/Bundles "Bundles"), or eating them for extra energy (even though they don't replenish very much health).

Every night, there is a chance of new fruit spawning. If the cave is empty, there is a 48% chance of no new fruit; 25% chance of 1 fruit; 13% chance of 2 fruit; 7% chance of 3 fruit; 7% chance of 4 or more fruit.[[1]](#cite_note-fruitchances-1) The average spawn rate is 1.06 fruit per night. There is no hard limit on the maximum number of fruit in one night, other than the available floor space (50 tiles).

Fruit spawned in the cave always remains there until the player collects it (the cave contents are never automatically reset, even at the end of the week or the end of the month). Uncollected fruit reduces the space available for new fruit to spawn. For example, ten uncollected fruit increases the chances of no new fruit from 48% to 54%. Placing equipment or [craftable flooring](/Crafting#Decor "Crafting") in the cave also reduces the space available for fruit.

Fruit found in the cave can be of any quality (normal, silver, gold, or iridium). If the [Botanist Profession](/Foraging#Foraging_Skill "Foraging") is chosen, the fruit will be iridium quality. The [Gatherer](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") profession gives the player a chance of obtaining two fruit. Each fruit obtained from the cave gives the player 7 [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") experience points.

## Mushrooms

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/02/CaveMushrooms.png/200px-CaveMushrooms.png)](/File:CaveMushrooms.png)

Mushrooms in the cave.

Six boxes will be added to the cave where certain types of [mushrooms](/Foraging "Foraging") will randomly grow. Five different types will appear, including all four that are required to craft the [Life Elixir](/Life_Elixir "Life Elixir"). Even though [Common Mushrooms](/Common_Mushroom "Common Mushroom"), [Morels](/Morel "Morel"), and [Chanterelles](/Chanterelle "Chanterelle") are the ones that most commonly appear, this is still the best way to craft the elixir as often as possible. This makes the Mushroom option a great one for those who love to go into [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), especially since most mushrooms can be hard to find otherwise. This is also a good choice for players that have grown, or plan to grow, fruit trees and do not need the extra [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") provided by the fruit bats. After the [Seed Maker](/Seed_Maker "Seed Maker") recipe is unlocked, the Common Mushrooms can also be processed into [Fall Seeds](/Fall_Seeds "Fall Seeds"), and then crafted into [Tea Saplings](/Tea_Sapling "Tea Sapling") to either plant or sell.

Cave mushroom boxes produce every second day.[[2]](#cite_note-mushroomtime-2) Mushrooms grown in the cave are always of normal quality, even if the [Botanist Profession](/Foraging#Foraging_Skill "Foraging") is chosen. Each mushroom collected awards the player with 5 [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") experience points.

The mushroom cave comes with a free [Dehydrator](/Dehydrator "Dehydrator").

## Notes

* [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius") will not visit the farm to open the Cave on [Green Rain](/Weather#Green_Rain "Weather") day.
* One of the stats on the [Farm Computer](/Farm_Computer "Farm Computer") is whether items are ready to be collected in the farm cave.

## References

1. ↑ [1.0](#cite_ref-fruitchances_1-0) [1.1](#cite_ref-fruitchances_1-1) Fruit spawning is controlled in the game code by `FarmCave::DayUpdate`. The code loops repeatedly, with a 34% chance of the loop terminating on each iteration. Otherwise, the spawn location is randomly selected from one of 90 tiles. Fruit only spawns if the selected tile is a valid location. 40 of the tiles are never valid (in the cave walls); existing fruit, equipment, or flooring in the cave will also make tiles invalid.
2. [↑](#cite_ref-mushroomtime_2-0) Mushroom processing times are controlled by the game code in `Object::DayUpdate` `case 128`.
3. [↑](#cite_ref-mushroomchances_3-0) See `Object::DayUpdate` in the game code.
