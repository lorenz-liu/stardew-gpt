The **Walleye** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught in the river (in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") or [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest")), the [mountain Lake](/The_Mountain "The Mountain"), and the [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") pond during [Fall](/Fall "Fall") when it is [raining](/Weather "Weather"). It can also be caught in [Winter](/Winter "Winter") if a [Rain Totem](/Rain_Totem "Rain Totem") is used. It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") cat with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals"). It can randomly be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during Fall and Winter, or at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="105"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)315–1,000g. If the player chooses the [Riverland Farm](/Farm_Maps#Riverland "Farm Maps") or the [Forest Farm](/Farm_Maps#Forest "Farm Maps"), Walleye can also be caught in the farm pond.

Walleye can be caught regardless of weather, time, or season using [Magic Bait](/Magic_Bait "Magic Bait").

Walleye is most common in the Cindersap Forest pond. Every cast in the pond during raining Fall days has a 32% chance of hooking a Walleye.

## Tailoring

Walleye can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Fishing Vest](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt157.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/0e/Shirt157.png/24px-Shirt157.png)](/File:Shirt157.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Walleye can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 3 days. The initial pond capacity is 3 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing three quests. The only possible output is yellow Walleye [Roe](/Roe "Roe").
