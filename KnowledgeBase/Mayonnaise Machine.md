The **Mayonnaise Machine** is a type of [Artisan Equipment](/Crafting#Artisan_Equipment "Crafting") used to make [Artisan Goods](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods"). It takes eggs and turns them into various kinds of [Mayonnaise](/Mayonnaise "Mayonnaise").

## Products

:   *See also: [Animal Products Profitability](/Animal_Products_Profitability "Animal Products Profitability")*

Regular white or brown chicken [eggs](/Egg "Egg") produce normal quality Mayonnaise, and [Large white or brown chicken eggs](/Large_Egg "Large Egg") produce gold-quality mayonnaise. A single [Ostrich Egg](/Ostrich_Egg "Ostrich Egg") will produce 10 jars of Mayonnaise, with the quality of the Mayonnaise equal to the quality of the Ostrich Egg used. A [Golden Egg](/Golden_Egg "Golden Egg") will produce three gold-quality Mayonnaise.

## History

* [1.0](/Version_History#1.0 "Version History"): Introduced.
* [1.1](/Version_History#1.1 "Version History"): Added [Void Mayonnaise](/Void_Mayonnaise "Void Mayonnaise") recipe.
* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Added [Dinosaur Mayonnaise](/Dinosaur_Mayonnaise "Dinosaur Mayonnaise") recipe. Mayonnaise Machines can now be stacked in a player's [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory") and in [chests](/Chest "Chest").
* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): Added [Ostrich Egg](/Ostrich_Egg "Ostrich Egg") and [Golden Egg](/Golden_Egg "Golden Egg") recipe.
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Mayonnaise is now edible. Mayonnaise Machines that are ready-to-harvest now show the quality star of the Mayonnaise.
