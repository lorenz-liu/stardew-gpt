The **Ms. Angler** is one of the five [Legendary Fish](/Fish#Legendary_Fish_II "Fish") that must be caught for [Mr. Qi](/Mr._Qi "Mr. Qi")'s [Extended Family Quest](/Quests#Extended_Family "Quests"). It has the same sell price, healing effect, location, and chance of hooking as the [Angler](/Angler "Angler"). It can only be caught when the quest is active, but more than one can be caught per quest.

## Map

Ms. Angler can be found in the river in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") north of [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart").

## Tailoring

Ms. Angler serves as a red dye color in the sewing machine and dye pots, both located at [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s and [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s house,
[2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Ms. Angler can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"). The pond capacity is 1 fish and this cannot be increased. The only possible output is Ms. Angler [Roe](/Roe "Roe").
