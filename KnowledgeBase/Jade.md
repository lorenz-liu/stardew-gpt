**Jade** is a [Mineral](/Minerals "Minerals") that can be found in [Jade Nodes](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining") and [Gem Nodes](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining") in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") (floors 40+), destroying barrels and crates (floors 40+), dropped by Blue [Slimes](/Slimes "Slimes") and [Dwarvish Sentries](/Dwarvish_Sentry "Dwarvish Sentry"), in [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing"), or received as a gift at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"). Jade may also occasionally be found in a [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") after the player has reached the bottom of the mines.

The [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") in the [Calico Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") will trade one [Staircase](/Staircase "Staircase") in exchange for one Jade on [Sunday](/Days_of_the_Week#Sunday "Days of the Week").

## Bundles

Jade is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Forging

Jade can be used at the [Forge](/Forge "Forge") to increase the critical hit damage of a melee weapon.

## Recipes

Jade is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Jade is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a ![Shirt147.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/6e/Shirt147.png/24px-Shirt147.png) [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a green dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* Jade may be randomly requested during any season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="600">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)600g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points. Jade will never be requested until you have reached floors 40+ of [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines").
* Two Jade may be requested by several fish in [Fish Pond quests](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond").
* Jade will be requested by [Emily](/Emily "Emily") as part of the "Rock Rejuvenation" quest at the [Special Orders](/Quests#List_of_Special_Orders "Quests") board outside Mayor [Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis")' house.
