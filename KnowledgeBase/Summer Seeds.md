**Summer Seeds** are a type of [Crafted](/Crafting#Seeds "Crafting") seed. The crafting recipe is labeled "Wild Seeds (Su)", but when crafted, the result is "Summer Seeds." Mature plants yield, at random, a [Grape](/Grape "Grape"), [Spice Berry](/Spice_Berry "Spice Berry"), or [Sweet Pea](/Sweet_Pea "Sweet Pea").

Summer Seeds are one of the season-specific crafted wild seeds, along with [Spring Seeds](/Spring_Seeds "Spring Seeds"), [Fall Seeds](/Fall_Seeds "Fall Seeds"), and [Winter Seeds](/Winter_Seeds "Winter Seeds") that produce crops normally found as [forage](/Foraging "Foraging"). While Farming Skill normally increases the chance to grow a quality crop, Foraging Skill increases the chance to grow a quality crop from Summer Seeds. Choosing the [Botanist profession](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") will ensure iridium-quality crops grown from Summer Seeds.

Crows do not attack crops grown from Summer Seeds, so the use of [Scarecrows](/Scarecrow "Scarecrow") is unnecessary.

## Sources

* [Crafting](/Crafting "Crafting") (each recipe produces 10 Summer Seeds)
* Placing a [Spice Berry](/Spice_Berry "Spice Berry") in a [Seed Maker](/Seed_Maker "Seed Maker") (yield: 1-3 Summer Seeds)
* Completing the ![Bundle Yellow.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2b/Bundle_Yellow.png/24px-Bundle_Yellow.png) [Summer Foraging Bundle](/Bundles#Summer_Foraging_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Crafts Room](/Bundles#Crafts_Room "Bundles") (reward: 30 Summer Seeds)
* [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") (pricedata-sort-value="55"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)165–1,000g)
* [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") on [Saturdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Saturday "Days of the Week") in exchange for 2 [Fall Seeds](/Fall_Seeds "Fall Seeds")
* [Treasure rooms](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern") in the Skull Cavern (5-20 each)

## Bugs

On [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island"), mature Summer Seeds may destroy the tilled soil (and fertilizer), leaving untilled soil in their place after harvest.
