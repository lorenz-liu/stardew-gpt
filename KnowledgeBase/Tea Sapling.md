The **Tea Sapling** is a seed that takes 20 days to grow into a [Tea Bush](/Tea_Bush "Tea Bush"). A Tea Bush produces one [Tea Leaves](/Tea_Leaves "Tea Leaves") item each day of the final week (days 22-28) of [spring](/Spring "Spring"), [summer](/Summer "Summer"), and [fall](/Fall "Fall") (and [winter](/Winter "Winter") if indoors).

Tea Saplings are planted like [Trees](/Trees "Trees") by placing them onto untilled soil or into [Garden Pots](/Garden_Pot "Garden Pot"). They do not need to be watered in order to grow. Tea Saplings will not die and will even grow if planted outside in winter.

A Tea Sapling can be purchased from the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart"), though not often. Up to 10 Tea Saplings may be purchased from Caroline's [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="10">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 10 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg"). Otherwise, it must be crafted. The crafting recipe is received via mail the day after [Caroline](/Caroline "Caroline")'s [2-Heart](/Caroline#Heart_Events "Caroline") event.

## Notes

* Crafting and selling a Tea Sapling is always more profitable than selling its ingredients. The exact profit depends on the source of the Wild Seeds but is typically between data-sort-value="75">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)75g and data-sort-value="200">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)200g. Using less profitable forage is more profitable for making Tea Saplings.
* The Wild Seeds used for crafting do not have to be from the same season.
* It is possible to use tea bushes as permanent fences, to help contain farm animals within a barnyard.
