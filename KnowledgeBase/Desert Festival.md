# Desert Festival

[![Desert Festival.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/f3/Desert_Festival.png/500px-Desert_Festival.png)](/File:Desert_Festival.png)

The **Desert Festival** is a [festival](/Festivals "Festivals") that takes place every year in [the Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") on [Spring](/Spring "Spring") 15th, 16th, and 17th. The festival officially starts at 10am each day, and requires the Bus to be repaired in order to visit the festival. [Pam's schedule](/Pam#Schedule "Pam") as bus driver varies from her usual schedule.

[Sandy](/Sandy "Sandy")'s shop will not be open during the festival; however, the player may still enter the [Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis"). The [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") is still present and her shop is accessible.

Several challenges and attractions offer the ability to earn this festival's currency, ![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg"). Calico Eggs are physical items within the game that can be used to purchase items from the various shops during the Desert Festival. Eggs carry over from day to day. However, they will disappear from chests or inventory at the end of the festival and are not saved for the following year.

The eggs can be sold in the [Shipping Bin](/Shipping "Shipping"), but they sell for data-sort-value="0">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)0g.

## Attractions

### The Races

Throughout the day, players may bet on races. If a player's chosen contestant wins, they will receive data-sort-value="20">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 20. Each day, the first winning bet a player makes on Escar-go, the slowest contestant, will instead earn the player data-sort-value="100">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 100.[[1]](#cite_note-racewin-1) Players may place bets from 10am, when the festival opens, until the start of the last race just after 10pm.[[2]](#cite_note-racebet-2) The race will not run its course if the player is not in the main festival area, so players *must* wait for it to finish to see the results.

The player can also cheat the race by giving the Suspicious Man next to the Oasis data-sort-value="1">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 1 in exchange for one of the race contestants of the player's choosing possibly losing the race.

The 5 possible contestants are Speed Rooster, King Sting, Shoebiscuit, Escar-go, and Cactus Crawler. Only 3 contestants will race each day.

Players are able to walk into the Villager Shops through the back, meaning these villagers are still available for gifting, talking, or quest completion while at their posts.

### Emily's Outfit Services

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/03/61_total_makeover_outfits.png/200px-61_total_makeover_outfits.png)](/File:61_total_makeover_outfits.png)

34 gender-neutral, 13 male-only, and 14 female-only

Every day, Emily and Sandy offer the player a random new outfit with a short cutscene in which Emily will pick the clothing that "embodies the player". The player's original outfit is placed in their inventory. If the player tries to re-enter the hut in which the clothing service happens, the player will be blocked from entering, with Emily saying that the player looks great in their new outfit. On days where Emily runs a Villager Shop, Sandy will take over after a bit of hesitation.

There are 61 total makeover outfits: 34 gender-neutral, 13 male-only, and 14 female-only.[[5]](#cite_note-emilyoutfit-5) Each outfit consists of a shirt, a pant, and possibly a hat. Of note, the [Dark Ballcap](/Dark_Ballcap "Dark Ballcap"), [Joja Cap](/Joja_Cap "Joja Cap"), and [Laurel Wreath Crown](/Laurel_Wreath_Crown "Laurel Wreath Crown") can be obtained with three of the gender-neutral outfits. In addition, the [Tight Pants](/Tight_Pants "Tight Pants") can be obtained from a few outfits, and a "soft edge" variant of [Basic Pullover (M)](/Tailoring#Shirts "Tailoring") can be obtained from one of the male-only outfits.

### Chef

[![Chef Desert Festival.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/86/Chef_Desert_Festival.png/200px-Chef_Desert_Festival.png)](/File:Chef_Desert_Festival.png)

The Chef gives the player a choice of ingredients to make them a meal with varying effects depending on the ingredients chosen. The player may do so repeatedly and choose different ingredients each time. The meal will be eaten; any effects will be applied immediately and last for 7 minutes.[[6]](#cite_note-answerdialogue-6) The buffs from dishes are a combination of buffs from both ingredient choices; e.g., Crystal Cake grants both ![Mining.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/41/Mining.png/24px-Mining.png) [Mining](/Mining "Mining") (+3) and ![Luck.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/f0/Luck.png/24px-Luck.png) [Luck](/Luck "Luck") (+1) buffs.

Any dish will restore the player to maximum health and energy.[[6]](#cite_note-answerdialogue-6) In addition, these dishes **will** stack with any food or drink [buffs](/Buffs "Buffs") from the player.

### Harvey's Medical Station

If the player loses all Health in the Skull Cavern and is knocked out, they will awaken at Harvey's "shop" located in the festival next to the Cavern entrance. The player will awaken with 10 Health, and they will not lose any additional Energy or [Gold](/Gold "Gold"). The player will keep their items, but they will lose 20% of the Calico Eggs in their inventory.[[19]](#cite_note-eggslost-19) Talking to Harvey will result in the same dialogue as before the player died.

### Scholar

The Scholar asks the player a series of four questions. If all four answers are correct, then the reward is data-sort-value="50">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 50. The player has one chance per day to achieve this. After a successful round of questions, the player must wait until next year to receive another reward. Questions are randomized every time they are asked.

One of the questions asks the player to recall their total number of steps taken. Three options are given. The order of the options is randomized, but the numerically middle value will always be correct.[[23]](#cite_note-scholarmiddle-23)
