The **Bamboo Pole** is a [tool](/Tools "Tools") used to catch [fish](/Fish "Fish"). One is obtained from [Willy](/Willy "Willy") in the cutscene following the completion of the [To The Beach](/Quests#To_The_Beach "Quests") quest. Afterwards, it can be purchased from his [Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop") for data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g. It cannot have [bait](/Bait "Bait") or [tackle](/Tackle "Tackle") attached to it.
