**Wheat** is a [vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from [Wheat Seeds](/Wheat_Seeds "Wheat Seeds") after 4 days. Wheat is harvested using a [Scythe](/Scythe "Scythe") or better. Wheat has a 40% chance to also drop [hay](/Hay "Hay") when harvested with any Scythe.[[1]](#cite_note-1)

In addition to [Beer](/Beer "Beer"), players have the ability to craft [Wheat Flour](/Wheat_Flour "Wheat Flour") from Wheat by using a [Mill](/Mill "Mill"). Once placed in the mill, one wheat will make one wheat flour. Wheat flour will appear in the box on the right side of the mill the following morning.

## Multi-Seasonal Crop

Wheat may be planted in both Summer and Fall. There is no difference in its behavior in either season.

If a Wheat plant (of any growth stage) is in the ground on the 28th of Summer, it will continue growing on the 1st of Fall as though nothing had changed. [Fertilizer](/Fertilizer "Fertilizer") in the ground underneath a Wheat plant on Summer 28 will not disappear on Fall 1. Fully-grown wheat plants may be left unharvested until the 1st of Fall to prevent fertilizer and tilled soil from disappearing.

## Tailoring

Wheat is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Crop Tank Top](/Tailoring "Tailoring").

* Male version: [![Shirt132.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/48/Shirt132.png/24px-Shirt132.png)](/File:Shirt132.png)
* Female version: [![Shirt133.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a6/Shirt133.png/24px-Shirt133.png)](/File:Shirt133.png)

It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* Wheat may be randomly requested in [Summer](/Summer "Summer") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="75">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)75g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* A fully-grown and unharvested Wheat is required for [Gourmand Frog's puzzle](/Ginger_Island#Gourmand_Frog "Ginger Island") on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island").

## Tips

* Since wheat grows quickly and can drop [hay](/Hay "Hay"), it may be beneficial to devote some farmland to growing it, even if the main focus of the farm is raising animals.
