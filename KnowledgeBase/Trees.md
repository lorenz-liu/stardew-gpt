# Trees

:   *This article is about non-fruit trees. For Fruit Trees, see the [Fruit Trees](/Fruit_Trees "Fruit Trees") page.*

**Trees** that do not yield fruit are of two types: [common](#Common_Trees) and [special](#Special_Trees). Common trees ([Maple](/Maple_Tree "Maple Tree"), [Oak](/Oak_Tree "Oak Tree"), [Pine](/Pine_Tree "Pine Tree"), and [Mahogany](/Mahogany_Tree "Mahogany Tree")) are valuable natural resources, being the primary source of multiple products: [Wood](/Wood "Wood") for construction and crafting, [Sap](/Sap "Sap"), [Maple Syrup](/Maple_Syrup "Maple Syrup"), [Oak Resin](/Oak_Resin "Oak Resin"), and [Pine Tar](/Pine_Tar "Pine Tar"). Special trees have their own unique characteristics.

## Common Trees

### Harvesting

A **mature tree** (stage 5) can be shaken for a seed (see [Growth Cycle](#Growth_Cycle)), [tapped](/Tapper "Tapper") for syrup, or felled by chopping it repeatedly with an [Axe](/Axes "Axes"). When chopped down, trees always fall left or right away from the player (if applicable) and produce wood or seeds in the direction of the fall. When chopped from above or below, the player should try to stand off-center on the tile to clearly ensure the direction. If the tree is adjacent to a wall, hug the wall to ensure it falls into playing space. If the tree falls into or near water, the items it drops may disappear into the water. High [Magnetism](/Magnetism "Magnetism") is very helpful for quickly collecting the widely-scattered products. The products (not including those from the stump) are:[[1]](#cite_note-treeproducts-1)

* ![Wood.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/df/Wood.png/24px-Wood.png) [Wood](/Wood "Wood") (12-16), *except* Mahogany trees. The possible 4 bonus pieces of wood are randomly added, based on [Luck](/Luck "Luck") and [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") skill. For players with the [Forester](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") profession, the amounts are 25% larger (*i.e.,* 15-20 pieces). If a player has read [Woody's Secret](/Woody%27s_Secret "Woody's Secret"), there is a 5% chance to double the amount of wood dropped (*i.e.,* 24-32 pieces normally or 30-40 pieces with the profession).
* ![Hardwood.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/ed/Hardwood.png/24px-Hardwood.png) [Hardwood](/Hardwood "Hardwood") (10) for Mahogany trees. For players with the [Forester](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") profession, the amounts are 25% larger (*i.e.,* 12 or 13 pieces).
* ![Sap.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/73/Sap.png/24px-Sap.png) [Sap](/Sap "Sap") (5)
* ![Maple Seed.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/36/Maple_Seed.png/24px-Maple_Seed.png) [Maple Seed](/Maple_Seed "Maple Seed") (0-2), ![Acorn.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/cd/Acorn.png/24px-Acorn.png) [Acorn](/Acorn "Acorn") (0-2), ![Pine Cone.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/90/Pine_Cone.png/24px-Pine_Cone.png) [Pine Cone](/Pine_Cone "Pine Cone") (0-2) or ![Mahogany Seed.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/84/Mahogany_Seed.png/24px-Mahogany_Seed.png) [Mahogany Seed](/Mahogany_Seed "Mahogany Seed") (0-1), depending on tree species. These are in addition to the 1 possible ["loose" seed](#Growth_Cycle) that may fall when shaking the tree. All seeds are only possible if [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") skill is at least 1.
* ![Hardwood.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/ed/Hardwood.png/24px-Hardwood.png) [Hardwood](/Hardwood "Hardwood"), if the player has the [Lumberjack](/Foraging#Foraging_Skill "Foraging") profession. The amount of wood is random: 50% chance for 0 pieces; 25% 1; 12.5% 2; 6.25% 3; 3.125% 4; etc. (without any fixed maximum number).
* 12 [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") experience points.

When tapped, a tree cannot be chopped down or shaken for a seed, unless the tapper is first removed. (However, if the tree is moss-covered, you can shake it without removing the tapper by using a scythe or weapon on the tree.)

A **stump** is left behind after a mature tree is chopped down. Removing the stump with an [Axe](/Axes "Axes") yields:[[2]](#cite_note-stumpproducts-2)

* ![Wood.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/df/Wood.png/24px-Wood.png) [Wood](/Wood "Wood") (4-9)
  + In single-player, 5 is the minimum, plus 4 bonus pieces are randomly added, based on [Luck](/Luck "Luck") and [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") skill. For [Foresters](/Skills#Foraging "Skills"), the total amount is 6-11 wood.
  + In multiplayer, the amount is fixed at 4 wood (5 for Foresters)
* ![Sap.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/73/Sap.png/24px-Sap.png) [Sap](/Sap "Sap") (1)
* 1 [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") experience point.

At **stage 4**, tree seedlings can be chopped down with an [Axe](/Axes "Axes"). Products are:[[3]](#cite_note-seedlingproducts-3)

* ![Wood.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/df/Wood.png/24px-Wood.png) [Wood](/Wood "Wood") (4) (5 for [Foresters](/Skills#Foraging "Skills"))

At **stages 2 and 3**, tree seedlings can be removed using an [Axe](/Axes "Axes"), [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes"), [Pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes"), [Scythe](/Scythe "Scythe"), or [Weapon](/Weapons "Weapons"). If an Axe is not used, no products are dropped.[[4]](#cite_note-axeOnSeedling-4)

* ![Wood.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/df/Wood.png/24px-Wood.png) [Wood](/Wood "Wood") (1) may be dropped if an Axe is used. The percent chance is ten times the player's Foraging level (*i.e.,* guaranteed at level 10), including [Buffs](/Buffs "Buffs").

At **stage 1**, tree seeds can be removed from the ground using an [Axe](/Axes "Axes"), [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes"), or [Pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes"). The only product is the seed.

### Moss

[Moss](/Moss "Moss") is found growing on [Maple Trees](/Maple_Tree "Maple Tree"), [Oak Trees](/Oak_Tree "Oak Tree"), [Pine Trees](/Pine_Tree "Pine Tree"), and two types of [Green Rain Trees](/Green_Rain_Trees "Green Rain Trees") during all seasons apart from Winter. Moss is on most trees during the [Green Rain](/Weather#GreenRain "Weather") weather event. Trees do not have to be chopped down to harvest Moss; it can be harvested with one use of any tool. Each tree will drop 1-2 Moss.

### Growth Cycle

Each day, there is a 5% chance that a mature tree will generate a "loose" seed. This seed will be dropped if the player shakes the tree. Normally the loose seed matches the mature tree species, with one exception: for the last two weeks of [Fall](/Fall "Fall"), shaking a Maple Tree produces a [Hazelnut](/Hazelnut "Hazelnut") instead of a [Maple Seed](/Maple_Seed "Maple Seed").[[5]](#cite_note-fallHazelnut-5) Note that the player must reach [Foraging Level 1](/Foraging#Foraging_Skill "Foraging") and sleep to view the level-up screen before seeds from chopping down trees will be available. Seeds from shaking trees become available as soon as the player levels up.

In addition, mature trees (excluding stumps) on the farm have a 15% chance each night of trying to spontaneously plant one seed in the ground.[[6]](#cite_note-treeDropSeed-6) One tile is randomly chosen, up to 3 tiles away from the parent tree in any direction. The seed becomes "planted", creating a new stage 1 tree, only if the tile is a valid location for a new tree. In particular, the chosen tile must be empty; trees will not destroy paths or crops.

Planted seeds grow through four stages before reaching maturity. Tree seeds do not require watering, nor do they require the surrounding land to be empty (unlike [Fruit Trees](/Fruit_Trees "Fruit Trees")): the land can be fully paved; trees can be planted next to water or other features. As long as the seed can be placed in the ground, it can grow. The only limitation on tree seedling growth is that two mature trees cannot be side by side: a seedling will never grow past stage 4 if there is a mature tree in any of its eight adjacent tiles.

With [Tree Fertilizer](/Tree_Fertilizer "Tree Fertilizer"), trees will grow one stage every night, except at stage 4, which takes two days. In total, a fertilized seed takes five days to reach maturity. Fertilized seeds and seedlings will grow even in [Winter](/Winter "Winter"). Otherwise, with the exception of trees in [the Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") and on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island"), trees do not grow at all in winter.

Without fertilizer, seedlings have a 20% chance each night of growing to the next stage, except at stage 4, where seedlings spend twice as long. The median growth time from seed to maturity is 24 days, although individual tree growth times will vary considerably. Statistically, 90% of seeds should reach maturity in 38 days (excluding winter), and 99% in 55 days.

Having reached stage 5 maturity, the tree continues growing up to stage 15 with the base change of 20% for the purpose of [Moss](/Moss "Moss") growing. Additionally, if it has a fully grown [Green Rain Tree (Type 1 or 2)](/Green_Rain_Trees "Green Rain Trees") in a 5x5 square centered around itself, it has an additional check of 50% for a growth stage increase after stage 5. [[7]](#cite_note-mossGrowthStage-7).
Collecting [Moss](/Moss "Moss") reduces the growth stage to 12 - amount of moss dropped in the stack. [[8]](#cite_note-mossCollectStageReduction-8).

### Outside the Farm

Common trees in [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest"), [The Mountain](/The_Mountain "The Mountain"), [Railroad](/Railroad "Railroad"), [Quarry](/Quarry "Quarry"), [Bus Stop](/Bus_Stop "Bus Stop") and [Backwoods](/Backwoods "Backwoods") can all be chopped down, tapped, or shaken for a seed, as can the trees in [Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") east of the river. The trees in Town west of the river cannot be chopped down or tapped, but can be shaken for a seed.

Outside the farm, trees never spontaneously spread planted seeds. However, the player can plant seeds in any tillable ground (as long as the ground is un-tilled), which will go through the standard growth stages.

Any pre-existing trees that are chopped down outside the farm, and the stumps removed, have a 20% chance per night of reappearing as a stage 3 seedling (even in winter). After reappearing, it will grow as normal into a mature tree. If the stump is not removed, the tree will not regrow.

### Lightning

Unlike [Fruit Trees](/Fruit_Trees "Fruit Trees"), which produce [Coal](/Coal "Coal") for 3-4 days after being hit by lightning, common trees hit by lightning are destroyed, leaving only a stump (and some wood or sap) behind.

## Special Trees

Special trees have uncommon properties and yields.

### Palm Tree

[Palm Trees](/Palm_Tree "Palm Tree") do not drop any seeds, so they cannot be planted by the farmer and are only found in [The Calico Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") and on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island"). Palm Trees may occasionally drop a [Coconut](/Coconut "Coconut") (and can also drop a [Golden Coconut](/Golden_Coconut "Golden Coconut") if the tree is on Ginger Island) when chopped down or shaken. They cannot be [tapped](/Tapper "Tapper").

### Mushroom Tree

[Mushroom Trees](/Mushroom_Tree "Mushroom Tree") grow from ![Mushroom Tree Seed.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/d4/Mushroom_Tree_Seed.png/24px-Mushroom_Tree_Seed.png) [Mushroom Tree Seeds](/Mushroom_Tree_Seed "Mushroom Tree Seed"). Mushroom Trees are a rare occurrence on the farm. Each [Fall](/Fall "Fall"), there is a chance for a fully grown untapped common tree (or stump) to change into a Mushroom Tree (or stump) during the night. A Mushroom Tree can also be grown from a [Mushroom Tree Seed](/Mushroom_Tree_Seed "Mushroom Tree Seed"). These seeds do not fall from Mushroom Trees when shaken or chopped - they can only be obtained from [Qi's Walnut Room](/Qi%27s_Walnut_Room "Qi's Walnut Room") or from seeds spread from them. They yield [Red](/Red_Mushroom "Red Mushroom") or [Purple Mushrooms](/Purple_Mushroom "Purple Mushroom") when chopped down. They can be [tapped](/Tapper "Tapper") and will yield ![Common Mushroom.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2e/Common_Mushroom.png/24px-Common_Mushroom.png) [Common](/Common_Mushroom "Common Mushroom"), ![Red Mushroom.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/e1/Red_Mushroom.png/24px-Red_Mushroom.png) [Red](/Red_Mushroom "Red Mushroom"), and ![Purple Mushroom.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/4b/Purple_Mushroom.png/24px-Purple_Mushroom.png) [Purple](/Purple_Mushroom "Purple Mushroom") mushrooms over time.

Mushroom Trees hit by lightning will be reduced to a stump and leave mushrooms behind, in the same way as common trees leave wood/sap behind. If the stump is left alone, the following Spring, it will regrow.

## History

* [1.1](/Version_History#1.1 "Version History"): You can no longer tap a stump, and trees no longer say "Wrong Season" message.
* [1.3.27](/Version_History#1.3.27 "Version History"): Breaking small tree stumps now provides +1 Foraging XP.
* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Fixed bug that caused a stage 2-3 sapling to make a scythe sound when watered. Fixed bug that prevented trees from dropping seeds and seeds disappearing when harvested if a Foraging level up occurred during the day. Planting tree seeds outside the farm no longer requires the spot to be tilled. (This also prevents planting in non-tillable areas dug up by Artifact Spots in Winter).
* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): Added Mahogany Tree.
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Added Mystic Tree and the Green Rain Trees. Fixed tree stumps dropping seeds like full-grown trees. Reading [Woody's Secret](/Woody%27s_Secret "Woody's Secret") has a chance to double wood output from trees. Maple and Oak trees now have a chance to turn into [Green Rain Trees](/Green_Rain_Trees "Green Rain Trees") and lose their leaves in the fall. Trees planted in [the Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") will now grow during [Winter](/Winter "Winter") without [Tree Fertilizer](/Tree_Fertilizer "Tree Fertilizer").
* [1.6.3](/Version_History#1.6.3 "Version History"): Added extended growth stages explanation to the growth cycle, explanation to growth change after collecting.
