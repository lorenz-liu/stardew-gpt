The **Barrel Brazier** is a [crafted lighting item](/Crafting#Lighting "Crafting") that can be placed indoors or outdoors. Right-clicking on the brazier toggles the fire on and off. When toggled on, the brazier influences a radius of approximately 7 tiles with a moderate amount of light.

Brazier recipes can be purchased at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop"). All brazier recipes are available from the start of the game on any day of the week as long as they haven't been purchased already.
