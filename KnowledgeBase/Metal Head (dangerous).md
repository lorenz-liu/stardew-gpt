**Metal Heads (dangerous)** are an [enemy](/Monsters "Monsters") found in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") after activating the [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") or during the [Danger In The Deep](/Quests#Danger_In_The_Deep "Quests") quest.

## Behavior

They behave identical to their weaker counterpart the [Metal Head](/Metal_Head "Metal Head"). They have very high defense but no special attacks.

## Strategy

Due to the fact that they are resistant to knockbacks, you should back up after 2-3 swings.
