The **Advanced Iridium Rod** is a [tool](/Tools "Tools") used to catch [fish](/Fish "Fish"). It can have [bait](/Bait "Bait") and 2 [tackles](/Tackle "Tackle") attached to it.

One is obtained after claiming [Fishing Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave"). Afterwards, additional rods can be purchased from the [Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop") for data-sort-value="25000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)25,000g.

## Notes

* The effects of the two tackles [stack](/Tackle#Stacking "Tackle") (with a few exceptions such as with 2 [Sonar Bobbers](/Sonar_Bobber "Sonar Bobber")). For example, using two [Cork Bobbers](/Cork_Bobber "Cork Bobber") will increase the [fishing bar size](/Fishing#Bar_Size "Fishing") by 48 pixels, compared to 24 with using only one.

## Secret

| **Details** |
| --- |
| Putting [Lucky Purple Shorts](/Secrets#Lucky_Purple_Shorts "Secrets") in the tackle slot of a fishing rod changes the [bobber style](/Fish_Shop#Bobber_Machine "Fish Shop") to a unique Lucky Purple Shorts bobber. If placed in the first slot of an Advanced Iridium Rod, the shorts prevent the tackle in the second slot from losing durability. This cannot be done with Trimmed Lucky Purple Shorts. |
