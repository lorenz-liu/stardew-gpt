The **Fairy Rose** is a [flower](/Flowers "Flowers") that can be [grown](/Crops "Crops") from [Fairy Seeds](/Fairy_Seeds "Fairy Seeds") in 12 days.

If grown within proper range of a [Bee House](/Bee_House#Range "Bee House") (5 squares in any cardinal direction), a Fairy Rose will cause [Fairy Rose Honey](/Honey "Honey") to be produced, increasing the honey base value from data-sort-value="100">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100g to data-sort-value="680">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)680g. This is the most valuable type of honey, compensating for the poor profit margins on the flower itself.

Fairy Roses can be purchased from the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="290"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)870–1,450g.

## Bundles

Fairy Rose is one of the options for the ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Garden Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Garden_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Pantry (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Pantry "Remixed Bundles").

## Tailoring

Fairy Rose can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [![Long Dress.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/9e/Long_Dress.png/36px-Long_Dress.png)](/File:Long_Dress.png) [Long Dress](/Tailoring#Pants "Tailoring"). The fairy rose can also be used as a red dye pot color for [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), located in [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"), [Emily](/Emily "Emily") and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house.

## Quests

Fairy Rose is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Notes

* Fairy Roses have an equal chance of being one of six different colors. Fairy Roses of different colors will occupy different slots in inventory or chests. Six colors × four [quality](/Crops#Crop_Quality "Crops") levels = 24 possible spaces used.

:   [![FairyRoseColors.png](/mediawiki/images/9/92/FairyRoseColors.png)](/File:FairyRoseColors.png)

* Despite not having a true red variation, Fairy Roses are used as a red dye in the dye pots.
* Having at least one mature Fairy Roses on the farm by the end of a day increase the chance for [the Crop Fairy event](/Random_Events#The_Crop_Fairy "Random Events") by 70% (from 1% to 1.7%).[[1]](#cite_note-cropfairychanceincrease-1)
