The **Treasure Appraisal Guide** is a power [book](/Books "Books") that can be obtained by opening [Artifact Troves](/Artifact_Trove "Artifact Trove") (≈3.57% chance).[[1]](#cite_note-trovechance-1) It can also be purchased from the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") for data-sort-value="20000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)20,000g starting in Year 3 (≈9% chance to appear).[[2]](#cite_note-y3chance-2)

The first reading grants the player a power that triples the sell price of all [artifacts](/Artifacts "Artifacts").[[3]](#cite_note-artifactsell-3) Once read, it can be found in [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s Menu on the [Special Items & Powers](/Special_Items_%26_Powers "Special Items & Powers") tab. Each subsequent reading gives the player 20 [XP](/Skills "Skills") in each of the 5 skills.

## Bundles

Treasure Appraisal Guide is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Treasure Appraisal Guide is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a brown dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

Treasure Appraisal Guide is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Trading

One Treasure Appraisal Guide can be traded to the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") for either 3 [Spicy Eel](/Spicy_Eel "Spicy Eel") or 3 [Artifact Troves](/Artifact_Trove "Artifact Trove"), provided the player has already obtained the power from this book.
