**Wilderness Golems** are a unique [enemy](/Monsters "Monsters") found on farms at night if the player selects the [wilderness map](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps") as the farm layout. They'll scale in difficulty based on the player's [combat](/Combat "Combat") level; as the player's level increases so will the monster's difficulty.

Wilderness Golems can also spawn on other farm layouts provided the player has given a [Strange Bun](/Strange_Bun "Strange Bun") to the Dark Shrine of Night Terrors in the [Witch's Hut](/Witch%27s_Hut "Witch's Hut"), or chosen the [Advanced Option](/Options "Options") "Spawn monsters on the farm" at new game creation.

## Behavior

They come out of the ground and wander aimlessly and slowly make their way to the player, damaging the player if they collide. Their behavior is the same as [Stone Golems](/Stone_Golem "Stone Golem").

## Strategy

Swinging at them with a sword is the most effective strategy.

## Trivia

A Wilderness Golem plays a central role in [Abigail](/Abigail "Abigail")'s 14 heart event.

## References

1. [↑](#cite_ref-combatxp_1-0) The value specified in `Data/Monsters.xnb` is 5XP. However, monsters killed on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") give 1/3 of the normal XP value, which is then truncated. So Wilderness Golems on the farm grant 1XP. See `GameLocation.onMonsterKilled` in the game code.
2. [↑](#cite_ref-riceshoot_2-0) Rice Shoots are added to Wilderness Golem drops by `RockGolem::getExtraDropItems`, only in Spring.
