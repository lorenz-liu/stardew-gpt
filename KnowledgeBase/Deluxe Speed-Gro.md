**Deluxe Speed-Gro** is a [Fertilizer](/Fertilizer "Fertilizer") that speeds [crop](/Crops "Crops") growth by 25% (or a total of 35% with the [Agriculturist Profession](/Skills#Farming "Skills")).[[1]](#cite_note-speedincrease-1) It can be [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting") or purchased from [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for data-sort-value="150">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150g from Year 2 onward, or purchased from the [Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis") for data-sort-value="80">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)80g on Thursdays. Ten Deluxe Speed-Gro may also be received as a gift at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"). Up to 100 Deluxe Speed-Gro may be purchased from [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius")' [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="2">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 2 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg") each. 10-20 Deluxe Speed-Gro may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") or [Golden Mystery Box](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box").

Deluxe Speed-Gro can be placed on tilled soil *before or after* planting a seed, or at any stage of crop growth. It does not reduce time between harvests for multi-harvest crops.

Applying Deluxe Speed-Gro days after planting a seed still decreases the time it takes to fully grow, but it reduces a fixed amount of days per stage for each crop regardless of the day it's applied, and the reduction in stages that the crop has already passed will not retroactively apply. Combined with the fact that the reduction disproportionately affects earlier stages more, this means that its reduction to the rest of the growth time is much lower than the advertised percentage of 25%.[[2]](#cite_note-speedincrease2-2).

## Bundles

Deluxe Speed-Gro is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Deluxe Speed-Gro can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [High-Waisted Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt175.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/24/Shirt175.png/24px-Shirt175.png)](/File:Shirt175.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Deluxe Speed-Gro is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
