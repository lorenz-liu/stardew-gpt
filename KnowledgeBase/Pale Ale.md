**Pale Ale** is an [Artisan Good](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods") made from the [Keg](/Keg "Keg") using [Hops](/Hops "Hops"), taking 1-2 days. It is also available at the [Ginger Island Resort](/Ginger_Island#Island_South "Ginger Island") for data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g. [Pam](/Pam "Pam") sells Pale Ale in her [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="30">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 30 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg"). When consumed, Pale Ale causes a [Tipsy](/Buffs "Buffs") buff that decreases [Speed](/Speed "Speed") by 1.

## Aged Values

Pale Ale can be placed inside a [Cask](/Cask "Cask") to age from normal quality to silver, gold, and eventually iridium quality. Iridium quality doubles the base sell price of Pale Ale.

Normal, silver, and gold quality Pale Ale can be prematurely removed from a cask at any time by striking the cask with an [Axe](/Axes "Axes"), [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes"), or [Pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes").

## Bundles

Pale Ale is one of the options for the ![Bundle Orange.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/95/Bundle_Orange.png/24px-Bundle_Orange.png) [Brewer's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Brewer.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Pantry (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Pantry "Remixed Bundles").

## Tailoring

Pale Ale can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [![Shirt138.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/e4/Shirt138.png/24px-Shirt138.png)](/File:Shirt138.png) [Black Leather Jacket](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* [Pam](/Pam "Pam") requests a Pale Ale by mail on the 14th of [Summer](/Summer "Summer") in the ["Pam Is Thirsty" quest](/Quests#Pam_Is_Thirsty "Quests"). The reward is data-sort-value="350">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)350g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
