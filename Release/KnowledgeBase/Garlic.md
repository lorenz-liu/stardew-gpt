**Garlic** is a [vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from [Garlic Seeds](/Garlic_Seeds "Garlic Seeds") after 4 days. The seeds are available at [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") starting in Year 2. One Garlic can sometimes be purchased for data-sort-value="2500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,500g from [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre")'s booth at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star").

## Tailoring

Garlic can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Shirt And Belt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt159.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/99/Shirt159.png/24px-Shirt159.png)](/File:Shirt159.png)

## Quests

* A fully-grown and unharvested Garlic is required for [Gourmand Frog's puzzle](/Ginger_Island#Gourmand_Frog "Ginger Island") on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island").
