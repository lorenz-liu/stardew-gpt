**Wood** is a basic [resource](/Category:Resources "Category:Resources") that can be obtained by felling [Trees](/Trees "Trees") and [branches](/The_Farm#Wood "The Farm") with an [axe](/Axes "Axes") or bought from [Robin](/Robin "Robin") at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop"). The [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") trades 100 Wood for 1 [Woodcutter's Weekly](/Woodcutter%27s_Weekly "Woodcutter's Weekly"). Robin sells up to 500 Wood in her [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="1">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 1 [Calico Egg](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg") each.

Wood can also be found in [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing"). Ninety-nine Wood can be received as a gift from Robin at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"). One to 10 Wood can be produced in a [Woodskip](/Woodskip "Woodskip") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), depending on the population of the pond. Wood can additionally be obtained from [chipped](/Wood_Chipper "Wood Chipper") [Hardwood](/Hardwood "Hardwood") and from [recycled](/Recycling_Machine "Recycling Machine") or [chipped](/Wood_Chipper "Wood Chipper") [Driftwood](/Driftwood "Driftwood").

Felling a fully grown tree will yield between 12 and 16 Wood. After felling the tree, the stump can be destroyed to yield between 5 and 9 Wood. The amount of Wood dropped will depend on [Luck](/Luck "Luck") and the player's [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") level. Additionally, the [Forester](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") profession will increase yields from both trees and stumps, while reading [Woody's Secret](/Woody%27s_Secret "Woody's Secret") will only increase yields from trees. Felling trees near water can result in the loss of some or all of the Wood. To prevent this, players can destroy the tree while standing on the side where they do not want the tree to fall to, as it will always fall away from them.

## Uses

Wood can be spent on a variety of different things, including:

* Building and upgrading buildings at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop").
* [Crafting](/Crafting "Crafting") various items.
* Creating 1 [coal](/Coal "Coal") by placing 10 wood into the [Charcoal Kiln](/Charcoal_Kiln "Charcoal Kiln").
* 198 Wood are used (in two stacks of 99) in the ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Construction Bundle](/Bundles#Construction_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Crafts Room](/Bundles#Crafts_Room "Bundles").
* 300 Wood are used for repairing the [tide pool bridge](/The_Beach#Tide_Pools "The Beach") at the Beach.
* 950 Wood are used for the [Community Upgrade](/Carpenter%27s_Shop#Community_Upgrades "Carpenter's Shop").

## Purchase Price

The price of wood at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop") increases from data-sort-value="10">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)10g to data-sort-value="50">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)50g starting in Year 2. The player will receive a letter in the mail on Spring 1 of Year 2 saying "Notice: The price of raw materials at [Robin's shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop") and [Clint's shop](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith") has increased." The gold obtained by selling wood to Robin or through the [Shipping Bin](/Shipping "Shipping") is unchanged.

It is a brown dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the orange dye pot at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

* Robin may ask the player to gather 1000 pieces of wood within one week in the ["Robin's Resource Rush"](/Quests#Robin.27s_Resource_Rush "Quests") special order. The player may keep the harvested wood. If completed on time, Robin will mail the farmer the recipe to create a [Stone Chest](/Stone_Chest "Stone Chest").

Added [Wood Sign](/Wood_Sign "Wood Sign") recipe.
* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): The [Forester Profession](/Foraging#Foraging_Skill "Foraging") no longer increases the sale price of Wood. Added [Deluxe Scarecrow](/Deluxe_Scarecrow "Deluxe Scarecrow") and [Tea Sapling](/Tea_Sapling "Tea Sapling") recipes. Can now be used in [Tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). Can be produced by [Fish Ponds](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") and [Wood Chippers](/Wood_Chipper "Wood Chipper").

* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Used as an ingredient to the [Dehydrator](/Dehydrator "Dehydrator"), [Text Sign](/Text_Sign "Text Sign"), and [Big Chest](/Big_Chest "Big Chest") recipes. Reading [Woody's Secret](/Woody%27s_Secret "Woody's Secret") has a chance to double wood output from trees. Can now be purchased at [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") and traded from the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller"). No longer used for building Plank Cabin and Log Cabin.
