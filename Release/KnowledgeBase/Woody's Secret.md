**Woody's Secret** is a power [book](/Books "Books") that can be obtained by chopping [trees](/Trees "Trees"). The chance for a tree to drop it starts at 0.03% and increases by 0.001% for each tree chopped until it is dropped. Then the chance stays at 0.1% permanently. It cannot drop until the player has chopped more than 20 trees.[[1]](#cite_note-treeDrop-1)

It can also be purchased from the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") for data-sort-value="20000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)20,000g starting in Year 3 (≈9% chance to appear).[[2]](#cite_note-y3chance-2)

The first reading grants the player a power that gives felled [trees](/Trees "Trees") a 5% chance to yield double [wood](/Wood "Wood"). This effect stacks with the [Forester](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") profession but not with the [Lumberjack](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") profession.[[3]](#cite_note-woodpower-3) Once read, it can be found in [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s menu on the [Special Items & Powers](/Special_Items_%26_Powers "Special Items & Powers") tab. Each subsequent reading gives the player 100 [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") [XP](/Skills "Skills").

## Bundles

Woody's Secret is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Woody's Secret is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a brown dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

Woody's Secret is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Trading

One Woody's Secret can be traded to the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") for 20 [Hardwood](/Hardwood "Hardwood"), provided the player has already obtained the power from this book.
