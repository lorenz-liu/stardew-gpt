A **Tea Bush** is a type of bush that grows from a [Tea Sapling](/Tea_Sapling "Tea Sapling"). It takes 20 days to grow to maturity, after which it will produce one [Tea Leaf](/Tea_Leaves "Tea Leaves") each day during the last week (22nd-28th) of [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), and [Fall](/Fall "Fall") (and [Winter](/Winter "Winter") if indoors). One Tea Bush exists inside of [Caroline](/Caroline "Caroline")'s sunroom, and can also be harvested at the same time as other Tea Bushes (the last week of each season).

An immature Tea Bush grows at any time, even outdoors, even in winter, without watering, the same as [fruit trees](/Fruit_Trees "Fruit Trees"). As with fruit trees, [Tree Fertilizer](/Tree_Fertilizer "Tree Fertilizer") cannot be used on tea bushes.

## Stages

Once planted, neither players nor animals can walk through a Tea Bush at any stage of growth. A mature bush can be chopped down by 2 hits with a [Gold or Iridium Axe](/Axes "Axes"), but is not vulnerable to other tools. They yield their sapling when chopped.

## Tips

* It is possible to use tea bushes as permanent fences, to help contain farm animals within a barnyard.
* While you cannot typically place tea bushes outside the farm, you can place [Garden Pots](/Garden_Pot "Garden Pot") and then place tea bushes into them. They will grow and bear leaves as usual, and of course don't need watering.
