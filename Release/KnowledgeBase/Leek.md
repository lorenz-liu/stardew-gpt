The **Leek** is found via [foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") in the [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), or grown from [Spring Seeds](/Spring_Seeds "Spring Seeds"). The [Statue Of Endless Fortune](/Statue_Of_Endless_Fortune "Statue Of Endless Fortune") produces one Leek per year on [George](/George "George")'s birthday, [Fall 24](/Fall "Fall"). It can randomly be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during Spring.

Leeks are most common on [the Mountain](/The_Mountain "The Mountain") and [Backwoods](/Backwoods "Backwoods") maps, where 58% of spring forage items will be Leeks. They can also be found at the [Bus Stop](/Bus_Stop "Bus Stop") (20%), [Railroad](/Railroad "Railroad") (20%) and the [Forest Farm](/Farm_Maps#Forest "Farm Maps") (25%).

## Tailoring

Leek is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt042.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/47/Shirt042.png/24px-Shirt042.png) [Lime Green Tunic](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a white dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

* [Evelyn](/Evelyn "Evelyn") requests a Leek by mail on the 15th of [Spring](/Spring "Spring") (year 2) in the ["Granny's Gift" quest](/Quests#Granny.27s_Gift "Quests"). The reward is data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* A Leek may be randomly requested in [Spring](/Spring "Spring") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="180">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)180g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* Evelyn requests 12 Leeks in the ["Gifts for George"](/Quests#Gifts_for_George "Quests") Special Order. The rewards are data-sort-value="2000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,000g and a [Coffee Maker](/Coffee_Maker "Coffee Maker").
