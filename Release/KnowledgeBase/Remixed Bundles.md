# Remixed Bundles

:   *This page is about remixed bundles. For normal bundles, see [Bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").*

When creating a new game, there is an [option](/Options#Advanced_Options "Options") to **remix** the Community Center [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles"). A room can have a mix of permanent and random bundles. Permanent bundles will always be present, though the items may vary slightly from their standard counterparts. Random bundles may or may not be present.

A few bundles display more items than there are slots to fill. In this case, the player can choose which of the shown items they want to use to fill the bundle. They do not have to use all shown items, only enough to fill the slots.

## Crafts Room

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/11/Community_Center_Crafts_Room.png/300px-Community_Center_Crafts_Room.png)](/File:Community_Center_Crafts_Room.png)

Crafts Room After Completion

The Crafts Room contains the first group of bundles available. Completing all Crafts Room bundles repairs the bridge east of [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), unlocking the [Quarry](/Quarry "Quarry").

## Bulletin Board

[![](/mediawiki/images/5/53/Community_Center_Bulletin_Board.png)](/File:Community_Center_Bulletin_Board.png)

Bulletin Board Before Completion

[![](/mediawiki/images/7/76/Finished_Bulletin_Board.png)](/File:Finished_Bulletin_Board.png)

Bulletin Board After Completion

The Bulletin Board appears after completing three bundles.

Completing all Bulletin Board bundles improves the player's [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") rating with every [non-datable villager](/Villagers#Non-marriage_candidates "Villagers") by two hearts (500 points). Note that this applies only to non-datable villagers whom the player has met in person. Villagers who do not show on the "Social" tab of the player menu and villagers whose names appear as "???' will not receive 500 points. If the player completes all the bundles before [Kent](/Kent "Kent") arrives on 1 Spring, Year 2 -- possible by buying a [Red Cabbage](/Red_Cabbage "Red Cabbage") from the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") -- he will not benefit from the friendship increase, and will start at 0 hearts. The [Dwarf](/Dwarf "Dwarf")'s friendship will be unaffected if the player does not have the [Dwarvish Translation Guide](/Dwarvish_Translation_Guide "Dwarvish Translation Guide").

The morning after, Mayor [Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis") will send a letter saying that packages containing items posted about "years ago" on the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") bulletin board have been appearing in villagers' homes. He says the packages are all addressed from "your farm". He expresses his gratitude and says "all of us in town are delighted!"

## Vault

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/5/59/Community_Center_Vault.png/300px-Community_Center_Vault.png)](/File:Community_Center_Vault.png)

Vault Before Completion

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/6a/Finished_Vault_Room.png/300px-Finished_Vault_Room.png)](/File:Finished_Vault_Room.png)

Vault After Completion

The Vault becomes available after completing four bundles. Pressing the large "purchase" button will purchase the bundle and deduct the gold from the total.

Completing all Vault bundles costs data-sort-value="42500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)42,500g, and repairs the [Bus Stop](/Bus_Stop "Bus Stop"). Taking the Bus grants access to [The Calico Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert").

## History

* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): Introduced.
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Fixed Specialty Fish, Quality Fish, and Master Fisher's Bundles giving only one [Dish O' The Sea](/Dish_O%27_The_Sea "Dish O' The Sea") instead of five. Added new crops to Spring, Summer and Fall Crops Bundles. Added 4 new remixed bundles: Forest Bundle, Helper's Bundle, Spirit's Eve Bundle and Winter Star Bundle. Forager's Bundle now only requires 15 Wild Plums instead of 20. Night Fishing Bundle now awards a Glow Ring instead of a Small Glow Ring. River Fish Bundle now rewards Deluxe Bait instead of Bait.
