**The Alleyway Buffet** is a power [book](/Books "Books") that can be obtained by searching a golden garbage can located in the easternmost region of [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). There is a hidden opening in the eastern fence, just north of the [Minecart](/Minecart "Minecart") track outside of the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith"). The player must have at least a steel or better [axe](/Axes "Axes") and [pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes") to clear a [large log](/Large_Log "Large Log") and [boulder](/Boulder "Boulder") blocking the path. The golden garbage can is hidden behind a tree in the clearing at the end of the path.

It can also be purchased from the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") for data-sort-value="20000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)20,000g starting in Year 3 (≈9% chance to appear).[[1]](#cite_note-y3chance-1)

The first reading grants the player a power that increases their chance to find items in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") by 20%.[[2]](#cite_note-trashpower-2) Once read, it can be found in [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s Menu on the [Special Items & Powers](/Special_Items_%26_Powers "Special Items & Powers") tab. Each subsequent reading gives the player 100 [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") [XP](/Skills "Skills").

## Bundles

The Alleyway Buffet is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

The Alleyway Buffet is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used as a dark gray [dye](/Dyeing "Dyeing") when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.
