**Krobus** is the only friendly [monster](/Monsters "Monsters") players will encounter, however he still refers to other hostile monsters as his friends. He is a shadow person who lives in [the Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers"). He sells a variety of rare goods. He is also available as a [roommate](#Roommate).

## Schedule

Krobus can always be found in the Sewers once [the Player](/The_Player "The Player") obtains the [Rusty Key](/Rusty_Key "Rusty Key").

## Shop

Krobus' Shop is open all hours every day, even [festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days.

## Relationships

Krobus is almost never seen outside interacting with other [villagers](/Villagers "Villagers"). He dislikes [dwarves](/Dwarf "Dwarf"), stating that a 1,000 year [war](/Setting#The_Elemental_Wars "Setting") between the dwarves and his people recently ended. He believes if the dwarves knew where he was, they'd send an assassin.

The [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard") is aware of his presence and [intervenes](/Dwarf#Heart_Events "Dwarf") to prevent conflicts.

## Roommate

If the player is not [married](/Marriage "Marriage"), Krobus will move into the [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") three days after accepting a [Void Ghost Pendant](/Void_Ghost_Pendant "Void Ghost Pendant"). Like the marriage candidates, he will add his own room on the right of the bedroom. He will not set up an area in the space behind the farmhouse.

Krobus leaves the Farmhouse only on rainy days, when he will stand on the front porch. At 13 hearts, he will give the player a [Stardrop](/Stardrop "Stardrop"), like a spouse, and may change [Wallpaper](/Wallpaper "Wallpaper") or [Flooring](/Flooring "Flooring") or add [Furniture](/Furniture "Furniture") like a spouse.

Although the player's relationship with Krobus is platonic, the game's roommate mechanics are almost identical to the marriage mechanics, with the following exceptions:

* Kissing is replaced with hugging.
* Instead of a heart emoji appearing when kissing, a smile emoji appears when hugging.
* No ceremony takes place.
* Krobus will never get [jealous](/Marriage#Jealousy "Marriage") if the player gives a gift to a marriage candidate.
* Being roommates with Krobus won't stop the [group 10-heart events](/Bouquet#Group_10-Heart_Event "Bouquet").
* Instead of [divorce](/Marriage#Divorce "Marriage"), Krobus can be evicted for free. However, the player still needs to do this through the book in the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"). After Krobus moves out, he is silent towards the player and will refuse gifts.
  + As with an ex-spouse, the [Dark Shrine of Memory](/Dark_Shrine_of_Memory "Dark Shrine of Memory") can be used to erase Krobus' memories of the player.
* You cannot have or adopt children with Krobus. However, he helps with raising your existing children from previous marriages.
* Krobus does not do chores like a spouse would.
* Krobus does not sleep, but he will stand in the center of his room at 10:00 P.M., He can still be interacted with, however, unlike spouses.

After Krobus moves into the [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse"), he is replaced in [the Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers") by a chest, which sells his usual items.

On rainy mornings, Krobus may gift you a [Sea Urchin](/Sea_Urchin "Sea Urchin"), [Void Egg](/Void_Egg "Void Egg"), [Void Mayonnaise](/Void_Mayonnaise "Void Mayonnaise"), or [Void Salmon](/Void_Salmon "Void Salmon"). On non-rainy days, he may gift you a [Fried Eel](/Fried_Eel "Fried Eel"), [Lucky Lunch](/Lucky_Lunch "Lucky Lunch"), or [Poppyseed Muffin](/Poppyseed_Muffin "Poppyseed Muffin"). He may also gift you a [Strange Bun](/Strange_Bun "Strange Bun") regardless of the current weather.

## Quests

* [Dark Talisman](/Quests#Dark_Talisman "Quests"): After the [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard") requests his [Magic Ink](/Magic_Ink "Magic Ink"), the player must talk to Krobus, who will open the force field blocking the passage to the [Mutant Bug Lair](/Mutant_Bug_Lair "Mutant Bug Lair"), where the talisman can be found.
* ["A Winter Mystery"](/Quests#A_Winter_Mystery "Quests"): Sent by mail the day after triggering a cutscene by entering the [Bus Stop](/Bus_Stop "Bus Stop") from [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") during [Winter](/Winter "Winter") between 6am and 4pm. To complete the quest, interact with a bush to the left of the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") in the north of [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). A cutscene ensues, during which a "Shadow Guy" (who may or may not be Krobus) gives the player a [Magnifying Glass](/Magnifying_Glass "Magnifying Glass"). This allows the player to collect [Secret Notes](/Secret_Notes "Secret Notes").

## Trivia

* Krobus states that he remains silent on Fridays out of respect for Yoba. His normal Friday dialogue is simply "..." Krobus will speak on Fridays under certain circumstances.
  + Rainy days
  + First meeting
  + Attempting to give him a gift after he has been evicted as a roommate
  + Speaking to him about the [Dark Talisman](/Dark_Talisman "Dark Talisman") quest
  + Gifting him a [Void Ghost Pendant](/Void_Ghost_Pendant "Void Ghost Pendant")
* Attempting to give Krobus a [Movie Ticket](/Movie_Ticket "Movie Ticket") on Friday results in a dialogue saying "Krobus looks puzzled..." Krobus will not attend movies on Fridays.
* Despite being considered a roommate, if there are monsters inside the house, such as [slimes](/Slimes "Slimes"), he will still say, *"Um... Honey? Why are there monsters in the house?"*
* Krobus says that he normally does not sleep. However, he will fall asleep while watching any movie except [Mysterium](/Movie_Theater#Movies "Movie Theater").
* While watching Mysterium, Krobus has negative dialogue about the depiction of shadow people within the movie. However, he still likes the movie overall and gains friendship from watching it.
* When drinking Void Mayonnaise in front of Krobus, he will respond by saying "Yummie!"

## Bugs

* If multiple players open Krobus' shop in [Multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer") simultaneously, the limited quantity of items can be bypassed (multiplied by number of players). As an example, this allows two players on Friday to purchase two [Iridium Sprinklers](/Iridium_Sprinkler "Iridium Sprinkler") instead of one, if neither player has already purchased the Sprinkler.
