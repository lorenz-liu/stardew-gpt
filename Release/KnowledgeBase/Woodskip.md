The **Woodskip** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught in the pond in the [Secret Woods](/Secret_Woods "Secret Woods") during any season. It is most prevalent there on non-rainy days, with each cast having a 15% to 27% chance to hook a Woodskip depending on [fishing skill](/Skills#Fishing "Skills"). The Woodskip is more likely to be caught when casting at least 3 tiles from shore.[[1]](#cite_note-spawnrate-1) It can also be caught on the [Forest Farm](/Farm_Maps#Forest "Farm Maps"), with each cast having a 0% to 17.5% chance to hook a Woodskip depending on daily [luck](/Luck "Luck").[[2]](#cite_note-fish-2) It can also randomly be found at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="75"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)225–1,000g.

## Tailoring

The Woodskip can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Fishing Vest](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt157.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/0e/Shirt157.png/24px-Shirt157.png)](/File:Shirt157.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Woodskips can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 2 days. The initial pond capacity is 1 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing four quests. Typical output includes orange Woodskip [Roe](/Roe "Roe") and [wood](/Wood "Wood"), but other tree-related products are possible starting at population 6.
