The **Governor** is the governor of the region that [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") is a part of. He attends the [Luau](/Luau "Luau") annually and tastes the local food. The Governor is also referenced in the [Lobster Bisque](/Lobster_Bisque "Lobster Bisque") episode of [The Queen of Sauce](/The_Queen_of_Sauce "The Queen of Sauce") and can appear in the [Movie Theater](/Movie_Theater "Movie Theater") lobby.
