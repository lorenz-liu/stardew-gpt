The **Rabbit's Foot** is an [animal product](/Category:Animal_Products "Category:Animal Products") obtained from a [Rabbit](/Rabbit "Rabbit") with sufficient [friendship](/Animals#Animal_Care "Animals"), [mood](/Animals#Animal_Care "Animals"), and [daily luck](/Luck "Luck"). It can also occasionally be purchased from the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="565"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,695–2,825g or dropped by [Serpents](/Serpent "Serpent") in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") (0.8% chance). One may be purchased from [Jas](/Jas "Jas") in her [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="100">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 100 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg"). It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") cat with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals").

[Luck buffs](/Luck#Luck_Buffs "Luck") do not influence the chances of getting a Rabbit's Foot. Despite the description, carrying a Rabbit's Foot does not increase the player's luck.

## Bundles

Rabbit's Foot is used in the ![Bundle Purple.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a1/Bundle_Purple.png/24px-Bundle_Purple.png) [Enchanter's Bundle](/Bundles#Enchanters_Bundle "Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board](/Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Bundles").

## Recipes

Rabbit's Foot is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Rabbit's Foot is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [![Shirt095.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/7c/Shirt095.png/24px-Shirt095.png)](/File:Shirt095.png) [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Rabbit's Foot is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Other Uses

* One Rabbit's Foot is used when solving the puzzle depicted in [Secret Note #20](/Secret_Notes#Secret_Note_.2320 "Secret Notes").
* Having a Rabbit's Foot in inventory prevents a negative outcome in the [Group 10-Heart Event](/Bouquet#Group_10-Heart_Event "Bouquet").
