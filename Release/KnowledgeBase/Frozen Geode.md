The **Frozen Geode** is a [Mineral](/Minerals "Minerals") deposit that contains [minerals](/Minerals "Minerals"), [artifacts](/Artifacts "Artifacts"), ores, or basic [resources](/Category:Resources "Category:Resources"). It can be found on floors 41 to 79 of [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") in the [Winter](/Winter "Winter"), or inside a [Fishing Treasure Chest](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing"). It can also be received as a gift at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"). An [Ice Pip](/Ice_Pip "Ice Pip") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") may produce 1 or 5 Frozen Geodes when the population of the pond reaches 9. Frozen Geodes can also be obtained by pickaxing [Frozen Geode Nodes](/Mining#Frozen_Geode_Node "Mining"). These are found in the small quarry on the [Hill-top Farm](/Farm_Maps#Hilltop "Farm Maps") and [Four Corners Farm](/Farm_Maps#Four_Corners "Farm Maps"). [Sebastian](/Sebastian "Sebastian") sells Frozen Geodes in his [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="10">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 10 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg").

The Frozen Geode can be broken open by [Clint](/Clint "Clint") in the [Blacksmith shop](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith") for data-sort-value="25">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)25g or by using a [Geode Crusher](/Geode_Crusher "Geode Crusher").

Neither daily [luck](/Luck "Luck") nor luck [buffs](/Buffs "Buffs") affect the contents of a Frozen Geode.

### Resources

Note that [Gold Ore](/Gold_Ore "Gold Ore") is available in the Frozen Geode only after the player has reached floor 76 of [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"). Before reaching floor 76, Iron Ore is substituted when breaking open a Frozen Geode. Except for clay and stone, the following items will be obtained in the following amounts: 30% for 1, 30% for 3, 30% for 5, 9% for 10, and 1% for 20. Stone has a 50% chance to use those values and a 50% chance to give only 1. Only 1 clay can be obtained from a single geode.

## Tailoring

Frozen Geode is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt161.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/88/Shirt161.png/24px-Shirt161.png) [Blue Hoodie](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a cyan dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the blue dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

* Five Frozen Geodes may be requested by [Ice Pip](/Ice_Pip "Ice Pip") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 5 to 7.
* One Frozen Geode may be requested by several ocean fish in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 3 to 5.
* Five Frozen Geodes may be requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his fifth request, or as part of any request starting with the sixth.
