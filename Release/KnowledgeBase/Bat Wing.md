A **Bat Wing** is a type of [Monster Loot](/Monster_Loot "Monster Loot") dropped by [Bats](/Bats "Bats") (any variant except for Iridium Bats) when slain. 10 Bat Wings can also be purchased for data-sort-value="30">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)30g each from [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") on [Sundays](/Days_of_the_Week#Sunday "Days of the Week"). It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") cat with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals").

The [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") in [the Calico Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") will trade one [Butterfly Hutch](/Butterfly_Hutch "Butterfly Hutch") for 200 Bat Wings.

## Tailoring

Bat Wing is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [![Shirt003.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/f5/Shirt003.png/24px-Shirt003.png)](/File:Shirt003.png) [Dark Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring").

## Quests

* Ten Bat Wings are requested by [Void Salmon](/Void_Salmon "Void Salmon") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 3 to 5.
* Ten Bat Wings may be requested by [Catfish](/Catfish "Catfish") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 5 to 7.
* Twenty Bat Wings may be requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his fifth request, or as part of any request starting with the sixth.
