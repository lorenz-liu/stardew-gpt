**Aged Roe** is an [Artisan Good](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods") made from the [Preserves Jar](/Preserves_Jar "Preserves Jar") using any type of [Roe](/Roe "Roe") except [Sturgeon](/Sturgeon "Sturgeon") Roe. The sell price is twice the unprocessed [Roe](/Roe "Roe") sell price. The health and energy gained from eating Aged Roe is the same for all types of Aged Roe.

Using Sturgeon Roe in a Preserves Jar produces [Caviar](/Caviar "Caviar") instead of Aged Roe.

## Bundles

15 Aged Roe are one of the options for the ![Bundle Blue.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/e4/Bundle_Blue.png/24px-Bundle_Blue.png) [Fish Farmer's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Fish_Farmer.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Pantry (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Pantry "Remixed Bundles").

## Tailoring

Aged Roe is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Globby Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt284a.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a5/Shirt284a.png/24px-Shirt284a.png)](/File:Shirt284a.png)

It can also be used as an orange dye in the dye pots located in [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s and [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Aged Roe is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
