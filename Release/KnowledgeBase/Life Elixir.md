**Life Elixir** is an [edible crafted item](/Crafting#Edible_Items "Crafting"). The recipe is earned at [Combat](/Combat "Combat") skill level 2. [Iridium Bats](/Bats "Bats") in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") may drop a Life Elixir when slain (5% chance). The [Dwarf](/Dwarf "Dwarf") sells Life Elixir for data-sort-value="2000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,000g each. Two to four Life Elixirs may occasionally be found in [treasure rooms](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern") in the Skull Cavern. Two Life Elixirs may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") or [Golden Mystery Box](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box").

## Tailoring

Life Elixir can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Dark Highlight Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt263a.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/91/Shirt263a.png/24px-Shirt263a.png)](/File:Shirt263a.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a purple dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Life Elixir is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
