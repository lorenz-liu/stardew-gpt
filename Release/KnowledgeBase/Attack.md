# Attack

:   *For technical details about attack damage calculation, see [Combat/Mechanics](/Combat/Mechanics "Combat/Mechanics").*

**Attack** [![Attack.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/00/Attack.png/24px-Attack.png)](/File:Attack.png) is a statistic that affects how much damage the player does. It can be gained in a variety of ways.

Each level of Attack buff adds a flat 3 damage to a player's attacks, added after the multiplicative bonus of an [Iridium Band](/Iridium_Band "Iridium Band") but before the multiplicative bonuses of the [Fighter or Brute Professions](/Combat#Combat_Skill "Combat").
