**Professor Snail** lives on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") and runs the [Island Field Office](/Island_Field_Office "Island Field Office"). Before freeing him, Professor Snail is trapped in a cave on the northeastern side of the Dig Site. The Dig Site is accessible to the player by paying the parrots data-sort-value="10">![Golden Walnut.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/5/54/Golden_Walnut.png/24px-Golden_Walnut.png) 10 [Golden Walnuts](/Golden_Walnut "Golden Walnut") to repair the bridge near the field office. Once the bridge has been repaired, he can be freed with any explosion (from a [bomb](/Crafting#Bombs "Crafting") or [Explosive Ammo](/Explosive_Ammo "Explosive Ammo")). One hit with any [slingshot](/Weapons#Slingshot "Weapons") works, but the player must aim carefully.

After freeing him, he explains that he has been conducting a survey of the island's flora and fauna for a year. He returns to his [tent](/Island_Field_Office "Island Field Office") on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island#Island_North "Ginger Island"), where he accepts donations in exchange for [Golden Walnuts](/Golden_Walnut "Golden Walnut").

The cave where he was trapped becomes a mushroom cave, where mushrooms can be foraged by the player.

## Schedule

After freeing Professor Snail from the mushroom cave, he does not leave the [Island Field Office](/Island_Field_Office "Island Field Office") desk.

## Quests/Events

Professor Snail will reward players for donating items to the [Island Field Office](/Island_Field_Office "Island Field Office").

Professor Snail will reward players for correctly answering questions on his Island Survey inside the [Island Field Office](/Island_Field_Office "Island Field Office").
