**Wild Horseradish** is found via [foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") in the [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), or grown from [Spring Seeds](/Spring_Seeds "Spring Seeds"). It can randomly be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during Spring. Putting a Wild Horseradish in a [Seed Maker](/Seed_Maker "Seed Maker") will generate 1-3 [Spring Seeds](/Spring_Seeds "Spring Seeds").

## Tailoring

Wild Horseradish is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt008.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/d5/Shirt008.png/24px-Shirt008.png) ["Good Grief" Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a brown dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the orange dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

* Wild Horseradish may be randomly requested during [Spring](/Spring "Spring") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="150">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.

## Trivia

* Wild Horseradishes are the only spring forageable that can be directly put into the [Seed Maker](/Seed_Maker "Seed Maker").
