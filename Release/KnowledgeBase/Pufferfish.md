The **Pufferfish** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught in the ocean at [The Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") or on the [Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps#Beach "Farm Maps") on [sunny](/Weather "Weather") [summer](/Summer "Summer") days between 12pm and 4pm, or on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") during any season on Island West (ocean), South, Southeast, and in the Pirate Cove. It can also randomly be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during Summer, or at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="200"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)600–1,000g.

Pufferfish can be caught regardless of weather, time, or season using [Magic Bait](/Magic_Bait "Magic Bait").

The Pufferfish and the [Blobfish](/Blobfish "Blobfish") are the only [fishing rod fishes](/Fish#Fishing_Pole_Fish "Fish") that have a "floater" behavior in the fishing mini-game.

## Tailoring

Pufferfish can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Sailor Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt259a.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/36/Shirt259a.png/24px-Shirt259a.png)](/File:Shirt259a.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Pufferfish can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 4 days. The initial pond capacity is 3 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing three quests. The only possible output is yellow Pufferfish [Roe](/Roe "Roe").

## Quests

* [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius") requests a pufferfish by mail on the 6th of [Summer](/Summer "Summer") (year 2) in the ["Aquatic Research" Quest](/Quests#Aquatic_Research "Quests"). The reward is data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* **Item Delivery:** One pufferfish may be randomly requested in [Summer](/Summer "Summer") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="600">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)600g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* **Fishing:** [Willy](/Willy "Willy") may randomly request 1-4 pufferfish during [Summer](/Summer "Summer") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="200">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)200g per fish. (You get to keep the fish).
