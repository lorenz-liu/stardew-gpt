## Recipes

Fire Opal is not used in any [recipes](/Cooking#Recipes "Cooking").

## Tailoring

Fire Opal is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Purple Blouse](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt197.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/79/Shirt197.png/24px-Shirt197.png)](/File:Shirt197.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a purple dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

Fire Opal is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
