A **Pressure Nozzle** is a sprinkler upgrade that increases the watering range of [Sprinklers](/Crafting#Sprinklers "Crafting"). The range is increased to:

* [Sprinkler](/Sprinkler "Sprinkler"): 3x3 (now reaches the corner tiles)
* [Quality Sprinkler](/Quality_Sprinkler "Quality Sprinkler"): 5x5
* [Iridium Sprinkler](/Iridium_Sprinkler "Iridium Sprinkler"): 7x7

Pressure Nozzles can be purchased in [Qi's Walnut Room](/Qi%27s_Walnut_Room "Qi's Walnut Room") at a cost of data-sort-value="20">![Qi Gem.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/78/Qi_Gem.png/24px-Qi_Gem.png) 20 for 4 nozzles, and rarely drop from monsters in the Skull Cavern during the [Qi's Hungry Challenge](/Quests#Qi.27s_Hungry_Challenge "Quests") or [Skull Cavern Invasion](/Quests#Skull_Cavern_Invasion "Quests") quests. Additionally, [Slimes](/Slimes "Slimes") may rarely drop it when slain in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") during [Danger In The Deep quest](/Quests#Danger_In_The_Deep "Quests") or after activating the [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines").

A sprinkler may not be equipped with both a pressure nozzle and an [Enricher](/Enricher "Enricher") at the same time.

*Note: While the item icon shows only one nozzle, it is sufficient to upgrade one entire sprinkler (one Pressure Nozzle covers all 4 sprinkler nozzles).*

## Tip

When using pressure nozzles on [Iridium Sprinklers](/Iridium_Sprinkler "Iridium Sprinkler"), it is possible to automatically water the entire [greenhouse](/Greenhouse#Sprinklers "Greenhouse") while leaving only a single tile unplanted.
