The **Golden Egg** is an [animal product](/Category:Animal_Products "Category:Animal Products") obtained from [Golden Chickens](/Golden_Chicken "Golden Chicken"). They will produce a Golden
Egg every day as long as they are fed.

To obtain Golden Eggs and Golden Chickens, [Perfection](/Perfection "Perfection") must have been achieved. Progress can be tracked in [Qi's Walnut Room](/Qi%27s_Walnut_Room#Perfection_Tracker "Qi's Walnut Room").

After reaching Perfection, the first Golden Egg can be obtained in four ways:

* [Marnie's Ranch](/Marnie%27s_Ranch "Marnie's Ranch") for data-sort-value="100000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100,000g.
* [Qi's Walnut Room](/Qi%27s_Walnut_Room "Qi's Walnut Room") for data-sort-value="100">![Qi Gem.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/78/Qi_Gem.png/24px-Qi_Gem.png) 100.
* [Random Event](/Random_Events#The_Witch "Random Events") that occurs after the player goes to sleep. A golden witch will fly over a [Coop](/Coop "Coop") and leave a Golden Egg inside.
* Randomly from a [Fishing Treasure Chest](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") (0.12% chance).

Golden Eggs can be put into an [incubator](/Incubator "Incubator") in a [Big or Deluxe Coop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop#Farm_Buildings "Carpenter's Shop") to hatch a Golden Chicken 6 days later, or 3 days with the [Coopmaster Profession](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming").

## Quests

* ["Gus' Famous Omelet"](/Quests#Gus.27_Famous_Omelet "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), [Gus](/Gus "Gus") may randomly request that the player collect and deliver 24 Eggs (of any type) to the fridge in [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon"). The reward is data-sort-value="3000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)3,000g and a [Mini-Fridge](/Mini-Fridge "Mini-Fridge").
* Five Eggs (any type, excluding Dinosaur) may be requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his third request, or as part of any request starting with the sixth.
