The **Price Catalogue** is a power [book](/Books "Books") that can be purchased from the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") for data-sort-value="3000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)3,000g.

The first reading grants the player a power that allows them to view the sell price of any sellable item in-game. If an item is stacked or its price is affected by professions, then the sell price is displayed accordingly in the hover menu.[[1]](#cite_note-hoverprice-1) Once read, it can be found in [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s Menu on the [Special Items & Powers](/Special_Items_%26_Powers "Special Items & Powers") tab. Subsequent readings do not give any effects or additional [XP](/Skills "Skills").[[2]](#cite_note-subsequent-2)

## Bundles

Price Catalogue is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Price Catalogue is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a gold dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

Price Catalogue is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Trivia

* This is one of the few items in the game that is spelled using British English instead of American English (where the *-ue* ending would be dropped, becoming *catalog*).
