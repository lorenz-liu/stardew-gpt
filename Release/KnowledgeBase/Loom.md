The **Loom** is a type of [Artisan Equipment](/Crafting#Artisan_Equipment "Crafting") used to make [Artisan Goods](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods"). It takes [wool](/Wool "Wool") and turns it into [cloth](/Cloth "Cloth").

Placing quality [Wool](/Wool "Wool") into a Loom gives a chance to produce 2 Cloth. Silver gives a 10% chance, gold gives a 50% chance, and iridium gives a 100% chance. This makes cloth one of the only artisan goods in the game that is actually affected by the quality level of its input.
