**Eggplant** is a [vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from [Eggplant Seeds](/Eggplant_Seeds "Eggplant Seeds") after 5 days.

## Stages

When harvested, each Eggplant bush gives 1 Eggplant every 5 days, with a 0.2% chance for more Eggplants.

## Tailoring

Eggplant is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a dyeable ![Shirt140.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/f9/Shirt140.png/24px-Shirt140.png) [Button Down Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a purple dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the purple dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

* Eggplant may be randomly requested in [Fall](/Fall "Fall") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="180">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)180g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
