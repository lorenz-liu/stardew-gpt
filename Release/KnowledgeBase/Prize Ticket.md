A prize ticket machine on prize 13

**Prize Tickets** are earned by completing [Quests](/Quests "Quests") and [Special Orders](/Quests#List_of_Special_Orders "Quests"). It can also be obtained by opening the chest at the end of the Maze during [Spirit's Eve](/Spirit%27s_Eve "Spirit's Eve"), and repeated [Egg Festival](/Egg_Festival "Egg Festival") and [Festival of Ice](/Festival_of_Ice "Festival of Ice") wins. One Prize Ticket can be bought at the [Stardew Valley Fair](/Stardew_Valley_Fair "Stardew Valley Fair") for data-sort-value="1000">![Token.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/29/Token.png/24px-Token.png)1,000 star tokens each year. One can also be found in a box behind the stump above the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller").

Prize Tickets can be exchanged for prizes using [the prize machine](/Mayor%27s_Manor#Prize_Machine "Mayor's Manor") located in Lewis's house next to the Town Ledger. When using the prize machine, the player can see how many Prize Tickets they currently have as well as the next three prizes to be dispensed. Claiming a prize gives the player the first prize in line and advances the remaining prizes forward by one space.

## Gifting

Prize Tickets cannot be given as [gifts](/Friendship#Gifts "Friendship") to [villagers](/Villagers "Villagers").

## Bundles

One Prize Ticket is required for the ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Helper's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Helper.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Remixed Bundles").

## Tailoring

Prize Ticket is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a red dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Prize Ticket is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
