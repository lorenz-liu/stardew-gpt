The **Jack-O-Lantern** is a [craftable](/Crafting "Crafting") decorative lighting item that can be held or placed to provide light. It can be purchased from [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre")'s booth at the [Spirit's Eve](/Spirit%27s_Eve "Spirit's Eve") festival for data-sort-value="750">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)750g. You can also buy the recipe from the booth for data-sort-value="2000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,000g.

The Jack-O-Lantern influences a radius of approximately 1 tile with dim lighting. It can be placed indoors or outdoors, or in places like [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"). It can be removed by striking it with a [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes"), a [Pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes"), or an [Axe](/Axes "Axes"). Unlike the braziers, which can toggle on and off, the Jack-O-Lantern is always on. They provide the least light out of all craftable lighting sources.

On the 17th of [Fall](/Fall "Fall"), after the [Stardew Valley Fair](/Stardew_Valley_Fair "Stardew Valley Fair"), Jack-o-Lanterns appear in front of the following locations: [1 Willow Lane](/1_Willow_Lane "1 Willow Lane") (Jodi, Kent, Sam, and Vincent), [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane") (Haley and Emily), [1 River Road](/1_River_Road "1 River Road") (Evelyn George, and Alex), [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop") (Robin, Demetrius, Maru, and Sebastian), and the [Trailer](/Trailer "Trailer") (Pam and Penny).

On the 1st of [Winter](/Winter "Winter"), the Jack-O-Lantern turns into a [Rotten Plant](/Rotten_Plant "Rotten Plant") (unless stored in a [chest](/Chest "Chest") or [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory")).

## Bundles

The Jack-O-Lantern is used in the ![Bundle Purple.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a1/Bundle_Purple.png/24px-Bundle_Purple.png) [Spirit's Eve Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Spirit.27s_Eve_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Remixed Bundles").

## Tailoring

The Jack-O-Lantern is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Pumpkin Mask.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/ba/Pumpkin_Mask.png/24px-Pumpkin_Mask.png) [Pumpkin Mask](/Pumpkin_Mask "Pumpkin Mask"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

The Jack-O-Lantern is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Notes

The amount of illumination it gives off is less than a [Torch](/Torch "Torch"). This may make it more useful as a decorative piece than as night-time lighting.
