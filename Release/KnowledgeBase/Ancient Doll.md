The **Ancient Doll** is an [Artifact](/Artifacts "Artifacts") that can be found by digging up an [Artifact Spot](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") in [The Mountain](/The_Mountain "The Mountain"), [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest"), [Bus Stop](/Bus_Stop "Bus Stop"), or [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"), and inside [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") and [Artifact Troves](/Artifact_Trove "Artifact Trove"). It can also be received as a gift from [Jas](/Jas "Jas"), [Leo](/Leo "Leo"), or [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent") at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"). It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") dog with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals").

It is sold by Jas at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="1">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 1 [Calico Egg](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg").

## Donation

Donation of this item contributes to the total count of donations for the [Museum](/Museum "Museum").

## Bundles

Ancient Doll is an option in the ![Bundle Green.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/b3/Bundle_Green.png/24px-Bundle_Green.png) [Children's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Children.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Remixed Bundles").

## Tailoring

Ancient Doll is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Doll Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt229.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/1f/Shirt229.png/24px-Shirt229.png)](/File:Shirt229.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a blue dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Ancient Doll is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Secrets

* The Ancient Doll is a recurring part of the [dove children secrets](/Secrets#Dove_Children "Secrets").
* The Ancient Doll can be used to obtain a [secret item](/Far_Away_Stone "Far Away Stone") by throwing it (right-clicking) into the lava pool on floor 100 of [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") or in the [Volcano Caldera](/Forge "Forge").
