The **Far Away Stone** is a [secret](/Secrets "Secrets") item found by right-clicking with an [Ancient Doll](/Ancient_Doll "Ancient Doll") next to the lava pool in either level 100 of [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") or in the [Volcano Caldera](/Forge "Forge"). This can be done multiple times.

The Far Away Stone can be used to obtain the [Meowmere](/Meowmere "Meowmere") by interacting with the Terraria pylon pedestal in the basement of the [Wizard's Tower](/Wizard%27s_Tower "Wizard's Tower").

The Far Away Stone can also be sold through the [Shipping Bin](/Shipping "Shipping") for data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g. It cannot be [gifted](/Friendship#Gifts "Friendship") to [villagers](/Villagers "Villagers").

* [![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/9c/WizardBasement.PNG/120px-WizardBasement.PNG)](/File:WizardBasement.PNG)

  The pedestal in the Wizard's Basement

## Notes

* Simply dragging the Ancient Doll out of inventory will not work.

## Trivia

* The method of obtaining the Far Away Stone is a reference to summoning the Wall of Flesh in Terraria, which also requires dropping a doll item into lava.
* Once placed on the plant pedestal, it resembles the Forest Pylon from Terraria.
