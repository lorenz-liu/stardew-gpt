**Jas** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives just outside [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). She's a young girl, and can often be found with her best friend [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent").

## Schedule

After the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island is unlocked, Jas may randomly spend the day there. After leaving the Island at 6pm, Jas will immediately go home to bed. Jas never visits the Resort on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, Fridays, [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days or her checkup day at [Harvey's Clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic"). Jas also never visits the Resort without an adult accompanying her.

## Relationships

Jas lives with her aunt [Marnie](/Marnie "Marnie") and her godfather [Shane](/Shane "Shane") on her ranch outside town. She's often seen alongside her friend [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent"), who is the only resident of town close to her age. [Penny](/Penny "Penny") takes time to teach both [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent") and Jas at the [museum](/Museum "Museum") since there's no school nearby. Jas' parents both died by unknown causes.

## Heart Events

### Eight Hearts

![Eight Hearts.png](/mediawiki/images/3/39/Eight_Hearts.png)

After reaching 8 hearts with both Jas and [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent"), enter [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") during [Spring](/Spring "Spring") on a sunny day between 6am and 5pm.

| **Details** |
| --- |
| Vincent will explain how to clean [Spring Onions](/Spring_Onion "Spring Onion") by removing insects. Jas insists that the insects not be killed after removing them from the onions. Afterward, the player gains [Spring Onion Mastery](/Spring_Onion_Mastery "Spring Onion Mastery"), which permanently increases the sell price of Spring Onions by 5x. |

## Trivia

* Ironically, Jas hates [Clay](/Clay "Clay") but she may gift it to you at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star").
* Gifting Jas [Ice Cream](/Ice_Cream "Ice Cream") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") raises [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") with her by 200 points. This can be done one time per player.
