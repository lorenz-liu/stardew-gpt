**Driftwood** is a type of [trash](/Trash "Trash") that can be caught when fishing during all seasons, in any location, using either a [Fishing Pole](/Tools#Fishing_Poles "Tools") or a [Crab Pot](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot"). Also, any crab pot fish may produce Driftwood when placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"). It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") cat or dog with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals").

Driftwood may be [recycled](/Recycling_Machine "Recycling Machine") into [Wood](/Wood "Wood") or [Coal](/Coal "Coal"), or [chipped](/Wood_Chipper "Wood Chipper") into [Wood](/Wood "Wood").

## Bundles

Driftwood is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Recipes

Driftwood is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Driftwood is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt232.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2a/Shirt232.png/24px-Shirt232.png) [Trash Can Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a brown dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the orange dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

Three Driftwood may be requested by several fish in [Fish Pond quests](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond").

During the special order quest [Community Cleanup](/Quests#List_of_Special_Orders "Quests") the player is tasked with gathering 20 trash items (except Joja Cola).
