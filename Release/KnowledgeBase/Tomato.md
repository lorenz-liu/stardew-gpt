The **Tomato** is a [vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from [Tomato Seeds](/Tomato_Seeds "Tomato Seeds") after 11 days.

## Stages

When harvested, each Tomato plant gives 1 Tomato every 4 days, with a 5% chance for more Tomatoes.

## Tailoring

Tomato is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Tomato Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt282.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/7e/Shirt282.png/24px-Shirt282.png)](/File:Shirt282.png) It can also be used as a red dye in the dye pots, located at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

The Tomato may be randomly requested in [Summer](/Summer "Summer") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="180">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)180g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.

## Trivia

The ambiguous status of the tomato - fruit or vegetable - is the subject of the argument in [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius")' Six Hearts event. For this reason, the Tomato Shirt appears to be a combination of his and [Robin](/Robin "Robin")'s shirts.
