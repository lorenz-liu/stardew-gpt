**Trash** can be caught when fishing during all seasons, in any location, using either a [Fishing Pole](/Tools#Fishing_Poles "Tools") or a [Crab Pot](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot"). It can also be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can"), and any crab pot fish may produce Trash when placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"). It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") cat with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals").

Trash can be recycled into useful items using a [Recycling Machine](/Recycling_Machine "Recycling Machine").

## Bundles

It is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Recipes

It is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

It can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Trash Can Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt232.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2a/Shirt232.png/24px-Shirt232.png)](/File:Shirt232.png)

## Quests

* During the special order quest "[Community Cleanup](/Quests#Community_Cleanup "Quests")" the player is tasked with gathering 20 trash items (except Joja Cola).
* Five Trash may be requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his fifth request, or as part of any request starting with the sixth.
