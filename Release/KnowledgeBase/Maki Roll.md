**Maki Roll** is a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit").

**Note:** This recipe is [profitable](/Cooking#Profitable_Recipes "Cooking") when using [Rice](/Rice "Rice") made in the mill and fish selling below data-sort-value="100">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100g.

Maki Roll may randomly appear in [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus")' shop on [Saturdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Saturday "Days of the Week") or in [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon")'s rotating stock. It can also be found occasionally in the [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") outside [The Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon"). [Linus](/Linus "Linus") may randomly send Maki Roll in the mail as a gift. One Maki Roll may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box").

## Bundles

Maki Roll is used in the ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Chef's Bundle](/Bundles#Chefs_Bundle "Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board](/Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Bundles").

## Recipes

Maki Roll is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Maki Roll is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt037.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/1e/Shirt037.png/24px-Shirt037.png) [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a black dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

Maki Roll is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
