**Forest Magic** permanently unlocks the ability to read the language of the [Junimos](/Junimos "Junimos"). It is rewarded to [the Player](/The_Player "The Player") upon finishing the [Meet The Wizard](/Quests#Meet_The_Wizard "Quests") quest, which is unlocked the morning after the player examines the first golden scroll in the Community Center.

Once obtained, the Forest Magic power can be found in the [Special Items & Powers](/Special_Items_%26_Powers "Special Items & Powers") tab in [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s Menu.
