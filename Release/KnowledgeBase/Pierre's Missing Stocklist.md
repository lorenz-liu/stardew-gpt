**Pierre's Missing Stocklist** lets [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre") sell seeds from all seasons year-round in [his shop](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). It can be purchased from [Qi's Walnut Room](/Qi%27s_Walnut_Room "Qi's Walnut Room") for data-sort-value="50">![Qi Gem.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/78/Qi_Gem.png/24px-Qi_Gem.png) 50.

Seeds sold out-of-season cost 50% more than they would if in-season. The price of Pierre's [year-round stock](/Pierre%27s_General_Store#Year-Round_Stock "Pierre's General Store") is unaffected.

The stocklist needs to be delivered to Pierre to work. Trying to give it to anyone other than Pierre displays a message "[Villager] looks puzzled..." The new inventory items become available for purchase immediately after giving Pierre the stocklist.

## Notes

* Pierre's Missing Stocklist does not unlock year 1 access to seeds normally sold only in year 2+ (*i.e.,* [Garlic Seeds](/Garlic_Seeds "Garlic Seeds"), [Red Cabbage Seeds](/Red_Cabbage_Seeds "Red Cabbage Seeds"), and [Artichoke Seeds](/Artichoke_Seeds "Artichoke Seeds")). However, it allows the purchase of all year 2 seeds starting Spring 1 of year 2.
  + The Stocklist does not unlock [Strawberry Seeds](/Strawberry_Seeds "Strawberry Seeds"), which must be purchased at the [Egg Festival](/Egg_Festival "Egg Festival").
