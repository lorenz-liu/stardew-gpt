The **Dandelion** is found via [foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") in the [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), or grown from [Spring Seeds](/Spring_Seeds "Spring Seeds"). Up to ten can be purchased from [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre") at the [Flower Dance](/Flower_Dance "Flower Dance"). Up to five can also be purchased from [Caroline](/Caroline "Caroline")'s [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="10">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 10 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg") each. It can randomly be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during Spring. Although it is a flower in its description (and in real life), the game classifies it as "Forage" and not "Flower."

## Bundles

Dandelion is used in the ![Bundle Green.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/b3/Bundle_Green.png/24px-Bundle_Green.png) [Spring Foraging Bundle](/Bundles#Spring_Foraging_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Crafts Room](/Bundles#Crafts_Room "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Dandelion is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt002.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/5/51/Shirt002.png/24px-Shirt002.png) [Mint Blouse](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a yellow dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the yellow dye pot at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"), for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

* Dandelion may be randomly requested during [Spring](/Spring "Spring") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="120">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)120g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
