**Grass Starter** can be planted in a tile on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") to create a full-grown patch of [Grass](/Grass "Grass") instantly. Over time, a patch of grass grows and spreads to surrounding tiles in spring through fall. In winter, it goes dormant until the next spring. Grass can be harvested with the [Scythe](/Scythe "Scythe") to produce [Hay](/Hay "Hay"), once a [Silo](/Silo "Silo") is built. Harvesting grass in winter is less productive than in the other seasons.

## Tips

* [Fences](/Crafting#Fences "Crafting"), [Lightning Rods](/Lightning_Rod "Lightning Rod"), and most decoration items can be placed on top of a grass starter after it is planted. This will prevent [animals](/Animals "Animals") from eating the grass starter, and let the grass expand continuously without the grass ever being entirely consumed by farm animals.
* Grass Starter is meant to be used outside where farm animals can eat it, but it can be placed inside a [Farm Building](/Carpenter%27s_Shop#Farm_Buildings "Carpenter's Shop"), or anywhere outside [The Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"). Animals will not eat it, and it will not grow/spread, but it can be harvested to create [Hay](/Hay "Hay").
* [Marnie](/Marnie "Marnie") sells Grass Starters at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="2">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 2 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg") each.

## Bugs

* Any grass patches on the Farm on Winter 28 will multiply up to 40x each on Spring 1, unless you exit the game.
