The **Crimsonfish** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught in the ocean at [The Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") during [Summer](/Summer "Summer"). It is one of the five [Legendary Fish](/Fish#Legendary_Fish "Fish"), so it can only be caught once per [save file](/Saves "Saves"), or once per player in [Multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer").

The special requirements necessary to hook a Crimsonfish are:[[1]](#cite_note-requirements-1)

* [Fishing level 5](/Fishing#Fishing_Skill "Fishing"), either naturally or using Fishing [Buffs](/Buffs "Buffs").
* The player must be standing at the far east end of the beach, which can be reached after repairing the small bridge for 300 [wood](/Wood "Wood") or purchasing the second [community upgrade](/Carpenter%27s_Shop#Community_Upgrades "Carpenter's Shop"). Any location on the eastern pier works, as does any location on the shore east of the pier.
* The bobber must be located in water with a [distance from land](/Fishing#Distance_from_Land "Fishing") of at least 3, *i.e.,* it must be at least three tiles away from any land (including the pier) in every direction.
  + The Crimsonfish can appear one tile closer to land if the bobber lands in [Bubbles](/Fishing#Bubbles "Fishing").

When all of these requirements are met, each cast has an 18% chance of hooking a Crimsonfish (25% if using a [Curiosity Lure](/Curiosity_Lure "Curiosity Lure")).

Although the Crimsonfish cannot be hooked again after it is caught, it is possible to obtain multiple Crimsonfish (per player) by using [Challenge Bait](/Challenge_Bait "Challenge Bait") to catch it.

The Crimsonfish can be caught regardless of season using [Magic Bait](/Magic_Bait "Magic Bait").

## Dyeing

Crimsonfish can be used as a red dye color at the dye pots in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").
