The **Clam** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught using a [Crab Pot](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot") in any [saltwater](/Crab_Pot#Saltwater "Crab Pot") body of water. It can also be [foraged](/Foraging "Foraging") from [The Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach"), gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") turtle with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals"), or randomly found at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="50"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150–1,000g. One Clam can sometimes be purchased for data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g from [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre")'s booth at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"). It may also be found foraging on levels 1-29 of [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") while the [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") is enabled, or the [Danger in The Deep](/Quests#Danger_In_The_Deep "Quests") quest is active. Like all fish caught from the crab pot, it cannot be eaten unless it is used in a [Cooking](/Cooking "Cooking") recipe or [smoked](/Smoked_Fish "Smoked Fish").

## Tailoring

Clam can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Sailor Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt261a.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3d/Shirt261a.png/24px-Shirt261a.png)](/File:Shirt261a.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a blue dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Clam can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 2 days. The initial pond capacity is 3 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing three quests. Typical output includes Clam [Roe](/Roe "Roe"), [Seaweed](/Seaweed "Seaweed"), and various [Trash](/Trash "Trash") items, but more valuable items are possible starting at population 5.

## Quests

* Two Clams may be requested by several ocean fish in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 5 to 7.
* Five Clams may be requested by [The Raccoon](/Giant_Stump#Questline "Giant Stump") as part of the first request or as part of the sixth request and beyond.
