**Magic Rock Candy** is a special edible item that can be obtained in variety of ways.

## Locations

* Dropped by [Haunted Skulls](/Haunted_Skull "Haunted Skull") (0.13% chance).
* Opening [Mystery Boxes](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") (0.2% chance) or [Golden Mystery Boxes](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box") (0.4% chance).
* One can be purchased each [Thursday](/Days_of_the_Week#Thursday "Days of the Week") from the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for 3 [Prismatic Shards](/Prismatic_Shard "Prismatic Shard").
* Can be purchased from the [Raccoon Wife's Shop](/Giant_Stump#Raccoon_Wife.27s_Shop "Giant Stump") for 20 [Golden Mystery Boxes](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box") after the 6th Raccoon family request is completed and [Foraging Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave") has been claimed.
* As the reward for donating 90 items to the [Museum](/Museum "Museum").
* As the 22nd item received from the [prize machine](/Mayor%27s_Manor#Prize_Machine "Mayor's Manor") in [Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis")'s [manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor").
* One is sold by the [Calico Egg Merchant](/Desert_Festival#Calico_Egg_Merchant "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="250">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 250 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg") on the last day of the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival").
* One is sold by [Jas](/Jas "Jas") for data-sort-value="300">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 300 Calico Eggs whenever she is hosting a [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival").
* One is rewarded for submitting an [Egg Rating](/Desert_Festival#Skull_Cavern "Desert Festival") of 55-999 to [Gil](/Gil "Gil") during the Desert Festival.

## Bundles

Magic Rock Candy is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Magic Rock Candy is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the prismatic [Magic Sprinkle Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt272.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/5/57/Shirt272.png/24px-Shirt272.png)](/File:Shirt272.png)

## Quests

Magic Rock Candy is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Notes

* Though it cannot be cooked, its underlying category in the game's data files is "Cooking", so Magic Rock Candy shows up on the "Cooked Items" tab of the Gift Log on the Social tab in-game after giving it as a gift.
* Because it is a "cooked" item, it cannot be added to the [Luau](/Luau "Luau") soup.
