**Algae Soup** is a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit"). It may also be dropped by a [Mutant Grub](/Mutant_Grub "Mutant Grub") (10% chance).

**Note:** This recipe is [profitable](/Cooking#Profitable_Recipes "Cooking") - Algae Soup sells for data-sort-value="100">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100g and 4 [Green Algae](/Green_Algae "Green Algae") sell for data-sort-value="60">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)60g.

## Bundles

Algae Soup is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Recipes

Algae Soup is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Algae Soup is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt069.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/d9/Shirt069.png/24px-Shirt069.png)](/File:Shirt069.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a green dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

One Algae Soup may be requested by [Slimejack](/Slimejack "Slimejack") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 5 to 7.
