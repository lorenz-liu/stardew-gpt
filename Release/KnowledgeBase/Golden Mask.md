The **Golden Mask** is an [Artifact](/Artifacts "Artifacts") that can be found by [digging](/Hoes "Hoes") up [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") in [the Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert"). It also has a chance to appear inside [Artifact Troves](/Artifact_Trove "Artifact Trove").

## Donation

Donation of this item contributes to the total count of donations for the [Museum](/Museum "Museum").

## Bundles

The Golden Mask is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

The Golden Mask is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Golden Mask (hat).png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/c7/Golden_Mask_%28hat%29.png/24px-Golden_Mask_%28hat%29.png) [Golden Mask (hat)](/Golden_Mask_(hat) "Golden Mask (hat)"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

The Golden Mask is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Trivia

The Golden Mask might be a reference to the Stone Mask from [JoJo's Bizarre Adventure: Battle Tendency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_Tendency "wikipedia:Battle Tendency"), as the two both have a large gemstone near the top.
