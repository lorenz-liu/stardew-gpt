The **Water Obelisk** is a type of [farm building](/Wizard%27s_Tower#Buildings "Wizard's Tower") purchasable from the [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard") at the [Wizard's Tower](/Wizard%27s_Tower "Wizard's Tower") after finishing the [Goblin Problem](/Quests#Goblin_Problem "Quests") quest. Its companion buildings, the [Earth Obelisk](/Earth_Obelisk "Earth Obelisk"), [Desert Obelisk](/Desert_Obelisk "Desert Obelisk"), and [Island Obelisk](/Island_Obelisk "Island Obelisk"), can also be purchased from the Wizard. Interacting with the obelisk transports the player to the warp totem location at [the beach](/The_Beach "The Beach"), similar to the craftable [Warp Totem: Beach](/Warp_Totem:_Beach "Warp Totem: Beach"). It cannot be interacted with while riding a horse.

Just as with other farm buildings available from the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop"), the obelisks are placeable and permanent objects on farm land, and are not consumed on use. Likewise, an obelisk's location on the farm can be moved any time by visiting the [Wizard's Tower](/Wizard%27s_Tower "Wizard's Tower"), just like with other farm buildings at the Carpenter's shop.

Constructing the Water Obelisk is necessary to achieve [Perfection](/Perfection "Perfection").
