Harvey's Clinic is located in the center of [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"), next to [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). [Harvey](/Harvey "Harvey") lives and works at the clinic. [Maru](/Maru "Maru") works there on [Tuesday](/Days_of_the_Week#Tuesday "Days of the Week") and [Thursday](/Days_of_the_Week#Thursday "Days of the Week"). Both of them often complain about the lack of patients.

Most [villagers](/Villagers "Villagers") will visit the clinic for their annual checkup. [George](/George "George") and [Evelyn](/Evelyn "Evelyn") visit the clinic once per season.

Although you can enter the Clinic most days between 9am and 3pm, you cannot purchase medical supplies unless [Maru](/Maru "Maru") or [Harvey](/Harvey "Harvey") is behind the supplies counter.

## Tailoring

Energy Tonic is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Letterman Jacket](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt137.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/c5/Shirt137.png/24px-Shirt137.png)](/File:Shirt137.png) It can be also used as a red dye color at the dye pots in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

Muscle Remedy is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Fake Muscles Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt153.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/96/Shirt153.png/24px-Shirt153.png)](/File:Shirt153.png) It can be also used as a yellow dye color at the dye pots in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Glitches

If you pass out from low [health](/Health "Health") while on your horse, both you and the horse will be transported to Harvey's Clinic. Your horse will be stuck inside the Clinic until the morning when it is transported to its stable.
