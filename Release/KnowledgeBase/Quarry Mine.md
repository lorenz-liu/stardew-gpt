# Quarry Mine

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/28/Quarry.png/300px-Quarry.png)](/File:Quarry.png)

The quarry mine entrance, north of the bridge

The **Quarry Mine** cave entrance is located on the left edge of the [Quarry](/Quarry "Quarry"), north of the bridge. It is outside, not located in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"). Inside the cave is a ladder that goes down to the mine. The mine itself consists of one long floor.

At the end of the cave is a statue of the Grim Reaper, and upon first encounter the statue holds the [Golden Scythe](/Golden_Scythe "Golden Scythe"). When the statue is right-clicked on, the scythe will be added to the tool set if the inventory is not full. If it is full, the player will get an "inventory full" message. Freeing up an inventory space will allow them to receive the Golden Scythe. Any time the statue is clicked on afterwards the player will be transported back to the entrance of the quarry mine.

The Quarry Mine contains several [monsters](/Monsters "Monsters"): [Haunted Skulls](/Haunted_Skull "Haunted Skull"), [Copper Slimes](/Slimes#Variations "Slimes"), and [Iron Slimes](/Slimes#Variations "Slimes"). The main mine path is populated with common gray and brown rocks and [Copper and Iron Nodes](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining"). An alternate path blocked by [Boulders](/Boulder "Boulder") contains several [Skeletons](/Skeleton "Skeleton"), including [Skeleton (dangerous)](/Skeleton_(dangerous) "Skeleton (dangerous)") and [Skeleton Mages](/Skeleton_Mage "Skeleton Mage"). This side path also contains [Coal nodes](/Mining#Coal_Node "Mining").

The quarry mine resets at the end of the day, instead of when exiting it, so it cannot be repeatedly "farmed" in a single day.

Passing out due to low health in the Quarry Mine will result in the player being woken up in [Harvey's Clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic"). A fee of data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g will be deducted for emergency surgery. Several items may also be lost. [[1]](#cite_note-minedeath-1)
If the player passes out due to low health while the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") is occurring, they will be woken up at [Harvey's medical station](/Desert_Festival#Harvey.27s_Medical_Station "Desert Festival") in the desert. [[2]](#cite_note-desertfestival-2)

## Special Drops

Monsters, crates, and barrels in the Quarry Mine may randomly drop the same special items that can be found in the [Skull Cavern](/The_Mines#Special_Items "The Mines").

## Notes

* Entering the Quarry Mine before entering [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") for the first time will trigger the cutscene where [Marlon](/Marlon "Marlon") meets the player in the mines and gives them the [Rusty Sword](/Rusty_Sword "Rusty Sword"). After the cutscene is over, the player will still be at the Quarry Mine.
* Unlocking the [Quarry](/Quarry "Quarry") will not enable [Dungeon Floors](/The_Mines#Dungeon_Floors "The Mines") in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") to randomly appear. The Quarry Mine itself must be entered in order to enable Dungeon Floors.
