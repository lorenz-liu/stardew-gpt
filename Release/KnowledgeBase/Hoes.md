# Hoes

The **Hoe** is a [tool](/Tools#Hoes "Tools") obtained at the beginning of the game. It is used primarily to till soil for [Farming](/Farming "Farming"), and to dig up [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot").

The hoe may also be used to till sand in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), on [The Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach"), or in [The Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert"), as well as other areas of Stardew Valley that have exposed soil. Tilling sand in the Mines may yield [Artifacts](/Artifacts "Artifacts"), [Cave Carrots](/Cave_Carrot "Cave Carrot"), and other items. Above ground, tilling soil may yield [Clay](/Clay "Clay"), metal ores, or [Mixed Seeds](/Mixed_Seeds "Mixed Seeds"). In [Winter](/Winter "Winter"), many more areas can be tilled. These may additionally yield [Snow Yams](/Snow_Yam "Snow Yam") or [Winter Roots](/Winter_Root "Winter Root").

The Hoe may be upgraded at the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith"). The cost and resource requirements for each upgrade are shown in the table below. To till more than one tile at a time, hold left-click to "power-up" an upgraded Hoe, then release it to till an area. While charging an upgraded Hoe, the tool hit area can be adjusted by moving the character, who will continue to face in the same direction.

## Notes

Tilling a square that contains an object (like a [Keg](/Keg "Keg") or a [Scarecrow](/Scarecrow "Scarecrow"), with the exception of [Sprinklers](/Crafting#Sprinklers "Crafting")) will remove the object. This can be advantageous for removing large numbers of items like [Kegs](/Keg "Keg") or [Preserves Jars](/Preserves_Jar "Preserves Jar"), but can be disadvantageous when attempting to till farmland. The Hoe will not remove objects from squares that contain crafted [floors or paths](/Crafting#Decor "Crafting"), so placing objects ([Scarecrows](/Scarecrow "Scarecrow"), etc.) on top of floors/paths will allow for tilling land without displacing them.
