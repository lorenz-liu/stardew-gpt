**Ginger** is a root found growing wild on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island"). It must be harvested with a [hoe](/Hoes "Hoes"). It can also be dropped by [Tiger Slimes](/Slimes "Slimes") and [Stick Bugs](/Stick_Bug "Stick Bug").

## Tailoring

It is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Forager's Hat.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/30/Forager%27s_Hat.png/24px-Forager%27s_Hat.png) [Forager's Hat](/Forager%27s_Hat "Forager's Hat"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* Three Ginger may be requested by [Lionfish](/Lionfish "Lionfish") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 3 to 5.
* [Caroline](/Caroline "Caroline") may request 100 Ginger in the ["Island Ingredients"](/Quests#Island_Ingredients "Quests") Special Order. The reward is gold equivalent to 50% of base crop price and the [Solar Panel](/Solar_Panel "Solar Panel") recipe.

## Notes

* Ginger and ginger ale are common remedies for nausea. Though it is not specified in the objects descriptions, eating Ginger or drinking [Ginger Ale](/Ginger_Ale "Ginger Ale") cures the [Nauseated debuff](/Buffs#Nauseated "Buffs"). This is hinted at by ![Birdie Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/97/Birdie_Icon.png/24px-Birdie_Icon.png) [Birdie](/Birdie "Birdie"), the pirate's widow who lives on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island").
