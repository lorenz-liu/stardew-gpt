The **Tulip** is a [flower](/Flowers "Flowers") that can be [grown](/Crops "Crops") from [Tulip Bulbs](/Tulip_Bulb "Tulip Bulb") in 6 days.

If grown within proper range of a [Bee House](/Bee_House#Range "Bee House") (5 squares in any cardinal direction), a Tulip will cause [Tulip Honey](/Honey "Honey") to be produced, increasing the honey base value from data-sort-value="100">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100g to data-sort-value="160">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)160g.

Tulips can be purchased from the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="30"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100–1,000g.

## Bundles

Tulip is one of the options for the ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Garden Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Garden_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Pantry (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Pantry "Remixed Bundles").

## Recipes

Tulip is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Tulip can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Skirt](/Tailoring#Pants "Tailoring"). [![Skirt.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/fa/Skirt.png/36px-Skirt.png)](/File:Skirt.png) It can also be used as a red dye in the dye pots, located at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Tulip is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Notes

* Tulips have an equal chance of being one of five different colors. Tulips of different colors will occupy different slots in inventory or chests. Five colors × four [quality](/Crops#Crop_Quality "Crops") levels = 20 possible spaces used.

:   [![TulipColors.png](/mediawiki/images/3/39/TulipColors.png)](/File:TulipColors.png)

* Due to its relatively small profit margin, it may be better to grow Tulips for aesthetic purposes or to use as gifts than for profit.
* Their cheap price, short growing period and reasonable energy restoration rate makes them surprisingly useful as early-game energy source.
