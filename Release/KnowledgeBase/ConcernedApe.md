# ConcernedApe

[![Developed By Image.gif](/mediawiki/images/0/05/Developed_By_Image.gif)](/File:Developed_By_Image.gif)

**ConcernedApe** is Eric Barone, the creator and lead developer of [Stardew Valley](/Stardew_Valley "Stardew Valley"), as well as the name of the company he founded to continue game development. From 2012 to 2019, he was the sole developer of the game (except the multiplayer networking code in [1.3](/Version_History#1.3.27 "Version History")) and created all art, sound effects, and music. He currently resides in [Seattle, Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle "wikipedia:Seattle").

In early 2019, ConcernedApe formed a small development team to continue updating the game. The [1.4 update](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History") of the game was developed by ConcernedApe, Arthur Lee (a.k.a. Mr. Podunkian), and Alex Erlandson (a.k.a. zillix). The [1.5 update](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History") of the game was developed by ConcernedApe and Arthur Lee. See *[Stardew Valley](/Stardew_Valley "Stardew Valley")* for more info about the game's development.

In late 2021 he announced [*ConcernedApe's Haunted Chocolatier*](https://www.hauntedchocolatier.net), a new game still in development. ConcernedApe began work on this game in late 2020 as the sole developer.

## Trivia

* Setting the [favorite thing](/Secrets#Favorite_Thing "Secrets") when starting the game to "ConcernedApe" will trigger special dialogue upon consumption of a [Stardrop](/Stardrop "Stardrop").
* When launching Stardew Valley, if the player [clicks on ConcernedApe's face](/Secrets#Intro_Screens "Secrets"), he will open his eyes or remove his sunglasses and a 'splat' or honk sound will play. There is also a 2% chance that the screen and ConcernedApe logo will turn green and have leaves fill the screen, similar to the effect when drinking the [Forest Magic](/Forest_Magic "Forest Magic") potion.[[1]](#cite_note-specialsurprised-1)
