**Banana Pudding** is a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit"). 1-2 Banana Pudding may occasionally be found in [Supply Crates](/Supply_Crate "Supply Crate") on the [Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps#Beach "Farm Maps") provided the player has upgraded the [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") at least two times.

Banana Pudding is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Recipes

Banana Pudding is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Banana Pudding is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Banana Shirt.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a6/Banana_Shirt.png/24px-Banana_Shirt.png) [Banana Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a yellow dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the yellow dye pot at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"), for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").
