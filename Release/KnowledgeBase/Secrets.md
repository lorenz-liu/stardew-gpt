# Secrets

This page lists many of the **Secrets** discovered in *[Stardew Valley](/Stardew_Valley "Stardew Valley")*.

## Recurring Easter Eggs

There are a few Easter eggs that are recurring or interlinked in Stardew Valley.

### Hats

[Hats](/Hats "Hats") can be placed on...

* [Children](/Children "Children") (of toddler age), [horses](/Animals#Horse "Animals"), [cats, and dogs](/Animals#Pets "Animals"). They can be removed by holding another hat and clicking on them again; that will pop off the old hat, but won't place the new hat unless you do it again.
* [Rarecrow 3 (Alien)](/Scarecrow#Rarecrows "Scarecrow"). They can then be swapped with another hat, but can only be fully removed by breaking the rarecrow which drops both items.
* [Sea urchins](/Sea_Urchin "Sea Urchin") in [fish tanks](/Fish_Tank "Fish Tank") by holding the hat and clicking the fish tank. They can be removed by opening the fish tank inventory and taking the hat.
* [Mannequins](/Mannequins "Mannequins") and [Cursed Mannequins](/Cursed_Mannequins "Cursed Mannequins") by holding the hat and clicking the mannequin.

### Lucky Purple Shorts

[![](/mediawiki/images/0/04/Springobjects789.png)](/File:Springobjects789.png)

Lucky Purple Shorts

[![](/mediawiki/images/e/e7/Springobjects071.png)](/File:Springobjects071.png)

Trimmed Lucky Purple Shorts

[Mayor Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis")' [lucky purple shorts](/Quests#Mayor.27s_.E2.80.9CShorts.E2.80.9D "Quests") have a number of secrets.

* Putting them in the [Luau](/Luau "Luau") soup gets a unique response from the [Governor](/Governor "Governor") and [Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis").
* Putting them in the [Stardew Valley Fair](/Stardew_Valley_Fair "Stardew Valley Fair") grange display disqualifies the player, and [Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis") gives the player data-sort-value="750">![Token.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/29/Token.png/24px-Token.png)750 star tokens as a bribe to keep his secret. [Marnie](/Marnie "Marnie") also gives unique dialogue at the Fair after seeing the shorts in the display.
* [Tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring") them with a [gold bar](/Gold_Bar "Gold Bar") produces Trimmed Lucky Purple Shorts. These are equivalent to the Lucky Purple Shorts for completing the quest or triggering some of the other secrets, but it can also be worn by the player for an amusing reaction from [Marnie](/Marnie "Marnie") and [Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis").
* Giving Lewis Trimmed Lucky Purple Shorts to complete the "Mayor's Shorts" quest while he is visiting [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") will cause him to wear them as beach attire any time he visits the island again for the next 28 days. This cannot be done with normal Lucky Purple Shorts.
* Attempting to gift them to [Marnie](/Marnie "Marnie") gives unique dialogue.
* Placing a [staircase](/Staircase "Staircase") in [Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis")' bedroom gives access to a secret maze room with these shorts at the end. Attempting to pick up the shorts will spawn a cursed Lucky Purple Shorts that slowly attacks the player and is immune to attacks. This process is repeatable to obtain multiple shorts.
* Putting them in the [tackle](/Tackle "Tackle") slot of a fishing rod changes the [bobber style](/Fish_Shop#Bobber_Machine "Fish Shop") to a unique Lucky Purple Shorts bobber. If placed in the first slot of an [Advanced Iridium Rod](/Advanced_Iridium_Rod "Advanced Iridium Rod"), the shorts prevent tackle in the second slot from losing durability. This cannot be done with Trimmed Lucky Purple Shorts.
* Lucky Purple Shorts can be placed into [Fish Tanks](/Fish_Tank "Fish Tank") as a decoration item. This cannot be done with Trimmed Lucky Purple Shorts.

### Dove Children

[Turning children into doves](/Children#Dismissing_Children "Children") unlocks several Easter eggs.

* You may receive a mysterious [telephone](/Telephone "Telephone") call: "*You place the receiver to your ear, and are met with a roiling, abrasive static. But wait... beneath it all, you can make out something else... an otherworldly voice... 'Y-O-U H-A-V-E F-O-R-S-A-K-E-N U-S'*". This no longer happens once you unlock the cursed doll spawn below.

* Watching TV on Fall 26 (the day before [Spirit's Eve](/Spirit%27s_Eve "Spirit's Eve")) and choosing "???" will show an [Ancient Doll](/Ancient_Doll "Ancient Doll") on the TV screen with an eerie message ("*You've brought this upon yourself... now I'm free... Hee hee hee!*"), and an [Ancient Doll](/Ancient_Doll "Ancient Doll") will pop out of the TV. Thereafter, a cursed Ancient Doll will fly around and attack players in front of the [Dark Shrine of Selfishness](/Dark_Shrine_of_Selfishness "Dark Shrine of Selfishness"). When slain, it will turn into a large black bird and fly away. Exiting and re-entering the Witch's Hut will respawn the doll. Killing this doll counts toward the [Monster Eradication goal](/Adventurer%27s_Guild#Monster_Eradication_Goals "Adventurer's Guild") of killing 200 bats, and awards 20 [Combat](/Combat "Combat") XP.

* On the [Four-Corners Farm](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps"), players may catch an Ancient Doll once while fishing.

* If you've turned multiple children into doves, there's a 15% chance they'll fly past in formation at [the Summit](/The_Summit "The Summit").

* Doves may appear at a [Bird House](/Bird_House "Bird House") placed on the farm after using the [Dark Shrine of Selfishness](/Dark_Shrine_of_Selfishness "Dark Shrine of Selfishness").

### Bad at Music

In [multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer") mode, if a player's randomized unique multiplayer ID (only editable in the [save file](/Saves "Saves")) is divisible by 111, music they produce will sound off:

* The [music emote](/Multiplayer/Emotes "Multiplayer/Emotes") will produce a random off-tune strum instead of one of the normal melodies.
* The piano in [Elliott's Cabin](/Elliott%27s_Cabin "Elliott's Cabin") will play off-key tones (F4, C5, D5, G#5) instead of the usual ones (B4, D#5, E5, F#5), and the [other piano Easter egg](#Elliott.27s_Piano) won't trigger.

### Feast of the Winter Star

If one of the following items is placed on a table or a long table, before the night of Winter 24, the eve before [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"), it will be replaced with a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") the next morning.

* [Milk](/Milk "Milk")
* [Large Milk](/Large_Milk "Large Milk")
* [Goat Milk](/Goat_Milk "Goat Milk")
* [Large Goat Milk](/Large_Goat_Milk "Large Goat Milk")
* [Cookies](/Cookies "Cookies")

## Character Creation

### Special Characters

When entering "Name", "Farm Name", or "Favorite Thing" on the [Character Creation](/The_Player#Character_customization "The Player") menu, certain keys produce special sounds and/or symbols.

* Pressing the following keys will produce special sounds.
  + Pressing `<` produces a "ding" sound
  + Pressing `$` produces a "plink" sound
  + Pressing `*` produces a "stone hit" sound
  + Pressing `=` produces a "pop" sound
  + Pressing `+` produces a "squish" sound
* Pressing the following keys will produce special characters.

:   * Pressing `<` produces `♡`, a heart
    * Pressing `>` produces `▷`, a right triangular arrow
    * Pressing `@` produces `◁`, a left triangular arrow
    * Pressing `$` produces `⌾`, a double circle
    * Pressing `*` produces `💢`, the "anger" symbol common in Japanese media
    * Pressing `=` produces `☆`, a star
:   [![SpecialChars.png](/mediawiki/images/9/9e/SpecialChars.png)](/File:SpecialChars.png)

* Pressing controller buttons also produces special sounds and/or symbols.

### Item Spawn Cheat

**PC/Mobile only:** naming your character using an internal [item ID](/Modding:Common_data_field_types#Item_ID "Modding:Common data field types") in brackets will give the corresponding item whenever an NPC mentions your character by name. For example, entering `[645][499][(O)163]` will give an [Iridium Sprinkler](/Iridium_Sprinkler "Iridium Sprinkler"), [Ancient Seeds](/Ancient_Seeds "Ancient Seeds"), and a [Legend](/Legend "Legend"). Since this works with qualified item IDs (`[(O)163]` in the preceding example), it can be used to spawn furniture, weapons, etc. This exploit also works with naming children and animals.

When using this cheat, one of five random messages will pop up in the chat window:

* *ConcernedApe: What kind of a name is that? Seems fishy...*
* *Mr. Qi: Easy <item>, huh? I'm disappointed, kid. I thought you wanted to beat this game fair and square?*
* *\*Grandpa is shaking his head... 'I built this farm the old fashioned way, not with unlimited <items>...'*
* *ConcernedApe is mad that you're cheating...*  
  *ConcernedApe: I'm not actually mad... have fun.*
* *Calling all cars! Calling all cars! We've got a/an <item> thief on the loose!*

### Lonely Stone

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/c9/EasterEggLonelyStone.png/200px-EasterEggLonelyStone.png)](/File:EasterEggLonelyStone.png)

The lonely stone on the Stardew Valley map.

The lonely stone is not an area, but it has a location on the Stardew Valley map. Upon clicking the lonely stone on the map, a rock sound effect will play.

The lonely stone also hasn't been mentioned by any villagers/characters nor is there any lore about it mentioned by ConcernedApe or hidden in the game's files.

### Gift Log

When you have at least four hearts of [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") with a character, clicking their sprite in the gift log (accessed from the social tab by clicking their entry) will play a sound and show a special animation (different for each character) for a few seconds.

### Meowmere

![Meowmere.png](/mediawiki/images/6/63/Meowmere.png)

If the player throws an [Ancient Doll](/Ancient_Doll "Ancient Doll") into the lava pool in floor 100 of [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), or in the lava next to the chest at the [Forge](/Forge "Forge") on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island"), a bone serpent from Terraria will rise from the pool and give them the [Far Away Stone](/Far_Away_Stone "Far Away Stone"). There are two secrets with the piano in In [Elliott's Cabin](/Elliott%27s_Cabin "Elliott's Cabin"):

* When [Elliott](/Elliott "Elliott") is working at his desk, playing the first eight notes of the Stardew Valley Overture on the piano will make Elliott turn towards the player with a heart emote. (This doesn't affect his friendship with the player.) If you label the piano keys 1–4 from left to right, the tune can be played with these notes at any speed: 2432 3421.
* See also the [bad at music](#Bad_at_Music) secret.

### Beach Creature

Very rarely, a [green creature](/The_Merpeople "The Merpeople") may appear south of the main docks at [the beach](/The_Beach "The Beach"). There's no way to interact with the creature; after a short while, it'll swim south and disappear off-screen.

There's a roughly 0.06% chance per second (0.001% per game tick) that the creature will try spawning in a random tile near the docks (see spawn area below). The creature will only spawn if that random tile is open water, and there's a vertical line of open water between that tile and the bottom of the map, which significantly reduces the likelihood. There's no limit to the number of creatures that may spawn, but more than one creature at once is extraordinarily rare.

The same creature can be seen in [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus")' 14 heart event, in which it carries the shadow person on its tentacles (note that in this event, the creature is not a random occurrence like the one found during regular gameplay).

This creature is larger than the dark creature that may appear after the [Strange Capsule event](#Strange_Capsule). There is a very small random chance that this creature will leave a footprint behind.

### Slimes

* Slimes can be hatched in odd places using a [Slime Incubator](/Slime_Incubator "Slime Incubator"), including [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") or the [Railroad](/Railroad "Railroad"). Slimes hatched in the town are known to interact with the townsfolk, including greeting them by name and having the townsfolk greet them back. Note that this doesn't work in all locations, in some places slimes will be removed overnight.
* Slimes can also be hatched inside the [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse"). If you are married, your spouse may react to the slime with various funny dialogue while trying to kill it.

### Summit

* Achieving perfection in [Mr. Qi](/Mr._Qi "Mr. Qi")'s walnut room unlocks access to [the Summit](/The_Summit "The Summit"). When you visit it, there's a chance you'll see something fly past in the distance:

:   * 25% chance of a seasonal bird;
    * else if you've turned multiple children into doves, there's a 15% chance they'll fly past in formation;
    * else if you've seen [Harvey's 8-heart event](/Harvey#Eight_Hearts "Harvey"), there's a 10% chance a jet flies past;
    * else if you've seen [Maru's 10-heart event](/Maru#Ten_Hearts "Maru"), there's a 5% chance that MarILDA will fly past.

* Santa Claus may show up on Winter 25th.
* Attempting to access the Summit before achieving [perfection](/Perfection "Perfection") triggers a cutscene in which [Mr. Qi](/Mr._Qi "Mr. Qi") scolds the player and pushes them off the Summit.

### Iridium Stackmaster Trophy

If playing in English only: When the player picks up items, a stack is displayed on the left of the screen. Having the stack reach 1 million of the same item will award the player with the "Secret Iridium Stackmaster Trophy Achieved" message in chat, followed by another message from [Mr. Qi](/Mr._Qi "Mr. Qi") that says: "*Qi:💢slow clap💢... Congratulations, kid. Ya did it. Now, on to the next challenge!*" [[6]](#cite_note-stackmaster-6)

Throughout the process, messages in chat will hint at the player that they are reaching the secret. They appear as follows:

* 100,000 items: "*...*"
* 200,000 items: "*......*"
* 250,000 items: "*Shooting for a million?*"
* 500,000 items: "*.......................*"
