**Sea Jelly** is an item that can be caught using a [Fishing Pole](/Tools#Fishing_Poles "Tools") during all seasons at [the Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach"), on the [Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps#Beach "Farm Maps"), the submarine ride at the [Night Market](/Night_Market "Night Market"), and at [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") West (ocean), South, Southeast, and the [Pirate Cove](/Ginger_Island#Pirate_Cove "Ginger Island"). Each level of [Luck Buff](/Luck#Luck_Buffs "Luck") applied significantly increases the chance of catching it. [Daily Luck](/Luck#Daily_Luck "Luck") has no effect.

It can also be acquired from a [Flounder](/Flounder "Flounder") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") with a population of at least 7. The [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") may trade 3 Sea Jelly for 1 [Jewels Of The Sea](/Jewels_Of_The_Sea "Jewels Of The Sea") each, provided the player has already earned the power from that book. It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") turtle with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals").

Sea Jelly is displayed in the [Fish section](/Collections#Fish "Collections") of the [collection](/Collections "Collections") tab, and counts towards the four fishing [Achievements](/Fishing#Achievements "Fishing"). However, it is otherwise not considered to be a fish, and therefore cannot be sold to [Willy](/Willy "Willy") at the [Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop"), does not benefit from fish price bonuses, cannot be used in place of "Any Fish" in [recipes](/Cooking#Recipes "Cooking"), and cannot be put in a [Bait Maker](/Bait_Maker "Bait Maker"), [Fish Smoker](/Fish_Smoker "Fish Smoker"), or [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond").

## Tailoring

Sea Jelly is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a blue dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a cyan dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Bundles

Sea Jelly is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Quests

One Sea Jelly is requested by [Flounder](/Flounder "Flounder") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 5 to 10.

## Strategy

The highest possible rates for Sea Jelly are when fishing on the beach in [Ginger Island West](/Ginger_Island#Island_West "Ginger Island") using a [Training Rod](/Training_Rod "Training Rod"). Under these conditions, there are only 2 possible catches: Sea Jelly and [Snake Skull](/Snake_Skull "Snake Skull"), as all of the fish that can be caught have difficulty greater than or equal to 50. Only if the farmer fails the random check to catch both of these will the farmer get [Trash](/Trash "Trash").

It is also possible to achieve similar rates however on [Ginger Island South](/Ginger_Island#Island_South "Ginger Island"), or on [the Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") during [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), although [Seaweed](/Seaweed "Seaweed") instead of Snake Skulls will be the alternative drop.

However, unlike when catching regular fish or other items, the random value used to determine whether a Sea Jelly is successfully caught is predetermined. This value only changes when catching a [Cave](/Cave_Jelly "Cave Jelly"), [River](/River_Jelly "River Jelly"), or Sea Jelly, or any [Fish](/Fish "Fish"). It is not changed by catching Seaweed, Trash, or any other catchable items.[[1]](#cite_note-jelly_RNG-1) Because of this, after catching Trash using the Training Rod, it is necessary to catch a regular fish before another Sea Jelly. This can easily be done by switching to any [Fishing Pole](/Fishing_Pole "Fishing Pole") other than the Training Rod, continuing to fish until an actual fish is caught, and then switching back to the Training Rod.

Ordinarily, 10% of possible random values result in a successful Sea Jelly catch. Each level of [Luck Buff](/Luck#Luck_Buffs "Luck") increases the chance of a successful catch by 5%.[[2]](#cite_note-jelly_rate-2) So with +8 Luck, 50% of random values will result in a successful catch. This allows for longer streaks without failing a Sea Jelly catch, although the player will still unavoidably catch some Snake Skulls or Seaweed, depending on the area chosen.
