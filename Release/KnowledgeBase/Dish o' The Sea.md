**Dish O' The Sea** is a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit"). It may also randomly appear in [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus")' shop on [Saturdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Saturday "Days of the Week") or the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="220"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)660–1,100g. Five Dish O' The Sea may occasionally be found in a [treasure room](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern") in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"). One Dish O' The Sea is a guaranteed silver tier reward on the 12th and 13th for the [SquidFest](/SquidFest "SquidFest") festival. Two Dishes O' The Sea may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") or [Golden Mystery Box](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box").

The reward for completing the ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Specialty Fish Bundle](/Bundles#Specialty_Fish_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Fish Tank](/Bundles#Fish_Tank "Bundles"), ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Master Fisher's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Master_Fishers_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Fish Tank (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Fish_Tank "Remixed Bundles"), or ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Quality Fish Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Quality_Fish_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Fish Tank (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Fish_Tank "Remixed Bundles") is 5 Dish O' The Sea.

## Bundles

Dish O' The Sea is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Recipes

Dish O' The Sea is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Dish O' The Sea is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Shirt O' The Sea](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt214.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/4c/Shirt214.png/24px-Shirt214.png)](/File:Shirt214.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Dish O' The Sea is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
