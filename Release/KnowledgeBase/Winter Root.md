The **Winter Root** is a [foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") item found only in [Winter](/Winter "Winter"). Wild winter roots do not grow above ground, but instead they are buried in the snow/dirt and must be dug up with a [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes") (no other tools, even [Bombs](/Crafting#Bombs "Crafting"), will work).

The highest chance of finding a Winter Root (17-24%, map-dependent) is when digging up an [Artifact Spot](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot").[[1]](#cite_note-artifact_chance-1) There is also a 4% chance of finding a Winter Root when digging standard tillable soil in any exterior location.[[2]](#cite_note-soil_chance-2) Exceptions are in [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"), where Winter Roots can be found at artifact spots, but not standard tillable soil, and both [the Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") and [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island"), where Winter Roots can not be found at all. Digging up a Winter Root provides no experience.

Winter Roots can also be grown from [Winter Seeds](/Winter_Seeds "Winter Seeds"), in which case they are a standard wild-seed crop: they grow above ground, can be harvested without using a tool, give 7 Foraging XP each, and are affected by the [Gatherer](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") and [Botanist](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") professions.

Occasionally they may be sold at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") or found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") in [Winter](/Winter "Winter"). They may also be dropped by Blue [Slimes](/Slimes "Slimes") (8% chance) in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") (floors 41-79) or [Secret Woods](/Secret_Woods "Secret Woods"). One Winter Root can sometimes be purchased for data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g from [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre")'s booth at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star").

Putting a Winter Root in a [Seed Maker](/Seed_Maker "Seed Maker") will generate 1-3 [Winter Seeds](/Winter_Seeds "Winter Seeds").

## Tailoring

Winter Root is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [![Shirt267.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/79/Shirt267.png/24px-Shirt267.png)](/File:Shirt267.png) [Orange Bow Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* Winter Root may be randomly requested in [Winter](/Winter "Winter") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="140">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)140g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.

## Trivia

* Winter Roots are the only winter forageable that can be directly put into the [Seed Maker](/Seed_Maker "Seed Maker").
