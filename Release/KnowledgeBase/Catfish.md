The **Catfish** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught in the river (in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") or [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest")), and the [Riverlands Farm](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps"), [Forest Farm](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps"), or [Hill-Top Farm](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps") during [Spring](/Spring "Spring") and [Fall](/Fall "Fall") when raining, or in the [Secret Woods](/Secret_Woods "Secret Woods") or [Witch's Swamp](/Witch%27s_Swamp "Witch's Swamp") during [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), and [Fall](/Fall "Fall") when raining. It can also be caught in [Winter](/Winter "Winter") at these locations if a [Rain Totem](/Rain_Totem "Rain Totem") is used.

In addition, it can also be acquired, from the following:

* A [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") cat with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals"), may gift it to the player.
* [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") may randomly contain one during Spring and Fall.
* The [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") may randomly sell one for data-sort-value="200"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)600–1,000g.
* [Linus](/Linus "Linus") may randomly send a Catfish in the mail as a gift.
* One Catfish is contained in the [Community Center Fish Tank](/Bundles#Fish_Tank "Bundles") right after it is repaired.
* Catfish can be caught regardless of weather, time, or season using [Magic Bait](/Magic_Bait "Magic Bait").

## Fish Pond

Catfish can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 4 days. The initial pond capacity is 3 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing three quests. The only possible output is dark gray Catfish [Roe](/Roe "Roe").

## History

* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Removed from garbage cans and most quests in Winter. Added iridium quality. Can now be used in [Tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring").
* [1.4.4](/Version_History#1.4.4 "Version History"): Fixed a mistake in the game data that prevented catfish-specific fish pond data from being applied (in earlier versions, catfish used the same fish pond data as all generic river fish). Catfish can no longer be requested by [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius") or [Willy](/Willy "Willy") in a [Fishing Help Wanted quest](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") during [Winter](/Winter "Winter").
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): One is contained in the [Community Center Fish Tank](/Bundles#Fish_Tank "Bundles") right after it is repaired. Can now be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") cat with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals").
