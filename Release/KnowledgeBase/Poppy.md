The **Poppy** is a [flower](/Flowers "Flowers") that can be [grown](/Crops "Crops") from [Poppy Seeds](/Poppy_Seeds "Poppy Seeds") in 7 days. Unlike all other flowers, Poppies are a [universally hated gift](/Friendship#Universal_Hates "Friendship"); [Penny](/Penny "Penny"), who loves Poppies, is the only exception.

If placed within proper range of a [Bee House](/Bee_House#Range "Bee House") (5 squares in any cardinal direction), a Poppy will cause [Poppy Honey](/Honey "Honey") to be produced, increasing the honey base value from data-sort-value="100">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100g to data-sort-value="380">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)380g.

Poppies can be purchased from the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="140"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)420–1,000g.

## Tailoring

Poppy can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Pleated Skirt](/Tailoring#Pants "Tailoring"). [![Pleated Skirt.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/92/Pleated_Skirt.png/36px-Pleated_Skirt.png)](/File:Pleated_Skirt.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Notes

* Poppies have an equal chance of being one of three different colors. Poppies of different colors will occupy different slots in inventory or chests. Three colors × four [quality](/Crops#Crop_Quality "Crops") levels = 12 possible spaces used.
