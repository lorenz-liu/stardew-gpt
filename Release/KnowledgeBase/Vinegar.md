**Vinegar** is a cooking ingredient that can be used in a variety of recipes. It can be purchased for data-sort-value="200">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)200g at [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). It can also be purchased from [George](/George "George")'s [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="5">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 5 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg"). Two Vinegar can also be made by putting [Rice](/Rice "Rice") into a [Keg](/Keg "Keg").

When used on a [tree](/Trees "Trees"), it prevents the tree from growing [Moss](/Moss "Moss").
