# Achievements

**Achievements** are displayed on the player menu, under the "[Collections](/Collections "Collections")" tab, on the "Achievements" tab found at the bottom left, on every platform other than Xbox and PlayStation. If playing the game on Steam or PlayStation, additional achievements are available.

Achievements can unlock specific rewards within the game. Achievements for specific platforms, such as Steam, only need to be achieved once, but the associated rewards must be earned separately for each new game.

Unearned achievements appear as grayed-out on the player menu. Some achievements do not display on the player menu until completing other achievements.

Note that only in-game achievements are required for [Perfection](/Perfection "Perfection").

Most achievements unlock a unique [hat](/Hats "Hats") for sale at the [abandoned house](/Abandoned_House "Abandoned House") in [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest"). In addition, the day after earning "Gofer" (Complete 10 [Help Wanted](/Quests "Quests") requests), [Mayor Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis") will send data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g in the mail. The day after earning "A Big Help" (Complete 40 [Help Wanted](/Quests "Quests") requests), Mayor Lewis will send data-sort-value="2500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,500g in the mail. The day after earning "Master Angler" (Catch every [fish](/Fish "Fish")), Willy will send a [Stardrop](/Stardrop "Stardrop") in the mail.

## Notes

### Fishing Achievements

Catching [Green Algae](/Green_Algae "Green Algae"), [White Algae](/White_Algae "White Algae"), [Seaweed](/Seaweed "Seaweed"), [River Jelly](/River_Jelly "River Jelly"), [Sea Jelly](/Sea_Jelly "Sea Jelly"), or [Cave Jelly](/Cave_Jelly "Cave Jelly") counts towards the 4 fishing achievements (Fisherman, Ol' Mariner, Master Angler, and Mother Catch). Catching [Trash](/Trash "Trash") does not count towards these achievements. Catching the [quest fish](/Fish#Legendary_Fish_II "Fish") for the [Extended Family Qi's quest](/Quests#Extended_Family "Quests") are not required to obtain the Master Angler achievement.

### Full Shipment

To earn the Full Shipment achievement ("Ship every item"), at least one of every item shown in the [Shipping Collection tab](/Shipping#Collection "Shipping") of the player menu must be shipped.

### 'Help Wanted' Quests

The weekly quests on the [Special Orders](/Quests#List_of_Special_Orders "Quests") board and the weekly quests on [Qi's Special Orders](/Quests#List_of_Mr._Qi.27s_Special_Orders "Quests") board count towards the Gofer ("Complete 10 'Help Wanted' requests") and A Big Help ("Complete 40 'Help Wanted' requests") achievements.

### Friendship Achievements

The level of [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") with the player's [children](/Children "Children") affects friendship achievements ("A New Friend", "Best Friends", "The Beloved Farmer", "Cliques", "Networking", and "Popular").

### Crafting Achievements

The [Wedding Ring](/Wedding_Ring "Wedding Ring") does not count towards crafting achievements ("D.I.Y.", "Artisan", and "Craft Master"), even in [Multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer") games.

## Retroactive Achievements

An achievement may not unlock properly in rare cases (*e.g.,* because the game couldn't send the achievement to the underlying platform like Steam). Most missed achievements will be unlocked automatically when loading the save or sleeping in-game; if a specific achievement doesn't unlock, see the table below.

On PlayStation and Xbox consoles only:

:   The game isn't allowed to resend trophies/achievements automatically. It will instead resend those when a relevant action is performed (see table below).
:   **Bug:** an achievement may unlock without the matching trophy getting unlocked (*e.g.* because you were offline). If you slept in-game after that happens, it's not possible to unlock the trophy again on that save. As of the upcoming Stardew Valley 1.6.16: this is fixed in the next update.
