# Night Market

[![NightMarket.jpg](/mediawiki/images/thumb/5/57/NightMarket.jpg/500px-NightMarket.jpg)](/File:NightMarket.jpg)

The **Night Market** is a [festival](/Festivals "Festivals") that occurs between the 15th and 17th of [Winter](/Winter "Winter"). Various boats and merchants will appear at the docks on [the Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach"), and offer to sell goods or take the player for a boat ride. The Night Market is open between 5pm and 2am, but the Submarine Ride closes at 11pm, and the Mermaid Boat closes at 12:30am.

Unlike other festivals, shops and homes throughout Stardew Valley (including those at the beach) are *not* closed on the days of the event, and time continues to pass during the festival. [Animals](/Animals "Animals") also need to be fed during the event; the game will not automatically consider them to be fed.

## Attractions

### Desert Trader

Available from 5pm to 2am. The [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") will give the player a free cup of [coffee](/Coffee "Coffee") each night.

### Decoration Boat

Available from 5pm to 2am. The blue boat between the docks sells a variety of purely aesthetic decorations, some of which change with the seasons. Players can purchase an unlimited number of each decoration. Decorations can be placed indoors or outdoors.

The Decoration Boat is the only place where the candy cane decorations can be purchased. [Seasonal Decor](/Seasonal_Decor "Seasonal Decor") and the [Seasonal Plants](/Seasonal_Plant "Seasonal Plant") can be purchased at other festivals/shops.

### Fishing Submarine

Available to enter from 5pm to 11pm. The Submarine houses a Captain who offers to take the player on a deep-sea [fishing](/Fishing "Fishing") tour for data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g. Upon accepting, the submarine descends for approximately 30 minutes in-game time, and then a trapdoor opens to allow the player to fish. Simply talking to the Captain again brings the submarine back up, also in about 30 minutes. **Fishing can continue until 2am**, when the player collapses exhausted. A [Warp Totem](/Warp_Totem "Warp Totem") or a [Return Scepter](/Return_Scepter "Return Scepter") can be used to leave, maximizing fishing time without collapse.

Three [fish](/Fish "Fish"), [Midnight Squid](/Midnight_Squid "Midnight Squid"), [Spook Fish](/Spook_Fish "Spook Fish"), and [Blobfish](/Blobfish "Blobfish"), can only be caught in the Submarine, unless the player uses [Magic Bait](/Magic_Bait "Magic Bait"). Additionally, [Seaweed](/Seaweed "Seaweed"), [Sea Jelly](/Sea_Jelly "Sea Jelly"), [Sea Cucumber](/Sea_Cucumber "Sea Cucumber"), [Octopus](/Octopus "Octopus"), and [Super Cucumber](/Super_Cucumber "Super Cucumber") can be caught here, and there is a rare chance to catch a [Pearl](/Pearl "Pearl").

However, when fishing with an [Iridium Rod](/Iridium_Rod "Iridium Rod") with a [Curiosity Lure](/Curiosity_Lure "Curiosity Lure"), chances of catching some of the items increase. Specifically, the chances of catching a [Blobfish](/Blobfish "Blobfish"), [Spook Fish](/Spook_Fish "Spook Fish"), [Super Cucumber](/Super_Cucumber "Super Cucumber"), or [Pearl](/Pearl "Pearl") is increased with the lure. Consequently, the chances of catching the other items decrease.

In addition, if the player uses a [Training Rod](/Training_Rod "Training Rod"), only the [Sea Cucumber](/Sea_Cucumber "Sea Cucumber"), [Pearl](/Pearl "Pearl"), [Seaweed](/Seaweed "Seaweed"), and [Sea Jelly](/Sea_Jelly "Sea Jelly") can be caught.

### Magic Shop Boat

Available from 5pm to 2am. The magic shop boat is located to the right of Famous Painter Lupini. It sells decorative items, seeds, a unique hat, and a unique fireplace, with different stock each night. Each item may be purchased in unlimited quantities. Seed sell prices are the same as at [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store").

Note that [Garlic Seeds](/Garlic_Seeds "Garlic Seeds"), [Red Cabbage Seeds](/Red_Cabbage_Seeds "Red Cabbage Seeds"), and [Artichoke Seeds](/Artichoke_Seeds "Artichoke Seeds") are not available until Year 2. Note also that the price of the [Cone Hat](/Cone_Hat "Cone Hat") changes over the 3 days of the Night Market, with the lowest price occurring on Day 2. The [Grave Stone](/Grave_Stone "Grave Stone") sold on Day 1 costs data-sort-value="150">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150g less at the Night Market than it does at the [Spirit's Eve](/Spirit%27s_Eve "Spirit's Eve") Festival. The [Log Section](/Log_Section "Log Section") sold on Day 3 costs data-sort-value="150">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150g less at the Night Market than it does at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop").

[Rarecrows](/Scarecrow#Rarecrows "Scarecrow") #7 and #8 and the [Crystal Chair](/Crystal_Chair "Crystal Chair") can be purchased only after earning the first ones by donating items to the [Museum](/Museum "Museum").

### Mermaid Boat

Available from 5pm to 12:30am. Upon entering, a mermaid will appear and begin singing to the player, accompanied by a cutscene. The performance lasts 1 hour and 40 minutes. The player can leave the boat at any time, but leaving will restart the show.

After the show, clam shells inside the boat can be clicked to play musical notes. Playing the correct notes in the correct sequence will award the player a special item.

The correct sequence of musical notes can be found in [Secret Note #15](/Secret_Notes#Secret_Note_.2315 "Secret Notes"), although finding the secret note is not required to complete the sequence. The tune played during the mermaid show also indicates the solution. Click below to see the solution and information about the special item awarded.

| Solution |
| --- |
| When played in the order 1-5-4-2-3 (left to right), the player will be awarded with a [Pearl](/Pearl "Pearl"). Only one Pearl can be obtained in the Mermaid Boat by each player per each [saved game](/Saves "Saves"), but the Mermaid Show can be viewed repeatedly by exiting and re-entering the boat at any time. There are also other ways of obtaining Pearls which are unaffected by obtaining this Pearl. |

### Shrouded Figure

Available from 5pm to 2am. The Shrouded Figure offers to teleport the player back to their farm for data-sort-value="250">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)250g. It functions the same as the [Warp Totem: Farm](/Warp_Totem:_Farm "Warp Totem: Farm").

### Traveling Cart

Available from 5pm to 2am. The [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") makes an appearance at the docks with the cart on a raft and the pig in a life-preserver. A selection of the usual items is sold (with a new re-randomized stock for each of the 3 days), including the [Wedding Ring](/Wedding_Ring "Wedding Ring") recipe in a Multiplayer game.

## Villager Attendees

Unlike other [Festivals](/Festivals "Festivals"), [villagers](/Villagers "Villagers") can be seen deviating from their normal schedules to walk to [the Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") to attend the Night Market. These villagers cannot be found outside the Night Market during the festival. The lists below show which villagers attend on which days each year.

Note that it's possible to complete Gathering, Slaying Monsters, and Fishing [Quests](/Quests#Types "Quests") during the Night Market. Talking to the villager will complete the quest, before scripted festival dialogue occurs.

Villagers who are [married](/Marriage "Marriage") to [the Player](/The_Player "The Player") do not attend the Night Market, but instead follow their normal Monday/Tuesday/Wednesday marriage schedules.

If [Robin](/Robin "Robin") is working on constructing a building on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"), she will not attend the Night Market.

During the Night Market, [Willy](/Willy "Willy") will head to [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon") earlier and return home later than usual for three days.

## Notes

* Since the game uses a unique beach map during festival days, items a player has placed on [the Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") ([Crab Pots](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot"), [Chests](/Chest "Chest"), etc.) may disappear during the Night Market. These items should return on Winter 18.
  + Additionally, [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") and forage items usually found on the Beach will be unavailable due to the festival, but will continue to spawn nightly. [Tilling](/Hoes "Hoes") sand will still yield the usual [Clay](/Clay "Clay"), [Snow Yams](/Snow_Yam "Snow Yam"), and [Winter Roots](/Winter_Root "Winter Root").
  + The bin next to the [Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop") that is used to complete the ["Juicy Bugs Wanted!"](/Quests#Juicy_Bugs_Wanted "Quests") special order is still present during the Night Market.
* Shimmering lights can appear in the water, and the [Pan](/Pans "Pans") can be used on them to obtain items.

## Trivia

* The ['Red Eagle'](/%27Red_Eagle%27 "'Red Eagle'") painting from Famous Painter Lupini is in the art style of the [Hopi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hopi "wikipedia:Hopi") Native American tribe.
* Occasionally, a mermaid may be seen swimming up past the window behind the submarine captain. Fish that share similar features to the [Spook Fish](/Spook_Fish "Spook Fish") and [Midnight Squid](/Midnight_Squid "Midnight Squid") can be spotted in the background as well, though these cannot be caught in the submarine.

## History

* [1.3.27](/Version_History#1.3.27 "Version History"): Introduced.
* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): The [Coffee Merchant](#Desert_Trader) now sells only one cup per night. Removed exploit where time was paused during the [Submarine](#Fishing_Submarine) ride ascent/descent by opening the player menu in singleplayer games. Removed time pause during the [Mermaid Show](#Mermaid_Boat) when opening the menu. Fixed bug preventing [Shane](/Shane "Shane") from attending the Night Market. Can no longer place items at the Beach during the Night Market.
* [1.4.1](/Version_History#1.4.1 "Version History"): Can now purchase museum [rarecrows](/Scarecrow#Rarecrows "Scarecrow") from the [Magic Shop Boat](#Magic_Shop_Boat) once earned by donating items. The Coffee Merchant is now the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader").
* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): [Fish](/Fish "Fish") can now be caught when fishing outside of the Submarine ride, not just [trash](/Trash "Trash"). Fixed bug where museum-related rarecrows were not sold at the Magic Shop Boat after being unlocked. Fixed bug in multiplayer where multiple people getting the free coffee gift at the same time could prevent anyone from getting it.
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Fixed bug allowing all Night Market fish to be hooked with the [Training Rod](/Training_Rod "Training Rod") during the Submarine ride. [Sea Jelly](/Sea_Jelly "Sea Jelly") can now be caught in the Submarine ride. [Upright Piano](/Upright_Piano "Upright Piano") and [Crystal Chair](/Crystal_Chair "Crystal Chair") can now be purchased from the Magic Shop Boat.
