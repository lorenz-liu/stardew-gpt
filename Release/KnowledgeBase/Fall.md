# Fall

[![Fall.png](/mediawiki/images/5/5d/Fall.png)](/File:Fall.png)

**Fall** is the third [season](/Seasons "Seasons") players play through. It is followed by [Winter](/Winter "Winter") and preceded by [Summer](/Summer "Summer"). Some of the most valuable [crops](/Crops "Crops") can grow during Fall. During this season the leaves of some trees will fall except for those that have been tapped.
