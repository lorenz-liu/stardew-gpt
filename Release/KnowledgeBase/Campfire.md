The **Campfire** is a [crafted lighting item](/Crafting#Lighting "Crafting"). It provides light at night. Right-clicking on the campfire toggles the fire on and off.

## Trivia

* The Campfire outside [Linus](/Linus "Linus")' [Tent](/Tent "Tent") can be toggled on and off, with no effect on Linus' dialogue or behavior.
