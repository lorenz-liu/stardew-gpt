The **Holy Blade** is a [sword](/Sword "Sword") that can be found as a [special item](/The_Mines#Special_Items "The Mines") in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"). When wielded, it grants +4 Speed and +2 Defense and is enchanted with [Crusader](/Forge#Combat_enchantments "Forge").

## Trivia

The Holy Blade was thought to be unobtainable until several years after the game's release.[[1]](#cite_note-caredditcomment-1) Since [v1.6](/Version_History#v1.6 "Version History"), the Holy Blade may be found in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") starting from level 80.
