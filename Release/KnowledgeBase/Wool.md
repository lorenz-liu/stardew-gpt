**Wool** is an [animal product](/Category:Animal_Products "Category:Animal Products") obtained from a [Rabbit](/Rabbit "Rabbit") or [Sheep](/Sheep "Sheep"). It can also be available at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="340"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,020–1,700g. [Emily](/Emily "Emily") may also send you one Wool in the mail as a gift. Wool sells for a base price of data-sort-value="340">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)340g.

## Bundles

Wool is one of the options for the ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Animal Bundle](/Bundles#Animal_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Pantry](/Bundles#Pantry "Bundles").

## Artisan Goods

:   *See also: [Animal Products Profitability](/Animal_Products_Profitability "Animal Products Profitability")*

Placing quality Wool into a [Loom](/Loom "Loom") gives a higher chance to produce 2 Cloth. Silver gives a 10% chance, gold gives a 50% chance, and iridium gives a 100% chance.

## Recipes

Wool is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Wool is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a [Plain Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring").

* Male version: [![Shirt038.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/13/Shirt038.png/24px-Shirt038.png)](/File:Shirt038.png)
* Female version: [![Shirt041.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/8a/Shirt041.png/24px-Shirt041.png)](/File:Shirt041.png)

It is a white dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

* Wool may be randomly requested during any season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="1020">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,020g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points. Wool will never be requested unless you own a [Rabbit](/Rabbit "Rabbit") or [Sheep](/Sheep "Sheep").
