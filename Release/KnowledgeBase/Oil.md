**Oil** is a cooking ingredient made from the [Oil Maker](/Oil_Maker "Oil Maker"). It can also be obtained from [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for data-sort-value="200">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)200g. [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre") sells Oil in his [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="4">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 4 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg"). 2-4 Oil may occasionally be found in [Supply Crates](/Supply_Crate "Supply Crate") on the [Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps#Beach "Farm Maps") provided the player has upgraded the [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") at least once.

Oil is given no quality rating, so its sell price doesn't benefit from quality increases. It also does not benefit from the [Artisan Profession](/Skills#Farming "Skills"). Its main benefit lies in its use as a [cooking](/Cooking "Cooking") ingredient. Oil made from base quality [sunflowers](/Sunflower "Sunflower"), grown [sunflower seeds](/Sunflower_Seeds "Sunflower Seeds"), and gold or lower qualities of [corn](/Corn "Corn") is profitable, though corn is more profitable in the [Keg](/Keg "Keg"), [Preserves Jar](/Preserves_Jar "Preserves Jar"), or [Seed Maker](/Seed_Maker "Seed Maker").

Note: From 2am to 6am, machines process at 100 minutes per hour. For the rest of the day, machines process at 60 minutes per hour.

## Tailoring

Oil is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [![Shirt256.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/21/Shirt256.png/24px-Shirt256.png)](/File:Shirt256.png) [Oil Stained Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

Oil is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
