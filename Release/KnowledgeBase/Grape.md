The **Grape** is a [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") that is both a [forageable](/Foraging "Foraging") item and a standard [crop](/Crops "Crops"):

* In [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), Grapes are a [forageable](/Foraging "Foraging") fruit found that grows wild throughout Stardew Valley or can be grown from [Summer Seeds](/Summer_Seeds "Summer Seeds"). Specifically, forageable Grapes can be found at: the [Backwoods](/Backwoods "Backwoods") (62% of all forageable items at this location); [the Mountain](/The_Mountain "The Mountain") (62%); the [Bus Stop](/Bus_Stop "Bus Stop") (27%); the [Railroad](/Railroad "Railroad") (27%); or, the [Forest Farm Map](/Farm_Maps#Forest "Farm Maps") (25%). It can also randomly be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during Summer.
* In [Fall](/Fall "Fall"), Grapes are a standard crop that takes ten days to grow from a [Grape Starter](/Grape_Starter "Grape Starter") to maturity; the plants can be re-harvested every three days.

Grapes grown as a crop (in Fall) receive the +10% [Tiller](/Skills#Farming "Skills") profession bonus when sold (as do all crops). However, foraged Grapes in summer do not get the Tiller bonus. If you combine Summer and Fall grapes into the same stack, the entire stack will receive the Tiller bonus (see [the Fruit page](/Fruits#Tiller_Profession_Details "Fruits") for more details).

Other than the Tiller profession bonus, foraged Grapes and crop-grown Grapes are identical and can be used interchangeably in crafting, gifts, bundles, recipes, and quests.

## Stages

You cannot walk through grape plants grown from a Grape Starter at any stage of growth. You can walk through dead Grape plants.

## Tailoring

Grape can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Shorts](/Tailoring#Pants "Tailoring"). [![Shorts.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/c5/Shorts.png/36px-Shorts.png)](/File:Shorts.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a purple dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

* The Grape may be randomly requested in [Summer](/Summer "Summer") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="240">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)240g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
