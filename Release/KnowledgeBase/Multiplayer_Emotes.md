# Multiplayer/Emotes

Emotes are short actions that the player character can act out, which will also show the emote over the players head in a thought bubble.

Emotes can be activated by typing it into [chat](/Multiplayer#Chat "Multiplayer") using the emote command (`/emote <emote>`), or by pressing the **Emote Menu** keybind (Default Y), and on controller Hold Right Thumb Stick.
