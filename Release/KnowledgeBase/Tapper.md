The **Tapper** is a type of [Refining Equipment](/Crafting#Refining_Equipment "Crafting") that can be placed on a [tree](/Trees "Trees") to produce [Maple Syrup](/Maple_Syrup "Maple Syrup"), [Oak Resin](/Oak_Resin "Oak Resin"), [Pine Tar](/Pine_Tar "Pine Tar"), [Sap](/Sap "Sap"), or [Mystic Syrup](/Mystic_Syrup "Mystic Syrup"). It can also be placed on a [Mushroom Tree](/Mushroom_Tree "Mushroom Tree") to produce different mushrooms. It can also be placed on a [Green Rain Tree (Type 3)](/Green_Rain_Trees "Green Rain Trees") to produce [Fiddlehead Ferns](/Fiddlehead_Fern "Fiddlehead Fern"). Other types of Green Rain Tree cannot be tapped.

When a tree has a Tapper on it, the tree is unable to be directly shaken (by interacting with it). However, if the tree is moss-covered, it will be shaken when the player swings a scythe or weapon on the tree to remove the moss.

The Tapper can be removed from a tree by hitting it once with an [axe](/Axes "Axes") or [pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes"). This leaves the Tapper intact and able to be reused. If the Tapper is on a tree when it is struck by [lightning](/Weather#Storm "Weather") or hit by an exploding [bomb](/Crafting#Bombs "Crafting"), both the Tapper and its contents are destroyed.

During [Winter](/Winter "Winter"), tappers placed on [Maple Trees](/Maple_Tree "Maple Tree"), [Oak Trees](/Oak_Tree "Oak Tree"), [Pine Trees](/Pine_Tree "Pine Tree"), [Mahogany Trees](/Mahogany_Tree "Mahogany Tree"), or [Mystic Trees](/Mystic_Tree "Mystic Tree") continue to produce; however since [Mushroom Trees](/Mushroom_Tree "Mushroom Tree") and [Green Rain Trees (Type 3)](/Green_Rain_Trees "Green Rain Trees") turn to stumps in [Winter](/Winter "Winter"), tappers placed on them produce nothing during that season.

The Tapper cannot be placed on [Fruit Trees](/Fruit_Trees "Fruit Trees").

Three Tappers are the reward for completing the ![Bundle Orange.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/95/Bundle_Orange.png/24px-Bundle_Orange.png) [Forager's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Forager.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Remixed Bundles").

## History

* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Fixed issue that could leave trees unchoppable if tapper was removed in unusual fashion. Tappers can now be stacked in a player's [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory") and in [chests](/Chest "Chest").
* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): Added the [Heavy Tapper](/Heavy_Tapper "Heavy Tapper"). Fixed bug where processing time was affected by the time of day. Can now be obtained from the Forager's Bundle.
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Recipe is now learned at [Foraging](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") Level 4 instead of Level 3. [Mystic Syrup](/Mystic_Syrup "Mystic Syrup") and [Fiddlehead Fern](/Fiddlehead_Fern "Fiddlehead Fern") are now possible products.
