# Gotoro Empire

The **Gotoro Empire** is the opposing force that is fighting against the [Ferngill Republic](/Setting#Nations "Setting"), in which [Stardew Valley](/Stardew_Valley "Stardew Valley") is located. Not much else is known about the empire other than a few villagers mentioning the existence of the opposition.

The [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") merchant claims that the items she has have been smuggled from the empire as well.

## Location

The Gotoro Empire is located south of the [Gem Sea](/Setting#Geography "Setting"). [The Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach"), south of [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") and where Willy's fish shop is, serves as the shoreline for the [Gem Sea](/Setting#Geography "Setting"). It can be assumed that The Gotoro Empire is south of this area.
