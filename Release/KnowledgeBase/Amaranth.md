## Tailoring

Amaranth is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a dyeable ![Shirt141.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/ca/Shirt141.png/24px-Shirt141.png) [Crop Top Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a pale violet red dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the purple dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

* Amaranth is requested by [Marnie](/Marnie "Marnie") in a letter on the 3rd of [Fall](/Fall "Fall") for the ["Cow's Delight" Quest](/Quests#Cow.27s_Delight "Quests").
