The **Dinosaur Egg** is an [artifact](/Artifacts "Artifacts") and an [animal product](/Animals "Animals").

Initially, a Dinosaur Egg can be found by digging up an [Artifact Spot](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") in [The Mountains](/The_Mountain "The Mountain") (including the [Quarry](/Quarry "Quarry")), in [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") after reaching [Fishing level 2](/Fishing#Fishing_Skill "Fishing") and having previously found at least one [artifact](/Artifacts "Artifacts"), dropped from a [Pepper Rex](/Pepper_Rex "Pepper Rex"), foraged on [Prehistoric Floors](/Skull_Cavern#Prehistoric_Floors "Skull Cavern") in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), or won in the Crane Game in the [Movie Theater](/Movie_Theater "Movie Theater"). Dinosaur Egg can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") dog with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals").

Dinosaur Eggs can be put into an [incubator](/Incubator "Incubator") in a [Big or Deluxe Coop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop#Farm_Buildings "Carpenter's Shop") to hatch a [Dinosaur](/Dinosaur "Dinosaur") ≈11 days later (≈6 days with the [Coopmaster Profession](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming")). Mature and fed dinosaurs will produce Dinosaur Eggs every 7 days.

## Donation

Donation of this item contributes to the total count of donations for the [Museum](/Museum "Museum").

**Tip:** Placing the first Dinosaur Egg found into an incubator instead of donating it to the Museum will ensure that the player both obtains a Dinosaur and completes the Museum collection.

## Bundles

Dinosaur Egg is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Recipes

Dinosaur Egg is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

A Dinosaur Egg can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Dinosaur Hat.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/25/Dinosaur_Hat.png/24px-Dinosaur_Hat.png) [Dinosaur Hat](/Dinosaur_Hat "Dinosaur Hat"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a green dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a green dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

* ["Gus' Famous Omelet"](/Quests#Gus.27_Famous_Omelet "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), [Gus](/Gus "Gus") may randomly request that the player collect and deliver 24 Eggs (of any type) to the fridge in [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon"). The reward is data-sort-value="3000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)3,000g and a [Mini-Fridge](/Mini-Fridge "Mini-Fridge").

## Secret

After hatching the first Dinosaur Egg, [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius") will gift the player a [Triple Shot Espresso](/Triple_Shot_Espresso "Triple Shot Espresso").[[1]](#cite_note-firsthatch-1)

Can now be used in [Tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring").

* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Dinosaur Egg is now liked by Demetrius and Alex. Can now be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") dog with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals"). Dinosaur Egg sell values are now modified by the Treasure Appraisal Guide.
