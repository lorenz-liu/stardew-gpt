The **Rustic Plank Floor** is a [crafted item](/Crafting#Decor "Crafting") used to create decorative paths. It can be displaced by lightning or destroyed by [weeds](/Weeds "Weeds").

The Rustic Plank Floor recipe is sold at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop") for data-sort-value="200">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)200g.

All types of flooring provide a +0.1 boost to [Player Speed](/Speed#Player_Speed "Speed"), but only if the flooring is located outside on [The Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"). They also have the indirect benefit of preventing [Grass](/Grass "Grass") or [Trees](/Trees "Trees") from growing in heavily used areas, which would otherwise slow down or obstruct movement.
