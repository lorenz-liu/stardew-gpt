The **Dehydrator** is a type of [Artisan Equipment](/Crafting#Artisan_Equipment "Crafting") that is used to create raisins and dried mushrooms and fruit. It can be crafted after purchasing the recipe from [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre") for data-sort-value="10000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)10,000g. It is also a possible reward as the 12th prize from the [Prize Machine](/Mayor%27s_Manor#Prize_Machine "Mayor's Manor") in the Mayor's Manor (50% chance). One is included if the player chooses the [mushroom cave](/The_Cave#Mushrooms "The Cave").

## Products

Dehydrator requires five [Mushrooms](/Category:Mushrooms "Category:Mushrooms") (except [Red Mushroom](/Red_Mushroom "Red Mushroom")) or [Fruits](/Fruits "Fruits") of the same **type** and **quality**.

## Notes

* The quality of the dried product is always normal and **not** affected by the quality of items used.
* Inedible fruits become edible after being turned into [Dried Fruit](/Dried_Fruit "Dried Fruit"). The formula for [Energy](/Energy "Energy") is: `[Base fruit sell price ÷ 2 × 0.5 × 5]`, or `[Base fruit sell price × 1.25]`. *This is the same as the calculation for base silver quality sell price, but rounded instead of truncated.*
  + Dried [Ancient Fruits](/Ancient_Fruit "Ancient Fruit") give 688 [Energy](/Energy "Energy") and 309 [Health](/Health "Health")
  + Dried [Coconuts](/Coconut "Coconut") give 125 [Energy](/Energy "Energy") and 56 [Health](/Health "Health")
  + Dried [Rhubarbs](/Rhubarb "Rhubarb") give 275 [Energy](/Energy "Energy") and 123 [Health](/Health "Health")
* The dehydrator effectively makes the [Mushrooms](/Category:Mushrooms "Category:Mushrooms") or [Fruits](/Fruits "Fruits") sell at gold quality price +5 each.
* [Truffles](/Truffle "Truffle") are not classified as [mushrooms](/Category:Mushrooms "Category:Mushrooms") and therefore cannot be placed into the Dehydrator.
