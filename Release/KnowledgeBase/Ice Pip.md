The **Ice Pip** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") on the 60th floor during all seasons. The only other fish that can be caught there is the [Ghostfish](/Ghostfish "Ghostfish").

The chances of hooking an Ice Pip depends on fishing level and on the distance from land of the spot fished, increasing by 4.5% if a [Curiosity Lure](/Curiosity_Lure "Curiosity Lure") is equipped and by 9% if [Ice Pip Bait](/Targeted_Bait "Targeted Bait") is equipped. For example, at level 10 fishing and distance of 5, the chances of hooking an Ice Pip is 6.45%. This increases to 10.95% with a Curiosity Lure equipped, to 15.45% with Ice Pip Bait equipped, and to 19.95% with both a Curiosity Lure and Ice Pip Bait equipped.[[1]](#cite_note-mineshaft-1)

## Tailoring

The Ice Pip can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Fishing Hat.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/d8/Fishing_Hat.png/24px-Fishing_Hat.png) [Fishing Hat](/Fishing_Hat "Fishing Hat"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a blue dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Ice Pips can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 5 days. The initial pond capacity is 1 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing four quests. The primary output is blue Ice Pip [Roe](/Roe "Roe"), but [Iron Ore](/Iron_Ore "Iron Ore") is possible at population 3 and several more valuable items are possible starting at population 9.

## References

1. [↑](#cite_ref-mineshaft_1-0) See `MineShaft::getFish` in the game code. While `Data\Fish.xnb` suggests that a fishing level of 5 is needed to hook an Ice Pip, the data in `MineShaft::getFish`, which does not have any level conditions, overrides it. This means that there is no fishing level requirement to hook an Ice Pip.
