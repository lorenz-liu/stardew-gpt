**Mystic Syrup** is a [Tree](/Trees "Trees") sap that can be obtained by placing a [Tapper](/Tapper "Tapper") on a [Mystic Tree](/Mystic_Tree "Mystic Tree") grown by the player. The process takes ![Time Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Time_Icon.png/24px-Time_Icon.png) 7 days, or ![Time Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Time_Icon.png/24px-Time_Icon.png) 3 days with the [Heavy Tapper](/Heavy_Tapper "Heavy Tapper").

Although Mystic Syrup is labeled "Artisan Good" in-game, it does not benefit from the [Artisan Profession](/Skills#Farming "Skills"), but instead from the [Tapper Profession](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") (+25% value).

## Tailoring

Mystic Syrup is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a blue dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a cyan dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.
