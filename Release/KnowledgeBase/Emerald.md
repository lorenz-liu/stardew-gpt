**Emerald** is a [Mineral](/Minerals "Minerals"). It can be obtained from [Emerald Nodes](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining") and [Gem Nodes](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining") in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), from the green [Gem Bird](/Ginger_Island#Gem_Birds "Ginger Island") on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") during [rainy days](/Weather#Weather_Types "Weather"), from [panning](/Pans "Pans"), or in [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing"). It has a 10% chance to be dropped by a slain [Dwarvish Sentry](/Dwarvish_Sentry "Dwarvish Sentry") in the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon"). It may occasionally be found in a [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") after the player has reached the bottom of the mines. It can also be received as a gift at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"). The [Statue Of Endless Fortune](/Statue_Of_Endless_Fortune "Statue Of Endless Fortune") produces an Emerald on the birthdays of [Clint](/Clint "Clint"), [Emily](/Emily "Emily"), and [Penny](/Penny "Penny").

[Cheese](/Cheese "Cheese") may be purchased at the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") on [Fridays](/Days_of_the_Week#Friday "Days of the Week") in exchange for 1 Emerald.

## Bundles

Emerald is one of the options for the ![Bundle Yellow.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2b/Bundle_Yellow.png/24px-Bundle_Yellow.png) [Treasure Hunter's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Treasure_Hunter.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Boiler Room (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Boiler_Room "Remixed Bundles").

## Forging

Emerald can be used at the [Forge](/Forge "Forge") to increase the speed of a melee weapon.

## Recipes

Emerald is not used in any [recipes](/Cooking "Cooking").

## Tailoring

Emerald is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt144.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/07/Shirt144.png/24px-Shirt144.png)](/File:Shirt144.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a green dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* Emerald may be randomly requested during any season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="750">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)750g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points. Emerald will never be requested until you have reached floors 80+ of [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines").
* Two Emeralds may be requested by [Octopus](/Octopus "Octopus"), [Rainbow Trout](/Rainbow_Trout "Rainbow Trout"), [Spook Fish](/Spook_Fish "Spook Fish"), or [Super Cucumber](/Super_Cucumber "Super Cucumber") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 5 to 7.
* Emerald will be requested by [Emily](/Emily "Emily") as part of the "Rock Rejuvenation" quest at the [Special Orders](/Quests#List_of_Special_Orders "Quests") board outside Mayor [Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis")' house.
* May be used in the [Gem Birds](/Ginger_Island#Gem_Birds "Ginger Island") puzzle on Ginger Island.
