The **Cave Carrot** is found via [foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"). It can also be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can"). Large quantities can be easily obtained by digging underground patches of dirt in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") (on levels 10-20) with the [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes"). It may also randomly be found in the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") stock for data-sort-value="25"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100–1,000g. [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent") sells a Cave Carrot in his [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="2">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 2 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg"). One Cave Carrot can sometimes be purchased for data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g from [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre")'s booth at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star").

## Tailoring

Cave Carrot is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt011.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/49/Shirt011.png/24px-Shirt011.png) [Green Belted Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a brown dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the orange dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

* The Cave Carrot may be randomly requested during any [season](/Seasons "Seasons") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="75">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)75g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* One, two, or five Cave Carrots may be requested by several fish in [Fish Pond quests](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond").
* After reaching 3 [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") hearts with [Marnie](/Marnie "Marnie"), she will request that you bring her a Cave Carrot in the ["Marnie's Request" quest](/Quests#Marnie.27s_Request "Quests"). The reward is 100 [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points with [Marnie](/Marnie "Marnie").
* 5 Cave Carrots may be requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his third request, or as part of any request starting with the sixth.
