**Ruby** is a type of [Mineral](/Minerals "Minerals") obtainable by:

* [Mining](/Mining "Mining") [Ruby Nodes](/Mining#Ruby_Node "Mining") and [Gem Nodes](/Mining#Gem_Node "Mining") in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") (Floors 80+), [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), [Quarry](/Quarry "Quarry"), and [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon"). (After reaching the bottom of the Mines, Ruby Nodes can be found on any floor).
* Defeating [Dwarvish Sentries](/Dwarvish_Sentry "Dwarvish Sentry") (10% chance).
* [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing").
* [Panning](/Pans "Pans") anywhere (1% chance).
* Gift at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star") by any adult except [Clint](/Clint "Clint"), [Evelyn](/Evelyn "Evelyn"), [Marnie](/Marnie "Marnie"), [Robin](/Robin "Robin"), or [Willy](/Willy "Willy").
* Breaking [crates and barrels](/The_Mines#Crates_and_Barrels "The Mines") in the Mines (Floors 80+), Skull Cavern, and Volcano Dungeon.
* Found in a [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") after the player has reached the bottom of the Mines.
* [Gem Birds](/Ginger_Island#Gem_Birds "Ginger Island") on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") during [rainy days](/Weather#Weather_Types "Weather").

## Bundles

Ruby is one of the options for the ![Bundle Yellow.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2b/Bundle_Yellow.png/24px-Bundle_Yellow.png) [Treasure Hunter's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Treasure_Hunter.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Boiler Room (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Boiler_Room "Remixed Bundles").

## Forging

Ruby can be used at the [Forge](/Forge "Forge") to increase the damage of a melee weapon.

## Recipes

Ruby is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Ruby is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt085.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/4d/Shirt085.png/24px-Shirt085.png) [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a red dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can also be placed in the red dye pot at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"), for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

* The Ruby may be randomly requested during any season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="750">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)750g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points. The Ruby will never be requested until the player has reached floors 80+ of [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines").
* Ruby will be requested by [Emily](/Emily "Emily") as part of the "Rock Rejuvenation" quest at the [Special Orders](/Quests#List_of_Special_Orders "Quests") board outside Mayor [Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis")' house.
* May be used in the [Gem Birds](/Ginger_Island#Gem_Birds "Ginger Island") puzzle on Ginger Island.

## Trading

One Ruby can be traded to the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for 1 [Spicy Eel](/Spicy_Eel "Spicy Eel").
