**Strawberry Seeds** are a type of seed. Mature plants yield [Strawberries](/Strawberry "Strawberry").

They can be purchased for data-sort-value="100">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100g at the [Egg Festival](/Egg_Festival "Egg Festival"), on the first and third days of the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") from the [Calico Egg Merchant](/Desert_Festival#Calico_Egg_Merchant "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="5">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 5 and data-sort-value="6">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 6 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg") respectively, or from [Maru](/Maru "Maru")'s [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the Desert Festival. They can also be obtained by using the [Seed Maker](/Seed_Maker "Seed Maker").

When harvested, each Strawberry plant gives 1 Strawberry every 4 days, with a small random chance for more Strawberries.[[1]](#cite_note-extraharvest-1)

## Notes

1. [↑](#cite_ref-extraharvest_1-0) The game generates a random number between 0 and 1, and if it is less than 0.02, then another Strawberry will spawn. This repeats until the random number is greater than or equal to 0.02, at which point the chance for an extra Strawberry ends. Daily [Luck](/Luck "Luck") can double the amount of extra Strawberries spawned by the above formula.

* Unlike most other types of seed, Strawberry Seeds have no sell value. This means that it is not profitable to process the crops with a [Seed Maker](/Seed_Maker "Seed Maker") to gain additional value.
