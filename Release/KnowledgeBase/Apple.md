The **Apple** is a [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") grown by planting an [Apple Sapling](/Apple_Sapling "Apple Sapling"), waiting 28 days for it to grow into an [Apple Tree](/Apple_Tree "Apple Tree"), and harvesting the tree during the [Fall](/Fall "Fall").

An Apple may be purchased at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="100"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)300–1,000g or found in [The Farm Cave](/The_Cave "The Cave"), if the fruit bat option is chosen.

## Tailoring

Apple can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Relaxed Fit Pants](/Tailoring#Pants "Tailoring").[![Relaxed Fit Pants.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/1e/Relaxed_Fit_Pants.png/36px-Relaxed_Fit_Pants.png)](/File:Relaxed_Fit_Pants.png) It can be also used as a red dye color at the dye pots in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Apple is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Trivia

* Despite their in-game description, they can neither be turned into juice nor cider. When placed in a [Keg](/Keg "Keg") to be turned into a drink, an Apple will instead produce [Apple Wine](/Wine "Wine").
