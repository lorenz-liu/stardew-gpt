The **Sturgeon** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught in the [mountain lake](/The_Mountain "The Mountain") during [Summer](/Summer "Summer") and [Winter](/Winter "Winter"). It is least common during [rainy](/Weather "Weather") winter days (only possible by using a [Rain Totem](/Rain_Totem "Rain Totem")) and most common on rainy summer days, comprising from 11% to 16% of all items caught. It is most likely to be caught when casting at least 3 tiles from shore.[[1]](#cite_note-spawnrate-1) It can also be caught during [Summer](/Summer "Summer") and [Winter](/Winter "Winter") on the [Wilderness Farm](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps"). It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") cat with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals"). It may also randomly appear in [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus")' shop on [Wednesdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Wednesday "Days of the Week") for data-sort-value="200">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)200g, or at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="200"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)600–1,000g.

Sturgeon can be caught regardless of time of day or season using [Magic Bait](/Magic_Bait "Magic Bait").

## Tailoring

Sturgeon can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Fishing Vest](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt157.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/0e/Shirt157.png/24px-Shirt157.png)](/File:Shirt157.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a blue dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Sturgeon can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 4 days. The initial pond capacity is 1 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing four quests. The only possible output is Sturgeon [Roe](/Roe "Roe"), which can be put into a [Preserves Jar](/Preserves_Jar "Preserves Jar") to make [Caviar](/Caviar "Caviar").
