The **Dragon Tooth** is a special item that can be obtained in the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon") within the skeletal remains of a dragon or by killing a [Lava Lurk](/Lava_Lurk "Lava Lurk") (15% drop chance). They can also be acquired from a [Stingray](/Stingray "Stingray") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") with a population of at least 9.

Five Dragon Teeth can be used to purchase a [Banana Sapling](/Banana_Sapling "Banana Sapling") from the [Island Trader](/Island_Trader "Island Trader").

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/c5/Skeletal_Dragon_Remains.png/250px-Skeletal_Dragon_Remains.png)](/File:Skeletal_Dragon_Remains.png)

Dragon remains with Dragon Teeth

## Forging

Dragon Teeth can be used at the [Forge](/Forge "Forge") to add an Innate Enchantment to a weapon.

## Tailoring

Dragon Tooth is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Swashbuckler Hat.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/c4/Swashbuckler_Hat.png/24px-Swashbuckler_Hat.png) [Swashbuckler Hat](/Swashbuckler_Hat "Swashbuckler Hat"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

One Dragon Tooth is requested by [Stingray](/Stingray "Stingray") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 5 to 10.
