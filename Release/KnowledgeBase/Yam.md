The **Yam** is a [vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from [Yam Seeds](/Yam_Seeds "Yam Seeds") after 10 days. It also has a 3% chance of dropping from [Duggies](/Duggy "Duggy").

## Tailoring

Yam can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Button Down Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt140.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/f9/Shirt140.png/24px-Shirt140.png)](/File:Shirt140.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* Yam may be randomly requested in [Fall](/Fall "Fall") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="480">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)480g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
