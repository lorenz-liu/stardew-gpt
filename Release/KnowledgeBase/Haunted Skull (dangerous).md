**Haunted Skulls (dangerous)** are a flying [enemy](/Monsters "Monsters") found in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") after activating the [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") or during the [Danger In The Deep](/Quests#Danger_In_The_Deep "Quests") quest. During the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival"), they may spawn in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") if a certain [Calico Statue effect](/Desert_Festival#Skull_Cavern "Desert Festival") is activated.

## Behavior

They behave identically to their weaker counterpart the [Haunted Skull](/Haunted_Skull "Haunted Skull"). They may fly away from the player and change the direction of attack.

## Strategy

The best weapon may be a regular sword swing (not a club or dagger), since its angle is large. If possible, move in a direction contrary to the Skull so a mob is all on one side of the player, and swing at them all at once.
