# Trash

:   *This article is about the category of items. For the specific item, see [Trash (item)](/Trash_(item) "Trash (item)").*

**Trash** is a [category](/Category:Trash "Category:Trash") of items in [Stardew Valley](/Stardew_Valley "Stardew Valley"). With the exception of the [Rotten Plant](/Rotten_Plant "Rotten Plant"), these items have a chance to be collected when [fishing](/Fishing "Fishing"), or obtained from [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can"). All except for [Joja Cola](/Joja_Cola "Joja Cola") and the Rotten Plant can be collected from [Crab Pots](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot"). Trash is the less favorable alternative when fishing, as [fish](/Fish "Fish") are worth more to sell and have far more uses. However, trash items (other than Joja Cola and the Rotten Plant) can be used in a [Recycling Machine](/Recycling_Machine "Recycling Machine") to create more useful materials.

A random trash item is caught while [fishing](/Fishing "Fishing"), after failing skill checks to get a bite ("hit") from every type of [fish](/Fish "Fish") and [other catchables](/Fish#Other_Catchables "Fish") currently available at a location. More types of fish currently available at a location generally results in a greater chance of a bite, which results in a decreased chance of catching trash.[[1]](#cite_note-1) Trash is therefore much more common when fishing in locations with few types of fish and rarer fish, such as [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), and trash is the only possible result when fishing in the [Farm Pond](/Farm_Pond "Farm Pond") on a [Standard Farm and Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps").[[2]](#cite_note-2)

The likelihood of catching trash at most locations will also decrease substantially as [fishing skill](/Fishing#Fishing_Skill "Fishing") increases. At fishing level 10, trash frequency can decrease by over 20 percentage points if few types of fish are present, and trash frequency approaches 0% when at least 5 types of fish (including algae and seaweed) are present.[[3]](#cite_note-3)

Catching Trash with a [fishing pole](/Tools#Fishing_Poles "Tools") grants 3 [Fishing](/Fishing "Fishing") XP. Harvesting Trash from a [Crab Pot](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot") grants 5 Fishing XP. In the fishing minigame at the [Stardew Valley Fair](/Stardew_Valley_Fair "Stardew Valley Fair"), trash items are worth one point.

The [Rotten Plant](/Rotten_Plant "Rotten Plant") can be obtained by a [Jack-O-Lantern](/Jack-O-Lantern "Jack-O-Lantern") which becomes a Rotten Plant when standing outside in [Winter](/Winter "Winter"). It can also be found as a part of a [secret](/Secret_Notes#Secret_Note_.2319 "Secret Notes").

During the special order quest [Community Cleanup](/Quests#List_of_Special_Orders "Quests") the player is tasked with gathering 20 trash items (except Joja Cola or Rotten Plants).
