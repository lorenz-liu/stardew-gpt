**Evelyn Mullner** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"), and is in charge of the town's flowers.

## Schedule

She can usually be found in her home's kitchen or tending to the gardens in the center of town.
On the 2nd of every season, she has an appointment at the [clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic"), and on the 23rd of every season she accompanies [George](/George "George") to his appointment. Evelyn never visits the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island.

## Relationships

Evelyn is married to [George](/George "George"), and lives with her grandson [Alex](/Alex "Alex"). Alex's deceased mother [Clara](/Clara_Mullner "Clara Mullner") was Evelyn's daughter.

In Evelyn's nightstand is a note written to her addressed as Momma. The note reads:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| “ | “Momma- If you're reading this, it means I've passed on. I'm so sorry you had to go through this. Know that I love you both. Don't be too upset, I'm with [Yoba](/Yoba "Yoba") now. My final request: take care of [Alex](/Alex "Alex"). He needs a family, something stable. He's a good kid. Love, Clara” |

This is possibly a note from Evelyn's daughter, Clara, who is the mother of [Alex](/Alex "Alex"). Clara passed away and left Alex to live with Evelyn and [George](/George "George").

In the beginning of [Stardew Valley](/Stardew_Valley "Stardew Valley"), when meeting all the townspeople, Evelyn will give you permission to call her "Granny".

## Quests

* ["Granny's Gift"](/Quests#Granny.27s_Gift "Quests"): Evelyn will send you a letter on the 15th of [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), year 2, asking for a [Leek](/Leek "Leek"). The reward is data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* Evelyn may randomly request an item at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). The reward is 3x the item's base value and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* ["Gifts for George"](/Quests#Gifts_for_George "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor") during [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), Evelyn may randomly request that the player collect and deliver 12 [Leeks](/Leek "Leek") to the stove in her house. The reward is data-sort-value="2000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,000g and a [Coffee Maker](/Coffee_Maker "Coffee Maker").
