## Bundles

Raisins are not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Raisins are not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). They can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a purple dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Raisins are not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Secrets

If Raisins are placed in a [Junimo Hut](/Junimo_Hut "Junimo Hut"), then the Junimos have a 20% chance to double harvest crops.[[1]](#cite_note-harvest-1)
This is hinted at in [Secret Note #24](/Secret_Notes#Secret_Note_.2324 "Secret Notes") and [Secret Note #26](/Secret_Notes#Secret_Note_.2326 "Secret Notes").
One bag of Raisins is consumed by the Junimos every week (except in [Winter](/Winter "Winter")), and a small sign is placed outside of the hut. Multiple bags can be stacked in the hut, and they will be consumed over time.
