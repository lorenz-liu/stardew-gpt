A **Palm Tree** is a type of [Tree](/Trees "Trees") found in the [Calico Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") and on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island"). It does not drop any seeds, so it cannot be planted elsewhere. It can be chopped down, and may drop a [Coconut](/Coconut "Coconut") when shaken or chopped down (but only after the player has reached [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") level 1). Palm Trees on Ginger Island can also drop [Golden Coconuts](/Golden_Coconut "Golden Coconut"). After being chopped down, a Palm Tree has approximately a 20% chance to grow to the next stage each day.

## Notes

* Despite being described in game as a "seed of the coconut palm," [coconuts](/Coconut "Coconut") cannot be planted to grow Palm Trees.
