# Warp Totem

**Warp Totems** are items that can be used as instant transport to different areas on the map. They can be [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting"), earned, or purchased at the [Casino](/Casino "Casino"), and are used to quickly travel to different parts of the Valley. Crafting recipes come from leveling up [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") skill or purchasing at various vendors. To use a Warp Totem, select it in inventory and "use" it (right-click).

Later in the game there are permanent teleporters available which replace consumable Warp Totems. They function much the same way, but are not consumed on use.

There are five locations that standard Warp Totems will teleport players to: [The Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"), [The Mountain](/The_Mountain "The Mountain"), [The Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach"), [The Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert"), and [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island").

Note that on the day of the [Luau](/Luau "Luau") and [Dance of the Moonlight Jellies](/Dance_of_the_Moonlight_Jellies "Dance of the Moonlight Jellies"), the [Warp Totem: Beach](/Warp_Totem:_Beach "Warp Totem: Beach") will be consumed upon use, but will not transport the player to [the Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") outside of festival time. During festival time, the totem will be consumed, and will transport the player to the festival, as if they had entered the beach via the bridge from [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town").

## Farm

* ![Return Scepter.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/f3/Return_Scepter.png/24px-Return_Scepter.png) [Return Scepter](/Return_Scepter "Return Scepter") warps you directly to [the farm house](/The_Farm "The Farm"). For farmhands in a multiplayer game, it warps you directly to [your cabin](/Cabin "Cabin"). It is not consumed on use.
* ![Farm Obelisk.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/34/Farm_Obelisk.png/24px-Farm_Obelisk.png) [Farm Obelisk](/Farm_Obelisk "Farm Obelisk") warps you directly to [the farm warp statue](/The_Farm "The Farm"). It's an unlockable building on the [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") farm, and is not consumed on use.
* ![Mini-Obelisk.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/c9/Mini-Obelisk.png/24px-Mini-Obelisk.png) [Mini-Obelisks](/Mini-Obelisk "Mini-Obelisk") transport you between two places on the Farm. They are not consumed on use.
