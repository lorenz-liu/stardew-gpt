# Jellies and Pickles

:   *For a detailed analysis of profit using the Preserves Jar, see [Preserves Jar Productivity](/Preserves_Jar_Productivity "Preserves Jar Productivity").*

**Jellies** and **Pickles** can be produced by inserting either a [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") or a [vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") / [foraged item](/Foraging#Foraged_Items "Foraging") in a [preserves jar](/Preserves_Jar "Preserves Jar"), respectively. The value of a single jar of jelly or pickles is equal to (2 \* Base [Crop](/Crops "Crops") Value + 50). Jelly and pickles are unaffected by the quality of the fruit or vegetable used.

A [preserves jar](/Preserves_Jar "Preserves Jar") requires 4000 minutes, or 2-3 days, to produce jelly or pickles.[[1]](#cite_note-timing_details-1)

## Products

Quality of ingredients makes no difference to the finished product. The value of the finished product is determined using the base value (*i.e.,* with no silver or gold star) of the [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits")/[vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables"). However, the preserved version is always at least as profitable as even gold star produce, although the profit margin is lower compared to normal produce.

**Notes**

* Pickles made from an inedible vegetable will instead give ![Energy.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a9/Energy.png/24px-Energy.png) 0.625 × Base [Vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") Price and ![Health.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/94/Health.png/24px-Health.png) 0.28125 × Base [Vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") Price.[[2]](#cite_note-pickle_energy-2)
* Jelly made from an inedible fruit will instead give ![Energy.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a9/Energy.png/24px-Energy.png) 0.5 × Base [Fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") Price and ![Health.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/94/Health.png/24px-Health.png) 0.225 × Base [Fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") Price.[[3]](#cite_note-jelly_energy-3)
* The following foraged items can be pickled: [Cave Carrot](/Cave_Carrot "Cave Carrot"), [Chanterelle](/Chanterelle "Chanterelle"), [Common Mushroom](/Common_Mushroom "Common Mushroom"), [Dandelion](/Dandelion "Dandelion"), [Ginger](/Ginger "Ginger"), [Hazelnut](/Hazelnut "Hazelnut"), [Leek](/Leek "Leek"), [Magma Cap](/Magma_Cap "Magma Cap"), [Morel](/Morel "Morel"), [Purple Mushroom](/Purple_Mushroom "Purple Mushroom"), [Snow Yam](/Snow_Yam "Snow Yam"), [Spring Onion](/Spring_Onion "Spring Onion"), [Wild Horseradish](/Wild_Horseradish "Wild Horseradish"), and [Winter Root](/Winter_Root "Winter Root").
* The color of Jelly or Pickles changes depending on the item used to make it.

## Bundles

Jelly is one of the options for the ![Bundle Purple.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a1/Bundle_Purple.png/24px-Bundle_Purple.png) [Artisan Bundle](/Bundles#Artisan_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Pantry](/Bundles#Pantry "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Jelly is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt092.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/16/Shirt092.png/24px-Shirt092.png)](/File:Shirt092.png)

Pickles are used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt091.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/ee/Shirt091.png/24px-Shirt091.png)](/File:Shirt091.png)

Jelly can also be used as a red dye color at the dye pots in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, located at [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"), and Pickles can be used as a green dye.

## Quests

* One Jelly (of any type) or one Pickles (of any type) may be requested by [Catfish](/Catfish "Catfish"), [Rainbow Trout](/Rainbow_Trout "Rainbow Trout"), [Spook Fish](/Spook_Fish "Spook Fish"), [Super Cucumber](/Super_Cucumber "Super Cucumber"), or [Sturgeon](/Sturgeon "Sturgeon") in [Fish Pond quests](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond").
* One Jelly is requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his second request. It may also be requested as part of any request starting with the sixth.
* One Pickles is requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his fourth request. It may also be requested as part of any request starting with the sixth.

## History

* [1.0](/Version_History#1.0 "Version History"): Introduced.
