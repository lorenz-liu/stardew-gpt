The **Heavy Furnace** is an improved version of the [Furnace](/Furnace "Furnace"). It can be [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting") after claiming [Mining Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave"). It takes 25 ores (or 5 quartz or fire quartz) and 3 [Coal](/Coal "Coal") to run.

## Smelting

To smelt any ore, equip it and activate the furnace while there is Coal in inventory. Three units of Coal are required for each smelting operation, regardless of the material or duration. Each possible output stack size is equally likely, *i.e.* ores have a 50% chance of yielding 5 bars and a 50% chance of yielding 6 bars.[[1]](#cite_note-extraBars-1)

When smelting bars, on average, 9% of the ore and 45% of the [Coal](/Coal "Coal") is saved in comparison to normal [Furnaces](/Furnace "Furnace").
