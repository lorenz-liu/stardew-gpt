The **Battery Pack** is a resource created in the [Lightning Rod](/Lightning_Rod "Lightning Rod") during [thunderstorms](/Weather "Weather") or the [Solar Panel](/Solar_Panel "Solar Panel") after 7 sunny days.

To acquire a Battery Pack from a Lightning Rod, during a thunderstorm a particular sound will be heard, which means that one of the rods is now charged, pulsating with energy. There is no guarantee that a specific lightning rod will be struck by lightning during a thunderstorm. On the day after the storm, a Battery Pack can be acquired from it.

Battery Packs can sometimes be purchased from the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="500"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,500–2,500g and can be sold to Robin at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop"). Very rarely, a Battery Pack can be dropped by breaking boxes or crates in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"). They may also be dropped by [Iridium Bats](/Bats "Bats") (5% chance). Three Battery Packs are the reward for completing the ![Bundle Green.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/b3/Bundle_Green.png/24px-Bundle_Green.png) [Children's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Children.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Remixed Bundles"). [Kent](/Kent "Kent") and [Pam](/Pam "Pam") may also send the player a Battery Pack in the mail. They can also be acquired from a [Stingray](/Stingray "Stingray") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") with a population of at least 9.

## Bundles

Battery Pack is used in the ![Bundle Purple.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a1/Bundle_Purple.png/24px-Bundle_Purple.png) [Engineer's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Engineer.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Boiler Room (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Boiler_Room "Remixed Bundles").

## Tailoring

Battery Pack is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt106.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/44/Shirt106.png/24px-Shirt106.png) [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a cyan dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the blue dye pot at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

* A Battery Pack is needed for ["The Mysterious Qi" quest](/Quests#The_Mysterious_Qi "Quests").
* [Pam](/Pam "Pam") requests a Battery Pack by mail on the 19th of [Fall](/Fall "Fall") (year 2) in the ["Pam Needs Juice" quest](/Quests#Pam_Needs_Juice "Quests"). The reward is data-sort-value="400">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)400g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* [Willy's Boat](/Fish_Shop#Willy.27s_Boat "Fish Shop") needs 5 Battery Packs to repair the ticket stand.
