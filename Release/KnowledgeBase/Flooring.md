# Flooring

:   *For crafted flooring and paths, see [Crafting](/Crafting#Decor "Crafting").*

:   *For wallpaper, see [Wallpaper](/Wallpaper "Wallpaper").*

**Flooring** is an item that can be used to decorate the floor of the [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse"), [cabins](/Cabin "Cabin"), [island farmhouse](/Island_Farmhouse "Island Farmhouse"), [sheds](/Shed "Shed"), and [slime hutches](/Slime_Hutch "Slime Hutch"). It is available from the catalogue and randomly sold at both [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") and [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart").

Flooring is single-use, and does not stack. It covers the whole room it's placed in and can also be placed on hallway floors between rooms.
