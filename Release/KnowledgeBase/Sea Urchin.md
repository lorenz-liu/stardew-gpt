The **Sea Urchin** is a [forageable](/Foraging "Foraging") item found year-round in the [Tide Pool](/The_Beach#Tide_Pools "The Beach") section of [the Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach"), *i.e.,* to the east of the wooden footbridge[[1]](#cite_note-location-1).

During [Summer](/Summer "Summer") 12-14 extra Sea Urchins appear anywhere on the beach (including possibly the western section). It may also be found foraging on levels 1-29 of [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") while the [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") is enabled, or the [Danger in The Deep](/Quests#Danger_In_The_Deep "Quests") quest is active.

The average spawn rate for Sea Urchin in the tide pool section of the beach is 0.23/day. An additional 0.8 (on average) Sea Urchins per day will spawn during Summer 12-14. These spawn rates are not affected by the presence of other forageable items on the beach; in particular, Sea Urchins are not part of the standard six item per map limit on forageable items. 7 [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") XP are granted for every Sea Urchin picked up, and all Foraging bonuses apply to Sea Urchins.

[Emily](/Emily "Emily") may occasionally send you a Sea Urchin in the mail as a gift. She also sells five Sea Urchins at her [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="5">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 5 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg") each. All saltwater [Crab Pot](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot") [fish](/Fish "Fish") may also produce a Sea Urchin in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), when the population of the pond reaches 5. It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") turtle with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals").

## Tailoring

Sea Urchin can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [![Shirt139.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/66/Shirt139.png/24px-Shirt139.png)](/File:Shirt139.png) [Strapped Top](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a purple dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Although Sea Urchins are not otherwise considered to be fish, they can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 4 days. The initial pond capacity is 10 Sea Urchins; no quests are necessary to increase the pond capacity. The only possible output is purple Sea Urchin [Roe](/Roe "Roe").
