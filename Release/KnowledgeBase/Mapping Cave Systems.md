**Mapping Cave Systems** is a power [book](/Books "Books") that can be obtained from a box in the back room of the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild"), which is accessible after killing 1,000 [monsters](/Monsters "Monsters").[[1]](#cite_note-guilddoor-1) It can also be purchased from the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") for data-sort-value="20000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)20,000g starting in Year 3 (≈9% chance to appear).[[2]](#cite_note-y3chance-2)

The first reading grants the player a power that gives them a 50% discount on [Marlon](/Marlon "Marlon")'s [Item Recovery Service](/Adventurer%27s_Guild#Item_Recovery_Service "Adventurer's Guild"). Once read, it can be found in [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s Menu on the [Special Items & Powers](/Special_Items_%26_Powers "Special Items & Powers") tab. Each subsequent reading gives the player 100 [Combat](/Combat "Combat") [XP](/Skills "Skills").
