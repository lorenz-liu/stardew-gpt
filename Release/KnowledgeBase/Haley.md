**Haley** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). She's one of the twelve characters available to [marry](/Marriage "Marriage").

## Schedule

After the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") is unlocked, Haley may randomly spend the day there. After leaving the Island at 6pm, Haley will immediately go home to bed. Haley never visits the Resort on [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days or her checkup day at [Harvey's Clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic").

## Relationships

Haley lives with her sister [Emily](/Emily "Emily"), and together they care for their [parents' home](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"), who have been traveling the world for the past two years.

Haley is friends with [Alex](/Alex "Alex"), and will dance with him at the [Flower Dance](/Flower_Dance "Flower Dance") if neither of them are dancing with the player.

![Two Hearts.png](/mediawiki/images/7/7c/Two_Hearts.png)

## Marriage

:   *Main article: [Marriage](/Marriage "Marriage")*

Once married, Haley will move into the [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse"). Like other marriage candidates, she will add her own room to the right of the bedroom. She'll also set up a small garden behind the farmhouse where she'll sometimes go to take pictures.

On rainy mornings, Haley may offer you something sweet for breakfast: [Cookie](/Cookie "Cookie"), [Blueberry Tart](/Blueberry_Tart "Blueberry Tart"), [Pancakes](/Pancakes "Pancakes"), [Poppyseed Muffin](/Poppyseed_Muffin "Poppyseed Muffin"), or [Maple Bar](/Maple_Bar "Maple Bar"). On mornings when Haley stays inside the farmhouse all day, she may offer you [Fried Egg](/Fried_Egg "Fried Egg"), [Omelet](/Omelet "Omelet"), [Hashbrowns](/Hashbrowns "Hashbrowns"), [Pancakes](/Pancakes "Pancakes"), or [Bread](/Bread "Bread") for breakfast. On rainy nights she may offer you dinner: [Chowder](/Chowder "Chowder"), [Eggplant Parmesan](/Eggplant_Parmesan "Eggplant Parmesan"), [Bean Hotpot](/Bean_Hotpot "Bean Hotpot"), or [Parsnip Soup](/Parsnip_Soup "Parsnip Soup").

## Trivia

* Even though Haley's ten heart event is about her dark room, a new dark room is not included in her spouse room after marriage. Additionally, her dark room remains locked even after the ten heart event.

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/cc/Seasonsgreetings3.png/200px-Seasonsgreetings3.png)](/File:Seasonsgreetings3.png)

Screenshot from [Dev Update 13 referencing peppermint coffee](https://www.stardewvalley.net/dev-update-13/)

* In the "[pink cake](/Pink_Cake "Pink Cake")" episode of "[The Queen of Sauce](/The_Queen_of_Sauce "The Queen of Sauce")" (the 21st of Summer, Year 2), The Queen says, "A viewer from Pelican Town wrote to me recently... let's see... Her name's Haley. She wrote, 'I tried your pink cake last time I was in [Zuzu City](/Setting#Zuzu_City "Setting") and I fell in love with it. Could you share the recipe on your next episode?'. Well, why not? It's a marvelous cake. And you'll never guess the secret ingredient... melon!"
* On Wednesdays in Winter, Haley may say "I wish someone would bring me a peppermint coffee. Don't even bother, I know you won't be able to make one." This may lead players to believe that Peppermint Coffee is available, and is one of Haley's loved or liked gifts. In fact, Peppermint Coffee cannot be crafted, cooked, or brewed, and is not available in-game anywhere. In early game development, Haley would say "The only thing I like about winter is peppermint coffee... hey, are you even listening?"
* In the 8-heart event, if the player is wearing a Prismatic [clothing item](/Tailoring "Tailoring"), it can be seen animated on the pictures, even though pictures are normally static.
* In the 8-heart event, Haley's sprite changes in the photographs to her pre-release outfit. For some reason the sprites were never updated to her final design.
* In a note on the tabletop next to the fridge there is a letter to Haley and Emily from their parents saying "We just left the Fern Islands last night, after staying for two months."
