# Trinkets

**Trinkets** are equippable [clothing](/Clothing "Clothing") items. Each one of them gives the [Player](/Player "Player") unique benefits. They can be found after [Combat Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave") is claimed. Most trinkets can be dropped by [monsters](/Monsters "Monsters"), found in [crates and barrels](/The_Mines#Crates_and_Barrels "The Mines") in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), and [Quarry Mine](/Quarry_Mine "Quarry Mine"), or obtained in [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") treasure rooms. The [Magic Hair Gel](/Magic_Hair_Gel "Magic Hair Gel") is the exception, as it can only be purchased from [Alex](/Alex "Alex")'s shop in the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival").

The player can obtain the same trinket multiple times. Trinkets do not stack. They can be sold at the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild") for data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g each.

All Trinkets, with the exceptions of the [Basilisk Paw](/Basilisk_Paw "Basilisk Paw") and the [Magic Hair Gel](/Magic_Hair_Gel "Magic Hair Gel"), can be re-forged on an [Anvil](/Anvil "Anvil"). This randomizes their stats and costs 3 [Iridium Bars](/Iridium_Bar "Iridium Bar") per re-roll.

## Drop Chances

All trinkets, other than the Magic Hair Gel, have a ≈0.63% chance to be found in a [Skull Cavern treasure chest](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern").[[1]](#cite_note-treasureroom-1) Calculations for the drop rates from monsters and containers are more complex and depend on several factors.[[2]](#cite_note-spawntrinket-2)

### Monsters

When slain, all monsters have a chance to drop a random trinket that is not the Magic Hair Gel. This is calculated as follows:

`chance = Min ( 0.4% + 0.001% × monster maxhealth + y, 2.5% ) + DailyLuck / 25.0 + LuckBuffs × 0.133%` [[3]](#cite_note-monsterinfo-3)

The value of y in the formula depends on the monster killed:

1. y = 0.2% if the monster is classified as a "Glider" (flying enemy) and its max health ≥ 150. This condition is applied to [Iridium Bats, Bats (dangerous), Lava Bats (dangerous)](/Bats "Bats"), [Haunted Skulls](/Haunted_Skull "Haunted Skull"), [Haunted Skulls (dangerous)](/Haunted_Skull_(dangerous) "Haunted Skull (dangerous)"), [Cave Flies (dangerous)](/Cave_Fly_(dangerous) "Cave Fly (dangerous)"), [Carbon Ghosts](/Carbon_Ghost "Carbon Ghost"), [Putrid Ghosts](/Putrid_Ghost "Putrid Ghost"), [Squid Kids (dangerous)](/Squid_Kid_(dangerous) "Squid Kid (dangerous)"), [Bugs (dangerous)](/Bug_(dangerous) "Bug (dangerous)"), [Armored Bugs (dangerous)](/Armored_Bug_(dangerous) "Armored Bug (dangerous)"), [Serpents](/Serpent "Serpent"), [Royal Serpents](/Royal_Serpent "Royal Serpent"), [Magma Sprites](/Magma_Sprite "Magma Sprite"), [Magma Sparkers](/Magma_Sparker "Magma Sparker"), [Dwarvish Sentries](/Dwarvish_Sentry "Dwarvish Sentry"), and [Blue Squids](/Blue_Squid "Blue Squid").[[4]](#cite_note-ghostfly-4)
2. y = -0.5% if the monster is classified as a "Leaper." This condition is only applied to [Spiders](/Spider "Spider").
3. y = 0 for all other monsters.

Note that the calculated chance for the first part of the formula ranges from 0.1% ([Spider](/Spider "Spider")) to 1.65% ([Royal Serpent](/Royal_Serpent "Royal Serpent") with 18 tail segments), so forcing the maximum to be 2.5% has no effect.

Trinkets are not directly added to a monster's drop pool; they are spawned separately. This means that the [Burglar's Ring](/Burglar%27s_Ring "Burglar's Ring") and [Monster Compendium](/Monster_Compendium "Monster Compendium") do not affect the chance for or number of trinkets dropped.

### Crates and Barrels

Breakable containers in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), and [Quarry Mine](/Quarry_Mine "Quarry Mine") have a chance to drop a random trinket that is not the Magic Hair Gel. This is calculated as follows:

`chance = ( 0.4% + DailyLuck / 25.0 + LuckBuffs × 0.133% ) × ( 1.0 + effectiveMineLevel * 0.001 ) × 0.8`

1. In the Mines, effectiveMineLevel = the level.
2. In the Skull Cavern, effectiveMineLevel = level + 120.
3. In the Quarry Mine, effectiveMineLevel = 5000.

The [difficulty levels](/The_Mines#Difficulty_Levels "The Mines") of the Mines and Skull Cavern do not affect the drop rates from containers in each location.
