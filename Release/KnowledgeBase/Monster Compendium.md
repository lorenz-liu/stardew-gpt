The **Monster Compendium** is a power [book](/Books "Books") that can be dropped by [monsters](/Monsters "Monsters"). The chance for a monster to drop the book starts at 0.01% and increases by 0.015% for each monster killed until it is dropped. Then the chance stays at 0.05% permanently. It cannot drop until the player has killed more than 10 monsters.[[1]](#cite_note-compendiumDrop-1)

It can also be purchased from the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") for data-sort-value="20000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)20,000g starting in Year 3 (≈9% chance to appear).[[2]](#cite_note-y3chance-2)

The first reading grants the player a power that gives all monsters a 3% chance to drop double loot. This effect stacks with that of the [Burglar's Ring](/Burglar%27s_Ring "Burglar's Ring").[[1]](#cite_note-compendiumDrop-1) Once read, it can be found in [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s Menu on the [Special Items & Powers](/Special_Items_%26_Powers "Special Items & Powers") tab. Each subsequent reading gives the player 100 [Combat](/Combat "Combat") [XP](/Skills "Skills").

## Bundles

Monster Compendium is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Monster Compendium is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used as a black [dye](/Dyeing "Dyeing") when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

Monster Compendium is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Trading

Two Monster Compendiums can be traded to the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") for either 1 [Slime Egg-Press](/Slime_Egg-Press "Slime Egg-Press") or 1 [Slime Incubator](/Slime_Incubator "Slime Incubator"), provided the player has already obtained the power from this book.

## Trivia

* It is possible to obtain two [Prismatic Jelly](/Prismatic_Jelly "Prismatic Jelly") using this book's power, although this has no effect on the Wizard's Special Order.
