# Crit. Chance

:   *For technical details about Crit. Chance mechanics, see [Combat/Mechanics](/Combat/Mechanics "Combat/Mechanics").*

**Crit. Chance** [![Crit. Chance.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a9/Crit._Chance.png/24px-Crit._Chance.png)](/File:Crit._Chance.png) (short for Critical Chance) is a [Combat](/Combat "Combat") statistic. When performing an attack, this stat will determine the likelihood to perform a stronger attack with increased damage.

A special sound plays when hitting a critical strike, and the damage numbers that pop up show the greater damage amount.

The crit chance tooltip is not in any way added to the chance to crit. It is rather calculated from the base crit chance, including the bonus received by daggers[[1]](#cite_note-tooltip-1). Each point of crit chance is roughly equivalent to a 2% chance to crit.
