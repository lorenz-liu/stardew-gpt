# The Tunnel

**The Tunnel** is a small (33x6) underground area accessed from the lower elevation section of the [Backwoods](/Backwoods "Backwoods"). To reach it, follow the road west from the [Bus Stop](/Bus_Stop "Bus Stop") and continue west along the road through the Backwoods.

The only feature of interest in the Tunnel is a panel near the middle of the Tunnel that can be opened using a [Battery Pack](/Battery_Pack "Battery Pack"). [Secret Note #22](/Secret_Notes#Secret_Note_.2322 "Secret Notes") hints at the existence of this panel, as does a [Lost Book](/Lost_Books#TunnelDoor "Lost Books"). Opening the panel starts ["The Mysterious Qi"](/Quests#The_Mysterious_Qi "Quests") quest, eventually providing access to the [Casino](/Casino "Casino").

## Tips

* [Artisan Equipment](/Crafting#Artisan_Equipment "Crafting") can be safely placed inside the Tunnel without worrying about [Villagers](/Villagers "Villagers") destroying it by walking through it. It also counts as an interior so will allow the use of [Garden Pots](/Garden_Pot "Garden Pot").
