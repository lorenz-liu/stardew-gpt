The **Rotten Plant** is a type of [trash](/Trash "Trash"). It is produced by a [Jack-O-Lantern](/Jack-O-Lantern "Jack-O-Lantern") that decomposes on the 1st of [Winter](/Winter "Winter") (unless it has been stored in [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory") or a [chest](/Chest "Chest")).

It cannot be placed in a [Recycling Machine](/Recycling_Machine "Recycling Machine").

## Bundles

The Rotten Plant is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Recipes

The Rotten Plant is not used in any [recipes](/Cooking "Cooking").

## Dyeing

Rotten Plant can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

The Rotten Plant can be used as an orange item in the Special Order ["Qi's Prismatic Grange"](/Quests#Qi.27s_Prismatic_Grange "Quests").

## Secret

After reading [Secret Note #19](/Secret_Notes#Secret_Note_.2319 "Secret Notes"), solving the puzzle, and obtaining the special reward item, a Rotten Plant may be produced under specific circumstances.

| **Details** |
| --- |
| The [Solid Gold Lewis](/Solid_Gold_Lewis "Solid Gold Lewis") statue can be placed anywhere in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). If not destroyed by a villager walking through it, the next day it will be replaced with a Rotten Plant. This Rotten Plant can be removed with a [tool](/Tools "Tools"), or by repeatedly left-clicking or holding left-click. |
