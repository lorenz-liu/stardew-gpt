The **Warp Totem: Island** is a [Warp Totem](/Warp_Totem "Warp Totem") that transports the player to [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") by selecting it in inventory and right-clicking. It can be [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting") after purchasing the recipe from the [Volcano Dwarf](/Volcano_Dungeon#Shop "Volcano Dungeon") for data-sort-value="10000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)10,000g. One is obtained after repairing the [Farm Obelisk](/Farm_Obelisk "Farm Obelisk") on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island").

## Bundles

The Warp Totem: Island is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

The Warp Totem: Island can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Totem Mask.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/cc/Totem_Mask.png/24px-Totem_Mask.png) [Totem Mask](/Totem_Mask "Totem Mask"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

The Warp Totem: Island is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
