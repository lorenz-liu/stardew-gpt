**Summer Spangle** is a [flower](/Flowers "Flowers") that can be [grown](/Crops "Crops") from [Spangle Seeds](/Spangle_Seeds "Spangle Seeds") in 8 days.

If grown within proper range of a [Bee House](/Bee_House#Range "Bee House") (5 squares in any cardinal direction), a Summer Spangle will cause [Summer Spangle Honey](/Honey "Honey") to be produced, increasing the honey base value from data-sort-value="100">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100g to data-sort-value="280">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)280g.

Spangles can be purchased from the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="90"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)270–1,000g.

## Bundles

Summer Spangle is one of the options for the ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Garden Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Garden_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Pantry (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Pantry "Remixed Bundles").

## Recipes

Summer Spangle is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Summer Spangle can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Pleated Skirt](/Tailoring#Pants "Tailoring"). [![Pleated Skirt.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/92/Pleated_Skirt.png/36px-Pleated_Skirt.png)](/File:Pleated_Skirt.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Summer Spangle is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Notes

* Summer Spangles have an equal chance of being one of six different colors. Summer Spangles of different colors will occupy different slots in inventory or chests. Six colors × four [quality](/Crops#Crop_Quality "Crops") levels = 24 possible spaces used.
