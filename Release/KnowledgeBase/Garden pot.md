A **Garden Pot** is a [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting") item that allows [crops](/Crops "Crops") to be grown indoors. It can also be used to grow crops on [The Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") or [the Ginger Island Farm](/Ginger_Island#The_Farm "Ginger Island"). The crops can be from any season, except outdoors on The Farm, where they must be from the current season. A Garden Pot cannot grow [Ancient Seeds](/Ancient_Seeds "Ancient Seeds"), rejecting them at any time, indoors or outdoors.

After the [Greenhouse](/Greenhouse "Greenhouse") is completed, [Evelyn](/Evelyn "Evelyn") visits the farm and gives the player one Garden Pot and the recipe for crafting more. The cutscene triggers when the player exits the [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") between 6am and 11:30am on a Sunny day (cannot be windy, rainy, stormy, or snowing), during any season. The event cannot trigger for the first 3 days after the greenhouse is built via completing the [Pantry bundles](/Bundles#Pantry "Bundles"), and it may take longer than 3 days. There is no 3-day delay in triggering the event when repairing the greenhouse via the [Joja Community Development Form](/Joja_Community_Development_Form "Joja Community Development Form")[[1]](#cite_note-jojaevent-1).

Flowers planted in Garden Pots will enable nearby [Bee Houses](/Bee_House "Bee House") to produce flower [honey](/Honey "Honey").

[Crows](/Animals#Crows "Animals") do not attack crops grown in Garden Pots. Giant crops cannot be grown in garden pots, just as they cannot be grown in the greenhouse.

## Use

Garden Pots can be placed and produce crops "indoors" - that is, in any space not affected by [weather](/Weather "Weather"). Available locations include the [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse"), [Island Farmhouse](/Island_Farmhouse "Island Farmhouse"), all [farm buildings](/Carpenter%27s_Shop#Farm_Buildings "Carpenter's Shop"), [The Cave](/The_Cave "The Cave"), [The Tunnel](/The_Tunnel "The Tunnel"), [The Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers"), the [Mutant Bug Lair](/Mutant_Bug_Lair "Mutant Bug Lair"), the [Witch's Swamp](/Witch%27s_Swamp "Witch's Swamp"), the entrances to [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") and [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), and [villagers](/Villagers "Villagers")' homes.

Garden Pots accept crop seeds (other than ancient seeds) in outdoor areas on [The Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") or [the Ginger Island Farm](/Ginger_Island#The_Farm "Ginger Island"). They accept only [Tea Saplings](/Tea_Sapling "Tea Sapling") elsewhere outdoors, however.

Garden Pots do not need to be tilled with a [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes") before planting. [Fertilizer](/Fertilizer "Fertilizer") of all types may be used in Garden Pots, with the same restrictions and benefits as in tilled soil.

Garden Pots cannot be watered with [sprinklers](/Crafting#Sprinklers "Crafting"). As a result, players must water crops in Garden Pots on a daily basis with a [Watering Can](/Watering_Cans "Watering Cans"), or less often if they use a type of Retaining Soil. If the player fertilizes the Garden Pot with [Deluxe Retaining Soil](/Deluxe_Retaining_Soil "Deluxe Retaining Soil"), the crop needs to be watered only once. Crops that don’t need to be watered such as [Tea Saplings](/Tea_Sapling "Tea Sapling") and [Fiber Seeds](/Fiber_Seeds "Fiber Seeds") don’t need to be watered in the Garden Pot.

Crops in Garden Pots can be harvested with the [Iridium Scythe](/Iridium_Scythe "Iridium Scythe").

Once placed, a Garden Pot may be removed with a [tool](/Tools "Tools") or by holding left-click. Removing a Pot that contains seeds or crops will destroy the seeds/crops.

## Notes

* An attempt to place [Ancient Seeds](/Ancient_Seeds "Ancient Seeds") in a Garden Pot displays the message "Her roots grow very deep... She wouldn't be happy in there."
* An attempt to plant a seed from the current growing season (including [Cactus Seeds](/Cactus_Seeds "Cactus Seeds")) in an outdoor area outside the farms displays the message "Must be planted on farm".
* [Cactus Seeds](/Cactus_Seeds "Cactus Seeds") planted in a Garden Pot outdoors on one of the farms will be dead the next day.
* Garden Pots placed outside the farm are subject to being destroyed if placed in a villager's path. See *[Crafting](/Crafting#Crafted_goods_outside_the_farm "Crafting")* for maps of safe locations.

## History

* [1.3.27](/Version_History#1.3.27 "Version History"): Introduced.
* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Can now be stacked in a player's [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory") and in [chests](/Chest "Chest").
* [1.5.5](/Version_History#1.5.5 "Version History"): Garden Pots no longer lose any applied fertilizers when they are harvested from. When placing a Garden Pot on Ginger Island on a rainy day, it will now spawn with its dirt correctly watered.
* [1.5.6](/Version_History#1.5.6 "Version History"): Fixed bug where [Tea Saplings](/Tea_Sapling "Tea Sapling") could not be harvested from Garden Pots on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island").
* [1.6.6](/Version_History#1.6.6 "Version History"): Bee houses now work with flowers in garden pots.
* [1.6.9](/Version_History#1.6.9 "Version History"): Fixed bug where crops grown in Garden Pots from [Wild Seeds](/Crafting#Seeds "Crafting") always produce normal quality crops and provide no XP/Mastery points.
