The **Melon** is a [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from [Melon Seeds](/Melon_Seeds "Melon Seeds") after 12 days. Melon is one of only five crops that can grow into a [giant crop](/Crops#Giant_Crops "Crops").

## Stages

Melons can become a [Giant Crop](/Crops#Giant_Crops "Crops").

## Tailoring

Melon is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a dyeable ![Shorts.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/c5/Shorts.png/24px-Shorts.png) [Shorts](/Tailoring#Pants "Tailoring"). It is a pink dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the red dye pot at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane") for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

* [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius") requests a melon by mail on the 20th of [Summer](/Summer "Summer") in the ["Crop Research" Quest](/Quests#Crop_Research "Quests"). The reward is data-sort-value="550">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)550g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* Melon may be randomly requested in [Summer](/Summer "Summer") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="750">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)750g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* A fully-grown and unharvested Melon is required for [Gourmand Frog's puzzle](/Ginger_Island#Gourmand_Frog "Ginger Island") on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island").
