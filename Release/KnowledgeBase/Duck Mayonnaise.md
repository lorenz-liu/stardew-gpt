## Bundles

Duck Mayonnaise is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Recipes

Duck Mayonnaise is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Duck Mayonnaise is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create an ![Shirt009.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/8a/Shirt009.png/24px-Shirt009.png) [Aquamarine Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a white dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

Duck Mayonnaise is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Secrets

If Duck Mayonnaise is placed in the partially-hidden purple box in the back room of the [The Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon"), the player will receive the [![??Pinky Lemon??.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/37/%3F%3FPinky_Lemon%3F%3F.png/16px-%3F%3FPinky_Lemon%3F%3F.png)](/File:%3F%3FPinky_Lemon%3F%3F.png) [??Pinky Lemon??](/%3F%3FPinky_Lemon%3F%3F "??Pinky Lemon??") statue.
