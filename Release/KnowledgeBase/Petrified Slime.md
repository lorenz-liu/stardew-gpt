## Bundles

Petrified Slime is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Recipes

Petrified Slime is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Petrified Slime is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Slime Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt207.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/b3/Shirt207.png/24px-Shirt207.png)](/File:Shirt207.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a green dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

One Petrified Slime is requested by [Slimejack](/Slimejack "Slimejack") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 7 to 10.
