**Pancakes** are a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). They are prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit").

Pancakes may randomly appear in [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus")' shop on [Saturdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Saturday "Days of the Week"), in the [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") outside the saloon, or in [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon")'s rotating stock. They are also sold by [Alex](/Alex "Alex") during the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="10">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 10 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg"). One Pancakes may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box").

## Tailoring

Pancakes are used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Track Jacket](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt164.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/d1/Shirt164.png/24px-Shirt164.png)](/File:Shirt164.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Pancakes are not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Notes

While this recipe is not profitable, it is the cheapest way to gain a +2 [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") boost. This can translate into high effective profit when shaking [Salmonberry](/Salmonberry "Salmonberry") or [Blackberry](/Blackberry "Blackberry") bushes during their respective seasons, since an additional berry will be harvested at Foraging levels 4, 8, and 12.
