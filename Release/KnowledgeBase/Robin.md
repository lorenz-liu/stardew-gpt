**Robin** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who resides at [24 Mountain Road](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop"), on [The Mountain](/The_Mountain "The Mountain"), with her husband [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius"), daughter [Maru](/Maru "Maru"), and son [Sebastian](/Sebastian "Sebastian").

Robin is the town carpenter and runs her own [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop"), at her home, from 9am to 5pm every day except Tuesday and part of Friday (closes early).

## Schedule

Robin's shop is closed on Tuesday while she has a weekly exercise class at [Caroline](/Caroline "Caroline")'s home. However, if you stand in her path on her way out (near the cash register at 9:40am), you can use her shop momentarily. You can also access her shop when she is returning around 8pm, by clicking the register while she is right behind you.

Robin won't leave her home on days when it's raining unless she has been hired to construct a [farm building](/Carpenter%27s_Shop#Farm_Buildings "Carpenter's Shop"). Her shop will be closed for the days that she is on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") completing the construction.

After the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island is unlocked, Robin may randomly spend Tuesday there. After leaving the Island at 6pm, Robin will immediately go home to bed. Robin never visits the Resort on [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days.

## Relationships

Robin is married to [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius") and lives with her daughter [Maru](/Maru "Maru") and son [Sebastian](/Sebastian "Sebastian"). Maru is her daughter with Demetrius, and Sebastian is from an earlier relationship. It is assumed that Demetrius is Robin's second husband because there are 2 books in the bookcase of their room titled "Maximizing Your Second Marriage" and "Practical Tips For First-Time Step-Dads."

Robin attends exercise classes on Tuesday with [Jodi](/Jodi "Jodi"), [Caroline](/Caroline "Caroline"), [Emily](/Emily "Emily") and [Marnie](/Marnie "Marnie").

## Quests

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/5/58/Robins_Lost_Axe.png/120px-Robins_Lost_Axe.png)](/File:Robins_Lost_Axe.png)

Robin's lost axe

* ["Robin's Lost Axe"](/Quests#Robin.27s_Lost_Axe "Quests"): On the 11th of [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), Robin will send a letter explaining that she lost her favorite axe, and offers a reward of data-sort-value="250">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)250g to anyone who finds and returns it to her. You also gain 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart as a reward.
* ["Robin's Request"](/Quests#Robin.27s_Request "Quests"): On the 21st of [Winter](/Winter "Winter"), Robin will send a letter requesting that you bring her 10 pieces of [Hardwood](/Hardwood "Hardwood"). The reward is data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* Robin may randomly request an item at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). The reward is 3x the item's base value and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* Robin may randomly request [Wood](/Wood "Wood") or [Stone](/Stone "Stone") at ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests"). The reward is 8x the number of items requested.
* ["Robin's Project"](/Quests#Robin.27s_Project "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), Robin may randomly request that the player collect 80 [Hardwood](/Hardwood "Hardwood"). The reward is data-sort-value="2000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,000g, 250 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points, and the [Deluxe Red Double Bed](/Deluxe_Red_Double_Bed "Deluxe Red Double Bed") for sale at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop").
* ["Robin's Resource Rush"](/Quests#Robin.27s_Resource_Rush "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), Robin may randomly request that the player collect 1000 [Wood](/Wood "Wood") or 1000 [Stone](/Stone "Stone"). The reward is data-sort-value="2500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,500g and the [Stone Chest](/Stone_Chest "Stone Chest") recipe.
