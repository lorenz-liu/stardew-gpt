**Carp** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught in the [mountain lake](/The_Mountain#Fishing "The Mountain") or [Wilderness Farm](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps") during [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), or [Fall](/Fall "Fall"). It can also be caught in the [Secret Woods](/Secret_Woods "Secret Woods"), [The Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers"), or the [Mutant Bug Lair](/Mutant_Bug_Lair "Mutant Bug Lair") during any season. It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") cat with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals"). It can also randomly be found at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="30"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100–1,000g. It can also randomly be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during Spring, Summer and Fall.

Carp is always most abundant in the Secret Woods, comprising between 41% and 53% of all items caught there when it isn't raining.[[1]](#cite_note-spawnrate-1)

## Tailoring

Carp can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [![Shirt157.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/0e/Shirt157.png/24px-Shirt157.png)](/File:Shirt157.png) [Fishing Vest](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye in the dye pots, located at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Carp can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every day. The initial pond capacity is 3 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing two quests. The only possible output is yellow Carp [Roe](/Roe "Roe").

## Quests

* **Item Delivery:** One carp may be randomly requested during [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), or [Fall](/Fall "Fall") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="90">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)90g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* **Fishing:** [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius") or [Willy](/Willy "Willy") may randomly request 1-5 carp during [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), or [Fall](/Fall "Fall") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="30">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)30g per fish. (You get to keep the fish).
* Two Carp may be requested by [Dorado](/Dorado "Dorado"), [Lingcod](/Lingcod "Lingcod"), or [Pike](/Pike "Pike") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 3 to 5.

## Note

* Carp are one of the easiest fish to catch, rarely moving past the 3rd bar on the fishing meter in the fishing mini-game, making it easy to get "Perfect" on its catches.
