Clint's **Blacksmith** shop provides multiple services primarily related to [resources](/Category:Resources "Category:Resources") found in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"). It chiefly serves as a location for upgrading [tools](/Tools "Tools"), and breaking open [geodes](/Minerals#Geodes "Minerals"), although he also trades in [Coal](/Coal "Coal") and ores ([copper](/Copper_Ore "Copper Ore"), [iron](/Iron_Ore "Iron Ore"), & [gold](/Gold_Ore "Gold Ore")). Finally, the building is also [Clint](/Clint "Clint")'s home, and his bedroom is out the back of the shop floor.

## Stock

The purchase prices of ore and coal increase in Years 2+. The player will receive a letter in the mail on [Spring](/Spring "Spring") 1 of Year 2 saying "Notice: The price of raw materials at [Robin's shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop") and Clint's shop has increased." The gold obtained by selling items to Clint (or through the [shipping bin](/Shipping "Shipping")) does not increase.

### Selling

Similar to how it is possible to sell crops directly to [Pierre](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"), it is also possible to sell [gems](/Minerals#Gems "Minerals"), [minerals](/Minerals "Minerals"), ore, and bars directly to Clint. Note that Clint will not directly buy any kind of [geodes](/Minerals#Geodes "Minerals") (although it is possible to sell any ores they contain back to him).

## Upgrade Tools

[Clint](/Clint "Clint") can upgrade the four main [tools](/Tools "Tools") and the inventory [Trash Can](/Trash_Cans "Trash Cans"). Each quality-upgrade must be completed in sequence and a quality-upgrade cannot be skipped. (If a tool is at the [copper](/Copper "Copper") quality, it can't be upgraded to [gold](/Gold "Gold") quality until the steel upgrade is completed.) It takes 2 days to upgrade a tool. Other Blacksmith services are still available while Clint is upgrading a tool. A notification will pop up on the left side of the screen on the morning a tool upgrade is ready.

When the Blacksmith's shop is closed for festival days (although the door will not be shut for the [Dance of the Moonlight Jellies](/Dance_of_the_Moonlight_Jellies "Dance of the Moonlight Jellies") in the Summer, and the [Spirit's Eve](/Spirit%27s_Eve "Spirit's Eve") festival in the Fall), Clint will still work on tools. If a player drops off a tool the day before a festival, it will be ready the day after the festival as normal. However, if a player drops off a tool 2 days before a festival and the Blacksmith's shop is closed due to the festival, the player will be unable to pick up the tool and must wait an additional day.

All [Axe](/Axes "Axes") and [Pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes") upgrades reduce the number of hits necessary to remove trees and rocks. In addition, higher level tools can break larger objects of their respective type (logs and stumps for Axes, boulders and meteorites for Pickaxes). Upgraded versions of the [Watering Can](/Watering_Cans "Watering Cans") and [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes") allow the player to water and till multiple squares of land in a single use (using click-and-hold). Upgrading the Watering Can also refills it. The trash can upgrades increase the auto-sell value of any objects placed into them up to 60%.

## Process Geodes

Clint can break open [geodes](/Minerals#Geodes "Minerals") for data-sort-value="25">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)25g. [Gems](/Minerals#Gems "Minerals") and [Minerals](/Minerals#Geode_Minerals "Minerals") can be found within, as well as common resources like [Stone](/Stone "Stone"), [Clay](/Clay "Clay"), and [Coal](/Coal "Coal").

If the sell price of the item inside the geode is data-sort-value="75">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)75g or less, a "clink" sound will be heard when it is broken open. Otherwise a short melody will be heard and the item will sparkle.

[Artifact Troves](/Artifact_Trove "Artifact Trove") can be purchased from the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for 5 [Omni Geodes](/Omni_Geode "Omni Geode"). Clint will break these open like a geode, but instead of [minerals](/Minerals "Minerals"), [artifacts](/Artifacts "Artifacts") or treasures can be found inside.

## Tips

* To avoid missing a day of watering, water crops, then upgrade the Watering Can when the weather report indicates rain the next day. Crops will be watered the day the can is taken to the Blacksmith, the following day by the rain, and the Watering Can can be picked up the day after that. It can also be a good idea to upgrade the watering can near the end of a season if there is nothing in the ground that will bear fruit until then.
* Regarding coal prices, during year 1, it's cheaper to buy wood from the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop") and use a [Charcoal Kiln](/Charcoal_Kiln "Charcoal Kiln") to make [coal](/Coal "Coal") than it is to buy coal from the Blacksmith's. Conversely, starting in year 2, the price of [wood](/Wood "Wood") increases from data-sort-value="10">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)10g to data-sort-value="50">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)50g, so it is cheaper to purchase coal.
* At [Mining Skill](/Skills#Mining "Skills") 7, it is cheaper to purchase [Iron Ore](/Iron_Ore "Iron Ore"), smelt [Iron Bars](/Iron_Bar "Iron Bar"), and use the Transmute (Au) recipe to create [Gold Bars](/Gold_Bar "Gold Bar") than it is to purchase [Gold Ore](/Gold_Ore "Gold Ore") and smelt it into Gold Bars.
* Although the door to the Blacksmith locks at 4pm, as long as the player is inside the building they can still interact with the counter after 4pm to make purchases, upgrade tools etc., even after Clint walks away from the counter at 5pm.
