The **Cheese Press** is a type of [Artisan Equipment](/Crafting#Artisan_Equipment "Crafting") used to make [Artisan Goods](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods"). It takes [Milk](/Milk "Milk") and turns it into [Cheese](/Cheese "Cheese").

Regular quality [Milk](/Milk "Milk") produces regular quality [Cheese](/Cheese "Cheese"), and regular quality [Goat Milk](/Goat_Milk "Goat Milk") produces regular quality [Goat Cheese](/Goat_Cheese "Goat Cheese"). [Large Milk](/Large_Milk "Large Milk") and [Large Goat Milk](/Large_Goat_Milk "Large Goat Milk") produce gold quality cheeses. Cheese and Goat Cheese can be aged in [Casks](/Cask "Cask") to increase their value.

A Cheese Press is the reward for completing the ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Animal Bundle](/Bundles#Animal_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Pantry](/Bundles#Pantry "Bundles").
