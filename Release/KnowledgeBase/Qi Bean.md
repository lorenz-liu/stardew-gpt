The **Qi Bean** is a seed found throughout Stardew Valley and on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") during the quest [Qi's Crop](/Quests#Qi.27s_Crop "Quests"). Mature plants yield [Qi Fruit](/Qi_Fruit "Qi Fruit").

All [Qi Fruit](/Qi_Fruit "Qi Fruit") and Qi Beans despawn when the quest is over (*i.e.,* the day after the player ships them or the quest timer ends), including growing crops, all players' inventories, [Chests](/Chest "Chest"), [Preserves Jars](/Preserves_Jar "Preserves Jar"), [Kegs](/Keg "Keg"), and [Marlon](/Marlon "Marlon")'s [Item Recovery Service](/Adventurer%27s_Guild#Item_Recovery_Service "Adventurer's Guild") list. [Giant Qi Fruit](/Crops#Giant_Crops "Crops") will not disappear.

## Locations

While the quest is active, Qi Beans can be found by:

* Breaking rocks - each rock has a 2% chance of dropping a Qi Bean[[1]](#cite_note-rock-1)
* Shaking [Trees](/Trees "Trees") - whenever shaking a tree drops a seed, there is a 50% chance it will also drop a Qi Bean[[2]](#cite_note-palm-2)
* Chopping [Trees](/Trees "Trees") - each tree has a 25% chance of dropping a Qi Bean[[3]](#cite_note-tree-3)
* Chopping [Large Stumps](/Large_Stump "Large Stump") and [Large Logs](/Large_Log "Large Log") - each one has a 25% chance of dropping a Qi Bean[[4]](#cite_note-stump-4)
* Cutting [Weeds](/Weeds "Weeds") - each weed has a 1% chance of dropping a Qi Bean[[5]](#cite_note-weed-5)
* Checking [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can")[[6]](#cite_note-garbage-6)
* Killing [Monsters](/Monsters "Monsters") - [Dust Sprites](/Dust_Sprite "Dust Sprite") have a 2% chance of dropping a Qi Bean, all other monsters have a 5% chance[[7]](#cite_note-monster-7)
* Breaking [Geodes](/Minerals#Geodes "Minerals"), [Artifact Troves](/Artifact_Trove "Artifact Trove"), or [Golden Coconuts](/Golden_Coconut "Golden Coconut") - each one has a 7.5% chance of dropping one Qi Bean, and a 2.5% chance of dropping 5[[8]](#cite_note-geode-8)
* Digging [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot")[[9]](#cite_note-artifactspot-9)
* Opening Volcano chests[[10]](#cite_note-chest-10)
* Breaking crates in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") and [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon") - each one has a 4% chance of dropping 1-2 Qi beans[[11]](#cite_note-crate-11)
* [Fishing](/Fishing "Fishing")[[12]](#cite_note-fish-12)
* Petting a [pet cat](/Animals#Pets "Animals") with max friendship[[13]](#cite_note-cat-13)

They can also be made by placing a Qi Fruit in a [Seed Maker](/Seed_Maker "Seed Maker").

## Dyeing

The Qi Bean can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a blue dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Tips

* Given that [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") have a 43% chance (51% chance for [Golden Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Golden_Fishing_Treasure_Chests "Fishing")) to yield at least one stack of up to 4 Qi Beans during the quest, fishing is a reliable source of Qi Beans when combined with the [Magnet](/Magnet "Magnet") bait, [Pirate Profession](/Skills#Fishing "Skills"), and a [Treasure Hunter](/Treasure_Hunter "Treasure Hunter") tackle.
* Putting Qi Fruit into [Seed Makers](/Seed_Maker "Seed Maker") and planting the resulting Qi Beans will, on average, nearly double the Qi Fruit supply. Qi Fruit produced early in the quest can be doubled several times with this method.
* The use of [Speed-Gro](/Speed-Gro "Speed-Gro"), [Deluxe Speed-Gro](/Deluxe_Speed-Gro "Deluxe Speed-Gro"), or [Hyper Speed-Gro](/Hyper_Speed-Gro "Hyper Speed-Gro"), and the [Agriculturist Profession](/Skills#Farming "Skills") can reduce the time between harvests by 1 or 2 days.
* Qi Beans can also be amassed quickly using [Treasure Totems](/Treasure_Totem "Treasure Totem"). During the quest, [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") have a 25% chance to drop 2-5 Qi Beans, so each Treasure Totem will produce 14 Qi Beans on average, assuming no obstacle blocks any of the 16 tiles.
