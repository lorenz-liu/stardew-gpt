**Bread** is a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit").

**Note:** This recipe is [profitable](/Cooking#Profitable_Recipes "Cooking") - it will result in profit when using [Wheat Flour](/Wheat_Flour "Wheat Flour") made in the [Mill](/Mill "Mill").

Bread is available at [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon") every day for data-sort-value="120">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)120g, and randomly from [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus")' shop on [Saturdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Saturday "Days of the Week"). It can also be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can"). One Bread can sometimes be purchased for data-sort-value="2500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,500g from [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre")'s booth at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"). One Bread may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box").

## Tailoring

Bread is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt061.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/6e/Shirt061.png/24px-Shirt061.png)](/File:Shirt061.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").
