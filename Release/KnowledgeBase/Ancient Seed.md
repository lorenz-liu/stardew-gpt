The **Ancient Seed** is an [Artifact](/Artifacts "Artifacts") that can be found by digging up an [Artifact Spot](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") in [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") or [The Mountain](/The_Mountain "The Mountain") (including the [Quarry](/Quarry "Quarry")), or obtained inside [Artifact Troves](/Artifact_Trove "Artifact Trove"), or [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") after reaching [Fishing level 2](/Fishing#Fishing_Skill "Fishing"). It can be dropped from [Bugs](/Bug "Bug"), [Grubs](/Grub "Grub"), [Cave Flies](/Cave_Fly "Cave Fly"), [Mutant Flies](/Mutant_Fly "Mutant Fly"), and [Mutant Grubs](/Mutant_Grub "Mutant Grub") (0.5% chance). It can also be found by cutting foliage on [Prehistoric Floors](/Skull_Cavern#Prehistoric_Floors "Skull Cavern"), in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") when they are in a "Dangerous" state, or in the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon")[[1]](#cite_note-cosmeticplant_performtoolaction-1). Placing a packet of ![Ancient Seeds.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/ec/Ancient_Seeds.png/24px-Ancient_Seeds.png) [Ancient Seeds](/Ancient_Seeds "Ancient Seeds") in a [Deconstructor](/Deconstructor "Deconstructor") yields an Ancient Seed.

This artifact can be [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting") into viable [seeds](/Ancient_Seeds "Ancient Seeds") that can be planted to grow [Ancient Fruit](/Ancient_Fruit "Ancient Fruit") during [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), [Summer](/Summer "Summer") and [Fall](/Fall "Fall"), or during any season in the [Greenhouse](/Greenhouse "Greenhouse") or the [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") farm.

## Donation

The only productive use of the first Ancient Seed artifact found is to donate it to the [Museum](/Museum "Museum"). The item contributes to the total count of Museum donations. [Gunther](/Gunther "Gunther") will reward the player with one pack of plantable Ancient Seeds and the recipe to craft any further Ancient Seed artifacts into more seeds.
