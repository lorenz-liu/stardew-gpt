**Triple Shot Espresso** is a [cooked](/Cooking "Cooking") drink. It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit"). Before it can be cooked, the recipe must be purchased from [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon") for data-sort-value="5000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)5,000g.

Triple Shot Espresso can also be purchased at the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") in exchange for a [Diamond](/Diamond "Diamond"), sometimes at the [Stardew Valley Fair](/Stardew_Valley_Fair "Stardew Valley Fair") for data-sort-value="400">![Token.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/29/Token.png/24px-Token.png)400, at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star") for data-sort-value="2500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,500g, or at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="450"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,350–2,250g. One to two Triple Shot Espresso may occasionally be found in [Supply Crates](/Supply_Crate "Supply Crate") on the [Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps#Beach "Farm Maps") if the player upgraded the [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") at least once. Three Triple Shot Espresso is the reward for donating 70 items to the [Museum](/Museum "Museum"). Wearing a [Hot Java Ring](/Hot_Java_Ring "Hot Java Ring") gives a 7.5% chance for monsters to drop Triple Shot Espresso when killed. Two Triple Shot Espresso is one of the prizes for the [Trout Derby](/Trout_Derby "Trout Derby") festival. Three Triple Shot Espresso is a guaranteed gold tier reward on the 13th for the [SquidFest](/SquidFest "SquidFest") festival. Three Triple Shot Espresso may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") or [Golden Mystery Box](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box"). Five Triple Shot Espresso can be obtained as a prize from the [Prize Machine](/Mayor%27s_Manor#Prize_Machine "Mayor's Manor"). One to five are rewarded for submitting an [Egg Rating](/Desert_Festival#Skull_Cavern "Desert Festival") of 10-54 to [Gil](/Gil "Gil") during the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival").

When consumed, it grants the player +1 [Speed](/Speed#Player_Speed "Speed") for 4m 12s. The speed of the player's [horse](/Animals#Horse "Animals") is also increased.
As a drink, its [buff](/Buffs "Buffs") stacks with any food buffs (including Speed), but replaces any other drink buffs (*i.e.,* from [Coffee](/Coffee "Coffee"), [Ginger Ale](/Ginger_Ale "Ginger Ale") or [Green Tea](/Green_Tea "Green Tea")).

## Bundles

Triple Shot Espresso is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Recipes

Triple Shot Espresso is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Triple Shot Espresso can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [![Shirt269.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/42/Shirt269.png/24px-Shirt269.png)](/File:Shirt269.png) [Pour-Over Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Triple Shot Espresso is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Secret

After hatching the first [Dinosaur Egg](/Dinosaur_Egg "Dinosaur Egg"), [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius") will gift the player a Triple Shot Espresso.
