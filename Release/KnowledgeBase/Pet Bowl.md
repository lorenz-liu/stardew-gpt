The **Pet Bowl** is a [farm building](/Carpenter%27s_Shop#Farm_Buildings "Carpenter's Shop") purchasable from [Robin](/Robin "Robin") at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop"). One free Pet Bowl is provided to the player at the start of their file for their free [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals"). This free Pet Bowl remains on the farm, even if the player declines to adopt the pet.

Additional Pet Bowls do not have to be constructed before adopting more pets from [Marnie's Ranch](/Marnie%27s_Ranch "Marnie's Ranch"). However, for every night that a pet does not have its own Pet Bowl, they will lose 10 [friendship points](/Animals#Friendship "Animals") the next morning.[[1]](#cite_note-petnobowl-1) Watering the pet's bowl will increase the pet's friendship by 6 points by the next morning. Note that this will not trigger if it is raining the next morning.

[Flooring](/Crafting#Decor "Crafting") can be used to cover the 4 tiles in the bowl's area, but the bowl itself will be visible over the flooring.

There are 3 varieties of Pet Bowl, which change slightly depending on the [season](/Seasons "Seasons"). A Pet Bowl is initially Wood, but that can be changed through [Painting](/Carpenter%27s_Shop#Painting "Carpenter's Shop").
