**Void Mayonnaise** is an [Artisan Good](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods") made by placing a [Void Egg](/Void_Egg "Void Egg") inside the [Mayonnaise Machine](/Mayonnaise_Machine "Mayonnaise Machine"). It can also be obtained by [fishing](/Fishing "Fishing") in the [Witch's Swamp](/Witch%27s_Swamp "Witch's Swamp") if the player does not have one in inventory and the [Henchman](/Henchman "Henchman") is guarding the [Witch's Hut](/Witch%27s_Hut "Witch's Hut") (5% chance).

Gifting a Void Mayonnaise to the Henchman outside the [Witch's Hut](/Witch%27s_Hut "Witch's Hut") will allow players to pass during the quest [Goblin Problem](/Quests#Goblin_Problem "Quests").

## Tailoring

Void Mayonnaise can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Polka Dot Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt272.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/5/57/Shirt272.png/24px-Shirt272.png)](/File:Shirt272.png) It can also be used as a red dye in the dye pots, located at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* Void Mayonnaise is required to complete the ["Goblin Problem" Quest](/Quests#Goblin_Problem "Quests"). One must be gifted to the [Henchman](/Henchman "Henchman") guarding the [Witch's Hut](/Witch%27s_Hut "Witch's Hut").
