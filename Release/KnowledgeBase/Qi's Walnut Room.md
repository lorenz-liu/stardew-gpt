The **Walnut Room** is located on the West side of [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island#Island_West "Ginger Island"). It can be opened once the player obtains a total of 100 [golden walnuts](/Golden_Walnut "Golden Walnut") (see [Note](#Notes) below). Inside is a [Perfection](/Perfection "Perfection") Tracker, a Special Order Board, and a Vending Machine that sells items in exchange for [Qi Gems](/Qi_Gem "Qi Gem").

Qi Gems can't be earned before unlocking the room, as the 3 methods to obtain them (via the Special Orders Board, by activating the [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines"), and by exchanging [Golden Walnuts](/Golden_Walnut "Golden Walnut")) require entering this room.

## Perfection Tracker

The perfection tracker is a statistics board that shows the player how much of the game they have completed.

The perfection score shown in the room is always rounded down (*e.x.* if the player has completed 80.6% of perfection, the percentage shown on the perfection tracker will be 80%). This also means that the perfection score will only show 100% if the player has actually achieved 100% Perfection. Reaching 100% Perfection unlocks [new content](/Perfection#Unlocked_content "Perfection").

## Notes

* When calculating Golden Walnuts needed to enter the Walnut Room, the first [Golden Walnut](/Golden_Walnut "Golden Walnut") found is not counted, meaning the player must actually find 101 Golden Walnuts in total before unlocking the room.

## Bugs

* On some occasions the [Golden Walnut](/Golden_Walnut "Golden Walnut") Count on the [Perfection Tracker](#Perfection_Tracker) will display an incorrect number of walnuts. This can be fixed by editing the save file. If this is not an option, the command `/recountnuts` can be typed into the chat interface. This will recount the walnuts found and repair the save.
