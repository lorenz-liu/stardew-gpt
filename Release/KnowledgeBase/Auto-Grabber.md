The **Auto-Grabber** automatically harvests milk from [cows](/Cow "Cow") and [goats](/Goat "Goat"), [wool](/Wool "Wool") from [sheep](/Sheep "Sheep") and [Ostrich Eggs](/Ostrich_Egg "Ostrich Egg") from [Ostriches](/Ostrich "Ostrich") when placed in a [Barn](/Barn "Barn"). It automatically harvests all products produced by [Coop](/Coop "Coop") animals when placed in a coop. It can be purchased from [Marnie's Ranch](/Marnie%27s_Ranch "Marnie's Ranch") for data-sort-value="25000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)25,000g after obtaining [Farming Level 10](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming"). Once unlocked, players may purchase as many Auto-Grabbers as they can afford. An Auto-Grabber may occasionally be found in [treasure rooms](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern") in the Skull Cavern.

Each morning, the Auto-Grabber will automatically harvest all products in the Barn or Coop where it is placed. The player does not gain any [Farming](/Farming "Farming") experience points or [Mastery points](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave") for products harvested by an Auto-Grabber.

The Auto-Grabber acts like a chest, holding up to 36 harvested items (or stacks of harvested items). Items can be viewed or removed by right-clicking on the Auto-Grabber. Note that an empty Auto-Grabber cannot be opened.

Auto-Grabbers can be placed anywhere, but will not do anything unless placed inside a barn or coop. To remove an Auto-Grabber, it must be struck with a [tool](/Tools "Tools") (repeated left-clicking or holding left-click will not work).

Auto-Grabbers cannot be sold or [deconstructed](/Deconstructor "Deconstructor").

## Animal Friendship

The Auto-Grabber prevents the player from earning +5 [friendship points](/Animals#Friendship "Animals") for manually milking or shearing an animal. It will take longer for animals who have not reached the maximum of 5 hearts to do so, if placed in a barn with an Auto-Grabber. Keep in mind, animals will lose 5-10 friendship if not interacted with, meaning animals who aren't hand-pet, milked, or sheared, will lose friendship gradually, despite still being fed and let outside.

This effect can be cancelled out by the use of an [Auto-Petter](/Auto-Petter "Auto-Petter").

## Mail

The day after reaching [Farming Level 10](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming"), the player will receive a letter in the mail from [Marnie](/Marnie "Marnie"). It is not necessary to read this letter to be able to purchase Auto-Grabbers; they can be purchased as soon as Farming level 10 is achieved.
