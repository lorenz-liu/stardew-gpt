**Penny** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). She's one of the twelve characters available to [marry](/Marriage "Marriage"). Her [trailer](/Trailer "Trailer") is just east of the center of town, west of the river. She tutors Vincent and Jas at the library.

## Schedule

Penny can usually be found in town reading or cleaning up at the [trailer](/Trailer "Trailer"). On Tuesday, Wednesday, and Friday she tutors [Jas](/Jas "Jas") and [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent") at the [museum](/Museum "Museum"), walking them both home afterwards. On Saturdays she'll take them to the [town playground](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town").

When it's raining Penny can either be found inside her [trailer](/Trailer "Trailer") or visiting the [museum](/Museum "Museum") looking at the selection of books.

After the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island is unlocked, Penny may randomly spend the day there. After leaving the Island at 6pm, Penny will immediately go home to bed. Penny never visits the Resort on Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday, [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days, or her checkup day at [Harvey's Clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic").

## Relationships

Penny lives with her mother [Pam](/Pam "Pam"), and she is friends with [Maru](/Maru "Maru") and [Sam](/Sam "Sam"). Penny dances with Sam at the [Flower Dance](/Flower_Dance "Flower Dance") if neither are dancing with the player.

She also teaches the local children, [Jas](/Jas "Jas"), [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent") and when he moves to [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") also [Leo](/Leo "Leo"), at the [museum](/Museum "Museum") a few days a week and accompanies them to the playground once in a while.

During the eight-heart cutscene, Vincent remarks that he once saw Sam and Penny climbing trees, at which Penny becomes visibly startled and scolds Vincent for gossiping. [ConcernedApe](/ConcernedApe "ConcernedApe") has explained that he "wasn't trying to imply anything serious" with the scene regarding a continuation of Penny's interest in Sam. "[A]t 8 hearts...she's not actually interested in Sam anymore."[[1]](#cite_note-1)

## Marriage

:   *Main article: [Marriage](/Marriage "Marriage")*

Once married, Penny will move into the [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse"). Like other marriage candidates, she will add her own room to the right of the bedroom. She'll also set up a small garden behind the farmhouse where she'll sometimes go to read. She will continue to work at the [Museum](/Museum "Museum") and travel to [town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") to work there on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Fridays.

On rainy mornings, Penny may offer you [Large Milk](/Large_Milk "Large Milk"), [Egg](/Egg "Egg"), [Mixed Seeds](/Mixed_Seeds "Mixed Seeds"), or a [Geode](/Geode "Geode"). On mornings when Penny stays inside all day, she may offer you [Omelet](/Omelet "Omelet"), [Hashbrowns](/Hashbrowns "Hashbrowns"), or [Pancakes](/Pancakes "Pancakes") for breakfast. On rainy nights she may offer you dinner: [Salmon Dinner](/Salmon_Dinner "Salmon Dinner"), [Crispy Bass](/Crispy_Bass "Crispy Bass"), [Fried Eel](/Fried_Eel "Fried Eel"), [Carp Surprise](/Carp_Surprise "Carp Surprise"), or [Vegetable Medley](/Vegetable_Medley "Vegetable Medley").

## Trivia

* At one point before the game was released, Penny was named "Dana".
* The dialogue choices for the player in Penny's 8-Heart and 10-Heart Events have the most punitive possible effect on friendship in the game, with each event having an option that will decrease friendship with her by 1500 points (equivalent to six hearts).
* The dish Penny cooks during her six heart event can be seen briefly when you eat it. It appears to be a [Vegetable Medley](/Vegetable_Medley "Vegetable Medley"), sharing the same sprite and restoring the same amount of energy as one.
* Penny's 14-heart event can occur one time only. It will not reoccur if the player divorces her, erases her memories, and then re-marries her.

## Bugs

* If Penny's 10-heart event is seen before her other heart events, occasionally the bachelorette Group Ten-Heart Event will not trigger when entering [Haley & Emily's house](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").
* Penny's marriage schedule overrides any deviations. This means Penny will still walk home the children even if it is raining, even though the children are not there.
