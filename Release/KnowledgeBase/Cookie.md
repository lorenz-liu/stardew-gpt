**Cookie** is a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit").

The Cookie recipe is normally obtained during [Evelyn](/Evelyn "Evelyn")'s 4-heart event. If, however, players experience a bug in which they view Evelyn's event but don't receive the recipe, it will be sold at [The Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon") for data-sort-value="300">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)300g.

Cookies can be received as a gift from [Evelyn](/Evelyn "Evelyn") at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"), and are rarely found in the [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") outside [George and Evelyn's home](/1_River_Road "1 River Road") or outside the saloon. Evelyn sells five Cookies in her [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="20">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 20 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg") each. Cookies may randomly appear in [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus")' shop on [Saturdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Saturday "Days of the Week") or in [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon")'s rotating stock. One Cookie may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box"). If the player's character is male, after he earns data-sort-value="5000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)5,000g he will receive a [letter from mom](/Mom_and_Dad "Mom and Dad") with cookies enclosed.

## Bundles

Cookie is an option in the ![Bundle Green.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/b3/Bundle_Green.png/24px-Bundle_Green.png) [Children's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Children.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Remixed Bundles").

## Tailoring

Cookie is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt077.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/0c/Shirt077.png/24px-Shirt077.png)](/File:Shirt077.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

One Cookie may be requested by [Blobfish](/Blobfish "Blobfish") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 5 to 7.

## Notes

[Chocolate Cake](/Chocolate_Cake "Chocolate Cake") uses the same ingredients as Cookies, but sells for more and is loved by [Abigail](/Abigail "Abigail"), [Evelyn](/Evelyn "Evelyn"), and [Jodi](/Jodi "Jodi"), and liked by [Leah](/Leah "Leah"), whereas Cookies are disliked by Leah and only liked by Abigail, Evelyn, and Jodi.
