**Jodi** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). She lives at [1 Willow Lane](/1_Willow_Lane "1 Willow Lane") with her husband [Kent](/Kent "Kent") (who is away serving in the military until the first of Spring Year 2) and two sons, [Sam](/Sam "Sam") and [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent").

## Schedule

After the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island is unlocked, Jodi may randomly spend the day there. After leaving the Island at 6pm, Jodi will immediately go home to bed. Jodi never visits the Resort on [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days or her checkup day at [Harvey's Clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic"). She also won't visit the Resort on [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent")'s checkup day.

## Relationships

Jodi is married to Kent and together they have two sons, Sam and Vincent.

Jodi will mention that [Caroline](/Caroline "Caroline") is her best friend and that she is able to tell her anything. They also attend exercise classes along with some of the other Pelican Town ladies.
