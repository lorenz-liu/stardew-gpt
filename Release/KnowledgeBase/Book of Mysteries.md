The **Book of Mysteries** is a power [book](/Books "Books") that can be obtained by opening either [Mystery Boxes](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") or [Golden Mystery Boxes](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box"). The chance to drop the book starts at ≈1% for Mystery Boxes and ≈2% for Golden Mystery Boxes, and it increases by 0.04% for each of either box opened until it is dropped. Then, the chance stays at ≈0.5% for Mystery Boxes and ≈1% for Golden Mystery Boxes. It cannot drop from blue Mystery Boxes until the player has opened more than 10 of either type of box.[[1]](#cite_note-mysterychance-1)

The first reading grants the player a power that increases the chance to get [Mystery Boxes](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") or [Golden Mystery Boxes](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box") (after claiming [Foraging Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave")) by 33.3% in all situations with a random chance of earning a mystery box.[[2]](#cite_note-rollMysteryBox-2) Once read, it can be found in [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s Menu on the [Special Items & Powers](/Special_Items_%26_Powers "Special Items & Powers") tab. Each subsequent reading gives the player 20 [XP](/Skills "Skills") in each of the 5 skills.

## Bundles

Book of Mysteries is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Book of Mysteries is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a blue dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a blue dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

Book of Mysteries is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Trading

One Book of Mysteries can be traded to the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") for 7 [Mystery Boxes](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box"), provided the player has already obtained the power from this book.
