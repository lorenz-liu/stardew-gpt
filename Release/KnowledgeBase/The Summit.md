**The Summit** is an exterior region located north of the [Railroad](/Railroad "Railroad"). The path to it is initially blocked by a boulder and becomes accessible the day after [Perfection](/Perfection "Perfection") is achieved. The [weather](/Weather "Weather") here is always sunny.

## Cutscene

Entering the Summit for the first time causes a special cutscene to play. The player will sit on the left side of the bench at the top of the summit, and the player's [spouse](/Marriage "Marriage") will occupy the right side. If the player is not married, the spot will be occupied by [Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis") if the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") was restored or [Morris](/Morris "Morris") if the [Joja Warehouse](/Joja_Warehouse "Joja Warehouse") was built. The player will then chat with them.

| **Details** |
| --- |
| If Lewis is sitting on the right side of the bench, he will talk about how he's been mayor for a long time, and how nothing has made him as proud as seeing the player make the farm blossom. He will say that the player has taken the farm beyond their Grandpa's wildest dreams and how they have changed the valley forever. He ends by thanking the player.  If Morris is sitting on the right side of the bench, he will talk about how before the player moved, he considered moving into Pelican Town a step back in his career. He then says that the valley has grown on him over the years, and that it's been a humbling experience and opened his eyes to a different way of life. He ends by thanking the player for all the work they have done for Joja.  If the player's spouse is sitting on the right side of the bench, they will first greet the player and talk about how beautiful (or great) it is up at the summit. They will then say how they were thinking about the last # of years (the # of in-game years the player has played so far, including the current one), and how they and the player have been through a lot together during that time but have come so far. There will then be unique dialogue depending on the spouse. This part of the cutscene ends with the player and their spouse kissing. |

Afterwards, the scene continues with [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus"), [Dwarf](/Dwarf "Dwarf"), and [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard") flying through the air followed by [the Witch](/Random_Events#The_Witch "Random Events"), [crop fairies](/Random_Events#The_Crop_Fairy "Random Events"), UFOs, and if the player has earned at least data-sort-value="100000000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100,000,000g, the [traveling merchant's](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") pig. Then, a credits-like sequence plays, which scrolls through every [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") (including [Dusty](/Dusty "Dusty")), farm animal (except the [brown cow](/Cow "Cow")), [fish](/Fish "Fish"), and [monster](/Monsters "Monsters"). If the player did not use any Joja services (*i.e.* they restored the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center"), did not use the [Golden Joja Parrot](/Golden_Walnut#Golden_Joja_Parrot "Golden Walnut"), and did not purchase any [Perfection Waivers](/Fizz "Fizz")), the end of the credits will say "No Joja services used!" with an animation of [Morris](/Morris "Morris") flying.

The cutscene ends with the player's [Grandpa](/Grandpa "Grandpa") and [Mr. Qi](/Mr._Qi "Mr. Qi") congratulating the player on their accomplishment.

Assuming the player reads all the dialogue at the beginning of the cutscene and plays through it entirely, the entire cutscene lasts for approximately five minutes.

## Illegitimate access

If the player manages to access the Summit before achieving Perfection via the [sword swing glitch](/Weapons#Glitches "Weapons"), the normal summit background will be replaced with a blank screen full of glitched sprites and a cutscene with [Mr. Qi](/Mr._Qi "Mr. Qi") will play instead.

Look who 'made it' to the summit.” |  |  |  | | --- | --- | | “ | “Thought you were really clever, didn't you?” |  |  |  | | --- | --- | | “ | “'How can I cheat the system for my own benefit?', you thought.” |  |  |  | | --- | --- | | “ | “Or, in layman's terms: 'Me me me me me me me me me me me me'.” |  |  |  | | --- | --- | | “ | “Despicable.” | |

Afterwards Mr. Qi rushes at the player, causing the player to pass out and wake up in [Harvey's Clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic"). Further attempts result in the player passing out immediately after entering the map.

## Trivia

* Despite the Summit being an exterior region, it is not visible on the map. Entering the region will automatically hide the player's HUD.
* The cutscene is the only part of the game that hints at a relationship between the player's [Grandpa](/Grandpa "Grandpa") and [Mr. Qi](/Mr._Qi "Mr. Qi").
* If the player has met certain criteria, some [secret objects](/Secrets#Summit "Secrets") may fly past.
* Prior to version 1.5, the Summit existed as an unimplemented area of the game with broken sprites. It was reachable using the "[Sword swing glitch](/Weapons#Glitches "Weapons")".
