The **Fish Smoker** is a type of [Artisan Equipment](/Crafting#Artisan_Equipment "Crafting") that is used to create [smoked fish](/Smoked_Fish "Smoked Fish") using any [fish](/Fish "Fish") and 1 [coal](/Coal "Coal"). It doubles the sell price of a fish while retaining quality, as well as multiplying the [energy](/Energy "Energy") and [health](/Health "Health") restoration by 1.5. The smoker accepts [Legendary Fish](/Fish#Legendary_Fish "Fish") as well as [Crab Pot](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot") specific fish, making the latter edible.[[1]](#cite_note-inedible-1)

It can be crafted after purchasing the recipe from the [Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop") for data-sort-value="10000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)10,000g. It is also a possible reward as the 12th prize from the [Prize Machine](/Mayor%27s_Manor#Prize_Machine "Mayor's Manor") in the Mayor's Manor (50% chance). Players on the [Riverland Farm](/Farm_Maps#Riverland "Farm Maps") start with one Fish Smoker.
