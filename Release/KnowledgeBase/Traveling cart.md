The **Traveling Cart** is a [shop](/Category:Stores "Category:Stores") that appears south of [The Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") in [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") on Fridays and Sundays, closing at 8pm. It also appears each day of the [Night Market](/Night_Market "Night Market") ([Winter](/Winter "Winter") 15-17) and each day of the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") ([Spring](/Spring "Spring") 15-17). Stock changes each day the Traveling Merchant appears. The merchant will sell a selection of ten random items in limited quantities (1 or 5 of each) as "standard stock", plus one piece of furniture at any one time. There may be an additional "special stock" item for sale as well.

Traveling Cart prices may vary from visit to visit. Prices are usually quite inflated, but every once in a while an item can be sold for less than its normal price. Do note that items are sometimes found for sale outside their normal seasons, which can be helpful for completing [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

The cart is pulled by a pig wearing a fez and spectacles. Interacting with the pig will result in it oinking at the character. During the [Night Market](/Night_Market "Night Market"), the pig floats behind the cart in an inner tube.

## Guaranteed Red Cabbage

If the *Guarantee Year 1 Completable* [advanced option](/Options "Options") was enabled when creating the save, the traveling cart is guaranteed to sell [Red Cabbage Seeds](/Red_Cabbage_Seeds "Red Cabbage Seeds") at least once in the first year, between spring 7 in [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") (the cart's second visit) and winter 16 at the [Night Market](/Night_Market "Night Market") (the cart's 30th visit). The day is selected at random when the save is created. The guarantee only applies on that single day, even if the player doesn't check the cart or buy the seeds on that day.[[2]](#cite_note-guaranteeseeds-2)

## External Links

Players with access to their [saved game files](/Saves "Saves") may find the "Stardew Predictor" utility helpful for predicting which items the Traveling Cart will offer each week, and during the [Night Market](/Night_Market "Night Market"). The utility is located at <https://mouseypounds.github.io/stardew-predictor/>.

## Bugs

* For the Android and iOS version of the game, after buying something from the cart and closing and reopening the shop menu, the item can be purchased again. This can be repeated indefinitely.

1. [↑](#cite_ref-teaset25_1-0) See `Content\Data\Shops.xnb`. In the Traveler's section, the condition for the Tea Set to appear is `YEAR 25, SYNCED_RANDOM day teaset .05`.
2. [↑](#cite_ref-guaranteeseeds_2-0) The game code initializes `Game1.netWorldState.Value.VisitsUntilY1Guarantee` in `Game1::loadForNewGame`, then decrements it for each traveling cart visit. Red Cabbage Seeds are guaranteed when the value reaches zero, after which it's decremented to -1 and no longer applies.
