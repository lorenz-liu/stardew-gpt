The **Green Bean** is a [vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from [Bean Starter](/Bean_Starter "Bean Starter") after 10 days.

This crop uses a trellis, and continues to produce after maturity. Be aware, you cannot move onto a tile with a trellis, but you must be adjacent to a planted tile in order to harvest the plant. You must plan the layout of your plantings in order to guarantee access for harvesting.

## Stages

You cannot walk through green beans at any stage of growth. You can walk through dead Green Bean plants.

## Tailoring

Green Bean is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a dyeable ![Shirt159.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/99/Shirt159.png/24px-Shirt159.png) [Shirt And Belt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a green dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the green dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").
