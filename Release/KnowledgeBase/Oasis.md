The **Oasis** is a store located in [The Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") and run by [Sandy](/Sandy "Sandy"), open from 9am to 11:50pm. It sells unique seeds as well as items purchasable from other shops, sometimes at a reduced price.

The path in the back leads to the [Casino](/Casino "Casino"), run by [Mr. Qi](/Mr._Qi "Mr. Qi"), which is accessible after completion of ["The Mysterious Qi" quest](/Quests#The_Mysterious_Qi "Quests").

The [Key To The Town](/Key_To_The_Town "Key To The Town") does not function at this store. However, this store is still open on [festival days](/Festivals "Festivals") with the exception of the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival").

## Notes

* On Winter 25, despite the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"), Pam will still drive the bus as on her regular schedule, allowing the player access to the desert.
* [Emily](/Emily "Emily") visits Sandy in the desert on Sandy's birthday (Fall 15), closing the store at 1pm.

## History

* [1.3.27](/Version_History#1.3.27 "Version History"): Added Cactus Seeds and Seasonal Plant to fixed stock.

* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Added mannequins, coat stand, and clothesline to shop inventory. If [Pam](/Pam "Pam") is not available the player can drive the bus. Coconut price changed from 400g to 200g. Omni Geode price changed from 1000g to 500g. Now open during festivals except for the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival").
