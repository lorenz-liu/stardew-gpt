# Minecart

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a3/Destinations.png/500px-Destinations.png)](/File:Destinations.png)

Destinations available from [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town")

The **Minecart** is a method of travel unlocked after completing the [Boiler Room Bundles](/Bundles#Boiler_Room "Bundles") or by purchasing "Minecarts" from the [Joja Community Development Form](/Joja_Community_Development_Form "Joja Community Development Form") at [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart") for data-sort-value="15000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)15,000g. They are available for travel the next day.

Minecarts allow the player to travel between four destinations throughout Stardew Valley.
