# Forge

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/b2/Forge_Interface.png/300px-Forge_Interface.png)](/File:Forge_Interface.png)

The Forge Interface

The **Forge** is located on Floor 10 in the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon"). It is used to upgrade [weapons](/Weapons "Weapons"), enchant weapons and [tools](/Tools "Tools"), combine [rings](/Rings "Rings"), and change weapon appearances.

After obtaining [Combat Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave"), the player can craft a [Mini-Forge](/Mini-Forge "Mini-Forge"), which acts exactly the same as this one and can be placed anywhere.

## Weapon forging

A melee weapon (dagger, hammer, or sword) can be forged up to three times to improve its stats. Each forge costs one gem and 10/15/20 ![Cinder Shard.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/fd/Cinder_Shard.png/24px-Cinder_Shard.png) [Cinder Shard](/Cinder_Shard "Cinder Shard")s depending on how many times it was previously forged.

Using a ![Diamond.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/ea/Diamond.png/24px-Diamond.png) [Diamond](/Diamond "Diamond") as the gem fills all remaining forge levels with random upgrades, but consumes only 10 [Cinder Shards](/Cinder_Shard "Cinder Shard").

Note that "level" refers to the number of times a weapon was forged with the specific gem. So for instance, if a weapon was forged with a [Ruby](/Ruby "Ruby"), then an [Emerald](/Emerald "Emerald"), and then an [Amethyst](/Amethyst "Amethyst"), the weapon stats will have +10% attack power, +2 speed, and +1 weight.

| Topaz.png [Topaz](/Topaz "Topaz") | More Defense.png [Defense](/Defense "Defense") (adds 1 per level). |

## Enchantments

A random enchantment can be added to any melee weapon or tool, with the exceptions of the [Scythe](/Scythe "Scythe"), [Golden Scythe](/Golden_Scythe "Golden Scythe"), [Iridium Scythe](/Iridium_Scythe "Iridium Scythe"), and [Trash Can](/Trash_Cans "Trash Cans"). This costs one ![Prismatic Shard.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/5/56/Prismatic_Shard.png/24px-Prismatic_Shard.png) [Prismatic Shard](/Prismatic_Shard "Prismatic Shard") and 20 ![Cinder Shard.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/fd/Cinder_Shard.png/24px-Cinder_Shard.png) [Cinder Shard](/Cinder_Shard "Cinder Shard")s.

A random innate enchantment can be added to any melee weapon, with the exceptions of [Galaxy weapons](/Galaxy_Sword "Galaxy Sword") and [Infinity weapons](/Forge#Infinity_Weapons "Forge"). This costs one ![Dragon Tooth.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/87/Dragon_Tooth.png/24px-Dragon_Tooth.png) [Dragon Tooth](/Dragon_Tooth "Dragon Tooth") and 10 ![Cinder Shard.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/fd/Cinder_Shard.png/24px-Cinder_Shard.png) [Cinder Shard](/Cinder_Shard "Cinder Shard")s.

Each weapon or tool can have only one enchantment and one set of innate enchantments at a time, and the applied enchantments are random. However, another enchantment can be applied (at the same price) to replace an existing one. Tools track the previous two enchantments applied to them so they're not reselected when applying a new enchantment.

Upgrading a tool keeps its enchantment.

Enchantments are determined based on the total number of times the player has enchanted any tool. Therefore, reloading the day and changing the order the tools are enchanted will result in getting different enchantments.

## Infinity Weapons

A [Galaxy Soul](/Galaxy_Soul "Galaxy Soul") can be forged into a galaxy weapon at the cost of 20 [Cinder Shards](/Cinder_Shard "Cinder Shard"). Doing this 3 times (at a total cost of 3 Galaxy Souls and 60 cinder shards) will turn the weapon into a more powerful "Infinity" weapon. Adding just 1 or 2 shows that Galaxy Souls have been applied but does not otherwise affect the weapon. The weapons keep their enchantments and gem forgings when they are made into Infinity weapons. They are given the new Infinity weapon appearance even if they were previously forged into a new appearance.

## Weapon Appearance

Two weapons can be merged through the forge. The resulting weapon will have all the stats and info of the first weapon, but the appearance of the second weapon. Only weapons of the same type can be merged. Slingshots cannot be merged. The second weapon will be consumed.

This costs 10 [Cinder Shards](/Cinder_Shard "Cinder Shard").

A Galaxy Weapon that changed its appearance in this way would lose its appearance after being forged into an Infinity Weapon.

## Combined Rings

Two different [rings](/Rings "Rings") can be combined into one with stacked effects. This costs 20 [Cinder Shards](/Cinder_Shard "Cinder Shard").

This has two restrictions:

* Two of the same ring cannot be combined into one. (However, two different rings with the same effect can be combined, like the [Glow Ring](/Glow_Ring "Glow Ring") and [Glowstone Ring](/Glowstone_Ring "Glowstone Ring"). Their effects will stack in the same way as wearing both rings separately.)
* Three or more rings cannot be combined into one. (*i.e.,* a combined ring can't be further combined).

## Unforge

Weapons and combined rings can be unforged to reset its properties.

* For a weapon, unforging removes all forge upgrades (but not enchantments) and resets its appearance.
* For a combined ring, unforge splits it back into the original rings.

Unforging is free, but only returns some of the [Cinder Shards](/Cinder_Shard "Cinder Shard") used to forge the item.

To begin the unforging process, place the item for unforging into the left square slot. Then click the red ⨯ symbol near the bottom right corner. The unforged item(s) will appear in inventory, along with any returned Cinder Shards.

## Fishing

The Forge is the only floor of the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon") where fishing can occur. It is one of two locations in the game where players can catch [Lava Eels](/Lava_Eel "Lava Eel"). [Crab pots](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot") cannot be placed in the forge. There is a 5% chance the player can catch a present that contains the painting ['Physics 101'](/%27Physics_101%27 "'Physics 101'"). This painting can only be caught once per player per game.[[2]](#cite_note-fishingchance-2)
