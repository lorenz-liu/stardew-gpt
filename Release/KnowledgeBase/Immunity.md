# Immunity

**Immunity** [![Immunity.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/d1/Immunity.png/24px-Immunity.png)](/File:Immunity.png) is a statistic that affects your chance to be afflicted with any [debuff](/Buffs "Buffs"). It has no effect on the length of time that you are invulnerable after getting damaged.

Each point of Immunity reduces the probability of being debuffed by 9.1%, additively.[[1]](#cite_note-DebuffingProjectile-1) For example, when wearing the Genie Shoes, which provides 6 points of Immunity, the chance of being debuffed is reduced by 55%. If wearing equipment that provide sufficient total Immunity (+11 or more), the player can stay completely free from any debuffs.
