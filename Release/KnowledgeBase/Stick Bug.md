**Stick Bugs** are an [enemy](/Monsters "Monsters") found in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") after activating the [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") or during the [Danger In The Deep](/Quests#Danger_In_The_Deep "Quests") quest.

## Behavior

They disguise themselves as pieces of [wood](/Wood "Wood"), sometimes surprising the player.

## Strategy

Due to their high HP, [bombs](/Crafting#Bombs "Crafting") may be effective at damaging them.
