**Vincent** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives in the southern part of [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). He's a young boy, and can often be found with his best friend [Jas](/Jas "Jas"). He looks up to his brother [Sam](/Sam "Sam") and wants to be just like him when he grows up.

## Schedule

On Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday, Vincent and [Jas](/Jas "Jas") spend the afternoon with [Penny](/Penny "Penny") being tutored at the [museum](/Museum "Museum"). During [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), Vincent is often at [the beach](/The_Beach "The Beach").

Vincent won't leave [his home](/1_Willow_Lane "1 Willow Lane") on rainy days, unless it's Spring 11, when he visits [Harvey's Clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic").

After the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island is unlocked, Vincent may randomly spend the day there. After leaving the Island at 6pm, Vincent will immediately go home to bed. Vincent never visits the Resort on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, Fridays, [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days or his checkup day at [Harvey's Clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic"). Vincent also never visits the Resort without an adult accompanying him.

## Relationships

Vincent lives with his mother [Jodi](/Jodi "Jodi") and his brother [Sam](/Sam "Sam"). His father [Kent](/Kent "Kent") is away at war in Year 1, but returns and lives with the family when he returns in Year 2.

He's often seen alongside his friend [Jas](/Jas "Jas"), who is the only resident of town close to his age.

[Penny](/Penny "Penny") takes time to teach both Vincent and [Jas](/Jas "Jas") at the [museum](/Museum "Museum") since there's no school nearby.
It is implied that Vincent cares about Penny a lot, specifically in a dialogue where he claims he'll marry her one day.

## Trivia

* Ironically, Vincent hates [Clay](/Clay "Clay") but he may gift it to the player at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star").
* During the spring onion mastery cutscene, Vincent says "sp...spwing onion", suggesting he is unable to say his R's. This is the only time in the game he speaks like this.
* Vincent will still tell the player that he wants to marry [Penny](/Penny "Penny") when he grows up even when the player is married to her.
