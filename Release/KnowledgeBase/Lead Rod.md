The **Lead Rod** is a [club weapon](/Weapons#Club "Weapons") that can be obtained by [breaking boxes and barrels](/The_Mines#Crates_and_Barrels "The Mines"), or as a [special monster drop](/The_Mines#Special_Items "The Mines"), on levels 40-120 in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") and on levels 1-39 in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern").
