**Cheese** is an [Artisan Good](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods") made from the [Cheese Press](/Cheese_Press "Cheese Press"), taking 3.3 hours. Using [Milk](/Milk "Milk") will produce normal quality Cheese, while [Large Milk](/Large_Milk "Large Milk") will produce gold quality Cheese. It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") cat with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals"). The [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") will trade one Cheese for one [Emerald](/Emerald "Emerald") on Fridays.

## Aged Values

Cheese can be placed inside a [Cask](/Cask "Cask") to age from normal quality to silver, gold, and eventually iridium quality. Iridium quality doubles the base sell price of Cheese.

Normal, silver, and gold quality Cheese can be prematurely removed from a cask at any time by striking the cask with an [Axe](/Axes "Axes"), [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes"), or [Pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes").

## Tailoring

Cheese is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt066.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/98/Shirt066.png/24px-Shirt066.png)](/File:Shirt066.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").
