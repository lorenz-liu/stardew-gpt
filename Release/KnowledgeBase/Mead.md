**Mead** is an [Artisan Good](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods") made from placing [Honey](/Honey "Honey") inside a [Keg](/Keg "Keg"), taking 10 hours. It is also available at the [Ginger Island Resort](/Ginger_Island#Island_South "Ginger Island") for data-sort-value="800">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)800g. [Pam](/Pam "Pam") sells Mead in her [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="20">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 20 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg"). Unlike [Wine](/Wine "Wine") and [Juice](/Juice "Juice") however, the value of the Mead is **not** affected by the type of honey used. The sell price of Mead is data-sort-value="300">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)300g, and only [aging](/Cask "Cask") or the [Artisan Profession](/Skills#Farming "Skills") can increase it. Several types of [Flower Honey](/Honey#Sell_Prices "Honey") sell for more than Mead, so the player can actually lose gold by making Mead.

Mead can also be obtained for free from the bar in the [Pirate Cove](/Ginger_Island#Pirate_Cove "Ginger Island") by speaking with the bartender while wearing the [Pirate Hat](/Pirate_Hat "Pirate Hat"), [Deluxe Pirate Hat](/Deluxe_Pirate_Hat "Deluxe Pirate Hat"), [Eye Patch](/Eye_Patch "Eye Patch"), or [Parrot Egg](/Parrot_Egg "Parrot Egg") trinket. This can only be done once per night.

When consumed, Mead causes a [Tipsy](/Buffs "Buffs") buff that decreases [Speed](/Speed "Speed") by 1.

## Aged Values

Mead can be placed inside a [Cask](/Cask "Cask") to age from normal quality to silver, gold, and eventually iridium quality. Iridium quality doubles the base sell price of Mead.

Normal, silver, and gold quality Mead can be prematurely removed from a cask at any time by striking the cask with an [Axe](/Axes "Axes"), [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes"), or [Pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes").

## Bundles

Mead is one of the options for the ![Bundle Orange.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/95/Bundle_Orange.png/24px-Bundle_Orange.png) [Brewer's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Brewer.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Pantry (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Pantry "Remixed Bundles").

## Recipes

Mead is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Mead can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [![Shirt138.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/e4/Shirt138.png/24px-Shirt138.png)](/File:Shirt138.png) [Black Leather Jacket](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Mead is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
