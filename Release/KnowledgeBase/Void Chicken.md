The **Void Chicken** is an [animal](/Animals "Animals") that lives in a [Big Coop](/Coop "Coop"). Void Chickens can be hatched by placing a [Void Egg](/Void_Egg "Void Egg") into an [Incubator](/Incubator "Incubator"), taking ![Time Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Time_Icon.png/24px-Time_Icon.png) 9,000m (5d 16h 40m) to incubate, or ![Time Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Time_Icon.png/24px-Time_Icon.png) 4,500m (2d 21h 40m) with the [Coopmaster Profession](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming"). There is no other way to obtain a Void Chicken.

## Produce

:   *Main article: [Animals#Produce](/Animals#Produce "Animals")*

Void Chickens who eat every day mature after 3 nights have passed. A mature and fed void chicken produces a [Void Egg](/Void_Egg "Void Egg") every day, which can be placed in an [Incubator](/Incubator "Incubator") to hatch another void chicken, or sold for data-sort-value="65">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)65g. The [Void Egg](/Void_Egg "Void Egg") can be used in cooking recipes calling for an egg.

A Void Egg can be placed in the [Mayonnaise Machine](/Mayonnaise_Machine "Mayonnaise Machine") to produce [Void Mayonnaise](/Void_Mayonnaise "Void Mayonnaise"), which sells for data-sort-value="275">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)275g.

## Selling

Void Chickens may be sold the same way as normal [chickens](/Chicken "Chicken"). The more hearts the animal has, the higher its sell price. A new-born chick sells for data-sort-value="252">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)252g, and a maximum-heart Void Chicken for data-sort-value="1040">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,040g, the same as for normal chickens.

To sell a coop or barn animal, right-click the animal after petting. The friendship/mood interface appears. Move the cursor over the gold coin button at the right to see how much you can sell the animal for. Click that button and the confirmation that follows to complete the sale.
