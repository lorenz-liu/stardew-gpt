**Crispy Bass** is a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit").

Crispy Bass may randomly appear in [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus")' shop on [Saturdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Saturday "Days of the Week"), in the [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") outside the saloon, or in [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon")'s rotating stock. [Jodi](/Jodi "Jodi") sells a set of three Crispy Bass in her [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="25">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 25 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg"). One Crispy Bass may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box").

## Bundles

Crispy Bass is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Crispy Bass is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt111.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/1c/Shirt111.png/24px-Shirt111.png)](/File:Shirt111.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").
