**Mayonnaise** is an [Artisan Good](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods") made using the [Mayonnaise Machine](/Mayonnaise_Machine "Mayonnaise Machine"), taking 3 hours.

[Normal sized White and Brown eggs](/Egg "Egg") produce regular quality Mayonnaise. [Large White and Brown eggs](/Large_Egg "Large Egg") produce gold quality Mayonnaise. The quality of the egg is not a factor in determining the quality of the Mayonnaise.

[Ostrich Eggs](/Ostrich_Egg "Ostrich Egg") produce 10 jars of Mayonnaise at once. Unlike chicken eggs, Ostrich Eggs produce Mayonnaise in the same quality as the egg. This is the only way to obtain a silver quality or iridium quality Mayonnaise.

[Golden Eggs](/Golden_Egg "Golden Egg") produce 3 jars of Mayonnaise at gold quality at once, regardless of the quality of the eggs used.

## Tailoring

Mayonnaise is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt005.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/1a/Shirt005.png/24px-Shirt005.png) [Light Blue Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a white dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

One Mayonnaise may be requested by [Blobfish](/Blobfish "Blobfish"), [Catfish](/Catfish "Catfish"), or [Spook Fish](/Spook_Fish "Spook Fish") in [Fish Pond quests](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond").

## Trivia

* Consuming mayonnaise near a villager will cause the villager to comment on it.
