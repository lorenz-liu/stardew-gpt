**Hops** is a [vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from [Hops Starter](/Hops_Starter "Hops Starter") after 11 days.

Hops can be put into a [Keg](/Keg "Keg") to make [Pale Ale](/Pale_Ale "Pale Ale").

## Stages

You cannot walk through Hops at any stage of growth. You can walk through dead Hops plants.

## Tailoring

Hops is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a dyeable ![Shirt159.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/99/Shirt159.png/24px-Shirt159.png) [Shirt and Belt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a green dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the green dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Tips

* Hops give the 2nd best experience for leveling up [Farming](/Farming "Farming") skill in a single season, giving 102 to 126 points, depending on speed growth fertilizers. (Mature [Ancient Fruit](/Ancient_Fruit "Ancient Fruit") gives the most farming XP.)
* Selling [Hops Starter](/Hops_Starter "Hops Starter") made from the [Seed Maker](/Seed_Maker "Seed Maker") can be more profitable than selling the Hops directly, and also potentially generates [Ancient Seeds](/Ancient_Seeds "Ancient Seeds"). Pale Ale and Pickled Hops are more profitable, but the Seed Maker takes only 20 in-game minutes.
