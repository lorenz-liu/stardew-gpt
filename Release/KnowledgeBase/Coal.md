**Coal** is a basic [resource](/Category:Resources "Category:Resources"). It can be obtained primarily from mining rocks, killing [Dust Sprites](/Dust_Sprite "Dust Sprite"), purchasing from the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith"), using a [Charcoal Kiln](/Charcoal_Kiln "Charcoal Kiln"), or [panning](/Pans "Pans"). Coal may also be obtained in a number of other ways.

Coal is used in some [crafting](/Crafting "Crafting") recipes. It is also necessary for smelting items in a [Furnace](/Furnace "Furnace") or [Heavy Furnace](/Heavy_Furnace "Heavy Furnace") and smoking fish in a [Fish Smoker](/Fish_Smoker "Fish Smoker").

## Locations

* Mining rocks — both in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") and anywhere else on the map. The chance to obtain Coal from most rocks is affected by [daily luck](/Luck#Daily_Luck "Luck"), [luck buffs](/Luck#Luck_Buffs "Luck"), [Mining level](/Skills#Mining "Skills"), the [Prospector Profession](/Mining#Mining_Skill "Mining"), and one of the powers from the [Statue Of The Dwarf King](/Statue_Of_The_Dwarf_King "Statue Of The Dwarf King").[[1]](#cite_note-stonefactors-1)
* Mining [Coal Nodes](/Mining#Coal_Node "Mining"), which can be found in the [Quarry](/Quarry "Quarry"), [Quarry Mine](/Quarry_Mine "Quarry Mine"), and [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon").
* Opening coal-filled minecarts and brown miner's bags on certain floors of the Mines and random floors in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"). Each minecart or bag contains 6 Coal. Minecarts in the Mines refresh their contents each year, while bags do not.
* Breaking [crates and barrels](/The_Mines#Crates_and_Barrels "The Mines") in the Mines, Quarry Mine, Skull Cavern, and Volcano Dungeon.
* Using [Charcoal Kilns](/Charcoal_Kiln "Charcoal Kiln") to convert 10 [Wood](/Wood "Wood") into 1 Coal.
* Recycling [Trash](/Trash_(item) "Trash (item)") (30% chance) or [Driftwood](/Driftwood "Driftwood") (25% chance) in a [Recycling Machine](/Recycling_Machine "Recycling Machine").
* Killing [Dust Sprites](/Dust_Sprite "Dust Sprite") between Floors 41-79 of the Mines (50% chance of dropping Coal).
  + Players may use the elevator to quickly and repeatedly travel between floors ending in 0 or 5 to spawn more Dust Sprites.
  + Using [Monster Musk](/Monster_Musk "Monster Musk") and wearing a [Burglar's Ring](/Burglar%27s_Ring "Burglar's Ring") may be helpful as well.
* Killing Red, Purple, and Black [Slimes](/Slimes "Slimes"), [Stone Golems](/Stone_Golem "Stone Golem"), [Wilderness Golems](/Wilderness_Golem "Wilderness Golem"), [Iridium Golems](/Iridium_Golem "Iridium Golem"), [Metal Heads](/Metal_Head "Metal Head"), [Shadow Brutes](/Shadow_Brute "Shadow Brute"), and [Shadow Shamans](/Shadow_Shaman "Shadow Shaman"). Each monster has a 10% chance to drop Coal.
* Digging up [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") in the [Backwoods](/Backwoods "Backwoods"), [the Mountains](/The_Mountain "The Mountain"), or [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm").
* [Panning](/Pans "Pans") in any area.
* Opening regular or golden [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing").
* Opening any of the 4 types of [geodes](/Minerals#Geodes "Minerals").
* The [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith") sells Coal for data-sort-value="150">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150g during Year 1 or data-sort-value="250">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)250g from Year 2 onwards.
* The [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") trades 20 Coal for 1 [Mining Monthly](/Mining_Monthly "Mining Monthly").
* The [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") may sell Coal for data-sort-value="15"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100–1,000g.
* Up to 50 Coal may be purchased from [Clint](/Clint "Clint")'s [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="5">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 5 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg") each.
* The [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") outside of the Blacksmith may contain Coal.
* If a [Fruit Tree](/Fruit_Trees "Fruit Trees") is struck by lightning, it will produce Coal instead of fruit for 4 days before returning to normal.
* [Trains](/Railroad#Trains "Railroad") may randomly drop Coal.

Coal purchased at the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith") costs data-sort-value="150">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150g during Year 1, so it is cheaper in Year 1 to buy 10 wood for data-sort-value="100">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100g from [Robin](/Robin "Robin") at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop") and convert that wood into coal in the [Charcoal Kiln](/Charcoal_Kiln "Charcoal Kiln"). After Year 1, wood pricing increases to data-sort-value="50">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)50g per piece but coal increases to only data-sort-value="250">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)250g, so it is cheaper to purchase coal directly.

## Bundles

Coal is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Coal is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt048.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/1f/Shirt048.png/24px-Shirt048.png) [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a black [dye](/Dyeing "Dyeing") when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

* Ten Coal may be requested by [Ice Pip](/Ice_Pip "Ice Pip") or [Stonefish](/Stonefish "Stonefish") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 5 to 7.
* Ten Coal are requested by [Midnight Carp](/Midnight_Carp "Midnight Carp") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 3 to 5.

## Trading

* 20 Coal can be traded at the [Raccoon Wife's Shop](/Giant_Stump#Raccoon_Wife.27s_Shop "Giant Stump") for 1 [Mahogany Seed](/Mahogany_Seed "Mahogany Seed").
* 999 Coal can be traded at the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for the [Dark Piano](/Dark_Piano "Dark Piano").

* [1.6.6](/Version_History#1.6.6 "Version History"): Added Coal for [Mahogany Seed](/Mahogany_Seed "Mahogany Seed") trade at the Giant Stump.
