An **Oak Tree** is a common [Tree](/Trees "Trees") that grows from an [Acorn](/Acorn "Acorn"). It yields [Oak Resin](/Oak_Resin "Oak Resin") every 7 days when [tapped](/Tapper "Tapper") (3 days with a [Heavy Tapper](/Heavy_Tapper "Heavy Tapper")). It can be chopped down with an axe, producing [Wood](/Wood "Wood"), [Sap](/Sap "Sap"), possible Acorns, and possible [Hardwood](/Hardwood "Hardwood") (if the player is a [Lumberjack](/Foraging#Foraging_Skill "Foraging")).

A planted Acorn grows through four stages before reaching maturity (with twice as much time spent at stage 4). If [fertilized](/Tree_Fertilizer "Tree Fertilizer"), this takes five days (even in [Winter](/Winter "Winter")). Otherwise, there is a 20% chance per day of growth, except in winter when trees do not grow. The median time to maturity is 24 days, although individual tree growth times will vary considerably.

Chopping a stage 2–3 tree with an [axe](/Axes "Axes") has a chance to drop one [wood](/Wood "Wood"), depending on foraging level. This doesn't happen when cutting it with other [tools](/Tools "Tools").

Oak Trees outside the farm, but not in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"), can also be tapped or chopped down. These trees will respawn as Stage 3 saplings (20% chance each day) and grow as normal after that, as long as the stump is removed, and there is nothing on the tree's tile.

Oak trees that are not in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") or the [Greenhouse](/Greenhouse "Greenhouse") may turn into [Green Rain Trees](/Green_Rain_Trees "Green Rain Trees") in [Fall](/Fall "Fall") and will not return to normal until the next [Spring](/Spring "Spring"). If a tapper is placed on the tree, this change will not occur. [[1]](#cite_note-leafshedding-1).

## History

* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Fixed bug that caused a stage 2-3 sapling to make a scythe sound when watered. Fixed bug that prevented trees from dropping seeds and seeds disappearing when harvested if a Foraging level up occured during the day. Planting tree seeds outside the farm no longer requires the spot to be tilled. (This also prevents planting in non-tillable areas dug up by Artifact Spots in Winter).
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Oak trees now have a chance to turn into [Green Rain Trees](/Green_Rain_Trees "Green Rain Trees") and lose their leaves in the fall.
