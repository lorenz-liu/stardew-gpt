**Blue Jazz** is a [flower](/Flowers "Flowers") that can be [grown](/Crops "Crops") from [Jazz Seeds](/Jazz_Seeds "Jazz Seeds") in 7 days.

If placed within proper range of a [Bee House](/Bee_House#Range "Bee House") (5 squares in any cardinal direction), Blue Jazz will cause [Blue Jazz Honey](/Honey "Honey") to be produced, increasing the honey base value from data-sort-value="100">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100g to data-sort-value="200">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)200g.

Blue Jazz can be purchased from the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="50"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150–1,000g.

## Tailoring

Blue Jazz can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Skirt](/Tailoring#Pants "Tailoring"). [![Skirt.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/fa/Skirt.png/36px-Skirt.png)](/File:Skirt.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a blue dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Blue Jazz is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Notes

* Blue Jazz has an equal chance of being one of six different colors. Blue Jazz of different colors will occupy different slots in inventory or chests. Six colors × four [quality](/Crops#Crop_Quality "Crops") levels = 24 possible spaces used.
