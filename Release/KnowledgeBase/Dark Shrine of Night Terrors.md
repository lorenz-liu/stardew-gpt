# Dark Shrine of Night Terrors

[![Dark Shrine of Night Terrors.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Dark_Shrine_of_Night_Terrors.png/120px-Dark_Shrine_of_Night_Terrors.png)](/File:Dark_Shrine_of_Night_Terrors.png)

The **Dark Shrine of Night Terrors** is an altar found in the [Witch's Hut](/Witch%27s_Hut "Witch's Hut"). It can be accessed after completing the [Goblin Problem](/Quests#Goblin_Problem "Quests") quest. [The player](/The_Player "The Player") can offer a [Strange Bun](/Strange_Bun "Strange Bun") to toggle [monsters](/Monsters "Monsters") spawning on [the farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"). This is available for all [farm maps](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps"), including [Wilderness](/Farm_Maps#Wilderness "Farm Maps").
