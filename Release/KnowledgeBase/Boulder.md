**Boulders** are oversized rocks, occupying 4 tiles, found on [The Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"), in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") and [Quarry Mine](/Quarry_Mine "Quarry Mine"). They are one of the main sources of [stone](/Stone "Stone") in the game. However, no [Mining](/Mining "Mining") Experience points are gained by destroying boulders.

## Boulders on the Farm

Multiple boulders are present on the farm at the start of the game, frequently blocking access to bridges or other features. Removing them requires an upgraded [Pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes"), steel quality or higher, yielding 15 [Stones](/Stone "Stone").

The original farm boulders will never reappear once removed. However, rare [meteorites](/Random_Events#Meteorite "Random Events") may occasionally crash into the farm.

## Boulders in the Mines

Boulders in the mine can be broken using any quality [Pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes"), yielding 10 [Stones](/Stone "Stone").
