The **Mayor's Manor** is the home of [Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis"). It is located east of the [Graveyard](/Graveyard "Graveyard") and south of [Pam](/Pam "Pam") and [Penny](/Penny "Penny")'s [Trailer](/Trailer "Trailer"), next to the bridge leading to the [Museum](/Museum "Museum") and [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith"). Lewis can often be found here either standing at his stove or tending to his garden at the front of the house.

## Interior

The interior of Lewis' home is simple, with an open concept kitchen that connects to his bedroom. Players must gain two hearts [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") before being able to enter [Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis")' room.

On the left hand side of the house are four objects to interact with, offering several services to [the Player](/The_Player "The Player"). From left to right the following can be done:

### Prize Machine

:   *For a list of prizes, see [Prize Ticket](/Prize_Ticket "Prize Ticket").*

A prize machine, located next to the Town Ledger, rewards various prizes in exchange for [Prize Tickets](/Prize_Ticket "Prize Ticket"). Prize Tickets are earned by completing [Quests](/Quests "Quests"), [Special Orders](/Quests#List_of_Special_Orders "Quests"), the [Stardew Valley Fair](/Stardew_Valley_Fair#Shop "Stardew Valley Fair") shop, and through repeated [Egg Festival](/Egg_Festival "Egg Festival") and [Festival of Ice](/Festival_of_Ice "Festival of Ice") wins. When using the prize machine, the player can see how many Prize Tickets they currently have as well as the next three prizes to be dispensed. Claiming a prize gives the player the first prize in line and advances the remaining prizes forward by one space.

### Multiplayer Funds

Next to the prize machine is a "Town Ledger" that can be used to change between shared and separate funds in a [Multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer") game. This change happens overnight.

### Divorce

Another book is next to the "Town Ledger", it gives players the option to [divorce](/Marriage#Divorce "Marriage") their spouses for data-sort-value="50000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)50,000g. This book can also be used to evict a housemate (for free).

### Lost and Found

The Lost & Found Box can be used to retrieve items donated to failed [special orders](/Quests#List_of_Special_Orders "Quests"), lost quest items and tools, items from offline players, hats from children that have been turned into doves, and items left behind in the [Stardew Valley Fair](/Stardew_Valley_Fair "Stardew Valley Fair") grange display.
