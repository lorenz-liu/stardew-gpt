# Axes

The **Axe** is a [tool](/Tools "Tools") used to chop branches, [trees](/Trees "Trees") and small stumps, as well as for removing or harvesting certain objects, especially important for obtaining [Wood](/Wood "Wood"). An axe may also be used as a [weapon](/Weapons "Weapons") to inflict a small amount of damage. An axe is required to harvest [Giant Crops](/Crops#Giant_Crops "Crops").

The basic Axe is obtained at the beginning of the game. It can be upgraded at the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith"). The cost and resource requirements for each upgrade are shown in the table below.

When upgraded, the axe can be used to chop down [large stumps](/Large_Stump "Large Stump") and eventually [large logs](/Large_Log "Large Log") that grant [Hardwood](/Hardwood "Hardwood"). The Steel Axe is required to remove the large log that blocks the entrance to the [Secret Woods](/Secret_Woods "Secret Woods").

On the Riverlands, Forest, Hill-top and Beach [Farm Maps](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps"), some bushes can be destroyed with a Copper Axe (or better).[[1]](#cite_note-isdestroyable-1)
