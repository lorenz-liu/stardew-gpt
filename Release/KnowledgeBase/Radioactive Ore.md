**Radioactive Ore** is a [resource](/Category:Resources "Category:Resources") found in [Radioactive Nodes](/Mining#Radioactive_Node "Mining").

## Locations

* [Radioactive Nodes](/Mining#Radioactive_Node "Mining") in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") during [Danger In The Deep](/Quests#Danger_In_The_Deep "Quests") quest or after activating the [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") on floor 120 to increase the difficulty.
* Radioactive Nodes in [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") during [Skull Cavern Invasion](/Quests#Skull_Cavern_Invasion "Quests") quest or after activating the statue at the Cavern's entrance.

The chance of finding Radioactive Nodes in either location increases as one goes deeper into the mine, and also with daily [luck](/Luck "Luck") and luck [buffs](/Buffs "Buffs"). It is most strongly affected by Daily Luck, and only slightly affected by depth in the mine and Luck Buffs.[[1]](#cite_note-probability-1)

## Smelting

Radioactive Ore can be smelted in a [Furnace](/Furnace "Furnace") to create a [Radioactive Bar](/Radioactive_Bar "Radioactive Bar"). The smelting requires 5 Radioactive Ore and 1 [Coal](/Coal "Coal") and takes 9 hours and 20 minutes in-game time.

## Tailoring

Radioactive Ore is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Turtleneck Sweater](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt178.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/89/Shirt178.png/24px-Shirt178.png)](/File:Shirt178.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a green dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Radioactive Ore is not used in any quests.

## Trivia

Radioactive Ore is currently the only ore in the game that cannot be used as ammunition in a [Slingshot](/Weapons#Slingshot "Weapons").
