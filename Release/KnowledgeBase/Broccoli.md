## Bundles

Broccoli may be chosen as one of the options for the ![Bundle Orange.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/95/Bundle_Orange.png/24px-Bundle_Orange.png) [remixed Fall Crops Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Fall_Crops_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Pantry](/Remixed_Bundles#Pantry "Remixed Bundles").

## Recipes

Broccoli is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Broccoli can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create the dyeable ![Shirt140.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/f9/Shirt140.png/24px-Shirt140.png) [Button Down Shirt](/Tailoring#Shirts "Tailoring"). It can also be used as a green [dye](/Dyeing "Dyeing") when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the green dye pot located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Broccoli is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
