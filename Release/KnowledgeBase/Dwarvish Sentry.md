The **Dwarvish Sentry** is an [enemy](/Monsters "Monsters") found in the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon"). It has a 19% chance to spawn from a broken [metal crate](/Volcano_Dungeon#Metal_Crates "Volcano Dungeon").[[1]](#cite_note-cratedrop-1)

## Behavior

They move extremely slowly and are immune to [knockback](/Weight "Weight").
