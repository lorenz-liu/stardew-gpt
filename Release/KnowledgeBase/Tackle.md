# Tackle

**Tackle** can be attached to the [Iridium Rod](/Iridium_Rod "Iridium Rod") or [Advanced Iridium Rod](/Advanced_Iridium_Rod "Advanced Iridium Rod") to make it easier to catch [fish](/Fish "Fish"). Most recipes unlock as the player's [fishing level](/Fishing#Fishing_Skill "Fishing") increases. Most tackle can also be purchased from the [Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop") once they are unlocked. Tackles have a durability bar, which decreases on use. Tackle wears out and disappear after 20 nibbles by fish (indicated by "HIT" popping up on screen). Tackle does not wear out when catching non-fish items (such as Algae, Jelly, [Seaweed](/Seaweed "Seaweed"), [Trash](/Trash "Trash"), and special items).

If the player uses a fishing rod with the [Preserving enchantment](/Forge#Tool_enchantments "Forge"), for each fish nibble there is a 50% chance for tackle to not be consumed. If the player uses an [Advanced Iridium Rod](/Advanced_Iridium_Rod "Advanced Iridium Rod") with two tackles equipped, these chances are rolled separately, so it is possible for the durability of only one of the tackles to be consumed.

Only the [Iridium Rod](/Iridium_Rod "Iridium Rod") or the [Advanced Iridium Rod](/Advanced_Iridium_Rod "Advanced Iridium Rod") can equip tackle. The Iridium Rod can only attach one tackle at a time, while the Advanced Iridium Rod can attach two. To attach tackle, left-click on the tackle, then right-click on the rod. To remove tackle, first right-click to remove any bait, then right-click to remove the tackle. For an Advanced Iridium Rod, the left tackle will be the first one removed.

Tackle does not stack in [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory") or [chests](/Chest "Chest"); each tackle takes up one inventory slot.

## Stacking

When using the [Advanced Iridium Rod](/Advanced_Iridium_Rod "Advanced Iridium Rod") with two tackles, the tackle effects will always stack if the tackles are different. If the tackles are the same, the tackle effects may stack depending on the tackle. Below is a list of the effects when two of the same tackle is equipped:

* 2 [Spinners](/Spinner "Spinner"): Reduces maximum delay before a nibble by 7.5 seconds, double the amount of time as with 1 Spinner.
* 2 [Dressed Spinners](/Dressed_Spinner "Dressed Spinner"): Reduces maximum delay before a nibble by 15 seconds, double the amount of time as with 1 Dressed Spinner.
* 2 [Trap Bobbers](/Trap_Bobber "Trap Bobber"): The progress bar at the right decreases 50% slower when the fish isn't within the fishing bar, compared to 33% with 1 Trap Bobber.
* 2 [Cork Bobbers](/Cork_Bobber "Cork Bobber"): Increase the bar size by 48 pixels, double the amount as with 1 Cork Bobber.
* 2 [Lead Bobbers](/Lead_Bobber "Lead Bobber"): Prevents the bar from bouncing even more compared to 1 Lead Bobber.
* 2 [Treasure Hunters](/Treasure_Hunter "Treasure Hunter"): Along with also preventing the fish from escaping when collecting treasures, treasure chance is increased by 10%, double the chance as with 1 Treasure Hunter.
* 2 [Barbed Hooks](/Barbed_Hook "Barbed Hook"): Makes the fishing bar cling to the catch even more compared to 1 Barbed Hook.
* 2 [Curiosity Lures](/Curiosity_Lure "Curiosity Lure"): Provides no additional benefit compared to using 1 Curiosity Lure.
* 2 [Quality Bobbers](/Quality_Bobber "Quality Bobber"): Increases the quality of the fish caught by 2 levels, double the amount of levels as with 1 Quality Bobber.
* 2 [Sonar Bobbers](/Sonar_Bobber "Sonar Bobber"): Provides no additional benefit compared to using 1 Sonar Bobber.

## Profitability

Unlike most items obtained by [crafting](/Crafting "Crafting") of metals and wood in the game, tackle can be sold (both to [Willy](/Willy "Willy") and via the [Shipping Bin](/Shipping "Shipping")). Several of these crafting recipes are actually profitable, depending on whether or not the player has the [Blacksmith profession](/Mining#Mining_Skill "Mining"):

## History

* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Tackles can now be attached to [fishing rods](/Tools#Fishing_Poles "Tools") directly from [treasure chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing"). Reduced sellback price for used tackle, so it can no longer be sold to [Willy](/Willy "Willy") for full price. Fixed bug where tackle durability could be restored by right-clicking the tackle in [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory").
* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): Tackle no longer loses endurance when catching junk. Using a Fishing rod with the [Preserving enchantment](/Forge#Tool_enchantments "Forge") now gives a 50% chance for tackle to not be consumed. Added [Curiosity Lure](/Curiosity_Lure "Curiosity Lure") and [Quality Bobber](/Quality_Bobber "Quality Bobber").
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Added [Sonar Bobber](/Sonar_Bobber "Sonar Bobber"). [Advanced Iridium Rod](/Advanced_Iridium_Rod "Advanced Iridium Rod") introduced which allows two tackles to be equipped at once. Added secret.
