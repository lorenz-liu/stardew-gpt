# Combat

:   *For technical details about combat mechanics, see [Combat/Mechanics](/Combat/Mechanics "Combat/Mechanics").*

**Combat** is the [skill](/Skills "Skills") associated with fighting [monsters](/Monsters "Monsters"), such as in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") or the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"). Combat experience is increased by killing monsters, with more difficult monsters granting more experience. It can also be gained by reading the [Combat Quarterly](/Combat_Quarterly "Combat Quarterly") or [Book Of Stars](/Book_Of_Stars "Book Of Stars").

Each increase in Combat Level adds 5 HP ([health](/Health "Health") points) to the player's health meter, with the exceptions of levels 5 and 10. Health points are only increased when levelling up to levels 5 or 10 if the Fighter and Defender professions are chosen. Level-ups may also add [Crafting](/Crafting "Crafting") or [Cooking](/Cooking "Cooking") recipes. Unlike other skills, there is no [proficiency](/Skills#Proficiency "Skills") effect from this skill since [weapons](/Weapons "Weapons") do not require energy.

During the first few days in Stardew Valley, [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") are closed due to an accident by [Joja Corporation](/Setting#Joja_Corporation "Setting"). On or around day 5, the player will receive a letter in the mail from Joja Corp, explaining that the landslide blocking the mines has been cleared, and the mine entrance will be open. At this point, combat becomes available to players. An exception is those who chose the [Wilderness Farm](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps"), which starts with monsters at the first night.

As with other skills, experience level is increased immediately upon slaying a monster, but the "level up" window doesn't appear until the player sleeps (or until all players sleep in [multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer")).

**Note that killing monsters on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") grants only 1/3 of the standard amount of experience.**[[1]](#cite_note-farmcombatxp-1)

## Substats

Consuming certain [foods](/Cooking "Cooking") or drinks, wearing particular [Footwear](/Footwear "Footwear") or [Rings](/Rings "Rings"), and choice of [Weapon](/Weapons "Weapons") can all give Stat Bonuses that affect the player's Combat ability.

![Attack.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/00/Attack.png/24px-Attack.png) [Attack](/Attack "Attack") affects how much damage the player does with each [weapon](/Weapons "Weapons") strike.

![Crit. Power.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/06/Crit._Power.png/24px-Crit._Power.png) [Crit. Power](/Crit._Power "Crit. Power") adds additional damage when hitting a critical strike.

![Defense.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/66/Defense.png/24px-Defense.png) [Defense](/Defense "Defense") affects how much damage the player takes from enemy strikes.

![Immunity.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/d1/Immunity.png/24px-Immunity.png) [Immunity](/Immunity "Immunity") affects the chance to be afflicted with certain [debuffs](/Buffs "Buffs").

The ![Weight.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/6f/Weight.png/24px-Weight.png) [Weight](/Weight "Weight") of a weapon affects how far an enemy will be knocked back when struck with the weapon.

![Speed w.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/26/Speed_w.png/24px-Speed_w.png) Weapon [Speed](/Speed "Speed") and ![Speed.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/94/Speed.png/24px-Speed.png) Player [Speed](/Speed "Speed") are also a factor in successful Combat.
