**Refined Quartz** is created by smelting 1 [Quartz](/Quartz "Quartz") in a furnace with 1 coal for fuel. 1 [Fire Quartz](/Fire_Quartz "Fire Quartz") can also be placed in a furnace with 1 coal to produce 3 Refined Quartz. It can also be obtained in a [variety of ways](#Locations).

It can be sold for data-sort-value="50">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)50g. Until obtaining the [Crystalarium](/Crystalarium "Crystalarium") it is a lot faster and easier to obtain Refined Quartz via the [Recycling Machine](/Recycling_Machine "Recycling Machine") than from [quartz](/Quartz "Quartz") itself.

## Locations

* Can very rarely be found in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") by breaking large crystals.
* Can be created by smelting 1 [Quartz](/Quartz "Quartz") in a [furnace](/Furnace "Furnace") with 1 [Coal](/Coal "Coal"). Smelting takes 1.5 in-game hours.
* Can be created by smelting 1 [Fire Quartz](/Fire_Quartz "Fire Quartz") in a [furnace](/Furnace "Furnace") with 1 [Coal](/Coal "Coal"), which produces 3 Refined Quartz. Smelting takes 1.5 in-game hours.
* Can be created by [recycling](/Recycling_Machine "Recycling Machine") a [Broken CD](/Broken_CD "Broken CD") or [Broken Glasses](/Broken_Glasses "Broken Glasses").
* Can occasionally be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") after receiving the [Furnace](/Furnace "Furnace") blueprints from [Clint](/Clint "Clint").
* Can be a rare drop from [Ghosts](/Ghost "Ghost") in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"). (Floors 51-79)
* Can be dropped by [Carbon Ghosts](/Carbon_Ghost "Carbon Ghost") in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"). (10% chance)
* Can be produced by [Ghostfish](/Ghostfish "Ghostfish") [Fish Ponds](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") when the population reaches 9.

## Bundles

5 Refined Quartz are used in the ![Bundle Purple.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a1/Bundle_Purple.png/24px-Bundle_Purple.png) [Engineer's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Engineer.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Boiler Room (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Boiler_Room "Remixed Bundles").

## Tailoring

Refined Quartz is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [![Shirt220.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/5/55/Shirt220.png/24px-Shirt220.png)](/File:Shirt220.png) [Necklace Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a purple dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* Refined Quartz may be randomly requested during any season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="150">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points. Refined Quartz will never be requested until you have the [Furnace](/Furnace "Furnace") blueprints.
* Two, three, five, or ten Refined Quartz may be requested by several fish in [Fish Pond quests](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond").
