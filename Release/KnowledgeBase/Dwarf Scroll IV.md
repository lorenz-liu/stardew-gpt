The **Dwarf Scroll IV** is an [Artifact](/Artifacts "Artifacts") that can be found as a drop from any [monster](/Monsters "Monsters") (other than the [Wilderness Golem](/Wilderness_Golem "Wilderness Golem"), [Serpent](/Serpent "Serpent"), [Skeleton](/Skeleton "Skeleton"), [Carbon Ghost](/Carbon_Ghost "Carbon Ghost"), [Iridium Bat](/Bats "Bats"), [Iridium Crab](/Iridium_Crab "Iridium Crab"), and [Haunted Skull](/Haunted_Skull "Haunted Skull")), or by using the [hoe](/Hoes "Hoes") on dirt in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") (floor 80+).

## Donation

Donation of this item contributes to the total count of donations for the [Museum](/Museum "Museum").

Donating all 4 Dwarf Scrolls rewards the player with the [Dwarvish Translation Guide](/Dwarvish_Translation_Guide "Dwarvish Translation Guide").

## Tailoring

Dwarf Scroll IV can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Gold Trimmed Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt222.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/ff/Shirt222.png/24px-Shirt222.png)](/File:Shirt222.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Tip

* Level 95 in the Mines has tillable soil near the entrance to the floor. The player can repeatedly revisit Floor 95 via the elevator to till the soil and farm for Dwarf Scrolls I and IV.
