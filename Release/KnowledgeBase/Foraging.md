# Foraging

**Foraging** is the [skill](/Skills "Skills") associated with gathering wild resources found on the ground throughout Stardew Valley, and with chopping down [Trees](/Trees "Trees"). Foraging skill also increases by harvesting crops grown from [Wild Seeds](/Crops#Wild_Seeds "Crops").

Most forageables can be found throughout their season. One exception is [Salmonberry](/Salmonberry "Salmonberry"), which can be found only during Salmonberry Season ([Spring](/Spring "Spring") 15-18) on bushes around town. Blackberries can be found on the ground during [Fall](/Fall "Fall"), but during Blackberry Season ([Fall](/Fall "Fall") 8-11) they can also be found on bushes throughout town.

## Quality

The quality of foraged goods comes in four levels: regular, silver, gold, and iridium. However, only players who have chosen the Botanist Profession can find iridium-quality foraged goods. Higher quality goods command higher prices when the goods are sold, and deliver greater [energy](/Energy "Energy") and [health](/Health "Health") benefits when they are consumed. If given as a gift to an NPC who likes or loves it, a quality gift also provides a higher number of [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.

The ![Botanist.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/86/Botanist.png/24px-Botanist.png) [Botanist profession](/Foraging#Foraging_Skill "Foraging") applies iridium quality to:

* All items labeled as "Forage" in-game (except [Sap](/Sap "Sap") and [Cave Carrot](/Cave_Carrot "Cave Carrot"), and buried items)
* [Fruits](/Fruits "Fruits"), [vegetables](/Vegetables "Vegetables"), and [flowers](/Flowers "Flowers") found as forage (including [Salmonberries](/Salmonberry "Salmonberry") and [Blackberries](/Blackberry "Blackberry") shaken from bushes during their respective seasons)
* [Coconuts](/Coconut "Coconut") shaken from [Palm Trees](/Palm_Tree "Palm Tree")
* Fruit left by fruit bats in [the cave](/The_Cave "The Cave"), provided the player chose that option when prompted by [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius") (mushrooms are not affected)
* All items foraged from [the beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") except for [Seaweed](/Seaweed "Seaweed")
* Crops grown from [Wild Seeds](/Crops#Wild_Seeds "Crops")
* [Truffles](/Truffle "Truffle")

The Botanist profession does **not** apply iridium quality in these cases:

* Mushrooms that are not picked up from the ground. Notably, this includes mushrooms gained from [tapping](/Tapper "Tapper") a [Mushroom Tree](/Mushroom_Tree "Mushroom Tree") or from [the Farm Cave](/The_Cave "The Cave").
* Forage items that are dug up with a hoe, such as the [Snow Yam](/Snow_Yam "Snow Yam") and the [Winter Root](/Winter_Root "Winter Root") (in the wild).
* [Seaweed](/Seaweed "Seaweed").

### Quality Ratios

For players without the Botanist Profession, the game first tests for gold quality according to the following formula: `foraging level/30`. If the item fails this check, then the game tests for silver quality, according to the following formula: `foraging level/15 and not gold`, or `(1-foraging level/30)*foraging level/15`. If both checks fail, the foraged item will be of normal quality.

### Tracker Profession

The ![Tracker.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/c5/Tracker.png/24px-Tracker.png) Tracker Profession makes spawned forageables in the current area easier to find. Small yellow arrows are added at the edge or corner of the screen that point towards each off-screen forageable (including inaccessible ones) and [artifact spot](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot"). A similar small green arrow points towards any off-screen shimmering lights for [panning](/Pans "Pans"). The tracker does not indicate berry bush locations.

## Experience Points

Experience points (XP) are granted as follows:

* **14 XP** for chopping down a [tree](/Trees "Trees") with an axe (granted all at once for the last chop that causes the tree to fall).
* **2 XP** for removing the tree stump after chopping down a tree.
* **1 XP** for each [Twig](/The_Farm#Debris "The Farm") chopped down.
* **1 XP** for each piece of [Moss](/Moss "Moss") harvested from trees.
* **1 XP** per berry for shaking [Blackberries](/Blackberry "Blackberry") and [Salmonberries](/Salmonberry "Salmonberry") off bushes in their respective seasons.
* **25 XP** for removing [Large Stumps](/Large_Stump "Large Stump") and [Large Logs](/Large_Log "Large Log").
* **7 XP** for each foraged item picked up from the ground.
  + If the Gatherer profession doubles the foraged item, another **7 XP** is granted for the extra item.
* **7 XP** for each [Ginger](/Ginger "Ginger") harvested.
* **3 XP** for each [Spring Onion](/Spring_Onion "Spring Onion") harvested from [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") in [Spring](/Spring "Spring").
* **2 XP** for items grown from [Wild Seeds](/Crafting#Seeds "Crafting") (also 3 [Farming](/Farming "Farming") XP).
  + If the Gatherer profession doubles the foraged item, another **2 XP** is granted for the extra item.
* **7 XP** for each item panned from [panning](/Pans "Pans"). For instance, if the player obtained 3 items from panning, they will receive 21 XP.
* **5 XP** for each mushroom collected from [the Farm Cave](/The_Cave "The Cave") if the mushroom option was selected, and from [Mushroom Logs](/Mushroom_Log "Mushroom Log").
* **7 XP** for each fruit collected from [the Farm Cave](/The_Cave "The Cave") if the fruit bats option was selected.
* **15 XP** for digging up a [Seed Spot](/Seed_Spot "Seed Spot") or [Artifact Spot](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot").
* **15 XP** for destroying a large [Green Rain](/Weather#Green_Rain "Weather") [weed](/Weeds "Weeds"). [[1]](#cite_note-greenrainweed-1)
* **250 XP** for reading a copy of the [Woodcutter's Weekly](/Woodcutter%27s_Weekly "Woodcutter's Weekly") or [Book Of Stars](/Book_Of_Stars "Book Of Stars").

The 7 XP for foraging items applies solely to those items visible sitting on the ground that are picked up only if the player clicks on them; it does not apply to items lying on the ground (*e.g.,* dropped by shaking) that are automatically added to the player inventory by [magnetism](/Magnetism "Magnetism"). Specific items that do grant 7 XP include:

* Standard randomly-spawned foraged items throughout Stardew Valley and Calico Desert.
* All items foraged from [the Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") and [the west side of Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island#Island_West "Ginger Island").
* All fruit collected from [the Farm Cave](/The_Cave "The Cave"), if the fruit bat option was selected.
* All mushrooms collected from the [Ginger Island Mushroom Cave](/Ginger_Island#Dig_Site "Ginger Island").
* [Truffles](/Truffle "Truffle").
* Forage items spawned on the ground in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") and [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), namely [Red Mushrooms](/Red_Mushroom "Red Mushroom"), [Purple Mushrooms](/Purple_Mushroom "Purple Mushroom"), and [Fiddlehead Ferns](/Fiddlehead_Fern "Fiddlehead Fern").

**The following Items do NOT grant any foraging experience:**

* Trees or [twigs](/The_Farm#Debris "The Farm") felled by [Bombs](/Crafting#Bombs "Crafting") (however, weakening a tree with bombs before felling it with an axe will still give full XP).
* Stage 4 [tree seedlings](/The_Farm#Debris "The Farm") chopped down by the player
* [Grapes](/Grape "Grape") grown from [Grape Starters](/Grape_Starter "Grape Starter") (grants [Farming](/Farming "Farming") XP instead).
* Items grown in [Garden Pots](/Garden_Pot "Garden Pot").
* Items dug up by tilling soil in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") or [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") (*e.g.,* [Cave Carrot](/Cave_Carrot "Cave Carrot"))
* Mushrooms obtained from chopping or tapping a [Mushroom Tree](/Mushroom_Tree "Mushroom Tree").
* Non-forage items spawned on the ground in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") and [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), namely [Quartz](/Quartz "Quartz"), [Earth Crystals](/Earth_Crystal "Earth Crystal"), [Frozen Tears](/Frozen_Tear "Frozen Tear"), [Fire Quartz](/Fire_Quartz "Fire Quartz"), and [Dinosaur Eggs](/Dinosaur_Egg "Dinosaur Egg") -- except that a [bug](#Bug) causes any duplicates created by the Gatherer profession to give 7 XP.
* Forageables obtained from killing [Monsters](/Monsters "Monsters").

## Spawning

New forageable items can potentially spawn (appear) overnight on any exterior map.[[2]](#cite_note-spawning-2) This section describes the rules used to spawn all typical forageable items. However, specialized items are handled differently and are described on their individual pages, namely [Spring Onions](/Spring_Onion "Spring Onion") in [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest"); [Coral](/Coral "Coral"), [Sea Urchins](/Sea_Urchin "Sea Urchin") and [Seaweed](/Seaweed "Seaweed") at [the Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach"); fruit in [the Farm Cave](/The_Cave "The Cave"); all forageables on the [Forest Farm Map](/Farm_Maps#Forest "Farm Maps"). Also, [Salmonberry](/Salmonberry "Salmonberry") bushes, [Blackberry](/Blackberry "Blackberry") bushes, [Winter Roots](/Winter_Root "Winter Root"), and [Snow Yams](/Snow_Yam "Snow Yam") don't spawn individual items.

There is a hard limit of six standard forageable items per map (*i.e.,* areas bounded by screen transitions) per week, reset on Sundays. Picking forageables does not reduce the weekly counter, so there is little-to-no advantage in picking forageables before Saturday (the day before the reset).

Only a subset of all the tiles on each map are valid spawning locations. On most maps, forageable items can spawn only on the green (grassy-type) tiles, and only on certain specific types of green tiles. [The Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") and [the Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") are exceptions, where spawning is possible on most yellow (sandy-type) tiles. Items never spawn in occupied locations (trees, chests, weeds, sticks, stones, etc.). They also never spawn in hidden tiles behind buildings, bushes, non-removable trees, or other permanent features. They can spawn behind removable trees (ones that can be chopped down; they also turn semi-transparent when the player walks behind them), but the spawn rate is 90% smaller in about half of the tiles behind a tree.

Therefore, the fraction of valid tiles differs for every map, which directly affects the chance of items spawning on each map. It is also affected by the player's actions. Removing debris (weeds, etc.) and chopping down trees can increase the number of valid tiles, whereas placing equipment (chests, etc.) may decrease the number. Planting trees can also have an effect: even though (on most maps) trees can be planted only in tiles where forage items can't spawn, they reduce the spawn chance in some tiles behind the tree (and also make it hard to find items that do spawn). However, these effects are generally small, unless a large fraction of the map tiles is occupied. All spawn rates are calculated assuming the default trees are in place and fully grown, but that all other tiles are cleared.

All uncollected forageable items are removed from the map overnight before Sunday morning and before the first day of a season.[[3]](#cite_note-removal-3) This cleanup applies both to standard forageable items and nearly all specialized items (fruit in [the Farm Cave](/The_Cave "The Cave") is the only known exception). Even non-season-specific items are removed at the start of a new season. To help repopulate the map, the chances of new items spawning are slightly higher on these days.[[4]](#cite_note-extra_spawn-4)

Forageable items will be destroyed if they are in the path of a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers").

## Bugs

* The Gatherer profession can cause some non-forage items to be doubled. When this happens, the player gains 7 XP for the duplicate item, even though the original item granted no experience. The items affected by this bug are items sitting on the ground in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), namely [Quartz](/Quartz "Quartz"), [Earth Crystal](/Earth_Crystal "Earth Crystal"), [Fire Quartz](/Fire_Quartz "Fire Quartz"), [Frozen Tear](/Frozen_Tear "Frozen Tear"), and [Dinosaur Egg](/Dinosaur_Egg "Dinosaur Egg").
  + Other items found on the ground in the Mines, namely [Red Mushroom](/Red_Mushroom "Red Mushroom"), [Purple Mushroom](/Purple_Mushroom "Purple Mushroom"), and [Fiddlehead Fern](/Fiddlehead_Fern "Fiddlehead Fern"), are considered by the game to be forage items, so they always grant foraging experience, and any Gatherer duplication is not a bug.

## References

1. [↑](#cite_ref-greenrainweed_1-0) See `TerrainFeatures.ResourceClump::destroy` in the game code. Large green rain weeds are indices 44 and 46 in the sprite sheet `TileSheets/Objects_2.xnb`.
2. [↑](#cite_ref-spawning_2-0) Spawning of standard forage items is done in `GameLocation::spawnObjects`, which is triggered by `GameLocation::dayUpdate`. The six item per map limit is controlled by `numberOfSpawnedObjectsOnMap`. The number of attempts to add an item is a random number ranging from 1 to `min(4, 6-numberOfSpawnedObjectsOnMap)`. For each attempt, the code checks up to 11 locations that are randomly located anywhere on the map. An item is only placed if one of those 11 locations is a valid spawning location, and if a randomly-selected forage item also passes its probability test.
3. [↑](#cite_ref-removal_3-0) End-of-week removal of forageables is part of `GameLocation::dayUpdate`. End-of-season removal is part of `gameLocation::seasonUpdate`. All items with the `IsSpawnedObject` flag are removed, and `numberOfSpawnedObjectsOnMap` is set to zero.
4. [↑](#cite_ref-extra_spawn_4-0) Extra calls are made to `GameLocation::spawnObjects` after clearing existing items (one extra call on the first of a season; two extra on Sunday morning). However, these extra calls frequently generate no extra items, because the same random-number seed (based on game ID and days played) is used every time. So the second and third calls retest the same list of locations and items as the first call.
5. [↑](#cite_ref-spawnpercent_5-0) The foraged items that can spawn in a given location each season are determined by `GameLocation::spawnObjects` in the game code, using input from the data file `Content\Locations.xnb`. The percentages provided here have been normalized to ensure that the percentages sum to 100% for each season in each location. The data was normalized by:
   1. Calculating the sum of all percentages listed in `Locations.xnb` for a season
   2. Dividing each percentage by the sum

## History

* [1.3.27](/Version_History#1.3.27 "Version History"): Breaking a regular tree stump now gives +1 Foraging experience.
* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Fixed bug where Foraging skill level up would cause trees to stop dropping seeds and seeds on the ground to disappear instead of being harvested until the player slept and saved the game. Changed sound when picking up forageables to a higher-pitched sound. Forester profession changed from "Wood worth 50% more" to "Trees drop 25% more wood."
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Botanist now affects items obtained from shaking fruit trees. Shaking berry bushes now grants 1 XP per berry. Foraged items grown from [wild seeds](/Crafting#Seeds "Crafting") now give just 2 XP instead of 7, but also give some farming XP. Mushrooms from the Farm Cave now give 5 forage experience each. Survival Burger recipe now learned at Level 8, up from Level 2. Charcoal Kiln recipe now learned at Level 2 instead of Level 4. Cookout Kit now learned at Level 3 instead of Level 9. Tapper now learned at Level 4 instead of Level 3. Positive energy Forage can be [pickled](/Jellies_and_Pickles "Jellies and Pickles"). Positive energy Forage (except Mushrooms) can be [juiced](/Juice "Juice"). Foraged fruits and positive energy Mushrooms can be [dried](/Dehydrator "Dehydrator"). Foraged [Seaweed](/Seaweed "Seaweed") now always normal quality. Foraging Experience can now be gained by reading the [Woodcutter's Weekly](/Woodcutter%27s_Weekly "Woodcutter's Weekly") or [Book Of Stars](/Book_Of_Stars "Book Of Stars").
* [1.6.3](/Version_History#1.6.3 "Version History"): Harvesting moss grants 1 [Foraging XP](/Foraging#Experience_Points "Foraging") per moss collected.
* [1.6.12](/Version_History#1.6.12 "Version History"): Digging up [Seed Spots](/Seed_Spot "Seed Spot") and [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot"), [panning](/Pans "Pans"), harvesting [ginger](/Ginger "Ginger"), and chopping [twigs](/The_Farm#Debris "The Farm") now grant foraging experience. Chopping trees now grants 14 XP instead of 12 and chopping stumps now grants 2 XP instead of 1.
