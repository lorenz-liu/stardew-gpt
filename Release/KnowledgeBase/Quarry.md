# Quarry

![Maplocation.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/5/5c/Maplocation.png/20px-Maplocation.png)

![Map.png](/mediawiki/images/4/43/Map.png)

:   *This article is about the [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") quarry. For [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island"), see [Ginger Island#Dig Site](/Ginger_Island#Dig_Site "Ginger Island").*

The **Quarry** consists of a large area with a wide selection of rocks and [mining nodes](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining") that are randomly generated. Each day there is a chance that new rocks and nodes as well as [oak and maple tree](/Trees "Trees") seeds will be generated. It is part of the same map as [the Mountain](/The_Mountain "The Mountain"), therefore it has the same [forageable](/Foraging#Foraged_Items "Foraging") items and [Artifact Spot](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") contents as the Mountain.

The Quarry is unlocked by completing the [Crafts Room bundles](/Bundles#Crafts_Room "Bundles") in the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") or by purchasing "Bridge" from the [Joja Community Development Form](/Joja_Community_Development_Form "Joja Community Development Form") for data-sort-value="25000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)25,000g.

The Quarry itself can be home to every ore and gem in the game. However, stones containing valuable items like [Iridium Ore](/Iridium_Ore "Iridium Ore") or [Prismatic Shards](/Prismatic_Shard "Prismatic Shard") are much rarer and the player may have to wait several seasons for new ones to spawn.

The Entrance to the [Quarry Mine](/Quarry_Mine "Quarry Mine") is on the western side of the quarry, above the bridge. The [Minecart](/Minecart "Minecart") can also take the player to the Quarry once the bridge is repaired. (The Minecart is unlocked by either completing the [Boiler Room bundles](/Bundles#Boiler_Room "Bundles") or by purchasing "Minecarts" from the [Joja Community Development Form](/Joja_Community_Development_Form "Joja Community Development Form") at [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart") for data-sort-value="15000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)15,000g.)

## Usable Space

[![Quarry tiles.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/29/Quarry_tiles.png/300px-Quarry_tiles.png)](/File:Quarry_tiles.png)

This image shows the usable space in the Quarry. Colored tiles (yellow or blue) can be used to place [paths](/Crafting#Decor "Crafting"), [artisan equipment](/Crafting#Artisan_Equipment "Crafting") and [refining equipment](/Crafting#Refining_Equipment "Crafting"). Yellow tiles (tillables) may also be used to plant [common trees](/Trees "Trees").

[Villagers](/Villagers "Villagers") do not travel through the Quarry or on its bridge, so items placed there are safe from destruction.
