The **Parsnip** is a [vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from [Parsnip Seeds](/Parsnip_Seeds "Parsnip Seeds") after 4 days.

The [Statue Of Endless Fortune](/Statue_Of_Endless_Fortune "Statue Of Endless Fortune") produces one Parsnip per year on [Pam](/Pam "Pam")'s birthday, [Spring 18](/Spring "Spring").

## Tailoring

Parsnip is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a dyeable ![Shirt159.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/99/Shirt159.png/24px-Shirt159.png) [Shirt And Belt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a yellow dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the yellow dye pot at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

* Cultivate and harvest a Parsnip for the ["Getting Started" Quest](/Quests#Getting_Started "Quests").
