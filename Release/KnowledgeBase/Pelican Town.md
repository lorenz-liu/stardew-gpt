# Pelican Town

![Pelican Town.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/25/Pelican_Town.png/600px-Pelican_Town.png)

**Pelican Town** is where the player moves to at the start of the game, located in Stardew Valley. Pelican Town is where most of the [villagers](/Villagers "Villagers") live, work, and socialize.

[Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis") is the mayor of Pelican Town and has been for over 20 years; he comments that he has not had competition for the role since first becoming mayor.

The northwest entrance to town connects to the [Bus Stop](/Bus_Stop "Bus Stop") and [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"). The southwest passage connects to [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest"), which contains [Marnie's Ranch](/Marnie%27s_Ranch "Marnie's Ranch"), [Leah's Cottage](/Leah%27s_Cottage "Leah's Cottage"), and the [Wizard's Tower](/Wizard%27s_Tower "Wizard's Tower"). Directly south of town is [the Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach"). North of town connects to [the Mountain](/The_Mountain "The Mountain"), with [Robin's Carpenter Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop"), [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild"), the [Railroad](/Railroad "Railroad"), and the [Quarry](/Quarry "Quarry").

Initially, there are [weeds](/Weeds "Weeds") located near [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"), in the area behind [1 Willow Lane](/1_Willow_Lane "1 Willow Lane"), behind [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart"), and to the west of the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center"), below the playground. These weeds will die during [Winter](/Winter "Winter"), but will respawn at the start of each new year.

When attempting to cut most trees in Pelican Town, a message will show saying "I shouldn't cut down trees in town."

## Special Orders Board

Pelican Town has a [special orders board](/Quests#List_of_Special_Orders "Quests") that allows you to perform quests for the various townspeople.

## Trash Bear

On the first non-rainy day in Year 3, Trash Bear will appear in the southern part of [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest"). Bringing 4 requested items to Trash Bear will trigger a cutscene in which Trash Bear cleans up the trash in Cindersap Forest, and the trash in the [Trailer](/Trailer "Trailer")'s front yard. [Dusty's dog pen](/Dog_Pen "Dog Pen") also gets an upgrade.

## Fishing

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/9d/TownDistances.png/400px-TownDistances.png)](/File:TownDistances.png)

[Distances from land](/Fishing#Distance_from_Land "Fishing") in Pelican Town.

River [fish](/Fish "Fish") can be caught in the river flowing through Pelican Town. In Fall, a [Legendary Fish](/Fish#Legendary_Fish "Fish"), the [Angler](/Angler "Angler"), can be caught at the north end of the river. [Ms. Angler](/Ms._Angler "Ms. Angler"), one of the [legendary fish II](/Fish#Legendary_Fish_II "Fish"), can also be caught there.

When fishing in the fountain to the left of the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center"), a present box containing a [Decorative Trash Can](/Decorative_Trash_Can "Decorative Trash Can") (10% chance) can be found. Otherwise, a single piece of [wood](/Wood "Wood") or [stone](/Stone "Stone") will be caught, with equal probability (45% chance each).[[5]](#cite_note-dectrashchance-5)

## Secrets

* If a [Super Cucumber](/Super_Cucumber "Super Cucumber") is placed in the hidden brown box located in the fenced area north of the Blacksmith, the player will receive the [secret](/Secrets "Secrets") [??HMTGF??](/%3F%3FHMTGF%3F%3F "??HMTGF??") statue.
* One [Prize Ticket](/Prize_Ticket "Prize Ticket") can be found in a hidden gift box to the northeast of the Bookseller, in a small fenced area blocked by a [large stump](/Large_Stump "Large Stump").
* [The Alleyway Buffet](/The_Alleyway_Buffet "The Alleyway Buffet") can be obtained from a hidden golden garbage can in the easternmost region of town, by entering a concealed opening in the eastern fence just north of the [Minecart](/Minecart "Minecart") track outside of the Blacksmith. The path is blocked by a [large log](/Large_Log "Large Log") and [boulder](/Boulder "Boulder").

## Trivia

* Pelican Town is located within the Ferngill Republic, at war with the [Gotoro Empire](/Gotoro_Empire "Gotoro Empire") across the Gem Sea.[[6]](#cite_note-catwitter-6)
* Dr. Harvey, in calling out on his shortwave radio, identifies his location (in Pelican Town) as [52 north, 43.5 east](https://www.google.com/maps/place/52%C2%B000'00.0%22N+43%C2%B030'00.0%22E/@53.0836475,33.8659055,5.75z/data=!4m5!3m4!1s0x0:0x0!8m2!3d52!4d43.5). Thus, if Stardew Valley is on Earth, it can be found near a village called Krasnoye (one of many such) in Russia - about 160 km (100 mi) WNW of Saratov, and about 600 km (375 mi) SE of Moscow. "Krasnoye" means "beautiful" or "red". However, it is not on a sea shore.
* The player cannot refill their [watering can](/Watering_Cans "Watering Cans") from the fountain.
