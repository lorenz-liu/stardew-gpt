# Clara Mullner

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/5/51/Alex%27s_Mum.png/200px-Alex%27s_Mum.png)](/File:Alex%27s_Mum.png)

Clara cradles baby [Alex](/Alex "Alex").

**Clara** is [Alex](/Alex "Alex")'s deceased mother. During Alex's [eight heart event](/Alex#Eight_Hearts "Alex"), he states that she died 12 years ago, sometime in the [summer](/Summer "Summer"). When she died, Alex had to move in with his grandparents, [George](/George "George") and [Evelyn](/Evelyn "Evelyn"), who were Clara's parents.

During Alex's memory of her, Clara is seen holding Alex as a baby and singing to him. She has brown hair, with two blue hair bands, and is wearing a purple shirt.

In the memory, Alex is wrapped in a blue blanket with the letter "A" sewn on it and has a green pacifier in his mouth.

Her relationship with Alex's father is assumed to have been strained since Alex describes him as being either absent or drunk.

Clara's surname is unknown; it may or may not be "Mullner". The name "Mullner" is used in only four places in the game:

1. In [Penny](/Penny "Penny")'s Two Heart event, Penny speaks to [George](/George "George"), calling him "Mr. Mullner".
2. [Secret Note #5](/Secret_Notes#Secret_Note_.235 "Secret Notes") refers to George as "Mr. Mullner", and to Evelyn as Granny Mullner.
3. [Secret Note #12](/Secret_Notes#Secret_Note_.2312 "Secret Notes") refers to George and Evelyn's home as "The Mullners'".
4. After the player marries [Alex](/Alex "Alex"), and talks to [Pam](/Pam "Pam"), she will say "So you married the Mullner boy, huh?"
