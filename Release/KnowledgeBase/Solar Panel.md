The **Solar Panel** is a piece of [Refining Equipment](/Crafting#Refining_Equipment "Crafting") that generates 1 [Battery Pack](/Battery_Pack "Battery Pack") the morning after 7 sunny days when placed outdoors (including the day it was placed).[[1]](#cite_note-solar-1) The player receives the recipe after completing [Caroline](/Caroline "Caroline")'s [Special Order](/Quests#List_of_Special_Orders "Quests") ["Island Ingredients"](/Quests#Island_Ingredients "Quests").

## Trivia

* The solar panel counts sunny days based on the location it is placed. Thus, a solar panel placed down on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") will not be affected by rain in Stardew Valley, and vice versa.
  + Arguably the best location to place solar panels is [the Calico Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") due to the desert having its own [weather](/Weather "Weather") (always sunny).
