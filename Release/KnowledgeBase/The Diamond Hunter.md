**The Diamond Hunter** is a power [book](/Books "Books") that can be traded from the [Volcano Dwarf](/Volcano_Dungeon#Shop "Volcano Dungeon") in the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon") for 10 [Diamonds](/Diamond "Diamond").

The first reading grants the player a power that makes all stones have a 0.66% chance to drop a diamond when mined with a [pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes"), with a 50% chance to drop double if the player has the [Geologist](/Skills#Mining "Skills") profession.[[1]](#cite_note-bookfunction-1) These drops are not affected by [Mining Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave"). Once read, it can be found in [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s menu on the [Special Items & Powers](/Special_Items_%26_Powers "Special Items & Powers") tab. Each subsequent reading gives the player 100 [Mining](/Mining "Mining") [XP](/Skills "Skills").

## Bundles

The Diamond Hunter is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

The Diamond Hunter is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a blue dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a cyan dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.
