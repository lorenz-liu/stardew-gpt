This page provides **fishing strategy** information for [Stardew Valley](/Stardew_Valley "Stardew Valley").

## Basics of Strategy

The availability of many types of fish can depend on various factors: the location of the fishing spot, the season, the time of day, and the weather. Match the fishing location to current conditions as necessary to get a chance to catch certain type(s) of fish.

### Locations

Fishing spots are commonly broken up into ocean, lake, and river types. However, while most fishing spots in a similar body of water will share catches, the map also plays a role. For instance, the river in [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") is not the same as the river in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). Ponds usually have a smaller selection of fish, if any, compared to larger bodies of water and the selection is usually specific to the map where the pond is found. Note that generally, the less fish there are in a certain location, the more likely it is that [trash](/Trash "Trash") will be caught.

**The Sewers**

[The Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers") are an easy and productive fishing location, especially to level up the fishing skill or farm treasure. [Carp](/Carp "Carp") is the least difficult fish to catch in the game and it is the only fish in the sewers (except for a [legendary fish](/Mutant_Carp "Mutant Carp")). Therefore, almost every cast of the line results in either an immediate item reward or a quick Carp catch.

### Bobber Position

In general, it is optimal to cast the bobber [as far from land as possible](/Fishing#Distance_from_Land "Fishing"). Longer casting distances are important, but also casting east/west instead of north/south can increase the distance, as can fishing in wide-open water. In addition to affecting the quality of the fish, distance from land also affects the chances of catching certain fish over other ones. While all non-legendary fish can be caught 1 tile from land, a lot of the more difficult and profitable fish have a higher chance to be caught further from land.

[Bubbles](/Fishing#Bubbles "Fishing") greatly increase the rate at which fish bite and can also alter the relative chances of each fish type at the location. Careful placement of the bobber on the edge of the bubbles allows the bubble bonus to be used in any of the adjacent tiles, providing some ability to increase the distance from land.

### Fishing Skill

Fishing level is important because each increase in the fishing level increases the fishing bar size by 8 pixels. The longer the fishing bar is, the easier it is to catch fish. The fishing bar will continue to increase by this amount even as the player goes beyond level 10 (with [buffs](/Buffs "Buffs")). This means the bar can get much bigger than it naturally would be even at fishing level 10. Using a fishing rod with the [Master enchant](/Forge#Tool_enchantments "Forge") will also increase the fishing bar size by 8 pixels. Each [Cork Bobber](/Cork_Bobber "Cork Bobber") used will increase the fishing bar size by 24 pixels. Using [Deluxe Bait](/Deluxe_Bait "Deluxe Bait") increases the fishing bar size by 12 pixels.

### Catching Difficult Fish

This concept of the fishing bar getting bigger is especially useful when trying to catch extremely hard fish such as the [Legendary Fish](/Fish#Legendary_Fish "Fish") and the [Octopus](/Octopus "Octopus"). Reaching Fishing level 10, using an iridium rod with a [Cork Bobber](/Cork_Bobber "Cork Bobber") and with the Master enchant, and eating food (better prepared with [Qi Seasoning](/Qi_Seasoning "Qi Seasoning")) that provides a temporary fishing [buff](/Buffs "Buffs") (**e.g.,** [Seafoam Pudding](/Seafoam_Pudding "Seafoam Pudding")) will all help make catching these difficult fish much more manageable.

The [Trap Bobber](/Trap_Bobber "Trap Bobber") is also a great combination with a large fishing bar, as it significantly reduces the speed at which difficult fish escape. The [Blessing Of Waters](/Statue_Of_Blessings#Blessing_Of_Waters "Statue Of Blessings") can also be used to make catching the fish easier, as it decreases the difficulty of legendary fish by 25% and non-legendary fish (except [Carp](/Carp "Carp")) by 50% and causes the progress bar to decrease 50% slower when the fish isn't being reeled in.
