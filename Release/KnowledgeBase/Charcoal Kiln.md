The **Charcoal Kiln** is a piece of [equipment](/Crafting#Refining_Equipment "Crafting") used to burn [Wood](/Wood "Wood") into [Coal](/Coal "Coal"). Coal is used in several [Crafting](/Crafting "Crafting") recipes, and to smelt different metals into their respective bars.

It takes ![Time Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Time_Icon.png/24px-Time_Icon.png) 30m[[1]](#cite_note-processtime-1) (≈23 seconds [real-time](/Day_Cycle "Day Cycle")) to change the Wood into Coal. For every 10 Wood, you will get 1 Coal.

It can also be obtained by completing the ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Construction Bundle](/Bundles#Construction_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Crafts Room](/Bundles#Crafts_Room "Bundles") (198 [wood](/Wood "Wood"), 99 [stone](/Stone "Stone"), 10 [hardwood](/Hardwood "Hardwood")), the ![Bundle Green.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/b3/Bundle_Green.png/24px-Bundle_Green.png) [Forest Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Forest_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Crafts Room (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Crafts_Room "Remixed Bundles"), or the ![Bundle Yellow.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2b/Bundle_Yellow.png/24px-Bundle_Yellow.png) [Sticky Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Sticky_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Crafts Room (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Crafts_Room "Remixed Bundles").

## History

* [1.07](/Version_History#1.07 "Version History"): Changed requirement for 1 [coal](/Coal "Coal") from 20 [wood](/Wood "Wood") to 10 wood.
* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Changed recipe from 1 [Gold Bar](/Gold_Bar "Gold Bar") to 2 [Copper Bars](/Copper_Bar "Copper Bar"). Charcoal Kilns can now be stacked in a player's [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory") and in [chests](/Chest "Chest").
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Recipe is now learned at [Foraging](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") Level 2 instead of Level 4.
