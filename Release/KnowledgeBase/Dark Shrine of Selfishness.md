# Dark Shrine of Selfishness

[![Dark Shrine of Selfishness.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/95/Dark_Shrine_of_Selfishness.png/120px-Dark_Shrine_of_Selfishness.png)](/File:Dark_Shrine_of_Selfishness.png)

The **Dark Shrine of Selfishness** is an altar found in the [Witch's Hut](/Witch%27s_Hut "Witch's Hut"). It can be accessed after completing the [Goblin Problem](/Quests#Goblin_Problem "Quests") quest. The player can offer a [Prismatic Shard](/Prismatic_Shard "Prismatic Shard") to turn their children into doves, which permanently removes them from the game.

Note: Killing this doll counts toward the [Monster Eradication goal](/Adventurer%27s_Guild#Monster_Eradication_Goals "Adventurer's Guild") of killing 200 bats, and [Clint](/Clint "Clint")'s ["Cave Patrol" Special Order](/Quests#Cave_Patrol "Quests") of killing bats as an option. |
