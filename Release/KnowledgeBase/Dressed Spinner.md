The **Dressed Spinner** is a [tackle](/Tackle "Tackle") that helps [fishing](/Fishing "Fishing") by reducing maximum delay before a nibble by 7.5 seconds (15 seconds if the player is using an [Advanced Iridium Rod](/Advanced_Iridium_Rod "Advanced Iridium Rod") with 2 Dressed Spinners). It can be [crafted](/Crafting#Fishing "Crafting") or purchased from [Willy's Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop") for data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g after reaching [Fishing level 8](/Fishing#Fishing_Skill "Fishing"). It can also be found in regular [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") (4% chance) after reaching Fishing level 6 and [Golden Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Golden_Treasure_Chests "Fishing") (9% chance).[[1]](#cite_note-chestchance-1) It may also randomly appear at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="500"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,500–2,500g or obtained as a gift from [Willy](/Willy "Willy") at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"). It can also be found in [Mystery Boxes](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") and [Golden Mystery Boxes](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box") after reaching Fishing level 6.

One Dressed Spinner is the reward for completing the ![Bundle Green.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/b3/Bundle_Green.png/24px-Bundle_Green.png) [Lake Fish Bundle](/Bundles#Lake_Fish_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Fish Tank](/Bundles#Fish_Tank "Bundles"). One Dressed Spinner is also one of the rewards the player gets the first time they win the fishing competition at the [Festival of Ice](/Festival_of_Ice "Festival of Ice").

Only the [Iridium Rod](/Iridium_Rod "Iridium Rod") or the [Advanced Iridium Rod](/Advanced_Iridium_Rod "Advanced Iridium Rod") can equip tackle. The Iridium Rod can only attach one tackle at a time, while the Advanced Iridium Rod can attach two. To attach tackle, left-click on the tackle, then right-click on the rod. To remove tackle, first right-click to remove any bait, then right-click to remove the tackle. For an Advanced Iridium Rod, the left tackle will be the first one removed.

Tackle does not stack in [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory") or [chests](/Chest "Chest"); each tackle takes up one inventory slot.
