The **Horse Flute** is an item that warps a player's [horse](/Animals#Horse "Animals") to them. As a horse is required for this item, the player must have a [Stable](/Stable "Stable").

It's purchasable from [Mr. Qi](/Mr._Qi "Mr. Qi")'s shop in [Qi's Walnut Room](/Qi%27s_Walnut_Room "Qi's Walnut Room"), for 50 [Qi Gems](/Qi_Gem "Qi Gem").

It only works outdoors while no other players are currently riding the horse (if in [Multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer")) and can be used to summon the horse to [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") and to [the Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert").

The Horse Flute will not work until the horse is named, even if the stable is already built.

The Horse Flute has three sounds, and one is picked randomly each time it is played.
