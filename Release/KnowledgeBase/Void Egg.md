The **Void Egg** is an [animal product](/Category:Animal_Products "Category:Animal Products") obtained from a [Void Chicken](/Void_Chicken "Void Chicken").

Initially, a Void Egg can be obtained through a [random event](/Random_Events#The_Witch "Random Events") involving a witch. The random event can occur as long as [the player](/The_Player "The Player") owns a [Big or Deluxe Coop](/Coop "Coop") that is not full. The witch will fly over the coop during the night and leave a Void Egg inside.

A Void Egg can also be purchased from [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") for data-sort-value="5000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)5,000g. If the player is married to [Shane](/Shane "Shane"), there is a chance he may present them with one as a gift on rainy days. A [Void Salmon](/Void_Salmon "Void Salmon") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") may produce a Void Egg when the population of the pond reaches 9.

A Void Egg sells for a base of data-sort-value="65">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)65g. This is data-sort-value="15">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)15g more than a Small [Egg](/Egg "Egg"), but data-sort-value="30">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)30g less than a [Large Egg](/Large_Egg "Large Egg").

A Void Egg can hatch a [Void Chicken](/Void_Chicken "Void Chicken") by placing it in a Big or Deluxe Coop's [incubator](/Incubator "Incubator").

## Tailoring

Void Egg can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a ![Shirt083.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Shirt083.png/24px-Shirt083.png) [Shirt](/Tailoring#Shirt "Tailoring"). It can be used as a black [dye](/Dyeing "Dyeing") when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

* One Void Egg may be requested by [Void Salmon](/Void_Salmon "Void Salmon") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 5 to 7.
* ["Gus' Famous Omelet"](/Quests#Gus.27_Famous_Omelet "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), [Gus](/Gus "Gus") may randomly request that the player collect and deliver 24 Eggs (of any type) to the fridge in [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon"). The reward is data-sort-value="3000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)3,000g and a [Mini-Fridge](/Mini-Fridge "Mini-Fridge").
* Five Eggs (any type, excluding Dinosaur) may be requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his third request, or as part of any request starting with the sixth.
