**Leah** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives in a [small cottage](/Leah%27s_Cottage "Leah's Cottage") outside [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). She's one of the twelve characters available to [marry](/Marriage "Marriage").

She spends each morning sculpting inside her cottage. Her home opens at 10am and closes at 6pm, but during [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), or [Fall](/Fall "Fall"), players must first acquire two hearts of [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") before being allowed inside. Players at any friendship level may enter during [Winter](/Winter "Winter") between 10am and 6pm.

## Schedule

After the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island is unlocked, Leah may randomly spend the day there. After leaving the Island at 6pm, Leah will immediately go home to bed. Leah never visits the Resort on [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days or her checkup day at [Harvey's Clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic").

## Relationships

Leah lives alone on the outskirts of [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). She thinks she doesn't have many friends in town. She moved from the city to Stardew Valley to pursue her dream of being an artist. She has an ex-partner, Kel, who will be mentioned in several Heart Events.

She is friends with [Elliott](/Elliott "Elliott"), and will dance with him at the [Flower Dance](/Flower_Dance "Flower Dance") if neither of them are dancing with the player.

## Marriage

:   *Main article: [Marriage](/Marriage "Marriage")*

Once married, Leah will move into the [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse"). Like other marriage candidates, she will add her own room to the right of the bedroom. She'll also set up a small area behind the farmhouse where she'll sometimes go to work on a sculpture.

On rainy mornings, Leah may offer you a gift she found while "mushroom-hunting": [Chanterelle](/Chanterelle "Chanterelle"), [Common Mushroom](/Common_Mushroom "Common Mushroom"), [Red Mushroom](/Red_Mushroom "Red Mushroom"), or [Morel](/Morel "Morel"). On mornings when Leah stays indoors all day, she may offer you [Coffee](/Coffee "Coffee") or a gift grown in "a little pot out back": [Wild Horseradish](/Wild_Horseradish "Wild Horseradish"), [Daffodil](/Daffodil "Daffodil"), [Leek](/Leek "Leek"), [Dandelion](/Dandelion "Dandelion"), [Cactus Fruit](/Cactus_Fruit "Cactus Fruit"), [Fiddlehead Fern](/Fiddlehead_Fern "Fiddlehead Fern"), [Spice Berry](/Spice_Berry "Spice Berry"), or [Sweet Pea](/Sweet_Pea "Sweet Pea"). She may also offer you a [Salad](/Salad "Salad") on days when she stays indoors all day. On New Years' Eve (Winter 28), she'll give you a bottle of [Wine](/Wine "Wine").
