**Miner's Treat** is a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit").

Miner's Treat can be purchased any day from the [Dwarf](/Dwarf "Dwarf") for data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g or randomly from [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus")' shop on [Saturdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Saturday "Days of the Week"). [Mummies](/Mummy "Mummy") in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") have a 4% chance of dropping a Miner's Treat. 5 Miner's Treat may occasionally be found in a [treasure room](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern") in the Skull Cavern. One Miner's Treat is a reward for submitting an [Egg Rating](/Desert_Festival#Skull_Cavern "Desert Festival") of 1-4 to [Gil](/Gil "Gil") during the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival").

## Bundles

Miner's Treat is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Miner's Treat is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Propeller Hat.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/96/Propeller_Hat.png/24px-Propeller_Hat.png) [Propeller Hat](/Propeller_Hat "Propeller Hat"). It can also serve as a red dye color at the dye pots at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Miner's Treat is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
