The **Staircase** is a [craftable](/Crafting "Crafting") item used to instantly create a ladder that leads down one floor in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") or [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"). It cannot be placed on floors 0 or 120 of the mines, in the [Quarry Mine](/Quarry_Mine "Quarry Mine"), or in the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon").

Staircases work just like the game-supplied down-ladders. Each one works only for one use. Leaving the mine or returning to a higher floor will result in the staircase being permanently gone. In addition, once a staircase has been placed, it cannot be picked up again, even if it has not been used.

Stone can be purchased from [Robin](/Robin "Robin") at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop") if the player does not have enough. In year 1, the cost is 99 Stones × data-sort-value="20">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)20g = data-sort-value="1980">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,980g per staircase. In year 2+, the cost is 99 Stones × data-sort-value="100">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100g = data-sort-value="9900">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)9,900g per staircase. The [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") will trade staircases for one [Jade](/Jade "Jade") each on Sundays. [Kent](/Kent "Kent") sells Staircases in his [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="30">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 30 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg").

## Tips

* Staircases can be used to escape difficult enemies or descend quickly in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") or [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern").
* Using [Jade](/Jade "Jade") from [Crystalariums](/Crystalarium "Crystalarium") for staircases at the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") on Sundays and putting them in [deconstructors](/Deconstructor "Deconstructor") can net the player massive amounts of [stone](/Stone "Stone") quickly.
* Using a staircase in [the Mayor's bedroom](/Mayor%27s_Manor#Interior "Mayor's Manor") will trigger a [secret event](/Secrets#Lucky_Purple_Shorts "Secrets").
