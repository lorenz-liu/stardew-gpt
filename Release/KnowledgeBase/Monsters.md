# Monsters

**Monsters** are enemies found in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), the [Quarry Mine](/Quarry_Mine "Quarry Mine"), the [Mutant Bug Lair](/Mutant_Bug_Lair "Mutant Bug Lair"), the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon"), the [Secret Woods](/Secret_Woods "Secret Woods"), and, optionally, on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"). Monsters can attack [the Player](/The_Player "The Player") and damage their [health](/Health "Health"). When killed, monsters have a chance of dropping [Monster Loot](/Monster_Loot "Monster Loot") and other items. Killing monsters increases the player's [combat](/Combat "Combat") skill.

## Monsters on the Farm

Monsters can appear at night on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") based on the "Spawn monsters on the farm" [advanced option](/Options "Options") (enabled by default for the Wilderness Farm). It can also be toggled in-game by offering a [Strange Bun](/Strange_Bun "Strange Bun") to the [Dark Shrine of Night Terrors](/Dark_Shrine_of_Night_Terrors "Dark Shrine of Night Terrors") in the [Witch's Hut](/Witch%27s_Hut "Witch's Hut").

Monsters on the farm scale with the player's [combat](/Combat "Combat") level, with higher levels spawning more difficult monsters and [Wilderness Golems](/Wilderness_Golem "Wilderness Golem"). Monsters killed on the farm grant [combat XP](/Skills "Skills") (though only 1/3 of the standard amount of XP), and count towards [monster eradication goals](/Adventurer%27s_Guild#Monster_Eradication_Goals "Adventurer's Guild"). However, they don't count towards quest and special order goals.

Slimes killed in a [Slime Hutch](/Slime_Hutch "Slime Hutch") don't provide XP or count towards eradication goals.

Regardless of whether monster spawning is enabled, each [Truffle](/Truffle "Truffle") dug up by a [Pig](/Pig "Pig") has a 0.2% chance of turning into a [Truffle Crab](/Truffle_Crab "Truffle Crab").

## Dangerous Monsters

Monsters in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") and the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") are more dangerous during the [Danger in the Deep](/Quests#Danger_In_The_Deep "Quests") and [Skull Cavern Invasion](/Quests#Skull_Cavern_Invasion "Quests") quests, or while the [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") is toggled on.

Dangerous monsters have unique sprites and stats. With few exceptions, their health, damage, and sometimes defense are higher, and their speed identical, when compared to their non-dangerous counterparts. Killing dangerous monsters earns the player the same amount of [combat](/Combat "Combat") experience points as most of their non-dangerous counterparts.

The [Putrid Ghost](/Putrid_Ghost "Putrid Ghost"), [Skeleton Mage](/Skeleton_Mage "Skeleton Mage"), and [Shadow Sniper](/Shadow_Sniper "Shadow Sniper") feature unique [debuffs](/Buffs#Available_Buffs "Buffs").
