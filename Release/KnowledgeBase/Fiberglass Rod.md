The **Fiberglass Rod** is a [tool](/Tools "Tools") used to catch [fish](/Fish "Fish"). It can be purchased at [Willy's Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop") for data-sort-value="1800">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,800g upon reaching level 2 [Fishing](/Fishing "Fishing"). It can have [bait](/Bait "Bait") but not [tackle](/Tackle "Tackle") attached to it.
