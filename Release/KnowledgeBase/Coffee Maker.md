The **Coffee Maker** is a piece of equipment that automatically brews a single cup of [Coffee](/Coffee "Coffee") in the morning. It is received in the mail after completing [Evelyn](/Evelyn "Evelyn")'s ["Gifts for George"](/Quests#Gifts_for_George "Quests") Special Order.

If the original is lost, a replacement can be purchased at the [Lost Items Shop](/Secret_Woods#Lost_Items_Shop "Secret Woods") in the Secret Woods for data-sort-value="10000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)10,000g.

* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): Introduced.
