The **Dark Talisman** can be found in a chest in the [Mutant Bug Lair](/Mutant_Bug_Lair "Mutant Bug Lair"). Obtaining it is part of the [Dark Talisman quest](/Quests#Dark_Talisman "Quests"). It is used to open the passage at the northeast end of the [Railroad](/Railroad "Railroad").

Once obtained, it can be found in the [Special Items & Powers](/Special_Items_%26_Powers "Special Items & Powers") tab in [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s Menu. Oddly enough, it remains there even after using it.
