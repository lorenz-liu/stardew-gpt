The **Warp Totem: Beach** is a [Warp Totem](/Warp_Totem "Warp Totem") that teleports the player to [The Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") by selecting it in inventory and right-clicking. It can be [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting") when the recipe becomes available at [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") Level 6.

The Warp Totem: Beach can be used to bypass events occurring in town without triggering them, but if used while an event is happening at the beach, it will start the event.

Note that using a Warp Totem: Beach on the day of the [Luau](/Luau "Luau") or [Dance of the Moonlight Jellies](/Dance_of_the_Moonlight_Jellies "Dance of the Moonlight Jellies") before the festival starts will consume the totem, but will not transport the player to the beach.

## Other Sources

* One Warp Totem: Beach may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box").
* The following [Crab Pot](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot") [fish](/Fish "Fish") have a small daily chance to produce one Warp Totem: Beach in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") once the population is 9 or higher: [Lobster](/Lobster "Lobster"), [Crab](/Crab "Crab"), [Cockle](/Cockle "Cockle"), [Mussel](/Mussel "Mussel"), [Shrimp](/Shrimp "Shrimp"), and [Oyster](/Oyster "Oyster"). All fish have the same daily chance (6% for population level 9, and 7% for population level 10).
* Twenty-five Warp Totem: Beach may be received as a gift from [Willy](/Willy "Willy") at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star").
* Five Warp Totem: Beach are the reward for completing the ![Bundle Blue.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/e4/Bundle_Blue.png/24px-Bundle_Blue.png) [Ocean Fish Bundle](/Bundles#Ocean_Fish_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Fish Tank](/Bundles#Fish_Tank "Bundles").
* Starting [Summer](/Summer "Summer") 1 of year 1 there is a 10% chance for a Warp Totem: Beach to replace [Clay](/Clay "Clay") in an [Artifact Spot](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot").

## Bundles

The Warp Totem: Beach is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

The Warp Totem: Beach can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Totem Mask.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/cc/Totem_Mask.png/24px-Totem_Mask.png) [Totem Mask](/Totem_Mask "Totem Mask"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

The Warp Totem: Beach is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
