**Fiber** is a [resource](/Category:Resources "Category:Resources") obtained by destroying [Weeds](/Weeds "Weeds") or harvesting fully-grown [Fiber Seeds](/Fiber_Seeds "Fiber Seeds"). Each weed has a 50% chance to drop 1 Fiber when cut with a [Scythe](/Scythe "Scythe"), [Axe](/Axes "Axes"), [Pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes"), [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes"), or [Sword](/Weapons#Sword "Weapons"), or blown up by a [bomb](/Crafting#Bombs "Crafting"). The [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") will also exchange five [stone](/Stone "Stone") for one fiber on [Tuesdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Tuesday "Days of the Week"). [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent") sells Fiber in his [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="1">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 1 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg") per set of 10.

Weeds that drop Fiber can frequently be found in high quantities on Floors 25-29 and 81-109 of [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"). The late-game [Mutant Bug Lair](/Mutant_Bug_Lair "Mutant Bug Lair") is also a good source, generating 40-70 fiber from its weeds, which regenerate every few days.

[Wilderness Golems](/Wilderness_Golem "Wilderness Golem") and [Iridium Golems](/Iridium_Golem "Iridium Golem") on the [Wilderness Farm map](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps") have a 90% chance of dropping Fiber, and a 50% chance of dropping a second Fiber, when slain.

Fiber is always normal quality.

## Tailoring

Fiber is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a dyeable [Basic Pullover](/Tailoring "Tailoring").

* Male version: [![Shirt176.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/22/Shirt176.png/24px-Shirt176.png)](/File:Shirt176.png)
* Female version: [![Shirt177.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/93/Shirt177.png/24px-Shirt177.png)](/File:Shirt177.png)

It is a green dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the green dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").
