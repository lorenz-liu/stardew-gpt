The **Infinity Blade** is a [sword weapon](/Weapons#Sword "Weapons") that can be obtained by combining the ![Galaxy Sword.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/44/Galaxy_Sword.png/24px-Galaxy_Sword.png) [Galaxy Sword](/Galaxy_Sword "Galaxy Sword") with ![Galaxy Soul.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/17/Galaxy_Soul.png/24px-Galaxy_Soul.png) [Galaxy Soul](/Galaxy_Soul "Galaxy Soul") (1) and ![Cinder Shard.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/fd/Cinder_Shard.png/24px-Cinder_Shard.png) [Cinder Shard](/Cinder_Shard "Cinder Shard") (20) three times in the [Forge](/Forge "Forge"). It is the most powerful sword in the game.

Obtaining it unlocks the [Infinite Power](/Achievements "Achievements") achievement.
