**Skeletons (dangerous)** are an [enemy](/Monsters "Monsters") found in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") after activating the [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") or during the [Danger In The Deep](/Quests#Danger_In_The_Deep "Quests") quest. They can also be found in the [Quarry Mine](/Quarry_Mine "Quarry Mine") at any time. During the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival"), they may spawn in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") if a certain [Calico Statue effect](/Desert_Festival#Skull_Cavern "Desert Festival") is activated. 50 Skeletons (any type) need to be killed for the [Monster Eradication Goal](/Adventurer%27s_Guild#Monster_Eradication_Goals "Adventurer's Guild") at the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild").

## Behavior

They behave identically to their weaker counterpart the [Skeleton](/Skeleton "Skeleton"). They approach and attack the player by collision, but also have a longer-ranged bone throw attack, which passes through rocks.

## Strategy

Simply hit them, and dodge any bones they throw.
