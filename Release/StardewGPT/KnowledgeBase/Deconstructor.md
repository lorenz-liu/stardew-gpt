A **Deconstructor** can be used to salvage some of the material of a crafted item. It can be purchased for data-sort-value="20">![Qi Gem.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/78/Qi_Gem.png/24px-Qi_Gem.png) 20 [Qi Gems](/Qi_Gem "Qi Gem") at [Qi's Walnut Room](/Qi%27s_Walnut_Room "Qi's Walnut Room").

## Use

The game will check the given crafting item's ingredients and compare their selling price in the [Shipping Bin](/Shipping "Shipping"). It will return 100% of the highest value material.[[1]](#cite_note-sellprice-1) It takes ![Time Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Time_Icon.png/24px-Time_Icon.png) 60m (1h) to process.[[2]](#cite_note-processtime-2)

Some items cannot be placed in the Deconstructor. If a player attempts to put something into the Deconstructor that it doesn't accept, they will receive a pop-up saying, "This item can't be scrapped."

* Some resources obtained with the deconstructed items listed above are cheaper than the equivalent raw materials bought from a shop, namely: bars are cheaper than buying [coal](/Coal "Coal") and the respective ores at the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith"), and [wood](/Wood "Wood") [chipped](/Wood_Chipper "Wood Chipper") from hardwood is cheaper than buying it from the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop") in year 2+.
* Fishing [Tackle](/Tackle "Tackle") can be deconstructed at any durability level. This allows certain tackle like [Treasure Hunter](/Treasure_Hunter "Treasure Hunter") and [Trap Bobber](/Trap_Bobber "Trap Bobber") to be refreshed at little to no cost.
* It is possible to repeatedly craft and deconstruct items like [Chests](/Chest "Chest"), [Staircases](/Staircase "Staircase"), [Carved Braziers](/Carved_Brazier "Carved Brazier"), or [Skull Braziers](/Skull_Brazier "Skull Brazier") to "collect" resources for a [Special Order](/Quests#List_of_Special_Orders "Quests").
* If the [Ancient Seed](/Ancient_Seed "Ancient Seed") artifact has not been found yet, one can deconstruct a packet of [Ancient Seeds](/Ancient_Seeds "Ancient Seeds") obtained from a [Seed Maker](/Seed_Maker "Seed Maker") to obtain the artifact so it can be donated.
* If an item's recipe was changed at some point (*e.g.,* the [Skull Brazier](/Skull_Brazier "Skull Brazier")), the Deconstructor will return the highest value item based on the *current* recipe - it will not look back to see the materials originally used, even if the particular item was crafted before the recipe change.
  + However, regardless of whether the player has [Trapper profession](/Fishing#Fishing_Skill "Fishing"), [Crab Pot](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot") would be deconstructed into 2 [Copper Bars](/Copper_Bar "Copper Bar").
* [Rain Totems](/Rain_Totem "Rain Totem") obtained from treasure rooms in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") can be deconstructed into [Truffle Oil](/Truffle_Oil "Truffle Oil") to greatly increase the sell price from data-sort-value="20">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)20g to data-sort-value="1065">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,065g, or data-sort-value="1491">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,491g with the [Artisan Profession](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming").
* The [Text Sign](/Text_Sign "Text Sign") Pam places at the [Bus Stop](/Bus_Stop "Bus Stop") on days she is unavailable can be collected and deconstructed into 25 [Wood](/Wood "Wood").
