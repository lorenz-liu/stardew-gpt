The **Crystalarium** is a [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting") item that will replicate any inserted [Gem](/Minerals#Gems "Minerals") (except [Prismatic Shard](/Prismatic_Shard "Prismatic Shard")), [Foraged Mineral](/Minerals#Foraged_Minerals "Minerals"), or [Geode Mineral](/Minerals#Geode_Minerals "Minerals"). The functions of the Crystalarium are explained in the Lost Book *[Technology Report!](/Lost_Books#Technology_Report.21 "Lost Books")*.

One Crystalarium is the reward for donating 50 [Minerals](/Minerals "Minerals") to the [Museum](/Museum "Museum"). One Crystalarium is also the reward for completing the ![Bundle Purple.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a1/Bundle_Purple.png/16px-Bundle_Purple.png) [25,000 Bundle](/Bundles#g25000_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Vault](/Bundles#Vault "Bundles"). Occasionally it can be found in a [treasure room](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern") in the Skull Cavern.

Once an item is inserted, it will replicate that item an infinite number of times. Once a gem or mineral has been inserted into the Crystalarium, it can be retrieved at any time by removing the Crystalarium with a tool.

## Use

Note that times to craft are approximate, since time passes differently from 2am to 6am. One hour = 60 minutes from 6am to 2am, but 1 hour = 100 minutes from 2am to 6am. One full day is 1600 minutes, where day is 1200 minutes and night is 400 minutes.

## Tips

* The most profitable gem to replicate is the [Diamond](/Diamond "Diamond"), yielding a profit of data-sort-value="150">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150g per day if harvested as soon as it is ready (five days). At this rate, the base monetary value of the materials used in crafting the Crystalarium can be recovered in about 32 days (not including a Diamond used to seed it, nor any increase in the value of the crafting materials due to profession bonuses).
  + [Diamonds](/Diamond "Diamond") can also be traded in with the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for the [triple shot espresso](/Triple_Shot_Espresso "Triple Shot Espresso").
  + Diamonds are also either liked or loved by all villagers, making the replication of Diamonds useful for gift-giving purposes.
* [Earth Crystals](/Earth_Crystal "Earth Crystal") can be replicated in about 13 in-game hours, providing two nearly universally liked gifts each day. This is more efficient than replicating Diamonds and either gifting them or selling them for [Salad](/Salad "Salad"), which is the cheapest universally liked item that can be purchased.
* Only 7 in-game hours is required to replicate [Quartz](/Quartz "Quartz"), the shortest time span for any mineral. While the Crystalarium is thus an efficient source of Quartz for making [Refined Quartz](/Refined_Quartz "Refined Quartz"), Quartz is found abundantly during mining and making Refined Quartz still requires [Coal](/Coal "Coal") for smelting. Refined Quartz can also be produced directly in a [Recycling Machine](/Recycling_Machine "Recycling Machine") from [Broken Glasses](/Broken_Glasses "Broken Glasses") and [Broken CDs](/Broken_CD "Broken CD"), without needing coal.
  + Five Quartz can be traded at the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for 1 [Bomb](/Bomb "Bomb"), making the replication of Quartz in Crystalariums a more cost-effective method of obtaining Bombs than purchasing them from the [Dwarf](/Dwarf "Dwarf")'s shop for data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g each.
* The Crystalarium can replicate [Jade](/Jade "Jade") for trading with the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for [Staircases](/Staircase "Staircase") on [Sundays](/Days_of_the_Week#Sunday "Days of the Week"). The staircases can then either be used in the Skull Caverns or inserted into a [Deconstructor](/Deconstructor "Deconstructor") to produce 99 [stone](/Stone "Stone"). This can save countless hours gathering stone.
* The Crystalarium can replicate [Rubies](/Ruby "Ruby") for trading with the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for [Spicy Eels](/Spicy_Eel "Spicy Eel"). The Spicy Eels can then be eaten to increase [luck](/Luck "Luck") and [player speed](/Speed#Player_Speed "Speed"). This can also save time from [cooking](/Cooking "Cooking") them with [eels](/Eel "Eel") and [hot peppers](/Hot_Pepper "Hot Pepper").
* Replicating [Fire Quartz](/Fire_Quartz "Fire Quartz") may be worthwhile; it takes only about 22 hours. It is required in the crafting recipe of a [Dehydrator](/Dehydrator "Dehydrator"). One Fire Quartz and one [Coal](/Coal "Coal") also produce three Refined Quartz when placed in a furnace, a greater efficiency than refining Quartz. Refined Quartz can be sold for data-sort-value="50">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)50g, so with a fast replication rate, Fire Quartz rivals [Diamond](/Diamond "Diamond") for best selling gem, and even beats it if the player uses [Heavy Furnace](/Heavy_Furnace "Heavy Furnace") (with avarage profit around data-sort-value="175">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)175g per day) but only if [Coal](/Coal "Coal") used in recipe is acquired for free.
