**Quality Fertilizer** is a [Fertilizer](/Fertilizer "Fertilizer") that improves the chance to grow a [quality crop](/Crops#Crop_Quality "Crops"). It can be [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting"), obtained from a [Bone Mill](/Bone_Mill "Bone Mill"), or purchased for data-sort-value="150">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150g from [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") beginning in Year 2. It may also occasionally be sold by the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") (in any year) for data-sort-value="10"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100–1,000g. Thirty Quality Fertilizer are the reward for completing the ![Bundle Orange.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/95/Bundle_Orange.png/24px-Bundle_Orange.png) [5,000 Bundle](/Bundles#g5000_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Vault](/Bundles#Vault "Bundles"). 10-20 Quality Fertilizer may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") or [Golden Mystery Box](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box").

Quality Fertilizer must be placed on tilled soil *before* a seed has sprouted.

Quality Fertilizer only affects the basic harvest, which means that extra crops gained at harvest (*i.e.,* [Coffee Bean](/Coffee_Bean "Coffee Bean"), [Blueberry](/Blueberry "Blueberry"), [Cranberries](/Cranberries "Cranberries")) will be of regular quality.

## Tailoring

Quality Fertilizer can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [High-Waisted Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt175.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/24/Shirt175.png/24px-Shirt175.png)](/File:Shirt175.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

Quality Fertilizer is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
