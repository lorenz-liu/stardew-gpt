The **Sea Cucumber** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught in the ocean at [The Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") or on the [Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps#Beach "Farm Maps") during [Fall](/Fall "Fall") and [Winter](/Winter "Winter"). It can also be randomly found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during [Fall](/Fall "Fall") and [Winter](/Winter "Winter"), or at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="75"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)225–1,000g. There is a 5% chance to catch a Sea Cucumber during the deep-sea submarine ride at the [Night Market](/Night_Market "Night Market") during Winter 15-17 (4% if using a [Curiosity Lure](/Curiosity_Lure "Curiosity Lure")).

Sea Cucumber can be caught regardless of time of day or season using [Magic Bait](/Magic_Bait "Magic Bait").

## Tailoring

Sea Cucumber can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Tube Top](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt142.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/65/Shirt142.png/24px-Shirt142.png)](/File:Shirt142.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Sea Cucumbers can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 2 days. The initial pond capacity is 3 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing three quests. The only possible output is yellow Sea Cucumber [Roe](/Roe "Roe").
