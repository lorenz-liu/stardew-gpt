**Topaz** is a type of [Mineral](/Minerals "Minerals") that can be obtained from [Topaz Nodes](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining") and [Gem Nodes](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining") in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), from a [Gem Bird](/Ginger_Island#Gem_Birds "Ginger Island") on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") during [rainy days](/Weather#Weather_Types "Weather"), from [panning](/Pans "Pans"), or found in [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing"). It has a 10% chance to be dropped by a slain [Dwarvish Sentry](/Dwarvish_Sentry "Dwarvish Sentry") in the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon"). It may occasionally be found in a [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can").

## Bundles

Topaz is one of the options for the ![Bundle Yellow.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2b/Bundle_Yellow.png/24px-Bundle_Yellow.png) [Treasure Hunter's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Treasure_Hunter.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Boiler Room (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Boiler_Room "Remixed Bundles").

## Forging

Topaz can be used at the [Forge](/Forge "Forge") to increase the defense of a melee weapon.

## Recipes

Topaz is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Topaz is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt146.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/9e/Shirt146.png/24px-Shirt146.png) [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a gold dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the yellow dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

* Topaz may be randomly requested during any season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="240">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)240g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* Two Topaz may be requested by [Catfish](/Catfish "Catfish") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 3 to 5.
* Topaz is requested by [Emily](/Emily "Emily") as part of her ["Rock Rejuvenation" special order](/Quests#Rock_Rejuvenation "Quests").
* May be used in the [Gem Birds](/Ginger_Island#Gem_Birds "Ginger Island") puzzle on Ginger Island.
* Three Topaz may be requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his fifth request, or as part of any request starting with the sixth.
