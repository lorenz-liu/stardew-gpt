The **Witch's Hut** is found in the [Witch's Swamp](/Witch%27s_Swamp "Witch's Swamp"), and becomes accessible after completing the [Dark Talisman Quest](/Quests#Dark_Talisman "Quests").

Inside the Witch's Hut are three shrines, the [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard")'s missing [Magic Ink](/Magic_Ink "Magic Ink") (on a table in a purple bottle), and a red teleportation rune on the ground. Returning the Magic Ink to the Wizard unlocks five "magic" buildings for [The Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"). Stepping on the rune will teleport the player directly to the [Wizard's Tower](/Wizard%27s_Tower "Wizard's Tower") basement room.

Once the player has gained 4 hearts of [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") with the Wizard, the teleportation runes located in the Witch's Hut and Wizard's Tower basement can be used at anytime to teleport between the two locations. Prior to earning 4 hearts, the player will be teleported to the Wizard's basement from the Witch's Hut, but will not be able to return.

## Quests

The questline to access the Witch's Hut involves two [quests](/Quests "Quests"), which become available after completing the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") [Bundles](/Bundles "Bundles") or the [Joja Community Development Form](/Joja_Community_Development_Form "Joja Community Development Form"). [The player](/The_Player "The Player") can trigger a cut scene at the [Railroad](/Railroad "Railroad"), where the [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard") will request they find a Dark Talisman, which can be found in a chest within the [Mutant Bug Lair](/Mutant_Bug_Lair "Mutant Bug Lair"). The Mutant Bug Lair can be accessed by speaking to [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus"), who will reveal the entrance in [the Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers"). After obtaining the Talisman, bring it back to the Railroad area and place it on the artifact blocking the cave. Inside the cave is a teleportation rune which leads to the [Witch's Swamp](/Witch%27s_Swamp "Witch's Swamp").

Once inside the Witch's Swamp, the Witch's [Henchman](/Henchman "Henchman") will be blocking the way to the Witch's Hut. He will only allow the player to pass if he is given [Void Mayonnaise](/Void_Mayonnaise "Void Mayonnaise"), which also completes the quest [Goblin Problem](/Quests#Goblin_Problem "Quests"). Recover the Magic Ink inside the Witch's Hut, then bring the ink back to the Wizard, who will unlock the six [Wizard buildings](/Wizard%27s_Tower "Wizard's Tower") for [The Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm").
