The **Wood Chipper** is used to convert [Hardwood](/Hardwood "Hardwood") and [Driftwood](/Driftwood "Driftwood") into other products. It can be purchased from the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop") for data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g starting the first day of [Winter](/Winter "Winter") in year 1. The player receives a letter about the wood chipper from [Robin](/Robin "Robin") on the second day of Winter in year 1. A Wood Chipper may be purchased from [Kent](/Kent "Kent")'s [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="25">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 25 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg").
