The **Dark Sword** is a [sword weapon](/Weapons#Sword "Weapons") that can be obtained as a [Monster](/Monsters "Monsters") drop from [Haunted Skulls](/Haunted_Skull "Haunted Skull") in the [Quarry Mine](/Quarry_Mine "Quarry Mine") or dungeon floors in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"). It is the slowest sword in the game, at −5 ![Speed w.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/26/Speed_w.png/24px-Speed_w.png) [Speed](/Speed "Speed").

The Dark Sword comes with the [vampiric enchantment](/Forge#Enchantments "Forge") when obtained from [Haunted Skulls](/Haunted_Skull "Haunted Skull"). The enchantment can be changed at the [Forge](/Forge "Forge").

## Notes

* Damage dealt by explosions may also trigger healing as long as the Dark Sword is held. This includes the 999 damage inflicted upon downed [Mummies](/Mummy "Mummy") by explosions.
* It has the slowest speed and highest weight of any sword in the game.
