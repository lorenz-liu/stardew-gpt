The **Wood Lamp-post** is a [crafted lighting item](/Crafting#Lighting "Crafting") that can be held or placed to provide light. The recipe can be purchased from the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop") for data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g.

The wood lamp-post influences a radius of approximately 7 tiles, with most of the illumination within the closest few tiles. The lamp can be placed indoors or outdoors, or in places like [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"). It can be removed by striking it with a [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes"), a [Pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes"), or an [Axe](/Axes "Axes"). Unlike the braziers, which can toggle on and off, the Wood and [Iron Lamp-posts](/Iron_Lamp-post "Iron Lamp-post") are always on. They provide more light than any other craftable lighting.
