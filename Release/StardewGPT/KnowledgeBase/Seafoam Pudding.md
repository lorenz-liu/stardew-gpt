**Seafoam Pudding** is a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit"). One can be purchased for data-sort-value="5000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)5,000g from the shop at the [Dance of the Moonlight Jellies](/Dance_of_the_Moonlight_Jellies "Dance of the Moonlight Jellies") festival each year. One Seafoam Pudding is part of the iridium tier reward on the 12th and 13th for the [SquidFest](/SquidFest "SquidFest") festival, assuming the player already earned [The Art O' Crabbing](/The_Art_O%27_Crabbing "The Art O' Crabbing") before from the festival. It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") turtle with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals"). It may also randomly appear at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="300"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)900–1,500g.

## Bundles

Seafoam Pudding is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Recipes

Seafoam Pudding is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Seafoam Pudding is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Mineral Dog Jacket](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Mineral Dog Jacket.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a6/Mineral_Dog_Jacket.png/24px-Mineral_Dog_Jacket.png)](/File:Mineral_Dog_Jacket.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a blue dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Seafoam Pudding is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
