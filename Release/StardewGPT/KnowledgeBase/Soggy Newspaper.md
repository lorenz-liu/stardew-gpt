**Soggy Newspaper** is a type of [trash](/Trash "Trash") that can be caught when fishing during all seasons, in any location, using either a [Fishing Pole](/Tools#Fishing_Poles "Tools") or a [Crab Pot](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot"). It can also be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can"), and any crab pot fish may produce Soggy Newspaper when placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"). It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") cat with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals").

Soggy Newspaper can be [recycled](/Recycling_Machine "Recycling Machine") into [Torches](/Torch "Torch") or [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth").

## Bundles

Soggy Newspaper can be recycled into [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth"), which is used in the ![Bundle Purple.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a1/Bundle_Purple.png/24px-Bundle_Purple.png) [Artisan Bundle](/Bundles#Artisan_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Pantry](/Bundles#Pantry "Bundles").

## Recipes

Soggy Newspaper is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Soggy Newspaper can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Trash Can Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt232.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2a/Shirt232.png/24px-Shirt232.png)](/File:Shirt232.png)

## Quests

During the special order quest [Community Cleanup](/Quests#List_of_Special_Orders "Quests") the player is tasked with gathering 20 trash items (except Joja Cola).
