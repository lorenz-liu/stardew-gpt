**Pizza** is a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit").

Pizza is also available for purchase daily at [The Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon") for data-sort-value="600">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)600g and may randomly appear in [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus")' shop on [Saturdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Saturday "Days of the Week"). [Shane](/Shane "Shane") sells Pizza in his [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="5">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 5 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg").
One Pizza may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box"). The [Statue Of Endless Fortune](/Statue_Of_Endless_Fortune "Statue Of Endless Fortune") produces one Pizza per year on [Shane](/Shane "Shane")'s birthday, [Spring 20](/Spring "Spring").

## Bundles

Pizza is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Pizza is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Party Hat (red).png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/96/Party_Hat_%28red%29.png/24px-Party_Hat_%28red%29.png) [Party Hat (red)](/Party_Hat_(red) "Party Hat (red)"). It can also be used as a red dye in the dye pots, located in [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s and [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

One Pizza may be requested by [Blobfish](/Blobfish "Blobfish") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 3 to 5.
