**Starfruit** is a [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from [Starfruit Seeds](/Starfruit_Seeds "Starfruit Seeds") after 13 days. It has the second highest sell price of any crop in the game, with only the [Sweet Gem Berry](/Sweet_Gem_Berry "Sweet Gem Berry") selling for more. One Starfruit can be purchased each year at the [Luau](/Luau "Luau") for data-sort-value="3000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)3,000g.

## Bundles

Starfruit is one of the options in the ![Bundle Teal.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/ce/Bundle_Teal.png/24px-Bundle_Teal.png) [remixed Dye Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Dye_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board](/Remixed_Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Remixed Bundles").

## Recipes

Starfruit is not used in any recipes.

## Buildings

Starfruit is required to build a [Junimo Hut](/Junimo_Hut "Junimo Hut"), purchased from the [Wizard's Tower](/Wizard%27s_Tower "Wizard's Tower").

## Tailoring

Starfruit can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Genie Pants](/Tailoring#Pants "Tailoring"). [![Genie Pants.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/8a/Genie_Pants.png/36px-Genie_Pants.png)](/File:Genie_Pants.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* [Kent](/Kent "Kent") requests a Starfruit by mail on the 15th of [Summer](/Summer "Summer") (year 2) in the ["A Soldier's Star" Quest](/Quests#A_Soldier.27s_Star "Quests"). The reward is data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
