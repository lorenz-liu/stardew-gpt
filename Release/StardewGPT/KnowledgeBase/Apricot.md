The **Apricot** is a [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") grown by planting an [Apricot Sapling](/Apricot_Sapling "Apricot Sapling"), waiting 28 days for it to grow into an [Apricot Tree](/Apricot_Tree "Apricot Tree"), and harvesting the tree during the [Spring](/Spring "Spring").

An Apricot may be purchased at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="50"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150–1,000g or found in [The Farm Cave](/The_Cave "The Cave"), if the fruit bat option is chosen.

## Tailoring

Apricot can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Relaxed Fit Pants](/Tailoring#Pants "Tailoring"). [![Relaxed Fit Pants.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/1e/Relaxed_Fit_Pants.png/36px-Relaxed_Fit_Pants.png)](/File:Relaxed_Fit_Pants.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

[Emily](/Emily "Emily") requests an apricot by mail on the 6th of [Spring](/Spring "Spring") (year 2) in the ["Fresh Fruit" Quest](/Quests#Fresh_Fruit "Quests"). The reward is data-sort-value="600">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)600g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
