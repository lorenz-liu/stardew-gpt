**The Calico Desert** is an exterior map region located far to the northwest of [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). The [weather](/Weather "Weather") here is always sunny and the seasons are all the same.

The desert is inaccessible until the Bus at the [Bus Stop](/Bus_Stop "Bus Stop") has been repaired by completing the [Vault Bundles](/Bundles#Vault "Bundles") at the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") or by purchasing "Bus Repair" for data-sort-value="40000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)40,000g from the [Joja Community Development Form](/Joja_Community_Development_Form "Joja Community Development Form"). The [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") is held annually from Spring 15th to 17th.

## Foraging

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a3/DesertForageLocations.png/400px-DesertForageLocations.png)](/File:DesertForageLocations.png)

Locations in the Desert where forageable items can spawn.

The same [forageable](/Foraging "Foraging") items are found in the Desert year-round:[[1]](#cite_note-forage_chance-1)

* ![Coconut.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2f/Coconut.png/24px-Coconut.png) [Coconut](/Coconut "Coconut") (50%) and ![Cactus Fruit.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/32/Cactus_Fruit.png/24px-Cactus_Fruit.png) [Cactus Fruit](/Cactus_Fruit "Cactus Fruit") (50%)

Forageable items spawn at an average rate of 2.0 per night year-round.[[2]](#cite_note-forage_spawn-2) The map shows the possible locations as red and magenta tiles; when the original trees are present, the magenta tiles have a 90% smaller chance of spawning items because they are identified as being behind the original trees.

The original [trees](/Trees "Trees") found in the Desert are all [Palm Trees](/Trees#Palm_Tree "Trees"). They can be chopped down. They do not drop seeds, but may occasionally drop a Coconut when chopped down or shaken. They cannot be [tapped](/Tapper "Tapper").

In addition, regular trees ([Maple](/Maple_Tree "Maple Tree"), [Oak](/Oak_Tree "Oak Tree"), [Pine](/Pine_Tree "Pine Tree"), and [Mahogany](/Mahogany_Tree "Mahogany Tree")) can all be planted throughout the Desert. Unlike in the main valley, trees planted in the Oasis will grow during [Winter](/Winter "Winter") even without [Tree Fertilizer](/Tree_Fertilizer "Tree Fertilizer").

## Artifacts

[Artifacts](/Artifacts "Artifacts") that can be found by digging up [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") in the Desert are:

* ![Palm Fossil.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/e1/Palm_Fossil.png/24px-Palm_Fossil.png) [Palm Fossil](/Palm_Fossil "Palm Fossil") (7%)
* ![Golden Relic.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/37/Golden_Relic.png/24px-Golden_Relic.png) [Golden Relic](/Golden_Relic "Golden Relic") (6%)
* ![Golden Mask.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/7e/Golden_Mask.png/24px-Golden_Mask.png) [Golden Mask](/Golden_Mask "Golden Mask") (3%)

Other possible items are:

* 1-3 ![Clay.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a2/Clay.png/24px-Clay.png) [Clay](/Clay "Clay") (48%)
* ![Lost Book.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/f3/Lost_Book.png/24px-Lost_Book.png) [Lost Book](/Lost_Books "Lost Books") (20%); once all Lost Books have been found, any potential Lost Book is replaced by ![Mixed Seeds.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2e/Mixed_Seeds.png/24px-Mixed_Seeds.png) [Mixed Seeds](/Mixed_Seeds "Mixed Seeds").
* 1-3 ![Stone.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/d4/Stone.png/24px-Stone.png) [Stone](/Stone "Stone") (16%)
* An unseen ![Secret Note.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/ec/Secret_Note.png/24px-Secret_Note.png) [Secret Note](/Secret_Notes "Secret Notes"): up to 4% chance, only if the player has a [Magnifying Glass](/Magnifying_Glass "Magnifying Glass").

Note that [Snow Yams](/Snow_Yam "Snow Yam"), [Winter Roots](/Winter_Root "Winter Root"), and [Rice Shoots](/Rice_Shoot "Rice Shoot") are never found in the Desert.

Artifact spots spawn at an average rate of 1.0 per night, except in winter when the average rate increases to 1.4 per night.[[3]](#cite_note-artifact_spawn-3)

## Fishing

[![](/mediawiki/images/4/4f/DesertPondFishingZones.png)](/File:DesertPondFishingZones.png)

[Distances from land](/Fishing#Distance_from_Land "Fishing") at the Desert Pond.

The primary location for fishing in the Desert is the pond in the northwest section. The player can catch two unique fish here, namely the [Sandfish](/Sandfish "Sandfish") and [Scorpion Carp](/Scorpion_Carp "Scorpion Carp"). The Sandfish is needed for the ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Specialty Fish Bundle](/Bundles#Specialty_Fish_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Fish Tank](/Bundles#Fish_Tank "Bundles").

It's also possible to fish in the pool of water on the southern edge of the map, if the player's [Fishing](/Fishing "Fishing") skill is high enough for the bobber to reach the water. However, the fish quality will always be minimal in the southern pond, as the [distance from land](/Fishing#Distance_from_Land "Fishing") is 0 for the entire pond. [[4]](#cite_note-south_pond-4) The [Pyramid Decal](/Pyramid_Decal "Pyramid Decal") can be fished here (10% chance).

## References

1. [↑](#cite_ref-forage_chance_1-0) For each forage item, the provided percentage is the average percentage of all forage items that will be the specified item for that season. The input data is `Locations.xnb`, which is processed by code in `GameLocation::spawnObjects`.
2. [↑](#cite_ref-forage_spawn_2-0) Of the 3000 total tiles in the Desert, 923 (31%) are valid spawn locations for standard forage items. For more information on forage item spawning, see [Foraging](/Foraging#Spawning "Foraging").
3. [↑](#cite_ref-artifact_spawn_3-0) Of the 3000 total tiles in the Desert, 1229 (41%) are valid spawn locations for artifact spots year-round. Even though the artifact spot locations are the same in winter as the rest of the year, the spawn rate is higher in winter because `chanceForNewArtifactAttempt` in `GameLocation:spawnObjects` is higher in winter. See [Artifact Spot](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") for more information.
4. [↑](#cite_ref-south_pond_4-0) The water tiles in the south pond are handled more like a fountain: the information is stored in the map's `Building` layer instead of the `Back` layer. Fishing is allowed, based on the `Building` data, but all distance-from-land calculations consider the tile to be a land tile, therefore the distance from land is set to zero.

## History

* [1.0](/Version_History#1.0 "Version History"): Introduced.
* [1.07](/Version_History#1.07 "Version History"): Fixed issue regarding Snow Yams appearing in the Desert.
* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Desert Trader added. Fixed bug where Snow Yams and Winter Roots could be dug up in Winter. Various visual fixes.
* [1.4.1](/Version_History#1.4.1 "Version History"): Now only one Magic Rock Candy can be purchased each Thursday from the Desert Trader.
* [1.4.4](/Version_History#1.4.4 "Version History"): Fixed issue where Crab Pots placed in the desert pond were rendered incorrectly. Objects can no longer be placed on top of the Desert Trader.
* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): Pyramid Decal can now be caught when fishing in the southern pond.
* [1.5.4](/Version_History#1.5.4 "Version History"): Palm trees can no longer produce [Golden Coconuts](/Golden_Coconut "Golden Coconut").
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Desert Festival introduced. Trees planted in the Desert will now grow during [Winter](/Winter "Winter") without [Tree Fertilizer](/Tree_Fertilizer "Tree Fertilizer").
