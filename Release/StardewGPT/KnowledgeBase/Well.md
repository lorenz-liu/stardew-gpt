The **Well** is a [farm building](/Carpenter%27s_Shop#Farm_Buildings "Carpenter's Shop") purchasable from [Robin](/Robin "Robin") at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop") that allows the player to refill their [Watering Can](/Watering_Cans "Watering Cans"). It takes two days for Robin to complete its construction.

## Behavior

Interacting with the well with a watering can equipped will refill it as if the player was next to water.
