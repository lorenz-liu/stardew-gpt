**Weather** changes daily and with the [seasons](/Seasons "Seasons"). The weather for the next day can be checked by viewing the [TV screen](/Television "Television") and selecting "Weather Report".

[![](/mediawiki/images/1/17/Weather_Report_Fern_Islands.png)](/File:Weather_Report_Fern_Islands.png)

The Fern Islands weather report

The weather on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") is different from in Stardew Valley, and once unlocked, the Fern Islands weather report will display immediately after the Stardew Valley report.

Daily weather affects the types of [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught, as well as [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") dialogues and behavior.

## Weather Chances

The chance that a particular day will be sunny/rainy/stormy/etc. depends on several different factors. See [Modding:Weather data](/Modding:Weather_data "Modding:Weather data").
Ginger Island only has two types of weather: sun and rain. The chance for rain is 24% (0% on first visit).[[1]](#cite_note-islandweather-1)

## Notes

### Storm

* It always rains on stormy days. [Fish](/Fish "Fish") that require rain can be caught on stormy days, and the [Old Mariner](/Old_Mariner "Old Mariner") will appear on stormy days.
* During a storm, lightning strikes can destroy random tiles on the farm. This includes [crops](/Crops "Crops") and [trees](/Trees "Trees"). If a [Fruit Tree](/Fruit_Trees "Fruit Trees") is hit, it will temporarily change into a coal tree, producing coal instead of the usual [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") that would otherwise be given.
  + If lightning hits a tile with a crafted [flooring or path](/Crafting#Decor "Crafting") on it, the flooring or path will be displaced but not permanently destroyed.
* There is a chance for a lightning strike to occur every 10 minutes.
* Lightning will not strike [Chests](/Chest "Chest") placed outside[[2]](#cite_note-2).
* If [Lightning Rods](/Lightning_Rod "Lightning Rod") are placed anywhere on the farm[[3]](#cite_note-3) they have a good chance to intercept lightning strikes, as long as they are not already processing a strike into a [Battery Pack](/Battery_Pack "Battery Pack").
* It is always stormy on Summer 13 and 26, every year.

### Snow

* [Winter Seeds](/Winter_Seeds "Winter Seeds") and [Powdermelon](/Powdermelon "Powdermelon") need to be watered even if it is snowing.
* Snow transparency can be changed in the [Options Menu](/Options#Graphics "Options"). Sliding the bar to the *left* will *increase* snow transparency (*i.e.,* decrease opacity).

### Green Rain

* Green rain occurs randomly once each [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), only possible on the following dates: 5, 6, 7, 14, 15, 16, 18, 23.
* A translucent green visual effect is visible even when indoors.
* [Green Rain Trees](/Green_Rain_Trees "Green Rain Trees") and [weeds](/Weeds#Special_Weed "Weeds") spawn in large quantities throughout the entire valley (except on [the Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") and in [the Calico Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert")).
  + Trees outdoors are affected by Green Rain as follows:[[4]](#cite_note-TreeonGreenRainDay-4)
    - Every tree sapling of stage 1 (seed) has a 50% chance to grow to stage 4. All tree saplings of stage 2 or 3 will grow to stage 4. After these two steps, all tree saplings of stage 4 will [grow](/Trees#Growth_Cycle "Trees") as if they had gone through 3 nights.
    - Every fully grown tree has a 50% chance of getting covered in [moss](/Moss "Moss").
    - Every fully grown [Oak Tree](/Oak_Tree "Oak Tree") or [Maple Tree](/Maple_Tree "Maple Tree") has a 75% chance to turn into Temporary [Green Rain Trees](/Green_Rain_Trees "Green Rain Trees") for the duration of Green Rain.
  + Most Green Rain Trees that are not on the farm, whether spawned or transformed, will revert back into their original form on the next day. Some Green Rain Trees will remain. Green Rain Trees in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") will be removed.
  + Weeds spawned by green rain do so non-destructively and disappear the next day.
  + Temporary Green Rain Trees on the farm have a chance to spread, meaning the next day you can find stage 1 green rain trees, harvestable for a [mossy seed](/Mossy_seed "Mossy seed"), next to trees that have reverted to their original types.
  + Large Green rain weeds yield 15 [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") xp each when destroyed.
* Certain electronic devices are inoperable during the event.
  + All [TV channels](/Television "Television") will show a green TV static instead.
  + The jukebox in [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon") and the [Mini-Jukebox](/Mini-Jukebox "Mini-Jukebox") do not play.
  + The [Telephone](/Telephone "Telephone") cannot be used.
* In the first year, most [villagers](/Villagers "Villagers") will stay indoors all day, except for [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius"), [Leah](/Leah "Leah"), and [Linus](/Linus "Linus").
  + Buildings accessible by the player can be entered at any time of day, regardless of their usual hours, and some bedrooms will be accessible without heart requirements.
  + Players cannot give [gifts](/Friendship#Gifts "Friendship") to villagers during Green Rain in Year 1.
* At the end of the day, the [shipping summary screen](/Secrets#Shipping_Screen "Secrets") displays a sky with green fog and dark gray storm clouds.

### Other

* On the first year, it is always sunny on [Spring](/Spring "Spring") 1, 2, 4 and 5,[[5]](#cite_note-day5-5) and it always rains on Spring 3. From Spring 6 and onwards, the weather is randomized, with certain exceptions.
* The [Rain Totem](/Rain_Totem "Rain Totem") will change the weather for the following day to rain unless the following day is a [festival](/Festivals "Festivals") or the 1st day of a season. (Spring 2-4 of Year 1 also cannot be changed by a Rain Totem.)
* The weather will always be "clear and sunny" for [Festivals](/Festivals "Festivals").
* It will never rain in [Winter](/Winter "Winter") unless a [Rain Totem](/Rain_Totem "Rain Totem") is used.
* If there is a [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") on a player's [wedding](/Marriage "Marriage") day, the heart icon will not display.
* For all [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") heart events, there are only two defined weather states: Sun and Rain. This means that any event that requires a "Sunny" day can trigger on windy or snowy days as well as sunny days. Thunderstorm days and Green Rain days count as rainy days, so "sunny" events will not trigger.

## Bugs

* The TV will not necessarily be accurate for Spring 2 or 4, or Summer 13 or 26.
* The TV will predict stormy weather on Summer 25th, but not the 26th. If the player does not check the TV forecast on the 25th, it may not be stormy. (See [Modding:Weather data](/Modding:Weather_data "Modding:Weather data") for more information.)

## Trivia

* While winter can't have windy weather without modding, there is still an unseen message for such weather in the weather forecast: "It's going to snow all day. Make sure you bundle up, folks!"
