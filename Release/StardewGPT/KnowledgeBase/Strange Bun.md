The **Strange Bun** is a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit").

Strange Bun may randomly appear in [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus")' shop on [Saturdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Saturday "Days of the Week"), in [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon")'s rotating stock or the [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") outside the Saloon, or at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="225"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)675–1,125g. It may also be dropped by a [Shadow Brute](/Shadow_Brute "Shadow Brute") (4% chance). One Strange Bun may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box"). If [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") is the player's roommate, he may gift a Strange Bun in the morning.

By offering a Strange Bun to the Dark Shrine of Night Terrors in the [Witch's Hut](/Witch%27s_Hut "Witch's Hut"), players can toggle [monster spawning](/Monsters "Monsters") on or off on their farm.

## Bundles

Strange Bun is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Recipes

Strange Bun is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Strange Bun is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Heart Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt028.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/f3/Shirt028.png/24px-Shirt028.png)](/File:Shirt028.png) It can also be used as a red dye in the dye pots, located at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Strange Bun is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Secrets

If a Strange Bun is placed in the toy chest inside [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent")'s room, at [1 Willow Lane](/1_Willow_Lane "1 Willow Lane"), the player will receive the [![??Foroguemon??.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/5/59/%3F%3FForoguemon%3F%3F.png/16px-%3F%3FForoguemon%3F%3F.png)](/File:%3F%3FForoguemon%3F%3F.png) [??Foroguemon??](/%3F%3FForoguemon%3F%3F "??Foroguemon??") statue.

## Trivia

* Although [Shane](/Shane "Shane") sends the recipe for the Strange Bun to the player, he's neutral to it as a gift.

## History

* [1.0](/Version_History#1.0 "Version History"): Introduced.
* [1.1](/Version_History#1.1 "Version History"): Changed cooking ingredients; replaced [Oak Resin](/Oak_Resin "Oak Resin") with [Void Mayonnaise](/Void_Mayonnaise "Void Mayonnaise"). Increased price.
* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): [Shane](/Shane "Shane") will now send a the recipe for Strange Bun in the mail after reaching 7 hearts, instead of 3 hearts. Fixed bug where an entire stack of Strange Buns would be consumed by interacting with [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent")'s toy chest. Can now be used in [Tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring").
* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): Can now be prepared using a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit").
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") now likes Strange Bun, [Shane](/Shane "Shane") is now neutral.
