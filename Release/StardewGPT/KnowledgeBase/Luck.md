# Luck

[![](/mediawiki/images/8/8b/Fortune_Teller.png)](/File:Fortune_Teller.png)

Fortune Teller

**Luck** [![Luck.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/f0/Luck.png/24px-Luck.png)](/File:Luck.png) is a statistic that affects many different aspects of the game, such as the chance of finding [geodes](/Minerals#Geodes "Minerals") and chance of getting treasure from [Fishing](/Fishing "Fishing"). There are several sources of luck: daily luck determined by the game, [buffs](/Buffs "Buffs") gained from eating certain [food and drink](/Cooking "Cooking"), a [Special Charm](/Special_Charm "Special Charm") which gives a small permanent luck boost, and the [Lucky Ring](/Lucky_Ring "Lucky Ring").

## Daily Luck

The base value for daily luck is a randomly generated number from -0.1 (unlucky) to 0.1 (lucky).[[1]](#cite_note-luck-1) The value is increased by 0.025 with the [Special Charm](/Special_Charm "Special Charm"). The value is used in numerous ways; though it looks like a percentage equal to 10%, it is in fact treated differently in every situation and may have a minuscule or enormous effect depending on the activity.

Daily luck can be checked by right-clicking on the [Television](/Television "Television") and selecting "Fortune Teller." The Fortune Teller will give an indication of the daily luck stat modifier. Luck is determined at the start of the day and is not affected by choosing to view the Fortune Teller Television Program. Daily luck does not change if the day is restarted, but it can change by replaying the previous day.

Before revealing the luck message, one of the following messages is shown randomly (the first two being gender-specific). They do not affect the luck in any way.

Daily luck has 201 possible values[[1]](#cite_note-luck-1), so the probability of getting best possible luck, worst possible luck, and absolutely neutral luck are 1/201 each. Due to a rounding error in the game code, it's impossible to get absolutely neutral luck with the Special Charm[[3]](#cite_note-absolutely_neutral-3).

### Special Charm

After reading [Secret Note #20](/Secret_Notes#Secret_Note_.2320 "Secret Notes") and solving the puzzle depicted, the player is awarded with a [Special Charm](/Special_Charm "Special Charm") that permanently increases daily Luck by 0.025.

### Daily Luck Effects

Daily luck affects the following:

[Mining](/Mining "Mining")

* Chance to discover ladders from breaking rocks while [Mining](/Mining "Mining").
* [Geode](/Minerals#Geodes "Minerals") and [coal](/Coal "Coal") drop chance from rocks.
  + *(Luck does not increase the chance to receive rare [minerals](/Minerals "Minerals") and [artifacts](/Artifacts "Artifacts") from breaking geodes. These results depend only on the save file and the sequence in which the geodes are broken.)*
* Chance of finding [Gem Nodes and Mystic Stones](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining") in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") or [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern").
* Chance that crates spawn in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines").
* Chance to find treasure rooms in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern").
* Amount of [gold](/Gold "Gold") and items lost when dying in the Mines or [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern").
* Chance of finding [Radioactive Nodes](/Mining#Radioactive_Node "Mining") in the Mines (while the [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") is active) or [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") (while [Skull Cavern Invasion](/Quests#Skull_Cavern_Invasion "Quests") is active).[[4]](#cite_note-radioactive-4)

[Fishing](/Fishing "Fishing")

* Chance of [treasure chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") appearing while [Fishing](/Fishing "Fishing").[[5]](#cite_note-treasure_chance-5)
  + Chance of treasure chest being golden once [Fishing Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave") has been claimed.[[5]](#cite_note-treasure_chance-5)
  + Chance of getting [Mystery Boxes](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") or [Golden Mystery Boxes](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box") from treasure chests.[[6]](#cite_note-treasure_contents-6)
  + Chance of getting special items (*e.g.,* [Neptune's Glaive](/Neptune%27s_Glaive "Neptune's Glaive"), [Prismatic Shard](/Prismatic_Shard "Prismatic Shard"), [Iridium Band](/Iridium_Band "Iridium Band")) from treasure chests.[[6]](#cite_note-treasure_contents-6)

## Luck Buffs

Luck Buffs are temporary bonuses gained from:

* consuming certain [food and drink](/Cooking "Cooking")
* consuming dishes from the [Chef](/Desert_Festival#Chef "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival")
* wearing the [Lucky Ring](/Lucky_Ring "Lucky Ring")
* receiving the Blessing of Luck buff from the [Statue Of Blessings](/Statue_Of_Blessings "Statue Of Blessings")

Only one drink-derived buff and one food-derived buff can be active at a given time. However, dishes from the [Chef](/Desert_Festival#Chef "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") can stack on top of the food and drink buffs, which includes any luck buffs potentially received from the dishes. A buff of +2 for example may have similar effects to a daily luck value of 0.1, though it is treated differently in every situation and may have a minuscule or enormous effect depending on the activity.

### Luck Buff Effects

Luck buff effects are very similar to daily luck effects. However, total luck buff is treated separately from total daily luck, and the effects of the two types of luck are not the same. The types of events affected by luck buffs are different, and for events affected by both, the magnitude of the effect can be very different.

Luck buffs affect the following:

* Everything listed in the [Daily Luck Effects](#Daily_Luck_Effects) section, **EXCEPT**:
  + Chance of receiving [Duck Feather](/Duck_Feather "Duck Feather") and [Rabbit's Foot](/Rabbit%27s_Foot "Rabbit's Foot") from [Ducks](/Duck "Duck") and [Rabbits](/Rabbit "Rabbit").
  + Chance of hooking a [Woodskip](/Woodskip "Woodskip") on the [Forest Farm](/Farm_Maps#Forest "Farm Maps").
  + Chance of obtaining a [Prismatic Shard](/Prismatic_Shard "Prismatic Shard") from the [Blessing Of The Butterfly](/Statue_Of_Blessings#List_of_Blessings "Statue Of Blessings").
  + Chance of obtaining a [Golden Animal Cracker](/Golden_Animal_Cracker "Golden Animal Cracker") after claiming [Farming Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave").
  + Chance of a [Fishing Treasure Chest](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") being golden after claiming [Fishing Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave").
  + Chance of getting special items and [Mystery Boxes](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box")/[Golden Mystery Boxes](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box") and chance of receiving a higher quantity of [roe](/Roe "Roe") from [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing").
  + Chance of finding [Gem Nodes and Mystic Stones](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining") in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") or [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern").
* Chance of getting double quantities of resources and gems from [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing"). Luck buffs also allow standard rings ([Glow Ring](/Glow_Ring "Glow Ring") and [Magnet Ring](/Magnet_Ring "Magnet Ring")) to appear in place of small rings ([Small Glow Ring](/Small_Glow_Ring "Small Glow Ring") and [Small Magnet Ring](/Small_Magnet_Ring "Small Magnet Ring")).[[6]](#cite_note-treasure_contents-6)
* Chance of hooking [Sea Jelly](/Sea_Jelly "Sea Jelly"), [River Jelly](/River_Jelly "River Jelly"), and [Cave Jelly](/Cave_Jelly "Cave Jelly").[[12]](#cite_note-jellies-12)
* Chance of obtaining extra [Cinder Shards](/Cinder_Shard "Cinder Shard"), [Bone Fragments](/Bone_Fragment "Bone Fragment"), [Clay](/Clay "Clay"), [Stone](/Stone "Stone"), and all [Ore](/Ore "Ore") from stones.[[13]](#cite_note-breakStone-13)
* Weapon [Crit. Chance](/Crit._Chance "Crit. Chance").[[14]](#cite_note-damagemonster-14)
* Chance of activating the [Ring of Yoba](/Ring_of_Yoba "Ring of Yoba") and [Warrior Ring](/Warrior_Ring "Warrior Ring").[[15]](#cite_note-farmer_takedamage-15)[[16]](#cite_note-ring_onmonsterslay-16)
* Chance of finding certain modified monster drops, including [Mahogany Seed](/Mahogany_Seed "Mahogany Seed"), [Galaxy Soul](/Galaxy_Soul "Galaxy Soul"), and [Qi Gem](/Qi_Gem "Qi Gem").[[17]](#cite_note-monsterdrop-17)
