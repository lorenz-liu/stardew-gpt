**Smoked Fish** is an [Artisan Good](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods") made from the [Fish Smoker](/Fish_Smoker "Fish Smoker") using any [fish](/Fish "Fish") and 1 [coal](/Coal "Coal"), taking 50 minutes. It doubles the sell price of the fish while retaining quality, as well as multiplying the [energy](/Energy "Energy") and [health](/Health "Health") restoration by 1.5.

Smoked fish benefit from the [Fisher](/Fishing#Fishing_Skill "Fishing") and [Angler](/Fishing#Fishing_Skill "Fishing") professions, as well as the [Artisan](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming") profession. Thus, if the player does not have the Artisan profession, the Smoked Fish will always sell for 2 times as much as the original fish. If the player has the Artisan profession, the Smoked Fish will always sell for 2.8 times as much as the original fish.

Because Smoked fish is an Artisan Good, it can be sold at [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") but cannot be sold to Willy at the [Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop").
