The **Sweet Gem Berry** is a [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from a [Rare Seed](/Rare_Seed "Rare Seed") after 24 days. It is not considered a [Fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") or [Vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") and cannot be used in a [Keg](/Keg "Keg") or [Preserves Jar](/Preserves_Jar "Preserves Jar"), nor does it benefit from the [Tiller profession](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming").

[Rare Seeds](/Rare_Seed "Rare Seed") can be purchased from the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g in [Spring](/Spring "Spring") & [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), or for data-sort-value="200"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)600–1,000g in any season. A [Seed Maker](/Seed_Maker "Seed Maker") can also produce Rare Seeds from a Sweet Gem Berry.

Despite its high sell price, the berry is regarded as a [neutral gift](/Friendship#Universal_Neutrals "Friendship") by all the NPCs in [Stardew Valley](/Stardew_Valley "Stardew Valley"). When given to [Old Master Cannoli](/Old_Master_Cannoli "Old Master Cannoli") in the [Secret Woods](/Secret_Woods "Secret Woods"), the player receives a [Stardrop](/Stardrop "Stardrop") in return. In [multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer"), all players receive a Stardrop with the gift of a single berry.

## Bundles

Sweet Gem Berry is one of the options for the ![Bundle Teal.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/ce/Bundle_Teal.png/24px-Bundle_Teal.png) [Rare Crops Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Rare_Crops_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Pantry (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Pantry "Remixed Bundles").

## Recipes

Sweet Gem Berry is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Sweet Gem Berry is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![White Turban.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/60/White_Turban.png/24px-White_Turban.png) [White Turban](/White_Turban "White Turban"). It can also be used as a red dye in the dye pots, located at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Sweet Gem Berry is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
