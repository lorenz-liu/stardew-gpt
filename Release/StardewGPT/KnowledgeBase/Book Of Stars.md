The **Book Of Stars** is a skill [book](/Books "Books") that can be purchased from the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") for data-sort-value="15000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)15,000g (25% chance to appear).[[1]](#cite_note-bookseller-1) It is also a possible reward for quests beyond the 5th one from the [Raccoon](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") (20% chance).[[2]](#cite_note-raccoon-2) After the player has received one [Book of Mysteries](/Book_of_Mysteries "Book of Mysteries"), the Book of Stars can be obtained randomly from opening [Mystery Boxes](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") or [Golden Mystery Boxes](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box").[[3]](#cite_note-mysterybox-3)

Upon reading the book, players will earn 250 [XP](/Experience "Experience") in all skills. If they have already reached Level 10 in all skills, they will instead earn 1,125 [Mastery](/Skills#Mastery "Skills") points.[[4]](#cite_note-farmingadjustment-4)

## Bundles

Book of Stars is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Book of Stars is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a purple dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as an iridium dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

Book of Stars is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Trading

One Book of Stars can be traded to the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") for 8 [Fairy Dust](/Fairy_Dust "Fairy Dust").
