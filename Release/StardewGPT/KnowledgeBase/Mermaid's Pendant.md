The **Mermaid's Pendant** is an item used to propose [marriage](/Marriage "Marriage") to a [marriage candidate](/Villagers#Marriage_Candidates "Villagers") and can be purchased from the [Old Mariner](/Old_Mariner "Old Mariner") for data-sort-value="5000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)5,000g. The Old Mariner appears on the beach past the bridge that costs 300 [wood](/Wood "Wood") to repair. He will appear between 6am – 7pm, and only on rainy days. He can appear in [Winter](/Winter "Winter") if a [Rain Totem](/Rain_Totem "Rain Totem") is used, but does not appear during the [Night Market](/Night_Market "Night Market"), even if it is raining.

The following conditions must be met to purchase it:

* Reached a 10-heart [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") level with a marriage candidate, which requires having given them a [bouquet](/Bouquet "Bouquet") at 8 hearts of friendship
* Have purchased and completed construction on the first [farmhouse upgrade](/Farmhouse#Upgrades "Farmhouse")

If the player is already married or has a housemate, the [Old Mariner](/Old_Mariner "Old Mariner") will appear but will not sell a Mermaid's Pendant. If the player gets divorced, they can purchase another one, which can be given to any marriage candidate. If the player is engaged but not yet married, they can return to the [Old Mariner](/Old_Mariner "Old Mariner") and purchase another pendant.

The Mermaid's Pendant cannot be sold.

## Trivia

* The Old Mariner will refer to the pendant as an "old amulet".
* If the player attempts to give a Mermaid's Pendant to anyone while engaged, they will say "You just got engaged! What are you thinking?" or "What? Didn't you just ask someone else to marry you? You're crazy!".
* If the player attempts to give a Mermaid's Pendant to anyone while married, they will say "Um, aren't you already married?" or "You're already married, you weirdo!".
