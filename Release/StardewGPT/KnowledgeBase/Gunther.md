**Gunther** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who operates the [Archaeology Center](/Museum "Museum") in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town").

He doesn't accept [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") gifts, and instead accepts donations to the Museum. He'll reward players as they reach milestones in number of donations.

## Schedule

Gunther doesn't ever leave the museum counter, even when the museum is closed.

## Quests

* The first time entering the Museum with an [artifact](/Artifacts "Artifacts") or [mineral](/Minerals "Minerals") in [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory") will trigger a cutscene where Gunther explains that the previous curator made off with the Museum's entire collection. He asks the player to consider donating any artifacts or minerals they find to the Museum. This triggers the ["Archaeology" Quest](/Quests#Archaeology "Quests"), to donate an item to the museum.

:   The morning after donating 60 items to the Museum, Gunther will visit the player to thank them and give them the [Rusty Key](/Rusty_Key "Rusty Key"). (If it's the player's [wedding day](/Marriage "Marriage"), he will visit the day after.)

* ["Fragments of the past"](/Quests#Fragments_of_the_past "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), Gunther may randomly request that the player collect and deliver 100 bone items to the box in the [Museum](/Museum "Museum"). The reward is data-sort-value="3500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)3,500g and the [Bone Mill](/Bone_Mill "Bone Mill") recipe.
