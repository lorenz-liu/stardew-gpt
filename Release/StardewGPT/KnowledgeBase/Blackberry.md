The **Blackberry** is a [Fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") found via [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") throughout Stardew Valley. It can be found on the ground during [Fall](/Fall "Fall") and on bushes during Blackberry Season ([Fall](/Fall "Fall") 8-11). It can also be grown from [Wild Seeds (Fa)](/Fall_Seeds "Fall Seeds") (also known as [Fall Seeds](/Fall_Seeds "Fall Seeds")), or found in the [Farm Cave](/The_Cave "The Cave") if the fruit bat option was selected. It can also randomly be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during Fall.

## Blackberry Bushes

During blackberry season, the number of berries obtained from each blackberry bush increases as the player's [Foraging Skill](/Foraging#Foraging_Skill "Foraging") increases. At Level 4, two berries will be harvested from each bush instead of one. At Level 8, three berries will be harvested from each bush. If Foraging Skill is temporarily increased to 12 or more with a Foraging [Buff](/Buffs "Buffs") (by consuming [food](/Cooking "Cooking")), then 4 berries will be harvested from each bush. Each bush has a 20% chance to spawn blackberries on the overnight update, plus a small random bonus chance.

Blackberries collected from bushes have several unusual characteristics:

* 1 Foraging XP is granted for each Blackberry collected this way. This XP stacks if multiple Blackberries drop from one bush.
* Unless the [Botanist](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") profession is chosen, bush berries are always normal quality, regardless of the player's Foraging skill. (Botanists always obtain iridium quality, as with all foraged items.)
* Bush berries benefit from the +10% [Tiller](/Skills#Farming "Skills") profession price bonus when sold. If bush berries and standard foraged berries are combined into one stack, the entire stack receives the Tiller bonus. The Tiller bonus will stack with [Bear's Knowledge](/Bear%27s_Knowledge "Bear's Knowledge"), allowing a maximum bonus of ×3.3.

## Tailoring

Blackberry is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [![Shirt079.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/78/Shirt079.png/24px-Shirt079.png)](/File:Shirt079.png) [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a blue dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").
