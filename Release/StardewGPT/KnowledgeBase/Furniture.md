**Furniture** can be purchased from [Robin](/Robin "Robin") at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop") or from the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") merchant. Both shops offer a random selection of furniture each day they are open. The cost of furniture at the Traveling Cart is random, but is always between data-sort-value="furniture"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)250–2,500g. Prices at the Carpenter's Shop do not vary.

Some furniture can be obtained only by donating items to the [Museum](/Museum "Museum"), purchased at [Festivals](/Festivals "Festivals"), the [Casino](/Casino "Casino"), [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart"), or other unique sources. These exceptions are listed in the tables below.

After the first [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") upgrade, [Robin](/Robin "Robin") also offers the [Furniture Catalogue](/Furniture_Catalogue "Furniture Catalogue") for sale at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop"). Once placed, "using" the furniture catalogue allows you to purchase furniture items for data-sort-value="0">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)0g in unlimited quantity.

There is a small chance of finding random furniture items when engaging in various tasks, such as slaying monsters, breaking open crates, shaking or chopping trees, tilling Artifact Spots, or panning.

Most furniture can be placed outside.[[1]](#cite_note-placement-1) However, some items cannot be placed anywhere other than inside the [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Shed](/Shed "Shed"). Furniture will display a green square on tiles where it can be placed. The tile will turn red if the furniture cannot be placed.

Players can sit in [chairs](#Chairs), [benches](#Benches), [couches](#Couches), and [armchairs](#Armchairs).

Furniture cannot be sold through the [Shipping Bin](/Shipping "Shipping") or to any merchant/shop, and cannot be gifted to [villagers](/Villagers "Villagers").

## Controls

* **Computer:** ![Right-Click.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/e2/Right-Click.png/24px-Right-Click.png) Right-click on furniture while selected to rotate or place it, depending on the item. Some furniture can be easily picked up and moved by ![Left-Click.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/bb/Left-Click.png/24px-Left-Click.png) left-clicking.
* **Nintendo Switch:** Use the **Y** button to pick up and place furniture, the **A** button to rotate furniture, and the **R Stick** to choose where to place furniture.
* **Xbox:** Press **X** or **A** to quick-place without placement guide and **X** to remove - it will not place if there’s not enough space. To remove an item, stand in front of it and click **X**. For more control, use the right toggle to move the item around the room and press **X** to place/remove - this displays a faded image of the item and the placement guide to show where it can or cannot be placed. Some items can be rotated using **A** before placing.
* On all systems, some furniture can only be picked up by using a [tool](/Tools "Tools"), or by holding the 'use tool' button (left-click on computer) to remove it once it is placed.

Chairs (and other furniture that the player can sit on) can be used to bypass normal barriers in gameplay. Several existing furniture items' description changed from "Can be placed inside your house." to "Can be placed as decoration."
