**Elliott** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives on the beach south of [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). He's one of the twelve characters available to [marry](/Marriage "Marriage").

During [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), and [Fall](/Fall "Fall"), players must first acquire two hearts of [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") with Elliott before being allowed inside his cabin. Players at any friendship level may enter in [Winter](/Winter "Winter") between 10am and 6pm.

## Schedule

After the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island is unlocked, Elliott may randomly spend the day there. After leaving the Island at 6pm, Elliott will immediately go home to bed. Elliott never visits the Resort on [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days or his checkup day at [Harvey's Clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic").

## Relationships

Elliott lives alone in a cabin on the beach near [Willy's Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop"). He is friends with [Willy](/Willy "Willy") and [Leah](/Leah "Leah"). He will dance with [Leah](/Leah "Leah") at the [Flower Dance](/Flower_Dance "Flower Dance") if neither of them are dancing with the player.

## Gifts

:   *See also: [List of All Gifts](/List_of_All_Gifts "List of All Gifts")*

You can give Elliott up to two gifts per week (plus one on
his
birthday), which will raise or lower
his
[friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") with you. Gifts on
his
birthday (![Fall.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/5/5d/Fall.png/24px-Fall.png) 5 [Fall](/Fall "Fall")) will have 8× effect and show a unique dialogue.  
For loved, liked, or neutral gifts, Elliott responds

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| “ | “I'm honored that you would remember my birthday! Thank you!” |

For disliked or hated gifts, Elliott responds

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| “ | “Another year gone by, another gray hair... I suppose this gift can commemorate my decline.” |

### Four Hearts

![Four Hearts.png](/mediawiki/images/8/80/Four_Hearts.png)

Note: The beverage consumed during the heart event will heal up to 50 [Energy](/Energy "Energy") and 22 [Health](/Health "Health"), and leave the player with the "[Tipsy](/Buffs "Buffs")" buff. |

If [Alex](/Alex "Alex") is the final bachelor you share a Ten-Heart Event with, the Group Ten-Heart Event will be unavoidable as it is triggered immediately afterwards.

| **Details** |
| --- |
| If the player has a [Rabbit's Foot](/Rabbit%27s_Foot "Rabbit's Foot") in inventory, the cutscene will consist of a friendly game of pool. If the player does not have a [Rabbit's Foot](/Rabbit%27s_Foot "Rabbit's Foot") in inventory, all bachelors will express anger about the player dating them all at one time. Regardless of the player's dialogue choice(s), all bachelors will decide to give the player the "cold shoulder" for about a week after the event. They will give angry dialogue when interacted with, and refuse gifts. After about a week, all bachelors will forgive the player, and dialogues return to normal. |

This event will trigger only one time per [save file](/Saves "Saves"). This event will not trigger if you are married or have given a [Wilted Bouquet](/Wilted_Bouquet "Wilted Bouquet") or [Mermaid's Pendant](/Mermaid%27s_Pendant "Mermaid's Pendant") to one of the marriage candidates.

## Marriage

:   *Main article: [Marriage](/Marriage "Marriage")*

Once married, Elliott will move into the [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse"). Like other marriage candidates, he will add his own room to the right of the bedroom. He'll also set up a small garden behind the farmhouse where he'll sometimes go to read.

On rainy mornings and mornings when Elliott stays inside the farmhouse all day, he may offer you [Coffee](/Coffee "Coffee"). On rainy nights, he may offer you dinner: [Baked Fish](/Baked_Fish "Baked Fish"), [Fried Calamari](/Fried_Calamari "Fried Calamari"), [Chowder](/Chowder "Chowder"), or [Fish Stew](/Fish_Stew "Fish Stew"). On New Years' Eve (Winter 28), he'll give you a bottle of [Wine](/Wine "Wine").
