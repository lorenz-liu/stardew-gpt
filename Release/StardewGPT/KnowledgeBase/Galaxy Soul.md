The **Galaxy Soul** is an item used to upgrade Galaxy weapons in the [Forge](/Forge "Forge"). Weapons forged using Galaxy Souls keep their enchantments and gem upgrades.

* Galaxy Souls can be purchased at [Qi's Walnut Room](/Qi%27s_Walnut_Room "Qi's Walnut Room") for data-sort-value="40">![Qi Gem.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/78/Qi_Gem.png/24px-Qi_Gem.png) 40 each.

* After killing at least 50 [Dangerous Monsters](/Monsters#Dangerous_Monsters "Monsters") [[1]](#cite_note-galaxy_soul-1), Galaxy Souls can be purchased from the [Island Trader](/Island_Trader "Island Trader") on the last day of a season for ![Radioactive Bar.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/7e/Radioactive_Bar.png/24px-Radioactive_Bar.png) [Radioactive Bar](/Radioactive_Bar "Radioactive Bar") (10).

* After killing at least 50 [Dangerous Monsters](/Monsters#Dangerous_Monsters "Monsters") [[2]](#cite_note-monsterdrop-2), Galaxy Souls can be dropped by any Dangerous Monster.

* Semi-transparent [Big Slimes](/Slimes "Slimes") containing a Galaxy Soul can be seen in the ["Dangerous" Mines](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") or ["Dangerous" Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern#Skull_Cavern_Invasion "Skull Cavern").[[3]](#cite_note-bigslime-3) The Galaxy Soul is dropped upon killing the Big Slime.

## Forge recipes

:   *See also: [Forge](/Forge#Infinity_Weapons "Forge")*
