The **Geode Crusher** is a piece of [Refining Equipment](/Crafting#Refining_Equipment "Crafting") that can be used to open [geodes](/Minerals#Geodes "Minerals") on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"). The player receives the recipe after completing [Clint](/Clint "Clint")'s [Special Order](/Quests#List_of_Special_Orders "Quests") ["Cave Patrol"](/Quests#Cave_Patrol "Quests"). It takes ![Time Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Time_Icon.png/24px-Time_Icon.png) 60m (1h) to process.[[1]](#cite_note-processtime-1)

The Geode Crusher cannot open [artifact troves](/Artifact_Trove "Artifact Trove"), [golden coconuts](/Golden_Coconut "Golden Coconut"), [mystery boxes](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box"), or [golden mystery boxes](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box").

This item provides an alternative to paying data-sort-value="25">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)25g for Clint [to open](/Blacksmith#Process_Geodes "Blacksmith") 1 geode.
