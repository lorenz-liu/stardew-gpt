**Hay** comes from dried plants and is used to feed farm [animals](/Animals "Animals").

Hay can be harvested from [Grass](/Grass "Grass") or [Wheat](/Wheat "Wheat") using any scythe. It can only be harvested from Grass if there is an unfilled [Silo](/Silo "Silo") on the farm. For grass, when using a [Scythe](/Scythe "Scythe"), there is a 50% chance of producing 1 Hay each time a tile is completely cleared of Grass; using a [Golden Scythe](/Golden_Scythe "Golden Scythe"), the chance is 75% and using an [Iridium Scythe](/Iridium_Scythe "Iridium Scythe") the chance is 100%; using any scythe on wheat, grants a 40% chance for hay. If successfully cut, a [Blue Grass](/Grass#Blue_Grass "Grass") will produce two pieces of hay, instead of the usual one. Note that although Grass can also be cleared using any [Weapon](/Weapons "Weapons") or [Bomb](/Crafting#Bombs "Crafting"), no Hay is produced in these cases. All Hay harvested from Grass is immediately placed directly into the farm's Silos. When harvesting [Wheat](/Wheat "Wheat") using any scythe there is a 40% chance that each harvested Wheat will drop one unit of Hay, which in this case added to the player's inventory (or the [Junimo Hut](/Junimo_Hut "Junimo Hut") storage, if harvested by [Junimos](/Junimos "Junimos")). Hay from Wheat does not go directly into Silos.

Hay may also be obtained by cutting [weeds](/Weeds "Weeds") with a weapon with the [Haymaker enchantment](/Forge#Enchantments "Forge"), which gives a 1/3 chance for each cut weed to produce a Hay. Hay obtained this way goes directly into Silos if cut on the farm, and into the player's inventory if outside of the farm.

Each Silo can store up to 240 pieces of Hay. Hay stored in Silos can only be withdrawn using the [Hoppers](/Hay_Hopper "Hay Hopper") found in [Coops](/Coop "Coop") and [Barns](/Barn "Barn"). When right-clicked, a Hopper will dispense Hay equal to the number of animals living in the building — but no more than the number of empty slots in the building's Feeding Bench. Therefore, it becomes impossible to withdraw Hay from a Hopper in deluxe Coops and Barns where the autofeeder always keeps the feeding bench full (see [Tip](#Tip)). Right-clicking on a Hopper if Hay is the active item in the player's inventory will transfer the entire stack of Hay into the Silos. Once retrieved from the hopper, hay can be transferred to chests for storage from the inventory, bypassing the need for multiple silos.

Hay can be purchased, either from [Marnie's Ranch](/Marnie%27s_Ranch "Marnie's Ranch") for data-sort-value="50">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)50g or from the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") who sells 3 Hay in exchange for 1 [Omni Geode](/Omni_Geode "Omni Geode") on Mondays. [Marnie](/Marnie "Marnie") may randomly send the player 30 Hay in the mail. Marnie also sells Hay at her [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="1">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 1 [Calico Egg](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg"). 100 Hay is sometimes sold at the [Stardew Valley Fair](/Stardew_Valley_Fair "Stardew Valley Fair") for data-sort-value="500">![Token.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/29/Token.png/24px-Token.png)500. Fifteen pieces of hay are provided by Mayor Lewis at the start of the game if the player has chosen the [Meadowlands Farm](/Farm_Maps#Map_Types "Farm Maps").

## Feeding Animals

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/1c/Chickens_fed.png/300px-Chickens_fed.png)](/File:Chickens_fed.png)

The interior of a basic [Coop](/Coop "Coop") showing the locations of the [Hopper](/Hay_Hopper "Hay Hopper") and feeding bench.

All [Coop](/Coop "Coop")-dwelling and [Barn](/Barn "Barn")-dwelling Animals eat 1 Hay per day as [food](/Animals#Food "Animals") whenever there is no fresh [Grass](/Grass "Grass") available. This Hay must manually be placed on the feeding bench in the animal's home building; the [Hopper](/Hay_Hopper "Hay Hopper") in the building facilitates withdrawing Hay from the Silos to place on the bench, although the Hay can come from any source. The autofeeder in Deluxe Barns and Coops automatically fills the feeding bench. If unfed, animals will not die, but they will be [unhappy](/Animals#Mood "Animals").

Given that animals cannot go outside during [Winter](/Winter "Winter"), it is advisable to Harvest as much Hay as possible by [Fall](/Fall "Fall") 28 in preparation for the winter. Building multiple [Silos](/Silo "Silo") may be necessary to provide sufficient storage capacity for Winter. Alternatively, Hay can be withdrawn via a Hopper and placed into Chests. If the farm runs out of stored Hay during the winter, the only ways to feed animals are by purchasing Hay, scything any grass that may be left over from Fall, or by growing [Wheat](/Wheat "Wheat") in the [Greenhouse](/Greenhouse "Greenhouse") or the [Ginger Island Farm](/Ginger_Island#The_Farm "Ginger Island").

## Quests

Hay is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Bundles

10 Hay are used in the ![Bundle Yellow.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2b/Bundle_Yellow.png/24px-Bundle_Yellow.png) [Fodder Bundle](/Bundles#Fodder_Bundle "Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board](/Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Hay is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") to create a [Grass Skirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Grass Skirt.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a4/Grass_Skirt.png/24px-Grass_Skirt.png)](/File:Grass_Skirt.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Tip

* The only way to remove Hay from a Feeding Bench is to use a [Bomb](/Bomb "Bomb"). This makes it possible to withdraw Hay from the building's [Hopper](/Hay_Hopper "Hay Hopper") when it is otherwise impossible, for example, if all buildings contain Autofeeders or if the player's [Spouse](/Marriage "Marriage") has fed the animals.
