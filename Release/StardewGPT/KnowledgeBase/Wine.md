**Wine** is an [Artisan Good](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods") made from the [Keg](/Keg "Keg"), taking 7 days. It can also be received as a gift at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star").

The sell price of Wine depends on the [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") used (3 × Base Fruit Value). When consumed, wine causes a [Tipsy](/Buffs "Buffs") buff that decreases [Speed](/Speed "Speed") by 1. The [energy](/Energy "Energy") and [health](/Health "Health") restored by Wine is equal to 1.75 × Base Fruit Value for edible fruit. For inedible fruit, Wine will restore energy equal to 25% Base Fruit Value and health equal to 11.25% Base Fruit Value.[[1]](#cite_note-wine_energy-1) The color of the Wine also depends on the fruit used.

**Note:** It is possible to acquire Wine that has no base fruit (from the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") or a [Statue Of Endless Fortune](/Statue_Of_Endless_Fortune "Statue Of Endless Fortune"), as a gift at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"), or a New Year's Eve gift from [Leah](/Leah "Leah") or [Elliott](/Elliott "Elliott")). This item is simply titled "Wine", has a selling price of data-sort-value="400">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)400g (or data-sort-value="560">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)560g with the [Artisan Profession](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming")), and restores 50 [energy](/Energy "Energy") and 22 [health](/Health "Health").

## Aged Values

Wine can be placed inside a [Cask](/Cask "Cask") to age from normal quality to silver, gold, and eventually iridium quality. Iridium quality doubles the base sell price of Wine.

Wine takes the longest to age of any item in [Casks](/Cask "Cask"). It takes 2 seasons of aging to go from basic wine to iridium quality wine.

Normal, silver, and gold quality Wine can be prematurely removed from a cask at any time by striking the cask with an [Axe](/Axes "Axes"), [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes"), or [Pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes").

## Bundles

* Wine is used in the ![Bundle Purple.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a1/Bundle_Purple.png/24px-Bundle_Purple.png) [Enchanter's Bundle](/Bundles#Enchanters_Bundle "Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board](/Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Bundles").
* One silver (or better) quality wine is an option for ![Bundle Purple.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a1/Bundle_Purple.png/24px-Bundle_Purple.png) [The Missing Bundle](/Bundles#The_Missing_Bundle "Bundles") in the abandoned [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart").
* Wine is one of the options for the ![Bundle Orange.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/95/Bundle_Orange.png/24px-Bundle_Orange.png) [Brewer's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Brewer.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Pantry (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Pantry "Remixed Bundles").

## Tailoring

Wine can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Black Leather Jacket](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt138.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/e4/Shirt138.png/24px-Shirt138.png)](/File:Shirt138.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a purple dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

One Wine (of any type) may be requested by [Blobfish](/Blobfish "Blobfish") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 5 to 7.
