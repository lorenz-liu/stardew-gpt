**Caroline** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town").

## Schedule

After the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island is unlocked, Caroline may randomly spend the day there. After leaving the Island at 6pm, Caroline will immediately go home to bed. Caroline never visits the Resort on [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days or her checkup day at [Harvey's Clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic").

## Relationships

Caroline is married to [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre") and they live with their daughter [Abigail](/Abigail "Abigail") in [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store").

[Jodi](/Jodi "Jodi") mentions that Caroline is her best friend, and that she is able to tell her anything. The two also attend aerobics class on Tuesdays along with [Marnie](/Marnie "Marnie"), [Emily](/Emily "Emily") and [Robin](/Robin "Robin").

### Two Hearts

![Two Hearts.png](/mediawiki/images/7/7c/Two_Hearts.png)

Enter the sunroom via the door in Caroline's kitchen between 9am and 5pm on a day when it's not raining.

| **Details** |
| --- |
| Sunroom interior  Caroline is excited to show you her new "private" sunroom. She asks what you think of it.   * **It's beautiful!** *(No effect on [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship").)* * **It's very relaxing.** *(No effect on [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship").)* * **It's too hot in here...** *(No effect on [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship").)* * **Not as good as my farm!** *(No effect on [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship").)*   Caroline continues telling you how peaceful her sunroom sanctuary is, and offers you a cup of her home-grown [Green Tea](/Green_Tea "Green Tea"). You can choose "yes" or "no" without penalty. In either case, a surrealistic cutscene plays, after which Caroline says "Feel free to come here and relax any time you want, okay?"  The sunroom is then unlocked and can be visited any time the house is open. Inside is a [Tea Bush](/Tea_Bush "Tea Bush") that can be harvested to obtain [Tea Leaves](/Tea_Leaves "Tea Leaves") once each day, during the last week of any season.  The next day, Caroline sends a letter in the mail with the recipe to make [Tea Saplings](/Tea_Sapling "Tea Sapling"). |

## Quests

* ["Carving Pumpkins"](/Quests#Carving_Pumpkins "Quests"): Caroline will send you a letter on the 19th of [Fall](/Fall "Fall") requesting a [Pumpkin](/Pumpkin "Pumpkin"). The reward is data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* Caroline may randomly request an item at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). The reward is 3x the item's base value and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* ["Island Ingredients"](/Quests#Island_Ingredients "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), Caroline may randomly request that the player grow and ship 100 [Ginger](/Ginger "Ginger"), [Pineapple](/Pineapple "Pineapple"), or [Taro Root](/Taro_Root "Taro Root"). The reward is [gold](/Gold "Gold") equivalent to 50% of the crop's base sell price and the [Solar Panel](/Solar_Panel "Solar Panel") recipe. This special order requires [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") to be unlocked.

## Trivia

* Caroline will send you a recipe for [Parsnip Soup](/Parsnip_Soup "Parsnip Soup"), which is ironically one of her husband's hated gifts.
* Despite disliking [Mayonnaise](/Mayonnaise "Mayonnaise"), [Fish Taco](/Fish_Taco "Fish Taco") is a loved gift.
* Caroline will sometimes say "It's a fine-looking day." followed by "On days like these I like to help [Evelyn](/Evelyn "Evelyn") with the public gardens", regardless of the weather.
  + She is never seen actually helping Evelyn with the gardens, no matter what the weather.
* If Caroline's 2-heart event is triggered during the last week of Spring, Summer, or Fall, the player collects one [Tea Leaves](/Tea_Leaves "Tea Leaves") from the [Tea Bush](/Tea_Bush "Tea Bush") when Caroline harvests it.
* Caroline will compliment [Jodi](/Jodi "Jodi") in the aerobics meet-up, even if [Jodi](/Jodi "Jodi") is gone visiting the Beach Resort on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island").
