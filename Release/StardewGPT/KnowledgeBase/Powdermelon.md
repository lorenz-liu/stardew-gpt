The **Powdermelon** is a [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from [Powdermelon Seeds](/Powdermelon_Seeds "Powdermelon Seeds") after 7 days. It is one of five crops that can grow into a [giant crop](/Crops#Giant_Crops "Crops"). One Powdermelon can sometimes be purchased for data-sort-value="2500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,500g from [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre")'s booth at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star").

## Bundles

Five Powdermelons may be used as one of the options for the ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Winter Star Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Winter_Star "Remixed Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Remixed Bundles").

## Recipes

Powdermelon is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Powdermelon can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create the dyeable ![Farmer Pants.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/78/Farmer_Pants.png/24px-Farmer_Pants.png) [Farmer Pants](/Tailoring#Pants "Tailoring"). It can be used as a white [dye](/Dyeing "Dyeing") when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

Powdermelon is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
