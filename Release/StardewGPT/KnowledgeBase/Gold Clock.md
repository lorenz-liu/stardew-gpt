The **Gold Clock** is a type of [building](/Wizard%27s_Tower#Buildings "Wizard's Tower") purchasable from the [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard") at the [Wizard's Tower](/Wizard%27s_Tower "Wizard's Tower") after finishing the quest "[Goblin Problem](/Quests#Goblin_Problem "Quests")".

After placing the Gold Clock, it can be turned on or off.

Constructing the Gold Clock is necessary to achieve [Perfection](/Perfection "Perfection").

## Behavior

When placed and turned on, the Gold Clock:

* Prevents [debris](/The_Farm#Debris "The Farm") from appearing on [the farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") and the [Ginger Island farm](/Ginger_Island#The_Farm "Ginger Island").
  + [Grass](/Grass "Grass") is not considered debris by the Gold Clock, so it continues to appear randomly.
  + [Trees](/Trees "Trees") no longer randomly appear on the farms, although they can still be manually planted and continue to grow. Existing trees will still randomly drop seeds. Off of the farm, trees continue to sprout automatically as before.
  + [Green Rain](/Weather#Green_Rain "Weather") weeds are not considered debris as they are non-destructive and disappear the next day, so they will continue to spawn during green rain.
* Keeps all [fences](/Crafting#Fences "Crafting") from decaying (including those placed outside the farms), so they never need repair.
* Keeps time with the in-game clock.

When turned off, the Gold Clock's hands are replaced with an image of an hourglass.

## Trivia

* The Gold Clock is the most expensive building available to purchase.
