The **Mango** is a [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") produced each day by a [Mango Tree](/Mango_Tree "Mango Tree") Tree during its harvest season. A Mango Tree on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") in Stardew Valley produces fruit during [Summer](/Summer "Summer"). On [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") or in the [Greenhouse](/Greenhouse "Greenhouse"), it produces fruit year-round.

The [Island Trader](/Island_Trader "Island Trader") exchanges one ['Volcano' Photo](/%27Volcano%27_Photo "'Volcano' Photo") for 5 Mangoes on [Thursdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Thursday "Days of the Week").

## Tailoring

Mango is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a dyeable ![Relaxed Fit Pants.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/1e/Relaxed_Fit_Pants.png/24px-Relaxed_Fit_Pants.png) [Relaxed Fit Pants](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a yellow dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the yellow dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").
