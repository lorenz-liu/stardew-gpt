The **Pumpkin** is a [vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from [Pumpkin Seeds](/Pumpkin_Seeds "Pumpkin Seeds") after 13 days. Pumpkin is one of only five crops that can grow into a [giant crop](/Crops#Giant_Crops "Crops"). After [Starfruit](/Starfruit "Starfruit"), it has the second-highest per unit base price of all the normal crops.

## Tailoring

Pumpkin is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a dyeable ![Shirt140.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/f9/Shirt140.png/24px-Shirt140.png) [Button Down Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is an orange dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the orange dye pot at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

* [Caroline](/Caroline "Caroline") requests a pumpkin by mail on the 19th of [Fall](/Fall "Fall") in the ["Carving Pumpkins" Quest](/Quests#Carving_Pumpkins "Quests"). The reward is data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* Pumpkin may be randomly requested in [Fall](/Fall "Fall") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="960">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)960g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
