The **Milk Pail** is a [tool](/Tools "Tools") used to gather [milk](/Milk "Milk") from [cows](/Cow "Cow") and [goats](/Goat "Goat"). It must be purchased from [Marnie's Ranch](/Marnie%27s_Ranch "Marnie's Ranch") for data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g.

Each use of the Milk Pail to milk a cow or goat consumes 4 points of [energy](/Energy "Energy"), and increases the player's [Farming](/Farming "Farming") experience by 5 points.

Using the Milk Pail on an animal other than a cow or goat will produce the message "[Animal name] doesn't produce milk."

Once the player has reached [Farming Level 10](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming"), an [Auto-Grabber](/Auto-Grabber "Auto-Grabber") can be purchased from Marnie's Ranch for data-sort-value="25000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)25,000g, which will automatically milk all cows and goats from the [barn](/Barn "Barn") in which it is placed.
