The **Raccoon Journal** is a power [book](/Books "Books") that can be obtained as the reward for the [second request](/Giant_Stump#Raccoon.27s_Requests "Giant Stump") from the Raccoon family at the Giant Stump. After fulfilling that request, 999 [Fiber](/Fiber "Fiber") can be traded for one Raccoon Journal at the [Raccoon Wife's Shop](/Giant_Stump#Raccoon_Wife.27s_Shop "Giant Stump"). It can also be purchased from the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") for data-sort-value="20000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)20,000g starting in Year 3 (≈9% chance to appear).[[1]](#cite_note-y3chance-1)

The first reading grants the player a power that increases the chances to receive [Mixed Seeds](/Mixed_Seeds "Mixed Seeds") or [Mixed Flower Seeds](/Mixed_Flower_Seeds "Mixed Flower Seeds") from cutting [Weeds](/Weeds "Weeds") by 4%.[[2]](#cite_note-weedchance-2) Once read, it can be found in [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s menu on the [Special Items & Powers](/Special_Items_%26_Powers "Special Items & Powers") tab. Each subsequent reading gives the player 100 [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") [XP](/Skills "Skills").

## Bundles

Raccoon Journal is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Raccoon Journal is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a green dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a green dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.
