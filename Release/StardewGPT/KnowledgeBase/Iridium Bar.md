An **Iridium Bar** is created primarily by smelting 5 ![Iridium Ore.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/e9/Iridium_Ore.png/24px-Iridium_Ore.png) [Iridium Ore](/Iridium_Ore "Iridium Ore") and 1 ![Coal.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a7/Coal.png/24px-Coal.png) [Coal](/Coal "Coal") in a [Furnace](/Furnace "Furnace"). It can also be obtained in a [variety of other ways](#Locations).

An Iridium Bar can be [shipped](/Shipping "Shipping") or sold to [Clint](/Clint "Clint") at the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith") Shop for data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g, or data-sort-value="1500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,500g with the [Blacksmith Profession](/Skills#Mining "Skills").

Iridium Bars can be used for upgrading tools, crafting, building and trading. 3 Iridium Bars are used to re-forge [Trinkets](/Trinkets "Trinkets").

## Locations

Aside from smelting Iridium Ore and Coal in a Furnace, Iridium Bars can be found in:

* 0.9% chance from Purple Slimes.
* 0.2% chance from [Shadow Shaman](/Shadow_Shaman "Shadow Shaman") and [Shadow Brutes](/Shadow_Brute "Shadow Brute").
* 0.8% chance from [Iridium Bats](/Bats "Bats").
* 3% chance from [Iridium Golems](/Iridium_Golem "Iridium Golem") on the [Wilderness Farm map](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps") (rolled twice).
* 7% chance for 1-5 to be found in [Golden Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Golden_Treasure_Chests "Fishing").[[1]](#cite_note-chestchance-1)
* 3.6% chance for 2-3 to be found in [Skull Cavern treasure rooms](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern").
* 25% chance from the [Statue Of Endless Fortune](/Statue_Of_Endless_Fortune "Statue Of Endless Fortune") each day it is not a villager's birthday.
* Obtained as a prize from the [Prize Machine](/Mayor%27s_Manor#Prize_Machine "Mayor's Manor").
* Sold by the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="1000"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)3,000–5,000g.
* Received as a gift from [Clint](/Clint "Clint") at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star").
* 2 are sold by [Maru](/Maru "Maru") in her [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="50">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 50 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg") each.

## Bundles

Iridium Bar may be an option in the ![Bundle Teal.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/ce/Bundle_Teal.png/24px-Bundle_Teal.png) [remixed Dye Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Dye_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board](/Remixed_Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Remixed Bundles").

## Tailoring

An Iridium Bar is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create an ![Shirt151.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3d/Shirt151.png/24px-Shirt151.png) [Iridium Breastplate](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is an iridium dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the purple dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

* The [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard") requests an Iridium Bar by mail on the 5th of [Winter](/Winter "Winter") (year 2) in the ["Staff of Power" Quest](/Quests#Staff_of_Power "Quests"). The reward is data-sort-value="5000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)5,000g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* 1 Iridium Bar is requested by [Lava Eel](/Lava_Eel "Lava Eel") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 7 to 10.
* [Willy's Boat](/Fish_Shop#Willy.27s_Boat "Fish Shop") needs 5 Iridium Bars to repair the anchor.

## Trading

* 10 Iridium Bars can be traded at the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for the [Warp Totem: Desert](/Warp_Totem:_Desert "Warp Totem: Desert") recipe.
* 15 Iridium Bars can be traded at the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for the [Midnight Beach Bed](/Midnight_Beach_Bed "Midnight Beach Bed").
* 30 Iridium Bars can be traded at the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for the [Midnight Beach Double Bed](/Midnight_Beach_Double_Bed "Midnight Beach Double Bed").

## Trivia

* In real life, iridium is a rare silver, brittle metal that can be hazardous in a fine dust and has toxic radioactive isotopes. It is often used to produce hard, durable surfaces for high-precision tools and machine parts. It is the second densest naturally occurring element, just behind osmium, and almost twice as dense as lead.
