The **Secret Woods** is an outdoor area that can be entered from the northwest corner of [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest"). The entrance is initially blocked by a [large log](/Large_Log "Large Log") that can be cleared after upgrading to at least a [Steel Axe](/Axes "Axes").

The Secret Woods contain seasonal [forageables](/Foraging "Foraging") and [Slimes](/Slimes "Slimes") that will attack the player. Slimes here have a chance of dropping [Mahogany Seeds](/Mahogany_Seed "Mahogany Seed") when slain.

The Secret Woods contain 6 [Large Stumps](/Large_Stump "Large Stump") that regrow daily and can be harvested for a total of 12 [Hardwood](/Hardwood "Hardwood") each day. Four stumps are in the upper part of the woods, and two are found by walking through one of two covered passages to a smaller lower area. One passage is through the trees slightly southeast of the upper set of three stumps, and the other is south of the entrance to the woods, approximately 2-3 tiles to the right of a bush. The former passage is a straight line, but the latter requires the player to walk south, then west, then south again.

## Bear's Knowledge

After reading [Secret Note #23](/Secret_Notes#Secret_Note_.2323 "Secret Notes") the ["Strange Note"](/Quests#Strange_Note "Quests") quest will activate. Entering the Secret Woods with [Maple Syrup](/Maple_Syrup "Maple Syrup") in [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory") between 6am and 7pm with this quest active while its sunny will result in a cutscene where the player encounters a grizzly bear who takes the maple syrup and thanks the player by sharing his special knowledge of berries. As a result, [Bear's Knowledge](/Bear%27s_Knowledge "Bear's Knowledge") is obtained, which permanently increases the sell price of [blackberries](/Blackberry "Blackberry") and [salmonberries](/Salmonberry "Salmonberry") by 3x.

## Old Master Cannoli

[Old Master Cannoli](/Old_Master_Cannoli "Old Master Cannoli") is a statue in the Secret Woods. When interacted with, it will give the description:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| “ | “--Old Master Cannoli-- ...Still searching for the sweetest taste...” |

If the statue is given a [Sweet Gem Berry](/Sweet_Gem_Berry "Sweet Gem Berry"), a [Stardrop](/Stardrop "Stardrop") is received in return. This will only occur once. In multiplayer games, only one Sweet Gem Berry is needed for all players to receive a Stardrop.

## Lost Items Shop

If a player has unlocked a unique item and then lost it, a secret shop appears overnight behind the fallen column to buy it back. The shop sells items that can't be re-obtained any other way. In [multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer"), any player can purchase an item that was lost (not only the player that lost theirs).

All items are sold for data-sort-value="10000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)10,000g.

## Foraging

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/7f/SecretWoodsForageLocations.png/400px-SecretWoodsForageLocations.png)](/File:SecretWoodsForageLocations.png)

Locations in the Secret Woods where forageable items can spawn.

[Forageable](/Foraging "Foraging") items found on the ground in the Secret Woods are:[[1]](#cite_note-forage_chance-1)

* In [Spring](/Spring "Spring"): ![Wild Horseradish.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/90/Wild_Horseradish.png/24px-Wild_Horseradish.png) [Wild Horseradish](/Wild_Horseradish "Wild Horseradish") (52%), ![Morel.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/b1/Morel.png/24px-Morel.png) [Morel](/Morel "Morel") (32%), and ![Common Mushroom.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2e/Common_Mushroom.png/24px-Common_Mushroom.png) [Common Mushroom](/Common_Mushroom "Common Mushroom") (16%)
* In [Summer](/Summer "Summer"): ![Fiddlehead Fern.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/48/Fiddlehead_Fern.png/24px-Fiddlehead_Fern.png) [Fiddlehead Fern](/Fiddlehead_Fern "Fiddlehead Fern") (78%), and ![Red Mushroom.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/e1/Red_Mushroom.png/24px-Red_Mushroom.png) [Red Mushroom](/Red_Mushroom "Red Mushroom") (22%)
* In [Fall](/Fall "Fall"): ![Common Mushroom.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2e/Common_Mushroom.png/24px-Common_Mushroom.png) [Common Mushroom](/Common_Mushroom "Common Mushroom") (46%), ![Chanterelle.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/1d/Chanterelle.png/24px-Chanterelle.png) [Chanterelle](/Chanterelle "Chanterelle") (38%), and ![Red Mushroom.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/e1/Red_Mushroom.png/24px-Red_Mushroom.png) [Red Mushroom](/Red_Mushroom "Red Mushroom") (15%)
* In [Winter](/Winter "Winter"): ![Holly.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/b8/Holly.png/24px-Holly.png) [Holly](/Holly "Holly") (100%)

Forageable items spawn at an average rate of 0.8-1.4 per night, depending upon the season: 0.9 in spring, 1.0 in summer, 0.8 in fall, and 1.4 winter.[[2]](#cite_note-forage_spawn-2) The map shows the possible locations as red tiles.

## Artifact Spots

No [Artifacts](/Artifacts "Artifacts") are possible when digging up [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") at the Secret Woods. Possible items are:

* 1-3 ![Clay.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a2/Clay.png/24px-Clay.png) [Clay](/Clay "Clay") (30-60%[[3]](#cite_note-artifact_extra-3))
* ![Lost Book.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/f3/Lost_Book.png/24px-Lost_Book.png) [Lost Book](/Lost_Books "Lost Books") (20%); once all Lost Books have been found, any potential Lost Book is replaced by ![Mixed Seeds.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2e/Mixed_Seeds.png/24px-Mixed_Seeds.png) [Mixed Seeds](/Mixed_Seeds "Mixed Seeds").
* Only in [Winter](/Winter "Winter"): ![Winter Root.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/11/Winter_Root.png/24px-Winter_Root.png) [Winter Root](/Winter_Root "Winter Root") (24%)
* Only in [Winter](/Winter "Winter"): ![Snow Yam.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Snow_Yam.png/24px-Snow_Yam.png) [Snow Yam](/Snow_Yam "Snow Yam") (16%)
* 1-3 ![Stone.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/d4/Stone.png/24px-Stone.png) [Stone](/Stone "Stone") (10-20%[[3]](#cite_note-artifact_extra-3))
* Only in [Spring](/Spring "Spring"): 2-5 ![Rice Shoot.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/84/Rice_Shoot.png/24px-Rice_Shoot.png) [Rice Shoot](/Rice_Shoot "Rice Shoot")s (5%)
* An unseen ![Secret Note.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/ec/Secret_Note.png/24px-Secret_Note.png) [Secret Note](/Secret_Notes "Secret Notes"): up to 5% chance, only if the player has a [Magnifying Glass](/Magnifying_Glass "Magnifying Glass").

Most of the year, there are only 8 tiles (near the pond) where artifact spots are possible, making them very rare, with an average rate of 0.01 per night. In winter the average rate increases to 0.7 per night.[[4]](#cite_note-artifact_spawn-4)

## Villager Paths

No [villagers](/Villagers "Villagers") walk through the Secret Woods, so items can be [safely placed](/Crafting#Crafted_goods_outside_the_farm "Crafting") in any location.

## Bugs

* With the addition of the ability to sit on [chairs](/Furniture#Chairs "Furniture") in [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"), it is possible to enter the Secret Woods without needing the [Steel Axe](/Axes "Axes") upgrade. Stand directly below the [large log](/Large_Log "Large Log") that blocks the path. Then place a chair on the other side of it. With the chair in place, it's possible to sit on it, effectively allowing the player to hop over the otherwise impassable obstruction after standing up again. The Secret Woods can be exited again using the same trick, by placing the chair on the [forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") side of the large log. The player can also use a [bench](/Furniture#Benches "Furniture") to enter, but cannot exit with one.
* Placing hats that can appear in the Lost Items Shop on the Alien Rarecrow will cause them to appear in the Lost Items Shop, even though they are not lost.
