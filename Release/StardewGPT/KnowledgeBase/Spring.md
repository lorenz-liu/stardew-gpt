# Spring

[![Spring icon](/mediawiki/images/9/9c/Spring.png)](/File:Spring.png)

**Spring** is the [season](/Seasons "Seasons") when players first begin the game. It's followed by [Summer](/Summer "Summer") and preceded by [Winter](/Winter "Winter").

In Spring, the [grass](/Grass "Grass") is green. [Trees](/Trees "Trees") regain their leaves, and pink petals blow through the air on [windy](/Weather "Weather") days. There is also a variety of [crops](/Crops "Crops"), [forageable items](/Foraging "Foraging"), and [fish](/Fish "Fish") which appear only during this [season](/Seasons "Seasons"). [Salmonberry](/Salmonberry "Salmonberry") season lasts between the 15th-18th of Spring. When Spring starts, new logs, rocks, and grass patches will spawn on the farm, in [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest"), and [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town").
