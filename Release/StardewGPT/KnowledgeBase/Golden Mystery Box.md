**Golden Mystery Boxes** are drop crates unlocked after claiming [Foraging Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave") and viewing the cutscene with [Mr. Qi](/Mr._Qi "Mr. Qi") informing the player that [Mystery Boxes](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") can now be found. They are similar to normal [Mystery Boxes](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box"), and they can be obtained in a variety of ways. With the exception of the [Golden Animal Cracker](/Golden_Animal_Cracker "Golden Animal Cracker") and [Auto-Petter](/Auto-Petter "Auto-Petter"), every item in a Golden Mystery Box can also be found in a Mystery Box.

Golden Mystery Boxes can be cracked by [Clint](/Clint "Clint") in the [Blacksmith shop](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith") for data-sort-value="25">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)25g, similar to [Geodes](/Minerals#Geodes "Minerals"). However, unlike Geodes, they cannot be broken open using a [Geode Crusher](/Geode_Crusher "Geode Crusher").

## Locations

The following table lists the different actions that can spawn a Golden Mystery Box.

The "Chance" column below indicates the probabilities that the game will *attempt* to spawn a Golden Mystery Box. When such an attempt is made, the game performs a further check to see if it succeeds. If the player does not have [Foraging Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave"), the check will fail. Otherwise, the probability of succeeding is 66% if the player does not have the power obtained from the [Book of Mysteries](/Book_of_Mysteries "Book of Mysteries") and 88% if they do.[[1]](#cite_note-rollMysteryBox-1)

Also note that the chances of obtaining Golden Mystery Boxes from some of the sources depends on and increases with higher daily [luck](/Luck "Luck"), and in some cases, with [Luck Buffs](/Luck#Luck_Buffs "Luck").

| Activity | Chance |
| --- | --- |
| Regular [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") [[2]](#cite_note-fishing-2) | 9-17% (dependent on daily luck) |
| [Golden Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Golden_Treasure_Chests "Fishing") [[2]](#cite_note-fishing-2) | 12-21% (dependent on daily luck) |
| [Panning](/Pans "Pans") [[3]](#cite_note-panning-3) | 5% |
| Shaking [trees](/Trees "Trees") with seeds [[4]](#cite_note-trees-4) | 3% |
| [Monsters](/Monsters "Monsters") [[5]](#cite_note-monsters-5) | `0.01 + (Daily Luck / 10) + (Luck Buffs / 125)` |
| Breaking containers in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), and [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon") [[6]](#cite_note-containers-6) | `0.0081 + (Daily Luck / 15)` |
| Chopping [trees](/Trees "Trees") (Each Axe hit) [[7]](#cite_note-axe-7) | 0.5% |

In addition, Golden Mystery Boxes can be obtained by trading 3 [Mystery Boxes](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump"). There is also a 3.6% chance to obtain 5 Golden Mystery Boxes from a [treasure chest](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern") in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") if Foraging Mastery has been claimed. This chance is not subject to an additional check for success, but it is slightly increased if Farming Mastery and/or Combat Mastery have not been claimed.[[8]](#cite_note-skullcavern-8)

## Bundles

Golden Mystery Box is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Golden Mystery Box is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a gold dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

Golden Mystery Box is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Trading

Twenty Golden Mystery Boxes can be traded to the [Raccoon's wife](/Giant_Stump#Raccoon_Wife.27s_Shop "Giant Stump") for 1 [Magic Rock Candy](/Magic_Rock_Candy "Magic Rock Candy"), after the [sixth request](/Giant_Stump#Raccoon.27s_Requests "Giant Stump") from the Raccoon family is completed and [Foraging Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave") is claimed.
