The **Wizard**, also known as **M. Rasmodius**, is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives in the [Wizard's Tower](/Wizard%27s_Tower "Wizard's Tower") to the far west of [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest").

## Schedule

The Wizard does not leave the [Wizard's Tower](/Wizard%27s_Tower "Wizard's Tower") (except for certain [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days), and can be found there when the tower is accessible (6am to 11pm). After the player has unlocked the [Witch's Hut](/Witch%27s_Hut "Witch's Hut"), he can be found in his tower after 11pm by using the warp spot in the [Witch's Hut](/Witch%27s_Hut "Witch's Hut"). The Wizard never visits the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island.

## Quests

* ["Meet the Wizard"](/Quests#Meet_the_Wizard "Quests"): The morning after examining the golden scroll in the [Crafts Room](/Bundles#Crafts_Room "Bundles") of the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center"), the Wizard will send you a letter saying he has information for you. Visit the [Wizard's Tower](/Wizard%27s_Tower "Wizard's Tower") and he will give you a potion that allows you to read the language of the [Junimos](/Junimos "Junimos").
* ["A Dark Reagent"](/Quests#A_Dark_Reagent "Quests"): The Wizard will send you a letter on the 12th of [Winter](/Winter "Winter") requesting that you bring him a [Void Essence](/Void_Essence "Void Essence"). The reward is data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* ["Staff of Power"](/Quests#Staff_of_Power "Quests"): The Wizard will send you a letter on the 5th of Winter, year 2, requesting that you bring him an [Iridium Bar](/Iridium_Bar "Iridium Bar"). The reward is data-sort-value="5000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)5,000g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* ["Dark Talisman"](/Quests#Dark_Talisman "Quests"): After completing the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") [Bundles](/Bundles "Bundles") or the [Joja Community Development Form](/Joja_Community_Development_Form "Joja Community Development Form"), trigger a cutscene at the [Railroad](/Railroad "Railroad"), where the Wizard will request that you find a [Dark Talisman](/Dark_Talisman "Dark Talisman"). Speak to [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") in [The Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers"), who will remove the force-field blocking the entrance to the [Mutant Bug Lair](/Mutant_Bug_Lair "Mutant Bug Lair"). Obtain the [Dark Talisman](/Dark_Talisman "Dark Talisman") from a chest in the Mutant Bug Lair, then bring it back to the Railroad area and place it on the artifact blocking the cave. Inside the cave is a teleportation rune which leads to the [Witch's Swamp](/Witch%27s_Swamp "Witch's Swamp"). This unlocks [The "Goblin Problem" Quest](/Quests#Goblin_Problem "Quests").
* ["Goblin Problem"](/Quests#Goblin_Problem "Quests"): Once inside the [Witch's Swamp](/Witch%27s_Swamp "Witch's Swamp"), the Witch's [Henchman](/Henchman "Henchman") will be blocking the way to the [Witch's Hut](/Witch%27s_Hut "Witch's Hut"). He will only allow the player to pass if he is given [Void Mayonnaise](/Void_Mayonnaise "Void Mayonnaise"), which completes the quest [Goblin Problem](/Quests#Goblin_Problem "Quests") and gives access to the Dark Shrines inside the Witch's Hut. Recover the [Magic Ink](/Magic_Ink "Magic Ink") inside the Witch's Hut, then bring the ink back to the Wizard, who will unlock six [Wizard buildings](/Wizard%27s_Tower "Wizard's Tower") for [The Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm").
* The Wizard may request that you slay a certain number of [Monsters](/Monsters "Monsters") or bring him a random item at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). The reward for slaying monsters varies by the number/type of monster, but is always an amount of gold. The reward for an item delivery quest is 3x the item's base value and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* The Wizard is to be gifted the [Arctic Shard](/Quests#Arctic_Shard "Quests") in [The Pirate's Wife](/Quests#The_Pirate.27s_Wife "Quests") quest.
* ["A Curious Substance"](/Quests#A_Curious_Substance "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), the Wizard may randomly request that the player obtain and bring him an [Ectoplasm](/Ectoplasm "Ectoplasm"). The reward is data-sort-value="2500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,500g and the [Mini-Obelisk](/Mini-Obelisk "Mini-Obelisk") recipe.
* ["Prismatic Jelly"](/Quests#Prismatic_Jelly "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), the Wizard may randomly request that the player obtain and bring him a [Prismatic Jelly](/Prismatic_Jelly "Prismatic Jelly"). The reward is data-sort-value="5000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)5,000g and the [Monster Musk](/Monster_Musk "Monster Musk") recipe.

## Trivia

* Despite being unreachable in most festivals, the Wizard has unique dialogue for every festival, some with backstory on the events.
* The Wizard's shirt can be tailored with 1 [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") and 1 [Void Essence](/Void_Essence "Void Essence"), creating the [Arcane Shirt](/Tailoring#Shirts "Tailoring").
