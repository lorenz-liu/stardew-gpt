The **Rare Disc** is an [Artifact](/Artifacts "Artifacts") that can be obtained from:

* Dropped by a [Bat](/Bats "Bats") (regular, frost, or lava).
* Dropped by a [Shadow Brute](/Shadow_Brute "Shadow Brute").
* Dropped by a [Shadow Shaman](/Shadow_Shaman "Shadow Shaman").
* Dropped by a [Shadow Sniper](/Shadow_Sniper "Shadow Sniper").
* Dropped by a [Spider](/Spider "Spider").
* Gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") dog with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals").
* Inside a [Fishing Treasure Chest](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing").
* Inside an [Artifact Trove](/Artifact_Trove "Artifact Trove").

## Donation

Donation of this item contributes to the total count of donations for the [Museum](/Museum "Museum"). To earn the ['Burnt Offering'](/%27Burnt_Offering%27 "'Burnt Offering'") reward, you must donate a Rare Disc, a [Dwarf Gadget](/Dwarf_Gadget "Dwarf Gadget"), and 11+ artifacts.
