**Maple Bar** is a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit"). It may rotate into stock at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") and is sold for data-sort-value="300"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)900–1,500g. It can be purchased from [Sam](/Sam "Sam")'s [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="20">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 20 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg"). One Maple Bar may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box").

## Bundles

Maple Bar is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Maple Bar is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Dirt Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt275.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/e6/Shirt275.png/24px-Shirt275.png)](/File:Shirt275.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Maple Bar is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
