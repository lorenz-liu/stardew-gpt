The **Qi Fruit** is a [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from [Qi Beans](/Qi_Bean "Qi Bean") after 4 days. This fruit is only available during the ["Qi's Crop" quest](/Quests#Qi.27s_Crop "Quests"). Qi Fruit is one of only five crops that can grow into a [giant crop](/Crops#Giant_Crops "Crops").

All Qi Fruit and [Qi Beans](/Qi_Bean "Qi Bean") despawn when the quest is over (*i.e.,* the day after the player ships them or the quest timer ends), including growing crops, all players' inventories, [Chests](/Chest "Chest"), [Preserves Jars](/Preserves_Jar "Preserves Jar"), [Kegs](/Keg "Keg"), and [Marlon](/Marlon "Marlon")'s [Item Recovery Service](/Adventurer%27s_Guild#Item_Recovery_Service "Adventurer's Guild") list.

Giant Qi Fruit do not disappear when the quest is over, and they can be harvested during future quests to yield more Qi Fruit. If a player chops down a giant Qi Fruit when the "Qi's Crop" quest is not active, then they will receive one [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") instead of fruit. The first time this is done, [Mr. Qi](/Mr._Qi "Mr. Qi") will send a taunting message to the player.

Qi Fruit is always normal quality.

## Bundles

Qi Fruit is not used in any bundles.

## Quests

* [Mr. Qi](/Mr._Qi "Mr. Qi") requests the player to ship 500 Qi Fruit in 28 days in the ["Qi's Crop" quest](/Quests#Qi.27s_Crop "Quests"). The reward is data-sort-value="100">![Qi Gem.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/78/Qi_Gem.png/24px-Qi_Gem.png) 100.

## Tailoring

Qi Fruit is used in the spool of [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Qi Mask.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/dd/Qi_Mask.png/24px-Qi_Mask.png) [Qi Mask](/Qi_Mask "Qi Mask"). It can be placed in the blue dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Notes

* Although Qi Fruit benefits from the [Tiller Profession](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming"), its sell price with the extra 10% is data-sort-value="1.1">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1.1g, which is truncated to data-sort-value="1">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1g in the game code.
* Qi Fruit [jelly](/Jellies_and_Pickles "Jellies and Pickles") and [wine](/Wine "Wine") do not despawn after the quest is completed.
