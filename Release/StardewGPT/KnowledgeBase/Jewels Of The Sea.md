**Jewels Of The Sea** is a power [book](/Books "Books") that can be obtained from [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") after reaching [Fishing level 5](/Fishing#Fishing_Skill "Fishing"). The chance for a treasure chest to contain the book starts at 2% and increases by 0.1% for each treasure chest opened until it is obtained. Then the chance stays at 2.1% permanently. It cannot appear until the player has opened at least 3 fishing treasure chests.[[1]](#cite_note-chance-1)

It can also be purchased from the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") for data-sort-value="20000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)20,000g starting in Year 3 (≈9% chance to appear).[[2]](#cite_note-y3chance-2)

The first reading grants the player a power that makes all fishing treasure chests have a 25% chance to yield 1-6 [Roe](/Roe "Roe").[[1]](#cite_note-chance-1) The type of roe obtained is the same as the fish that was caught. Once read, it can be found in [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s menu on the [Special Items & Powers](/Special_Items_%26_Powers "Special Items & Powers") tab. Each subsequent reading gives the player 100 [Fishing](/Fishing "Fishing") [XP](/Skills "Skills").

## Bundles

Jewels Of The Sea is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Jewels Of The Sea is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as an orange dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

Jewels Of The Sea is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Trading

One Jewels Of The Sea can be traded to the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") for either 3 [Cave Jelly](/Cave_Jelly "Cave Jelly"), 3 [River Jelly](/River_Jelly "River Jelly"), or 3 [Sea Jelly](/Sea_Jelly "Sea Jelly"), provided the player has already obtained the power from this book.

## Trivia

* The power from this book can result in the player receiving types of roe which are otherwise unobtainable, namely [Squid](/Squid "Squid") and [Midnight Squid](/Midnight_Squid "Midnight Squid") roes.
