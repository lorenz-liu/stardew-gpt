The **Ostrich** is an [animal](/Animals "Animals") that lives in a [Barn](/Barn "Barn"). Ostriches can be hatched by placing an [Ostrich Egg](/Ostrich_Egg "Ostrich Egg") into an [Ostrich Incubator](/Ostrich_Incubator "Ostrich Incubator"), taking ![Time Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Time_Icon.png/24px-Time_Icon.png) 15,000m (9d 10h) to incubate, or ![Time Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Time_Icon.png/24px-Time_Icon.png) 7,500m (4d 18h 20m) with the [Coopmaster Profession](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming").

An Ostrich Egg can be initially found by solving [Journal Scrap #10](/Journal_Scraps#Journal_Scrap_.2310 "Journal Scraps"), or as a random drop from the chests in the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon").

## Produce

:   *Main article: [Animals#Produce](/Animals#Produce "Animals")*

Ostriches who eat every day mature after 7 nights have passed. A mature and fed Ostrich produces an [Ostrich Egg](/Ostrich_Egg "Ostrich Egg") every 7 days. An Ostrich Egg can be placed in an [Ostrich Incubator](/Ostrich_Incubator "Ostrich Incubator") to hatch another ostrich, or sold for data-sort-value="600">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)600g.

An Ostrich Egg can be placed in a [Mayonnaise Machine](/Mayonnaise_Machine "Mayonnaise Machine") to produce 10 [Mayonnaise](/Mayonnaise "Mayonnaise") of the same quality as the egg.

## Selling

It is possible to sell Ostriches. The selling price is dependent on how many [hearts](/Animals#Friendship "Animals") the animal has (data-sort-value="20800">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)20,800g max).

To sell a coop or barn animal, right-click the animal after petting. The friendship/mood interface appears. Move the cursor over the gold coin button at the right to see how much you can sell the animal for. Click that button and the confirmation that follows to complete the sale.
