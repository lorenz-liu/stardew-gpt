# Animal Products Profitability

Not all processing of [animal products](/Category:Animal_Products "Category:Animal Products") results in a price gain. This page lists the profitability of processing animal products at different quality levels and [Farming Professions](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming").

Note that regular chicken [eggs](/Egg "Egg") always produce normal quality [Mayonnaise](/Mayonnaise "Mayonnaise") and [Large Eggs](/Large_Egg "Large Egg") produce gold quality mayonnaise. [Duck Mayonnaise](/Duck_Mayonnaise "Duck Mayonnaise"), [Void Mayonnaise](/Void_Mayonnaise "Void Mayonnaise"), and [Dinosaur Mayonnaise](/Dinosaur_Mayonnaise "Dinosaur Mayonnaise") are always normal quality. The quality of these eggs is not a factor. However, [Ostrich Eggs](/Ostrich_Egg "Ostrich Egg") produce 10 jars of [Mayonnaise](/Mayonnaise "Mayonnaise"), where the quality of the Mayonnaise will be equal to the quality of the Ostrich Egg used.

Similarly, normal sized [Milk](/Milk "Milk") always produces normal quality [Cheese](/Cheese "Cheese") and [Large Milk](/Large_Milk "Large Milk") always produces gold quality Cheese. Normal sized [Goat Milk](/Goat_Milk "Goat Milk") always produces normal quality [Goat Cheese](/Goat_Cheese "Goat Cheese") and [Large Goat Milk](/Large_Goat_Milk "Large Goat Milk") always produces gold quality Goat Cheese. The quality of the milk is not a factor.

When using quality [Wool](/Wool "Wool") with a [Loom](/Loom "Loom"), there is a chance of receiving two [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth"):

* ![Silver Quality.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/eb/Silver_Quality.png/18px-Silver_Quality.png) Silver quality wool = 10% chance
* ![Gold Quality.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/9f/Gold_Quality.png/18px-Gold_Quality.png) Gold quality wool = 50% chance
* ![Iridium Quality.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/bc/Iridium_Quality.png/18px-Iridium_Quality.png) Iridium quality wool = 100% chance

Mayonnaise produced using Ostrich Eggs and Cloth are the only artisan goods in the game that are actually affected by the quality level of the input.

## When to process animal products

Below is a quick reference table indicating when to process animal products, without regard to aging in [casks](/Cask "Cask"). Aging cheese or goat cheese in a cask will almost always result in profit. This table also does not take into account the chance of obtaining 2 [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") from 1 [Wool](/Wool "Wool") of gold quality. Obtaining 2 cloth from 1 wool is guaranteed only if the cloth is iridium quality. Looming a gold wool can result in a net loss if only 1 cloth is produced. However, in the long run the chance for extra cloth makes processing wool profitable on average for all quality levels (with iridium wool producing 2 cloth, gold 1.5 on average, and silver 1.1).
