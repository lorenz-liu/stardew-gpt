The **Blueberry** is a [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from [Blueberry Seeds](/Blueberry_Seeds "Blueberry Seeds") after 13 days.

## Stages

Each harvest yields 3 Blueberries, with a 2% chance of more blueberries. You can walk through blueberries.

## Fertilizer

[Basic Fertilizer](/Basic_Fertilizer "Basic Fertilizer") and [Quality Fertilizer](/Quality_Fertilizer "Quality Fertilizer") only affect the first berry from every harvest, which means 2 out of 3 berries harvested from each bush are going to be regular quality crops. That makes [Speed-Gro](/Speed-Gro "Speed-Gro") an optimal fertilizer for this crop, as it gives one extra harvest before the Fall (assuming planting on day 1 of the summer), increasing season earnings from Blueberry by 25% compared to at most 6% with [Quality Fertilizer](/Quality_Fertilizer "Quality Fertilizer").[[1]](#cite_note-qual_price-1)

[Deluxe Speed-Gro](/Deluxe_Speed-Gro "Deluxe Speed-Gro") yields the same number of harvests as regular [Speed-Gro](/Speed-Gro "Speed-Gro"), usually at a higher cost. (One exception is purchasing Deluxe Speed-Gro from the [Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis") for data-sort-value="80">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)80g vs. purchasing Speed-Gro from [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for data-sort-value="100">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100g.)

## Tailoring

Blueberry can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Shorts](/Tailoring#Pants "Tailoring"). [![Shorts.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/c5/Shorts.png/36px-Shorts.png)](/File:Shorts.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a blue dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Blueberry is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Trivia

On Sundays in [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), Mayor [Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis") may say "Say, are you growing any blueberries on your farm? I do enjoy a fresh blueberry." Mayor Lewis does in fact like blueberries as gifts.
