The **Farmhouse** is the player's permanent residence in Stardew Valley. Initially, it has only one small room that contains a single bed, a fireplace, an old TV (either a [Budget TV](/Budget_TV "Budget TV") or [Floor TV](/Floor_TV "Floor TV")), various [decorations](/Furniture "Furniture") depending on what farm style was chosen, and a [floor mat](/Furniture#Rugs "Furniture") of varying design, which can all be changed and expanded later on. The outer footprint of the farmhouse is a 9×5 rectangle. The inner footprint of the farmhouse without upgrades is a 10×7 rectangle. The fireplace can be moved by left-clicking or turned on by right-clicking on it.

[Flooring](/Flooring "Flooring") and [Wallpaper](/Wallpaper "Wallpaper") can be changed using patterns bought from [Pierre's](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") or [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart"), or fetched from a [Catalogue](/Catalogue "Catalogue"). New [furniture](/Furniture "Furniture") (including fireplaces) can be added at any time.

Upgrades can be purchased at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop"). Each new room added when upgrading can have its own flooring and wallpaper patterns. You can also change the color of the exterior at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop").

Upon getting [married](/Marriage "Marriage") or having [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") move in as a roommate, the game automatically adds an extra room that is based on that character's style.

## Upgrades

[Robin](/Robin "Robin") requires three days to complete each farmhouse upgrade.

| Upgrade | Outdoor Image | Indoor Image | Cost | Changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [House (tier 2).png](/File:House_(tier_2).png) | [House indoors (tier 2).png](/File:House_indoors_(tier_2).png) | data-sort-value="10000">Gold.png10,000g Wood.png [Wood](/Wood "Wood") (450) | Adds a kitchen, with a kitchen counter that allows [cooking](/Cooking "Cooking"), and a fridge that functions as a chest. Items in the fridge can be used when cooking even though they are not in the player's inventory. Adds a bedroom. Upgrades bed from single to double. Allows [marriage](/Marriage "Marriage"). |
| 2 | [House (tier 3).png](/File:House_(tier_3).png) | [House indoors (tier 3).png](/File:House_indoors_(tier_3).png) | data-sort-value="65000">Gold.png65,000g Hardwood.png [Hardwood](/Hardwood "Hardwood") (100) | Adds two new rooms, one empty, and one with a crib and two [child beds](/Child_Bed "Child Bed"), thus allowing the player to have [children](/Children "Children"). Kitchen and bedroom are larger. Unlocks renovations and the ability to paint the house. |
| 3 | [House (tier 3).png](/File:House_(tier_3).png) | [Cellar Inside.png](/File:Cellar_Inside.png) | data-sort-value="100000">Gold.png100,000g | Adds a cellar under the house, allowing access via the kitchen. The cellar houses [casks](/Cask "Cask") which can age products such as [cheese](/Cheese "Cheese") and [wine](/Wine "Wine") to increase quality and value. The cellar comes with 33 casks, though it may house as many as 189 casks. |

## Achievements

There are 2 [Achievements](/Achievements "Achievements") related to completing Farmhouse upgrades.

* Moving Up (Upgrade the house one time)
* Living Large (Upgrade the house two times)

Note that there are no achievements for upgrading the farmhouse the third time (which adds the cellar) or for purchasing any renovations.

## Notes

* The magnifying glass icon over the woodpile at the left of the farmhouse is related to ["The Mysterious Qi" Quest](/Quests#The_Mysterious_Qi "Quests").
* Instead of gathering 450 [wood](/Wood "Wood") for the Farmhouse Upgrade 1, it can all be purchased at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop") during year 1 for data-sort-value="4500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)4,500g, or during year 2+ for data-sort-value="22500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)22,500g. The total cost for the upgrade would then be data-sort-value="14500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)14,500g in year 1, or data-sort-value="32500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)32,500g in year 2+.
* The kitchen sink can be used to refill the player's [watering can](/Watering_Cans "Watering Cans").
