**Sandy** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives in [the Calico Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") where she runs the store, the [Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis"). Players won't meet her until they've repaired the bus by completing [the vault bundle](/Bundles#Vault "Bundles") or purchasing "Bus" for data-sort-value="40000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)40,000g through the [Joja Community Development Form](/Joja_Community_Development_Form "Joja Community Development Form").

## Schedule

Sandy does not leave the [Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis"), and can be found there when the store is open, from 9am to 11:50pm except on her birthday, Fall 15. On Fall 15, the Oasis is open in the morning but at 1pm Sandy leaves to walk around the desert with Emily.

Sandy never visits the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island.

## Relationships

Sandy allows [Mr. Qi](/Mr._Qi "Mr. Qi") to operate his [casino](/Casino "Casino") behind her shop. It's clear they're not close, but she accepts his money and "looks the other way" with whatever he's doing back there. She mentions that she's friends with [Emily](/Emily "Emily"); during the first meeting with the player she mentions that Emily wrote to her about "the new farmer" in town. She also mentions that "my friend runs a shop in the desert", referring to the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader").

## Quests

* Sandy may randomly request an item at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). The reward is 3x the item's base value and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* Sandy is to be gifted the [Stardew Valley Rose](/Quests#Stardew_Valley_Rose "Quests") in [The Pirate's Wife](/Quests#The_Pirate.27s_Wife "Quests") quest.

## Trivia

* As stated at the top of the page, Sandy is not her real name. The game never discloses what her real name is.
* On Saturdays Sandy says "You look really thirsty. Here, have a taste of this ice cold milk." and gives the player [Milk](/Milk "Milk") when talked to.
* Sandy may attend [movies](/Movie_Theater "Movie Theater") with [Emily](/Emily "Emily").
* Sandy's top can be tailored with 1 [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") and 1 [Tom Kha Soup](/Tom_Kha_Soup "Tom Kha Soup"), creating the [Oasis Gown](/Tailoring#Shirts "Tailoring").
