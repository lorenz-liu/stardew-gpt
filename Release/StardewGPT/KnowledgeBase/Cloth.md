**Cloth** is an [Artisan Good](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods") made from the [Loom](/Loom "Loom") using [Wool](/Wool "Wool"), taking 4 hours. Occasionally the Traveling Cart will offer it in her rotating stock. It can also be made by recycling a [Soggy Newspaper](/Soggy_Newspaper "Soggy Newspaper") using the [Recycling Machine](/Recycling_Machine "Recycling Machine"), or dropped from [Mummies](/Mummy "Mummy") when slain. [Emily](/Emily "Emily") may occasionally send you a Cloth in the mail. She also sells a set of five Cloth at her [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="25">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 25 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg"). The [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") will trade three [Aquamarines](/Aquamarine "Aquamarine") for one cloth on Wednesdays.

Placing quality [Wool](/Wool "Wool") into a [Loom](/Loom "Loom") gives a chance to produce 2 Cloth. Silver gives a 10% chance, gold gives a 50% chance, and iridium gives a 100% chance.
