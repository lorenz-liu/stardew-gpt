The **Crayfish** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught using a [Crab Pot](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot") in any [freshwater](/Crab_Pot#Freshwater "Crab Pot") body of water. It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") turtle with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals"). It can also randomly be found at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="75"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)225–1,000g.

Like all fish caught from the crab pot, Crayfish cannot be eaten unless it is used in a [Cooking](/Cooking "Cooking") recipe or [smoked](/Smoked_Fish "Smoked Fish").

## Tailoring

Crayfish can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [![Shirt260.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/1b/Shirt260.png/24px-Shirt260.png)](/File:Shirt260.png) [Rain Coat](/Tailoring "Tailoring").

## Fish Pond

Crayfish can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 2 days. The initial pond capacity is 3 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing three quests. Typical output includes light gray Crayfish [Roe](/Roe "Roe"), [Green Algae](/Green_Algae "Green Algae"), and various [Trash](/Trash "Trash") items, but more valuable items are possible starting at population 5.
