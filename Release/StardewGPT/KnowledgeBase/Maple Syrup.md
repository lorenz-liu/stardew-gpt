**Maple Syrup** is [Tree](/Trees "Trees") sap made from the [Tapper](/Tapper "Tapper") when it is placed on a [Maple Tree](/Maple_Tree "Maple Tree"), taking ![Time Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Time_Icon.png/24px-Time_Icon.png) 9 days, or ![Time Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Time_Icon.png/24px-Time_Icon.png) 4 days with a [Heavy Tapper](/Heavy_Tapper "Heavy Tapper").

Maple Syrup can be obtained by tapping [Maple Trees](/Maple_Tree "Maple Tree") grown by the player or growing naturally in Stardew Valley (*e.g.,* in [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest")). It can also rarely be obtained after processing [Hardwood](/Hardwood "Hardwood") in a [Wood Chipper](/Wood_Chipper "Wood Chipper").

Although Maple Syrup is labeled "Artisan Good" in-game, it does not benefit from the [Artisan Profession](/Skills#Farming "Skills"), but instead from the [Tapper Profession](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") (+25% sell price).

## Tailoring

Maple Syrup can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Floppy Beanie.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/0d/Floppy_Beanie.png/24px-Floppy_Beanie.png) [Floppy Beanie](/Floppy_Beanie "Floppy Beanie"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a brown dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

* One Maple Syrup is required to complete the ["Strange Note" quest](/Quests#Strange_Note "Quests") that is triggered by reading [Secret Note #23](/Secret_Notes#Secret_Note_.2323 "Secret Notes").
* One or two Maple Syrup may be requested by several fish in [Fish Pond quests](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond").
