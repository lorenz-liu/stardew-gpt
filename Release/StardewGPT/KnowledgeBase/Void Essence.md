**Void Essence** is a [Monster Loot](/Monster_Loot "Monster Loot") item which can drop from [Shadow Brutes](/Shadow_Brute "Shadow Brute"), [Shadow Shamans](/Shadow_Shaman "Shadow Shaman"), [Shadow Snipers](/Shadow_Sniper "Shadow Sniper"), [Haunted Skulls](/Haunted_Skull "Haunted Skull"), [Serpents](/Serpent "Serpent") and [Spiders](/Spider "Spider"). [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") also sells 10 a day for data-sort-value="100">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100g each. A [Void Salmon](/Void_Salmon "Void Salmon") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") may produce 5-10 Void Essence when the population of the pond reaches 8.

The [Void Ghost Pendant](/Void_Ghost_Pendant "Void Ghost Pendant") can be purchased from the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") in exchange for 200 Void Essence, once the player has reached 10 hearts of [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") with [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus").

## Tailoring

Void Essence is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Arcane Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt215.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3b/Shirt215.png/24px-Shirt215.png)](/File:Shirt215.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a purple dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* Void Essence is requested in a letter on [Winter](/Winter "Winter") 12th by the [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard") in the ["A Dark Reagent" Quest](/Quests#A_Dark_Reagent "Quests"). The reward is data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* 5 Void Essence are requested by [Void Salmon](/Void_Salmon "Void Salmon") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 1 to 3.
