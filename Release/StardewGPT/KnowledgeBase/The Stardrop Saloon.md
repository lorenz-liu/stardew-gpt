**The Stardrop Saloon** is owned by [Gus](/Gus "Gus"), and is located in the center of [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). It's a meeting place for many [villagers](/Villagers "Villagers"), and hosts a variety of entertainment, including fully playable arcade machines, a jukebox, and a [Joja Cola](/Joja_Cola "Joja Cola") vending machine.

## Menu

[Gus](/Gus "Gus") offers a variety of [food and recipes](/Cooking "Cooking") for sale. He also has a rotating supply of [cooked dishes](/Cooking "Cooking").

To shop at the Saloon, interact with the cash register on the counter. It is possible to shop at the register as long as Gus is anywhere behind the counter. When Gus visits [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island"), he leaves a cash box on the bar, so the player is still able to make purchases.

Note: The [Cookie](/Cookie "Cookie") recipe will be available only for players who have seen [Evelyn](/Evelyn "Evelyn")'s 4-heart event but experienced a bug where they have not received the Cookie recipe.

## Jukebox

The player can interact with the Jukebox to change the current song. It's possible for the Jukebox to play any [music track](/Soundtrack "Soundtrack") in the game, as long as the player has heard them. Many of them are soundtracks of seasons, as well as soundtracks from Heart Events, or soundtracks from Festivals.

[![Jukebox.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/65/Jukebox.png/60px-Jukebox.png)](/File:Jukebox.png)

## Arcade

Each of the arcade machines inside the saloon are playable. However, Junimo Kart is initially locked and needs the [Skull Key](/Skull_Key "Skull Key") to unlock it.

### Journey of the Prairie King

[Journey of the Prairie King](/Journey_of_the_Prairie_King "Journey of the Prairie King") is an Arcade Shooter with 3 worlds. Beat them all for a [special reward](/Prairie_King_Arcade_System "Prairie King Arcade System").

[![Prairie King Arcade System.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/eb/Prairie_King_Arcade_System.png/48px-Prairie_King_Arcade_System.png)](/File:Prairie_King_Arcade_System.png)

### Junimo Kart

[Junimo Kart](/Junimo_Kart "Junimo Kart") is an arcade game which is unlocked with the [Skull Key](/Skull_Key "Skull Key"). Complete "Progress Mode" for a [special reward](/Junimo_Kart_Arcade_System "Junimo Kart Arcade System").

[![Junimo Kart Arcade System.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/e6/Junimo_Kart_Arcade_System.png/48px-Junimo_Kart_Arcade_System.png)](/File:Junimo_Kart_Arcade_System.png)

### Joja Vending Machine

Next to the Arcade Machines in the Saloon, there is a Joja Vending Machine that dispenses [Joja Cola](/Joja_Cola "Joja Cola") for data-sort-value="75">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)75g.
