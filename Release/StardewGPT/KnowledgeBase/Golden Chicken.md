The **Golden Chicken** is an [animal](/Animals "Animals") that lives in a [Big Coop](/Coop "Coop"). Golden Chickens can be hatched by placing a [Golden Egg](/Golden_Egg "Golden Egg") into an [Incubator](/Incubator "Incubator"), taking ![Time Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Time_Icon.png/24px-Time_Icon.png) 9,000m (5d 16h 40m) to incubate, or ![Time Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Time_Icon.png/24px-Time_Icon.png) 4,500m (2d 21h 40m) with the [Coopmaster Profession](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming").

## Produce

:   *Main article: [Animals#Produce](/Animals#Produce "Animals")*

Golden Chickens who eat every day mature after 3 nights have passed. A mature and fed golden chicken produces a [Golden Egg](/Golden_Egg "Golden Egg") every day, which can be placed in an [Incubator](/Incubator "Incubator") to hatch another golden chicken, or sold for data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g.

A Golden Egg can be placed in a [Mayonnaise Machine](/Mayonnaise_Machine "Mayonnaise Machine") to produce 3 gold quality [Mayonnaise](/Mayonnaise "Mayonnaise").

## Selling

Golden Chickens may be sold the same way as normal [chickens](/Chicken "Chicken"). The more hearts the animal has, the higher its sell price. A new-born chick sells for data-sort-value="252">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)252g, and a maximum-heart Golden Chicken for data-sort-value="1040">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,040g, the same as for normal chickens.
