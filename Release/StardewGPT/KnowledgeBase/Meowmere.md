The **Meowmere** is a [secret](/Secrets "Secrets") [sword](/Sword "Sword") weapon obtained by placing a [Far Away Stone](/Far_Away_Stone "Far Away Stone") on the plant pedestal located in the basement of [Wizard's Tower](/Wizard%27s_Tower "Wizard's Tower"). It is based on the weapon of the same name in [Terraria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terraria "wikipedia:Terraria"). When enemies are slain with it (or a weapon forged to have its appearance), they will explode into rainbow sparkles.

## Trivia

* Despite Meowmere being the sword with the highest base damage in its origin game, in Stardew Valley it is superseded by any weapon above level four, such as the [Templar's Blade](/Templar%27s_Blade "Templar's Blade") and [Bone Sword](/Bone_Sword "Bone Sword").
* Meowmere is the only sword that deals exactly the same amount of attack damage every swing.
