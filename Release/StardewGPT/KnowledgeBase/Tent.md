The **Tent** is home to [Linus](/Linus "Linus"), who spends much of his day in and around the tent. It's located on [the Mountain](/The_Mountain "The Mountain") northeast of the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop") and east of the passage to the [Railroad](/Railroad "Railroad"). It can be entered without first gaining any [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
