**Mining Monthly** is a skill [book](/Books "Books") that can be obtained through:

* Purchasing from the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") for data-sort-value="5000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)5,000g to data-sort-value="10000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)10,000g.
* Purchasing from the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="6000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)6,000g (1% chance to appear).[[1]](#cite_note-travelcart-1)
* Catching regular (0.12% chance) or golden (1.5% chance) [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing").[[2]](#cite_note-treasure-2)
* Using the [Prize Machine](/Mayor%27s_Manor#Prize_Machine "Mayor's Manor") in the Mayor's Manor (20% chance to be the 15th prize).[[3]](#cite_note-tickets-3)
* Opening [Mystery Boxes](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") or [Golden Mystery Boxes](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box").
* Slaying [Iridium Golems](/Iridium_Golem "Iridium Golem") (0.2% chance).[[4]](#cite_note-golemchance-4)
* Slaying any [monster](/Monsters "Monsters") (0.018% chance).[[5]](#cite_note-rareobject-5)
* Breaking [crates and barrels](/The_Mines#Crates_and_Barrels "The Mines") in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), or [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon") (0.024% chance).[[5]](#cite_note-rareobject-5)
* Shaking [trees](/Trees "Trees") with seeds (0.024% chance) or chopping trees (0.004% chance per hit).[[5]](#cite_note-rareobject-5)
* Digging [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") (0.11% chance).[[5]](#cite_note-rareobject-5)

Upon reading the book, players will earn 250 [Mining](/Mining "Mining") [XP](/Experience "Experience").

## Bundles

Mining Monthly is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Mining Monthly is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a brown dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

Mining Monthly is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Trading

One Mining Monthly can be traded to the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") for 20 [Coal](/Coal "Coal").
