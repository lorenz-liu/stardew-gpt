**Beer** is an [Artisan Good](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods") made from the [Keg](/Keg "Keg") using [Wheat](/Wheat "Wheat"), taking 1-2 days. It is also available at [The Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon") for data-sort-value="400">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)400g and the [Ginger Island Resort](/Ginger_Island#Island_South "Ginger Island") for data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g. It can also be received as a gift at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"). [Pam](/Pam "Pam") sells Beer in her [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="20">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 20 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg").

When consumed, Beer causes a [Tipsy Buff](/Buffs "Buffs") that decreases [Speed](/Speed "Speed") by 1.

## Aged Values

Beer can be placed inside a [Cask](/Cask "Cask") to age from normal quality to silver, gold, and eventually iridium quality. Iridium quality doubles the base sell price of Beer.

Normal, silver, and gold quality Beer can be prematurely removed from a cask at any time by striking the cask with an [Axe](/Axes "Axes"), [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes"), or [Pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes").

## Bundles

## Tailoring

Beer is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt138.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/e4/Shirt138.png/24px-Shirt138.png) [Black Leather Jacket](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a yellow dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the yellow dye pot at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"), for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").
