The **Ancient Fruit** is a [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from [Ancient Seeds](/Ancient_Seeds "Ancient Seeds") after 28 days. Thereafter, the mature plant yields another Ancient Fruit every 7 days. Seeds can be obtained from a variety of sources (see [Ancient Seeds](/Ancient_Seeds "Ancient Seeds")).

## Bundles

* Five gold quality Ancient Fruit are an option for ![Bundle Purple.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a1/Bundle_Purple.png/24px-Bundle_Purple.png) [The Missing Bundle](/Bundles#The_Missing_Bundle "Bundles") in the abandoned [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart").
* Ancient Fruit is one of the options for the ![Bundle Teal.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/ce/Bundle_Teal.png/24px-Bundle_Teal.png) [Rare Crops Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Rare_Crops_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Pantry (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Pantry "Remixed Bundles").

## Recipes

Ancient Fruit is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Ancient Fruit can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [![Genie Pants.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/8a/Genie_Pants.png/36px-Genie_Pants.png)](/File:Genie_Pants.png) [Genie Pants](/Tailoring#Pants "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a blue dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Ancient Fruit is not used in any quests.
