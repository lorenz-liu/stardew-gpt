**Fiber Seeds** are a type of [crafted](/Crafting#Seeds "Crafting") seed. They do not require daily watering and can even grow in [Winter](/Winter "Winter"), but they must still be planted on tilled soil. They can also be eaten by [Crows](/Animals#Crows "Animals") if not protected by a [Scarecrow](/Scarecrow "Scarecrow").

The recipe is obtained from [Linus](/Linus "Linus") in the mail the day after completing the Special Order ["Community Cleanup"](/Quests#Community_Cleanup "Quests").

Each fully-grown plant drops between 4 and 7 [Fiber](/Fiber "Fiber") when harvested with a [Scythe](/Scythe "Scythe"). There is also a 10% chance to drop 1 [Mixed Seeds](/Mixed_Seeds "Mixed Seeds").[[1]](#cite_note-mixedseeds-1)

## Tips

* Fiber Seeds can be planted during the last week of [Fall](/Fall "Fall") to prevent [fertilizers](/Fertilizer "Fertilizer") from disappearing on the first day of [Winter](/Winter "Winter").
* Although they are not considered "[Crops](/Crops "Crops")", [Junimos](/Junimos "Junimos") will still harvest them if in range of a [Junimo Hut](/Junimo_Hut "Junimo Hut").
