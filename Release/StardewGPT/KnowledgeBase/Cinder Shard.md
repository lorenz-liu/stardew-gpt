**Cinder Shard** is a [resource](/Category:Resources "Category:Resources") that can be obtained by mining [Cinder Shard nodes](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining") or obtained from [Common or Rare Chests](/Volcano_Dungeon#Treasure "Volcano Dungeon"), [Magma Sprites](/Magma_Sprite "Magma Sprite"), [Magma Sparkers](/Magma_Sparker "Magma Sparker"), [Magma Duggies](/Magma_Duggy "Magma Duggy"), or [False Magma Caps](/False_Magma_Cap "False Magma Cap") in the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon"). It can also be acquired from a [Stingray](/Stingray "Stingray") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") with a population of at least 7, or found in Skull Cavern [treasure rooms](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern") after the [Volcano Shortcut](/Volcano_Dungeon#Floor_10 "Volcano Dungeon") has been unlocked.

## Forging

:   *Main article: [Forge](/Forge "Forge")*

Cinder Shards are used in the [Forge](/Forge "Forge") to combine rings, enchant tools, and forge melee weapons with gemstones. 20 Cinder Shards are used to combine two rings, apply a random enchantment to a tool or weapon, or add a [Galaxy Soul](/Galaxy_Soul "Galaxy Soul") to a Galaxy weapon, while 10/15/20 are used to forge a tool for the first, second, and third time, respectively.

## Tailoring

It is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create ![Sunglasses.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/92/Sunglasses.png/24px-Sunglasses.png) [Sunglasses](/Sunglasses "Sunglasses"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

7 Cinder Shards are requested by [Stingray](/Stingray "Stingray") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 3 to 5.
