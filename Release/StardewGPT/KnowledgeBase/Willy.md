**Willy** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who grew up traveling among the [Fern Islands](/Setting#Fern_Islands "Setting") and now lives south of [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") on [The Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach"). He sells [bait](/Bait "Bait") and [fishing](/Fishing "Fishing") supplies in his [shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop"). His shop is open every day but will be closed on Saturday if the weather is nice enough for him to go fishing.

## Schedule

Most days Willy fishes at [the beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") for a few hours before operating his [fish shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop") between 9am and 5pm. During winter he'll go to [the saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon") after work.

Willy's shop is closed on Saturday while he's out fishing unless it's raining. Willy never visits the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island, though he ferries other villagers to and from the Island.

## Relationships

Willy spends most of his time fishing just outside his shop when not operating the sale counters at his [shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop"). He's often seen at [the saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon") in the evenings, especially during the [winter](/Winter "Winter"), when he sits with [Clint](/Clint "Clint"). He lives close to [Elliott](/Elliott "Elliott"); the two of them are the only residents of [the beach](/The_Beach "The Beach"). He is also friends with [Elliott](/Elliott "Elliott").

## Quests

* ["To The Beach"](/Quests#To_The_Beach "Quests"): Willy will send you a letter on the 2nd of [Spring](/Spring "Spring") asking you to meet him on the beach south of town. The reward is a [Bamboo Pole](/Bamboo_Pole "Bamboo Pole").
* ["Catch a Squid"](/Quests#Catch_a_Squid "Quests"): Willy will send you a letter on the 2nd of [Winter](/Winter "Winter") asking you to catch and bring him a [Squid](/Squid "Squid"). The reward is data-sort-value="800">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)800g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* ["Catch a Lingcod"](/Quests#Catch_a_Lingcod "Quests"): Willy will send you a letter on the 13th of [Winter](/Winter "Winter"), year 2, asking you to catch and bring him a [Lingcod](/Lingcod "Lingcod"). The reward is data-sort-value="550">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)550g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* **Fishing**: Willy may randomly request that you catch a certain number of a specific fish from the current season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). The reward is 3x the fish's base value (per fish). (You get to keep the fish).
* **Item Delivery**: Willy may request that you bring him a random item at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). The reward is 3x the item's base value and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* Willy is to be gifted the [Wriggling Worm](/Quests#Wriggling_Worm "Quests") in [The Pirate's Wife](/Quests#The_Pirate.27s_Wife "Quests") quest.
* ["Juicy Bugs Wanted!"](/Quests#Juicy_Bugs_Wanted "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), Willy may randomly request that the player collect 100 [Bug Meat](/Bug_Meat "Bug Meat") and place it in the bin next to the [Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop"). The reward is data-sort-value="3000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)3,000g and the [Quality Bobber](/Quality_Bobber "Quality Bobber") recipe.
* ["Tropical Fish"](/Quests#Tropical_Fish "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), Willy may randomly request that the player catch 5 [Stingray](/Stingray "Stingray"), 5 [Blue Discus](/Blue_Discus "Blue Discus"), and 5 [Lionfish](/Lionfish "Lionfish"). The reward is data-sort-value="2500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,500g and a [Deluxe Fish Tank](/Deluxe_Fish_Tank "Deluxe Fish Tank"). Completing this special order also unlocks the Deluxe Fish Tank for sale at the [Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop"). This special order requires [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") to be unlocked and the Island Resort to be built.
* Willy offers a different [fishing quest](/Desert_Festival#Willy.27s_Fishing_Quests "Desert Festival") for each day of the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival"):
  + Spring 15: He challenges the player to catch 3 [Sandfish](/Sandfish "Sandfish"). The reward is data-sort-value="25">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 25.
  + Spring 16: He challenges the player to catch 1 [Scorpion Carp](/Scorpion_Carp "Scorpion Carp"). The reward is data-sort-value="50">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 50.
  + Spring 17: He asks the player to return his [Golden Bobber](/Golden_Bobber "Golden Bobber"). The reward is data-sort-value="30">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 30.

## Trivia

* At one point before the game was released, Willy was named "Dick".
* It is implied that Willy catches fish for a living. However, the only time the player sees Willy catch a fish is at the [Festival of Ice](/Festival_of_Ice "Festival of Ice").
* One [Pearl](/Pearl "Pearl") may be obtained from Willy after filling a Fish Pond with 10 [Crabs](/Crab "Crab").
* Willy's shirt can be tailored with 1 [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") and 1 [Dish O' The Sea](/Dish_O%27_The_Sea "Dish O' The Sea"), creating the [Shirt O' The Sea](/Tailoring#Shirts "Tailoring").
