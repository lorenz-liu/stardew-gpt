**Shears** are a [tool](/Tools "Tools") used to collect [wool](/Wool "Wool") from [sheep](/Sheep "Sheep"). They must be purchased from [Marnie's Ranch](/Marnie%27s_Ranch "Marnie's Ranch") for data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g.

Each use of the Shears to shear a sheep consumes 4 points of [energy](/Energy "Energy"), and increases the player's [Farming](/Farming "Farming") experience by 5 points.

Once the player has reached [Farming Level 10](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming"), an [Auto-Grabber](/Auto-Grabber "Auto-Grabber") can be purchased from Marnie's Ranch for data-sort-value="25000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)25,000g, which will automatically shear all sheep from the [barn](/Barn "Barn") in which it is placed.
