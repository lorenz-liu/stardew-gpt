# Artifacts

[![](/mediawiki/images/4/41/Artifact_tile.gif)](/File:Artifact_tile.gif)

An [Artifact Spot](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot")

:   *For an index to the completed artifacts collection, see [Collections#Artifacts](/Collections#Artifacts "Collections").*

**Artifacts** are items found primarily by digging up [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot"), though they can be acquired in other ways. There are 42 artifacts in total, and one of each must be donated to the [Museum](/Museum "Museum") to complete its collection. Donating to the [Museum](/Museum "Museum") will yield rewards when donation milestones are reached, or when specific artifacts are donated.

## Acquisition

Artifacts can be acquired in the following ways:

* Using the [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes") on an [Artifact Spot](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot"). Different parts of the map will produce different artifacts. Artifact Spots are much more common in [winter](/Winter "Winter").
* Using the [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes") on dirt in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") and the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern").
* Killing [Monsters](/Monsters "Monsters").
* Solving [Secret Notes](/Secret_Notes "Secret Notes").
* Breaking open [Artifact Troves](/Artifact_Trove "Artifact Trove").
* Breaking open [Geodes](/Geode "Geode"), [Frozen Geodes](/Frozen_Geode "Frozen Geode"), [Magma Geodes](/Magma_Geode "Magma Geode"), and [Omni Geodes](/Omni_Geode "Omni Geode").
* [Fishing](/Fishing "Fishing") for treasure chests.
* Breaking [Bone Nodes](/Mining#Bone_Node "Mining").

The table below lists specific locations for each artifact.

## Use

All artifacts can be used for:

* Donation to the museum
* Gifting to [Penny](/Penny "Penny") and the [Dwarf](/Dwarf "Dwarf") as liked gifts
* [Tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring") at a [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine")

Some artifacts have other specific uses:

* [Anchor](/Anchor "Anchor") and [Ancient Sword](/Ancient_Sword "Ancient Sword") can be used in [Fish Tanks](/Fish_Tank "Fish Tank") as decoration.
* [Ancient Doll](/Ancient_Doll "Ancient Doll") is an option for the ![Bundle Green.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/b3/Bundle_Green.png/24px-Bundle_Green.png) [Children's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Children.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Remixed Bundles"). It is a loved gift for [Jas](/Jas "Jas"). It can be used to obtain a [secret item](/Secrets#Meowmere "Secrets").
* [Ancient Seed](/Ancient_Seed "Ancient Seed") may be [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting") into a plantable [Ancient Seeds](/Ancient_Seeds "Ancient Seeds") pack after one is donated to the museum.
* [Ancient Sword](/Ancient_Sword "Ancient Sword") and [Bone Flute](/Bone_Flute "Bone Flute") are liked gifts for [Abigail](/Abigail "Abigail").
* [Dinosaur Egg](/Dinosaur_Egg "Dinosaur Egg") may be used to hatch a [Dinosaur](/Dinosaur "Dinosaur") in an [Incubator](/Incubator "Incubator") or turned into [Dinosaur Mayonnaise](/Dinosaur_Mayonnaise "Dinosaur Mayonnaise"). It is a liked gift for [Alex](/Alex "Alex") and [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius").
* [Dwarf Gadget](/Dwarf_Gadget "Dwarf Gadget") is required to craft the [Farm Computer](/Farm_Computer "Farm Computer"). It is a loved gift for [Maru](/Maru "Maru").
* [Dwarf Scroll I](/Dwarf_Scroll_I "Dwarf Scroll I"), [Dwarf Scroll II](/Dwarf_Scroll_II "Dwarf Scroll II"), [Dwarf Scroll III](/Dwarf_Scroll_III "Dwarf Scroll III"), [Dried Starfish](/Dried_Starfish "Dried Starfish"), and [Golden Relic](/Golden_Relic "Golden Relic") may be required for some [Fish Pond quests](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond").
* [Rusty Spoon](/Rusty_Spoon "Rusty Spoon") may be requested by the [Raccoon](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump").
* [Strange Doll (green)](/Strange_Doll_(green) "Strange Doll (green)") and [Strange Doll (yellow)](/Strange_Doll_(yellow) "Strange Doll (yellow)") are loved gifts for [Jas](/Jas "Jas").
* [Bone Flute](/Bone_Flute "Bone Flute") and all fossil (including [Trilobite](/Trilobite "Trilobite")), prehistoric (excluding [Prehistoric Handaxe](/Prehistoric_Handaxe "Prehistoric Handaxe") and [Prehistoric Tool](/Prehistoric_Tool "Prehistoric Tool")), and skeletal artifacts may be processed in the [Bone Mill](/Bone_Mill "Bone Mill") to obtain [Fertilizer](/Fertilizer "Fertilizer").

### Donation

Donating the following artifacts grant unique rewards:

* Donating the [Ancient Drum](/Ancient_Drum "Ancient Drum"), along with at least 2 other artifacts, rewards the player with a [Drum Block](/Drum_Block "Drum Block").
* Donating an [Ancient Seed](/Ancient_Seed "Ancient Seed") rewards the player with an Ancient Seed crafting recipe and 1 Ancient Seeds pack, which can then be planted to harvest [Ancient Fruit](/Ancient_Fruit "Ancient Fruit").
* Donating the [Bone Flute](/Bone_Flute "Bone Flute"), along with at least 2 other artifacts, rewards the player with a [Flute Block](/Flute_Block "Flute Block").
* Donating the [Chicken Statue](/Chicken_Statue "Chicken Statue"), along with at least 4 other artifacts, rewards the player with a [Chicken Statue furniture](/Chicken_Statue_(furniture) "Chicken Statue (furniture)") item.
* Donating all four Dwarf Scrolls unlocks the [Dwarvish Translation Guide](/Dwarvish_Translation_Guide "Dwarvish Translation Guide"), allowing the ability to speak to the [Dwarf](/Dwarf "Dwarf") merchant in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") and in the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon").
* Donating the prehistoric bone pieces rewards the player with the [Sloth Skeleton](/Sloth_Skeleton "Sloth Skeleton") furniture pieces.
* Donating the [Rare Disc](/Rare_Disc "Rare Disc") and [Dwarf Gadget](/Dwarf_Gadget "Dwarf Gadget"), along with at least 9 other artifacts, rewards the player with the ['Burnt Offering'](/%27Burnt_Offering%27 "'Burnt Offering'") painting.
