The **Egg** and the **Brown Egg** are [animal products](/Category:Animal_Products "Category:Animal Products") obtained from White, Brown, or Blue [Chickens](/Chicken "Chicken"). [Marnie](/Marnie "Marnie") may also gift you 12 eggs at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"). [Shane](/Shane "Shane") sells Eggs in his [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="1">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 1 [Calico Egg](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg").

Eggs can be hatched in an [incubator](/Incubator "Incubator"), which comes with a Big or Deluxe [Coop](/Coop "Coop").

## Tailoring

Egg can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable ![Shirt216.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/8d/Shirt216.png/24px-Shirt216.png) [Plain Overalls](/Tailoring#Shirts "Tailoring"). Both colors of eggs can be used as a white [dye](/Dyeing "Dyeing") when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

* An Egg may be randomly requested during any season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="150">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points. This will only be requested if the player owns a White Chicken, Brown Chicken, or Duck. However, owning a Duck will still result in a chicken egg being requested.
* ["Gus' Famous Omelet"](/Quests#Gus.27_Famous_Omelet "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), [Gus](/Gus "Gus") may randomly request that the player collect and deliver 24 Eggs (of any type) to the fridge in [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon"). The reward is data-sort-value="3000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)3,000g and a [Mini-Fridge](/Mini-Fridge "Mini-Fridge").
* Five Eggs (any type, excluding Dinosaur) may be requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his third request, or as part of any request starting with the sixth.
