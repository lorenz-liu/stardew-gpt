The **Seed Maker** is a piece of [Refining Equipment](/Crafting#Refining_Equipment "Crafting") that produces seeds from a harvested crop or one of four [foraged items](/Foraging "Foraging"): [Wild Horseradish](/Wild_Horseradish "Wild Horseradish"), [Spice Berry](/Spice_Berry "Spice Berry"), [Common Mushroom](/Common_Mushroom "Common Mushroom"), or [Winter Root](/Winter_Root "Winter Root"). It takes ![Time Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Time_Icon.png/24px-Time_Icon.png) 20m to process seeds. [[1]](#cite_note-processtime-1)

A Seed Maker can be [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting") after reaching [Farming](/Farming "Farming") Level 9. One Seed Maker is the reward for completing the ![Bundle Teal.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/ce/Bundle_Teal.png/24px-Bundle_Teal.png) [Dye Bundle](/Bundles#Dye_Bundle "Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board](/Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Bundles"). A Seed Maker may occasionally be found in a [treasure room](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern") in the Skull Cavern.

## Seeds per Crop

The Seed Maker can produce 1-3 seeds of the same variety as the input crop (97.51% chance, average of 2 seeds), 1-4 [Mixed Seeds](/Mixed_Seeds "Mixed Seeds") (1.99% chance), or 1 packet of [Ancient Seeds](/Ancient_Seeds "Ancient Seeds") (0.5% chance).

To avoid buying new seeds, an average of 51% of harvests would need to be sacrificed (= 1 / (0.9751 \* 2)) to obtain new seeds for the next growing cycle.

The type and number of seeds produced is determined by the time of day and location of the Seed Maker.[[1]](#cite_note-processtime-1)
