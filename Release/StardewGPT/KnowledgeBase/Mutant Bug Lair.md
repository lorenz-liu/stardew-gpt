# Mutant Bug Lair

[![Mutant Bug Lair.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/47/Mutant_Bug_Lair.png/320px-Mutant_Bug_Lair.png)](/File:Mutant_Bug_Lair.png)

The **Mutant Bug Lair** is an underground location that becomes accessible as part of the [Dark Talisman Quest](/Quests#Dark_Talisman "Quests"). The entrance to the lair is in the northwest corner of [The Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers"). Talking to [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") during this quest will open the passage to this location.

The player should be prepared to encounter [monsters](/Monsters "Monsters") before entering the Mutant Bug Lair because there are a number of [Mutant Grubs](/Mutant_Grub "Mutant Grub") and [Mutant Flies](/Mutant_Fly "Mutant Fly") inside.

Even though plants are growing here and [fishing](/Fishing "Fishing") is possible, the game considers this location to be underground. Therefore, [forageable](/Foraging "Foraging") items and [artifact spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") never spawn here.

## Fishing

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/d2/MutantBugLairDistances.png/300px-MutantBugLairDistances.png)](/File:MutantBugLairDistances.png)

[Distances from land](/Fishing#Distance_from_Land "Fishing") in the Mutant Bug Lair

The [Slimejack](/Slimejack "Slimejack") is a unique [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can only be caught in the Mutant Bug Lair. [Carp](/Carp "Carp"), [Green Algae](/Green_Algae "Green Algae"), and [White Algae](/White_Algae "White Algae") are also possible. The available fish are the same year-round. Fishing here is dangerous because [Mutant Flies](/Mutant_Fly "Mutant Fly") continuously spawn and attack the player. To avoid this, fish right at the entrance before any bugs are triggered.
