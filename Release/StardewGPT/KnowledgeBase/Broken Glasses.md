**Broken Glasses** are a type of [trash](/Trash "Trash") that can be caught when fishing during all seasons, in any location, using either a [Fishing Pole](/Tools#Fishing_Poles "Tools") or a [Crab Pot](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot"). They can also be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can"), and any crab pot fish may produce Broken Glasses when placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"). It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") cat with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals").

Broken Glasses can be [recycled](/Recycling_Machine "Recycling Machine") into [Refined Quartz](/Refined_Quartz "Refined Quartz").

## Bundles

Broken Glasses are not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Recipes

Broken Glasses are not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Broken Glasses can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Trash Can Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt232.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2a/Shirt232.png/24px-Shirt232.png)](/File:Shirt232.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a blue dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

During the special order quest [Community Cleanup](/Quests#List_of_Special_Orders "Quests") the player is tasked with gathering 20 trash items (except Joja Cola).
