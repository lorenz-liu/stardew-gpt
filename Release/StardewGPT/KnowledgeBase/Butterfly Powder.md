**Butterfly Powder** is an item that can be purchased from [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") for data-sort-value="20000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)20,000g. If a player uses it on a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals"), then that pet will be permanently removed from [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"). A dialogue box pops up before the pet is removed, asking the player to confirm their choice. Once the pet is gone, their [Pet Bowl](/Pet_Bowl "Pet Bowl") is unassigned and can be used for a new pet. If the pet was wearing a [hat](/Hats "Hats") when it was removed, then that hat will be dropped on the ground.[[1]](#cite_note-petgone-1)

## Bundles

Butterfly Powder is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Butterfly Powder is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a blue dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a blue dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

Butterfly Powder is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
