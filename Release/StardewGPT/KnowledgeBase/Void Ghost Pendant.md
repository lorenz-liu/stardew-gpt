The **Void Ghost Pendant** is an item that can be purchased from the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for 200 [Void Essence](/Void_Essence "Void Essence") after the player has obtained all ten hearts with [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus"). It can also be dropped by [monsters](/Monsters "Monsters") in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern").

It allows the player to invite [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") to move into their house as a roommate, similar to how the [Mermaid's Pendant](/Mermaid%27s_Pendant "Mermaid's Pendant") is used to propose [marriage](/Marriage "Marriage"). However, unlike the Mermaid's Pendant, multiples can be obtained at a time.

If given to anyone other than Krobus, it is a hated gift.

## Notes

* The Void Ghost Pendant is impossible to obtain if the player is already married or engaged to one of the marriage candidates.
* After purchasing the Pendant and moving Krobus in, the [Old Mariner](/Old_Mariner "Old Mariner") will respond "Glad the Amulet worked out for ye, lad/miss." when spoken to.
