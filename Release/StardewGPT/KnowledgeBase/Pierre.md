**Pierre** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). He owns and runs [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store").

## Schedule

Pierre can be found running his [general store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") each day between 9am and 5pm (except Wednesday when the store is closed). After the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") has been fully restored his store will be open every day of the week.

After the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island is unlocked, Pierre may randomly spend the day there. (He will leave a cash box on the counter of the [store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"), so the player is still able to make purchases.) After leaving the Island at 6pm, Pierre will immediately go home to bed. Pierre never visits the Resort on [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days.

## Relationships

Pierre lives with his wife [Caroline](/Caroline "Caroline") and their daughter [Abigail](/Abigail "Abigail") behind his shop. On Friday evenings he socializes with other [villagers](/Villagers "Villagers") at [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon"), but outside that he only interacts with others at [his store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store").

He feels threatened by [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart") and [Morris](/Morris "Morris")' attempts to draw his customers away from the store, and worries that he won't be able to make as much money because of this.

He has a strained relationship with Abigail due to their different beliefs, as shown in a few cutscenes.

## Quests

* ["Pierre's Notice"](/Quests#Pierre.27s_Notice "Quests"): Pierre will send you a letter on the 21st of [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), Year 2, requesting [Sashimi](/Sashimi "Sashimi"). The reward is data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* Pierre may randomly request an item at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [his store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). The reward is 3x the item's base value and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* ["Pierre's Prime Produce"](/Quests#Pierre.27s_Prime_Produce "Quests"): On the Special Orders board in front of the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor"), Pierre may randomly request that the player harvest 25 Gold-quality [vegetables](/Vegetables "Vegetables") and place them in the bin inside [the General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). The reward is data-sort-value="2500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,500g and a [Mini-Shipping Bin](/Mini-Shipping_Bin "Mini-Shipping Bin").
