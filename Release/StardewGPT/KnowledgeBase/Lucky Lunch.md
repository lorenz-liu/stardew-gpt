**Lucky Lunch** is a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit").

**Note:** This recipe is [profitable](/Cooking#Profitable_Recipes "Cooking") - it will result in profit when using the lowest quality ingredients.

Lucky Lunch may randomly appear in [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus")' shop on [Saturdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Saturday "Days of the Week"), at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart"), in the [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") outside the saloon,
or in [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon")'s rotating stock. It is also sold by the [Calico Egg Merchant](/Desert_Festival#Calico_Egg_Merchant "Desert Festival") in the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="10">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 10 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg").

One or two Lucky Lunches may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box"). Two Lucky Lunches may be received from opening a [Golden Mystery Box](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box"). One Lucky Lunch is the reward for completing the ![Bundle Yellow.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2b/Bundle_Yellow.png/24px-Bundle_Yellow.png) [Treasure Hunter's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Treasure_Hunter.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Boiler Room (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Boiler_Room "Remixed Bundles").

## Recipes

## Tailoring

Lucky Lunch is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt052.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/88/Shirt052.png/24px-Shirt052.png)](/File:Shirt052.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").
