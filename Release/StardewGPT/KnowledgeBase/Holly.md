## Bundles

* Holly has a chance to be used in the ![Bundle Teal.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/ce/Bundle_Teal.png/24px-Bundle_Teal.png) [remixed Winter Foraging Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Winter_Foraging_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Crafts Room](/Remixed_Bundles#Crafts_Room "Remixed Bundles").
* Five Holly may be used as one of the options for the ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Winter Star Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Winter_Star "Remixed Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Remixed Bundles").

## Recipes

Holly is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Holly is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [![Shirt035.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/72/Shirt035.png/24px-Shirt035.png)](/File:Shirt035.png) [Fancy Red Blouse](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can also be used as a red dye color at the dye pots in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, located at [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Holly is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
