The **Taro Tuber** is a type of seed. Mature Plants yield [Taro Roots](/Taro_Root "Taro Root").

Taro Tubers can be obtained from [Common Chests](/Volcano_Dungeon#Common_Chests "Volcano Dungeon"), [Tiger Slimes](/Slimes "Slimes"), or [Magma Duggies](/Magma_Duggy "Magma Duggy") in the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon"), from Tiger Slimes in the [Tiger Slime Grove](/Ginger_Island#Tiger_Slime_Grove "Ginger Island"), from [Seed Makers](/Seed_Maker "Seed Maker") using [Taro Roots](/Taro_Root "Taro Root"), by breaking open [Golden Coconuts](/Golden_Coconut "Golden Coconut"), from the [Island Trader](/Island_Trader "Island Trader") for 2 [Bone Fragments](/Bone_Fragment "Bone Fragment"), from cutting [weeds](/Weeds "Weeds") in  [Ginger Island Jungle](/Ginger_Island#Island_East "Ginger Island"), from [Lionfish](/Lionfish "Lionfish") [Fish Ponds](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") when the population reaches 4, or from [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") [artifact spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") and [panning](/Pans "Pans").

Taro Tubers planted in most farm tiles take 10 days to mature with watering each day, as with other crops. However, Taro Tubers planted approximately within 3 tiles of a water source (such as a [Farm Pond](/Farm_Pond "Farm Pond") or a river) in any direction are considered to be irrigated, as in a paddy field. Paddies need not be continuous with each other or adjacent to water in order to count as irrigated. Irrigated Taro Tubers mature in 7 days and do not require watering.
