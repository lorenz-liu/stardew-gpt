The **Truffle** is a type of mushroom that can only be spawned by an adult [Pig](/Pig "Pig") on [The Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") when the pig is outside the [Barn](/Barn "Barn"). Since pigs do not go out of the barn in winter, they spawn no truffles in that season. It can also be dropped from [Truffle Crabs](/Truffle_Crab "Truffle Crab"). It may also randomly appear at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="625"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,875–3,125g.

Despite being spawned by pigs, truffles are not considered to be animal products, like eggs or milk. They do not receive a price increase from the [Rancher](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming") profession.

Despite being mushrooms, Truffles technically are also not considered to be [foraged](/Foraging "Foraging") items, but give Foraging XP when picked up on the farm. They are not among the items included in the foraging category at the [Stardew Valley Fair](/Stardew_Valley_Fair "Stardew Valley Fair"), nor are they labeled as "Forage" in [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory"). They do, however, benefit from the [Gatherer](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") and [Botanist](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") professions. Truffles and [Red Mushroom](/Red_Mushroom "Red Mushroom") are also the only mushrooms that cannot be placed into a [Dehydrator](/Dehydrator "Dehydrator").

Truffles not gathered on the 28th day of a season disappear on the following morning, the 1st day of the new season.

## Recipes

Truffle is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Truffle can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Dark Bandana Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt262a.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/85/Shirt262a.png/24px-Shirt262a.png)](/File:Shirt262a.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* The Truffle may be randomly requested during any season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="1875">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,875g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points. The Truffle will never be requested unless the player owns a [Pig](/Pig "Pig").
