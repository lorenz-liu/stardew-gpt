The **Golden Bobber** is a special item which is required for the ["Golden Bobber" quest](/Desert_Festival#Willy.27s_Fishing_Quests "Desert Festival") from [Willy](/Willy "Willy") during the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") on [Spring](/Spring "Spring") 17.

It is obtained from [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing"). After activating the quest, the next treasure chest obtained that day is guaranteed to have the Golden Bobber. Multiple Golden Bobbers can be obtained in the same day as long as the player does not currently have a Golden Bobber in their inventory while fishing.[[1]](#cite_note-fishtreasure-1)

If the Golden Bobber is not returned to Willy, it will not be removed from the player's inventory. The Golden Bobber can be gifted to any villager as a [universally hated](/Friendship#Universal_Hates "Friendship") gift. Oddly, even Willy hates receiving the Golden Bobber as a gift.
