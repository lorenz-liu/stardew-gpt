The **Spa** is located in the southwest area of the [Railroad](/Railroad "Railroad").

By entering the Spa, passing through the changing room, and remaining stationary in the pool, [energy](/Energy "Energy") and [health](/Health "Health") are restored at 10 points per second.[[1]](#cite_note-regenerationspeed-1)

There are two changing rooms - one for women and one for men. It is possible to enter the changing room opposite to [the player](/The_Player "The Player")'s gender, but only after attempting to enter several times in quick succession from the pool area.

While passing through the covered path from the locker room to the pool, the player is forced into walking and changes into a swimsuit whose color is the same as the player's chosen pants color. Prismatic pants result in a prismatic swimsuit. Undershorts result in a white swimsuit.

## Secret

It is possible to [fish](/Fishing "Fishing") in the water outside the spa, except in [Winter](/Winter "Winter") when the water is frozen. After reading [Secret Note #25](/Secret_Notes#Secret_Note_.2325 "Secret Notes"), the first time fishing there...

| **Details** |
| --- |
| The player will catch an [Ornate Necklace](/Ornate_Necklace "Ornate Necklace"), which can be given to [Caroline](/Caroline "Caroline") for 50 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points with her or to [Abigail](/Abigail "Abigail") for 100 friendship points with her. |

If the player has not found Secret Note #25 or has already solved it, a painting titled ['Vista'](/%27Vista%27 "'Vista'") can be obtained while fishing. [Trash](/Trash "Trash") is the only other item that can be caught.

## Trivia

It is possible to access the spa before unlocking the railroad by using the [sword-swing glitch](/Weapons#Glitches "Weapons") to get outside the map and walk around to the trigger area behind the blocked path. Unfortunately, this will cause the character to be stuck in the area as trying to leave via the normal exit will cause them to get stuck in the rocks blocking the path.
