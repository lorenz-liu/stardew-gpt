**Leah's Cottage** is the home of [Leah](/Leah "Leah"), located south of [Marnie's Ranch](/Marnie%27s_Ranch "Marnie's Ranch") in [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest").

During [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), and [Fall](/Fall "Fall"), players must first acquire two hearts of [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") with Leah before being allowed inside her cottage. Players at any friendship level may enter during [Winter](/Winter "Winter") between 10am and 6pm. The cottage is inaccessible outside these hours, even if the player marries her.
