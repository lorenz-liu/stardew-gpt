The **Snow Yam** is a [foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") item found only in [Winter](/Winter "Winter"). Wild snow yams do not grow above ground, but instead they are buried in the snow/dirt and must be dug up with a [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes") (no other tools, even [Bombs](/Crafting#Bombs "Crafting"), will work).

The highest chance of finding a Snow Yam (11-16%, map-dependent) is when digging up an [Artifact Spot](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot").[[1]](#cite_note-artifact_chance-1) There is also a 4% chance of finding a Snow Yam when digging standard tillable soil in any exterior location.[[2]](#cite_note-soil_chance-2) Exceptions are in [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"), where Snow Yams can be found at artifact spots, but not standard tillable soil, and in [the Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert"), where Snow Yams can not be found at all. Digging up a Snow Yam provides no experience.

Snow Yams can also be grown from [Winter Seeds](/Winter_Seeds "Winter Seeds"), in which case they are a standard wild-seed crop: they grow above ground, can be harvested without using a tool, give 7 Foraging XP each, and are affected by the [Gatherer](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") and [Botanist](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") professions.

Snow Yam can also randomly be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during Winter.
