The **Rusty Key** is used to enter [the Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers"). It is obtained from [Gunther](/Gunther "Gunther") in a cutscene the morning after 60 items have been donated to the [Museum](/Museum "Museum"). During the cutscene, Gunther greets the player at their front door, thanks them for their contributions to the museum, and gives them the Rusty Key. In [Multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer"), the host must speak with Gunther to get the key.

Its existence is first referenced by [Jas](/Jas "Jas") and [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent") in a cutscene in [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest"). This cutscene triggers on or after Spring 11 of Year 1 by entering the Forest between 9am and 4pm on a Monday, Thursday, or Sunday.

Once obtained, it can be found in [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s Menu on the [Special Items & Powers](/Special_Items_%26_Powers "Special Items & Powers") tab.
