The **Magma Geode** is a [Mineral](/Minerals "Minerals") deposit that contains [minerals](/Minerals "Minerals"), [artifacts](/Artifacts "Artifacts"), ores, or basic [resources](/Category:Resources "Category:Resources"). It can be found on levels 81 to 119 of [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") or in [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing"). It can also be received as a gift at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"). It may be additionally be found by breaking boxes and barrels in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"). Five Magma Geodes may be produced by [Lava Eel](/Lava_Eel "Lava Eel") in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") containing at least 9 fish, and 10 Magma Geodes may be produced in a pond containing 10 fish. Magma Geodes can also be obtained by pickaxing [Magma Geode Nodes](/Mining#Magma_Geode_Node "Mining"). These are found in the small quarry on the [Hill-top Farm](/Farm_Maps#Hilltop "Farm Maps") and [Four Corners Farm](/Farm_Maps#Four_Corners "Farm Maps"). Magma Geodes may also drop from a [train](/Railroad#Trains "Railroad").

The Magma Geode can be broken open by [Clint](/Clint "Clint") in the [Blacksmith shop](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith") for data-sort-value="25">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)25g or by using a [Geode Crusher](/Geode_Crusher "Geode Crusher").

Neither daily [luck](/Luck "Luck") nor luck [buffs](/Buffs "Buffs") affect the contents of a Magma Geode.

### Resources

Coal, copper ore, iron ore, and gold ore will be obtained in the following amounts: 30% for 1, 30% for 3, 30% for 5, 9% for 10, and 1% for 20. Stone has a 50% chance to use those values and a 50% chance to give only 1. Iridium Ore has a 30% chance for 1, 30% for 2, 30% for 3, 9% for 6, and 1% for 11. Only 1 clay can be obtained from a single geode.

## Tailoring

Magma Geode is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Red Hoodie](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt162.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/1e/Shirt162.png/24px-Shirt162.png)](/File:Shirt162.png) It can also be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a red dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily") and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Three Magma Geodes may be requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his fifth request, or as part of any request starting with the sixth.
