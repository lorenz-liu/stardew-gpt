The **Golden Pumpkin** is an item that can be obtained every other year during the [Spirit's Eve](/Spirit%27s_Eve "Spirit's Eve") [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") by [completing the maze](/Spirit%27s_Eve#Surviving_the_Haunted_Maze "Spirit's Eve"). It can also be found in [Artifact Troves](/Artifact_Trove "Artifact Trove") (3.7% chance), [Mystery Boxes](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box"), and [Golden Mystery Boxes](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box").

It can be sold through the [Shipping Bin](/Shipping "Shipping") for data-sort-value="2500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,500g. Despite its description, it has two other uses:

* It can be gifted as a [universally loved gift](/Friendship#Universal_Loves "Friendship").
* It can be [tailored](/Tailoring#Hats "Tailoring") into a [Witch Hat](/Witch_Hat "Witch Hat") at the [sewing machine at Emily's house](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") or the [sewing machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") gifted by [Emily](/Emily "Emily").

## Bundles

The Golden Pumpkin is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

A Golden Pumpkin is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Witch Hat.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/18/Witch_Hat.png/24px-Witch_Hat.png) [Witch Hat](/Witch_Hat "Witch Hat"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

The Golden Pumpkin is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Notes

The Golden Pumpkin must be sold at the [Shipping Bin](/Shipping "Shipping") on [the farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"), as [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre") will not buy it.

The Golden Pumpkin is no longer the prize each year at the [Spirit's Eve](/Spirit%27s_Eve "Spirit's Eve") festival; instead, it appears every other year.
