**Serpents** are an [enemy](/Monsters "Monsters") found in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"). 250 Serpents (any type) need to be killed for the [Monster Eradication Goal](/Adventurer%27s_Guild#Monster_Eradication_Goals "Adventurer's Guild") at the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild").

If the player is [Combat](/Combat "Combat") Level 10, Serpents can spawn on the [Wilderness farm](/Farm_Maps#Wilderness "Farm Maps") by default. This is true for any farm type if the player has activated the [Dark Shrine of Night Terrors](/Dark_Shrine_of_Night_Terrors "Dark Shrine of Night Terrors") or enabled monsters in [advanced options](/Options#Advanced_Options "Options") when starting a new game. Serpents slain on the Farm will only yield 1/3 of their normal experience points.[[2]](#cite_note-farmxp-2)

## Behavior

Serpents will fly at the player at high velocities, dealing a fairly large amount of damage on hit. They are common, meaning that they can easily swarm the player if they do not have a strong enough weapon.

## Strategy

Swing early as the hitbox for the enemy is larger than the sprite. Even quicker weapons like the [Galaxy Sword](/Galaxy_Sword "Galaxy Sword") can have a trade of hits if swung too late.

## Quests

* [Marlon](/Marlon "Marlon") may request the player to slay 10 Serpents in the ["Monster Hunt" quest](/Desert_Festival#Skull_Cavern "Desert Festival") during the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival").
