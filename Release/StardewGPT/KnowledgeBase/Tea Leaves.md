**Tea Leaves** are [vegetables](/Vegetables "Vegetables") that are harvested from [Tea Bushes](/Tea_Bush "Tea Bush") each day during the last week (22nd-28th) of [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), and [Fall](/Fall "Fall") (and [Winter](/Winter "Winter") if indoors).

Tea Leaves can be put into a [Keg](/Keg "Keg") to make [Green Tea](/Green_Tea "Green Tea"), though Pickled Tea Leaves are more profitable.

## Tailoring

Tea Leaves can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable green [Shirt And Belt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt159.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/99/Shirt159.png/24px-Shirt159.png)](/File:Shirt159.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a green dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

One Tea Leaves may be requested by [Woodskip](/Woodskip "Woodskip") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 7 to 10.
