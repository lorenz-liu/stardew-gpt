**Red Cabbage** is a [vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from [Red Cabbage Seeds](/Red_Cabbage_Seeds "Red Cabbage Seeds") after 9 days. The seeds are available at [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") starting in Year 2, or can be found starting in Year 1 in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") or [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart").

## Tailoring

Red Cabbage can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Tank Top](/Tailoring "Tailoring").

* Male Version: [![Shirt129.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/23/Shirt129.png/24px-Shirt129.png)](/File:Shirt129.png)
* Female Version: [![Shirt130.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/eb/Shirt130.png/24px-Shirt130.png)](/File:Shirt130.png)

It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a purple dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").
