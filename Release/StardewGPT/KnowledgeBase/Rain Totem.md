The **Rain Totem** is a [crafted item](/Crafting#Consumables "Crafting") that sets the [weather](/Weather "Weather") for the next day to rainy. A Rain Totem can be activated by selecting it in inventory and pressing the [*Check* button](/Controls "Controls") ([![Right-Click.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/e2/Right-Click.png/24px-Right-Click.png)](/File:Right-Click.png) on PC, **Y** on Nintendo Switch, **A** on Xbox, **X** on PlayStation).

The crafting recipe becomes available at [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") Level 9. One to three Rain Totems may also occasionally be found in [treasure rooms](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern") in the Skull Cavern.

## Caveats

* The weather in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") and [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") can be different, and the weather will only be changed in the region where the Rain Totem is activated.
* A Rain Totem will not force the weather in Pelican Town to be rainy on [festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days or on the 1st day of a season.

## Bundles

Rain Totem is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

A Rain Totem can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Totem Mask.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/cc/Totem_Mask.png/24px-Totem_Mask.png) [Totem Mask](/Totem_Mask "Totem Mask"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

The Rain Totem is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Bugs

* If a Rain Totem is used during [Winter](/Winter "Winter") on the day before a snowy day, it will cause it to rain and snow simultaneously the following day.

## History

* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Can now be used in [Tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring").
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Fixed a bug where using a Rain Totem on a stormy day would turn the next day's [weather](/Weather "Weather") to stormy instead of rainy; storms could be chained in this way to obtain large numbers of [Battery Packs](/Battery_Pack "Battery Pack").
