The **Coconut** is an inedible [Foraged](/Foraging#Foraged_Items "Foraging") [Fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") found in [the Calico Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") and by shaking [palm trees](/Palm_Tree "Palm Tree") on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island"). It can also be bought at the [Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis") on Mondays for data-sort-value="200">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)200g or may randomly appear in the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart")'s stock for data-sort-value="100"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)300–1,000g. It can also randomly be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") after unlocking The Desert. [Sandy](/Sandy "Sandy") may also send one in the mail as a gift. The [Statue Of Endless Fortune](/Statue_Of_Endless_Fortune "Statue Of Endless Fortune") produces one Coconut per year on [Linus](/Linus "Linus")' birthday, [Winter](/Winter "Winter") 3.

Although it is inedible, it may be put in a [Preserves Jar](/Preserves_Jar "Preserves Jar") to make Coconut Jelly, or in a [Keg](/Keg "Keg") to make Coconut Wine. Coconuts cannot be planted to create [Palm Trees](/Palm_Tree "Palm Tree").

The [Island Trader](/Island_Trader "Island Trader") will trade 1 [Golden Coconut](/Golden_Coconut "Golden Coconut") for 10 Coconuts, provided the player already previously found and cracked open at least one Golden Coconut.

## Tailoring

Coconut is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt204.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/c6/Shirt204.png/24px-Shirt204.png) [Vacation Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a dark brown dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the orange dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

* [Gus](/Gus "Gus") requests a Coconut by mail on the 19th of [Winter](/Winter "Winter") (Year 2) in the ["Exotic Spirits" Quest](/Quests#Exotic_Spirits "Quests"). The reward is data-sort-value="600">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)600g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* Coconut may be randomly requested after the player has completed the [Vault Bundles](/Bundles#Vault "Bundles") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="300">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)300g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* 3 Coconuts may be requested by [Sandfish](/Sandfish "Sandfish") or [Scorpion Carp](/Scorpion_Carp "Scorpion Carp") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 3 to 5.

## Notes

* The player must reach [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") level 1 before coconuts will drop from [Palm Trees](/Palm_Tree "Palm Tree"), either shaken or chopped down.
* Normally, coconuts do not receive the +10% [Tiller](/Skills#Farming "Skills") price bonus because they are obtained by foraging (see [the Fruit page](/Fruits#Tiller_Profession_Details "Fruits") for details). However, coconuts obtained as gifts or purchases do, and therefore sell for data-sort-value="110">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)110g. If stacked with other normal quality coconuts, the entire stack will receive the price bonus.
* Botanically, the coconut is classed as a drupe, or stone fruit (other examples of Drupes are cherries and peaches).
