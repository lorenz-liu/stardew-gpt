**Dried Fruit** is an [Artisan Good](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods") made from placing [Fruits](/Fruits "Fruits") inside a [Dehydrator](/Dehydrator "Dehydrator"), taking one in-game day. Dried Fruit is always normal quality.

The amount of [Energy](/Energy "Energy") and [Health](/Health "Health") restored from Dried Fruit is 3 times the base [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") Energy/Health restoration. The color of Dried Fruit is also determined by the base fruit used.

## Profitability

Since the Dehydrator requires 5 fruit per use, the sell price of Dried Fruit is (1.5 x Fruit Base Price + 5) per fruit used. With the [Artisan Profession](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming"), the sell price is (2.1 x Fruit Base Price + 7) per fruit used.

Since sell price is based on Fruit Base Price alone, the profit compared to selling the raw [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") will decrease for fruit of higher quality, and from the [Tiller Profession](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming").

For example, if neither the Artisan nor Tiller Professions are chosen, iridium quality [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") will generally make a loss if dehydrated, and gold quality will make a profit of only data-sort-value="5">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)5g per fruit.

## Bundles

Dried Fruit is not used in any [Bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Recipes

Dried Fruit is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Dried Fruit is a yellow [dye](/Dyeing "Dyeing") when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the yellow dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in dyeing.

## Quests

* One Dried Fruit is requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his second request. It may also be requested as part of any request starting with the sixth.
