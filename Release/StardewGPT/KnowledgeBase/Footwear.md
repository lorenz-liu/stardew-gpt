# Footwear

**Footwear** is a type of [clothing](/Clothing "Clothing") item that provides a boost to [Defense](/Defense "Defense") and/or [Immunity](/Immunity "Immunity"). Footwear is either found or purchased at the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild"). All Footwear can be sold to the Adventurer's Guild.

Footwear can be [custom-tailored](/Tailoring "Tailoring") to transfer the stats from one pair to another. The resulting item in the feed will be called "Custom-tailored [name of shoe/boot]".

## History

* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Added Leprechaun Shoes and Emily's Magic Boots. Sneakers, Leather Boots, Tundra Boots, Combat Boots, Firewalker Boots, Dark Boots, and Space Boots may now be purchased from the Adventurer's Guild.
* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): Added Cinderclown Shoes, Mermaid Boots, Dragonscale Boots, and Crystal Shoes. Work Boots, Combat Boots, Thermal Boots, Dark Boots, and Crystal Shoes are now possible chest rewards in The Mines if remixed rewards are selected at the start of the game.
