## Bundles

Dinosaur Mayonnaise is an option for ![Bundle Purple.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a1/Bundle_Purple.png/24px-Bundle_Purple.png) [The Missing Bundle](/Bundles#The_Missing_Bundle "Bundles") in the abandoned [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart").

## Tailoring

Dinosaur Mayonnaise is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [![Dinosaur Pants.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/8d/Dinosaur_Pants.png/36px-Dinosaur_Pants.png)](/File:Dinosaur_Pants.png) [Dinosaur Pants](/Tailoring#Pants "Tailoring").

## Quests

Dinosaur Mayonnaise is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Trivia

Although [Sam](/Sam "Sam") hates [Mayonnaise](/Mayonnaise "Mayonnaise"), [Duck Mayonnaise](/Duck_Mayonnaise "Duck Mayonnaise"), and [Void Mayonnaise](/Void_Mayonnaise "Void Mayonnaise"), he likes Dinosaur Mayonnaise as a gift.
