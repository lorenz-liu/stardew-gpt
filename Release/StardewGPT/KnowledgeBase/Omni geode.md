The **Omni Geode** is a [Mineral deposit](/Minerals#Geodes "Minerals") found primarily by breaking rocks in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"). It can be broken open at the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith") for data-sort-value="25">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)25g or placed in a [Geode Crusher](/Geode_Crusher "Geode Crusher") to obtain the items inside. Omni Geodes can contain [minerals](/Minerals "Minerals"), [artifacts](/Artifacts "Artifacts"), ores, or basic [resources](/Category:Resources "Category:Resources"). They can also be traded to the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for a variety of items.

**Note:** Neither daily [luck](/Luck "Luck") nor luck [buffs](/Buffs "Buffs") affect the contents of an Omni Geode.

## Locations

* Rocks in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") (Floors 21+) or [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern").
* [Omni Geode Nodes](/Mining#Omni_Geode_Node "Mining") in the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon").
* [Crates and barrels](/The_Mines#Crates_and_Barrels "The Mines") in the Mines (Floors 80+), Skull Cavern, or Volcano Dungeon.
* [Dirt patches](/The_Mines#Dirt_Patches "The Mines") in the Mines or Skull Cavern.
* [Carbon Ghost](/Carbon_Ghost "Carbon Ghost") drop (99% chance).
* [Haunted Skull](/Haunted_Skull "Haunted Skull") drop (1.3% chance, only if all [Vault Bundles](/Bundles#Vault "Bundles") are not complete).
* [Wilderness Golem](/Wilderness_Golem "Wilderness Golem") and [Iridium Golem](/Iridium_Golem "Iridium Golem") drop (5% chance, after reaching [Combat](/Combat "Combat") Level 5).
* [Panning](/Pans "Pans") in any area.
* [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island").
* 1, 3, or 10 Omni Geodes can be produced by an [Octopus](/Octopus "Octopus") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") when the population reaches 9.
* Up to 3 can be purchased from the [Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis") on [Wednesdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Wednesday "Days of the Week") for data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g each.
* One can be purchased from [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus")' shop on [Tuesdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Tuesday "Days of the Week") for data-sort-value="300">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)300g.
* 5 Omni Geodes are the reward for completing the ![Bundle Purple.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a1/Bundle_Purple.png/24px-Bundle_Purple.png) [Geologist's Bundle](/Bundles#Geologists_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Boiler Room](/Bundles#Boiler_Room "Bundles").
* 3 Omni Geodes are one of the prizes from the [Trout Derby](/Trout_Derby "Trout Derby").
* 8 Omni Geodes are the 18th prize from the [Prize Machine](/Mayor%27s_Manor#Prize_Machine "Mayor's Manor").
* 5 or 10 Omni Geodes can be obtained from Skull Cavern [treasure rooms](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern").
* 2-4 Omni Geodes may occasionally be found in [Supply Crates](/Supply_Crate "Supply Crate") on the [Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps#Beach "Farm Maps") if the player upgraded the [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") at least once.
* Crane Game in the [Movie Theater](/Movie_Theater "Movie Theater") lobby, during any season.
* The [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") outside of the [Museum](/Museum "Museum").
* A fallen [meteorite](/Random_Events#Meteorite "Random Events") on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") will drop 2 Omni Geodes when mined.

## Omni Geode Contents

It is impossible for the player to receive a Prismatic Shard before cracking at least 15 Omni Geodes. Therefore, the probability of receiving a Prismatic Shard is 0 and receiving any minerals or artifacts is 1/88 if the player has not yet cracked 15 Omni Geodes. The probabilities shown below reflect the probability after cracking at least 15 Omni Geodes.

### Geode Minerals

An Omni Geode can contain any of the 41 [Geode Minerals](/Minerals#Geode_Minerals "Minerals") in the game. Each has a 31/2750 chance to appear.

### Resources

Coal, copper ore, iron ore, and gold ore will be obtained in the following amounts: 30% for 1, 30% for 3, 30% for 5, 9% for 10, and 1% for 20. Stone has a 50% chance to use those values and a 50% chance to give only 1. Iridium Ore has a 30% chance for 1, 30% for 2, 30% for 3, 9% for 6, and 1% for 11. Only 1 clay can be obtained from a single geode.

## Bundles

Omni Geode is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Omni Geode can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Shirt264.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/90/Shirt264.png/24px-Shirt264.png) [Omni Shirt](/Tailoring#Shirts "Tailoring"). It can be used as a gray [dye](/Dyeing "Dyeing") when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

* 2-3 Omni Geodes may be requested by [Octopus](/Octopus "Octopus"), [Rainbow Trout](/Rainbow_Trout "Rainbow Trout"), [Spook Fish](/Spook_Fish "Spook Fish"), [Sturgeon](/Sturgeon "Sturgeon"), or [Super Cucumber](/Super_Cucumber "Super Cucumber") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 5 to 7.
* [Marlon](/Marlon "Marlon") may request the player to obtain 12 Omni Geodes in the ["Treasure Hunt" quest](/Desert_Festival#Skull_Cavern "Desert Festival") during the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival").

## Trading

* 5 Omni Geodes can be traded to the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for 1 [Artifact Trove](/Artifact_Trove "Artifact Trove").
* 3 Omni Geodes can be traded to the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for 1 [Warp Totem: Desert](/Warp_Totem:_Desert "Warp Totem: Desert").
* 50 Omni Geodes can be traded to the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for 1 [Green Turban](/Green_Turban "Green Turban").
* 1 Omni Geode can be traded to the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for 3 [Hay](/Hay "Hay") on Mondays.
* 333 Omni Geodes can be traded to the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for 1 [Magic Turban](/Magic_Turban "Magic Turban") on even-numbered days.
* 333 Omni Geodes can be traded to the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for 1 [Magic Cowboy Hat](/Magic_Cowboy_Hat "Magic Cowboy Hat") on odd-numbered days.
