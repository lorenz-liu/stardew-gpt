**Slime** is a [Monster Loot](/Monster_Loot "Monster Loot") item which is dropped by [Slimes](/Slimes "Slimes"). Killing a single [Slime](/Slimes "Slimes") can drop up to two Slime. Slime can also be obtained from [Slime Balls](/Slime_Ball "Slime Ball") or purchased from [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") for data-sort-value="10">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)10g on [Mondays](/Days_of_the_Week#Monday "Days of the Week") (up to 50 per Monday). It may randomly appear in the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="15"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100–1,000g. A [Slimejack](/Slimejack "Slimejack") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") may also produce 10, 30, or 50 Slime daily when the population reaches 9.

## Tailoring

Slime is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [![Shirt096.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/b6/Shirt096.png/24px-Shirt096.png)](/File:Shirt096.png) [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring").

## Quests

* 20 Slime are requested by [Slimejack](/Slimejack "Slimejack") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 1 to 3.
* 5 Slime may be requested by [Bullhead](/Bullhead "Bullhead") or [Largemouth Bass](/Largemouth_Bass "Largemouth Bass") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 3 to 5.
* 99 Slime may be requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his fifth request, or as part of any request starting with the sixth.
