The **Strange Doll** is an [Artifact](/Artifacts "Artifacts") that can be found by digging up an [Artifact Spot](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") in many locations ([The Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"), the [Bus Stop](/Bus_Stop "Bus Stop"), [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"), [The Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach"), or [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest")), or in [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") after reaching [Fishing level 2](/Fishing#Fishing_Skill "Fishing"). It may also rarely be found by [tilling](/Hoes "Hoes") soil in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") or [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"). One Strange Doll (yellow) is always obtained after solving the secret in [Secret Note #18](/Secret_Notes#Secret_Note_.2318 "Secret Notes").

## Donation

Donation of this item contributes to the total count of donations for the [Museum](/Museum "Museum").

## Tailoring

The Strange Doll (yellow) is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Bobo Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt245.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/0b/Shirt245.png/24px-Shirt245.png)](/File:Shirt245.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

The Strange Doll (yellow) is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Trivia

Both the Strange Dolls resemble characters in a series of webcomics called [Wumbus World](http://wumbusworld.com/index.php?strip=1), created by [ConcernedApe](/ConcernedApe "ConcernedApe") in 2011.
