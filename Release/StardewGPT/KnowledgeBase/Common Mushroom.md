The **Common Mushroom** is a type of mushroom obtainable by:

* [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") throughout Stardew Valley in the [Fall](/Fall "Fall").
* In the [Secret Woods](/Secret_Woods "Secret Woods") during [Spring](/Spring "Spring") and Fall.
* In the [Forest Farm](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps") during [Summer](/Summer "Summer") and Fall.
* Inside of the [Ginger Island mushroom cave](/Ginger_Island#Dig_Site "Ginger Island").
* Searching [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during the Fall.
* Grown from [Wild Seeds (Fa)](/Fall_Seeds "Fall Seeds").
* Collected from the mushroom bins in [the Cave](/The_Cave "The Cave") if the mushroom option was chosen.
* By [tapping](/Tapper "Tapper") a [Mushroom Tree](/Mushroom_Tree "Mushroom Tree").
* Collected from a [Mushroom Log](/Mushroom_Log "Mushroom Log").
* Found on levels 41-69 of [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") while the [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") is enabled, or the [Danger in The Deep](/Quests#Danger_In_The_Deep "Quests") quest is active.

Placing a Common Mushroom in a [Seed Maker](/Seed_Maker "Seed Maker") will generate 1-3 [Fall Seeds](/Fall_Seeds "Fall Seeds").

## Tailoring

Common Mushroom is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [![Shirt074.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/f2/Shirt074.png/24px-Shirt074.png)](/File:Shirt074.png) [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

This shirt is very likely a reference to the [Super Mario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Mario "wikipedia:Super Mario") game series, where the titular character [Mario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mario "wikipedia:Mario") commonly eats mushrooms.

## Quests

* The Common Mushroom may be randomly requested in [Fall](/Fall "Fall") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="120">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)120g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* 2-3 Common Mushrooms may be requested by [Bullhead](/Bullhead "Bullhead"), [Largemouth Bass](/Largemouth_Bass "Largemouth Bass"), or [Woodskip](/Woodskip "Woodskip") in [Fish Pond quests](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond").

## Trivia

* Common Mushrooms are the only fall forageable that can be directly put into the [Seed Maker](/Seed_Maker "Seed Maker").
