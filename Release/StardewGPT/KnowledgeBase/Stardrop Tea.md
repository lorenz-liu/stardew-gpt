**Stardrop Tea** is an item that can be obtained from the Prize Machine in the [Mayor's Manor](/Mayor%27s_Manor "Mayor's Manor") or randomly found in [Golden Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Golden_Treasure_Chests "Fishing") (7% chance).[[1]](#cite_note-chestchance-1) It is also a possible reward for quests beyond the 5th one from the [Raccoon](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump"). One Stardrop Tea is the reward for completing the ![Bundle Red.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3f/Bundle_Red.png/24px-Bundle_Red.png) [Helper's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Helper.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Remixed Bundles").

Stardrop Tea can be [gifted](/Friendship#Gifts "Friendship") for 250 friendship points (1 heart). If given as a birthday gift or during the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"), it is worth 750 friendship points (3 hearts).

Stardrop Tea does not count towards the daily or weekly gift limits, and can be freely gifted even if the limits have been reached. Players can also give multiple Stardrop Teas to the same person on a single day.[[2]](#cite_note-gift-2)

Stardrop Tea does not count as a loved gift for the [Qi's Kindness](/Quests#Qi.27s_Kindness "Quests") quest.

## Bundles

Stardrop Tea is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Stardrop Tea is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a purple dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as an iridium dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

Stardrop Tea is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Tips

* Stardrop Tea can be gifted alongside another item on a villager's birthday. Both will benefit from the increased friendship point gain.
