# Luau

[![Luau.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/af/Luau.png/500px-Luau.png)](/File:Luau.png)

The **Luau** is a [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") that takes place on the 11th of every [Summer](/Summer "Summer"). The player attends the Luau by entering [The Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") between 9am and 2pm. The beach cannot be entered before 9am. When the Luau ends, the player will be returned to [The Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") at 10pm.

On the day of the Festival, every home and shop in Stardew Valley is "locked" and cannot be entered. This does not apply to the [Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis"), which is open as normal.

A central feature of the Luau is the communal potluck soup, made with ingredients added by [villagers](/Villagers "Villagers"). The player may choose to add an ingredient to the soup, then the [Governor](/Governor "Governor") tastes and judges it. The soup-tasting begins by talking to [Mayor Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis") and choosing "yes, let's start," at the dialogue option.

The reaction of the Governor at the soup tasting depends upon what item the player has added to it, if any. [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points with most villagers will also increase, decrease, or not change, depending on the item added to the soup. Friendships with [Sandy](/Sandy "Sandy"), the [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard"), [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus"), the player's [Children](/Children "Children"), and the [Dwarf](/Dwarf "Dwarf") are never affected.

The Luau ends automatically after the [Governor](/Governor "Governor") tastes the soup.

## Potluck

When adding an item to the potluck soup, only edible, uncooked items are valid (although [Life Elixir](/Life_Elixir "Life Elixir") and [Oil of Garlic](/Oil_of_Garlic "Oil of Garlic") are [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting") and not [cooked](/Cooking "Cooking"), the game considers them "cooked" items, so they are not eligible. [Magic Rock Candy](/Magic_Rock_Candy "Magic Rock Candy") and [Piña Colada](/Pi%C3%B1a_Colada "Piña Colada") are considered "cooked" items as well, so they are not eligible. [Field Snack](/Field_Snack "Field Snack") and [Bug Steak](/Bug_Steak "Bug Steak"), however, are eligible). Note that the game does not inform the player before the event that the "potluck" is a soup, nor that cooked or inedible items are not valid.

Before the soup-tasting, [Mayor Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis") states, "I trust that you all put high-quality ingredients in the pot this year. We don't want the governor to regret his visit to the valley!" At this point it is too late for the player to change the item already added to the soup.

Note that item quality, base sell price, and edibility (healing effect) are the three determining factors of the Governor's reaction. [Profession bonuses](/Skills "Skills") that increase the sell price of items (such as [Rancher](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming"), [Angler](/Fishing#Fishing_Skill "Fishing"), etc.) are not considered in the game's calculations.[[1]](#cite_note-governortaste-1)

There are 7 possible outcomes depending on the item added to the soup.

In a [multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer") game, all players must add an item to the soup, or the "Missing Something" response will occur. Following that, the final result is determined by the worst item added.

## History

* [1.2.31](/Version_History#1.2.31 "Version History"): Fixed grammar in Luau community soup text.
* [1.3.27](/Version_History#1.3.27 "Version History"): All players in a multiplayer game can contribute to the soup. [Mayor Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis")' shorts can be added to the soup.
* [1.3.28](/Version_History#1.3.28 "Version History"): Fixed music not playing at the Luau festival.
* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Fixed bug where NPCs would follow non-festival schedules after the festival.
* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): Added [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre")'s booth. Fixed bug where a [staircase](/Staircase "Staircase") could be added to the soup.
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): New items (including Mayonnaise, Jelly, and Pickles) can be added to the soup. The Luau's layout now changes every second year.
* [1.6.2](/Version_History#1.6.2 "Version History"): Wine and Juice have scaling edibility, changing some items' responses when added to the soup. [Void Eggs](/Void_Egg "Void Egg") are now poisonous, so they give the worst potluck response.
