The **Mystic Tree Seed** is a [tree](/Trees "Trees") seed that grows into a [Mystic Tree](/Mystic_Tree "Mystic Tree"). It can be [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting") after claiming [Foraging Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave").

Note that Mystic Tree Seeds can only be obtained by crafting. They cannot be obtained from chopping or shaking Mystic Trees. Also, Mystic Trees do not spread seeds when fully grown.[[1]](#cite_note-mysticseed-1)

Mystic Tree Seeds fertilized with [Tree Fertilizer](/Tree_Fertilizer "Tree Fertilizer") have a 30% chance of growing to the next stage each night, including during [Winter](/Winter "Winter"). Unfertilized seeds have a 15% chance of growing to the next stage each night during [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), or [Fall](/Fall "Fall").[[1]](#cite_note-mysticseed-1)
