It may also randomly appear at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="500"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,500–2,500g.

## Bundles

Caviar is an option for the ![Bundle Purple.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a1/Bundle_Purple.png/24px-Bundle_Purple.png) [The Missing Bundle](/Bundles#The_Missing_Bundle "Bundles") in the abandoned [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart").

## Tailoring

Caviar is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Fashion Hat.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/69/Fashion_Hat.png/24px-Fashion_Hat.png) [Fashion Hat](/Fashion_Hat "Fashion Hat").
