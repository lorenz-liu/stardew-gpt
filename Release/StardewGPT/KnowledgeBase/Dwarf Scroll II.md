The **Dwarf Scroll II** is an [Artifact](/Artifacts "Artifacts") that can be found by [tilling](/Hoes "Hoes") soil in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") (floors 1-39, 0.1% chance), as a drop from a [Frost Bat](/Bats "Bats") (floors 40-79), as a drop from a [Dust Sprite](/Dust_Sprite "Dust Sprite") (floors 40-79), or as a drop from Blue [Slimes](/Slimes "Slimes") or [Ghosts](/Ghost "Ghost").

## Donation

Donation of this item contributes to the total count of donations for the [Museum](/Museum "Museum").

Donating all 4 Dwarf Scrolls rewards the player with the [Dwarvish Translation Guide](/Dwarvish_Translation_Guide "Dwarvish Translation Guide").

## Tailoring

Dwarf Scroll II can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Gold Trimmed Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt222.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/ff/Shirt222.png/24px-Shirt222.png)](/File:Shirt222.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a green dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

One Dwarf Scroll II may be requested by [Ghostfish](/Ghostfish "Ghostfish") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 7 to 10.

## Tip

* Level 15 in the Mines has tillable soil near the entrance to the floor. The player can repeatedly revisit Floor 15 via the elevator to till the soil and farm for Dwarf Scrolls I and II.
