**Squid Ink** is an item which is dropped from [Squid Kids](/Squid_Kid "Squid Kid") in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") and [Blue Squids](/Blue_Squid "Blue Squid") in [the Dangerous Mines](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines"). It can also be produced by [Squids](/Squid "Squid") and [Midnight Squids](/Midnight_Squid "Midnight Squid") in [Fish Ponds](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"). [Elliott](/Elliott "Elliott") sells five Squid Inks in his [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="30">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 30 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg") each. It's used as an ingredient in [clothing dye](/Dyeing "Dyeing") and [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring").

It cannot be sold to the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild"). However, it can be sold to [Willy](/Willy "Willy") at his [Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop"). It must be shipped to complete the Shipping [Collection](/Collections#Items_Shipped "Collections").
