**Sam** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). He's one of the twelve characters available to [marry](/Marriage "Marriage"). He lives in the southern part of town, just north of the river at [1 Willow Lane](/1_Willow_Lane "1 Willow Lane").

Sam is an outgoing and energetic young man with a passion for music. He works part-time at [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart"). In his free time, he plays guitar. Despite his description in Dev Update 12, he does not actually ever play drums. He also skateboards and can be found playing videogames.

## Schedule

Sam can often be found practicing his guitar in [his bedroom](/1_Willow_Lane "1 Willow Lane"), sometimes with [Sebastian](/Sebastian "Sebastian") playing an electric piano. He's also frequently seen outside of his house playing a handheld video game, or attempting skateboard tricks. On weekends he can be found playing pool at [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon").

He works part-time on Monday and Wednesday at [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart") (if it is in business), or at the [Museum](/Museum "Museum") in the [Artifacts](/Artifacts "Artifacts") and [Minerals](/Minerals "Minerals") display (if the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") has been restored). He frequents the Stardrop Saloon during [spring](/Spring "Spring"), goes to [the Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") during [summer](/Summer "Summer"), and visits [the woods](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") west of town in the [fall](/Fall "Fall").

When it's raining he'll often stay home all day, or visit [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon") for a few hours.

On the 11th of Fall, he has an appointment at the [clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic").

His schedule can deviate if there are specific conditions like season, weather or certain days of the week.

After the [Beach Resort](/Ginger_Island#Beach_Resort "Ginger Island") on Ginger Island is unlocked, Sam may randomly spend the day there. After leaving the Island at 6pm, Sam will immediately go home to bed. Sam never visits the Resort on [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days or his checkup day at [Harvey's Clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic").

## Relationships

Sam lives with his mother [Jodi](/Jodi "Jodi") and younger brother [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent"). His mother spoils both of them, and claims that neither have ever had to do any chores. He is close to Vincent, and feels responsible for him during his dad's absence. In Spring, Year 2, Sam's father [Kent](/Kent "Kent") returns and resides in the house with the rest of the family. After marriage, Sam will visit his family regularly to check up on them.

[Sebastian](/Sebastian "Sebastian") and [Abigail](/Abigail "Abigail") are his bandmates and closest friends, and they often spend time together. When they are hanging out at [The Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon"), Sebastian is always seen beating Sam at pool, while Abigail watches from the couch. He is also friends with [Penny](/Penny "Penny"), and will dance with her at the [Flower Dance](/Flower_Dance "Flower Dance") if neither of them is dancing with the player.

## Marriage

:   *Main article: [Marriage](/Marriage "Marriage")*

Once married, Sam will move into the [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse"). Like other marriage candidates, he will add his own room to the right of the bedroom. He'll also set up a half-pipe behind the farmhouse where he'll sometimes go to practice his skateboarding tricks.

On rainy days, Sam may offer you a gift that he found "rolling around in the back of a drawer": [Cactus Fruit](/Cactus_Fruit "Cactus Fruit"), [Coconut](/Coconut "Coconut"), [Earth Crystal](/Earth_Crystal "Earth Crystal"), or [Geode](/Geode "Geode"). On rainy nights, he may offer you [Pizza](/Pizza "Pizza"). On days when Sam stays inside the farmhouse all day, he may offer you instant [Pancakes](/Pancakes "Pancakes"), explaining that he never learned to cook.

## Trivia

* Sam is allergic to pollen, which he mentions during the [Egg Festival](/Egg_Festival "Egg Festival"). He also mentions that his "nose is really happy" during summer due to the typically lower pollen count.
* "Sam" is short for "Samson", which he reveals to the player once they have a good relationship with him. He also states that he doesn't want the player to tell anyone.
* On one of Sam's portraits, his undershirt is yellow, but in his sprite, it is red.
