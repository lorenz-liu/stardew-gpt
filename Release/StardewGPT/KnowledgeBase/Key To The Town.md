The **Key To The Town** is used to enter all buildings in [Stardew Valley](/Stardew_Valley "Stardew Valley"), regardless of the time. It can be purchased for data-sort-value="20">![Qi Gem.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/78/Qi_Gem.png/24px-Qi_Gem.png) 20 [Qi Gems](/Qi_Gem "Qi Gem") from [Qi's Walnut Room](/Qi%27s_Walnut_Room "Qi's Walnut Room").

Once obtained, it can be found in the [Special Items & Powers](/Special_Items_%26_Powers "Special Items & Powers") tab in [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s Menu.

Despite its description, it has some restrictions, outlined in the "Notes" section listed below.

## Notes

* The key functions for homes and most shops in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"), and at the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild"), [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop"), [Marnie's Ranch](/Marnie%27s_Ranch "Marnie's Ranch"), and the [Wizard's Tower](/Wizard%27s_Tower "Wizard's Tower").
* The key does not function at homes outside of [Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") (*i.e.,* [Leah's Cottage](/Leah%27s_Cottage "Leah's Cottage") and [Elliott's Cabin](/Elliott%27s_Cabin "Elliott's Cabin")).
* The key does not function at the [Movie Theater](/Movie_Theater "Movie Theater") or the [Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis").
* The key does not function during [festival days](/Festivals "Festivals").
* For a complete list of shop schedules (with and without the Key To The Town), see [Shop Schedules](/Shop_Schedules "Shop Schedules").
