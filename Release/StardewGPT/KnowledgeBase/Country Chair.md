The **Country Chair** is a piece of [furniture](/Furniture "Furniture"). It can rotate into daily stock at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop") for data-sort-value="750">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)750g or the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for between data-sort-value="furniture"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)250–2,500g. It's also available from the [Furniture Catalogue](/Furniture_Catalogue "Furniture Catalogue") for data-sort-value="0">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)0g.

There is a small chance to receive this furniture item from slaying [monsters](/Monsters "Monsters"), breaking [crates and barrels](/The_Mines#Crates_and_Barrels "The Mines"), chopping [trees](/Trees "Trees"), shaking trees with seeds, digging [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot"), or [panning](/Pans "Pans").[[1]](#cite_note-rarecosmeticdrop-1)

## References

1. [↑](#cite_ref-rarecosmeticdrop_1-0) See `Utility::getRandomCosmeticItem` and `Utility::trySpawnRareObject` in the game code. Chances are determined in `GameLocation::monsterDrop`, `BreakableContainer::releaseContents`, `Tree::performToolAction`, `Tree::shake`, `GameLocation::digUpArtifactSpot`, and `Pan::getPanItems`.
