The **Keg** is a type of [Artisan Equipment](/Crafting#Artisan_Equipment "Crafting") used to make [Artisan Goods](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods"). A Keg is the reward for completing the ![Bundle Purple.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a1/Bundle_Purple.png/24px-Bundle_Purple.png) [Artisan Bundle](/Bundles#Artisan_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Pantry](/Bundles#Pantry "Bundles") **or** the ![Bundle Orange.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/95/Bundle_Orange.png/24px-Bundle_Orange.png) [Brewer's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Brewer.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Pantry (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Pantry "Remixed Bundles"). Four Kegs can be obtained as the 19th prize from the [Prize Machine](/Mayor%27s_Manor#Prize_Machine "Mayor's Manor") (50% chance).

## Preserves Jar vs. Keg

The [Preserves Jar](/Preserves_Jar "Preserves Jar") increases the profit of a crop using the following equation: (2 × Base Crop Value + 50), while the Keg multiplies the base value of [Fruits](/Fruits "Fruits") by 3, and most [Vegetables](/Vegetables "Vegetables") by 2.25. Because of this, low-value high-yield crops like [Corn](/Corn "Corn") or [Tomatoes](/Tomato "Tomato") are more valuable in the preserves jar. The Keg, on the other hand, favors crops with a much higher base value, such as [Ancient Fruit](/Ancient_Fruit "Ancient Fruit") or [Melons](/Melon "Melon"). The breakpoint is data-sort-value="50">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)50g for fruits and data-sort-value="200">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)200g for vegetables.

## Storage Options

### Inside Farm Buildings

Kegs can be stored in farm buildings.

A [Shed](/Shed "Shed"), optimally filled with kegs, holds 67 of them, while occupying only 21 tiles of land. An upgraded Big Shed, optimally filled, holds 137 kegs on the same land space. A [Barn](/Barn "Barn"), however, is cheaper, and holds 90 kegs on 28 tiles of land. The Deluxe Barn increases building costs considerably, but has so far been shown to be able to fit 136 kegs on the same land space as the initial Barn. [Coops](/Coop "Coop") are cheaper still, but smaller, permitting fewer kegs per tile of external farmland used. [Cabins](/Cabin "Cabin") are the cheapest initially, occupy the smallest land area of 15 tiles, but can only hold 42 kegs without upgrades, assuming the cabin is unclaimed and the gift and bed must remain. The first level of upgraded cabin can hold 114 kegs at a lower cost than any other option. While the second upgrade significantly raises costs, 351 kegs can be stored, in version 1.6, new cabin renovations were added, and 593 kegs can be stored, allowing the largest number of kegs per external farmland used.

## History

* [1.0](/Version_History#1.0 "Version History"): Introduced.
* [1.1](/Version_History#1.1 "Version History"): Mead Added. Removed [Clay](/Clay "Clay") from crafting recipe.
* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Green Tea Added. Adjusted processing time so that machines process 100 minutes from 2am to 6am, not every hour the player is asleep. Kegs can now be stacked in a player's [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory") and in [chests](/Chest "Chest").
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Positive energy foraged items (other than Mushrooms) can now be made into Juice. Green Tea now gives +0.5 Speed. Rice can now be placed into a Keg to make Vinegar. Can now be obtained as a prize from the [Prize Machine](/Mayor%27s_Manor#Prize_Machine "Mayor's Manor").
* [1.6.2](/Version_History#1.6.2 "Version History"): Wine Health and Energy now scale with base fruit's values. Juice Health and Energy now scale with base vegetable's values.
