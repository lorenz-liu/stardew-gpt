The **Wilted Bouquet** is a special gift that you can give to any or all [marriage](/Marriage "Marriage") candidates whom you are dating.

Giving a wilted bouquet immediately ends the dating relationship and reduces the [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") level with the marriage candidate to 5 hearts (1250 points). However, unlike having a [divorce](/Marriage#Divorce "Marriage") with a spouse, you are still able to talk and give gifts to them.

A Wilted Bouquet is created by putting a [Bouquet](/Bouquet "Bouquet") into a [furnace](/Furnace "Furnace") (requires one [coal](/Coal "Coal")). It will be finished crafting when the clock ticks over to the next 10-minute interval.

The bouquet can be sold for data-sort-value="100">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100g via the [shipping box](/Shipping "Shipping"), the same sale price as a regular Bouquet. Pierre will not buy them back.

## Tailoring

Wilted Bouquet is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Darkness Suit](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt291.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/d3/Shirt291.png/24px-Shirt291.png)](/File:Shirt291.png)

## Trivia

* Attempting to give your spouse a Wilted Bouquet will bring up a text box that says, "It wouldn't make sense to give this to <spouse name>.".
