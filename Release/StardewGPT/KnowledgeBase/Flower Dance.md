# Flower Dance

[![Flower Festival.jpg](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/ad/Flower_Festival.jpg/500px-Flower_Festival.jpg)](/File:Flower_Festival.jpg)

The **Flower Dance** is a [festival](/Festivals "Festivals") that takes place on the 24th of every [Spring](/Spring "Spring"). The player enters the dance by entering [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") between 9am and 2pm. The forest cannot be entered before 9am.

The dance takes place in the far west of the forest, across the bridge that is south of the [Wizard's Tower](/Wizard%27s_Tower "Wizard's Tower"). This area is accessible only during the Flower Dance.

On the day of the Festival, every home and shop in Stardew Valley is "locked" and cannot be entered.

During this festival, the player can dance with one of the [bachelors or bachelorettes](/Marriage#Marriage_Candidates "Marriage"). The player must talk to them until the "Ask <name> to be your dance partner?" dialogue pops up (normally 2x), but they will refuse unless the player has at least four hearts of [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") with them. The player can talk to the other villagers and they will have event-related dialogue.

Dancing with an NPC will increase friendship with that NPC by 1 heart (250 points). In a [multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer") game, players may also dance with each other. If the player is divorced, their ex will refuse their dance proposal and respond with angry dialogue instead.

Once the player talks to [Mayor Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis"), the dance begins. After the dance, the festival ends and the player is returned to [The Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") at 10pm.

## Trivia

* It is technically possible, though not easy, to get 4 hearts of [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") with all twelve marriage candidates in the first year.
* After the player is married, they can choose to dance with other partners. Their spouse may dance with someone else as well.
* Talking to [Haley](/Haley "Haley") before starting the Flower Dance will result in her saying that she is "practicing her dance moves". However, her sprite appears to just be wandering around aimlessly.
