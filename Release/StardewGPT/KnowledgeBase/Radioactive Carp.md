The **Radioactive Carp** is one of the five [Legendary Fish](/Fish#Legendary_Fish_II "Fish") that must be caught for [Mr. Qi](/Mr._Qi "Mr. Qi")'s [Extended Family Quest](/Quests#Extended_Family "Quests"). It has the same sell price, healing effect, location, and chance of hooking as the [Mutant Carp](/Mutant_Carp "Mutant Carp"). It can only be caught when the quest is active, but more than one can be caught per quest.

## Map

The Radioactive Carp can be found in [The Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers").

## Dyeing

It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a green dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Radioactive Carp can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"). The pond capacity is 1 fish and this cannot be increased. The only possible output is Radioactive Carp [Roe](/Roe "Roe").
