**Cauliflower** is a [vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from [Cauliflower Seeds](/Cauliflower_Seeds "Cauliflower Seeds") after 12 days. Cauliflower is one of only five crops that can grow into a [giant crop](/Crops#Giant_Crops "Crops").

## Stages

Cauliflower can become a [Giant Crop](/Crops#Giant_Crops "Crops").

## Tailoring

Cauliflower is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a dyeable ![Shirt159.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/99/Shirt159.png/24px-Shirt159.png) [Shirt And Belt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a white dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

* [Jodi](/Jodi "Jodi") will request a fresh cauliflower by mail on [Spring](/Spring "Spring") 19th, for the ["Jodi's Request" Quest](/Quests#Jodi.27s_Request "Quests"). The reward is data-sort-value="350">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)350g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
* Cauliflower may be randomly requested in [Spring](/Spring "Spring") at the "Help Wanted" board outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="525">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)525g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
