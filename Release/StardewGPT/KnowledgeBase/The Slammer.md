**The Slammer** is a [club weapon](/Weapons#Club "Weapons") that can be obtained as a [special item drop](/The_Mines#Special_Items "The Mines") on Floors 81-99 of [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern")[[1]](#cite_note-specialitem-1), or in the [Quarry Mine](/Quarry_Mine "Quarry Mine") by killing [special monsters](/The_Mines#Special_Items "The Mines") or by breaking [crates and barrels](/The_Mines#Crates_and_Barrels "The Mines"). It is a possible reward for the chest on floor 110 of the Mines if "remixed" mine rewards are selected in the [Advanced Options](/Options "Options") menu when starting a new game.
