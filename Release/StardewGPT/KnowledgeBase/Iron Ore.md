**Iron Ore** is obtained from [Iron Nodes](/Mining#Iron_Node "Mining") with a [pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes"). Mining an Iron Node will yield between 1 and 3 Iron Ore. The [Miner Profession](/Skills#Mining "Skills") can further increase this.

## Locations

* Can be bought at the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith") for data-sort-value="150">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150g each in Year 1 and for data-sort-value="250">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)250g each in Year 2+
* [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") (Floors 41 - 79) have a high concentration of [Iron Nodes](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining") with potential abundance at Floors 71 - 79.
* Nodes can spawn in the [Quarry](/Quarry "Quarry"), the [Quarry Mine](/Quarry_Mine "Quarry Mine"), or in the quarry on the [Hill-top](/Farm_Maps#Hilltop "Farm Maps") and [Four Corners](/Farm_Maps#Four_Corners "Farm Maps") Farm Maps.
* Can be found at any floor of the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") and [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon").
* Can be found by [tilling](/Hoes "Hoes") soil in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines").
* Can be found in breakable crates and barrels in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines").
* Can be dropped by [Metal Heads](/Metal_Head "Metal Head") in Floors 81-119 of [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), by [Stone Golems](/Stone_Golem "Stone Golem") in Floors 31-39 or by [Iron Slimes](/Slimes "Slimes").
* Can be found inside any of the 4 types of [Geode](/Minerals#Geodes "Minerals").
* Can be found in [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing").
* Can be found by [panning](/Pans "Pans").
* Occasionally sold at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="10"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100–1,000g.
* Can be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") outside the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith").
* Can be [recycled](/Recycling_Machine "Recycling Machine") from [Trash](/Trash_(item) "Trash (item)") with a 21% chance.
* An [Ice Pip](/Ice_Pip "Ice Pip") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") can produce 5 Iron Ore when the population of the pond reaches 3.
* Can be purchased from [Clint](/Clint "Clint")'s [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="4">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 4 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg") each.

## Smelting

Iron ore can be smelted in a [Furnace](/Furnace "Furnace") to create an [Iron Bar](/Iron_Bar "Iron Bar"). The smelting requires 5 iron ore and 1 [coal](/Coal "Coal") to begin and takes 2 in-game hours to complete.

## Tailoring

Iron Ore can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Turtleneck Sweater](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt178.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/89/Shirt178.png/24px-Shirt178.png)](/File:Shirt178.png)

## Quests

Ten Iron Ore are requested by [Ice Pip](/Ice_Pip "Ice Pip") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 1 to 3.
