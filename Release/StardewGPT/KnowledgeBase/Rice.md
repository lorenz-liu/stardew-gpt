**Rice** is a cooking ingredient that can be used in a variety of recipes. It can be purchased for data-sort-value="200">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)200g at [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") and for data-sort-value="250">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)250g at [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart").

Players have the ability to craft Rice from [Unmilled Rice](/Unmilled_Rice "Unmilled Rice") by using a [Mill](/Mill "Mill"). Once placed in the mill, one Unmilled Rice will make one Rice. Unmilled Rice placed inside will appear as Rice in the box beside the Mill the next morning. The building can process an unlimited amount of rice per day.

**Note**: Rice made using the mill is profitable; it has a higher sell price than Unmilled Rice. Using [Preserves Jars](/Preserves_Jar "Preserves Jar") would be slightly more profitable per unit of Unmilled Rice if there is sufficient capacity, as the resulting pickles would sell for 110g. However, Rice can be further processed into [Vinegar](/Vinegar "Vinegar") with a [Keg](/Keg "Keg"), producing two Vinegar per Rice. As Vinegar sells for 100g each, this ultimately results in more profit than using Preserves Jars.
