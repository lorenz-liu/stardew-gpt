A **Mystic Tree** is a [tree](/Trees "Trees") that grows from a [Mystic Tree Seed](/Mystic_Tree_Seed "Mystic Tree Seed"). The [crafting](/Crafting "Crafting") recipe for the seed becomes available after [Foraging Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave") is claimed. The tree yields [Mystic Syrup](/Mystic_Syrup "Mystic Syrup") every 7 days when [tapped](/Tapper "Tapper") (3 days with a [Heavy Tapper](/Heavy_Tapper "Heavy Tapper")). It can be chopped down with an [axe](/Axes "Axes"), producing 7-11 [Hardwood](/Hardwood "Hardwood"), plus 1 Hardwood from the stump.

By default, Mystic Trees have a 15% chance to grow to the next stage each night during [Spring](/Spring "Spring"), [Summer](/Summer "Summer"), or [Fall](/Fall "Fall"). With [Tree Fertilizer](/Tree_Fertilizer "Tree Fertilizer"), Mystic Trees have a 30% chance to grow to the next stage each night, including during [Winter](/Winter "Winter").[[1]](#cite_note-mystictrees-1) This means that unfertilized mystic trees usually grow in 26±12 days, while fertilized ones usually grow in 13±5 days.

Unlike other trees, Mystic Trees do not spread seeds on their own. Their seeds also do not drop when shaken or chopped. The tree will interact with [Mushroom Logs](/Mushroom_Log "Mushroom Log") to make more [Purple Mushrooms](/Purple_Mushroom "Purple Mushroom") available for harvest.
