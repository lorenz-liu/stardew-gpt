**Pumpkin Soup** is a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit").

One Pumpkin Soup can be purchased each year from [Traveling Merchant](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart")'s shop at the [Festival of Ice](/Festival_of_Ice "Festival of Ice") for data-sort-value="250">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)250g. It also may randomly appear in [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus")' shop on [Saturdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Saturday "Days of the Week"), in the [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") outside the saloon, or in [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon")'s rotating stock. 5 Pumpkin Soup may occasionally be found in a [treasure room](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern") in the Skull Cavern. It is also sold by the [Calico Egg Merchant](/Desert_Festival#Calico_Egg_Merchant "Desert Festival") in the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="10">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 10 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg"). One Pumpkin Soup may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box").

## Tailoring

Pumpkin Soup is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt059.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a6/Shirt059.png/24px-Shirt059.png)](/File:Shirt059.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").
