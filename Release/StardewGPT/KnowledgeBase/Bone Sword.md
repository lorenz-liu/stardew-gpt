The **Bone Sword** is a [sword weapon](/Weapons#Sword "Weapons") that can be purchased from the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild") for data-sort-value="6000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)6,000g after reaching floor 75 in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"). It also has a 4% chance to be dropped from [Skeletons](/Skeleton "Skeleton"). It is a possible reward for the chest on floor 90 of the Mines if "remixed" mine rewards are selected in the [Advanced Options](/Options "Options") menu when starting a new game.

## History

* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): Skeletons now have a 4% chance of dropping a Bone Sword.
