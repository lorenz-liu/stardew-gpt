**Spicy Eel** is a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit").

**Note:** This recipe is [profitable](/Cooking#Profitable_Recipes "Cooking") - it will result in profit when using the lowest quality ingredients.

Spicy Eel can be traded from the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for 1 [Ruby](/Ruby "Ruby") each. It may also randomly appear in [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus")' shop on [Saturdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Saturday "Days of the Week"), in the [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") outside the saloon, or in [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon")'s rotating stock. The [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") may trade 3 Spicy Eel for 1 [Treasure Appraisal Guide](/Treasure_Appraisal_Guide "Treasure Appraisal Guide"), provided the player has already obtained the power from that book. Up to 5 Spicy Eel may be sold by the [Calico Egg Merchant](/Desert_Festival#Calico_Egg_Merchant "Desert Festival") in the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="10">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 10 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg") each.

Spicy Eel can also be dropped by [Serpents](/Serpent "Serpent") in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") (6% chance). A [Lava Eel](/Lava_Eel "Lava Eel") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") may produce 5 Spicy Eel when the population of the pond is at least 9 (3-4% chance). One Spicy Eel may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box"). Five Spicy Eel can be obtained as a prize from the [Prize Machine](/Mayor%27s_Manor#Prize_Machine "Mayor's Manor").

## Tailoring

Spicy Eel is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Flames Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt226.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/4f/Shirt226.png/24px-Shirt226.png)](/File:Shirt226.png) It can also be used as a red dye in the dye pots, located at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").
