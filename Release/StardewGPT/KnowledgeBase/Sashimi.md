**Sashimi** is a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit").

Sashimi may randomly appear in [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus")' shop on [Saturdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Saturday "Days of the Week"), in the [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") outside the saloon, or in [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon")'s rotating stock. [Linus](/Linus "Linus") may randomly send one Sashimi in the mail as a gift. One Sashimi may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box").

Note: This recipe can be [profitable](/Cooking#Profitable_Recipes "Cooking") if using [fish](/Fish "Fish") worth less than data-sort-value="75">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)75g (see [When to turn fish into sashimi](/Fish#When_to_turn_fish_into_Sashimi "Fish") for more info). Saving fish worth less than data-sort-value="75">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)75g for [Help Wanted quests](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") may be a better option, though, since these quests generally pay triple the fish's base value. ([Periwinkle](/Periwinkle "Periwinkle") is an exception -- at data-sort-value="20">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)20g base price it's more profitable to turn into Sashimi than save for quests).

## Bundles

Sashimi is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Recipes

Sashimi is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Sashimi is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt036.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/20/Shirt036.png/24px-Shirt036.png)](/File:Shirt036.png) It can also be used as a red dye in the dye pot, located at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre") requests Sashimi by mail on the 21st of [Spring](/Spring "Spring") (year 2) in the ["Pierre's Notice" quest](/Quests#Pierre.27s_Notice "Quests"). The reward is data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.
