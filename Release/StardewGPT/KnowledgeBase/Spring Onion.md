The **Spring Onion** is a [foraged](/Foraging "Foraging") item found in [Spring](/Spring "Spring") in [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest"), but only on the southeast island (*i.e.,* north of [the Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers") entrance). Unlike other foraged items, spring onions are respawned daily: all unharvested onions are removed at the end of each day, and new onions may appear. The [Livin' Off The Land](/Livin%27_Off_The_Land "Livin' Off The Land") [Television](/Television "Television") channel airs a tip about Spring Onions on day 4. Spring Onions may randomly appear in the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart")'s stock for data-sort-value="8"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100–1,000g.

Harvesting a Spring Onion grants the player 3 Foraging XP. Only one onion can be harvested per plant; the [Gatherer](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") profession does not apply to spring onions.

Once the player has earned 8 hearts of [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") with [Jas](/Jas "Jas") and [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent"), [Spring Onion Mastery](/Spring_Onion_Mastery "Spring Onion Mastery") can be obtained, which permanently increases the sell price of Spring Onions by 5×.

## Bundles

Spring Onion is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Spring Onion is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [![Shirt258.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/1b/Shirt258.png/24px-Shirt258.png)](/File:Shirt258.png) [Spring Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring").

## Quests

Spring Onion is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
