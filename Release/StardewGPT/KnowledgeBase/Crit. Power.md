# Crit. Power

:   *For technical details about Crit. Power mechanics, see [Combat/Mechanics](/Combat/Mechanics "Combat/Mechanics").*

**Crit. Power** [![Crit. Power.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/06/Crit._Power.png/24px-Crit._Power.png)](/File:Crit._Power.png) (short for Critical Power) is a [Combat](/Combat "Combat") statistic. A critical strike unaffected by Crit. Power does 3x base damage. Crit. Power causes this multiplier to increase so that critical strikes do more damage.

## Increasing Crit. Power

The following items increase Crit. Power. The effect is permanent when the item is equipped. [Forging](/Forge#Weapon_forging "Forge") a weapon with [Jades](/Jade "Jade") will increase its natural Crit. Power stat by 5 per Jade.
