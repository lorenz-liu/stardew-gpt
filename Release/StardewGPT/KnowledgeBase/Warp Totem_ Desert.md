The **Warp Totem: Desert** is a [Warp Totem](/Warp_Totem "Warp Totem") that transports the player to [The Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") by selecting it in inventory and right-clicking. It can be [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting") after purchasing the recipe from the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for 10 [Iridium Bars](/Iridium_Bar "Iridium Bar"). It can also be purchased from the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for 3 [Omni Geodes](/Omni_Geode "Omni Geode").

## Bundles

The Warp Totem: Desert is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

The Warp Totem: Desert can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Totem Mask.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/cc/Totem_Mask.png/24px-Totem_Mask.png) [Totem Mask](/Totem_Mask "Totem Mask"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

The Warp Totem: Desert is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Trivia

If warping to the desert then returning before [Pam](/Pam "Pam") arrives at the [Bus Stop](/Bus_Stop "Bus Stop"), the player will be shown driving the bus.
