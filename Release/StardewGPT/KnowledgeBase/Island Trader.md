The **Island Trader** is a bluebird merchant located on the North side of [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island#Island_North "Ginger Island"). Like the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader"), the Island Trader does not accept gold but only barters in exchange for other items.

The Island Trader is unlocked for data-sort-value="10">![Golden Walnut.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/5/54/Golden_Walnut.png/24px-Golden_Walnut.png) 10 [Golden Walnuts](/Golden_Walnut "Golden Walnut") after purchasing the [Island Farmhouse](/Island_Farmhouse "Island Farmhouse").
