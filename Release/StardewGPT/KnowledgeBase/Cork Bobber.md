The **Cork Bobber** is a [tackle](/Tackle "Tackle") that helps [fishing](/Fishing "Fishing") by increasing the [fishing bar size](/Fishing#Bar_Size "Fishing") by 24 pixels (48 pixels if the player is using an [Advanced Iridium Rod](/Advanced_Iridium_Rod "Advanced Iridium Rod") with 2 Cork Bobbers).[[1]](#cite_note-bobberbar-1) It can be [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting") or purchased from [Willy's Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop") for data-sort-value="750">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)750g after reaching [Fishing level 7](/Fishing#Fishing_Skill "Fishing"). It may also randomly appear at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="250"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)750–1,250g. It can also be found in [Mystery Boxes](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") and [Golden Mystery Boxes](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box") after reaching Fishing level 6.

Only the [Iridium Rod](/Iridium_Rod "Iridium Rod") or the [Advanced Iridium Rod](/Advanced_Iridium_Rod "Advanced Iridium Rod") can equip tackle. The Iridium Rod can only attach one tackle at a time, while the Advanced Iridium Rod can attach two. To attach tackle, left-click on the tackle, then right-click on the rod. To remove tackle, first right-click to remove any bait, then right-click to remove the tackle. For an Advanced Iridium Rod, the left tackle will be the first one removed.

Tackle does not stack in [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory") or [chests](/Chest "Chest"); each tackle takes up one inventory slot.
