**Shrimp Cocktail** is a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit"). It can also be found in [Golden Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Golden_Treasure_Chests "Fishing") (7% chance).[[1]](#cite_note-chestchance-1) One Shrimp Cocktail may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box").

## Tailoring

Shrimp Cocktail is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Shrimp Enthusiast Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt286.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3c/Shirt286.png/24px-Shirt286.png)](/File:Shirt286.png) It can also be used as a red dye in the dye pots, located at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").
