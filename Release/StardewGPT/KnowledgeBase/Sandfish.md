The **Sandfish** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught in the pond at [The Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") during all seasons. It can also randomly be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") after unlocking The Desert, or at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="75"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)225–1,000g.

Sandfish can be caught regardless of time of day using [Magic Bait](/Magic_Bait "Magic Bait").

## Tailoring

Sandfish can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [Fish Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt158.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/e7/Shirt158.png/24px-Shirt158.png)](/File:Shirt158.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Sandfish can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 4 days. The initial pond capacity is 3 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing two quests. The primary output is yellow Sandfish [Roe](/Roe "Roe"), but [Cactus Seeds](/Cactus_Seeds "Cactus Seeds") are also possible at population 10.

## Quests

* **Item Delivery:**  After unlocking [The Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert"), Sandfish may be randomly requested at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="225">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)225g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* Two Sandfish may be requested by [Dorado](/Dorado "Dorado"), [Lingcod](/Lingcod "Lingcod"), or [Pike](/Pike "Pike") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 7 to 10.
* [Willy](/Willy "Willy") requests the player to catch 3 Sandfish during the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for his [fishing challenge](/Desert_Festival#Willy.27s_Fishing_Quests "Desert Festival") on Spring 15. The reward is data-sort-value="25">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 25.

## Notes

* While the Sandfish has a theoretical maximum [Size](/Fishing#Fish_Size_.26_Quality "Fishing") of 25", in actual gameplay it is impossible to catch a Sandfish longer than 23" due to the Desert pond's tiles only having a maximum [distance from land](/Fishing#Distance_from_Land "Fishing") of 2. A Fishing level of at least 18 is required to catch a 23" Sandfish.
