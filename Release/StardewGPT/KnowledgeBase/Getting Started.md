*[Stardew Valley](/Stardew_Valley "Stardew Valley")* is an open-ended game, allowing [the player](/The_Player "The Player") to [achieve](/Achievements "Achievements") goals non-linearly.  
There are various ways to earn [income](/Gold "Gold") and many activities to choose from including [farming](/Farming "Farming"), [fishing](/Fishing "Fishing"), [foraging](/Foraging "Foraging"), [mining](/Mining "Mining"), [combat (fighting enemies)](/The_Mines "The Mines"), [crafting](/Crafting "Crafting"), [cooking](/Cooking "Cooking"), [gift giving](/Villagers "Villagers"), completing [quests](/Quests "Quests"), donating to the [Museum](/Museum "Museum") and restoring the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center").

## Character Creation

[![Character creation menu.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/29/Character_creation_menu.png/350px-Character_creation_menu.png)](/File:Character_creation_menu.png)

:   *Main article: [The Player](/The_Player "The Player")*

The character creation screen allows customization options, such as character Appearance, Gender, Name, Farm Name, Favorite Thing, and Animal Preference.

* There are 2 genders, 24 skin tones, 74 hairstyles, 112 [shirts](/Tailoring#Shirts "Tailoring"), 4 [pants](/Tailoring#Pants "Tailoring"), 31 accessories, and 10 [animals](/Animals#Cat_or_Dog "Animals") (5 cats and 5 dogs) to choose from.
* The [villagers](/Villagers "Villagers") will refer to you matching the case you put your name in - for example, if you put all lowercase, the first letter will not be corrected to uppercase. {confirmed on mobile iOS and Nintendo Switch}
* The sliders next to eye color, hair color, and pants color represent hue, saturation, and value.
* Animal Preference determines which pet will be brought by [Marnie](/Marnie "Marnie").
* Gender does not affect in-game [marriage](/Marriage "Marriage").
* The "Favorite Thing" will be referenced in-game when the player eats a [Stardrop](/Stardrop "Stardrop"). If the player chooses [ConcernedApe](/ConcernedApe "ConcernedApe") (Capital C and A) as their favorite thing, the following message will appear when eaten.

[![ConcernedApe Favourite.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/ec/ConcernedApe_Favourite.png/500px-ConcernedApe_Favourite.png)](/File:ConcernedApe_Favourite.png)

If the player puts anything containing Stardew (capital S), then the following message will appear.

[![StardewFavorite.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/4d/StardewFavorite.png/500px-StardewFavorite.png)](/File:StardewFavorite.png)

Players can later change their appearance for data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g, in the [Wizard's Tower](/Wizard%27s_Tower "Wizard's Tower"), after reaching 4 Hearts of [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") with him or gaining access to the [Witch's Hut](/Witch%27s_Hut "Witch's Hut") and using the portal within.

At the character creation menu, there is a button with a wrench icon ![Advanced Options Button.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/cd/Advanced_Options_Button.png/24px-Advanced_Options_Button.png) that contains all of the [Advanced Options](/Options "Options"), which are:

* Changing the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") bundles between normal and remixed
* Making it so the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") is completable in year one by guaranteeing that the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") will sell a [Red Cabbage Seed](/Red_Cabbage_Seeds "Red Cabbage Seeds") sometime in the first year
* Changing the chest rewards in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") between normal and remixed
* Making it so [monsters](/Monsters "Monsters") can spawn on the farm
* Changing the profit margin
* Choosing how many starter cabins there are and how far apart they are
* Setting the randomization in the game from the new randomization system to the old one

## Preparing Your First Harvest

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/85/Parsnipseeds.png/48px-Parsnipseeds.png)](/File:Parsnipseeds.png)

Gift from Mayor Lewis

On all farm maps except for the [Meadowlands](/Farm_Maps#Meadowlands "Farm Maps") farm, [Mayor Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis") gives you 15 [Parsnip Seeds](/Parsnip_Seeds "Parsnip Seeds") to start with. On the Meadowlands farm, you start with a [coop](/Coop "Coop"), 2 [chickens](/Chicken "Chicken"), and Mayor Lewis gifts you 15 [Hay](/Hay "Hay").

* Use your [axe](/Axes "Axes"), [pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes"), and [scythe](/Scythe "Scythe") on the logs, stones, grass and bushes on your farm to clear about 15 tiles.
* Use your [hoe](/Hoes "Hoes") to till the soil and plant the 15 [Parsnip Seeds](/Parsnip_Seeds "Parsnip Seeds") provided by [Mayor Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis"). You obtain these on your first day on the floor inside your home.
* Use your [watering can](/Watering_Cans "Watering Cans") to water your seeds and soil.
* Continue watering your [crops](/Crops "Crops") every day until they grow in to harvestable crops. Your parsnips will be ready to harvest in 4 days.

### Watering Can

:   *Main article: [Watering Cans](/Watering_Cans "Watering Cans")*

[Crops](/Crops "Crops") needs to be watered daily in order to grow. The starting watering can only waters one tile at a time and can be refilled at any pond, river, or other body of water. This includes the kitchen sink, after getting your first house upgrade.

Crops need to be watered every day. Crops that are not watered will not die, but they will not grow either.

The best times to upgrade the watering can are the day before it is predicted to rain, as the rain will water the crops, or the 27th day of the [season](/Seasons "Seasons") after watering any crops that are expected to ripen on the 28th. The [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith") will need 2 days to upgrade any tools, including the watering can.

[Sprinklers](/Crafting#Sprinklers "Crafting"), which must be crafted and placed, will automatically water surrounding tiles every morning. Throughout the gameplay, sprinklers become available in 3 tiers that can water an increasing number of surrounding tiles, starting with 4 tiles (immediately adjacent to the sprinkler), 8 tiles (in the surrounding 3x3 area), and up to 24 tiles (in the surrounding 5x5 area). The sprinkler range can be increased with [Pressure Nozzles](/Pressure_Nozzle "Pressure Nozzle").

## Energy

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/35/HealthBar.png/60px-HealthBar.png)](/File:HealthBar.png)

Energy & Health

:   *Main article: [Energy](/Energy "Energy")*

Energy limits productivity. One of the main ways to overcome this is by eating [food](/Cooking "Cooking"). Eating food is an action, for which default controls are Right Click or type X with the food highlighted in your inventory and then click Yes in the confirmation dialogue to eat the food. Most [crops](/Crops "Crops") and many [foraged items](/Foraging "Foraging") can be eaten in order to regain energy. Some foods can also subtract energy; hover the cursor over the food to see how much energy it gives/takes away.

Sleeping at the end of the day will restore the players' energy. After 12:00AM, the player won't restore 100% of the energy. If the player is exhausted when they go to bed or forced sleep at 2:00AM, they'll wake up in the morning with their energy level about half restored.

In [Multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer"), lying in bed will regain energy over time.

The [Spa](/Spa "Spa") will fully restore energy over a short period of time.

## Skills

:   *Main article: [Skills](/Skills "Skills")*

To level up skills, the player must complete certain actions related to that skill. [Farming](/Farming "Farming") XP is earned by harvesting a fully grown crop and taking care of animals. [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") XP is earned by foraging or chopping down trees. [Mining](/Mining "Mining") XP is earned by breaking rocks and ore deposits. Fishing and using [crab pots](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot") will increase [fishing](/Fishing "Fishing") XP. Killing monsters will increase [combat](/Combat "Combat") XP.

As the skill level increases, the player will earn crafting recipes (every level except level 5 and 10) or professions (on level 5 and 10).

Professions (level 5 and 10) can give the player a boost on yielding products, including more products gained, additional selling price, using less inventories and others. The player's choice of a level 5 profession will determine which profession choices are available at level 10.

## Villagers and Friendship

:   *Main articles: [Villagers](/Villagers "Villagers"), [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship")*
:   *See also: [Marriage](/Marriage "Marriage"), [List of All Gifts](/List_of_All_Gifts "List of All Gifts")*

Talking and giving gifts to villagers will increase their friendship rating. You can talk to a villager by right-clicking on them. You can give a gift by highlighting it in your inventory or selecting it by typing its hotkey number and then left-clicking on the intended recipient.

As friendship is earned villagers will send [cooking](/Cooking "Cooking") recipes or gifts through the mail, give you access to their bedrooms, and treat you differently in dialogue. Unique cutscenes and interactions can also occur as friendship is earned. The player must also earn a high level of friendship in order to date, and eventually marry, an eligible villager.

Whenever you talk or give a gift to a villager you get friendship points. You need 250 points to get 1 heart with them. Talking gives +20 points which can only be done once per day. Two gifts can be given per week, 1 gift per day.

## Television

:   *Main article: [Television](/Television "Television")*

The television provides useful information, such as [weather](/Weather "Weather") forecasts, daily [luck](/Luck "Luck"), free [cooking](/Cooking "Cooking") recipes, and other general tips. All players will have one inside their home when beginning a new game.
