**Pepper Poppers** is a [cooked dish](/Cooking "Cooking"). It is prepared using either the kitchen inside an upgraded [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") or a [Cookout Kit](/Cookout_Kit "Cookout Kit").

The [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") trades 2 Pepper Poppers for 1 [Stardew Valley Almanac](/Stardew_Valley_Almanac "Stardew Valley Almanac"). It may also randomly appear in [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus")' shop on [Saturdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Saturday "Days of the Week"), in the [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") outside the saloon, or in [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon")'s rotating stock. [Shane](/Shane "Shane") may occasionally send the player Pepper Poppers in the mail as a gift. He also sells up to 3 sets of 3 Pepper Poppers in his [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="30">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 30 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg") each. One can sometimes be purchased at the [Stardew Valley Fair](/Stardew_Valley_Fair "Stardew Valley Fair") for data-sort-value="250">![Token.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/29/Token.png/24px-Token.png)250. One Pepper Poppers may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box").

## Bundles

Pepper Poppers are not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Pepper Poppers are used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Shirt180.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/17/Shirt180.png/24px-Shirt180.png) [Tunnelers Jersey](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a brown dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

Pepper Poppers are not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
