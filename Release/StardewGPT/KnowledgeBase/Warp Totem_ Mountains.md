The **Warp Totem: Mountains** is a [Warp Totem](/Warp_Totem "Warp Totem") that transports the player to [The Mountain](/The_Mountain "The Mountain") by selecting it in inventory and right-clicking. It can be [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting") when the recipe becomes available at [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") Level 7.

One Warp Totem: Mountains may be received from opening a [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box").

The following [Crab Pot](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot") [fish](/Fish "Fish") have a small daily chance to produce one Warp Totem: Mountains in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") once the population is 9 or higher: [Crayfish](/Crayfish "Crayfish"), [Periwinkle](/Periwinkle "Periwinkle"), and [Snail](/Snail "Snail"). All fish have the same daily chance (7% for population level 9, and 7% for population level 10).

Starting [Summer](/Summer "Summer") 1 of year 1, there is a 10% chance for a Warp Totem: Mountains to replace [Clay](/Clay "Clay") in an [Artifact Spot](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot").

## Bundles

The Warp Totem: Mountains is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

The Warp Totem: Mountains can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Totem Mask.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/cc/Totem_Mask.png/24px-Totem_Mask.png) [Totem Mask](/Totem_Mask "Totem Mask"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

The Warp Totem: Mountains is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
