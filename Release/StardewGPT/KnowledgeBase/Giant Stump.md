The **Giant Stump** is located in the northern area of [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") to the west of [Marnie's Ranch](/Marnie%27s_Ranch "Marnie's Ranch").

It begins as a huge tree with no notable attributes. It becomes the Giant Stump after a [strong wind storm](/Random_Events#Strong_Wind_Storm "Random Events") fells the tree. Interacting with the stump starts "The Giant Stump" quest. Later, it becomes the home to a raccoon, who populates it with his wife and their children over the course of his request questline.

## Questline

**Note:** After ["The Giant Stump"](/Quests#The_Giant_Stump "Quests") quest, the rest isn't a true "quest" as it is not one of the journaled [quests](/Quests "Quests") but rather a series of interactable requests, similar to those requests given by the [Trash Bear](/Cindersap_Forest#Trash_Bear "Cindersap Forest").

### Pre-habitation

After the [strong wind storm random event](/Random_Events#Strong_Wind_Storm "Random Events") occurs, the resulting stump has a "?" dialog which may be interacted with to start ["The Giant Stump"](/Quests#The_Giant_Stump "Quests") quest, where the player must repair the stump with 100 [Hardwood](/Hardwood "Hardwood").

### Raccoon's Requests

The day after the stump is repaired, a raccoon moves in. Following this, he has a series of requests for items, each yielding rewards. The requested items often require the use of various [Artisan Equipment](/Crafting#Artisan_Equipment "Crafting"), such as the [Dehydrator](/Dehydrator "Dehydrator"), [Fish Smoker](/Fish_Smoker "Fish Smoker"), and [Preserves Jar](/Preserves_Jar "Preserves Jar").

After completing a request, a new request will be made after 7 days, which is indicated by the appearance of his wife (and later on, children). Completing 9 requests will earn the player the Good Neighbors [Achievement](/Achievement "Achievement"). The player can continue to complete additional requests for more rewards. The items requested will usually be related to and obtainable in the current season. If a request is generated in the last week (Days 22-28) of any season, then the Raccoon will ask for items related to the following season (*e.g.*, a request made on Spring 24 will ask for Summer items).[[1]](#cite_note-seasonend-1)

## Raccoon Wife's Shop

After completing the Raccoon's first request, his wife may be interacted with to access her shop. Her shop does not use [Gold](/Gold "Gold"), but rather barters for other items.

Her shop initially offers all seed spot crop seeds. Additional items are unlocked after completing [requests](#Raccoon.27s_Requests).
