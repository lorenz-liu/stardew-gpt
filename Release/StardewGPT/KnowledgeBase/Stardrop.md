A **Stardrop** is a special item that permanently increases [the player](/The_Player "The Player")'s maximum [energy](/Energy "Energy") by 34 points. The player must immediately consume any Stardrop as soon as it is obtained; it is impossible to add a Stardrop to [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory") or save it to eat later.

A maximum of seven Stardrops are available in any game, which together result in 508 maximum energy, nearly doubling the initial value of 270. Finding all Stardrops is necessary to achieve [Perfection](/Perfection "Perfection").

Consuming a Stardrop will restore all of the player's energy as well as increase maximum energy, regardless of how low the energy was before consuming it. No health is restored when consuming a stardrop.

## Locations

One Stardrop can be obtained from each of the following sources:

* Sold for data-sort-value="2000">![Token.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/29/Token.png/24px-Token.png)2,000 at the [Stardew Valley Fair](/Stardew_Valley_Fair "Stardew Valley Fair").
* Obtained from the treasure chest on floor 100 in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines").
* From the player's spouse or roommate when friendship level reaches 12.5 hearts.
  + In [multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer") games where two players marry each other, a stardrop appears in a giftbox in front of each player when they enter their home after the wedding.
  + Only one spouse/roommate stardrop can be obtained, regardless of divorce or remarriage.
* Sold by [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") for data-sort-value="20000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)20,000g in [the Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers").
* Obtained from [Old Master Cannoli](/Old_Master_Cannoli "Old Master Cannoli") in the [Secret Woods](/Secret_Woods "Secret Woods") after giving him a [Sweet Gem Berry](/Sweet_Gem_Berry "Sweet Gem Berry").
* Received in a letter from [Willy](/Willy "Willy") the day after attaining the *[Master Angler Achievement](/Achievements "Achievements")*.
* Reward for donating all 95 items to the [Museum](/Museum "Museum").

## Text

When consumed, one of two possible texts is displayed, both referencing the "Favorite Thing" set when creating a new profile:

* "It's strange, but the taste reminds you of [Favorite Thing]."
* "Your mind is filled with thoughts of [Favorite Thing]."

## Trivia

* Stardrops used to be called "Cosmic Fruit" in early versions of the game. ([forum post](https://forums.stardewvalley.net/threads/help-have-all-the-stardrops-but-game-says-im-missing-one.3746/#post-23498))
* [Willy](/Willy "Willy") mentions a Stardrop in his story at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star"). He says that some people claim the Stardrop is somehow connected to the Winter Star that is only visible from Stardew Valley.
* After achieving [Perfection](/Perfection "Perfection"), a stone carving of a Stardrop will appear above [Grandpa's Shrine](/The_Farm#Grandpa.27s_Shrine "The Farm").
* Prior to [version 1.3.27](/Version_History#1.3.27 "Version History") a Stardrop could be taken into [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory") and then placed in a [Chest](/Chest "Chest"), gifted (as a [universally hated](/Friendship#Universal_Hates "Friendship") gift), used in the Grange Display at the [Stardew Valley Fair](/Stardew_Valley_Fair "Stardew Valley Fair"), or sold in the [shipping bin](/Shipping "Shipping") for data-sort-value="7777">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)7,777g. As of v1.3.27, a stardrop obtained using the [item ID glitch](/The_Player#Exploits "The Player") can still be sold in the shipping bin or gifted as a universally hated gift in PC or mobile versions of the game.
* The Stardrop was added to the game [Terraria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terraria "wikipedia:Terraria") with ConcernedApe's permission in October 2022.
