The **Mango Tree** is a type of [fruit tree](/Fruit_Trees "Fruit Trees"). The [sapling](/Mango_Sapling "Mango Sapling") can be purchased at the [Island Trader](/Island_Trader "Island Trader") for 75 [Mussels](/Mussel "Mussel") or found in a [Golden Coconut](/Golden_Coconut "Golden Coconut") when broken. It takes 28 days to grow to maturity, after which it will produce one [Mango](/Mango "Mango") each day during the [Summer](/Summer "Summer") in Stardew Valley or year-round on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") or in the [Greenhouse](/Greenhouse "Greenhouse"). It can only grow in the center of a 3x3 square, each square of which must be kept completely clear of objects, flooring, and terrain features (including grass). The 3x3 grid must not overlap the 3x3 grid of another Fruit Tree.

For each full year after maturing, fruit trees will produce higher quality fruit, up to iridium star quality after three years. Note that the 3x3 square around the tree doesn't have to be kept clear once the tree has fully grown.

If struck by lightning during a [storm](/Weather "Weather"), a fruit tree will produce [Coal](/Coal "Coal") for 4 days instead of fruit.

Fruit trees can be planted in the [Greenhouse](/Greenhouse "Greenhouse").
