The **Prehistoric Scapula** is an [Artifact](/Artifacts "Artifacts") that can be found from:

* Digging up an [Artifact Spot](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") in [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") or [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town").
* Mining [Bone Nodes](/Mining#Bone_Node "Mining") on the [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") Dig Site.
* Dropped by killing [Skeletons](/Skeleton "Skeleton") in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines").
* Gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") dog with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals").

## Donation

Donation of this item contributes to the total count of donations for the [Museum](/Museum "Museum").

Donating this item, along with the [Prehistoric Skull](/Prehistoric_Skull "Prehistoric Skull") and [Skeletal Hand](/Skeletal_Hand "Skeletal Hand"), will reward the player with the [Sloth Skeleton L](/Sloth_Skeleton "Sloth Skeleton") statue.

## Bone Mill

The Prehistoric Scapula can be turned into [Quality Fertilizer](/Quality_Fertilizer "Quality Fertilizer"), [Speed-Gro](/Speed-Gro "Speed-Gro"), [Deluxe Speed-Gro](/Deluxe_Speed-Gro "Deluxe Speed-Gro") or [Tree Fertilizer](/Tree_Fertilizer "Tree Fertilizer") in the [Bone Mill](/Bone_Mill "Bone Mill").

## Bundles

Prehistoric Scapula is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Recipes

Prehistoric Scapula is not used in any recipes.

## Tailoring

Prehistoric Scapula is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt014.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/0f/Shirt014.png/24px-Shirt014.png) [Skeleton Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a white dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests
