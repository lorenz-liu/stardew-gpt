The **Movie Theater** is a building that allows players to watch movies, alone or with a guest, once every week. It is unlocked either by completing the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") or all [community upgrades](/Joja_Community_Development_Form "Joja Community Development Form") offered by [Morris](/Morris "Morris").

If players choose to restore the Community Center, the night before the first rainy or stormy day after restoring the Center, there is a cutscene of a lightning bolt striking the doors to the abandoned [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart"). Afterwards, the player may enter the abandoned JojaMart and find [the Missing Bundle](/Bundles#The_Missing_Bundle "Bundles"). The night after completing the Missing Bundle, the [Junimos](/Junimos "Junimos") will build a Movie Theater.

If players choose the Joja route, they can purchase the Movie Theater from Morris for data-sort-value="500000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500,000g after completing all other [community upgrades](/Joja_Community_Development_Form "Joja Community Development Form"). The [Joja Warehouse](/Joja_Warehouse "Joja Warehouse") will then become a Movie Theater.

Players must purchase a [Movie Ticket](/Movie_Ticket "Movie Ticket") for data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g to enter the Movie Theater. A second Movie Ticket can also be purchased for data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g and given to a villager in the same way a gift is given. This will invite the villager to attend the movie with the player. To see the movie with the villager, enter the theater on the same day the ticket is gifted; otherwise, the ticket is forgotten.

In a [Multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer") game, it is possible to watch a movie with other players. To do this, all participating players must gather in the lobby before the movie starts. A message suggesting you will watch the movie by yourself still displays when entering the building, regardless of other players being present.

## Movies

There are 8 movies in total. Movies repeat on a 2-year cycle, based on the season in which the player unlocks the Movie Theater. All four "year 1" movies will cycle, followed by year 2 movies, then repeat. The year in the table below refers to the year in which the player first unlocks the Theater.

Asking a guest to a loved movie earns 200 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points with the guest, a liked movie earns 100 Friendship points, and a disliked movie neither earns nor costs friendship points with the guest.

Note that [Penny](/Penny "Penny") will love any movie if [Pam](/Pam "Pam") is also present. However, this is only possible in multiplayer, as Pam will not attend the movie when inviting Penny.

## Concessions

Players can choose to purchase a snack for their guest at the concessions counter. Purchasing a loved snack earns 50 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points with the guest, a liked snack earns 25 Friendship points, and a disliked snack neither earns nor costs friendship points with the guest.

Only five foods out of the following are randomly offered at a time for the player to select for their guest. The game tries to populate the list with one loved snack, two liked snacks, and one disliked snack among the five snacks available at the counter, if they exist. If the theater is built from the [Joja Warehouse](/Joja_Warehouse "Joja Warehouse"), JojaCorn is always available for purchase.[[1]](#cite_note-concessions-1)

## Crane Game

[![CraneGame.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3c/CraneGame.png/200px-CraneGame.png)](/File:CraneGame.png)

Inside the lobby on the lefthand side is a Crane Game machine. Players can play the Crane Game for data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g to win prizes. The crane game will always be occupied and unplayable during the player's first visit to the Movie Theater. Afterwards, there is a 25% chance that the Crane Game will be occupied prior to the movie. There is no limit to how many times the Crane Game can be played when it is available, but it must be played before watching the movie as it will always be occupied once the movie has finished.

After paying to play, the player has 3 chances to grab a prize. There is a 15-second timer for each attempt, which begins when the player first moves the crane. Successfully grabbing a prize on chance 1 or 2 does not end the game, giving the player the opportunity to win 3 prizes. Each time the player pays for a new game, the prizes will be randomized.

The crane is positioned using 2 movements. The first movement is horizontal, and places the crane (right-arrow on keyboard). The second movement is vertical, and drops the crane (down-arrow on keyboard). Once the down-arrow button is released, the crane will drop to the position of its shadow. When the crane reaches its shadow, it will stop and grab any prize at that location.

The crane may drop the prize on its way to the collection chute on the left. The further away from the chute the item is, and the less firm the crane's grasp on the item is, the more likely it is to drop it. Centering the crane with the item will give it a firmer grasp and reduce the chance of the item being dropped before reaching the collection chute.

Prizes include items on the conveyor belts as well as some of the stationary items shown. Some of the more rare prizes (like the [Dinosaur Egg](/Dinosaur_Egg "Dinosaur Egg") or [Movie Ticket](/Movie_Ticket "Movie Ticket")) can be hidden behind foliage in the Crane Game and may become dislodged when the crane drops another prize.

## Notes

* [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") will not attend movies on [Fridays](/Days_of_the_Week#Friday "Days of the Week").
* The cooldown to watch a movie resets on Mondays. The cooldown does not apply if the player enters the theater and leaves without watching the movie.
* Even though Shane hates and Pam is neutral towards [Joja Cola](/Joja_Cola "Joja Cola") as a gift, Pam likes and Shane loves the Joja Cola concession snack.
* Despite being clear analogs for one another, the Personal Pizza and the [Pizza](/Pizza "Pizza") have notably different tastes among the NPCs; many NPCs who like Pizza dislike the Personal Pizza.
* NPCs that can appear in the lobby include [Gunther](/Gunther "Gunther"), the [Governor](/Governor "Governor"), [Famous Painter Lupini](/Night_Market#Famous_Painter_Lupini "Night Market"), [Welwick](/Television#Fortune_Teller "Television") the Fortune Teller, and Marcello, the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller"), who also appears in [Harvey](/Harvey "Harvey")'s 10-heart event.
* [Movie tickets](/Movie_Ticket "Movie Ticket") can be purchased on [festival days](/Festivals "Festivals"), but entry to the theater is not permitted. Attempting to give a ticket to a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") on a festival day will result in a popup box saying: "The movie theater isn't open on festival days!".
* The Movie Theater cannot be entered when it's closed by using the [Key To The Town](/Key_To_The_Town "Key To The Town").
* [Leo](/Leo "Leo") cannot be invited to the Movie Theater before the player reaches 6 hearts with him.
