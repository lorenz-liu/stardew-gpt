# Green Rain Trees

**Green Rain Trees** comprise three varieties of [trees](/Trees "Trees") that can appear during the [Green Rain](/Weather#Green_Rain "Weather") weather event. On the day of the Green Rain weather event, approximately 75% of trees have a chance to turn into one of the varieties of Green Rain Trees. These trees revert back to their original form on the next day.

All three types of Green Rain Trees can spawn naturally during the Green Rain event, can grow from [Mossy Seeds](/Mossy_Seed "Mossy Seed"), or can have a fully-grown Common Tree grow into one. Planted Mossy Seeds have about a 20% chance per day of growth, except in [Winter](/Winter "Winter") when trees do not grow. If the seeds are [fertilized](/Tree_Fertilizer "Tree Fertilizer"), then Green Rain trees will grow in five days (even in [Winter](/Winter "Winter")).[[1]](#cite_note-treechance-1) Each of the three Green Rain Tree varieties may spread seeds like a common [tree](/Trees "Trees"), including the temporary trees that exist only for one day during the green rain.

When a fully grown Green Rain Tree with moss on it is placed near a [Common Tree](/Trees#Common_Trees "Trees") (within the 5X5 square centred on the common tree), it speeds up the time it takes for a common tree to grow or regrow its moss, provided the tree has reached at least 5th stage. The exact math is 50% chance to have an additional growth stage gain at the start of the day. This growth can only impact fully grown trees, so its only impact is on the moss growth. See  [Moss Growth](/Moss#Moss_Growth "Moss").

In [Fall](/Fall "Fall"), [Oak Trees](/Oak_Tree "Oak Tree") and [Maple Trees](/Maple_Tree "Maple Tree") that are not in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") or the [Greenhouse](/Greenhouse "Greenhouse") may temporarily turn into Green Rain Trees Type 1 and 2, respectively, and will return to their original form the next [Spring](/Spring "Spring"). If a [tapper](/Tapper "Tapper") is placed on the tree, this change will not occur. [[2]](#cite_note-leafshedding-2)

### Type 1

Some appear with [Moss](/Moss "Moss"), while others do not. They may drop a [Mossy Seed](/Mossy_Seed "Mossy Seed") when shaken. When chopped with an [axe](/Axes "Axes"), they drop possible Moss, [Wood](/Wood "Wood"), [Sap](/Sap "Sap"), possible Mossy Seeds, and possible [Hardwood](/Hardwood "Hardwood") (if the player has the [Lumberjack](/Foraging#Foraging_Skill "Foraging") profession).

Some appear with [Moss](/Moss "Moss"), while others do not. They may drop a [Mossy Seed](/Mossy_Seed "Mossy Seed") when shaken. When chopped with an [axe](/Axes "Axes"), they drop possible Moss, [Wood](/Wood "Wood"), [Sap](/Sap "Sap"), possible Mossy Seeds, and possible [Hardwood](/Hardwood "Hardwood") (if the player has the [Lumberjack](/Foraging#Foraging_Skill "Foraging") profession).

### Type 3

These trees cannot grow [Moss](/Moss "Moss"). They may drop a [Mossy Seed](/Mossy_Seed "Mossy Seed") when shaken. When chopped with an [axe](/Axes "Axes"), they drop 5 [Fiddlehead Ferns](/Fiddlehead_Fern "Fiddlehead Fern") plus an additional 1 Fiddlehead Fern from the stump, and up to 2 Mossy Seeds. During [Winter](/Winter "Winter"), these trees become stumps. When [tapped](/Tapper "Tapper"), each tree yields 1 [Fiddlehead Fern](/Fiddlehead_Fern "Fiddlehead Fern") every 2 days (except during Winter).[[1]](#cite_note-treechance-1)
