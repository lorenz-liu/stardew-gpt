An **Artifact Spot** (also known as **worms**, **stems**, or **twigs**) is a tile that is guaranteed to provide an item when dug up with a [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes") (other tools do not work). Artifact Spots can be found in various tillable (but fallow) tiles on maps all over Stardew Valley (including Ginger Island). Up to 16 can also be created in a given area by using a [Treasure Totem](/Treasure_Totem "Treasure Totem").

A variety of location-dependent items can be obtained from artifact spots; they are a key source for [Artifacts](/Artifacts "Artifacts") and [Lost Books](/Lost_Books "Lost Books"). Artifact spots are much more common in [Winter](/Winter "Winter"), when they can also yield [Snow Yams](/Snow_Yam "Snow Yam") and [Winter Roots](/Winter_Root "Winter Root").

The possible contents of an artifact spot are controlled by the map where it is located. The specific item found in any artifact spot is mostly fixed for a given day and number of Treasure Totems used, even if the game is reloaded.[[1]](#cite_note-contents-1) However, a different item is possible if a treasure totem is used, or the next day (assuming the artifact spot does not disappear overnight). Using a [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes") with the [Archaeologist](/Forge#Tool_enchantments "Forge") enchantment doubles the chance of finding most artifacts, but otherwise does not affect what item is found. Conversely, unlocking farming mastery will likely change the item that is found, even if the animal cracker is not found.

The pool of potential items contained within artifact spots is checked against a list, in the order listed below. The chances to find items later in the list vary depending upon the details of which earlier items still remain possible. The percentages provided are valid for a hoe without the Archaeologist enchantment (values are 20-40% smaller with the enchantment, except for lost books), and - with the exception for the season specific drops - for summer and fall (values are generally 60% smaller in winter and 6.25% smaller in spring, with the exception of Qi Beans in spring and lost books in both).

* 20-32% chance of a [Lost Book](/Lost_Books "Lost Books").[[2]](#cite_note-lost_book-2) Not possible on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"). Note that once all Lost Books are found, any potential Lost Book is replaced by [Mixed Seeds](/Mixed_Seeds "Mixed Seeds").
* Various [Artifacts](/Artifacts "Artifacts"), dependent upon location. Note, however, that some artifacts can never be found in artifact spots (namely, Dwarf Scrolls, [Dwarvish Helm](/Dwarvish_Helm "Dwarvish Helm"), [Dwarf Gadget](/Dwarf_Gadget "Dwarf Gadget"), [Rare Disc](/Rare_Disc "Rare Disc"), and [Skeletal Tail](/Skeletal_Tail "Skeletal Tail")), 3-26% chance.
* In [Winter](/Winter "Winter") only: 35-49% chance of a [Winter Root](/Winter_Root "Winter Root") or a [Snow Yam](/Snow_Yam "Snow Yam") (Winter Root is 50% more likely than Snow Yam in all locations). Not possible in [the Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert").
* During [Qi's Crop](/Quests#Qi.27s_Crop "Quests") quest only: 14-20% chance of 2-5 [Qi Beans](/Qi_Bean "Qi Bean"). If selected, it will continue to produce more drops.
* In [Spring](/Spring "Spring") only: 4-5% chance of 1-5 [Rice Shoots](/Rice_Shoot "Rice Shoot"). Not possible in [the Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") or [Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach").
* 2-5 [Bone Fragments](/Bone_Fragment "Bone Fragment"), only during or after completing [Gunther](/Gunther "Gunther")'s Special Order "[Fragments of the past](/Quests#Fragments_of_the_past "Quests")" (12-16% chance). If selected, it will continue to produce more drops.
* A location-dependent list of other items. Some artifacts can reappear in this second list, but most of the items are resources.
  + 1-3 [Coal](/Coal "Coal"): [Backwoods](/Backwoods "Backwoods") (48%), [the Mountains](/The_Mountain "The Mountain") (36%), [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") (40%)
  + 1-3 [Copper Ore](/Copper_Ore "Copper Ore"): [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") (14%), [Railroad](/Railroad "Railroad") (11%), [Bus Stop](/Bus_Stop "Bus Stop") (9%), [Backwoods](/Backwoods "Backwoods") (3%), [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") (5%), [the Mountains](/The_Mountain "The Mountain") (2%)

  + 1-3 [Stone](/Stone "Stone"): (4-20% depending on location)

If prior to Summer 1 of year 1, [Clay](/Clay "Clay") will drop instead as the last item in the list.
* 1-3 [Clay](/Clay "Clay"): (10-48%)

On Ginger Island, there are 3 separate attempts to spawn an item from an artifact spot.[[6]](#cite_note-gingerislandall-6)
In the first attempt, the game checks through the following items in order, with the final chances as shown:

* 1 [Golden Coconut](/Golden_Coconut "Golden Coconut") (10% after a golden coconut was cracked)

* 1-2 [Artifact Troves](/Artifact_Trove "Artifact Trove") (10%) (9% after a golden coconut was cracked)

After this it can then spawn the items below, with each check carried out separately, so it can spawn all four.[[4]](#cite_note-rare_object-4)

* 1-2 [Mystery Boxes](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") (0-11.25% chance depending on [daily luck](/Luck "Luck") after the player has viewed the Mr. Qi plane cutscene)
* A [Golden Animal Cracker](/Golden_Animal_Cracker "Golden Animal Cracker") (0.8-1% chance depending on [daily luck](/Luck "Luck") after claiming [Farming Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave")).
* Either [Bait And Bobber](/Bait_And_Bobber "Bait And Bobber"), [Combat Quarterly](/Combat_Quarterly "Combat Quarterly"), [Mining Monthly](/Mining_Monthly "Mining Monthly"), [Stardew Valley Almanac](/Stardew_Valley_Almanac "Stardew Valley Almanac"), or [Woodcutter's Weekly](/Woodcutter%27s_Weekly "Woodcutter's Weekly") (0.54% chance overall, 0.11% chance to obtain each one).

Finally, it then goes through a list of possibilties in order, stopping as soon as it generates an item. The list below shows the chance for each item at that step. The actual chance to obtain that item will depend on the chances of the items available before it.[[7]](#cite_note-gingerislandbase-7)

* [Fossilized Ribs](/Fossilized_Ribs "Fossilized Ribs") (10%, only on Island South, only if the island cave has been opened)

## Spawning

Overnight, artifact spots randomly spawn in exterior locations at a rate that is highly dependent upon the map.[[8]](#cite_note-day_update-8) Average spawn rates range from 0.1/day ([Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest"), [Bus Stop](/Bus_Stop "Bus Stop")) to 1/day ([Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert")). Other than during [Winter](/Winter "Winter"), artifact spots only appear in tillable soil, *i.e.,* yellow/brown tiles that can be tilled with a [Hoe](/Hoes "Hoes"). In Winter, the artifact spot spawn rate is higher and they can also appear in grass-type tiles (*i.e.,* tiles that are colored green during non-winter months; this designation is not based on whether or not harvestable [Grass](/Grass "Grass") is growing). Winter-time average spawn rates range from 0.4/day ([Railroad](/Railroad "Railroad")) to 1.4/day ([Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert")).

Existing artifact spots left in an area overnight block new artifact spots from spawning, according to the following rules:

* Each area is processed independently; for example, an existing artifact spot on the beach has no effect on spawning of other areas (*e.g.,* the [Bus Stop](/Bus_Stop "Bus Stop"), or [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest")).
* Spawning is blocked if there are two or more artifact spots already existing in the same area[[9]](#cite_note-spawnobjects-9) (for [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"), spawning is blocked if even one exists already).
  + In [Winter](/Winter "Winter"), for all locations, the limit is relaxed. Spawning is no longer blocked unless there are five or more already.
* Spawning is calculated after possible [removal](#Removal) of any existing spots, so even when there is an artifact spot left overnight, there is some chance it may be removed and replaced with new spawns.

Artifact spots never spawn in hidden tiles behind buildings, bushes, non-removable trees, or other permanent features. However, they can spawn in difficult-to-see locations behind removable trees (ones the player can chop down). Artifact spots also never spawn in tiles where other objects are present (chests, trees, equipment, weeds, debris, etc.), or in any tilled soil (anywhere a crop is planted or could be planted). Thus, placing equipment or planting trees in tillable soil can reduce the chance of artifact spots spawning, but only if a large fraction of the tiles is occupied.

## Removal

If the player does not dig up an artifact spot, it is likely to remain in place for multiple days. Normally, there is a 15% chance[[10]](#cite_note-removal-10) of any artifact spot disappearing overnight.

Artifact spots do not get destroyed for reasons other than the player or random removal. For example, [villagers](/Villagers "Villagers") and farm [animals](/Animals "Animals") can walk through tiles containing artifact spots without damaging them or otherwise altering them.

Digging up an artifact spot also tills the soil. Off the farm, tilled artifact spots always revert to standard tillable soil overnight. On the farm, tilled soil typically remains in place until the end of the season. To immediately restore standard tillable soil an [Axe](/Axes "Axes") or [Pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes") must be used on the tile.

## Spawning & Removal Exceptions

Spawning and removal is accelerated on certain days. By default, the processing for the spawning and removal logic occurs one time each night, except:

* On the first day of the season, processing is repeated twice.[[8]](#cite_note-day_update-8) For most maps, this increases the chances of at least one artifact spot appearing, but for [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest"), the resulting spawn rate may be actually lower because the effective spawn rate is less than the removal rate of 15%.
* On Sunday, processing is repeated in triplicate.[[8]](#cite_note-day_update-8)
* On [the Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach"), between Summer 12-14, processing is run 6+ times.[[11]](#cite_note-dayupdate_beach-11)
* All winter-time artifact spots that spawned on grass-type tiles are always removed at the end of the winter.

## Notes

* The [Tracker](/Skills#Foraging "Skills") profession can make it easier to find artifact spots, by adding small yellow arrows pointing to any off-screen artifact spots.
* The [Archaeologist](/Forge#Tool_enchantments "Forge") enchantment doubles the likelihood of finding an [artifact](/Artifacts "Artifacts") in an artifact spot.
* The [Generous](/Forge#Tool_enchantments "Forge") enchantment gives a 50% chance of a double item from an artifact spot, but does not apply to all possible items.

[↑](#cite_ref-contents_1-0) The contents of an Artifact Spot are determined by `GameLocation::digUpArtifactSpot`, using data from `ObjectInformation.xnb` (for artifacts) and `Locations.xnb` (for extra items). The random number generator is seeded by the save game ID, the days played, the location and the number of treasure totems used. However, the number of treasure totems used is not used for the seed to check for Calico Eggs and the first round on Ginger Island. Each item is checked sequentially to see whether a random number is less than the item's specified chance; the first item that succeeds is the one found. One result of this algorithm is that the actual chance of finding a given item is smaller than the value in the input file, because it can only be found if all previous artifacts failed their tests. Extra items are affected the most strongly, because they are checked last, and therefore they are less likely in winter (by 50%) and in spring (by 6.25%).
