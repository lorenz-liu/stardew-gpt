# Mining

**Mining** is the [skill](/Skills "Skills") associated with breaking rocks, mainly in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"). Larger or more valuable rocks and nodes take more hits, and in some cases, require a stronger [Pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes") to break.

## Mines Access

During the first four days of the game, [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") are closed due to a landslide caused by the [Joja Corporation](/JojaMart "JojaMart"). On [Spring](/Spring "Spring") 5 in the first year, a letter will be received in the mail saying that Joja Corp. has cleared the landslide, and then the Mines are unlocked.

## [Mining Skill Icon.png](/File:Mining_Skill_Icon.png) Mining Skill

Mining Skill is increased by breaking rocks, [panning](/Pans "Pans"), or by reading [Mining Monthly](/Mining_Monthly "Mining Monthly") or the [Book Of Stars](/Book_Of_Stars "Book Of Stars"). Each level adds +1 to [Pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes") [proficiency](/Skills#Proficiency "Skills").

Mining skill points are awarded when rocks are destroyed -- it doesn't matter if this is done by [Pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes") or [Bombs](/Crafting#Bombs "Crafting"). Rocks destroyed by [Monsters](/Monsters "Monsters") grant no XP.

## Experience Points

250 Mining XP is gained for reading a copy of [Mining Monthly](/Mining_Monthly "Mining Monthly") or the [Book Of Stars](/Book_Of_Stars "Book Of Stars"). One XP is given for each piece of ore gained from [panning](/Pans "Pans") (applies to [Copper](/Copper_Ore "Copper Ore"), [Iron](/Iron_Ore "Iron Ore"), [Gold](/Gold_Ore "Gold Ore"), or [Iridium](/Iridium_Ore "Iridium Ore") ore) [[1]](#cite_note-getPanItems-1).

Different rock types give different numbers of experience points. While generic rocks inside [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") provide no experience bonus on their own, if [coal](/Coal "Coal") is dropped, 5 experience points are awarded (except in the case of the two gray rocks pictured below). No other "extra" drops from breaking rocks inside the Mines award experience points.

Breaking open a meteor dropped on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") during a [Meteorite event](/Random_Events#Meteorite "Random Events") awards no experience.

Experience level is increased immediately upon breaking a rock/boulder/node, but the "level up" window doesn't appear until after going to sleep.

The Geologist profession gives the specific gem nodes a 50% chance of spawning twice the gems. If this happens, then the amount of experience gained for each node is reduced, this is labelled as "Multiple Spawns" in the table below.[[2]](#cite_note-geologistexp-2). Note that the mining [mastery](/Mastery "Mastery") results in the number of gems always being doubled, but does not reduce the experience gained.

[Diamond Nodes](#Diamond_Node) begin appearing at floor 50 in the Mines, at approximately .0020 chance (1 in 500) of appearing in place of a stone. For every floor after floor 50, frequency increases by .000016 per floor. This makes for a .0028 chance for Diamond Node to appear in place of a stone at floor 100 (1.4 in 500 or 7 in 2500), and ultimately a .003104 chance for a diamond node to appear in place of a stone at floor 119 (1.552 in 500 or 194 in 62500).

The chance for Gem Nodes to appear is very small, but is slightly increased by [Mining Skill](#Mining_Skill) level and daily [luck](/Luck "Luck"). The chance for [Mystic Stones](#Mystic_Stone) to appear is also increased by Mining Skill Level and daily luck.

Prospector causes `breakStone` to roll twice for the coal drop (but the extra roll grants no XP), while doubling the chances for `onStoneDestroyed` to 7%.
