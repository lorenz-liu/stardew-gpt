**Seaweed** is an item that can be caught using a [Fishing Pole](/Tools#Fishing_Poles "Tools") in the ocean during all [seasons](/Seasons "Seasons"), including a 41% chance to catch Seaweed while fishing in the deep-sea [submarine](/Night_Market#Fishing_Submarine "Night Market") ride at the [Night Market](/Night_Market "Night Market"). It is never caught in [Crab Pots](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot").

It can also be foraged from a narrow strip of shore by the [Tide Pools](/The_Beach#Tide_Pools "The Beach"), *i.e.,* to the east of the wooden footbridge[[1]](#cite_note-location-1), but it spawns relatively infrequently (3% chance per night). The saltwater [crab pot fish](/Fish#Crab_Pot_Fish "Fish") ([Cockle](/Cockle "Cockle"), [Crab](/Crab "Crab"), [Lobster](/Lobster "Lobster"), [Mussel](/Mussel "Mussel"), [Oyster](/Oyster "Oyster"), and [Shrimp](/Shrimp "Shrimp")) may produce 1-3 Seaweed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") when the population of the pond is at least 1. It's also possible to find Seaweed in [the "Dangerous Mines"](/The_Mines "The Mines") on floors 1-29 when the [Shrine of Challenge](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") is turned on. Seaweed can also randomly be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can").

Catching Seaweed with a Fishing Pole always gives 3 [Fishing](/Fishing "Fishing") XP, and picking up from the beach gives 7 [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") XP. Seaweed, either fished or foraged, is always normal quality.

Seaweed is displayed in the [Fish section](/Collections#Fish "Collections") of the [collection](/Collections "Collections") tab, and counts towards the four fishing [Achievements](/Fishing#Achievements "Fishing").[[2]](#cite_note-quasi_fish-2) However, it is otherwise not considered to be a fish, and therefore cannot be sold to [Willy](/Willy "Willy") at the [Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop"), does not benefit from fish price bonuses, cannot be used in place of "Any Fish" in [recipes](/Cooking#Recipes "Cooking"), and cannot be put in a [Bait Maker](/Bait_Maker "Bait Maker"), [Fish Smoker](/Fish_Smoker "Fish Smoker"), or [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond").

One Seaweed can be placed in any [Fish Tank](/Fish_Tank "Fish Tank") as decoration, including the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center")'s fish tank after the [fish tank bundle](/Bundles#Fish_Tank "Bundles") is completed. Its appearance in player-placed fish tanks can be changed by picking up and re-placing the fish tank.

## Tailoring

Seaweed is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [![Shirt168.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/16/Shirt168.png/24px-Shirt168.png)](/File:Shirt168.png) [Kelp Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a green dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* Seaweed may be randomly requested during any season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="60">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)60g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* 1-2 Seaweed may be requested by several ocean fish in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 3 to 5.
