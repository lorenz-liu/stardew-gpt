The **Joja Warehouse** is a building that replaces the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") if the player chooses to purchase the [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart") membership for data-sort-value="5000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)5,000g.

After purchasing a [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart") membership, all town restorations are purchased through the [Joja Community Development Form](/Joja_Community_Development_Form "Joja Community Development Form") at JojaMart, rather than by completing [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles"), and all completion cutscenes will show the JojaMart workers completing the task instead of the [Junimos](/Junimos "Junimos").

Improvements made prior to purchasing a membership ([Minecarts](/Minecart "Minecart"), the [Greenhouse](/Greenhouse "Greenhouse"), etc.) will remain in place and show as already completed after purchasing a membership. Any unfinished improvements will cost full price, regardless of how much progress was made towards it prior to purchasing a membership.

## After Form Completion

After completion of the Community Development Form, [Morris](/Morris "Morris") will make a new offer when the player talks to him at JojaMart:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| “ | “Ah, our favorite customer... I have exciting news for you! Joja headquarters has approved the construction of a new Joja "entertainment opportunity" to replace the nearby Joja Warehouse. We just need an investor willing to support our vision! So, naturally, I turn to you... our most devoted and generous Joja member. We could make this happen for a small investment of... \*cough\*... 500,000g...” |

Paying Morris the data-sort-value="500000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500,000g will result in turning the warehouse into the [Movie Theater](/Movie_Theater "Movie Theater").

## Trivia

* The old boiler room does not get filled with boxes and supplies, unlike the other rooms that were replaced to be made more Joja style. It will be left in a state of disrepair. If the boiler room was previously completed through the Junimo bundles before buying a membership, it will revert to its dilapidated state.
