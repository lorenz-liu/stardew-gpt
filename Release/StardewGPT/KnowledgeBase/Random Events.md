# Random Events

:   *See also: [Secrets](/Secrets "Secrets").*

**Random Farm Events** happen overnight when the player sleeps.

## Farm events

One farm event is randomly selected each night. An event is always selected (except if there's a [wedding](/Marriage "Marriage")), but it might have extra requirements that make it do nothing; in that case, it still prevents other events from triggering. The event is chosen in the order listed below.

Farm events can still be chosen while sleeping in the [Island Farmhouse](/Island_Farmhouse "Island Farmhouse"), or in a [Tent Kit](/Tent_Kit "Tent Kit") anywhere, but will always affect the mainland farm.

### Nothing

No event happens on the night before a wedding, or if another event is selected below but does nothing.

### Earthquake

An earthquake on the 3rd day of [Summer](/Summer "Summer") Year 1 unlocks the [railroad](/Railroad "Railroad") area.

### Leo Moved

The night after seeing [Leo](/Leo "Leo")'s 6 heart event, parrots build him a treehouse in the tree to the west of Linus' tent.

### Bundle Completion

An event is shown for completing a [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") or [Joja](/Joja_Community_Development_Form "Joja Community Development Form") bundle.

### Abandoned JojaMart

On the first rainy night after completing the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center"), lightning strikes the abandoned JojaMart, allowing access to the [Missing Bundle](/Bundles#Abandoned_JojaMart "Bundles").

### Boat Repair

The night after providing all the materials for the repair of [Willy's Boat](/Fish_Shop#Willy.27s_Boat "Fish Shop"), [Robin](/Robin "Robin") and [Willy](/Willy "Willy") can be seen working on it.

### Golden Parrot

The night after paying the [Golden Joja Parrot](/Golden_Walnut#Golden_Joja_Parrot "Golden Walnut"), Golden Parrots can be seen collecting the missing Walnuts.

### Strong Wind Storm

A strong wind storm blows the Big Tree in Cindersap Forest down and replaces it with the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump"). The quest has a 10% chance to trigger each night after the [Greenhouse](/Greenhouse "Greenhouse") construction. The stump may then be interacted with to begin the ["The Giant Stump"](/Quests#The_Giant_Stump "Quests") quest.

### Mystery Boxes

On Summer 22, year 1 or after collecting the second [Prize Ticket](/Prize_Ticket "Prize Ticket") from the Billboard, there is a cutscene with Mr. Qi in a plane after which [Mystery Boxes](/Mystery_Boxes "Mystery Boxes") can be found.

### The Crop Fairy

Except in winter or on the last day of a season, there's a 1% chance of the fairy event being chosen. If there is a fully grown [Fairy Rose](/Fairy_Rose "Fairy Rose")
anywhere this is increased to 1.7%. If it's not raining, the event chooses a random crop that's not from [Wild Seeds](/Crafting#Seeds "Crafting") and not fully-grown or dead. The crop fairy fully grows all crops in a 5x5 area around the selected crop, so they're ready to harvest.

Although the center crop can't be from [Wild Seeds](/Crafting#Seeds "Crafting"), one of the surrounding crops can be; in that case, they will reach the "fully grown" stage but not be replaced by a forageable. Harvesting them in this state gives a [Wild Horseradish](/Wild_Horseradish "Wild Horseradish"), [Spice Berry](/Spice_Berry "Spice Berry"), [Common Mushroom](/Common_Mushroom "Common Mushroom"), or [Winter Root](/Winter_Root "Winter Root") corresponding to the season. If they are left overnight, they will be replaced by random forageables from the season.

If it's raining or no crop was found, the event does nothing.

### The Witch

There's a 1% chance of the witch event being chosen. The event randomly chooses one [coop](/Coop "Coop") (each with an 80% chance if it's been upgraded at least once, and has fewer than 50 placed objects), or one [Slime Hutch](/Slime_Hutch "Slime Hutch") (each with a 50% chance if it contains at least one [slime](/Slimes "Slimes") or other NPC, and has no [Wicked Statue](/Wicked_Statue "Wicked Statue")). If one of the following buildings were not selected, the event does nothing.

The witch flies over the selected building.

* If a coop was selected, she leaves a [Void Egg](/Void_Egg "Void Egg") that can be incubated into a [Void Chicken](/Void_Chicken "Void Chicken").
  + If 100% [Perfection](/Perfection "Perfection") has been reached, there is a 60% chance that a gold witch will instead appear and leave a [Golden Egg](/Golden_Egg "Golden Egg") that can be incubated into a [Golden Chicken](/Golden_Chicken "Golden Chicken").
* If a slime hutch was selected, she turns any slimes inside into [black slimes](/Slimes#Variations "Slimes").

In late-game, the [Witch's Hut](/Witch%27s_Hut "Witch's Hut") can be visited, but the witch is never present.

### Meteorite

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/71/Meteorite_2.png/300px-Meteorite_2.png)](/File:Meteorite_2.png)

A meteorite on the Farm.

There's a 1% chance of the meteorite event being chosen. The event chooses a random 3x3 area on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") and checks whether it contains any water, placed objects that block movement, buildings, or resource clumps. If it does, the event does nothing. Otherwise, the meteorite will spawn in the lower left 2x2 square within this area.

A unique `Meteorite` sound effect plays and a meteorite appears the next day on the farm in the selected area. Any crops or paths in that area are destroyed. This can be counter-productive, especially if the crops being grown are of more value than the loot from the meteorite. Before the shipping screen is displayed, a message is shown saying "An explosion was heard in the night..."

Meteorites don't despawn naturally and require a [Gold Pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes") or better to mine. (A Gold Pickaxe requires 7 hits, and an Iridium Pickaxe requires 6 hits.) They drop 10 [Iridium Ore](/Iridium_Ore "Iridium Ore"), 8 [Stone](/Stone "Stone"), and 2 [Omni Geodes](/Omni_Geode "Omni Geode"), and have a 25% chance to drop a [Prismatic Shard](/Prismatic_Shard "Prismatic Shard").

### Stone Owl

[![](/mediawiki/images/4/46/Stone_Owl_Random.png)](/File:Stone_Owl_Random.png)

The Stone Owl

There's a 0.5% chance of the stone owl event being chosen. The event tries 50 times to pick a random tile on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") which is clear to allow item placement.

An `owl` sound effect plays, and a unique [Stone Owl](/Furniture#Special_Items "Furniture") appears on the chosen tile. This owl is unrelated to the [Stone Owl](/Stone_Owl "Stone Owl") sold at the [Night Market](/Night_Market "Night Market").

The description reads: "It's not clear where this came from, but it looks very fine."

If no tile allowing item placement is found, this event does nothing.

### Strange Capsule

After Year 1, there's a 0.8% chance of the strange capsule event being chosen. The event tries 50 times to pick a random tile on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") which is clear and allows item placement. Before the shipping screen is displayed, a message is shown saying "A strange sound was heard in the night..."

A unique `UFO` sound effect plays, and a Strange Capsule appears on a random tile of the farm.[[1]](#cite_note-strangecapsule-1) The strange capsule can be picked up and placed around the farm. It will emit a small amount of light when placed. It turns into a broken Empty Capsule after about three days (unless it is stored in a chest or inside of the player's inventory), which enables a [further Easter egg](/Secrets#Strange_Capsule "Secrets"). It can also turn into a broken Empty Capsule if applied with [Fairy Dust](/Fairy_Dust "Fairy Dust"). Once this event has happened in a save file, it cannot happen again.

If no tile allowing item placement is found, this event does nothing, but still cannot happen again.

## Personal events

If no farm event was selected from the above list (regardless of whether the event did something), the game chooses from one of these events instead.

### Child is Born or Delivered

:   *See also: [Children](/Children "Children")*

This event is chosen if the player agreed to have a child 14 days ago in the [spouse asks for a child](#Spouse_asks_for_a_child) event. The child appears in the farmhouse crib the next day.

**Bug:** if another event is selected above, the child is never born or delivered.

### Spouse asks for a child

If married to an NPC:

:   This event has a 5% chance of being chosen if the player has been married for at least 7 days, sleeping at home, have at least 10 hearts with their spouse, have the [nursery upgrade](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse"), have not [removed the crib](/Carpenter%27s_Shop#House_Renovations "Carpenter's Shop"), aren't already having a child, and have no children or one child at the toddler age.

:   The player's spouse asks them if they want a child. If agreed to, the [child is born or delivered](#Child_is_born_or_delivered) after 14 days.

If married to another player:

:   This event has a 5% chance of being chosen if the player is sleeping at an eligible home and aren't already having a child. A home is eligible for children if it's owned by one of the married players, has the [nursery upgrade](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse"), and doesn't already have two children.

:   One player is randomly chosen to propose having children; if both players accept, the [child is born or delivered](#Child_is_born_or_delivered) after 14 days.

In either case, the player can safely decline without repercussions; the event will be rechecked on later nights with the same conditions. See [Marriage](/Marriage "Marriage"), [Children](/Children "Children"), and [Multiplayer#Marriage](/Multiplayer#Marriage "Multiplayer") for more info.

### Farm animal birth

:   *See also: [Animals](/Animals#Animal_Births "Animals")*

There's a 50% chance the farm animal birth is selected. The event randomly chooses one upgraded [Barn](/Barn "Barn") that isn't full (each with a chance equal to *0.55% \* number of animals inside*, *e.g.,* 1.65% for three animals), then one random animal in that building. If no building is selected or the animal can't give birth (*e.g.,* it's a baby or pregnancy is disabled for that animal), the event does nothing.

The selected farm animal in the barn gives birth. A message is shown asking the player to name them.

### Wild animal attack

:   *See also: [Animals](/Animals#Wild_Animal_Attacks "Animals")*

This event is guaranteed to be chosen if no other event was, but has a 50% chance of doing nothing. The event randomly chooses one [barn](/Barn "Barn") or [coop](/Coop "Coop") whose door is closed with some animals outdoors (each with a chance equal to *1 / number of buildings*, *e.g.,* 100% for one building and 50% for two).

A unique sound of dogs plays, and one animal left outside of the selected building disappears permanently. All other animals show a message indicating "Something bad happened last night.", but are otherwise unaffected.

## History

* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): Fixed spawn requirements for the random [Stone Owl](#Stone_Owl) and [Strange Capsule](#Strange_Capsule) night events, making them much less rare.[[2]](#cite_note-twitter-2) There is a new Strange Capsule secret.
* [1.5.3](/Version_History#1.5.3 "Version History"): The Strange Capsule now only spawns once per game. Made the Stone Owl rarer and touched up the sprite.
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Added Strong Wind Storm random event. Changed [Meteorite](#Meteorite) drops: added a [Prismatic Shard](/Prismatic_Shard "Prismatic Shard"), increased [Iridium Ore](/Iridium_Ore "Iridium Ore") from 6 to 10, increased [Stone](/Stone "Stone") from 6 to 8, and replaced [Geodes](/Geode "Geode") with [Omni Geodes](/Omni_Geode "Omni Geode"). Rebalanced the crop fairy event: the chance no longer depends on the number of planted crops, it can no longer happen on the last day of the season, and it can no longer choose dead crops to grow. It is also now more likely with a fully grown [Fairy Rose](/Fairy_Rose "Fairy Rose"). Some overnight events that play sound now show a speaker icon on the lower left of the screen.
* [1.6.3](/Version_History#1.6.3 "Version History"): Reduced rate of the [Meteorite](#Meteorite) dropping a [Prismatic Shard](/Prismatic_Shard "Prismatic Shard") from 100% to 25%.
