# Flowers

**Flowers** are a category that includes several [crops](/Crops "Crops") grown on the farm. In addition, two [foraged](/Foraging "Foraging") plants, [Crocus](/Crocus "Crocus") and [Sweet Peas](/Sweet_Pea "Sweet Pea"), are considered to be flowers. Other foraged items that are real-life flowers (*e.g.,* [Dandelions](/Dandelion "Dandelion") and [Daffodils](/Daffodil "Daffodil")) are categorized as [Forage](/Foraging#Foraged_Items "Foraging") instead of Flower and therefore are not included here.

All flowers share several in-game characteristics:

* Their sell prices are increased by 10% if the player has the [Tiller](/Skills#Farming "Skills") profession (even foraged Crocus and Sweet Pea).
* Flowers grown as crops (*i.e.,* other than Crocus and Sweet Pea) will affect the type of [Honey](/Honey "Honey") produced by nearby [Bee Houses](/Bee_House "Bee House").
* Flowers (other than [Poppy](/Poppy "Poppy")) are a [universally liked gift](/Friendship#Universal_Likes "Friendship"); all villagers other than [Clint](/Clint "Clint"), [George](/George "George"), and [Sebastian](/Sebastian "Sebastian") like them. Gifting foraged flowers can be an inexpensive way to level up [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship").
* Flowers (combined with [Forage](/Foraging#Foraged_Items "Foraging") and [Tree Sap](/Tapper#Products "Tapper")) are one category used when scoring items in the [Grange Display](/Stardew_Valley_Fair#Grange_Display "Stardew Valley Fair") at the [Stardew Valley Fair](/Stardew_Valley_Fair "Stardew Valley Fair").
* They can be sold at the [General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") (as well as in the [Shipping Bin](/Shipping "Shipping")).
* All shipped flowers are added to the [Items Shipped](/Collections#Items_Shipped "Collections") page of the Collections tab.
