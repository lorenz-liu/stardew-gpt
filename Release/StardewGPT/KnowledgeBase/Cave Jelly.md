**Cave Jelly** is an item that can be caught using a [Fishing Pole](/Tools#Fishing_Poles "Tools") at floors 20, 60, and 100 of [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"). Each level of [Luck Buff](/Luck#Luck_Buffs "Luck") applied significantly increases the chance of catching it. [Daily Luck](/Luck#Daily_Luck "Luck") has no effect.

It can also be acquired from a [Lava Eel](/Lava_Eel "Lava Eel") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") with a population of at least 8. The [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") may trade 3 Cave Jelly for 1 [Jewels Of The Sea](/Jewels_Of_The_Sea "Jewels Of The Sea") each, provided the player has already earned the power from that book. It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") turtle with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals").

Cave Jelly is displayed in the [Fish section](/Collections#Fish "Collections") of the [collection](/Collections "Collections") tab, and counts towards the four fishing [Achievements](/Fishing#Achievements "Fishing"). However, it is otherwise not considered to be a fish, and therefore cannot be sold to [Willy](/Willy "Willy") at the [Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop"), does not benefit from fish price bonuses, cannot be used in place of "Any Fish" in [recipes](/Cooking#Recipes "Cooking"), and cannot be put in a [Bait Maker](/Bait_Maker "Bait Maker"), [Fish Smoker](/Fish_Smoker "Fish Smoker"), or [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond").

## Tailoring

Cave Jelly is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a red dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a red dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Strategy

The highest possible rates for Cave Jelly are when fishing on Floor 100 of [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"). When fishing in this location there are only 3 possible catches: Cave Jelly, [Lava Eel](/Lava_Eel "Lava Eel"), and [Trash](/Trash "Trash"). Any rod other than the [Training Rod](/Training_Rod "Training Rod") may be used, as the Training Rod removes both Cave Jelly and Lava Eel from the pool of possible catches. The chance of catching a Lava Eel can be reduced by fishing closer to the shore, marginally increasing the chance of catching a Cave Jelly.[[1]](#cite_note-mineshaft-1)

At this level, ordinarily, 5% of casts that do not catch a Lava Eel will result in a successful Cave Jelly catch. Each level of [Luck Buff](/Luck#Luck_Buffs "Luck") increases the chance by 5%.[[1]](#cite_note-mineshaft-1) So with +5 Luck, 30% of non-Lava Eel catches will be Cave Jelly.

Unlike other locations, fishing on Floor 100 of the Mines does not use a predetermined value to determine whether the farmer successfully catches a Jelly.[[1]](#cite_note-mineshaft-1)
