The **Crocus** is a [flower](/Flowers "Flowers") found via [Foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") in [Winter](/Winter "Winter"). It can be found in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"), the [Railroad](/Railroad "Railroad"), the [Bus Stop](/Bus_Stop "Bus Stop"), [the Mountains](/The_Mountain "The Mountain"), [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest"), or the [Backwoods](/Backwoods "Backwoods"). It can also be grown from [Winter wild seeds](/Winter_Seeds "Winter Seeds"). It can randomly be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during Winter.

## Healing

The Crocus is edible, but does not increase [Energy](/Energy "Energy") or [Health](/Health "Health").

## Tailoring

Crocus is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [![Shirt078.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/85/Shirt078.png/24px-Shirt078.png)](/File:Shirt078.png) [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a purple dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").
