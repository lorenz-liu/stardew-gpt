The **Hazelnut** is found via [foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") throughout Stardew Valley in the [Fall](/Fall "Fall"). It can also be grown from [Fall Seeds](/Fall_Seeds "Fall Seeds"). Shaking a [Maple Tree](/Maple_Tree "Maple Tree") from the 15th through 28th of [Fall](/Fall "Fall") has a chance to cause a hazelnut to drop from the tree. Five Hazelnuts can be purchased from [Kent](/Kent "Kent")'s [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="10">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 10 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg") each. Hazelnut can also randomly be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during Fall.

## Tailoring

Hazelnut is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [![Shirt266.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/f0/Shirt266.png/24px-Shirt266.png)](/File:Shirt266.png) [Brown Overalls](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* The Hazelnut may be randomly requested in [Fall](/Fall "Fall") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="270">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)270g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
