# Witch's Swamp

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/9f/Witch%27s_Swamp.png/500px-Witch%27s_Swamp.png)](/File:Witch%27s_Swamp.png)

The [henchman](/Henchman "Henchman") inside the Witch's Swamp

The **Witch's Swamp** is an underground location that becomes accessible after completing the ["Dark Talisman" Quest](/Quests#Dark_Talisman "Quests"). The cave which leads to the swamp is located in the northeast part of the [Railroad](/Railroad "Railroad") area. Found within the Witch's Swamp protected by a [Henchman](/Henchman "Henchman") is the [Witch's Hut](/Witch%27s_Hut "Witch's Hut").

Even though plants are growing here and [fishing](/Fishing "Fishing") is possible, the game considers this location to be underground. Therefore, [forageable](/Foraging "Foraging") items and [artifact spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") never spawn here.

## Quests

The questline to access the Witch's Hut involves two quests, which become available after completing the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles") or the [Joja Community Development Form](/Joja_Community_Development_Form "Joja Community Development Form"). [The player](/The_Player "The Player") can trigger a cutscene at the [Railroad](/Railroad "Railroad") where the [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard") will request they find a [Dark Talisman](/Dark_Talisman "Dark Talisman"), which can be found in a chest within the [Mutant Bug Lair](/Mutant_Bug_Lair "Mutant Bug Lair"). The Mutant Bug Lair can be accessed by speaking to [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus"), who will reveal the entrance in [the Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers"). After obtaining the Talisman, bring it back to the Railroad area and place it on the artifact blocking the cave. Inside the cave is a teleportation rune which leads to the Witch's Swamp.

Once inside the Witch's Swamp, the Witch's [Henchman](/Henchman "Henchman") will be blocking the way to the [Witch's Hut](/Witch%27s_Hut "Witch's Hut"). As hinted by the [Lost Book](/Lost_Books "Lost Books") *[Goblins](/Lost_Books#Goblins "Lost Books")*, the Henchman can be bribed into letting the player pass using [Void Mayonnaise](/Void_Mayonnaise "Void Mayonnaise"), completing the quest [Goblin Problem](/Quests#Goblin_Problem "Quests"). Recovering the [Magic Ink](/Magic_Ink "Magic Ink") inside the Witch's Hut then bringing the ink back to the Wizard will unlock six [Wizard buildings](/Wizard%27s_Tower#Buildings "Wizard's Tower") for [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm").

## Fishing

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/46/SwampDistances.png/300px-SwampDistances.png)](/File:SwampDistances.png)

[Distances from land](/Fishing#Distance_from_Land "Fishing") in the Witch's Swamp

Two unique items can be caught by [fishing](/Fishing "Fishing") in the Witch's Swamp: [Void Salmon](/Void_Salmon "Void Salmon") and [Void Mayonnaise](/Void_Mayonnaise "Void Mayonnaise"). [Catfish](/Catfish "Catfish"), [Green Algae](/Green_Algae "Green Algae"), and [White Algae](/White_Algae "White Algae") are also possible. The available fish are the same year-round, even though Catfish is normally unavailable during summer.

There is a 5% chance of catching a Void Mayonnaise, but only if the "Goblin Problem" quest is active and the player does not have any Void Mayonnaise in their inventory.[[1]](#cite_note-void_mayo-1)
