**Truffle Oil** is an [Artisan Good](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods") made in the [Oil Maker](/Oil_Maker "Oil Maker") from a [Truffle](/Truffle "Truffle"), taking 6 hours to produce. It can also be dropped from [Truffle Crabs](/Truffle_Crab "Truffle Crab"). One may be purchased from [Marnie](/Marnie "Marnie")'s [shop](/Desert_Festival#Villager_Shops "Desert Festival") at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="50">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 50 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg"). Occasionally the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") will offer it in her rotating stock.

Despite being listed as "A gourmet cooking ingredient" there are no cooking recipes that use it, but it is used to make the [Rain Totem](/Rain_Totem "Rain Totem").

## Notes

Without the [Artisan Profession](/Farming#Farming_Skill "Farming"), iridium quality [truffles](/Truffle "Truffle") are worth more to sell outright rather than turn into oil.

## Tailoring

Truffle Oil can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [![Shirt262a.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/85/Shirt262a.png/24px-Shirt262a.png)](/File:Shirt262a.png) [Dark Bandana Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* Mayor [Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis") requests Truffle Oil by mail on the 21st of [Summer](/Summer "Summer") (year 2) in the ["Mayor's Need" Quest](/Quests#Mayor.27s_Need "Quests"). The reward is data-sort-value="750">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)750g and 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart.  
  Note that Lewis will send the letter triggering the quest regardless of whether or not the player owns a [pig](/Pig "Pig").
