# Cindersap Forest

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/0c/CindersapForest.png/500px-CindersapForest.png)](/File:CindersapForest.png)

Cindersap Forest during Spring

**Cindersap Forest** is a large exterior region found in the southwest part of Stardew Valley. It has exits to the north into [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"), to the east into [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"), to the south into [The Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers"), and to the northwest into the [Secret Woods](/Secret_Woods "Secret Woods"). Cindersap Forest is the only place where [Spring Onions](/Spring_Onion "Spring Onion") are found.

Three festivals take place in Cindersap Forest: the [Flower Dance](/Flower_Dance "Flower Dance") on [Spring](/Spring "Spring") 24, the [Trout Derby](/Trout_Derby "Trout Derby") on [Summer](/Summer "Summer") 20-21, and the [Festival of Ice](/Festival_of_Ice "Festival of Ice") on [Winter](/Winter "Winter") 8.

At the start of the game, there are many twigs, rocks, and [weeds](/Weeds "Weeds") throughout the forest. If cleaned up, the debris will reappear each Spring 1.

## Trash Bear

[![](/mediawiki/images/5/5d/Trash_Bear.png)](/File:Trash_Bear.png)

Trash Bear

The Trash Bear is located at the bottom of Cindersap Forest, by the [Spring Onion](/Spring_Onion "Spring Onion") patch and the entrance to [The Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers"). Trash Bear will appear on non-rainy, non-festival days beginning year 3. Interacting with Trash Bear will show a dialogue bubble of a random desired item. After giving Trash Bear the requested item, clicking on Trash Bear will reveal another request. Fulfilling four requests will trigger a cutscene in which Trash Bear plays pan-pipes, flies up on an umbrella made of leaves, and then cleans up the trash in the unreachable area near the water, replacing the trash with bushes and trees. Trash Bear continues flying on the umbrella to [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"), where the trash in [Pam's front yard](/Trailer "Trailer") is removed. [Dusty's dog pen](/Dog_Pen "Dog Pen") also gets an upgrade.

Trash Bear will request 2 random seasonal [fish](/Fish "Fish") or [foraged items](/Foraging "Foraging"), then 2 [cooked dishes](/Cooking "Cooking"). There is no time limit in fulfilling these requests. Note, however, that requests will not be able to be fulfilled on rainy days as Trash Bear does not appear on those days. A request will never change until it is completed, unless the [season](/Seasons "Seasons") changes and the previous request was seasonal. In this case, Trash Bear's request will change to fit the new season. Shown below is a complete list of the **random seasonal items** Trash Bear may request:

Pot of Gold

## Foraging

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/c4/ForestForageLocations.png/400px-ForestForageLocations.png)](/File:ForestForageLocations.png)

Locations in Cindersap Forest where forageable items can spawn.

Standard [forageable](/Foraging "Foraging") items found on the ground in Cindersap Forest are:[[3]](#cite_note-forage_chance-3)

* In [Spring](/Spring "Spring"): ![Wild Horseradish.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/90/Wild_Horseradish.png/24px-Wild_Horseradish.png) [Wild Horseradish](/Wild_Horseradish "Wild Horseradish") (50%), and ![Dandelion.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/b1/Dandelion.png/24px-Dandelion.png) [Dandelion](/Dandelion "Dandelion") (50%)
* In [Summer](/Summer "Summer"): ![Sweet Pea.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/d9/Sweet_Pea.png/24px-Sweet_Pea.png) [Sweet Pea](/Sweet_Pea "Sweet Pea") (60%), and ![Spice Berry.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/c6/Spice_Berry.png/24px-Spice_Berry.png) [Spice Berry](/Spice_Berry "Spice Berry") (40%)
* In [Fall](/Fall "Fall"): ![Common Mushroom.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2e/Common_Mushroom.png/24px-Common_Mushroom.png) [Common Mushroom](/Common_Mushroom "Common Mushroom") (50%), and ![Blackberry.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/25/Blackberry.png/24px-Blackberry.png) [Blackberry](/Blackberry "Blackberry") (50%)
* In [Winter](/Winter "Winter"): ![Crocus.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2f/Crocus.png/24px-Crocus.png) [Crocus](/Crocus "Crocus") (39%), ![Crystal Fruit.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/16/Crystal_Fruit.png/24px-Crystal_Fruit.png) [Crystal Fruit](/Crystal_Fruit "Crystal Fruit") (39%), and ![Holly.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/b8/Holly.png/24px-Holly.png) [Holly](/Holly "Holly") (22%)

Forageable items spawn at an average rate of 1.8 per night in spring and fall; in summer and winter the rate is 1.6 per night.[[4]](#cite_note-forage_spawn-4) The map shows the possible locations as red and magenta tiles; when the original trees are present, the magenta tiles have a 90% smaller chance of spawning items because they are identified as being behind the original trees.

[Spring Onions](/Spring_Onion "Spring Onion") are also found in Cindersap Forest during the [Spring](/Spring "Spring"). They appear only on tillable soil on the large southeastern island (the green tiles on the map). Every night, any existing onions are removed, and new onions spawn. These onions are not standard forageable items, and therefore do not count as part of the six item per map limit.

The forest also contains a large variety of [Trees](/Trees "Trees"), which can be chopped down to harvest [Wood](/Wood "Wood") and other items. As with all pre-existing trees outside [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"), they will automatically regrow as long as the stump is removed.

## Fishing

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/6e/ForestRiverFishingZones.png/400px-ForestRiverFishingZones.png)](/File:ForestRiverFishingZones.png)

[Distances from land](/Fishing#Distance_from_Land "Fishing") in the Cindersap Forest river.

Cindersap Forest is the only map with different bodies of water that contain different [fish](/Fish "Fish"): the river and the pond.

Fishing is also part of the festivities during the [Trout Derby](/Trout_Derby "Trout Derby") and [Festival of Ice](/Festival_of_Ice "Festival of Ice").

The forest is bordered to the south by the ocean. While it is possible to cast a fishing rod from the cliffs down to the ocean (near the bridge that leads to the area where the [Flower Dance](/Flower_Dance "Flower Dance") takes place), only river fish can be caught there, not ocean fish.

### River

The primary [fishing](/Fishing "Fishing") location in Cindersap Forest is the river flowing through it. It is an extension of the river found in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"), and therefore contains mostly the same river fish. Two fish, [Chub](/Chub "Chub") and [Dorado](/Dorado "Dorado"), are found in the forest river but not in town; the [Smallmouth Bass](/Smallmouth_Bass "Smallmouth Bass") is found in town but not in the forest river (it is, however, found in the forest pond). Also, the forest river is home in [Winter](/Winter "Winter") to a [Legendary Fish](/Fish#Legendary_Fish "Fish"), the [Glacierfish](/Glacierfish "Glacierfish"), as well as the [Glacierfish Jr.](/Glacierfish_Jr. "Glacierfish Jr."), one of the [legendary fish II](/Fish#Legendary_Fish_II "Fish").

### Pond

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/d3/ForestPondFishingZones.png/200px-ForestPondFishingZones.png)](/File:ForestPondFishingZones.png)

[Distances from land](/Fishing#Distance_from_Land "Fishing") in the Cindersap Forest pond.

The second body of water for fishing in Cindersap Forest is the large pond in its northwest corner. Its selection of fish is more limited than the river ([Carp](/Carp "Carp"), [Catfish](/Catfish "Catfish"), [Midnight Carp](/Midnight_Carp "Midnight Carp"), [Perch](/Perch "Perch"), [Pike](/Pike "Pike"), [Smallmouth Bass](/Smallmouth_Bass "Smallmouth Bass"), and [Walleye](/Walleye "Walleye"), dependent on season).

### Waterfalls

The last body of water for fishing in Cindersap Forest is beneath the waterfalls near the Abandoned House. It has all the forest river fish, and also the [Goby](/Goby "Goby") that can be caught at any season and time, and [Salmon](/Salmon "Salmon") can be caught in [Fall](/Fall "Fall") at any time. (This is the only location where Salmon can be caught after 7pm.)

### Secret

By fishing from the southernmost point of Cindersap Forest near the entrance to the sewers towards the ocean, with a fishing level of at least 15 (only possible temporarily via [buffs](/Fishing#Food "Fishing")), an [Iridium Krobus](/Iridium_Krobus "Iridium Krobus") statue can be obtained.
