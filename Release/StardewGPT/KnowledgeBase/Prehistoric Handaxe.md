The **Prehistoric Handaxe** is an [Artifact](/Artifacts "Artifacts") that can be found by digging up an [Artifact Spot](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") in [the Mountains](/The_Mountain "The Mountain"), [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest"), or the [Bus Stop](/Bus_Stop "Bus Stop") area. It also has a chance to appear inside [Artifact Troves](/Artifact_Trove "Artifact Trove").

## Donation

Donation of this item contributes to the total count of donations for the [Museum](/Museum "Museum").
