# Farm Maps

:   *See also: [Cabin](/Cabin "Cabin"), [Cabin Locations](/Cabin/Cabin_Locations "Cabin/Cabin Locations")*

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/e5/Farm_Map_Selection.png/300px-Farm_Map_Selection.png)](/File:Farm_Map_Selection.png)

The selection area for maps at character creation.

There are eight different **Farm Maps** to choose from in *[Stardew Valley](/Stardew_Valley "Stardew Valley")*. Each map features a unique layout of [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm"), offering different benefits and favoring specific [skills](/Skills "Skills"). Only one map can be chosen when starting a new game, and cannot be changed once selected.

The Standard Farm offers the most space to plant [crops](/Crops "Crops") and raise [animals](/Animals "Animals"). The other seven farms lack farming area, but offer special perks in comparison:

:   The Riverland Farm encourages [Fishing](/Skills#Fishing "Skills").
:   The Forest Farm encourages [Foraging](/Skills#Foraging "Skills").
:   The Hill-top Farm encourages [Mining](/Skills#Mining "Skills").
:   The Wilderness Farm encourages [Combat](/Skills#Combat "Skills") (but only gives 1/3 Combat experience for it).
:   The Four Corners Farm offers perks from other maps and encourages [Multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer").
:   The Beach Farm offers extra items and encourages [Fishing](/Skills#Fishing "Skills") and [Foraging](/Skills#Foraging "Skills"), but discourages [Farming](/Skills#Farming "Skills") late game.
:   The Meadowlands Farm encourages [Farming](/Skills#Farming "Skills"), specifically raising [animals](/Animals "Animals"), and comes with two [chickens](/Chicken "Chicken") by default.

The interior of the [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") is decorated to match the theme of the chosen map. This includes [furniture](/Furniture "Furniture"), [wallpaper](/Wallpaper "Wallpaper") and [flooring](/Flooring "Flooring"). The exterior of the house is the same regardless of map choice.

There is an abandoned building on all maps which can later be turned into the [Greenhouse](/Greenhouse "Greenhouse"). Once repaired, it can be moved by visiting the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop") or [Wizard's Tower](/Wizard%27s_Tower "Wizard's Tower").

## History

* [1.0](/Version_History#1.0 "Version History"): Introduced Standard Farm Map.
* [1.1](/Version_History#1.1 "Version History"): Added Forest, Hill-top, Riverland, and Wilderness Farm Maps.
* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Added Four Corners Farm Map.
* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): Added Beach Farm Map. Chat interface changes, which eliminated a bug preventing placement of path tiles near [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest"). [Greenhouse](/Greenhouse "Greenhouse") can now be moved.
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Added Meadowlands Farm Map. Players on the Riverland Farm Map get a [Fish Smoker](/Fish_Smoker "Fish Smoker") by default. [Iridium Golems](/Iridium_Golem "Iridium Golem") now spawn exclusively on the Wilderness Farm Map. Monsters now give 1/3 of normal Combat experience when spawned on the Farm. [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") can now be moved.
