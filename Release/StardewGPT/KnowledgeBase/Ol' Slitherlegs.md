**Ol' Slitherlegs** is a power [book](/Books "Books") that can be purchased from the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller") for data-sort-value="25000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)25,000g.

The first reading grants the player a power that reduces their [speed](/Speed "Speed") penalty while moving through [grass](/Grass "Grass") and [crops](/Crops "Crops") by ≈67%.[[1]](#cite_note-slowdown-1) Once read, it can be found in [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s Menu on the [Special Items & Powers](/Special_Items_%26_Powers "Special Items & Powers") tab. Each subsequent reading gives the player 20 [XP](/Skills "Skills") in each of the 5 skills.

## Bundles

Ol' Slitherlegs is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Ol' Slitherlegs is not used in any [tailoring](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a green dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a green dye when used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.
