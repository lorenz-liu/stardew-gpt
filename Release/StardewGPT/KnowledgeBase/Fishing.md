# Fishing

:   *For a list of fish and their locations, see the [Fish](/Fish "Fish") page.*
:   *For an index to the completed fish collection, see the [Fish Collection](/Collections#Fish "Collections") page.*
:   *For the guide to catching all fish, see [Fishing Strategy](/Fishing_Strategy "Fishing Strategy").*
:   *See also: [Tackle](/Tackle "Tackle"), [Bait](/Bait "Bait")*

**Fishing** is a [skill](/Skills "Skills") associated with catching [fish](/Fish "Fish") with a [fishing rod](/Tools#Fishing_Poles "Tools") or by collecting items from [crab pots](/Crab_Pot "Crab Pot").

## Overview & Controls

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Fishingicons.png/200px-Fishingicons.png)](/File:Fishingicons.png)

Fishing interface showing normal and [Legendary Fish](/Fish#Legendary_Fish "Fish")

To cast a fishing rod into a body of water, hold down the Use Tool button ([![Left-Click.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/bb/Left-Click.png/24px-Left-Click.png)](/File:Left-Click.png) on PC, [![360 X.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a6/360_X.png/24px-360_X.png)](/File:360_X.png) button on Xbox, [![PS4 Square.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/ec/PS4_Square.png/24px-PS4_Square.png)](/File:PS4_Square.png) on PlayStation, [![Switch Y.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3b/Switch_Y.png/24px-Switch_Y.png)](/File:Switch_Y.png) on Switch). A meter appears, indicating how far the line will go. Release to cast. It is also possible to slightly alter the line's placement mid-cast with the movement keys (or controller stick), allowing for better control over aiming the cast and allowing to "cast diagonally" by 1 tile, which can make it possible to hit bubbles that would otherwise be unreachable.

When a fish bites, the bobber wiggles and an exclamation point appears above the player character's head along with a distinct sound. Click the Use Tool button to start the mini-game.

The mini-game display contains a movable green rectangle that indicates the area of effect for the fishing line. The goal of the fishing mini-game is to keep the small fish icon within the green rectangle until the progress bar to the right is completely filled. If the fish's icon moves above or below the green rectangle, the progress bar decreases, and the fish escapes if the progress bar reaches the bottom.

The green rectangle can be raised by pressing the Use Tool button. Repeated pressing holds the green in place when it is not on the bottom, and holding the button raises it at increasing speed. Be careful moving it though, for it gathers momentum quickly and takes time to reverse, making it easy to overshoot the fish and drain the progress bar.

Upon catching a fish, its name and size are displayed. Players can keep track of how many fish they have caught and their record lengths in their [Collections menu](/Collections#Fish "Collections").

The first time a player catches each specific kind of fish, a message saying "First catch!" appears.

## Specific Mechanics

### Types of Fish

The types of fish that can be found is determined by:

* the [season](/Seasons "Seasons")
* the [weather](/Weather "Weather") (*i.e.,* sunny or rainy)
* the body of water
* the time of day

For example, the forest lake, the forest river, and the town river each contain their own types of fish, though there are some overlaps. The full list can be found on the [Fishing Strategy](/Fishing_Strategy#Location_Tables "Fishing Strategy") page.

Also note that the [Legendary Fish](/Fish#Legendary_Fish "Fish") can only be caught on specific water tiles, but in general, the specific water tile does not matter.

### Bar Size

The green rectangle increases in size with higher fishing skill level. The total number of pixels of the entire rectangle is 568. At Fishing level 0, the bar size has a length of 96 pixels. This is increased by 8 pixels for every increase in fishing level, so that at level 10, the bar size has a length of 176 pixels. The bar size can be further increased by using either the [Cork Bobber](/Cork_Bobber "Cork Bobber") or [Deluxe Bait](/Deluxe_Bait "Deluxe Bait") or increasing the fishing level past level 10 by using an enchant at the [Forge](/Forge "Forge") or with food [buffs](/Buffs "Buffs"). The maximum possible bar size is therefore 308 pixels, which can be achieved using the [Advanced Iridium Rod](/Advanced_Iridium_Rod "Advanced Iridium Rod") and having level 10 fishing, using 2 cork bobbers, using deluxe bait, using the Master Enchant at the Forge, and eating [Seafoam Pudding](/Seafoam_Pudding "Seafoam Pudding") with [Qi Seasoning](/Qi_Seasoning "Qi Seasoning") and a dish with the Shrimp ingredient from the [Chef](/Desert_Festival#Chef "Desert Festival") at the Desert Festival. Outside of the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival"), the maximum possible bar size is 284 pixels, exactly half of the total number of pixels of the rectangle.

The upgraded fishing rods (*i.e.,* [Fiberglass Rod](/Fiberglass_Rod "Fiberglass Rod"), [Iridium Rod](/Iridium_Rod "Iridium Rod"), and [Advanced Iridium Rod](/Advanced_Iridium_Rod "Advanced Iridium Rod")) do **not** affect the bar size. However, using the [Training Rod](/Training_Rod "Training Rod") does affect the bar size; it sets the player's fishing skill equal to level 5 for the purposes of determining how big to make the green bar, if the player has not reached level 5 yet.

### Casting Distance

The player's [skill](/Skills "Skills") level determines the maximum possible casting distance. At level 0, the maximum cast possible is 3 tiles to the south or north and 4 tiles to the east or west. The distance increases by 1 tile (in all directions) at levels 1, 4, 8 and 15.

Reaching a skill level of 15 is possible with a combination of [food](/Cooking "Cooking"), [Qi Seasoning](/Qi_Seasoning "Qi Seasoning") and/or a Fishing Pole [enchanted with the Master enchant](/Forge#Tool_enchantments "Forge").

The actual casting distance will be a fraction of the maximum distance, based on the fullness of the mini-game casting meter. Any cast that is more than 99% of the maximum distance is highlighted by the text "Max".[[1]](#cite_note-max_cast-1) "Max" casts do not receive any special benefit.

The length of the cast is less important than where the bobber lands. Longer casts only improve fishing results if the bobber lands further from the shore. Standing next to the water's edge allows casts to go further from land, as does avoiding locations with other nearby shores (*e.g.,* try to fish where a river is as wide and straight as possible).

### Fish Bite Time

The fish bite time measures the amount of time that passes before a fish bites once the fishing rod is in the water. This base time is a random number between 0.6 and 30 seconds. Each increase in fishing level decreases the maximum amount of time it takes for a fish to bite by 0.25 seconds. Each [Spinner](/Spinner "Spinner") used decreases the maximum amount of time it takes for a fish to bite by 5 seconds and each [Dressed Spinner](/Dressed_Spinner "Dressed Spinner") used decreases the maximum amount of time it takes for a fish to bite by 10 seconds.

The minimum and maximum time it takes for a fish to bite are then reduced by 25% if it will be the player's first fish bite after the fishing rod is the water (*i.e.* if they didn't fail to hook a fish before).

Then, using [Bait](/Bait_(item) "Bait (item)"), [Magnet](/Magnet "Magnet"), [Magic Bait](/Magic_Bait "Magic Bait"), or any type of [Targeted Bait](/Targeted_Bait "Targeted Bait") decreases both the minimum and maximum amount of time it takes for a fish to bite by 50%. [Wild Bait](/Wild_Bait "Wild Bait") and [Challenge Bait](/Challenge_Bait "Challenge Bait") further decreases this by another 25%, for a total of a 62.5% decrease compared to no bait. [Deluxe Bait](/Deluxe_Bait "Deluxe Bait") decreases both the minimum and maximum amount of time it takes for a fish to bite by 67% compared to no bait.

The minimum amount of time it takes for a fish to bite cannot drop below 0.5 seconds.

### Distance from Land

The number of tiles from "land" that a fishing rod is cast in can impact the chances of catching a fish. The game considers most walkable surfaces, including islands, piers, and stone bridges to be "land"; wooden footbridges are one exception. The distance from land in all directions that the bobber is cast in is capped at 5, regardless of if the bobber physically landed further than 5 tiles away. Maps showing each tile's distance from land are available on the [Fishing Strategy](/Fishing_Strategy#Fishing_Zones "Fishing Strategy") page. Note, however, that calculating distance from land on iOS and Android is more complicated because of a [bug](#Bugs).

Casting further from land provides several benefits:

* The chance of catching [Trash](/Trash "Trash") decreases.
* The size and quality of the fish is on average better.[[2]](#cite_note-2)
* The chance of hooking some fish like [Sturgeon](/Sturgeon "Sturgeon") or [Legendary Fish](/Fish#Legendary_Fish "Fish") increases.[[3]](#cite_note-3)
* [Treasure Chests](#Treasure_Chests) may contain more valuable items.

Darker shades of blue indicate deeper water. However, the color of the water is not a reliable indicator of a tile's distance from land. In some cases, dark blue (deep) water is 0 tiles from land (*e.g.,* next to the pier at [the Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach")). Conversely, light blue (shallow) water can be 5 tiles from land (*e.g.,* at the east end of the Beach).

### Perfect Catches

The game displays a "Perfect!" catch if the fish never leaves the green rectangle or if the [Treasure Hunter](/Treasure_Hunter "Treasure Hunter") tackle is equipped and the fish leaves the green rectangle only while the treasure chest is being captured. Perfect catches grant two benefits:

* If it is a silver or gold quality fish, the quality of the fish is increased by one. (*i.e.,* a silver quality fish becomes a gold quality fish, a gold quality fish becomes an iridium quality fish).
* The amount of experience awarded for catching the fish is multiplied by 2.4.

### Fish Size & Quality

The size of each fish caught using a Fishing Rod is dependent on the Distance from Land, the farmer's Fishing Level, the type of fish, and how close to "Perfect" the catch was. When catching a fish, a "size factor", `fishSize`, is determined by the equation `fishSize = Distance/5 * (Skill+2)/10 * Random/100`, where "Distance" refers to the Distance from Land, "Skill" refers to the farmer's Fishing Level (rounded down to the nearest even value), and "Random" is a random integer value between 90 and 110, inclusive. Also note that if the player has not reached Fishing level 10 yet, the value for Skill ranges ranges between all possible even numbers from the farmer's Fishing Level (rounded down to the nearest even value) and 10 inclusive. This size factor is then limited to a range of 0 to 1 inclusive.[[4]](#cite_note-fishSize-4)

Each type of fish has a minimum and maximum size. When a fish is hooked, it is given an initial size based on these values and the size factor, equal to `minFishSize + (maxFishSize - minFishSize) * fishSize + 1`, rounded down, in inches.[[5]](#cite_note-fishSizeRange-5) The size shrinks by 1" for every 0.8 seconds that the fish is not being reeled in. However, the size cannot go below `minFishSize`. Additionally, if the catch was imperfect and the final size is equal to `maxFishSize` exactly, the fish size is reduced by 1".[[6]](#cite_note-fishShrinking-6) A consequence of this procedure is that the maximum possible fish size observed in game is always 1 higher than the value of `maxFishSize`, which can be obtained with perfect or near-perfect catch (less than 0.8 seconds not reeling in the fish). In addition, some fish have the same values for `minFishSize` and `maxFishSize`, such as the [Legendary Fish](/Fish#Legendary_Fish "Fish") and non-Ghostfish Mines fish. Due to the final adjustment for non-perfect catches, the minimum possible size for these fish is 1 lower than the value of `minFishSize` specified in the data.

Some fish, namely the [Sandfish](/Sandfish "Sandfish"), [Scorpion Carp](/Scorpion_Carp "Scorpion Carp"), and [Goby](/Goby "Goby"), cannot be caught at their maximum theoretical size, as they are limited by the size of their ponds which only have a maximum [Distance from Land](#Distance_from_Land) of 2.

Base fish quality is determined directly by the value of `fishSize`. A value below 0.33 corresponds to normal quality, a value between 0.33 and 0.66 corresponds to silver quality, and a value of 0.66 or higher corresponds to gold quality. This quality can be improved by one grade for each [Quality Bobber](/Quality_Bobber "Quality Bobber") equipped. It can also be improved by one grade with a perfect catch as long as the fish was at least silver quality initially. These boosts stack, with the Quality Bobber boost considered before the Perfect Catch boost. Since the maximum base fish quality is gold, the only way to get an iridium quality fish is by either getting a perfect catch or using the Quality Bobber.[[7]](#cite_note-fishQuality-7)

Any fish caught using a [Training Rod](/Training_Rod "Training Rod") has its size set to the minimum and its quality set to normal, overriding the above procedure.[[8]](#cite_note-fishSizeTrainingRod-8)

### Bubbles

Sometimes, a pool of bubbles appears in the water. Casting into them results in blue sparkles appearing. Bubbles grant two benefits:[[9]](#cite_note-bubbles-9)

* Fish bite four times faster.
* When determining the type of fish that is hooked, the effective [Distance from Land](#Distance_from_Land) is increased by 1, *i.e.,* trash is less likely to be caught and difficult-to-catch are more likely. Some [Legendary Fish](/Fish#Legendary_Fish "Fish") can be found closer to the shore when bubbles are present.
  + This adjustment to the distance from land only applies to the type of fish that is caught. Bubbles have no effect on fish size or quality or on treasure chests.

No more than one bubble spot can exist per area (*e.g.,* [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"), [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest")) at any given time, but different bubble spots can occur in multiple areas simultaneously. Some bubble spots are not possible for the player to reach with the fishing rod. Bubble spots cannot spawn on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") unless the player has chosen the [Riverland Farm Map](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps")[[10]](#cite_note-performtenminuteupdate-10).

### Fish Frenzies

Fish Frenzies are events that can randomly happen during the day (before 11 PM) at either [The Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach"), [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest"), [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"), or [The Mountain](/The_Mountain "The Mountain"). They can appear once the player has met at least one of these two requirements:

* They have played for at least 4 days and have caught at least 3 fish.
* They have played for at least 15 days.

Fish Frenzies cannot happen on [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days, except during the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival"), [Trout Derby](/Trout_Derby "Trout Derby"), [SquidFest](/SquidFest "SquidFest") and [Night Market](/Night_Market "Night Market"). If it is a non-Festival day and at least one of the two requirements are met, each time bubbles spawn in one of the four areas listed above, there is a 1% chance for the bubbles to become a fish frenzy instead.

During a frenzy, bubbles will spawn for a specific fish (one that can be caught in that location on that day) in that location. When a Fish Frenzy happens, players are notified with the message " A(n) [Fish name] frenzy has begun in [location]." When fishing in these bubbles, only the fish for the frenzy can be caught. [Legendary Fish](/Fish#Legendary_Fish "Fish"), [Legendary Fish II](/Fish#Legendary_Fish_II "Fish"), and catchable items will never be the specific fish for the frenzy. The effects of these bubbles are similar to that of the combination of [normal bubbles](#Bubbles) and [Targeted Bait](/Targeted_Bait "Targeted Bait"):

* Fish Frenzies cut bite time in half.
* The specific fish for the frenzy becomes the only fish that can be caught in that area.

Fish Frenzies will always last for at least 90 minutes. Once 90 minutes have passed, there is a 10% chance for the frenzy to end. There is also a chance for the frenzy to end every 10 minutes afterwards assuming it has not ended yet. This chance increases by 0.56% every time. So for instance, if a Fish Frenzy starts at 2:00 PM, there is a 15.6% chance for the frenzy to end at 5:10 PM, assuming it was not ended earlier.

### Energy

At fishing skill level 0, casting a fishing pole consumes 8 energy. Each subsequent fishing level decreases the amount of energy used by 0.1. (While the game shows whole numbers, internally it does not round numbers.) Thus, on the 10th cast at level 1 fishing, the game shows an energy cost of only 7 instead of 8. At level 10 fishing, all casts use 7 energy.

### Angled Casts

It is possible to slightly alter the line's placement mid-cast with the movement keys, allowing better control over aiming the cast. Angling the cast is useful in two scenarios:

* In some non-standard fishing spots, it is necessary to angle the cast to get further from land.
* When bubbles spawn in an awkward location, it is sometimes necessary to angle the cast to get the bobber to land in them. Some bubble spots are completely unreachable.

### Time

* In single-player:
  + Time is paused while the mini-game is active.
    - If the player is attacked (for example, by a [slime](/Slimes "Slimes") while fishing in the [Secret Woods](/Secret_Woods "Secret Woods")), the enemy will continue to attack and damage the player during the minigame, even though the clock is paused.
    - Active buffs will wear out during the mini-game.
  + Time is not paused during:
    - The animation when the casting bar is increasing.
    - The animation when the line is going out.
    - The animation when the fish is flying back towards the player.
    - The popup that shows the player what type of fish they have caught.
  + If the player caught a treasure chest, time is paused while looking through the chest's inventory.
* In [multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer"), time is never paused.
* During the fishing minigame at the [Stardew Valley Fair](/Stardew_Valley_Fair "Stardew Valley Fair") and the ice-fishing contest at the [Festival of Ice](/Festival_of_Ice "Festival of Ice"), the contest timer is never paused.

## Experience Points

All non-fish items caught with the fishing pole earn 3 XP each, and crab pots earn the player 5 XP each time the player collects from them, no matter what is collected. How Fishing Experience is gained by Fish Ponds is detailed on the [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") page.

250 Fishing XP is gained for reading a copy of the [Bait And Bobber](/Bait_And_Bobber "Bait And Bobber") or [Book Of Stars](/Book_Of_Stars "Book Of Stars").

Otherwise, for each catch of fish, the amount of experience earned is as follows: [[11]](#cite_note-fishexp-11)

`XP = ((Fish Quality + 1) * 3) + (Fish Difficulty / 3)`

:   Fish Quality = 0 for normal, 1 for silver, 2 for gold, or 4 for iridium
:   Fish Difficulty is a number from 5-110, listed in the data file `Fish.xnb`.

For example, a regular quality sardine (difficulty 30) would net the player (0 + 1) \* 3 + (30 / 3) = 13 XP. Trash also follows this formula with a quality and difficulty of zero leaving only the base 3 XP.

XP is multiplied by 2.2 if the player also catches a treasure chest, by 2.4 if the catch was "perfect" and by 5 if the catch was a Legendary fish. These multiplications stack, and are performed in order (first treasure chest, then perfect, then Legendary). If at any point in the calculation the XP is not a whole number, the decimal part of the number is truncated off before performing the next calculation.

For example, catching a perfect Sardine with a treasure chest would earn the player 13 \* 2.2 = 28.6 (truncated to 28) XP, then 28 \* 2.4 = 67.2 (truncated to 67) XP.

A perfect catch will increase a silver quality fish to gold quality, and a gold quality fish to iridium quality, but it is the original quality that is used in the XP equation.

For example, catching a perfect Iridium quality Sardine would use the original gold quality multiplier, so the player would earn ((2 + 1) \* 3) + (30 / 3) \* 2.4 = 45 XP.

As another example, catching a perfect gold quality Crimsonfish (difficulty 95) with a treasure chest would net the player ((1 + 1) \* 3) + (95 / 3) = 37.67 (truncated to 37) base XP; 37 \* 2.2 = 81.4 (truncated to 81) XP; 81 \* 2.4 = 194.4 (truncated to 194) XP; 194 \* 5 = 970 XP.

Experience level is increased immediately upon harvesting a Crab Pot or catching an item with a fishing pole, but the "level up" window doesn't appear until after going to sleep.

## Treasure Chests

During the fishing mini-game, between 1 and 3 seconds after it starts, a treasure chest may appear inside the vertical bar. The base chance of finding a chest is 15%. The probability can be increased by 15% with the [Magnet](/Magnet "Magnet"), by 5% with the [Treasure Hunter](/Treasure_Hunter "Treasure Hunter") tackle equipped (by 10% with two equipped), and by 15% with the [Pirate profession](#Fishing_Skill). This is adjusted by half the value of daily [luck](/Luck "Luck"), increasing by 5% at best or decreasing by 5% at worst, with the [Special Charm](/Special_Charm "Special Charm") adding 1.25%. Food/drink buffs add 0.5% for every point of Luck.[[12]](#cite_note-treasure_chance-12) Treasure chests will never appear when playing the fishing minigame at the [Stardew Valley Fair](/Stardew_Valley_Fair "Stardew Valley Fair") or during the fishing contest at the [Festival of Ice](/Festival_of_Ice "Festival of Ice").

Treasure chests have their own progression bar, fillable by keeping the green bobber bar behind the treasure chest icon ![Fishing Treasure Chest.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/bb/Fishing_Treasure_Chest.png/24px-Fishing_Treasure_Chest.png). If obtained, the player is awarded random bonus loot after catching the fish. **Losing the fish also loses the treasure chest.**

After successfully catching a fish and a treasure chest, the treasure chest items will appear in a menu. In the case of successfully catching a fish and a treasure chest while [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory") is full, the caught fish will appear in the treasure chest menu alongside any treasures caught. Dismissing the menu before putting all of the items into inventory will forfeit the rest of the items.

Multiple items can appear in a treasure chest. If multiple items appear in a chest, the same item can appear in multiple slots (with some exceptions, such as special items from [festivals](/Festivals "Festivals")). The number of items specified in the table is the number that can appear in a single stack (*e.g.,* at most one lost book appears in a stack, but two lost books are possible if each appears in a different stack).

Daily Luck, along with the Distance from Land, slightly increases the chances that various special items appear (as indicated by ≈ in the following table). Luck Buffs increase the chance that a stack of resources or geodes will double in size and also allow better rings to appear ([Magnet Ring](/Magnet_Ring "Magnet Ring") instead of [Small Magnet Ring](/Small_Magnet_Ring "Small Magnet Ring") and [Glow Ring](/Glow_Ring "Glow Ring") instead of [Small Glow Ring](/Small_Glow_Ring "Small Glow Ring")).

### Golden Treasure Chests

After claiming [Fishing Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave"), the treasure chest that appears when fishing may be golden. ![Golden Fishing Treasure Chest.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/c8/Golden_Fishing_Treasure_Chest.png/24px-Golden_Fishing_Treasure_Chest.png) The base chance of a fishing treasure chest to be golden is 25%. This is adjusted by the value of daily [luck](/Luck "Luck"), increasing by 10% at best or decreasing by 10% at worst, with the [Special Charm](/Special_Charm "Special Charm") adding 2.5%.[[12]](#cite_note-treasure_chance-12) So the maximum possible chance for a fishing treasure chest to be golden is 37.5%, which happens at max daily luck and with the Special Charm obtained.

Golden treasure chests provide on average more items than regular treasure chests. Excluding items that cannot be found in multiple slots, such as [Roe](/Roe "Roe") and [Jewels Of The Sea](/Jewels_Of_The_Sea "Jewels Of The Sea"), the exact percentage is equal to 27.4%. In addition, each item (aside from a few such as [Qi Beans](/Qi_Bean "Qi Bean"), [Golden Animal Crackers](/Golden_Animal_Cracker "Golden Animal Cracker"), and [Roe](/Roe "Roe")) has a 50% chance of being one of the following items instead:[[13]](#cite_note-treasure-13)

* [Iridium Bar](/Iridium_Bar "Iridium Bar")
* [Bait And Bobber](/Bait_And_Bobber "Bait And Bobber"), [Combat Quarterly](/Combat_Quarterly "Combat Quarterly"), [Mining Monthly](/Mining_Monthly "Mining Monthly"), [Stardew Valley Almanac](/Stardew_Valley_Almanac "Stardew Valley Almanac"), or [Woodcutter's Weekly](/Woodcutter%27s_Weekly "Woodcutter's Weekly")
* [Carrot Seeds](/Carrot_Seeds "Carrot Seeds"), [Summer Squash Seeds](/Summer_Squash_Seeds "Summer Squash Seeds"), [Broccoli Seeds](/Broccoli_Seeds "Broccoli Seeds"), or [Powdermelon Seeds](/Powdermelon_Seeds "Powdermelon Seeds"), depending on the season and date the treasure chest was opened
* [Fish Taco](/Fish_Taco "Fish Taco")
* [Fairy Dust](/Fairy_Dust "Fairy Dust")
* [Dressed Spinner](/Dressed_Spinner "Dressed Spinner")
* [Challenge Bait](/Challenge_Bait "Challenge Bait")
* [Magnet](/Magnet "Magnet")
* [Stardrop Tea](/Stardrop_Tea "Stardrop Tea")

* Master Angler (Catch every [fish](/Fish "Fish"))

## Bugs

* On iOS and Android, there is a bug that alters the calculated [Distance from Land](#Distance_from_Land) at most locations. The distance is effectively calculated as if the bobber were located one tile northwest of its actual location. Therefore, casts to the south and east typically have a lower distance than they should; casts to the north and west typically have a higher distance. This bug affects fish size/quality and treasure chest contents, but it does not affect the locations where Legendary Fish can be caught.
* On Nintendo Switch and mobile platforms, casting the rod at exactly 2am can cause the game to fail to end the day. The player can select items from inventory, but cannot move or take action, and the character doesn't fall asleep. ([Forum thread.](https://forums.stardewvalley.net/threads/game-softlocked-at-2-am.34383/))

## History

* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Introduced [Training Rod](/Training_Rod "Training Rod"). Can now attach [baits](/Bait "Bait"), [tackles](/Tackle "Tackle"), etc., directly from a [treasure chest](#Treasure_Chests). Bottom edge of fishing bobber bar made slightly more generous. Effect of fishing level on fish size no longer caps at 10. Removed exploit where clicking the journal icon would pause time but still allow fish to bite. Fixed bug where player's fishing level and bait were not taken into account for the wait time if the first nibble was missed.
* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): Added [fishing presents](#Presents). Added [Golden Eggs](/Golden_Egg "Golden Egg") and [Qi Beans](/Qi_Bean "Qi Bean") to potential treasure chest rewards. Catching a non-fish item no longer triggers the minigame. Added option to choose between multiple fish-bite chime sounds.
* [1.5.2](/Version_History#1.5.2 "Version History"): Fixed crash when fishing on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") using [Magic Bait](/Magic_Bait "Magic Bait").
* [1.5.4](/Version_History#1.5.4 "Version History"): Fixed softlock when fishing with multiple rods in inventory after catching a treasure and casting from a different rod.
* [1.5.5](/Version_History#1.5.5 "Version History"): Fixed issue preventing certain spots in the [Mountain lake](/The_Mountain "The Mountain") from spawning bubbles.
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Added [Advanced Iridium Rod](/Advanced_Iridium_Rod "Advanced Iridium Rod"). Submerged fishing bobber now automatically recolored to match the water. Added [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box"), [Golden Mystery Box](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box"), [Golden Animal Cracker](/Golden_Animal_Cracker "Golden Animal Cracker"), [Sonar Bobber](/Sonar_Bobber "Sonar Bobber"), [Deluxe Bait](/Deluxe_Bait "Deluxe Bait"), skill books, [Golden Bobber](/Golden_Bobber "Golden Bobber"), [Roe](/Roe "Roe"), [Jewels Of The Sea](/Jewels_Of_The_Sea "Jewels Of The Sea"), and Golden Tag to potential treasure chest rewards. Added Golden Fishing Tresure Chests with more exclusive rewards. Players are now notified when they first catch each specific type of fish. If you haven't gained fishing experience after obtaining the Bamboo Pole, before spring 28, year 1, Willy comes to your house and give you a training rod after giving you a quick lesson on fishing basics that makes you reach level 1 fishing. Fishing Experience can now be gained by reading the [Bait And Bobber](/Bait_And_Bobber "Bait And Bobber") or [Book Of Stars](/Book_Of_Stars "Book Of Stars").
* [1.6.3](/Version_History#1.6.3 "Version History"): Fixed bug introduced in v1.6 which caused other types of bait to act as [targeted bait](/Targeted_Bait "Targeted Bait") for no fish, resulting in excess trash fished.
* [1.6.4](/Version_History#1.6.4 "Version History"): Added [Fish Frenzies](#Fish_Frenzies). Slightly reduced chance to find [Mystery Boxes](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") and [Golden Mystery Boxes](/Golden_Mystery_Box "Golden Mystery Box").
