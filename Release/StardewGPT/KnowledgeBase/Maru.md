**Maru** is a [villager](/Villagers "Villagers") who lives in [The Mountains](/The_Mountain "The Mountain") north of [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town"). She's one of the twelve characters available to [marry](/Marriage "Marriage").

She lives north of town with her family in a house attached to [Robin's carpenter shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop").

In the Social Status menu, Maru's outfit will change to a nursing uniform when she is at her job at the [clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic").

## Schedule

After leaving the Island at 6pm, Maru will immediately go home to bed. Maru never visits the Resort on Tuesdays, Thursdays, or [Festival](/Festivals "Festivals") days.

## Relationships

Maru lives with her parents [Demetrius](/Demetrius "Demetrius") and [Robin](/Robin "Robin"), as well as her half-brother [Sebastian](/Sebastian "Sebastian") who is Robin's son. She has a strained relationship with Sebastian and wishes they were closer.

Maru works for [Harvey](/Harvey "Harvey") at the clinic, and both worry that it doesn't receive enough patients. Maru dances with Harvey at the Flower dance if neither is dancing with the player. She is also friends with [Penny](/Penny "Penny"), and can frequently be seen sitting down with her at the bench south of the Saloon.

## Marriage

:   *Main article: [Marriage](/Marriage "Marriage")*

Once married, Maru will move into the [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse"). Like other marriage candidates, she will add her own room to the right of the bedroom. She'll also set up a small area behind the farmhouse where she'll sometimes go to work on her gadgets. She will continue to work at the [clinic](/Harvey%27s_Clinic "Harvey's Clinic") and travel to [town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") to work there on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

On rainy mornings, Maru may offer you a gift she made in her workshop: [Warp Totem: Farm](/Warp_Totem:_Farm "Warp Totem: Farm"), [Quality Fertilizer](/Quality_Fertilizer "Quality Fertilizer"), [Refined Quartz](/Refined_Quartz "Refined Quartz"), a [Gate](/Gate "Gate"), or a [Bomb](/Bomb "Bomb"). On rainy nights she may offer you dessert: [Rice Pudding](/Rice_Pudding "Rice Pudding"), [Blueberry Tart](/Blueberry_Tart "Blueberry Tart"), [Cookies](/Cookie "Cookie"), or [Chocolate Cake](/Chocolate_Cake "Chocolate Cake"). On days when Maru stays indoors all day, she may offer you a [Cherry Bomb](/Cherry_Bomb "Cherry Bomb"), a [Bomb](/Bomb "Bomb"), [Fried Mushroom](/Fried_Mushroom "Fried Mushroom"), or [Crab Cakes](/Crab_Cakes "Crab Cakes"), saying she found it in her "scrap pile".

## Trivia

* Maru's portrait displays her with glasses; however, there are no visible glasses on her sprite.
* Maru has three portraits that do not have a nurse uniform equivalent. If her dialogue would normally call for one of these portraits, she will default to the surprised portrait.
