The **Frozen Tear** is a [Mineral](/Minerals "Minerals") that can be found:

* via [foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") between floors 40 and 79.
  + this is reduced to floors 71-79 if the [Dangerous Mines](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") are active.
* from slain [Dust Sprites](/Dust_Sprite "Dust Sprite") in The Mines (2% chance).
* in containers in The Mines between floors 40 and 79 after the player has reached the bottom of The Mines (4.62% chance)
* by [panning](/Pans "Pans") (4.9% chance).
* in [Frozen Geodes](/Frozen_Geode "Frozen Geode") (6.25% chance) and [Omni Geodes](/Omni_Geode "Omni Geode") (2.0833% chance).
* in [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") (4% chance).
* occasionally in a [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") after the player has reached the bottom of The Mines.
* in an [Ice Pip](/Ice_Pip "Ice Pip") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), which may produce 5 Frozen Tears when the population of the pond reaches 9.
* through the mail from the [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard") at any [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") level greater than zero friendship points.
* at the [Statue Of Endless Fortune](/Statue_Of_Endless_Fortune "Statue Of Endless Fortune") on Winter 10, [Sebastian](/Sebastian "Sebastian")'s birthday.
* as an item sometimes sold for data-sort-value="2500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,500g from [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre")'s booth at the [Feast of the Winter Star](/Feast_of_the_Winter_Star "Feast of the Winter Star").
* via [foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern")

Frozen Tear can be destroyed by [bombs](/Crafting#Bombs "Crafting").

## Tailoring

Frozen Tear is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt211.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/cd/Shirt211.png/24px-Shirt211.png) [Dark Jacket](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a cyan dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the blue dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

* Frozen Tear may be randomly requested during any season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="225">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)225g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* 2 Frozen Tears may be requested by [Blobfish](/Blobfish "Blobfish") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 1 to 3.
* 5 Frozen Tears may be requested by [Ice Pip](/Ice_Pip "Ice Pip") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 3 to 5.
