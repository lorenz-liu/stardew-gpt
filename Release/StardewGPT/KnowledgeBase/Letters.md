# Letters

[![Mail.png](/mediawiki/images/f/fe/Mail.png)](/File:Mail.png)

**Letters** are messages sent to the player that can be read by interacting with the mailbox that is located on [The Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") and the [Ginger Island Farm](/Ginger_Island#The_Farm "Ginger Island"). They appear at the start of the day in the player's mailbox on specific days or the day after certain conditions have been met. Letters contain rewards, information about the game's mechanics, hints, reminders and lore.

Closing a letter with an unclaimed item will automatically attempt to place it into the [inventory](/Inventory "Inventory"), provided the player has enough inventory space. Otherwise, a menu will appear, similar to the [treasure chest menu](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing"), allowing the player to make room for the item. Dismissing this menu will forfeit the rewarded item.

Most letters received in the mail will be added to the [collection tab](/Collections "Collections"). The tab does not record notices received from passing out at 2am or from low [health](/Health "Health"). Letters shown do not include any attachments that came with the original letter, such as recipes or gifts.

Added ability to reread received letters anytime via the collections tab. Dismissing a letter with unclaimed items now automatically picks up the items.
