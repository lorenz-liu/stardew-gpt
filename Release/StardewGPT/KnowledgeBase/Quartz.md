**Quartz** is a [Mineral](/Minerals "Minerals") that can be found by foraging in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") or [Quarry Mine](/Quarry_Mine "Quarry Mine") and in [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"). It can also occasionally be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can"). [Stone Golems](/Stone_Golem "Stone Golem") have a 10% chance to drop Quartz when slain. A [Ghostfish](/Ghostfish "Ghostfish") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") may produce 1 Quartz when the population reaches 3, and 3 Quartz when the population reaches 9.

Quartz can be destroyed by [bombs](/Crafting#Bombs "Crafting").

Quartz can be used along with 1 [coal](/Coal "Coal") in a [furnace](/Furnace "Furnace") to produce [Refined Quartz](/Refined_Quartz "Refined Quartz"), taking 1.5 in-game hours to smelt.

## Bundles

Quartz is used in the ![Bundle Purple.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a1/Bundle_Purple.png/24px-Bundle_Purple.png) [Geologist's Bundle](/Bundles#Geologists_Bundle "Bundles") in the [Boiler Room](/Bundles#Boiler_Room "Bundles").

## Recipes

Quartz is not used in any recipes.

## Smelting

To smelt Quartz, equip it and activate the [furnace](/Furnace "Furnace") while there is [Coal](/Coal "Coal") in inventory. One unit of Coal is required for each smelting operation, regardless of the material or duration.

## Trading

Five Quartz can be traded at the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") in [the Calico Desert](/The_Desert "The Desert") for one [Bomb](/Bomb "Bomb").

## Tailoring

Quartz is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Gray Vest](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt167.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/cf/Shirt167.png/24px-Shirt167.png)](/File:Shirt167.png)

## Quests

* Quartz may be randomly requested during any season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="75">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)75g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* 5 Quartz are requested by [Ghostfish](/Ghostfish "Ghostfish") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 1 to 3.
* 5 Quartz may be requested by [Crab](/Crab "Crab"), [Cockle](/Cockle "Cockle"), [Lobster](/Lobster "Lobster"), [Mussel](/Mussel "Mussel"), [Oyster](/Oyster "Oyster"), or [Shrimp](/Shrimp "Shrimp") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 5 to 7.
