The **Diamond** is a [Mineral](/Minerals "Minerals") that can be obtained primarily from [Diamond Nodes](/Mining#Diamond_Node "Mining") and [Gem Nodes](/Mining#Gem_Node "Mining") in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), [Quarry](/Quarry "Quarry"), and [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon"). After reading [The Diamond Hunter](/The_Diamond_Hunter "The Diamond Hunter"), any rock has a small chance of yielding a Diamond when mined by hand. Diamonds may also be obtained in a number of other ways.

The Lost Book *[A Study on Diamond Yields](/Lost_Books#A_Study_on_Diamond_Yields "Lost Books")* contains information about the chances of finding diamond nodes in the mines on a given floor.

A Diamond is arguably the best gem to duplicate in the [Crystalarium](/Crystalarium "Crystalarium"), providing the largest source of money as a gem. Diamonds are also liked or loved by all giftable NPCs.

If a player has not received 4 candles from [Grandpa's Evaluation](/Grandpa#Grandpa.27s_Evaluation "Grandpa"), then they may be re-evaluated at any time by placing a Diamond into [Grandpa's Shrine](/The_Farm#Grandpa.27s_Shrine "The Farm").

## Locations

* [Diamond Nodes](/Mining#Diamond_Node "Mining") and [Gem Nodes](/Mining#Gem_Node "Mining") in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), [Quarry](/Quarry "Quarry"), and [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon"). Diamond Nodes are only found on Floors 50+ in the Mines.
* Any rock mined with a [pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes"), after obtaining the power from [The Diamond Hunter](/The_Diamond_Hunter "The Diamond Hunter") (0.66% chance).
* [Metal crates](/Volcano_Dungeon#Metal_Crates "Volcano Dungeon") in the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon").
* Red, Purple, Copper, or Iron [Slime](/Slimes "Slimes"), [Dwarvish Sentry](/Dwarvish_Sentry "Dwarvish Sentry"), [Duggy](/Duggy "Duggy"), [Iridium Golem](/Iridium_Golem "Iridium Golem"), or [Wilderness Golem](/Wilderness_Golem "Wilderness Golem") drop.
* Any [Monster](/Monsters "Monsters") after reaching the bottom of the Mines (0.05% chance).
* [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") (regular and golden).
* [Panning](/Pans "Pans") in any area.
* Any [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") after reaching the bottom of the Mines.
* One Diamond is one of the prizes from the [Trout Derby](/Trout_Derby "Trout Derby") festival.
* Five Diamonds can be obtained as the 20th prize from the [Prize Machine](/Mayor%27s_Manor#Prize_Machine "Mayor's Manor").
* An [Ice Pip](/Ice_Pip "Ice Pip") or [Stonefish](/Stonefish "Stonefish") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") may produce a Diamond when the population of the pond reaches 9.
* The [Statue Of Endless Fortune](/Statue_Of_Endless_Fortune "Statue Of Endless Fortune") produces seven Diamonds per year on the birthdays of [Evelyn](/Evelyn "Evelyn"), [Gus](/Gus "Gus"), [Jodi](/Jodi "Jodi"), [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus"), [Marnie](/Marnie "Marnie"), [Maru](/Maru "Maru"), and [Willy](/Willy "Willy"), as well as having a 25% chance to produce one daily on non-birthdays.

## Tailoring

Diamond is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create the dyeable ![Shirt224.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/7f/Shirt224.png/24px-Shirt224.png) [Pendant Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a white [dye](/Dyeing "Dyeing") when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

* The Diamond may be randomly requested during any season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="2250">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,250g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points. The Diamond will never be requested until the player has reached floor 40 of [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines").
* One or two Diamonds may be requested by several fish in [Fish Pond quests](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond").
* One Diamond may be requested by the Raccoon at the [Giant Stump](/Giant_Stump "Giant Stump") as part of his fifth request, or as part of any request starting with the sixth.

## Trading

* One Diamond can be traded to the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for 1 [Triple Shot Espresso](/Triple_Shot_Espresso "Triple Shot Espresso").
* Ten Diamonds can be traded to the [Volcano Dwarf](/Volcano_Dungeon#Shop "Volcano Dungeon") in the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon") for [The Diamond Hunter](/The_Diamond_Hunter "The Diamond Hunter").
