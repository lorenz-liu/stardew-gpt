# Television

[![TVprogram.png](/mediawiki/images/4/4d/TVprogram.png)](/File:TVprogram.png)

The **Television** is available in [the Player](/The_Player "The Player")'s [farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") when beginning the game. There is a variety of channels with content that changes daily.

Players on the [standard map](/Farm_Maps#Standard "Farm Maps") start with a [Budget TV](/Budget_TV "Budget TV") and players on non-standard maps start with a [Floor TV](/Floor_TV "Floor TV"). Additional televisions can be purchased at the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop"). After the first farmhouse upgrade, the larger [Plasma TV](/Plasma_TV "Plasma TV") becomes available. The [Tropical TV](/Tropical_TV "Tropical TV") can be purchased from the [Island Trader](/Island_Trader "Island Trader") for 30 [Taro Roots](/Taro_Root "Taro Root"). The [Retro TV](/Retro_TV "Retro TV") can be purchased from George's shop at the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="200">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 200 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg").

All televisions, with the exception of the Tropical TV and Retro TV, can also be acquired from the [Furniture Catalogue](/Furniture_Catalogue "Furniture Catalogue") for data-sort-value="0">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)0g.

There is no benefit to using certain types of TVs, as all TVs have access to all of the channels available in the game.

## Weather Report

[![Weather Report.png](/mediawiki/images/3/38/Weather_Report.png)](/File:Weather_Report.png)

:   *See also: [Weather](/Weather "Weather")*

The weather report predicts the [weather](/Weather "Weather") for the next day. After using a [Rain Totem](/Rain_Totem "Rain Totem"), the forecast is updated to reflect any change. Knowing about an upcoming rainy day is especially useful, since [crops](/Crops "Crops") will not need watering then. Thus, continuity of crop watering can be assured even if the watering can is turned in for an upgrade today.

Once [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") is unlocked, KOZU-5 also displays the island forecast immediately after the one for Stardew Valley itself.

## Fortune Teller

[![Fortune Teller.png](/mediawiki/images/8/8b/Fortune_Teller.png)](/File:Fortune_Teller.png)

:   *See also: [Luck](/Luck "Luck")*

The Fortune Teller, named Welwick, tells the player what their daily [luck](/Luck "Luck") is for the current day.

## Livin' Off The Land

[![Livin' Off The Land.png](/mediawiki/images/4/40/Livin%27_Off_The_Land.png)](/File:Livin%27_Off_The_Land.png)

:   *Main page: [Livin' Off The Land](/Livin%27_Off_The_Land "Livin' Off The Land")*

Livin' Off The Land is a channel that plays every Monday and Thursday, which gives the player a tip related to [farming](/Farming "Farming"), [fishing](/Fishing "Fishing"), [foraging](/Foraging "Foraging"), or town life. There are no new episodes after the end of Year 2; the show plays re-runs on a 2-year cycle thereafter.

## The Queen of Sauce

[![Queen of Sauce.png](/mediawiki/images/a/af/Queen_of_Sauce.png)](/File:Queen_of_Sauce.png)

:   *Main page: [The Queen of Sauce](/The_Queen_of_Sauce "The Queen of Sauce")*

The Queen of Sauce is a television channel which teaches the player [cooking](/Cooking "Cooking") recipes. Beginning on Spring 7, a new recipe airs each Sunday for the first 2 years of the game. On Wednesday, a random recipe that has previously aired on a Sunday airs as a "Re-run", prioritizing recipes the player does not yet know. Simply watching the channel will teach the player the recipe if it's not yet known. The rotation of Sunday recipes repeats on a 2-year schedule.

## F.I.B.S.

[![F.I.B.S..png](/mediawiki/images/5/5a/F.I.B.S..png)](/File:F.I.B.S..png)

:   *Main page: [Fishing Information Broadcasting Service](/Fishing_Information_Broadcasting_Service "Fishing Information Broadcasting Service")*

The Fishing Information Broadcasting Service is a channel unlocked after completing Pam's special order "[The Strong Stuff](/Quests#The_Strong_Stuff "Quests")". [Pam](/Pam "Pam") sends the player a letter in the mail the day after the "Potato Juice" cutscene, asking if the player has ever tuned in to channel 736. It provides information about the current season's [fishing](/Fishing "Fishing") opportunities (including species, time, weather requirement, and location).

## Green Rain

[![Green rain static closeup.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/90/Green_rain_static_closeup.png/168px-Green_rain_static_closeup.png)](/File:Green_rain_static_closeup.png)

All TV channels will show a green TV static during a [Green Rain](/Weather#Green_Rain "Weather") day. TVs placed on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") will still work normally.
