The **Banana** is a [fruit](/Fruits "Fruits") produced each day by a [Banana Tree](/Banana_Tree "Banana Tree") during its harvest season. A Banana Tree on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") in Stardew Valley produces fruit during [Summer](/Summer "Summer"). On [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") or in the [Greenhouse](/Greenhouse "Greenhouse"), it produces fruit year-round.

A [Blue Discus](/Blue_Discus "Blue Discus") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") has a 5-15% chance of producing a banana daily once the population of the pond reaches 4.

## Tailoring

Banana is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a dyeable ![Relaxed Fit Pants.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/1e/Relaxed_Fit_Pants.png/24px-Relaxed_Fit_Pants.png) [Relaxed Fit Pants](/Tailoring#Pants "Tailoring"). It is a yellow dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the yellow dye pot at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"), for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Golden Walnuts

Banana is used to activate the Banana Shrine on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island#Island_East "Ginger Island"), to the left of the staircase leading to [Leo](/Leo "Leo")'s house. When a banana is placed on the shrine the torches light and a gorilla appears and gives the player 3 [Golden Walnuts](/Golden_Walnut "Golden Walnut").
