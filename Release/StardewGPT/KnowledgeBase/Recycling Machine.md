The **Recycling Machine** is a [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting") item that is used to turn [trash](/Trash "Trash") (aside from the [Joja Cola](/Joja_Cola "Joja Cola") and [Rotten Plant](/Rotten_Plant "Rotten Plant")) into useful items. It takes ![Time Icon.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/70/Time_Icon.png/24px-Time_Icon.png) 60m (1h) to process items.[[1]](#cite_note-processtime-1) The recipe is earned at [Fishing level 4](/Skills#Fishing "Skills").

One Recycling Machine is the reward for completing the ![Bundle Blue.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/e4/Bundle_Blue.png/24px-Bundle_Blue.png) [Field Research Bundle](/Bundles#Field_Research_Bundle "Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board](/Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Bundles").
