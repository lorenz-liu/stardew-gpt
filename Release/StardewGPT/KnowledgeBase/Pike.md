The **Pike** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught in the river (in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") or [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest")) and the pond in [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") during [Summer](/Summer "Summer") and [Winter](/Winter "Winter"). The Pike can also be caught anywhere on the [Riverland Farm](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps") and in the large pond on the [Forest Farm](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps"). It can also be gifted by a [pet](/Animals#Pets "Animals") cat with max [friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals"). It can also randomly be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during Summer and Winter.

Pike can be caught regardless of season using [Magic Bait](/Magic_Bait "Magic Bait").

## Map

Pike can be found in the [town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") river, the [Riverland Farm](/Farm_Maps "Farm Maps"), and in the river and the pond in the [forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest").

## Tailoring

Pike can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Fishing Vest](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt157.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/0e/Shirt157.png/24px-Shirt157.png)](/File:Shirt157.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Pike can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 3 days. The initial pond capacity is 3 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing three quests. The primary output is yellow Pike [Roe](/Roe "Roe") (data-sort-value="80">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)80g), but [Bug Meat](/Bug_Meat "Bug Meat") is also possible starting at population 9.
