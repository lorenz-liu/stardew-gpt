**Rice Shoot**s are a type of seed. Mature plants yield [Unmilled Rice](/Unmilled_Rice "Unmilled Rice"). Unmilled Rice is harvested using a [Scythe](/Scythe "Scythe").

Rice Shoots can be purchased at [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") starting in year 2 and occasionally from the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart"). They can also be obtained in regular [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") and [Golden Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Golden_Treasure_Chests "Fishing") (both in Spring), [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") (in Spring), by using the [Seed Maker](/Seed_Maker "Seed Maker"), or as a drop from [Grubs](/Grub "Grub") (10% chance) in the [Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") or [Mutant Grubs](/Mutant_Grub "Mutant Grub") in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") (Prehistoric Floors) or [Mutant Bug Lair](/Mutant_Bug_Lair "Mutant Bug Lair"). In [Winter](/Winter "Winter") they can also drop from [Haunted Skulls](/Haunted_Skull "Haunted Skull") (21.43% chance); in [Spring](/Spring "Spring") from [Wilderness Golems](/Wilderness_Golem "Wilderness Golem") (6.25% chance).

Rice Shoots planted in most farm tiles take 8 days to mature with watering each day, as with other crops. However, Rice Shoots planted within 3 tiles of a [Farm Pond](/Farm_Pond "Farm Pond") or natural water source (excluding [Wells](/Well "Well") and [Fish Ponds](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond")) in any direction are considered to be irrigated, as in a rice paddy. Paddies need not be continuous with each other or adjacent to a pond in order to count as irrigated. Irrigated Rice Shoots take only 6 days to mature and do not require watering.

## Stages

When harvested, each Rice Shoot has a 50/50 chance of giving either one or two Unmilled Rice at farming level 0, or one or three Unmilled Rice at farming level 10 or higher, plus an additional 10% chance of one or more extra rice (1.61 rice on average, or 2.11 at farming level 10+).[[1]](#cite_note-extra_rice-1)

## References

1. [↑](#cite_ref-extra_rice_1-0) The game randomly selects with equal odds either the minimum harvest value of 1, or the maximum harvest value (which is also 1) + 1 + Farming Level / 10. This is the base number of Unmilled Rice to spawn. It can only be an integer and decimals are ignored, so only farming levels of 10 and above make a difference and add one. After that, the game generates a random number between 0 and 1, and if it is less than 0.1, then yet another Unmilled Rice will spawn. This repeats until the random number generated is greater than or equal to 0.1, at which point the chance for an extra Unmilled Rice ends. This, on average, will give 0.11 extra Unmilled Rice on top of the initial 50/50 chance for one or two extra Unmilled Rice to be produced. Keep in mind that [Luck](/Luck "Luck") can also double the total amount of extra Unmilled Rice spawned by the above formula.
