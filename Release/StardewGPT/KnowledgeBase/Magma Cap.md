The **Magma Cap** is a mushroom found via [foraging](/Foraging "Foraging") throughout the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon"). It can be confused at a distance with a [False Magma Cap](/False_Magma_Cap "False Magma Cap"). False Magma Caps have a 99% chance to drop a Magma Cap when slain. It can also be acquired from a [Stingray](/Stingray "Stingray") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") with a population of at least 4.

Magma Caps can be used to purchase [Pineapple Seeds](/Pineapple_Seeds "Pineapple Seeds") from the [Island Trader](/Island_Trader "Island Trader") (1 Pineapple Seed for 1 Magma Cap).

## Bundles

Magma Cap is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Magma Cap is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create a [![Magenta Shirt.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/b5/Magenta_Shirt.png/24px-Magenta_Shirt.png)](/File:Magenta_Shirt.png) [Magenta Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a purple dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

Magma Cap is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
