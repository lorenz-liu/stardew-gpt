The **Heater** is a [tool](/Tools "Tools") used to keep [animals](/Animals "Animals") warm and happy during the [winter](/Winter "Winter"). It can be purchased from [Marnie](/Marnie "Marnie") for data-sort-value="2000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)2,000g. A heater is the reward for completing the ![Bundle Yellow.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2b/Bundle_Yellow.png/24px-Bundle_Yellow.png) [Fodder Bundle](/Bundles#Fodder_Bundle "Bundles") on the [Bulletin Board](/Bundles#Bulletin_Board "Bundles").

A heater has an effect on animal **[Mood](/Animals#Mood "Animals")** (which is different than [Friendship](/Animals#Friendship "Animals")) during the Winter when an animal is inside a barn or coop past 6PM, and the animal has 150 mood or above. Only a single heater is required to have an effect, additional heaters provide no additional benefit.

If the animal is able to enter a building with a heater without being trapped outside, they will gain 10 mood every 10 in-game minutes past 6PM, up to the maximum value of 255, or until the Farmer goes to sleep.
