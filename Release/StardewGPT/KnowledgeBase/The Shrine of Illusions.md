# The Shrine of Illusions

[![](/mediawiki/images/7/7d/The_Shrine_of_Illusions.png)](/File:The_Shrine_of_Illusions.png)

The Shrine of Illusions

**The Shrine of Illusions** is an altar found in the basement of the [Wizard's Tower](/Wizard%27s_Tower "Wizard's Tower"). [The player](/The_Player "The Player") can offer data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g to the shrine to change their appearance, name, favorite thing, and gender.

The [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard")'s basement becomes accessible from a trapdoor in his house when the player has at least [four hearts](/Friendship "Friendship") with him. It can also be accessed through a red portal in the [Witch's Hut](/Witch%27s_Hut "Witch's Hut") as soon as the Witch's Hut is accessible.

## Trivia

* Changing the character's name to include item spawn codes [triggers an Easter egg](/Secrets#Shrine_of_Illusions "Secrets").
* Changing the favorite thing to be "ConcernedApe" or anything containing "Stardew" [gives special dialogue when collecting a stardrop](/Stardrop#Secret_Text "Stardrop").
