**The Player** refers to the playable character in *[Stardew Valley](/Stardew_Valley "Stardew Valley")*.

## Character customization

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/29/Character_creation_menu.png/300px-Character_creation_menu.png)](/File:Character_creation_menu.png)

The character creation screen.

The following options are available at the beginning of a new game:

* **Appearance** - This includes 24 skin tones, 74 hairstyles, 112 shirts, 4 pants, and 31 accessories. All of them can be used by either gender, with the option to randomize appearance. The player's eye color, hair color, and pants color can also be adjusted using the sliders on the right side. This has no effect on gameplay, [can be changed later](/Wizard%27s_Tower#Basement_Room "Wizard's Tower"), and can be augmented with additional [shirts and pants](/Tailoring "Tailoring"), as well as [hats](/Hats "Hats"), and [boots](/Footwear "Footwear").
* **Name / Farm Name** - These are limited to *most* of the [ASCII printable characters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ASCII#Printable_characters "wikipedia:ASCII"), which are spaces, uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and `` `~!@#$%^&*()-=+[{]}\|;:'",<.>/?_ ``. Although `"` is in the valid ASCII printable character range, the textbox does not accept this character. Name and Farm Name are sometimes used in dialogues during gameplay. The player's name [can be changed later](/Wizard%27s_Tower#Basement_Room "Wizard's Tower"), but not the farm name.
  + Both fields accept copied text (paste text using Ctrl + v).
  + The length of the name is determined by the total pixel width, and the allowed maximum is restricted to the width of the textbox.
  + The letter case used when the name is inputted will not change (*e.g.,* if the name is inputted in all lowercase; the first letter will not be corrected to uppercase).
* **Animal Preference** - This determines the type of [pet](/Animals#Cat_or_Dog "Animals") that arrives in Spring of year 1. There are 10 options; five dogs, five cats.
* **Favorite Thing** - This affects the text displayed after eating a [Stardrop](/Stardrop "Stardrop"). The character limits are the same as the Name / Farm Name text boxes.
* **Gender** - This has minor effects on gameplay and [can be changed later](/Wizard%27s_Tower#Basement_Room "Wizard's Tower").
  + Characters will use the appropriate pronoun in dialogue.
  + Restriction to the men's or women's [spa](/Spa "Spa") entrance. (Note: [Alex](/Alex "Alex") sometimes works out in the men's locker room.)
  + It is possible to [marry](/Marriage "Marriage") an eligible NPC regardless of gender. In a different-gender marriage, the woman can become pregnant for 14 days (with no effect on speed or movement) and give birth to a child. In a same-gender marriage, children can be adopted.
  + Female characters will receive letters from [Dad](/Mom_and_Dad "Mom and Dad"), while male characters will receive letters from Mom. There are some differences in the gifts they send.

## Special Keys

When entering "Name", "Farm Name", or "Favorite Thing" on the [Character Creation](/The_Player#Character_customization "The Player") menu, certain keys produce special sounds and/or symbols.

* Pressing the following keys will produce special sounds.
  + Pressing `<` produces a "ding" sound
  + Pressing `$` produces a "plink" sound
  + Pressing `*` produces a "stone hit" sound
  + Pressing `=` produces a "pop" sound
  + Pressing `+` produces a "squish" sound
* Pressing the following keys will produce special characters.

:   * Pressing `<` produces `♡`, a heart
    * Pressing `>` produces `▷`, a right triangular arrow
    * Pressing `@` produces `◁`, a left triangular arrow
    * Pressing `$` produces `⌾`, a double circle
    * Pressing `*` produces `💢`, the "anger" symbol common in Japanese media
    * Pressing `=` produces `☆`, a star
:   [![SpecialChars.png](/mediawiki/images/9/9e/SpecialChars.png)](/File:SpecialChars.png)

* Pressing controller buttons also produces special sounds and/or symbols.

:   [![SpecialCharsXbox.png](/mediawiki/images/6/65/SpecialCharsXbox.png)](/File:SpecialCharsXbox.png)

## Notes

* Entering certain terms as the Favorite Thing [triggers an Easter egg](/Secrets#Favorite_Thing "Secrets").
* There are no character traits in *Stardew Valley*.
* Choices in cutscenes and dialogues don't significantly impact the story. They may increase or decrease [friendship points](/Friendship "Friendship"), or change a future cutscene or dialogue slightly.
* It is possible to befriend most NPCs and there are no mutually exclusive friendships. (Some NPCs like [Gunther](/Gunther "Gunther") have no friendship status.)
* While NPCs have a breathing animation, the player does not. Instead, the player's eyes blink.

## Exploits

* [Naming the character with a special format](/Secrets#Item_Spawn_Cheat "Secrets") allows spawning items whenever an NPC mentions the character by name.
