# Days of the Week

:   *See also: [Day Cycle](/Day_Cycle "Day Cycle")*

This article lists events/conditions that change based on the **day of the week.**

## Monday

* [Marnie](/Marnie "Marnie") at [Marnie's Ranch](/Marnie%27s_Ranch "Marnie's Ranch") does not sell or buy anything on Mondays, although the building can still be entered.
  + If it is raining, Marnie does not leave the Ranch and is open during normal business hours.
* [Sandy](/Sandy "Sandy") at the [Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis") sells 10 [Coconuts](/Coconut "Coconut") for data-sort-value="400">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)400g each.
* [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") in [The Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers") sells a limited quantity (50) [Slimes](/Slime "Slime") for data-sort-value="10">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)10g each.
* The [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") sells 3 [Hay](/Hay "Hay") for 1 [Omni Geode](/Omni_Geode "Omni Geode").
* The [Island Trader](/Island_Trader "Island Trader") sells 1 [Small Cap](/Small_Cap "Small Cap") for 30 [Taro Roots](/Taro_Root "Taro Root").
* The Special Orders Board in [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") and in [Qi's Walnut Room](/Qi%27s_Walnut_Room "Qi's Walnut Room") refreshes each Monday, with two new quests to choose from.
* Lone [Weeds](/Weeds "Weeds") spawn on [Ginger Island West](/Ginger_Island#Island_West "Ginger Island").

## Tuesday

* [Robin](/Robin "Robin"), [Jodi](/Jodi "Jodi"), [Caroline](/Caroline "Caroline"), [Emily](/Emily "Emily") and [Marnie](/Marnie "Marnie") attend exercise class inside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store"). As a result, the [Carpenter's Shop](/Carpenter%27s_Shop "Carpenter's Shop") and [Marnie's Ranch](/Marnie%27s_Ranch "Marnie's Ranch") do not buy or sell on Tuesdays, although the buildings can still be entered.
  + If it is raining, exercise class is canceled and both shops are open for normal business hours.
* [Sandy](/Sandy "Sandy") at the [Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis") sells [Cactus Fruit](/Cactus_Fruit "Cactus Fruit") for data-sort-value="150">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150g.
* [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") in [The Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers") sells a single [Omni Geode](/Omni_Geode "Omni Geode") for data-sort-value="300">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)300g.
* The [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") sells 1 [Fiber](/Fiber "Fiber") for 5 [Stone](/Stone "Stone").
* The [Island Trader](/Island_Trader "Island Trader") sells 1 [Palm Wall Ornament](/Palm_Wall_Ornament "Palm Wall Ornament") for 1 [Pineapple](/Pineapple "Pineapple").

## Wednesday

* [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") is closed on Wednesdays, and the building cannot be entered. Once the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") is restored or [the Player](/The_Player "The Player") has obtained the [Key To The Town](/Key_To_The_Town "Key To The Town"), the store stays open all week.
* The [Television](/Television "Television") plays a re-run of an episode of [The Queen of Sauce](/The_Queen_of_Sauce "The Queen of Sauce") (except on Year 1 Spring 3).
* [Sandy](/Sandy "Sandy") at the [Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis") sells 3 [Omni Geodes](/Omni_Geode "Omni Geode") for data-sort-value="1000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)1,000g each.
* [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") in [The Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers") sells a random [Fish](/Fish "Fish") or [Magnet](/Magnet "Magnet") for data-sort-value="200">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)200g.
* The [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") sells 1 [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") for 3 [Aquamarines](/Aquamarine "Aquamarine").
* The [Island Trader](/Island_Trader "Island Trader") sells 1 [Bluebird Mask](/Bluebird_Mask "Bluebird Mask") for 30 [Taro Roots](/Taro_Root "Taro Root").

## Thursday

* [Sandy](/Sandy "Sandy") at the [Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis") sells [Deluxe Speed-Gro](/Deluxe_Speed-Gro "Deluxe Speed-Gro") for data-sort-value="80">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)80g.
* [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") in [The Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers") sells a limited quantity (10) of [Mixed Seeds](/Mixed_Seeds "Mixed Seeds") for data-sort-value="30">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)30g each.
* The [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") sells a single [Magic Rock Candy](/Magic_Rock_Candy "Magic Rock Candy") for 3 [Prismatic Shards](/Prismatic_Shard "Prismatic Shard").
* The [Island Trader](/Island_Trader "Island Trader") sells 1 ['Volcano' Photo](/%27Volcano%27_Photo "'Volcano' Photo") for 5 [Mangoes](/Mango "Mango").

## Friday

* The [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") is open in [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") from 6am to 8pm.
* If the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") has been restored, villagers can be found hanging out there.
  + This includes [Clint](/Clint "Clint") who spends his Fridays there. As a result, he is unavailable for purchasing supplies, upgrading [Tools](/Tools "Tools") or breaking open [geodes](/Minerals#Geodes "Minerals") at the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith "Blacksmith").
* A large number of villagers congregate at [the Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon") after 6pm.
* [Sandy](/Sandy "Sandy") at the [Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis") sells [Honey](/Honey "Honey") for data-sort-value="200">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)200g.
* [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") in [The Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers") sells a single [Iridium Sprinkler](/Iridium_Sprinkler "Iridium Sprinkler") for data-sort-value="10000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)10,000g.
* The [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") sells 1 [Cheese](/Cheese "Cheese") for 1 [Emerald](/Emerald "Emerald").
* The [Island Trader](/Island_Trader "Island Trader") sells 1 [Deluxe Cowboy Hat](/Deluxe_Cowboy_Hat "Deluxe Cowboy Hat") for 30 [Taro Roots](/Taro_Root "Taro Root").

## Saturday

* [Willy](/Willy "Willy") goes fishing all day unless it is raining, so the [Fish Shop](/Fish_Shop "Fish Shop") is closed, although the building can still be entered.
* [Sandy](/Sandy "Sandy") at the [Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis") sells [Quality Retaining Soil](/Quality_Retaining_Soil "Quality Retaining Soil") for data-sort-value="200">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)200g.
* [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") in [The Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers") sells five of a random cooked dish.
* The [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") sells [Wild Seeds](/Crafting#Seeds "Crafting") for [Wild Seeds](/Crafting#Seeds "Crafting") from a different [season](/Seasons "Seasons").
* The [Island Trader](/Island_Trader "Island Trader") sells 1 [Oceanic Rug](/Oceanic_Rug "Oceanic Rug") for 3 [Blue Discus](/Blue_Discus "Blue Discus").

## Sunday

* The [gifting](/Friendship#Gifts "Friendship") limit of 2 gifts per villager per week is reset at the start of the day.
* [Forageable](/Foraging "Foraging") items that had not been picked up disappear overnight between Saturday and Sunday, and new forageables spawn at triple the normal rate.
* The [Artifact Spot](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot") spawning and removal procedure is run three times (as though 3 days have passed), so artifact spots are more likely to be seen in new positions.
* The [Television](/Television "Television") plays an episode of [The Queen of Sauce](/The_Queen_of_Sauce "The Queen of Sauce"); during years one and two the episode is new and always teaches the player a [recipe](/Cooking#Recipes "Cooking").
* Several villagers attend morning [religious services](/Yoba "Yoba") in the chapel inside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store").
* The [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") is open in [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") from 6am to 8pm.
* [Sandy](/Sandy "Sandy") at the [Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis") sells [Ice Cream](/Ice_Cream "Ice Cream") for data-sort-value="240">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)240g.
* [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") in [The Sewers](/The_Sewers "The Sewers") sells ten [Bat Wings](/Bat_Wing "Bat Wing") for data-sort-value="30">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)30g each.
* The [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") sells 1 [Staircase](/Staircase "Staircase") for 1 [Jade](/Jade "Jade").
* The [Island Trader](/Island_Trader "Island Trader") sells 1 [Tropical Double Bed](/Tropical_Double_Bed "Tropical Double Bed") for 5 [Bananas](/Banana "Banana").
