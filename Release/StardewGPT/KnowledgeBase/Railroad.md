# Railroad

![Maplocation.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/5/5c/Maplocation.png/20px-Maplocation.png)

![Map.png](/mediawiki/images/4/43/Map.png)

The **Railroad** is an exterior region located north of [the Mountain](/The_Mountain "The Mountain"). It becomes accessible after an earthquake clears the blocked pass on the 3rd of [Summer](/Summer "Summer") in the first year. As you wake up a message reads "There was an earthquake during the night."

## Trains

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/ba/Railroad.png/300px-Railroad.png)](/File:Railroad.png)

Front of Train Platform

On random days between 9am and 6pm, a train can pass through the area. If the player is outdoors, a message will pop up saying "A train is passing through Stardew Valley" accompanied by a train whistle sound.

If the player triggers a Railroad cutscene (*e.g.,* the wizard's [Dark Talisman quest](/Quests#Dark_Talisman "Quests")) while a train is passing, the train will be invisible (but audible) except for smoke clouds from the locomotive.

A player who stands in front of the train as it approaches or attacks the train with certain [tools](/Tools "Tools") or [weapons](/Weapons "Weapons") will suffer minor damage and be knocked back. The player can get stuck between the passing train and the platform, suffering damage repeatedly, losing all Health, and passing out.

### Train Types

Each train typically consists of a locomotive and 8 to 24 cars, which can be open-topped resource cars, passenger cars, or sealed freight cars. Each time a train appears, there is a 10% chance for it to be a long train, in which case the number of cars is doubled, for a maximum length of 48 cars.[[1]](#cite_note-train_types-1)

There are five special types of train that can appear, determined at random when the train appears:

* A Joja Train, containing only dark gray freight cars emblazoned with the Joja logo. (9% chance)
* A Passenger Train, containing only passenger cars, that moves twice as fast as regular trains.(10% chance)
* A "Coal Train", containing approximately 70% resource cars, 30% freight cars, and no passenger cars. (8.1% chance)
* A Prison Train, containing only special blue cars with barred windows, that moves twice as fast as regular trains. (3.65% chance)
* A "Christmas Train", containing only red resource cars filled with presents. (13.85% chance during [Winter](/Winter "Winter"))

Otherwise, a generic train will appear, containing approximately 16% resource cars, 20% freight cars, and 64% passenger cars.[[1]](#cite_note-train_types-1)

### Resource Cars

When trains containing resource cars pass through the Railroad, they will sometimes spill their cargo onto the ground, dropping up to a maximum of between 4 and 9 items, dependent on the type of resource being transported.[[2]](#cite_note-resource_cars-2) Items will only drop if the farmer is present at the Railroad, and the train is in view.

* Coal cars drop![Coal.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a7/Coal.png/24px-Coal.png) [Coal](/Coal "Coal")
* Metal cars drop![Copper Ore.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/78/Copper_Ore.png/24px-Copper_Ore.png) [Copper Ore](/Copper_Ore "Copper Ore"),![Iron Ore.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/87/Iron_Ore.png/24px-Iron_Ore.png) [Iron Ore](/Iron_Ore "Iron Ore"), or![Gold Ore.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/f/f7/Gold_Ore.png/24px-Gold_Ore.png) [Gold Ore](/Gold_Ore "Gold Ore") depending on the color of the car.
* Wood cars drop![Wood.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/df/Wood.png/24px-Wood.png) [Wood](/Wood "Wood"). Approximately 5% of drops are replaced with![Hardwood.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/ed/Hardwood.png/24px-Hardwood.png) [Hardwood](/Hardwood "Hardwood")
* Rock cars drop![Geode.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/43/Geode.png/24px-Geode.png) [Geode](/Geode "Geode") prior to Spring 10 of Year 2, or![Magma Geode.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/89/Magma_Geode.png/24px-Magma_Geode.png) [Magma Geode](/Magma_Geode "Magma Geode") after. During Winter, they drop![Frozen Geode.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/bf/Frozen_Geode.png/24px-Frozen_Geode.png) [Frozen Geode](/Frozen_Geode "Frozen Geode") instead.
* Brick cars drop![Stone.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/d4/Stone.png/24px-Stone.png) [Stone](/Stone "Stone")
* Present cars rarely drop![Mystery Box.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/87/Mystery_Box.png/24px-Mystery_Box.png) [Mystery Box](/Mystery_Box "Mystery Box") (~98.5% of drops will fail.)

Whenever a resource car attempts to drop an item, it also has a 1% chance of dropping![Leprechaun Shoes.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/e0/Leprechaun_Shoes.png/24px-Leprechaun_Shoes.png) [Leprechaun Shoes](/Leprechaun_Shoes "Leprechaun Shoes")[[2]](#cite_note-resource_cars-2). On average, each resource car has a ~6.8% chance to drop one or more pairs of Leprechaun Shoes.

Resource cars can also carry Compartments, Grass, Hay, or Packages. These do not drop regular items, but can still drop Leprechaun Shoes each time they would have dropped an item if they were carrying a different resource.

## Areas

### Train Platform

The Train Platform is located north of the tracks. There is an empty box that is used for the ["The Mysterious Qi" Quest](/Quests#The_Mysterious_Qi "Quests"). Harvey's 10 [Heart Event](/Harvey#Heart_Events "Harvey") also takes place here.

### The Spa

In the southwest of the Railroad is the [Spa](/Spa "Spa"). Standing still when in the pool causes [energy](/Energy "Energy") and [health](/Health "Health") to be quickly restored.
Penny’s 10 [Heart Event](/Penny#Heart_Events "Penny") takes place here.

There is a small pond against the outside of the spa. An [Ornate Necklace](/Ornate_Necklace "Ornate Necklace") can be caught there after reading [Secret Note #25](/Secret_Notes#Secret_Note_.2325 "Secret Notes"). A painting called ['Vista'](/%27Vista%27 "'Vista'") can also be caught there. Neither of these items can be obtained during [Winter](/Winter "Winter") as the water will be frozen.

### Witch's Swamp

There's a passage to the [Witch's Swamp](/Witch%27s_Swamp "Witch's Swamp") through the cave northeast of the train platform. This cave will be unlocked by completing a series of quests given by the [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard"). A cutscene initiating the quests will start when the player first enters the Railroad area after completing either the [Community Center](/Community_Center "Community Center") bundles or [Joja Community Development Form](/Joja_Community_Development_Form "Joja Community Development Form"). After completing these quests players can use the cave to travel to the [Witch's Swamp](/Witch%27s_Swamp "Witch's Swamp") at anytime.

### The Summit

[The Summit](/The_Summit "The Summit") can be accessed by a staircase in the northwest area of the railroad. Initially, the stairs are blocked by a boulder that the player can not move. The player can access the summit once [Perfection](/Perfection "Perfection") is achieved.

## Foraging

* In [Summer](/Summer "Summer"): ![Sweet Pea.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/d9/Sweet_Pea.png/24px-Sweet_Pea.png) [Sweet Pea](/Sweet_Pea "Sweet Pea") (47%), ![Spice Berry.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/c6/Spice_Berry.png/24px-Spice_Berry.png) [Spice Berry](/Spice_Berry "Spice Berry") (27%), and ![Grape.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/c2/Grape.png/24px-Grape.png) [Grape](/Grape "Grape") (27%)
* In [Fall](/Fall "Fall"): ![Wild Plum.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3b/Wild_Plum.png/24px-Wild_Plum.png) [Wild Plum](/Wild_Plum "Wild Plum") (37%), ![Blackberry.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/25/Blackberry.png/24px-Blackberry.png) [Blackberry](/Blackberry "Blackberry") (37%), and ![Hazelnut.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/31/Hazelnut.png/24px-Hazelnut.png) [Hazelnut](/Hazelnut "Hazelnut") (25%)

There are only 31 locations where forageable items can spawn, and initially they are mostly covered by [weeds](/Weeds "Weeds"), sticks, and rocks. Once the debris is cleared, items can spawn at an average rate of 0.1 per night.[[4]](#cite_note-forage_spawn-4) The map shows the possible locations as red and magenta tiles; when the original trees are present, the magenta tiles have a 90% smaller chance of spawning items because they are identified as being behind the original trees.

## Notes

* Standing in front of a train will cause damage to the player. Standing inside the right side of the train tunnel will cause the player to get stuck, and eventually pass out if their health is drained. Opening the in-game player menu until the train passes can prevent damage in this case.
* Pausing the game does not prevent the train from rolling.
