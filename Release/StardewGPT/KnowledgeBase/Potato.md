The **Potato** is a [vegetable](/Vegetables "Vegetables") [crop](/Crops "Crops") that grows from [Potato Seeds](/Potato_Seeds "Potato Seeds") after 6 days.

## Stages

When harvested, each Potato plant gives 1 potato, with a random chance to produce extra potatoes, partially based on Daily [Luck](/Luck "Luck").[[1]](#cite_note-extraluck-1)

## Tailoring

A Potato can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [![Shirt159.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/99/Shirt159.png/24px-Shirt159.png)](/File:Shirt159.png) [Shirt And Belt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

* Twelve Potatos must be [kegged](/Keg "Keg") for the ["The Strong Stuff"](/Quests#The_Strong_Stuff "Quests") special order.
* The Potato may be randomly requested in [Spring](/Spring "Spring") at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="240">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)240g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.

## References

1. [↑](#cite_ref-extraluck_1-0) The game generates a random number between 0 and 1, and if it is less than 0.2, then another Potato will spawn. This repeats until the random number is greater than or equal to 0.2, at which point the chance for an extra Potato ends. This, on average, will give 0.25 extra Potatoes. Keep in mind that Daily Luck can also double the amount of extra Potatoes spawned by the above formula.
