# Spirit's Eve

[![Spirits Eve.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a9/Spirits_Eve.png/500px-Spirits_Eve.png)](/File:Spirits_Eve.png)

The **Spirit's Eve** [festival](/Festivals "Festivals") takes place on the 27th of [Fall](/Fall "Fall") every year. The player enters the festival by entering [Pelican Town](/Pelican_Town "Pelican Town") between 10:00 pm and 11:50 pm (note that at 11:50 pm it is too late to enter), and upon leaving the festival, the player is returned to [The Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") at 12:00 am.

The festival features a maze where the [Golden Pumpkin](/Golden_Pumpkin "Golden Pumpkin") or a [Prize Ticket](/Prize_Ticket "Prize Ticket") can be obtained. According to townsfolk rumours, the maze was supposedly created by the [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard"). [Pierre](/Pierre "Pierre") has a shopping booth where the player can purchase festive items including a [rarecrow](/Scarecrow#Rarecrows "Scarecrow"), a [Jack-O-Lantern](/Jack-O-Lantern "Jack-O-Lantern"), and the Jack-O-Lantern recipe.

## Haunted Maze

The haunted maze is supposedly set up by M. Rasmodius, the [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard"). The [villagers](/Villagers "Villagers") say he was so committed to creating a frightening maze that it took him all day to set it up.

A large majority of the townspeople are either frightened or confused by the maze (especially [Abigail](/Abigail "Abigail"), who can't continue because she cannot walk past spiders). [Jodi](/Jodi "Jodi") does not allow [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent") to go in the maze because it is "too scary for children". However, in the second year maze, [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent") goes in the maze with [Sam](/Sam "Sam") and [Abigail](/Abigail "Abigail") does not participate.

## Surviving the Haunted Maze

Like most mazes, the "keep going left" mentality is present here. Just keep on going left; it isn't a huge maze. It may be worth it to stop and talk to the characters, as some of the dialogue can be amusing.

The maze layout alternates between years: A fairly simple maze wherein players can earn a [Golden Pumpkin](/Golden_Pumpkin "Golden Pumpkin") appears in odd years, while a more complex and larger maze with the reward of a [Prize Ticket](/Prize_Ticket "Prize Ticket") appears in even years. Players can quickly exit the maze after reaching the treasure chest by using a "secret shortcut" mine cart, exiting to the entrance of the maze next to Pierre's booth.

**Odd Years**

The trick to reaching the end is to take the secret passage, which begins just underneath and to the left of the "?" signpost, and then continues to the right inside the cave to the North. It weaves to the left and right, which requires a little bit of blind navigation. Wearing a ring which provides light, such as the Glow Ring, will allow the player to track their movement more easily. They will exit down into the treasure section of the maze. Inside the treasure chest is a Golden Pumpkin.

**Even Years**

Again, a secret passage begins just underneath and to the left of the first "?" signpost. Following the path to the end, passing [Sebastian](/Sebastian "Sebastian"), an unmarked secret passage is located at the top right of the maze. This continues over to the section with [Sam](/Sam "Sam") and [Vincent](/Vincent "Vincent"). In the hedges behind the chicken-legged house there are three secret passages to the right of the sign. Only the middle one leads the player into the treasure section of the maze. Inside the treasure chest is a Prize Ticket.

## Trivia

* The top right of the maze contains a hut on chickens legs, a reference to the Slavic folklore character [Baba Yaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baba_Yaga). The path leading to the hut has three pairs of disembodied hands, a reference to the Russian fairy-tale [Vasilisa the Beautiful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasilisa_the_Beautiful), which features Baba Yaga.
* A transparent dialogue bubble can be seen above the [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard") or [Linus](/Linus "Linus") while the player is hovering their mouse over them. Clicking on either character won't produce a dialogue, but the dialogue can be found in the game's data files.
  + [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard"), Year 1: "The affairs of mundane folk matter little to me, but the elementals like a chance to see you up close. It was for them that I created this silly maze."
  + [Linus](/Linus "Linus"), Year 1: "Good show, old friend."
  + [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard"), Year 2: "The trials continue... Every magician must have an apprentice..."
  + [Linus](/Linus "Linus"), Year 2: "Haha! Another fine creation, my old friend."
* [Gus](/Gus "Gus") refers to [pumpkins](/Pumpkin "Pumpkin") as fruits. Despite not being classified as such in-game and in popular usage due to their taste, this is botanically accurate and pumpkins are classified as a [pepo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berry_(botany)#Modified_berries "wikipedia:Berry (botany)") or hard-rinded berry, due to the fact that pumpkins grow from flowers.
* In even years, [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") can be seen to the left of the monster cages, between the trees across from the bushes. His dialogue in data files is: "Wh-What are you doing here?"
