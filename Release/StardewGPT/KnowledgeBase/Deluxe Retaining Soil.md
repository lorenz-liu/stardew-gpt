**Deluxe Retaining Soil** is a [Fertilizer](/Fertilizer "Fertilizer") that keeps soil watered. It can be [crafted](/Crafting "Crafting") after purchasing the recipe from the [Island Trader](/Island_Trader "Island Trader") for 50 [Cinder Shards](/Cinder_Shard "Cinder Shard"). 5-7 Deluxe Retaining Soil may occasionally be found in [Supply Crates](/Supply_Crate "Supply Crate") on the [Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps#Beach "Farm Maps") provided the player has upgraded the [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") at least two times.

Deluxe Retaining Soil can be placed on tilled soil *before or after* planting a seed, or at any stage of crop growth. If the soil is not already watered, the fertilizer has no water to retain and plants will not grow until watered once. The chance for retaining water is 100%, which means after watering once the soil will be guaranteed to be watered up until the crop is ready to be harvested. This makes it especially useful in situations where [Sprinklers](/Crafting#Sprinklers "Crafting") are unavailable, such as in [Garden Pots](/Garden_Pot "Garden Pot") or in most areas on the [Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps#Beach "Farm Maps").

If a crop that yields multiple produce from one planting is fertilized with Deluxe Retaining Soil, water retention continues through all repeating harvests. In the [Greenhouse](/Greenhouse "Greenhouse"), on the [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") Farm, or in [Garden Pots](/Garden_Pot "Garden Pot") in buildings, where seasonal changes do not affect a planting's lifetime, the fertilizer remains until the tile is un-tilled.

## Bundles

Deluxe Retaining Soil is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Deluxe Retaining Soil can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable ![Shirt174.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/65/Shirt174.png/24px-Shirt174.png) [High-Waisted Shirt](/Tailoring#Shirts "Tailoring"). It is not used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

Deluxe Retaining Soil is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").
