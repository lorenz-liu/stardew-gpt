The **Octopus** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught in the ocean at [The Beach](/The_Beach "The Beach") or on the [Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps#Beach "Farm Maps") during [Summer](/Summer "Summer") or in the ocean on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") West during any season. It can also randomly be found in [Garbage Cans](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can") during Summer, or at the [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") for data-sort-value="150"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)450–1,000g. There is a 2.2% chance to hook an Octopus during the deep-sea submarine ride at the [Night Market](/Night_Market "Night Market") during [Winter](/Winter "Winter") 15-17 (1.6% if using a [Curiosity Lure](/Curiosity_Lure "Curiosity Lure")).

Octopus can be caught regardless of time of day or season using [Magic Bait](/Magic_Bait "Magic Bait").

The Octopus and the [Stonefish](/Stonefish "Stonefish") are the only [fish caught with a fishing rod](/Fish#Fishing_Pole_Fish "Fish") that cannot be eaten unless used in a [recipe](/Cooking#Recipes "Cooking").

Other than [Legendary Fish](/Fish#Legendary_Fish "Fish"), this is the most difficult fish to catch in the game.

## Strategy

This is an extremely difficult fish to catch, even compared to Legendary Fish. To make it more demanding, the chances of hooking an octopus and the window of time to catch it are very small.

* The difficulty of Octopus can be halved by having the [Blessing Of Waters](/Statue_Of_Blessings#Blessing_Of_Waters "Statue Of Blessings") Buff active, which decreases the difficulty of the Octopus by 50%, making its difficulty lower than the normal difficulty of the [Largemouth Bass](/Largemouth_Bass "Largemouth Bass"). However, the buff is only active for the first three fish hooked (even if they escape).
  + Using [Octopus Bait](/Targeted_Bait "Targeted Bait") will significantly increase the chances of hooking an Octopus on each cast, making it very likely at least one Octopus will be among the first three fish hooked.
* If the player does not have a [Statue Of Blessings](/Statue_Of_Blessings "Statue Of Blessings"), the most efficient way to try to catch an Octopus is to use an [Iridium Rod](/Iridium_Rod "Iridium Rod") or [Advanced Iridium Rod](/Advanced_Iridium_Rod "Advanced Iridium Rod"), eat a food that provides a fishing [Buff](/Buffs "Buffs"), and attach a [Trap Bobber](/Trap_Bobber "Trap Bobber") and/or [Cork Bobber](/Cork_Bobber "Cork Bobber") to the fishing pole. [Deluxe Bait](/Deluxe_Bait "Deluxe Bait") can be used to slightly increase the size of the fishing bar, making it less likely for the Octopus to escape.

## Tailoring

Octopus is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [![Shirt278b.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/e3/Shirt278b.png/24px-Shirt278b.png)](/File:Shirt278b.png) [Blue Buttoned Vest](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as an orange dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Octopus can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 4 days. The initial pond capacity is 3 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing three quests. The primary output is orange Octopus [Roe](/Roe "Roe"), but [Omni Geodes](/Omni_Geode "Omni Geode") are also possible starting at population 9.
