The **Magic Quiver** is a [trinket](/Trinkets "Trinkets") item obtainable after claiming [Combat Mastery](/Mastery_Cave#Masteries "Mastery Cave"). It can be obtained by slaying [monsters](/Monsters "Monsters") or breaking [crates and barrels](/The_Mines#Crates_and_Barrels "The Mines") in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), or [Quarry Mine](/Quarry_Mine "Quarry Mine"). Drop chances from both methods are affected by [daily luck](/Luck#Daily_Luck "Luck") and [luck buffs](/Luck#Luck_Buffs "Luck"), among other factors. The [Burglar's Ring](/Burglar%27s_Ring "Burglar's Ring") and [Monster Compendium](/Monster_Compendium "Monster Compendium") do not impact the chance to obtain it from monsters.[[1]](#cite_note-tryspawntrinket-1) There is also a ≈0.6% chance to obtain a Magic Quiver from a [treasure chest](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern") in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern").[[2]](#cite_note-treasureroom-2)

While the Magic Quiver is equipped in the trinket slot, the player occasionally shoots a magic arrow that damages the first [monster](/Monsters "Monsters") in its path. Any overlapping monsters will also be damaged. When enemies are within 7-8 tiles of the player, a magic arrow is automatically shot straight towards the nearest enemy. Magic arrows cause some knockback and pass through all environmental objects (like [mining nodes](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining") or [weeds](/Weeds "Weeds")).[[3]](#cite_note-magicarrow-3)

There are four types of Magic Quivers: regular, "Perfect", "Rapid", and "Heavy". The quiver's type will determine the possible values for the time between shots (*X*), minimum damage (*Y*), and maximum damage (*Z*).

## Types

The Magic Quiver is a random type when dropped, and it can be changed to any of four possible types if re-forged on an [Anvil](/Anvil "Anvil"). The generation rates listed below are the chances to obtain each particular type.[[4]](#cite_note-code-4)

Cooldown is determined to the tenth of a second for the regular and Heavy types, and it is determined to the hundredth of a second for the Rapid type. For all types, damage is determined to the whole number. All possible values of both stats are equally likely for their specific types (*e.g.*, a "**Rapid Magic Quiver**" has a 9.09% chance to have a 0.65 second cooldown and a 20% chance to have a damage range of 9-14).[[4]](#cite_note-code-4) These stats are also re-rolled when the trinket is re-forged on an [Anvil](/Anvil "Anvil"), so it is possible to receive the same type of quiver with different stats.

| Modifier | Cooldown | Min Damage | Max Damage | Generation Rate[[4]](#cite_note-code-4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (None) | 1.1 - 2.1 seconds | 13 - 28 | Min Damage + 5 | 86.4% |
| **Perfect** | 0.9 seconds | 30 | 35 | 4% |
| **Rapid** | 0.6 - 0.7 seconds | 8 - 12 | Min Damage + 5 | 4.8% |
| **Heavy** | 1.5 - 2.0 seconds | 23 - 38 | Min Damage + 5 | 4.8% |

## Notes

* The damage dealt by the Magic Quiver can benefit from [Combat](/Combat "Combat") skill damage bonuses. However, it does not receive damage bonuses from [Rings](/Rings "Rings") like the [Ruby Ring](/Ruby_Ring "Ruby Ring") or [Iridium Band](/Iridium_Band "Iridium Band").
* The arrows can trigger explosive effects from the [Napalm Ring](/Napalm_Ring "Napalm Ring").
* If an arrow is fired while the player holds a weapon with the Bug Killer, Crusader, or Vampiric [Enchantment](/Enchantments "Enchantments"), the arrow will receive that Enchantment's bonuses.
  + Similarly, if an arrow is fired at a [Rock Crab](/Rock_Crab "Rock Crab") while the player holds a [pickaxe](/Pickaxe "Pickaxe"), it will damage and then remove the crab's shell as the pickaxe would.
* The Magic Quiver will not shoot arrows at any [Slimes](/Slimes "Slimes") in a [Slime Hutch](/Slime_Hutch "Slime Hutch") or [Truffle Crabs](/Truffle_Crab "Truffle Crab").[[3]](#cite_note-magicarrow-3)
