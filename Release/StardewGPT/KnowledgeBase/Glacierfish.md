The **Glacierfish** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught in [Cindersap Forest](/Cindersap_Forest "Cindersap Forest") during [Winter](/Winter "Winter"). It is one of the five [Legendary Fish](/Fish#Legendary_Fish "Fish"), so it can only be caught once per [save file](/Saves "Saves"), or once per player in [Multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer").

The special requirements necessary to hook a Glacierfish are:[[1]](#cite_note-requirements-1)

* [Fishing level 6](/Fishing#Fishing_Skill "Fishing"), either naturally or using Fishing [Buffs](/Buffs "Buffs").
* The player must be standing on one specific tile: the one at the southernmost tip of Arrowhead Island. This exact location is revealed in *[Secrets of the Legendary Fish](/Lost_Books#Secrets_of_the_Legendary_Fish "Lost Books")*.
* The bobber must be located in water with a [distance from land](/Fishing#Distance_from_Land "Fishing") of at least 3, *i.e.,* it must be at least three tiles away from any land in every direction.
  + The Glacierfish can appear one tile closer to land if the bobber lands in [Bubbles](/Fishing#Bubbles "Fishing").

When all of these requirements are met, each cast has a 50% chance of hooking a Glacierfish. Unlike with other Legendary Fish, using a [Curiosity Lure](/Curiosity_Lure "Curiosity Lure") does not increase the chance of hooking it.

Although the Glacierfish cannot be hooked again after it is caught, it is possible to obtain multiple Glacierfish (per player) by using [Challenge Bait](/Challenge_Bait "Challenge Bait") to catch it or by [Panning](/Pans "Pans") in the correct [tiles](#Map) with the [Fisher enchantment](/Forge#Enchantments "Forge").

The Glacierfish can be caught regardless of season using [Magic Bait](/Magic_Bait "Magic Bait").

## Dyeing

Glacierfish can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a blue dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Glacierfish can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"). The pond capacity is 1 fish and this cannot be increased. The only possible output is Glacierfish [Roe](/Roe "Roe").
