The **Lava Eel** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught during all times and seasons in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") on the 100th floor and at the [Volcano Caldera](/Forge "Forge"). It is the only fish that can spawn in lava.

In the Mines, the chances of hooking a Lava Eel depends on fishing level and equipment of the farmer, as well as the [distance from land](/Fishing#Distance_from_Land "Fishing") of the fished tile. The base chance of hooking a Lava Eel is 1.8%, which is increased by 0.32% per Fishing Level and 0.08% per tile from land. This is increased by a further 4% if a [Curiosity Lure](/Curiosity_Lure "Curiosity Lure") is equipped, and by 8% if [Lava Eel Bait](/Targeted_Bait "Targeted Bait") is equipped.[[1]](#cite_note-mines_rates-1) For example, with a Fishing Level of 10 and 5 tiles from land, the chance of hooking a Lava Eel is 5.4%. This increases to 9.4% with a Curiosity Lure equipped, to 13.4% with Lava Eel Bait equipped, and to 17.4% with both a Curiosity Lure and Lava Eel Bait equipped.

At the Volcano Caldera, the percent chance of hooking a Lava Eel is equivalent to 10 times the [distance from land](/Fishing#Distance_from_Land "Fishing") of the spot fished, assuming the ['Physics 101'](/%27Physics_101%27 "'Physics 101'") painting was not caught. For example, there is a 50% chance to hook a Lava Eel if the bobber lands 5 tiles from land.[[2]](#cite_note-forgechance-2)

Of the non-[legendary fish](/Fish#Legendary_Fish "Fish"), it is the fish with the highest sell price and the second highest difficulty fish (after [Octopus](/Octopus "Octopus"), tied with the [Scorpion Carp](/Scorpion_Carp "Scorpion Carp")).

## Tailoring

Lava Eel is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Logo Cap.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/c/ca/Logo_Cap.png/24px-Logo_Cap.png) [Logo Cap](/Logo_Cap "Logo Cap"). It can also be used as a red dye color at the dye pots in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, located at [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Lava Eels can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 5 days. The initial pond capacity is 1 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing four quests. After the first quest is completed, the color of the pond water changes to red. The primary output is red Lava Eel [Roe](/Roe "Roe"), but [Gold Ore](/Gold_Ore "Gold Ore") is possible starting at population 3 and several more valuable items are possible starting at population 8.
