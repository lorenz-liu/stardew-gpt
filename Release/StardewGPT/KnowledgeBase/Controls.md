### Tips

* Shift + left-click an inventory item/stack to move it to the top row.
* Shift + left-click to continuously purchase items inside a shop/merchant's inventory window.
* Shift + left-click to craft 5 items at a time inside the crafting menu.
  + Hold Ctrl + Shift to craft stacks of 25 items at a time.
* Shift + right-click to purchase 5 items. Hold Shift + right-click to keep purchasing items 5 at a time.
  + Hold Ctrl + Shift to purchase stacks of 25 items at a time.
  + Hold Ctrl + Shift + 1 to purchase stacks of 999 items at a time.
* Right-click a stack of inventory/[chest](/Chest "Chest") items to single out one of them.
* Shift + right-click to select half a stack of inventory/[chest](/Chest "Chest") items.
* Hold right-click or left-click while moving the mouse over a group of equipment (*e.g.,* [Kegs](/Keg "Keg"), [Preserves Jars](/Preserves_Jar "Preserves Jar"), [Casks](/Cask "Cask"), [Bee Houses](/Bee_House "Bee House")) to collect items quickly.
  + Have another item selected in inventory to refill equipment automatically.
  + If the item is edible, you must use left-click, or the game will ask if you want to consume the item.
* Delete to permanently delete a selected item in the inventory window. (Use E/Escape to open inventory, then Left-click on an item/stack to select it, then press the delete key.)
* Pressing the Esc key while on the opening splash screen takes you directly to the main menu select screen.
* When viewing a dialogue box with a "yes/no" answer, pressing E/Escape or N indicates "no", and pressing the Y key indicates "yes".
* Shift + left-click on an item in the [Catalogue](/Catalogue "Catalogue") or [Furniture Catalogue](/Furniture_Catalogue "Furniture Catalogue") to add it directly to inventory, instead of attaching it to the mouse cursor. This also works when removing items from the Grange Display at the [Stardew Valley Fair](/Stardew_Valley_Fair "Stardew Valley Fair").
* Rebind the chat box key to avoid opening it accidentally, or temporarily enter screenshot mode with F4 to place objects at the bottom left of the screen.

### Advanced Controls

* **With a shop menu open**:
  + Hold ![PS4 Square.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/ec/PS4_Square.png/24px-PS4_Square.png) and ![PS4 Cross.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/6b/PS4_Cross.png/24px-PS4_Cross.png) to purchase items in bulk at a much faster rate and drastically speed up the buying process
* **With the inventory menu open**:
  + Press the ![PS4 Touch Pad.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/e0/PS4_Touch_Pad.png/24px-PS4_Touch_Pad.png) Touchpad button to automatically sort the items.
  + Press ![PS4 Circle.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/18/PS4_Circle.png/24px-PS4_Circle.png) after selecting an item, and the cursor will automatically hover over the trash icon.
* **When interacting with a** [chest](/Chest "Chest"):
  + Press ![PS4 R1.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/83/PS4_R1.png/24px-PS4_R1.png) to quickly jump to the *Add to Existing Stacks* icon. (This will automatically pull any items from your inventory which already have a stack in the chest’s storage.)
* **Placing / rotating [furniture](/Furniture "Furniture")**:
  + Press ![PS4 Square.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/e/ec/PS4_Square.png/24px-PS4_Square.png) while facing towards and in contact with the item to select it.
  + Press ![PS4 Cross.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/6b/PS4_Cross.png/24px-PS4_Cross.png) to rotate a selected item.

    :   Note, *not all items have this capability*. For example, the [default house](/Farmhouse#Interior "Farmhouse") has a [bed](/Furniture#Beds "Furniture") which cannot be rotated, a [rug](/Furniture#Rugs "Furniture") which can rotate by 180°, and a [chair](/Furniture#Chairs "Furniture") which can rotate by 90 °.

### Object Placement Tips

* One of the most useful controls is the ![Switch Right Stick.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/46/Switch_Right_Stick.png/24px-Switch_Right_Stick.png) **Right Stick**. It can be used to exactly place furniture, rugs, windows, etc. where you want them. It's pretty sensitive but absolutely required especially for placing windows.
* While moving a rug into place using the ![Switch Right Stick.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/46/Switch_Right_Stick.png/24px-Switch_Right_Stick.png) **Right Stick**, use ![Switch A.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/07/Switch_A.png/24px-Switch_A.png) to *Rotate* the rug.

### Toolbar Tips

* To "power-up" tools such as the [Watering Can](/Watering_Cans "Watering Cans") (*i.e.,* act on multiple tiles at once), press and hold ![Switch Y.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3b/Switch_Y.png/24px-Switch_Y.png).
* Interacting with villagers with any item other than tools will gift that item to the villager.

### Inventory Tips

* When moving inventory, the ![Switch Y.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3b/Switch_Y.png/24px-Switch_Y.png) button will move one item at a time or hold to move multiple.
* Pressing ![Switch B.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/bc/Switch_B.png/24px-Switch_B.png) after selecting an item(s) from your inventory will move it to the trash icon.
* Pressing ![Switch Minus.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/bd/Switch_Minus.png/24px-Switch_Minus.png) while in your inventory will quickly organize items.
* Pressing both ![Switch A.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/07/Switch_A.png/24px-Switch_A.png) and ![Switch Y.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3b/Switch_Y.png/24px-Switch_Y.png) while purchasing items from any shop will allow the player to purchase items twice as fast.

### Weapons

* Pressing ![Switch Y.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/3b/Switch_Y.png/24px-Switch_Y.png) performs basic attack while pressing ![Switch A.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/07/Switch_A.png/24px-Switch_A.png) performs block or alternate attack.

## History

* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Removed "new" input from button press until the button is released. This makes harvesting large numbers of kegs/preserves jars/crystalariums, etc. easier by holding the button without releasing it. Added option to shift toolbar (active inventory bar) to PC. Added emotes menu.
* [1.5.5](/Version_History#1.5.5 "Version History"): Holding Control + Shift now lets you purchase or craft stacks of 25 items at a time.
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Holding Control + Shift + 1 now lets you purchase stacks of 999 items at a time.
