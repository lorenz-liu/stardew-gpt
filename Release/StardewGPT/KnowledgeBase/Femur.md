The **Femur** is a [club weapon](/Weapons#Club "Weapons") that can be purchased from the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild") for data-sort-value="350">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)350g after reaching floor 10 in [The Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"). It is a possible reward for the chest on floor 10 of the Mines if "remixed" mine rewards are selected in the [Advanced Options](/Options "Options") menu when starting a new game.

## History

* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): Previously unobtainable weapon introduced.[[1]](#cite_note-unimplemented-1) Weapon level increased from 1 to 2, sell price from 50 to 100.
* [1.6.9](/Version_History#1.6.9 "Version History"): Added to [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild") shop inventory.
