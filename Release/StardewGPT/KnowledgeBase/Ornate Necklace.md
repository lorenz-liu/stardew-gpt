The **Ornate Necklace** is a special item that is unlocked after reading [Secret Note #25](/Secret_Notes#Secret_Note_.2325 "Secret Notes"). To find it, fish in the water outside the [Spa](/Spa "Spa") during any season except [Winter](/Winter "Winter").

To give the Ornate Necklace, place it in the inventory. It does not need to be held as the game automatically tries to give it to any NPC that is spoken to thereafter. However, it can be received only by [Caroline](/Caroline "Caroline") or [Abigail](/Abigail "Abigail"). Trying to give it to anyone else fails, and results in a dialogue saying "Wrong Person". Make sure to speak first to the intended recipient, since transfer is automatic.

Giving the Ornate Necklace to [Caroline](/Caroline "Caroline") awards 50 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points with her. Giving the Ornate Necklace to [Abigail](/Abigail "Abigail") awards 100 Friendship points with her.
