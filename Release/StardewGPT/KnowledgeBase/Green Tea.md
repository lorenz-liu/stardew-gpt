**Green Tea** is a drinkable [Artisan Good](/Artisan_Goods "Artisan Goods") made with the [Keg](/Keg "Keg") using [Tea Leaves](/Tea_Leaves "Tea Leaves"), taking 3 hours.

When consumed, it grants the player +30 [Max Energy](/Energy "Energy") and +0.5 [Speed](/Speed "Speed") for 4m 12s. As a drink, its [buff](/Buffs "Buffs") stacks with any food buffs (including Max Energy), but replaces any other drink buffs (*i.e.,* from [Coffee](/Coffee "Coffee"), [Ginger Ale](/Ginger_Ale "Ginger Ale") or [Triple Shot Espresso](/Triple_Shot_Espresso "Triple Shot Espresso")).

It is sold by [Caroline](/Caroline "Caroline") during the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival") for data-sort-value="10">![Calico Egg.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/d/da/Calico_Egg.png/24px-Calico_Egg.png) 10 [Calico Eggs](/Calico_Egg "Calico Egg").

## Bundles

Green Tea is one of the options for the ![Bundle Orange.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/9/95/Bundle_Orange.png/24px-Bundle_Orange.png) [Brewer's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Brewer.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Pantry (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Pantry "Remixed Bundles").

## Tailoring

Green Tea is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [![Shirt287.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/44/Shirt287.png/24px-Shirt287.png)](/File:Shirt287.png) [Tea Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a green dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Quests

One Green Tea may be requested by [Blobfish](/Blobfish "Blobfish") or [Octopus](/Octopus "Octopus") in [Fish Pond quests](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond").
