**Amethyst** is a [Mineral](/Minerals "Minerals") that can be obtained from [Amethyst Nodes](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining") and [Gem Nodes](/Mining#Mining_Nodes "Mining") in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines"), from the purple [Gem Bird](/Ginger_Island#Gem_Birds "Ginger Island") on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") during [rainy days](/Weather#Weather_Types "Weather"), from [panning](/Pans "Pans"), or as a rare drop from [Green Slimes](/Slimes "Slimes"). It has a 10% chance to be dropped by a slain [Dwarvish Sentry](/Dwarvish_Sentry "Dwarvish Sentry") in the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon"). It may also occasionally be found in [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing") or in a [Garbage Can](/Garbage_Can "Garbage Can"). A [Super Cucumber](/Super_Cucumber "Super Cucumber") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") may produce 1-3 Amethyst when the population of the pond reaches 9. The [Statue Of Endless Fortune](/Statue_Of_Endless_Fortune "Statue Of Endless Fortune") produces one Amethyst per year, on [Abigail](/Abigail "Abigail")'s birthday, [Fall 13](/Fall "Fall").

## Bundles

Amethyst is one of the options for the ![Bundle Yellow.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2b/Bundle_Yellow.png/24px-Bundle_Yellow.png) [Treasure Hunter's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Treasure_Hunter.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Boiler Room (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Boiler_Room "Remixed Bundles").

## Forging

Amethyst can be used at the [Forge](/Forge "Forge") to increase the knockback of a melee weapon.

## Recipes

Amethyst is not used in any [recipes](/Cooking "Cooking").

## Tailoring

Amethyst is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") with [Cloth](/Cloth "Cloth") in the feed to create a ![Shirt089.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/b/bf/Shirt089.png/24px-Shirt089.png) [Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It is a purple dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed. It can be placed in the purple dye pot at Emily's and Haley's house for use in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing").

## Quests

* In the ["Clint's Attempt" Quest](/Quests#Clint.27s_Attempt "Quests"), [Clint](/Clint "Clint") sends you a letter on the 6th of [Winter](/Winter "Winter") requesting that you give [Emily](/Emily "Emily") an amethyst and tell her it's from him. The reward is 1 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") heart with Emily.
* Amethyst may be randomly requested during any season at the ["Help Wanted" board](/Quests#Help_Wanted_Quests "Quests") outside [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for a reward of data-sort-value="300">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)300g and 150 [Friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") points.
* 1-3 Amethyst may be requested by [Catfish](/Catfish "Catfish"), [Spook Fish](/Spook_Fish "Spook Fish"), or [Sunfish](/Sunfish "Sunfish") in [Fish Pond quests](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond").
* Amethyst will be requested by [Emily](/Emily "Emily") as part of the "Rock Rejuvenation" quest at the [Special Orders](/Quests#List_of_Special_Orders "Quests") board outside Mayor [Lewis](/Lewis "Lewis")' house.
* May be used in the [Gem Birds](/Ginger_Island#Gem_Birds "Ginger Island") puzzle on Ginger Island.
