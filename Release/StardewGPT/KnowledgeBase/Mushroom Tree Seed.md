The **Mushroom Tree Seed** is a seed that grows into a [Mushroom Tree](/Mushroom_Tree "Mushroom Tree"). It's purchasable from [Mr. Qi](/Mr._Qi "Mr. Qi")'s shop in [Qi's Walnut Room](/Qi%27s_Walnut_Room "Qi's Walnut Room"), for data-sort-value="5">![Qi Gem.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/78/Qi_Gem.png/24px-Qi_Gem.png) 5 [Qi Gems](/Qi_Gem "Qi Gem"). They can also be obtained from the natural seed spread near a Mushroom Tree. They *cannot* be obtained by shaking Mushroom Trees.

## Tailoring

Mushroom Tree Seed can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Star Helmet.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2a/Star_Helmet.png/24px-Star_Helmet.png) [Star Helmet](/Star_Helmet "Star Helmet"). It can also serve as a red dye color in the sewing machine or dye pots, located at [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").
