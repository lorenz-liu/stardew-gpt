# Defense

**Defense** [![Defense.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/6/66/Defense.png/24px-Defense.png)](/File:Defense.png) is a statistic that affects how much damage the player takes. Defense can be increased by food and equipment, or decreased by [debuffs](/Buffs "Buffs").

Each point of total Defense reduces [damage taken](/Combat/Mechanics#Player_defense "Combat/Mechanics") by 1 point, to a minimum of 1 damage per hit. For example, a [Green Slime](/Slimes "Slimes") normally deals between 4 and 6 damage. If [Sneakers](/Sneakers "Sneakers") are equipped, providing 1 Defense, it deals between 3 and 5 damage instead. On the other hand, if [Space Boots](/Space_Boots "Space Boots") are equipped, providing 4 Defense, a Green Slime deals 1 damage 66% of the time and 2 damage 33% of the time. Negative Defense increases damage taken. For example, the [Shadow Shaman](/Shadow_Shaman "Shadow Shaman") Jinxed effect causes damage from each hit to be increased by 8.
