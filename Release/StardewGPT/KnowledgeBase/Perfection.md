# Perfection

The **Perfection** score measures how much of the game content a player has completed. Achieving a score of 100% unlocks extra endgame content.

## Perfection system

:   *Main article: [Qi's Walnut Room#Perfection Tracker](/Qi%27s_Walnut_Room#Perfection_Tracker "Qi's Walnut Room")*

The "Perfection Tracker" statue in [Qi's Walnut Room](/Qi%27s_Walnut_Room "Qi's Walnut Room") on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") shows the current perfection value. This value cannot be seen in-game before unlocking the room. The value is the average score across these categories:

## Multiplayer

In [Multiplayer](/Multiplayer "Multiplayer") games, the Perfection Tracker will show a single perfection score shared across all players. The percentage breakdown for each category (with the exception of "Golden Walnuts Found", since [Golden Walnuts](/Golden_Walnut "Golden Walnut") are shared among all players) will show the highest score any of the players in the game have completed. For example, this means that only one person needs to catch every fish for the score to show 100% for that category. Similarly, only one Gold Clock needs to be built to get a value of "Yes" for "Golden Clock on Farm." However, the scores cannot be split amongst players. For example, if one player completes half of the [Monster Eradication Goals](/Adventurer%27s_Guild#Monster_Eradication_Goals "Adventurer's Guild") and another player completes the other half, the value for "Monster Slayer Hero" will be "No" as no player in the game has actually completed all of them.

The [Wedding Ring](/Wedding_Ring "Wedding Ring") does *not* need to be crafted to achieve Perfection.

## Unlocked content

Reaching a perfection score of 100% unlocks additional endgame content the next day. That includes:

* Text appears the morning after Perfection is achieved, saying *"You feel it in your heart: somewhere, somehow... Grandpa is beaming with pride. The legacy of (Farm's name) Farm is eternal."*
* A message appears the morning after Perfection is achieved, saying *"A rumbling sound was heard in the distance..."*. This unlocks access to [the Summit](/The_Summit "The Summit"). The player can view a [special cutscene](/The_Summit#Cutscene "The Summit") the first time they visit.
* A flock of [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") parrots flies over [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm").
* The [Statue Of True Perfection](/Statue_Of_True_Perfection "Statue Of True Perfection") is unlocked, obtained by interacting with the [Perfection Tracker](/Qi%27s_Walnut_Room#Perfection_Tracker "Qi's Walnut Room") statue in [Qi's Walnut Room](/Qi%27s_Walnut_Room "Qi's Walnut Room").
* [Golden Eggs](/Golden_Egg "Golden Egg") and [Golden Chickens](/Golden_Chicken "Golden Chicken") are unlocked, with the first egg available either via [Marnie's Ranch](/Marnie%27s_Ranch "Marnie's Ranch"), [Qi's Walnut Room](/Qi%27s_Walnut_Room "Qi's Walnut Room"), a random [Golden Witch](/Random_Events#The_Witch "Random Events") event, or from a [Fishing Treasure Chest](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing").
* The [??? hat](/%3F%3F%3F_(hat) "??? (hat)") can be found by interacting with a monkey in the [Volcano Caldera](/Forge "Forge").
* A stone carving of a [Stardrop](/Stardrop "Stardrop") appears above [Grandpa's Shrine](/The_Farm#Grandpa.27s_Shrine "The Farm").

## Perfection Waivers

After having interacted with the "Perfection Tracker" statue in [Qi's Walnut Room](/Qi%27s_Walnut_Room "Qi's Walnut Room") on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") for the first time, [Fizz](/Fizz "Fizz"), a Joja employee working in the "Joja Special Services Division", will send the player a letter inviting them to meet him in the "watery cave" on Ginger Island.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| “ | “Hello.  I have the 'perfect' solution to help you out.  Meet me in the watery cave on Ginger Island. -Fizz  Joja Special Services Division” |

The "watery cave" is the mushroom cave on Ginger Island where [Professor Snail](/Professor_Snail "Professor Snail") is initially found.

Fizz will offer to sell the player perfection waivers in order to bypass perfection challenges. For data-sort-value="500000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500,000g players can increase their perfection score by 1%.

Players can receive this letter even if they have already achieved perfection.

## Notes

* If a player reached perfection in a previous version of the game, the "Perfection Tracker" statue in [Qi's Walnut Room](/Qi%27s_Walnut_Room "Qi's Walnut Room") will show that the player has not reached perfection if they haven't cooked all the new foods, caught the new fish, crafted all the new recipes, and shipped all of the new items. However, [The Summit](/The_Summit "The Summit") remains accessible if it was unlocked before.
* It is cheaper to buy perfection waivers than to build the obelisks and golden clock. Those buildings cost a total of data-sort-value="13000000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)13,000,000g to build and provide 14% of perfection. 14 perfection waivers only cost data-sort-value="7000000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)7,000,000g, saving a total of data-sort-value="6000000">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)6,000,000g.
