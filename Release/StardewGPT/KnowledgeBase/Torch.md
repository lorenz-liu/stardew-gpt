The **Torch** is a [craftable lighting](/Crafting#Lighting "Crafting") item that can be held to provide light at night, or in dark areas of [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") for a radius of approximately 3 tiles. They can also be gathered by using a [hoe](/Hoes "Hoes"), a [pickaxe](/Pickaxes "Pickaxes"), or an [axe](/Axes "Axes"). Torches can also be placed indoors or outdoors to provide lighting.

When placing a [Soggy Newspaper](/Soggy_Newspaper "Soggy Newspaper") into the [Recycling Machine](/Recycling_Machine "Recycling Machine"), it can sometimes produce 3 torches.

Torches do not provide as much light as other sources, but they can be walked on by the player. Torches can also be placed on top of [Fences](/Crafting#Fences "Crafting") and [Sprinklers](/Crafting#Sprinklers "Crafting").

## Tailoring

Torch is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [High Heat Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt225.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/88/Shirt225.png/24px-Shirt225.png)](/File:Shirt225.png) It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a yellow dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").
