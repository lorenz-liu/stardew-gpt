# Hats

[![](/mediawiki/images/thumb/8/80/Hat_Shop.png/200px-Hat_Shop.png)](/File:Hat_Shop.png)

The hat shop in [fall](/Fall "Fall").

**Hats** are cosmetic [clothing](/Clothing "Clothing") items that alter the appearance of [the player](/The_Player "The Player") but otherwise have no effect.

Hats cannot be sold through the [Shipping Bin](/Shipping "Shipping"), to the [Hat Mouse](/Abandoned_House "Abandoned House"), or to any store/shop in the game.

## Obtaining hats

The day after earning their first [Achievement](/Achievements "Achievements"), players will receive a letter from the mouse in the [Abandoned House](/Abandoned_House "Abandoned House") alerting them that hats can now be purchased there. Most [Achievements](/Achievements "Achievements") unlock a new hat for sale.

A few hats can only be obtained at certain [festivals](/Festivals "Festivals"), the [Casino](/Casino "Casino"), the [Adventurer's Guild](/Adventurer%27s_Guild "Adventurer's Guild"), or in [treasure rooms](/Skull_Cavern#Treasure_Rooms "Skull Cavern") in the Skull Cavern.

Many hats can also be [tailored](/Tailoring "Tailoring") at the sewing machine in [Emily's house](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane") or the [Sewing Machine](/Sewing_Machine "Sewing Machine") received as a reward for the ["Rock Rejuvenation"](/Quests#Rock_Rejuvenation "Quests") special order.

There is a small chance to receive certain hats from slaying [monsters](/Monsters "Monsters"), breaking [crates and barrels](/The_Mines#Crates_and_Barrels "The Mines"), chopping [trees](/Trees "Trees"), shaking trees with seeds, digging [Artifact Spots](/Artifact_Spot "Artifact Spot"), or [panning](/Pans "Pans").[[1]](#cite_note-rarecosmeticdrop-1) These hats are indicated below in the "How to Obtain" column with "Random drop during various player actions".

## History

* [1.3.27](/Version_History#1.3.27 "Version History"): Added 3 new hats: [Blue Cowboy Hat](/Blue_Cowboy_Hat "Blue Cowboy Hat"), [Red Cowboy Hat](/Red_Cowboy_Hat "Red Cowboy Hat"), and [Cone Hat](/Cone_Hat "Cone Hat"). Hats can now be placed on [horses](/Animals#Horse "Animals").
* [1.4](/Version_History#1.4 "Version History"): Added 35 new hats. Hats can now be placed on [children](/Children "Children"), and removed from horses and children.
* [1.5](/Version_History#1.5 "Version History"): Added 19 new hats. Hats can now be placed on [sea urchins](/Sea_Urchin "Sea Urchin") in [fish tanks](/Fish_Tank "Fish Tank").
* [1.6](/Version_History#1.6 "Version History"): Added 28 new hats. Hats can now be placed on [cats and dogs](/Animals "Animals"). Certain hats can now be found via various player actions, similar to the [Living Hat](/Living_Hat "Living Hat"). Increased the price of many hats sold by the mouse in the [Abandoned House](/Abandoned_House "Abandoned House").
