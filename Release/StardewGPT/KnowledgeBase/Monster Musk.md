**Monster Musk** is a [crafted item](/Crafting "Crafting") used to encounter more [monsters](/Monsters "Monsters"). The recipe is earned after completing the [Wizard](/Wizard "Wizard")'s Special Order ["Prismatic Jelly"](/Quests#Prismatic_Jelly "Quests"). It can also be obtained by trading a [Combat Quarterly](/Combat_Quarterly "Combat Quarterly") with the [Bookseller](/Bookseller "Bookseller").

Monster Musk has a duration of 10 minutes. If any player has the [Monster Musk buff](/Buffs#Monster_Musk "Buffs"), the number of enemies on a given floor of [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") or [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") when entering the floor is doubled.[[1]](#cite_note-spawn-1) It doesn't have an effect in areas resetting at the end of the day like the [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon"), the [Quarry Mine](/Quarry_Mine "Quarry Mine") or the [Mutant Bug Lair](/Mutant_Bug_Lair "Mutant Bug Lair"). However, it will still affect the number of off map spawns.

It is a special-effect buff, meaning that using Monster Musk will not remove any buffs from consumed drinks or food, or vice-versa.

## Bundles

Monster Musk is not used in any [bundles](/Bundles "Bundles").

## Tailoring

Monster Musk can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable ![Shirt263b.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/4/41/Shirt263b.png/24px-Shirt263b.png) [Dark Highlight Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a purple dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as a purple dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.
