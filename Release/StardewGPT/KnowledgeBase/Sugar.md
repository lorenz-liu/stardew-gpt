**Sugar** is a [cooking](/Cooking "Cooking") ingredient that can be used in a variety of recipes. It can be purchased year round from [Pierre's General Store](/Pierre%27s_General_Store "Pierre's General Store") for data-sort-value="100">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)100g or from [JojaMart](/JojaMart "JojaMart") for data-sort-value="125">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)125g. 4-7 Sugar may occasionally be found in [Supply Crates](/Supply_Crate "Supply Crate") on the [Beach Farm](/Farm_Maps#Beach "Farm Maps") provided the player has upgraded the [Farmhouse](/Farmhouse "Farmhouse") at least once.

Players have the ability to craft Sugar from [Beets](/Beet "Beet") by using a [Mill](/Mill "Mill"). Once placed in the mill, one [Beet](/Beet "Beet") will make three Sugar. [Beets](/Beet "Beet") placed inside will appear as Sugar in the box beside the [Mill](/Mill "Mill") the next morning.

## Tailoring

Sugar is used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the [Sugar Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). [![Shirt254.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/7/75/Shirt254.png/24px-Shirt254.png)](/File:Shirt254.png)

## Quests

Sugar is not used in any [quests](/Quests "Quests").

## Tips

* Sugar made using the mill is profitable when using regular or silver quality beets, at data-sort-value="150">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)150g for 3 sugar it has a higher sell price than 1 beet at regular or silver quality, even with the [Tiller Profession](/Skills#Farming "Skills"). However, this is far less than the data-sort-value="250">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)250g sell price of beet pickles made using the [Preserves Jar](/Preserves_Jar "Preserves Jar").
