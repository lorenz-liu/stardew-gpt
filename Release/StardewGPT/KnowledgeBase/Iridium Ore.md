**Iridium Ore** is a [resource](/Category:Resources "Category:Resources") that can be obtained in a variety of locations (see "Locations" below).

## Locations

* Mining [Iridium Nodes](/Mining#Iridium_Node "Mining") and [Mystic Stones](/Mining#Mystic_Stone "Mining").
  + Iridium Nodes can be found in the [Quarry](/Quarry "Quarry"), [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern"), and [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon"). They can also spawn on [Hill-top](/Farm_Maps#Hilltop "Farm Maps") and [Four Corners](/Farm_Maps#Four_Corners "Farm Maps") farm maps at Mining Skill level 10.  
    Tips: The chance to find Iridium Ore increases steadily the further one descends into the Skull Cavern. Large amounts of [staircases](/Staircase "Staircase") or [bombs](/Crafting#Bombs "Crafting") can get one deep into the Skull Cavern quickly.
  + Mystic Stones can be found in [the Mines](/The_Mines "The Mines") (Floor 100+), [Quarry](/Quarry "Quarry"), [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") and [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon").
* Opening [Magma Geodes](/Magma_Geode "Magma Geode"), [Omni Geodes](/Omni_Geode "Omni Geode"), and [Golden Coconuts](/Golden_Coconut "Golden Coconut").
  + To further enhance geode frequency, choose the [Geologist Profession](/Mining#Mining_Skill "Mining") at [Mining](/Mining "Mining") Level 5 and the [Excavator Profession](/Mining#Mining_Skill "Mining") at [Mining](/Mining "Mining") Level 10.
  + [Krobus](/Krobus "Krobus") sells 1 [Omni Geode](/Omni_Geode "Omni Geode") every [Tuesday](/Days_of_the_Week#Tuesday "Days of the Week") for data-sort-value="300">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)300g each, and the [Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis") sells 3 Omni Geodes every [Wednesday](/Days_of_the_Week#Wednesday "Days of the Week") for data-sort-value="500">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)500g each.
  + The [Oasis](/Oasis "Oasis") sells 10 [Coconuts](/Coconut "Coconut") every [Monday](/Days_of_the_Week#Monday "Days of the Week") for data-sort-value="200">![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)200g each, which can then be traded with the [Island Trader](/Island_Trader "Island Trader") for 1 [Golden Coconut](/Golden_Coconut "Golden Coconut") after having cracked at least one Golden Coconut.
* Rare drop from high level [Monsters](/Monsters "Monsters").
  + [Purple Slimes](/Slimes "Slimes") in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") have a chance to drop both Iridium Ore and [Iridium Bars](/Iridium_Bar "Iridium Bar"). They can be raised in [Slime Hutches](/Slime_Hutch "Slime Hutch").
  + [Iridium Bats](/Bats "Bats") in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") may drop an Iridium Bar (0.8% chance) and up to 4 Iridium Ore when slain.
  + [Iridium Crabs](/Iridium_Crab "Iridium Crab") in the [Skull Cavern](/Skull_Cavern "Skull Cavern") may drop up to 3 Iridium Ore when slain.
  + [Iridium Golems](/Iridium_Golem "Iridium Golem") have a 20% chance to drop Iridium Ore. After reaching [Combat](/Combat "Combat") level 10, both [Iridium Golems](/Iridium_Golem "Iridium Golem") and [Wilderness Golems](/Wilderness_Golem "Wilderness Golem") have a 1% chance to drop Iridium Ore, and a 1% chance of dropping a second one.
* Breaking [crates and barrels](/The_Mines#Crates_and_Barrels "The Mines") in the [dangerous Mines](/The_Mines#Shrine_of_Challenge "The Mines") and [Volcano Dungeon](/Volcano_Dungeon "Volcano Dungeon").
* Catching regular and golden [Fishing Treasure Chests](/Fishing#Treasure_Chests "Fishing").
* A [Super Cucumber](/Super_Cucumber "Super Cucumber") [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond") may produce 1-3 Iridium Ore when the population of the pond reaches 9.
* [Panning](/Pans "Pans") in any area.
* A fallen [meteorite](/Random_Events#Meteorite "Random Events") on [the Farm](/The_Farm "The Farm") will drop 10 Iridium Ore when mined.
* The [Traveling Cart](/Traveling_Cart "Traveling Cart") will occasionally sell it for data-sort-value="100"![Gold.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/10/Gold.png/18px-Gold.png)300–1,000g.
* The [Statue Of Perfection](/Statue_Of_Perfection "Statue Of Perfection"), which can be obtained after getting four candles during [Grandpa's Evaluation](/Grandpa#Grandpa.27s_Evaluation "Grandpa"), gives the player 2-8 Iridium Ore per day.

## Smelting

Iridium Ore can be smelted in a [Furnace](/Furnace "Furnace") to create an [Iridium Bar](/Iridium_Bar "Iridium Bar"). The smelting requires 5 Iridium Ore and 1 [Coal](/Coal "Coal") and takes 8 in-game hours.

## Bundles

One Iridium Ore is used in the ![Bundle Purple.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a1/Bundle_Purple.png/24px-Bundle_Purple.png) [Engineer's Bundle](/Remixed_Bundles#Engineer.27s_Bundle "Remixed Bundles") in the [Boiler Room (Remixed)](/Remixed_Bundles#Boiler_Room "Remixed Bundles").

## Tailoring

Iridium Ore can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the ![Shirt179.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/2/2a/Shirt179.png/24px-Shirt179.png) [Iridium Energy Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a purple dye at the dye pots located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane"). It can also be used as an iridium dye when used in the spool of the Sewing Machine with a dyeable clothing item in the feed.

## Quests

* One Iridium Ore may be requested by [Rainbow Trout](/Rainbow_Trout "Rainbow Trout"), [Spook Fish](/Spook_Fish "Spook Fish"), [Super Cucumber](/Super_Cucumber "Super Cucumber"), or [Void Salmon](/Void_Salmon "Void Salmon") in a [Fish Pond quest](/Fish_Pond#Quests "Fish Pond") to increase the capacity of the pond from 7 to 10.
* [Marlon](/Marlon "Marlon") may request the player to obtain 15 Iridium Ore in the ["Treasure Hunt" quest](/Desert_Festival#Skull_Cavern "Desert Festival") during the [Desert Festival](/Desert_Festival "Desert Festival").

## Trading

Five Iridium Ore can be traded to the [Desert Trader](/Desert_Trader "Desert Trader") for 1 [Mega Bomb](/Mega_Bomb "Mega Bomb").

## Notes

When used with [Slingshots](/Weapons#Slingshot "Weapons"), Iridium Ore deals more damage to monsters than any other ammunition.
