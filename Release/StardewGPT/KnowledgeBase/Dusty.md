# Dusty

[![Dusty3.png](/mediawiki/images/d/d3/Dusty3.png)](/File:Dusty3.png)

**Dusty** is [Alex](/Alex "Alex")'s dog who lives in an [old fenced-off box](/Dog_Pen "Dog Pen") near [The Stardrop Saloon](/The_Stardrop_Saloon "The Stardrop Saloon").

Dusty is involved in two of Alex's heart scenes: [four hearts](/Alex#Four_Hearts "Alex") and [ten hearts](/Alex#Ten_Hearts "Alex"). If you have a high [friendship](/Friendship "Friendship") level with [Pam](/Pam "Pam"), she will tell you that Dusty may look scary, but he is too lazy to attack you.

Normally only Dusty's glowing eyes are visible or you can't see them at all, but he partly appears during the heart events with Alex, and after completing the [Trash Bear's](/Cindersap_Forest#Trash_Bear "Cindersap Forest") requests.

Dusty is at least 12 years old, having apparently witnessed Alex's home life prior to Alex moving in with [George](/George "George") and [Evelyn](/Evelyn "Evelyn").
