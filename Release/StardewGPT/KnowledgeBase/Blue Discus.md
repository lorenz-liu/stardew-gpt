The **Blue Discus** is a [fish](/Fish "Fish") that can be caught on [Ginger Island](/Ginger_Island "Ginger Island") during any season on Island North and West (freshwater).

The [Island Trader](/Island_Trader "Island Trader") sells 1 [Oceanic Rug](/Oceanic_Rug "Oceanic Rug") for 3 Blue Discus on [Saturdays](/Days_of_the_Week#Saturday "Days of the Week").

## Tailoring

Blue Discus can be used in the spool of the [Sewing Machine](/2_Willow_Lane#Sewing_Machine "2 Willow Lane") to create the dyeable [![Shirt259a.png](/mediawiki/images/thumb/3/36/Shirt259a.png/24px-Shirt259a.png)](/File:Shirt259a.png) [Sailor Shirt](/Tailoring "Tailoring"). It can be used in [dyeing](/Dyeing "Dyeing"), serving as a blue dye at the dye pots, located in [Emily](/Emily "Emily")'s and [Haley](/Haley "Haley")'s house, [2 Willow Lane](/2_Willow_Lane "2 Willow Lane").

## Fish Pond

Blue Discus can be placed in a [Fish Pond](/Fish_Pond "Fish Pond"), where they will reproduce every 3 days. The initial pond capacity is 3 fish, but the capacity can be increased to 10 by completing two quests. The primary output is blue Blue Discus [Roe](/Roe "Roe"), but more valuable items are possible starting at population 4.
